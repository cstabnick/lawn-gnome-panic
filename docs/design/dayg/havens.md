# Havens: the safe zones

A **haven** is a place where a group keeps a peace inside its walls: no fighting, no theft, guards who act on it.
Havens are not a game rule; they are **settlements whose norms forbid violence** ([society.md](society.md#norms-and-institutions)),
running on the same simulation as everything else. That means they can grow, change hands, close their gates or fall.

The map has seven hand-made **haven sites** (zones with the `HAVEN` trait): places that are high, have a water source
nearby and a route that can be cut. The seed decides which are up at the start; at least two are always up (the Tap
and one more).

## The sites

| Haven | Where | Usual holder | Character | What you get there |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Tap** | the standpipe on the Yew Walk | the **Truce of the Tap** (a council of every faction that signed) | neutral ground; the one place enemies trade face to face | water, the biggest market, the notice board, bounty office |
| **The Lychgate Waystation** | the gate roof | whoever holds the gate | where the outside traders camp; newcomers start here | the trader market, a bed, cheap jobs, rumours from outside |
| **The Columbarium** | niche rows 3–6 | the **Lantern Keepers** | ordered, lawful, council-run, picky about who comes up | safe sleep, storage niches to rent, skills teachers, the rose market |
| **The Chapel Infirmary** | the nave | the **Kiln Sisters** | heals anyone, bitten or not, in the quarantine pews | glazing, moss scraping, medicine, the only cure attempts |
| **The Willow** | the willow over the pond | the **Willow Folk** | small, fishers, slow to trust | fish, rafts, ferry across the pond |
| **The Bath House** | the stone birdbath in the Gnome Garden | a small band (seed) | a frontier outpost at the edge of the Pile | water, a lookout, a last stop before the Pile |
| **The Shelves** | shed shelves 2–4 | the **Shelf Wrights** | builders and crafters | crafting stations, repairs, tools for hire |

## How a haven keeps the peace

- **The Haven norm**: violence and theft inside are punished by the holder's guards: a warning, then being thrown out,
  then being shot on sight. The **ban** spreads by rumour to allied havens.
- **Who gets in** follows the holder's openness norm: open (anyone), questioned (anyone not wanted, after a check for
  moss), members and friends only, or closed.
- **The moss check**: most havens check for open cracks at the gate. Bitten gnomes are turned away, sent to the
  Infirmary, or (in a harsh haven) worse. Getting a bitten friend in is a classic quest.
- **Inside**, a haven offers: a safe sleep (the strongest pull of all: no watch needed), a market, storage to rent,
  quests on the board, teachers, and a place for players to meet without risk.
- **Players and havens**: killing another player inside a haven gets you banned there and wanted by its guards. This is
  where the PvPvE is softened for groups who don't want PvP: **havens are where solo and small-group players are always
  safe** ([multiplayer.md](multiplayer.md)).

## What it costs to keep one

A haven is expensive: guards to pay or feed, walls to mend, a big signature (fires, smoke, noise) that pulls herds, and
many mouths. The sim balances that against what a haven earns: market tolls, rent, trade, and members' work. A haven
that can't pay closes its gate (fewer visitors, less income) or falls.

## How havens change

| Change | Cause | What players see |
|---|---|---|
| A new haven | a faction holds an empty haven site long enough with a surplus | a new safe market on the map |
| Handover | a stronger faction takes it (war, merger, or the holder breaks up) | new rules at the gate; old bans dropped, new ones made |
| Closing the gate | food short, a Drift coming, a war | "members only"; the quests become about getting in |
| Turning | a strongest-rules leader takes over and drops the peace | a haven becomes a raider den (it loses the `HAVEN` status) |
| Falling | Hatless or raiders break the defences | a loot rush, refugees, a quest storm |

[evaluation.md](evaluation.md) holds the targets: at least 2 havens up in 95% of worlds at every point, and haven
handovers happening at a healthy rate (some, not constant).
