## 1. Life in the Broadcast Archipelago

The Broadcast Archipelago is not an empty skatepark—it is a chain of floating sky-towns, industrial kilns, highway diners, and rooftop resorts that exist *because* Turfton's pirate radio signals leak upward into the Aether.

- **Why Fired-Clay Tourists Ride Up:** Gnomes from the lower Aether islands (and occasional day-trippers who ride the pink ley-line gondolas) come to the Archipelago for **Aether-acoustics tourism**: sipping espresso-dew in Velvet Keys, eating acorn-chili at Bongo Basin's Big Acorn Diner, watching molten glaze poured at Chrome Kiln's public overlook, and honeymooning beneath Neon Birdbath Spire's glowing mist lagoon.
- **Why Each Gang Holds Its Turf:** The gangs are not cartoon villains—they are stubborn local scenes protecting their craft, pride, or nostalgia. Maestro Upright believes only D Dorian conservatory jazz deserves a rooftop stage; Conga King Kora keeps the parcel-truckers' polyrhythm alive; Furnace Vera's union smelters refuse to let their acid-funk boilers cool; Fountainhead Finn guards DJ Broadcast's ancestral birdbath like a museum curator.
- **How Woken Objects & Gnomes Share Space:** Woken instruments and tools (`gsr-saxophone-woken`, `gsr-glaze-trolley`, `gsr-metronome`, `gsr-fire-hydrant`) work alongside fired-clay gnomes—busking on street corners, hauling molten glaze vats, or running tourist kiosks. Nobody bleeds; tagging out a Woken object simply detunes it into harmless clay-glaze sparks.

## 2. The 9 Regions: Spatial Topology, Buildings, Environmental Life & Writing Beats

### Area 1 — Velvet Keys Plaza *(Playable)*
- **Spatial Topology:** Asymmetric Jazz Courtyard & Cultural Quarter. A grand central Conservatory Terrace flanked by a western Vinyl Rotunda, an eastern Espresso-Dew Jazz Cafe, a northeastern Brass-Bell Amphitheater, and a southwestern Tourist Kiosk, connected by piano-key walkways and a bubbling fountain plaza.
- **4–5 Unique Buildings:**
  1. **Grand Conservatory & Penthouse Stage** (`conservatory-hall`) — Stained-glass arched halls, piano-key facade frieze, and Maestro Upright's 7m open-air recital roof.
  2. **The Vinyl Rotunda** (`vinyl-tower`) — Pressing plant with a giant spinning vinyl marquee on the facade and twin broadcast antennas.
  3. **Espresso-Dew Jazz Cafe** (`cafe-terrace`) — Striped awnings, warm glowing storefront windows, and rooftop pergola patio.
  4. **Brass-Bell Amphitheater** (`rotunda-dome`) — Colonnaded dome-rim hall where brass Woken rehearse and tourists peer through coin telescopes.
  5. **Velvet Keys Postcard & Ticket Kiosk** (`cafe-terrace`) — Compact southwestern booth selling conservatory programs and Aether postcards.
- **Environmental & Tourist Life:** Central **Piano Fountain** (`gsr-piano-fountain`) with animated mist jets, **Piano-Key Walkways**, outdoor **Cafe Tables** (`gsr-cafe-table`) with espresso-dew mugs, **Postcard Kiosk** (`gsr-postcard-kiosk`), **Face-in-Hole Photo Standee** (`gsr-photo-cutout`), **Scenic Telescope** (`gsr-telescope-stand`) on the Amphitheater roof, **Mist Benches** (`gsr-mist-bench`), and a festival **Banner Arch**.
- **Cast & New Characters:**
  - *Pippi Pitchpipe* (Helper Gnome — Conservatory Tuner)
  - *Maestro Upright* & *Pluck* (Ivory Syndicate)
  - *Saxy Sal* (`gsr-saxophone-woken`) — Street-corner alto saxophonist who riffs on the plaza
  - *Bohemian Jazz Critic*, *Honeymooning Photo Tourists*, *Conservatory Student*, *Crate-Digger Gnome*
- **Writing Beats & Voice:** *Bohemian conservatory snobbery vs. warm street-busker groove.* Critics argue over whether D Dorian ninths count as "street music," tourists pose at the cutout while dropping their postcards, and Saxy Sal insists the best acoustics are by the fountain.

### Area 2 — Bongo Basin Overpass *(Playable)*
- **Spatial Topology:** Sweeping S-Curve Highway Interchange & Roadside Diner. Diagonal tarmac arcs replace the rigid grid, with a Southbound Viaduct, a West Toll-Gate Rhythm Deck, an East Conga Grain-Silo, a roadside Big Acorn Diner, and Kora's Cloverleaf Crown.
- **4–5 Unique Buildings:**
  1. **Southbound Viaduct Span** (`overpass-deck`) — Heavy concrete piers, amber highway barriers, and overhead steel gantry signs.
  2. **The Big Acorn Diner & Rest Stop** (`diner-stop`) — Cantilevered chrome roof visor, neon diner window band, and glowing rooftop fin.
  3. **West Toll-Gate Rhythm Deck** (`overpass-deck`) — Elevated 6.2m toll-plaza deck where parcel-gnomes pay in rhythm claps.
  4. **Conga Grain-Silo Tower** (`drum-silo`) — Cylindrical 7.5m percussion silo bound with brass tension-lugs and glowing drum-hoops.
  5. **Cloverleaf Crown & Kora's Stage** (`overpass-deck` + `drum-silo`) — 12m stacked interchange crown overlooking the S-curve.
- **Environmental & Tourist Life:** Towering **Big Acorn Diner Neon Sign** (`gsr-roadside-diner-sign`), **Rhythm Toll Booths** (`gsr-toll-booth`), **Highway Chevrons**, **"World's Biggest Bongo" Photo Cutout** (`gsr-photo-cutout`), diner **Picnic Tables** (`gsr-cafe-table`), **Postcard Kiosk**, and **Highway Welcome Banner Arch**.
- **Cast & New Characters:**
  - *Bam-Bam Bramble* (Helper Gnome — Viaduct Percussion Rigger)
  - *Conga King Kora* (Rip-Skin Collective)
  - *Spout* (Runaway Hydrant) & *Subsoil Cassette Deck*
  - *Diner Waitress Brenda* (serves hot acorn-chili), *Long-Haul Parcel Trucker*, *Toll-Gate Collector*, *Road-Trip Tourist*
- **Writing Beats & Voice:** *Blue-collar road-trip & roadside-diner camaraderie.* Truckers trade shortcut tips over acorn-chili, Brenda yells orders over the conga line, and toll collectors cheer when skaters clear the barrier without paying.

### Area 3 — Chrome Kiln Foundry *(Playable)*
- **Spatial Topology:** Split Industrial Canyon & Molten Glaze Valley. An open central north-south **Molten Glaze Channel** slices straight down the middle (`x = 0`), flanked by massive asymmetrical East and West smelter halls, towering kiln chimneys, and Furnace Vera's Crucible Bridge at the north end.
- **4–5 Unique Buildings:**
  1. **West Blast-Smelter** (`smelter-furnace`) — Twin roaring smokestacks, glowing molten grate vents, and heavy industrial framing.
  2. **East Glaze-Press Hall** (`foundry-hall`) — External steel buttress ribs and glowing lime skylight strips where clay boots are glazed.
  3. **North-West Kiln Chimney** (`kiln-chimney`) — 9m cylindrical-ringed stack wrapped in glowing heat-collars and corner exhaust pipes.
  4. **North-East Cooling Tower & Union Canteen** (`foundry-hall`) — 7.5m terraced foundry hall hosting the union lunch tables and tour overlook.
  5. **Crucible Bridge & Crown** (`smelter-furnace` + `kiln-chimney`) — 13m central smelter span where Furnace Vera hammers out 138 BPM acid-funk.
- **Environmental & Tourist Life:** Central **Molten Glaze Channel** (`molten-channel`) with iron grate walkways, bubbling **Molten Glaze Vats** (`gsr-glaze-vat`), towering **Gantry Cranes** (`gsr-gantry-crane`), **Union Canteen Tables**, **Public Tour Telescope** (`gsr-telescope-stand`), **"DAYS SINCE GLAZE SPILL: 0" Banner Arch**, and rolling **Woken Glaze-Trolleys** (`gsr-glaze-trolley`).
- **Cast & New Characters:**
  - *Sooty Slagcap* (Helper Gnome — Foundry Updraft Spotter)
  - *Furnace Vera* (Glaze Grinders)
  - *Trundle* (`gsr-glaze-trolley`) — Loyal wheeled glaze cart who mutters about kiln temperatures
  - *Union Foreman*, *Nervous Safety Inspector*, *Lunch-Break Apprentice*, *Foundry Tour Guide*
- **Writing Beats & Voice:** *Proud union-craftsman swagger & dry safety-inspector humor.* Craftsmen brag that half the boots in the Aether were glazed right here, the safety inspector sighs at skaters using exhaust vents as elevators, and Trundle warns everyone not to splash his vat.

### Area 4 — Neon Birdbath Spire *(Playable)*
- **Spatial Topology:** Concentric Spiral Sky-Resort Lagoon. A shimmering central **Mist Lagoon** (`mist-pool`) and grand **Fountain Plaza** ringed by five spiral-ascending resort & glass-blower towers (SW 3.5m Arrival Pier → SE 5.5m Glass Pavilion → E 8.5m Mist-Spa → NW 11m Sky-Lounge Observatory → N 16.5m Birdbath Apex Lighthouse).
- **4–5 Unique Buildings:**
  1. **Arrival Pier & Tourist Boardwalk** (`resort-terrace`) — 3.5m welcoming pier with lantern posts, postcard stall, and photo cutout.
  2. **Glass-Blower's Neon Pavilion** (`glass-pavilion`) — 5.5m translucent cyan/pink glass workshop crowned with a floating neon halo ring.
  3. **East Mist-Spa Basin** (`fountain-basin`) — 8.5m scalloped water-rim terrace with a rooftop Piano Fountain and mist benches.
  4. **West Observatory & Sky-Lounge** (`glass-pavilion`) — 11m stargazing deck with coin telescopes, cafe tables, and a midnight saxophonist.
  5. **Spire Basin & Birdbath Apex** (`fountain-basin` + `lighthouse-spire`) — 16.5m spiral neon-ringed lighthouse where DJ Broadcast spun his very first record.
- **Environmental & Tourist Life:** Central **Mist Lagoon** (`mist-pool`) + animated **Fountain Plaza**, rooftop **Piano Fountain**, **Postcard Kiosk**, **Photo Cutout**, **Cafe Tables**, **Scenic Telescopes**, **Mist Benches**, and **Midnight Saxophone Woken**.
- **Cast & New Characters:**
  - *Drizzle Dewdrop* (Helper Gnome — Spire Glass-Bender)
  - *Fountainhead Finn* & *Gus* (Splash-Back Skaters)
  - *Honeymooning Clay Couple*, *Master Glass-Blower*, *Midnight-Radio Nostalgist*, *Postcard Vendor*, *Lydian Lounge Sax* (`gsr-saxophone-woken`)
- **Writing Beats & Voice:** *Dreamy late-night rooftop resort & tourist-postcard nostalgia.* Honeymooners debate which postcard filter looks best under cyan neon, the glass-blower remembers when DJ Broadcast was just a kid with a bent birdbath, and midnight listeners watch the cloud deck below.
