# Gnom Set Radio — World & Lore Bible

> **Setting:** `the-aether` (The Broadcast Archipelago, Upper Aether)
> **Crossovers:** Bridges `aether-board` (the candy-pink ethereal plane and glowing ley-lines) with leaked radio signals from `gnome-theft-auto` (Turfton FM, 108.0 Subsoil Radio, Black Dew boosters, Clay-Mart, Mulch Club, HOA Code-Enforcement).

---

## 1. Premise: The Broadcast Archipelago

High above the snowboard islands of `aether-board`, the glowing ley-lines do not end—they braid upward into a vertical archipelago of floating plazas, terraced highway overpasses, kiln-stacks, and suspended rooftops. This upper stratum is **The Broadcast Archipelago**.

Nobody knows how radio waves from Turfton's streets climb into the ethereal plane (a loose thread shared with `the-aether`), but pirate broadcasts—**Turfton FM**, **The Blade**, **Fescue Public Radio**, and **108.0 Subsoil Radio**—saturate the pink sky. Where those frequencies knot around ley-lines, they form solid, grindable **Signal Rails** and wake up ordinary yard and street objects.

---

## 2. The Woken (Living Inanimate Objects)

When a ley-line resonates with a strong Turfton broadcast, inanimate objects **wake up**. They sprout clay-glaze eyes, glowing frequency vents, and rhythmic voices (spoken through `@lawn/mutter` in-game), adopting the musical style and attitude of the frequency that woke them:

- **No Tall Ones, No Gore:** Core gnome canon holds everywhere. No humans appear. Woken objects and fired-clay gnomes never bleed—when defeated or "tagged out," they detune, pop into harmless clay-glaze sparks, or go quiet until re-tuned.
- **Iconic Woken:**
  - **Maestro Upright** (a lacquered Upright Piano) and **Pluck** (a carved Upright Bass) lead the Ivory Syndicate in Velvet Keys Plaza.
  - **Conga King Kora** (a crowned hover-conga) and her living Conga/Timbale sentries patrol Bongo Basin Overpass.
  - **Tick-Tock Tilda** (a brass Metronome), **Sly Vinyl** (a bootleg Mulch Club record crate), **Spout** (a Turfton fire hydrant), and **Subsoil Cassette Deck** (humming DJ Moleman's 108.0 Subsoil Radio) populate the side-stories.

---

## 3. The Player Crew: Ley-Line Runners

- **The Player:** Your own gnome from `@lawn/gnome` (the Gnome Creator), wearing inline **Aether Rollerblades**—twin-rail clay boots fitted with glowing ley-line wheels that carve, wall-ride, and grind any frequency rail.
- **Guide:** **DJ Broadcast**, spinning from a floating birdbath spire at the edge of the Archipelago (echoing Turfton's "DJ Lawn-Dart" birdbath mythos). DJ Broadcast's mission: reclaim the 8 jammed broadcast districts from turf-war gangs by tagging their billboards, out-skating their lieutenants, and retuning their bosses' rigs—before an unexplained ninth signal wakes up.
