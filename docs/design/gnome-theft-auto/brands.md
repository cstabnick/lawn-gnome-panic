# Gnome Theft Auto: Fictional Brands, Logos & Corporate Rivalries

> **Companion to:** [story.md](story.md) · [world-lore.md](world-lore.md)  
> **Setting:** `apocrypha` ([docs/lore/settings.md](../../lore/settings.md))

In *Grand Theft Auto*, half the storytelling happens on billboards, bumper stickers, soda cans, and radio ads. In Turfton, every blade of grass is sponsored. All brands below are fictional—inspired by lawn-care gear, garden supplies, and everyday consumer culture, without copying real-world trademarks.

---

## 1. Mower & Heavy Equipment Manufacturers

### **BUCK-IRON** *(Heavy Green & Gold)*
- **Inspired by:** Classic agricultural tractor & ride-on giants.
- **Slogan:** *"Nothing Runs Like A Buck (Until You Take The Keys)."*
- **What They Make:** The standard **Ride-On Mower** (`ride-on-mower`) and the **Combine Harvester** (`combine-harvester`). Overbuilt, loud, and famously easy to hotwire with a butter knife.
- **Visual Presence:** Giant green billboards along the `south` highway in Cloverfield Farms; mudflaps on Combines.

### **RAZOR-BACK** *(Safety Orange & Matte Black)*
- **Inspired by:** High-RPM zero-turn & power-tool makers.
- **Slogan:** *"Zero Turning Radius. Zero Regrets."*
- **What They Make:** **Zero-Turn Racers** (`zero-turn-mower`) and the civilian **Leaf-Blower Cannon**. Favored by **The Carburettors** street crew.
- **Visual Presence:** Neon orange banners at Frostbite Fells' midnight birdbath derbies; bright orange decals on street-tuned racers.

### **HUSKY-BLADE** *(Blue & Silver)*
- **Inspired by:** Two-stroke outdoor power equipment brands.
- **Slogan:** *"Loud Enough To Wake The Tall Ones."*
- **What They Make:** **Push-Mower Bikes** (`push-mower-bike`), chainsaws-turned-hedge-trimmers, and illegal two-stroke exhaust kits sold at *Side-Valve Salvage*.

### **The Great Mower Rivalry: BUCK-IRON vs. RAZOR-BACK**
- **The Beef:** Buck-Iron runs smug radio ads calling Razor-Back *"over-caffeinated blenders on caster wheels."* Razor-Back claps back with billboards showing a Zero-Turn doing donuts around a stuck Buck-Iron Combine: *"Still Turning Since Tuesday."*
- **In-World Impact:** Turfton mechanics refuse to work on the rival brand without charging a 20% "Emotional Damage Surcharge." Drivers in Buck-Iron hats will honk aggressively at Razor-Back riders at red lights.

## 2. Fuel, Fertilizer & Garden Chemicals

### **BLACK DEW™** *(Amber & Chrome)*
- **What It Is:** Turfton's premier refined petrol brand (drawn from the Compost Heap & Cloverfield Dew-Wells).
- **Slogan:** *"87 Octane. 100% Unlicensed."*
- **Visual Presence:** Roadside pump canopies on the `east` and `south` highways, branded fuel barrels you can shoot to cause dramatic (water-and-soot, zero-gore) chain reactions.

### **STINK-GRO** *(Neon Lime & Purple)*
- **Inspired by:** Miracle-brand synthetic plant foods.
- **Slogan:** *"Grow Twice As Tall By Thursday. Don't Ask How."*
- **What They Make:** Hyper-concentrated fertilizer pellets, glowing neon-green lawn paint (used by the HOA to fake healthy grass), and pressurized **Stink-Gro** fertilizer canisters.
- **Secret:** Rumored to be distilled from the *Chapel of Mulch*'s leftover runoff.

### **PURE-LOAM CO-OP** *(Earthy Brown & Hemp)*
- **What It Is:** The Unmowed's artisanal, organic compost collective in Mossy Wood.
- **Slogan:** *"No Chemicals. No Engines. Just Dirt."*
- **Rivalry (STINK-GRO vs. PURE-LOAM):** Stink-Gro billboards mock Pure-Loam as *"Expensive Mud For Gnomes With Beards."* Pure-Loam counters by quietly sabotaging Stink-Gro delivery trucks with dandelion bombs.

## 3. Food, Drink & Everyday Society

### **ACORN-COLA** *(Crimson & White)*
- **Inspired by:** Classic red-can cola giants.
- **Slogan:** *"Open A Cold One. Crack A Smile (Not Your Hat)."*
- **What You See:** Vending machines on every Turfton sidewalk, glowing neon signs outside *The Gnomecino*, and crushed cans in gutters.

### **DANDELION FIZZ** *(Electric Yellow)*
- **Inspired by:** Extreme neon citrus sodas.
- **Slogan:** *"110% Daily Value Of Pollen & Bad Decisions."*
- **Rivalry (ACORN-COLA vs. DANDELION FIZZ):** Acorn-Cola sponsors the **Hat Watch** softball team; Dandelion Fizz sponsors illegal **Zero-Turn** street races and **Aether-Ramp** jumps in Frostbite Fells. John Bodega keeps both in his cooler, though Asphalt the cat only sleeps on the Acorn-Cola crates.

### **CHIRP & CRUMB** *(Warm Bakery Pink)*
- **What It Is:** Turfton's fast-food birdseed-and-pastry chain, locked in a bitter cold war with **Gnoma's Food Truck** (*Cooking Gnoma*).
- **Slogan:** *"Freshly Baked Since 4:00 AM. Hard Enough To Patch A Wall."*

### **CLAY-MART** *(Blue & Yellow)*
- **Inspired by:** Big-box suburban superstores.
- **Slogan:** *"Everything Under One Giant Upside-Down Tupperware."*
- **What You See:** A massive warehouse on the edge of Turfton's grid selling discount garden hats, plastic flamingos, and budget umbrellas.
- **Rivalry (CLAY-MART vs. BODEGA & SONS):** Clay-Mart tried to open across the street from **John Bodega** (`x: -60, z: 60`). Their delivery snail mysteriously got lost in the Under-Deck for three weeks, and now nobody speaks of it.

## 4. Services, Finance & Civic Parodies

### **THORNE LAWN & ORDER LLC** *(Obsidian & Gold)*
- **Owner:** Vesper Thorne.
- **Slogan:** *"Uniformity Is Peace. Peace Is Mandatory."*
- **What You See:** Sleek black-and-gold HOA enforcement vans, foreclosure notices taped to flowerpots, and spotless astro-turf samples in Patio Heights.

### **PAY 'N' GLAZE** *(Hot Pink & Chrome)*
- **Slogan:** *"New Coat. Who Dis?"*
- **What You See:** Roll-up garage doors in downtown Turfton and the Greenhouse Strip. Drive in with 3-Hat Heat and a smoking mower; drive out 5 seconds later in a fresh `MOWER_TINTS` colour while the Hat Watch drives right past you.

### **KILN-SHIELD INSURANCE** *(Teal & White)*
- **Slogan:** *"We Cover Chips, Cracks, And 'Acts Of Mower.'"*
- **Fine Print (on billboards):** *"Does NOT cover porcelain, Fort Sandpit trespassing, Crop Duster landings, or anything involving Pip Potsworth."*

### **SNAIL-MAIL EXPRESS** vs. **PIGEON-DROP COURIERS**
- **The Rivalry:** Snail-Mail Express (*"Eventually Is Still On Time"*) is slow, reliable, and unionized (featuring **Shelldon "No-Trail" Mucilage**). Pigeon-Drop Couriers (*"Dropped From Altitude, Good Luck"*) is fast, chaotic, and regularly flattens mailboxes in Cloverfield Farms.

| **Highway Billboards** | Giant roadside boards along `east`, `west`, `north`, and `south` highways pitching *Buck-Iron*, *Kiln-Shield*, *Stink-Gro*, and *The Gnomecino*. |

| **Sidewalk Clutter** | *Acorn-Cola* vending machines, *Clay-Mart* shopping baskets, and *Chirp & Crumb* paper bags blowing across `ROAD_LINES`. |
