## 1. Purpose in the story

The high of the game (batch-3 notes: tension 7, `triumph`). Granny's pie ladder pays its #64 rung, "I brought pie.":
the one gag. Then the sincere last minute with nothing on top: Tansy does what she did with her wrenches in the
prologue ("Forty-five. Forty-six. Forty-seven.") and at the party ("No speech. Inventory."), but the inventory is
people now. "All hats." names the coalition (lore: coalition-of-all-hats).

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** they hung every cap themselves.
- **Out:** everyone they helped is at one table, and Tansy counted them.
