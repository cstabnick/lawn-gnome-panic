## 1. Purpose in the story

The Carburettors' entrance into the coalition (By Road). Lulu shows she remembers the player (the Capwood Run, the
impound) and pays it back with a road. R-233: only three stayed; the rest took the free tyres.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** a long drive out to Rutbottom.
- **Out:** a small, loud, loyal convoy, and the job: stay behind Lulu's bike.

## 3. Characters

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lulu | bring everyone she has | everyone she has is three cars | laugh first, tease | owes the player a road, says so |
| Fennimore | drive the captain's car | he is the youngest | ask | gets the keys |
| Bertha, Dunstan | be counted | | one line each | |

## 4. Subtext

Most of her club sold out for tyres; she makes it a joke so it does not have to be anything else.

## 6. Shot list

Track on the bike from up the pits; `coverage(lulu, player, 1)` throughout, Lulu low (she sits high for once); the
column-low stage spot for the reveal; MCU singles on the Carburettors; the high gate wide for the button.

## 8. Music and sound

`turfton` (the crew's world, good news), bed `wind`; engine-rev, skid, crunch, horn, keys; no stinger (m40 spent the
triumph stinger on four horns; this one is lights, then horns).

## 10. The button

Three sets of headlights coming on down the line in the gold dust, and the horns: the road is ready.
