## 1. Purpose in the story

The laugh after Diggs. Cam, in a chain-link cell, apologises to his broom (the broom ladder's payoff starts). Juniper, one side of a phone call, sets the job: Cam knows the Combine's PA code and route.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** the Diggs outro's quiet.
- **Out:** a grin, then a plan.

## 3. Characters

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cam | to be forgiven by someone | a broom does not answer | apologises to it | flinches at the speaker |
| Juniper | to get the code | Cam is in the lockup | dry, in full sentences | hands the choice to the player |

## 4. Subtext

Cam's list of apologies hides that nobody has spoken to him all day. Juniper "tries not to enjoy" it and does.
