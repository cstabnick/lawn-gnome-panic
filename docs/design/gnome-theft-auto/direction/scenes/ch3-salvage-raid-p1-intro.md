## 1. Purpose in the story

Opens the act II closer. The morning after the party, Cam is not in, and nobody has ever had to say that before. It sets
the clock running on the betrayal without naming it: the cot nobody slept in, the broom left behind (the broom ladder:
it is the first time it is out of his hand), and Pip remembering the word Cam kept saying. The player saw Cam's call in
row 34's outro; this scene lets the crew catch up one word at a time.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** warm from the party, the bunting still up.
- **Out:** a small cold stone: Cam is not late, Cam is gone somewhere on purpose, and today is the day he named.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 4. Subtext

Tansy knows what a sweeper who leaves his broom means; she will not say it in front of Pip. Pip drank both teas so
nobody would know Cam stood him up: he is protecting Cam, the last time anyone does.
