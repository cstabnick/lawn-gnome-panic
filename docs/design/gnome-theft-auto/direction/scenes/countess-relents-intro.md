## 1. Purpose in the story

The Countess reverses. She funded the Even Line for six harvests and never asked where it ran; it runs through her first vine. She trades the key to the Combine's shield grates for the vine taken to safety, to the one corner no mower goes (R-234). Brisk, not tender.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** summoned.
- **Out:** a woman who has just learned to say thank you in advance, and a vine to save.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |

| Chicory | to save the vine | his duty | a sigh, dry corrections | carries the key |
