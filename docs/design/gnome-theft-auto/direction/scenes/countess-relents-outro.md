## 1. Purpose in the story

The vine is in the book. Chicory walks out of the meadow with the key, hands it over ("Both hands") and waters the vine before he speaks to anyone. The Countess rings: she paid for six harvests and never looked; she is looking now; thank you.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** the quiet after hard work.
- **Out:** moved; the key is heavy.
