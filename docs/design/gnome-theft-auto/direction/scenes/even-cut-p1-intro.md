## 1. Purpose in the story

The first scene of the finale. It says where the story is (the Combine turned onto the meridian at dawn), pays off
R-234's beacon (lit at dawn on the longest day, as Hogweed promised in row 64 p2), and shows the coalition built in act
IV answering it at once, place by place. Then the air wing and the job. Chapter title.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** the night before is over; a lollipop sign in an empty road; a machine gone north up the middle of town.
- **Out:** everybody is in. Two old men are in the air for you. Four marshals on a road are yours.

## 4. Subtext

Wally stands up at the camp: he stands only for something in the air (characters.md). Diggs salutes in the cockpit:
the Guard is with the city now. Hector, who would not bring the Mayor a coffee in three days, takes an order from a fire
on a hill: even Pardonlin is in.

## 6. Shot list

Montage shots are locked (spots and a ground-level cut at the Burrow Door: moles at eye height, batch-3 notes 6.5). The
formation is a ground `track` up the meridian, then two seated `ms` frames swung 60 degrees off each pilot. The air view
is a cut from 24 m behind the aircraft. The table is `coverage('tansy', 'player', 1)`, a reaction on the player and a
low push-in on Tansy for the order; the title dolly comes off the hut side. The Combine is a prop at the runner's scale
5, never moved inside a shot.
