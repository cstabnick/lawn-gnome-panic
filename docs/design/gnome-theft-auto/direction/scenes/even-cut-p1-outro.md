## 1. Purpose in the story

The Vanguard is down and the machine comes on alone: its shadow on the road (outline). It also hands the Countess's key
from the player to Pip, so the next scene can open with him at the lock, and pays the key's "Both hands" (row 65).

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** four marshals cracked under friendly strafing.
- **Out:** the escort was the easy part; the thing itself is a street wide and it is still coming.
