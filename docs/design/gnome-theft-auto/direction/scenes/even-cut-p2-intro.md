## 1. Purpose in the story

The money part of the Combine pays out: the Countess's key opens the shield grates (row 65's trade), and she says so
herself, with what it cost. The guidance part pays out: Betony's Even Line makes the machine's position a sum, so the
drop is a jump to where it will be. Then the plane.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** back at the overlook, the escort down, Pip somewhere at the machine with the key.
- **Out:** the way in is open, from above only; jump where the line says. The plane is warm.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pip | turn the key | it will not turn | both arms, then panic | the grates open; he says nothing for once |
| Countess Wisteria | see her grates used | her pride | observes, never asks | admits the six harvests out loud |
| Betony | give the last mark | her own digressions | "As I was saying" | the Even Line, used against its owner |

## 4. Subtext

The Countess can "hear" a key not turning from Trellisdown because she has been waiting by the phone. "Six harvests"
repeats what it cost her in row 65; Pip's silent nod is the respect nobody says.
