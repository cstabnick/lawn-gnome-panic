## 1. Purpose in the story

The payoff of the quest and the sincere beat of the row: the third signature is exactly Reginald's. Juniper hoped for a forgery.
This is the first crack in the siblings' double act (they always insult each other; here she does not), and it sets up
`m12-quarter-inch-at-home`.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** the adrenaline of the night-watch and a long drive.
- **Out:** quiet, and a little sorry for her.
