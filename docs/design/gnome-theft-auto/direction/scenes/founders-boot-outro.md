## 1. Purpose in the story

The act I turn, E20 rewrite. The crew's public act becomes the city's: Juniper, at the Amphitheater with the Boot still
in the jury box, rezones Vesper's three condemned blocks as Founders' Green (ribbon, crowd). Juniper changes from client
to ally; Tansy's "Stamped. Filed. Ours." now covers a park as well as her deed; Hedda's headline pays off her p1 price.
Asphalt's ladder rung ("she knew") stays. Then the threat: Vesper alone at the glass of his tower. The rezoning is why
"Then we stop asking." lands: he needs the Council's permission to build, and will no longer ask for it (sets up act IV,
City Hall seized). "Freeze the Black Dew. Cloverfield first." stays as row 16's plant.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** the escape was loud; the outro opens on a public, hopeful moment.
- **Out:** cold. Hope first, then the threat on its heels; the act ends on Vesper, never on a gag.

## 4. Subtext

Juniper rezones her brother's employer's land in front of the tribunal her brother ran; they are not in the same frame
(the siblings' double act). Reginald says "As a park" with a quarter-inch of satisfaction he would never admit. Vesper is
not angry; he is deciding.
