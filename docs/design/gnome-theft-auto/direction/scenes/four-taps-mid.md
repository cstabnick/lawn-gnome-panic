## 1. Purpose in the story

The reunion with the Mayor somewhere with its own character: Pardonlin, where no HOA letter can be served past the Wickets (R-183) and the waiter Hector Disdain (first card) has not taken an order in three days. The Mayor sits out his second coat in the sun with his arms held away from him, and picks the winning side the moment he is told which it is.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** a long drive, a gate that stamped the hat.
- **Out:** a laugh before the work, and a Mayor to keep clean for the whole road home.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |

| Mayor Mulch | to be on the winning side | his drying coat; a fear of four | slogans, then relief | agrees to come |

## 4. Subtext

- "Who is winning, back home?" is the Mayor's whole character in one line.
- Hector's "Yes." to "It is Friday!" is the Pardonlin joke: the service is the delay.
