## 1. Purpose in the story

The Mayor signs the counter-order with the Club's own pen, asks whether the ink matches his coat ("No."), and the Don taps a fifth time, which Lou has never heard and translates as yes. Three Lowriders put their lamps on and roll out: the Club's crews. Coat ladder: the coat is wrong, so a third one waits in the epilogue.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** relief, the no-towels gag still in the ear.
- **Out:** the Club is in the war, and it looks like it.
