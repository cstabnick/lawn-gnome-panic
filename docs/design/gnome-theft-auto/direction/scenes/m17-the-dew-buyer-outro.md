## 1. Purpose in the story

Show where the Dew goes (burned in frost-pots) and who stands above it (the Countess, glimpsed), and give no reason. The
Countess does not see the player and her clipboard is never read; Chicory says his own line to the pot.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** hunched among the vines, camera in hand.
- **Out:** that the money is bigger than a vineyard, and that nobody saw them.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chicory | to light the pots | his conscience | work, one dry line | lit |
| Countess Wisteria | to observe | nothing | stillness, glasses, a clipboard | turns away |
| The player | to take the proof | being seen | quiet | slips out |

## 4. Subtext

Chicory's secret (he burns Granny's Dew on the Countess's order) is under his line; the scene shows the order's
shape (an HOA clipboard) and nothing more. The reason Dew is burned is not given.
