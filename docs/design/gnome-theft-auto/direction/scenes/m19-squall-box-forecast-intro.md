## 1. Purpose in the story

Introduces Rooster (first proper scene, intro card) and sets a comic job: three barometers, three fields, a front that will not come. Plants the white-hat forecast the diner pays off.

## 2. The player's feeling

In: Granny's farm is quiet. Out: amused, sure of the job (carry three barometers to three fields, do not bounce them).

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rooster | be believed | a cloudless noon | shouts, flaps, capitals | sends the player off |
| Bramble | keep the weather honest | Rooster | one flat line each | unmoved |
| Player | understand the job | the shouting | two dry lines | takes the job |

## 4. Subtext

Rooster's fear is the barometer sticking at Fair; the three needles are all on FAIR in the insert. He is right more often than he is believed.
