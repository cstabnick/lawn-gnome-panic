## 1. Purpose in the story

It plants act III's four parts without answering any: this was one van of four; it carried a deck, a crate, a place name and a
page with no route. The rest of the convoy does not wait for the lead van. It goes over the snow line.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** pleased with the job, a little cold.
- **Out:** uneasy: the road north is longer than the map says, and the others went three other ways.

## 3. Characters

The player, with the clipboard, who says what is on the page; Tansy, by phone, who says "one of four". Nobody else is on screen.

## 4. Subtext

Tansy counts the vans because counting is how she calms down. "Bring me the paper" is a thing she can do something with.
