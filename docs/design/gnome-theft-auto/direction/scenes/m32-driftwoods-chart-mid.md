## 1. Purpose in the story
The laugh after Granny's cracked scene. Baroness Brenda tolls everything that floats. Driftwood's one calm sentence, "the canal does not end at your sea", is the first time she has no proclamation, and she answers by extending the Republic to wherever it does not end. The toll stays four spark plugs.

## 2. The player's feeling
- **In:** concentrating on a steady tow.
- **Out:** a laugh, then the long drive south with a sense that the world is bigger than Brenda's claim.

## 4. Subtext
