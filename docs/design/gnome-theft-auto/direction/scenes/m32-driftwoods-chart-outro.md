## 1. Purpose in the story
Plants the edges of the map: the sea Turfton calls a puddle is real, and something out there answers a bell. Driftwood sees the glow first and cannot cross yet. Nothing says what is across; the player is left looking at a light.

## 2. The player's feeling
- **In:** a long drive after a laugh.
- **Out:** quiet wonder, a little dread, a reason to come back.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Driftwood | cross | a raft is not a ship, and he is not a captain | long looks, short sentences, the bell | certainty becomes humility |
| Player | understand | a light that is not the sunset | flat questions | shares the look |

## 4. Subtext
Six months of being laughed at. He is not afraid of the sea. He is afraid of arriving as a hat on a raft.
