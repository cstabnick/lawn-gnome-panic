## 1. Purpose in the story
The crew is on the run and the bend is no longer safe. Theo turns "we are hiding" into "we are going to someone": Gentian, the only one who owes him. The warm start of the act's warm stretch.

## 2. The player's feeling
- **In:** just glued Pip's arm, a scroll from Tansy in hand, the plows one step behind.
- **Out:** a mug in the hand, a name (Gentian), a direction (north-west), and a diesel that is not a friend.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Theo | get them fed, then gone | they want to hide in his barrel | tea first, proverbs, a pointing arm | the bend stops being a refuge |
| Pip | stay put and keep safe | the plows | questions nobody asked | hears "a bend is for resting" |
| Player | a plan | nowhere to go | flat questions | takes the mug |

## 4. Subtext
Theo is afraid of the day the barrel goes out; he is sending them away to save them, and he will not say so. The forty socks are how he says he loves Gentian.

## 7. Dialogue pass
Eleven lines, all under 16 words, Theo's gentle rhyme ("A bend is for resting, friend") and his bible line ("Roads are slick, friend. Clay is brittle"). Pip's second joke is his answer-nobody-asked ("hide in the barrel").

## 8. Music and sound

## 9. Blocking and vehicles

## 10. The button
Pip flinching at an engine we hear and do not see. The warmth is already being taken away.
