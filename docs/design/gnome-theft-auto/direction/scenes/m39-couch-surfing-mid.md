## 1. Purpose in the story

The row's laugh. Hyssop Doormat asks, for the record, if Pip is a lodger, and her old crossing-warden's paddle does the
rest. It is the rung of Hyssop's ladder that W09 turns into a note on her door ("NO LODGERS"), and it keeps the gag away
from Tansy's reunion, which needs the next scene clean.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** tense after a ride past the watchers; one door left.
- **Out:** relieved and laughing: Pip is in, Hyssop is on the record, the couch is real.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hyssop | to know exactly who is in her stairwell, and to log it | a toolbox, a plain cap, a friend | questions that rise at the end, the paddle | rules: one couch, one tenant; writes it down |
| Pip | to be luggage | Hyssop's paddle, which he obeys by reflex | tries "luggage", freezes, folds | gets in |
| The player | to get Pip upstairs | a neighbour with a chain on the door | one short answer | waits |

## 4. Subtext

- Hyssop is more worried about a rent rise than a lodger; "for the record" is her armour.
- Pip, who runs from everything, cannot disobey a stop paddle: forty years of crossings are in the air.
- She lets him pass because he stopped when told, which is the only thing she has ever wanted from anyone.
