## 1. Purpose in the story

The first pinwheel freed, and the act's first quiet moment. It is Ragwort's arc in one gesture: she names every pinwheel,
and she tips her hard hat to this one. It makes the player care about what they are cutting for before the fights start.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** just cut a cable, a little proud and a little guilty about the HOA's reaction.
- **Out:** moved by a pinwheel (no gag on it), sure of the next job (Sulks, two linemen).

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ragwort | to see Old Spin turn again | eleven years of silence | quiet, short, then back to numbers | the first pinwheel freed; she is steadier |
| The player | to know how long, what next | none | two one-word questions | has the next job |

## 4. Subtext

Ragwort cried at the first pinwheel she ever freed (characters.md). The tipped hat is what is left of that.
