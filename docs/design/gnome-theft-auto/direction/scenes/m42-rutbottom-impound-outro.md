## 1. Purpose in the story

The Carburettors get their rides back, and the Dirt Bowl becomes the player's: the dirt bike's keys change hands. It is
Lulu's moment, a loud one, and Clary's, a quiet one.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** just beat the HOA and drove a ride out.
- **Out:** warm and rewarded; they have a bike, a friend in Lulu, and a track to come back to.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clary | to see the rides out fairly | none | flat bark, flag, a rule | admits she is the best marshal Lulu has |
| Lulu | to roll out with her crew | none | loud, teasing | thanks Clary in her own way |
| The player | the keys | none | "Thanks, Clary." | owns the dirt bike |

## 4. Subtext

Clary has never won a race she marshalled, and has never wanted to. She hands over the keys the way she hands over a rule.
