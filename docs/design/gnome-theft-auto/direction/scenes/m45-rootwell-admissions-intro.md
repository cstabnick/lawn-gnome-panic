## 1. Purpose in the story
Starts the guidance part of the Combine: the stolen star survey is Betony Sextant's. She speaks for the first time as a voice, in a letter: precise, apologetic, a little distracted. Plants the act's one sincere fear in a joke-sized sentence: the level line is the whole problem.

## 2. The player's feeling
- **In:** the sea, the flag flying, a job well done on a boat.
- **Out:** amused, curious about the professor, and clear on the next step (enrol at Rootwell, find her in the library, mind the Dean).

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Betony (voice) | recruit a stranger | she cannot go home to her own observatory | polite, exact, trailing into asides | none; she has asked |
| Player | read the letter | a pigeon that will not be hurried | flat, short | agrees to be a student |

## 4. Subtext
She knew someone would want the survey and drew it anyway. The pigeon is the best-run part of her postal service, and the only one.
