## 1. Purpose in the story

Betony's farewell: the warm beat of the row. She gets what she wanted (the survey back with her name on it) by
signing the lens and giving it away. Pip stays to help with the rent: he has gone from "I do not like ants" to
carrying their sugar. Then the turn into the release: the vans, and the quick way down.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** a getaway coming.
- **Out:** trusted with something precious, and about to drive off a cliff on purpose.

saying sorry for it. No gag on top of her line.
