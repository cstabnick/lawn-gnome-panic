## 1. Purpose in the story

The comic toll. The ants are the landlords and they do not speak: the scene is built so they never need to. One ant, a
bowl, a tapping; Pip's fear; the ant turns and points at the refectory. It names the job (sugar, from the Trencher) and
plants the ants' manners for the outro.
