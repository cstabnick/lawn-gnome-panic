## 1. Purpose in the story

The turn of the row: Basil learns that the mole he has hated since a final was saving his ball from the sprinkler. The scene
is the evidence (the sprinkler head, the ball, Nubbin), not a speech. It ends with Basil putting the sack of traps down.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** they led his cart down his own traps; tension.
- **Out:** the click of a thing being understood; sympathy for Basil.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basil | to find and trap the mole | Thrift standing in the way | superstitious, "as they say" | drops the sack |
| Thrift | to protect the family | Basil's certainty | refuses, then demonstrates | wins by showing |
| Nubbin | to save the ball again | his fear of Basil | does it anyway | seen |

## 4. Subtext

- Basil's "I called him a thief for a year" is the first crack in the grudge; the apology waits for the night.
- Thrift never says "I told you"; he lets the sprinkler do the talking.
