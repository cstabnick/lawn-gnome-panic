## 1. Purpose in the story

The sincere beat of the act's middle: Basil kneels on the ninth green and puts a hand on a mole's back, and a grudge is over.
Then, with the same hand, he points the player at Loamdeep. **Nothing here is a joke.**

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** the reveal and an hour of waiting.
- **Out:** moved, and ready to go down and see the hall.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basil | to say sorry without knowing how | pride, a year of grudge | says nothing; puts a hand down | an ally |
| Nubbin | to be forgiven, and to apologise first | his fear of Basil | formal politeness | forgiven |
| The player | to see it done | nothing | one line | agrees to go |

## 4. Subtext

- "I called you a thief for a year" is Basil's apology, and the hand on the back is the rest of it.
- Nubbin's "Most kind" accepts it in his own formal way. Thrift's "Goodnight, my dears" is for the moles and for Basil.
