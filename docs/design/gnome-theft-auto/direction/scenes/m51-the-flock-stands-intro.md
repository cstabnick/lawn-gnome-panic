## 1. Purpose in the story
The gentle laugh before the act turns. Row 25 foretold the Even Cut; now the Flock will stand in its path at Hedgeworth, but not with the Lady in it. Sets up a rhyme the outro answers: six people on one leg in a row, in a fixed order, and a cup.

## 2. The player's feeling
- **In:** a long drive from a tasting.
- **Out:** soothed and amused; a clear, odd job (take the Lady, slowly, to Hedgeworth).

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Saxifrage | save the Lady without moving the Flock | the Flock cannot move | sing-song ritual, "so it is stood" | hands the Lady over |
| Godwit | understand | his own thought | blurts it | is told "Mind the cup", drops it |
| Player | find out what the Flock is doing | none | flat questions | takes the job |

## 4. Subtext
The Flock stand for each other; the Lady was only ever the thing they face. The Combine cuts what stands tallest and Saxifrage has no idea how she knows.
