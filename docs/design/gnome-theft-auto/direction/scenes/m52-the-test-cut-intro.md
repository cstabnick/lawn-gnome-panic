## 1. Purpose in the story

Vesper on every loudspeaker (the horns planted in the p1 intro). The test is concluded; please remain. The crew,
together for the first time tonight, decides to go, and Peony refuses the horn to its face.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** beaten.
- **Out:** moving: a way out (the arches, the board) and someone who knows it.
