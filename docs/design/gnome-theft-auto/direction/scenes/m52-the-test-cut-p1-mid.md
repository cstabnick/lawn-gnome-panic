## 1. Purpose in the story

Vesper says what the test is: a straight line through the ninth that goes round nothing. The Countess flinches at
"vines": the first crack in her backing, planted for #65 (she learns the Even Line crosses her vines). Vesper and the
player do not share a frame (#50 was the one break); he dismisses the caddy without turning round.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** standing still behind a parasol, holding a button, feeling clever.
- **Out:** caught. Vesper knew; he did not need to look.

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |

## 4. Subtext

Wisteria's secret (she funded it without understanding it) sits under "Round the green, one presumes." Vesper's "a very
even view of them" is a threat to her vines said as a courtesy.
