## 1. Purpose in the story

The line is drawn across the course: the survey flags go one by one, the Flock stand in its path as promised (#51) and
step aside on one leg (the Flock ladder, #52 rung), the gala flag snaps, and Peony, who "does not look back at a course"
(the p2 ride's banter), turns round and looks.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** relief: everyone is out.
- **Out:** the wrongness of how calmly it works; one dry smile at the Flock, then Peony's face.

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic | Change |

## 4. Subtext

Saxifrage's fear (the Even Cut, the only one who says it aloud) under a very literal prophecy kept to the letter.
