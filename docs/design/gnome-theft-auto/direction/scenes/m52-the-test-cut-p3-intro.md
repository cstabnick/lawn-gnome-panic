## 1. Purpose in the story

The deck rolls toward the ninth green. Tansy and Pip come in on the radio ("Tansy and Pip join by radio call"), the
escort mowers come round in front of the machine, and Peony refuses to concede the hole.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** having watched it draw its line.
- **Out:** ready to throw everything at it.
