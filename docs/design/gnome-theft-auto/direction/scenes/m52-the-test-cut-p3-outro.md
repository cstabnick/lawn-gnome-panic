## 1. Purpose in the story

Nothing works. The player's best attack, a drive off Peony's own driver, rings off the porcelain hood and the blades pass
over the ball and keep turning. The jokes stop for this part (batch-3 notes, act III tone ladder): the camera stays with
the deck and does not cut away to a reaction gag.

- **In:** a minute of everything, survived.
- **Out:** the floor going: it did not even slow down.
