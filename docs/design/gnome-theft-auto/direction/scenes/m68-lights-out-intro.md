## 1. Purpose in the story

Warm and a little comic, and the last time everyone is in one place. Sets the night: dinner, then everybody home. Hyssop's head count is the scene's one gag; Juniper laughs for the first time since City Hall.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** the tension of the declaration. **Out:** soft, a little afraid, clear on the job: drive everyone home.

## 4. Subtext

Everyone has heard the radio. The stairs are counted because nobody wants to count tomorrow.
