The four goodnights, one exchange each, as a montage of real places. Pays off: Juniper laughs and leads; Bodega's "Sign is on. Go."; Tansy stops saying "not my problem"; Granny's roof to come home to.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** a long quiet drive. **Out:** tender, and aware that tomorrow is the real thing.
