## 1. Purpose in the story

Hogweed calls the dispatch radio and tilts a slate of ratios at the box so Pip can see it. A small mortar and "a parachute that almost certainly opens". The Dewdrop rolls past behind him and he calls "Run slower!" without turning: row 10's line, second rung of the Dewdrop ladder. Plants "almost" for the outro.

## 2. Beat sheet

1. Static, Pip lunges at the radio. 2. Hogweed at the chalkboard: "...seven to two!" 3. Pip loves it. 4. "I will tilt it." The player: "It is a radio." Pip: "I can almost see it." 5. The offer; "How almost?" "The sums disagreed in a hopeful direction." 6. The job; the Dewdrop; "Run slower!"

## 3. Shots, sound, blocking

Intercut: Pip/player on one `coverage`; Hogweed faces the lens (straight-on frame). Music `heist`, bed `night`, foley `static`, `whoosh`. Lit by a lantern post (overlook) and a lamp post (Underspan); `sky: 'night'`.

## 4. The button
