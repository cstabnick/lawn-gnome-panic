## 1. Purpose

Pip cannot house the player; a flat on credit answers Act I's question (where do they live). Plants Mabel's refusal ladder (R-071: she refuses mower mud here; "Hop in, dear" is two-coat-intro's) and Cam sweeping in the background (his broom is in every Side-Valve shot before chapter 3). L95: Pip's "bench" is a bedroll under crates and a lamp.

## 2. The player's feeling

In: fired, owing, sleeping on a bench. Out: knows the job (follow Mabel to the bank, sign for a flat) and is smiling at Pip.

## 3. Characters, subtext

Pip wants to be useful at once and dodges who pays (a second sentence that undoes the first). Mabel wants a clean cab and says "dear". The player is the straight man. Subtext: Pip is passing the cost to a bank.

## 4. Beats and shots

Hook: Pip's line over a clatter (0-4 s). Setup: the player's question, Pip's answer. Turn: Mabel's cab arrives and refuses the mud; intro card. Button: the cab creeps off at walking pace with Pip walking alongside. Shots: Pip/player mcu coverage on one side of the line; Mabel from the apron side of the kerb (a still, not from inside her cab); tracks on the cab.

## 5. Dialogue

The fare is on the bank. / Player: Who pays? / Pip: The bank! Anyway, nice weather! / Mabel: Not with that mud on my seats, dear. / Player: I'll ride behind. / Mabel: Walking pace, dear. The meter's running anyway. / Pip: She said walking pace! I am walking!

## 6. Music and sound

## 7. Button
