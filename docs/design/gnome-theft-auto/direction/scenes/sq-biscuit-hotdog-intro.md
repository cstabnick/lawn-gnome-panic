## 1. Purpose in the story

Chapter 2's Cloverfield favour: the player is asked for a job no one else will touch, a giant's toy in a giant's field.
The scene plants the tall-tale stakes (Biscuit is a giant and is never shown) and sets the reward (Granny will find a
buyer). Setup it plants: the nap crater as the place Biscuit sleeps, and the rule that nobody looks up.

The old scene was a whisper on wide stage spots: Bramble at 1-8% of the frame, lines of 17-19 words, two wides held for
10-12 s, and a walk-off with no turn.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** curious, a little amused by a farmer who will not look at the sky.
- **Out:** uneasy and in on the job: haul the giant's squeaky hotdog out of the crater and back to this barn, in daylight,
  without looking up.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Farmer Bramble | to have the toy back and Biscuit gone | his own fear, the thumps | whisper, rule-giving ("Don't look up") | sends the player out, still whispering |
| Granny Gnertrude | to keep her yard and her pie in the oven | Bramble's panic | calm, deadpan, pie as currency, "sugar" once | sets the price: a buyer, no questions |
| The player | to know the job and its risk | Bramble's whispers | two short questions | takes the job |
| Scarecrow Tender | to keep the verge tidy | nothing (local colour) | walks the verge | (unseen by the crew) |

## 4. Subtext

- Bramble is not hiding from Biscuit only: he is ashamed of the ditch. The line "Loud enough to wake the Watch" is his
  guilt, not a joke.
- Granny is not scared, and her calm is the reason the player trusts her. Her deadpan at the button ("Nobody asks what a
  giant's toy is worth") is the first hint she knows exactly what it fetches.
- The thumps are the only thing in the scene that is bigger than the people in it. Nobody says what they are.

## 9. Blocking and vehicles

- The player at the yard edge (stage mark `player`, facing the porch); they turn to Bramble on the reply.
- Bramble leans on his parked combine (stage mark `bramble`); he turns to face the player on each line and stays still.
- Granny rocks on her porch chair (stage mark `granny`), facing the player. She does not stand.
- The Scarecrow Tender walks the verge (the stage's walk loop) during the hook only.
- Vehicles: Bramble's combine stays parked at its mark (`castRide('bramble', 'combine')`). No vehicle moves in the scene.
- The player ends at the yard edge; the quest's steal-deliver objective starts from the crater run.

## 10. The button

The last image is Bramble's low close single with the next thump under it and the shake, and his whisper "Now. Don't
look up." It is the right last moment because it is the rule the whole quest is built on, said as the ground moves.
