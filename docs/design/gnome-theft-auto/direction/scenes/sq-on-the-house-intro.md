## 1. Purpose in the story

Introduces Chrome-Knuckle Cass and her one rule: she gives nothing away. Sets the job (the HOA clamped a pink ride-on)
and the prize (repairs "on my tab").

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** just finished their first contract; the mower is dented.
- **Out:** amused by Cass's flat refusal to call it a gift, and keen to get free repairs.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cass | get the mower back, pay as little as possible | her own rule against giving | deadpan, short sentences, a tap of chrome | grudgingly offers a tab |
| Player | find out the price | none | short questions | takes the job |

## 4. Subtext

Cass cares a great deal about her clients and would rather die than say so. "Pink is a deposit" is how she says it.

## 10. The button
