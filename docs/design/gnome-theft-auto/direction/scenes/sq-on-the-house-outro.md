## 1. Purpose in the story

Pays off the job: the client cries (Cass's only measure of a finished car) and Cass puts the player on her tab, so Pay
'n' Glaze repairs are free from now on.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** relief the pink survived.
- **Out:** pleased with the perk, and with Cass's refusal to call it a gift.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client | get her mower back | her own pride | denial ("I am not crying") | cries happy |
| Cass | pay the debt without calling it one | her rule | rename the gift a tab | the tab is open |
| Player | confirm the prize | none | one dry question | gets the perk |

## 4. Subtext

Cass is moved by the client's tears and shows it only by paying up.
