## 1. Purpose in the story

The tender ending: she says goodbye in her own way, and Driftwood gives the player a piece of her. A sincere beat
(bible law 5): no stinger, slower cuts, one held silence; the only joke is Driftwood's own, as a man coping.

## 2. The player's feeling

- **In:** chose her over the money. **Out:** warm; a keepsake they will see on their gnome in every game.

## 3. Characters: objective, obstacle, tactic

| Character | Objective | Obstacle | Tactic(s) | Change by the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Driftwood | to thank the player without making a fuss | he is not good at thanks | a nod, a joke, a gift | gives the one thing he has |

## 4. Subtext

The shell pack is the most valuable thing he owns; "the only thing that has ever kept me dry" is a castaway saying it.
