# Bestiary

Every enemy in Fescue. HP and damage come from level × rank using [combat.md](combat.md) §9, so only overrides are listed. **Family** sets weaknesses (combat §7). **Wants** is lore rule 6: every pest wants something, and its examine text or quest text should say what.

Rank key: T trivial, N normal, E elite, MB mini-boss, B boss, WB world boss.

## The Mower (boss sheet)

*The Turning Blade, Old Rumble, the Saint of the Even Cut.* A red rotary lawnmower about forty gnomes tall. Its paint is faded, its deck rusted, and a gap in its blade shows where the Shard broke off 3,000 years ago. There's a grey stone dust ring in its mouth: the last of the Stopping Stone.

| Part | HP (solo) | Family | Targetable when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wheels ×4 | 1,500 each | Metal | Phase 2. Each wheel destroyed slows it 15%. |
| Deck (main body) | 12,000 | Metal | Phase 2 |
| The Heartspark | 6,000 | Metal (Rain ×2 instead of ×1.5) | Phase 3, from on top of the deck |
| The Waking Rope | 1,000 | Plant (Slash ×2) | Phase 3, the last 30 s |

Total about 25,000 HP solo, for a fight of about 6-8 minutes. +70% per extra co-op player.

**Attacks**

| Attack | Telegraph | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Blade Sweep** | A spinning ring of grass flecks around the deck, 2 s | 120 damage in a circle (about ⅓ of a L30 player's HP) |
| **Chute Blast** | The side chute glows, 1.5 s | Cone: 60 damage plus knockback; spits debris that becomes cover |
| **Belly Spill** | The bag bulges | Spawns 4 grass-bag moths |
| **Black Dew Cloud** | Black drips under the tank | Poison pool for 10 s; Sun ignites it (bad: 80 area damage) |
| **Rolling Charge** | Wheels dig in, lane drawn, 3 s | 200 damage in a lane (dodge it!). Hits barricades. |
| **Pull the Rope** (phase 3) | The cord tightens and shudders | If the Rope isn't cut within 30 s, the Mower restarts and heals the Heartspark 25% |
