# Items

Numbers come from item level (ilvl) using [combat.md](combat.md): weapon average `3 + 2·ilvl` (±40%), armor `(40 + 20·ilvl) × typeFactor × slotShare`. Each item here needs only a name, ilvl, slot, type, rarity and flavour. The examine text follows the lore rules: gnome names for garden things, dry jokes.

## Rarity

Gnomes grade things the way they grade plants:

| Rarity | Colour | Bonus | Extra stats | Where from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Plain** | white | +0% | – | Shops, common drops |
| **Sprouted** | green | +8% | +1 attribute line | Drops, quest rewards |
| **Blooming** | blue | +16% | +2 lines | Elites, dungeon trash, chapter rewards |
| **Heirloom** | purple | +25% | +2 lines plus a special effect | Bosses |
| **Kiln-fired** | orange | +35% | +3 lines plus a special effect, with lore | The final battle, the Kiln, one per class per chapter finale |

Extra stat lines are drawn from: +Strength, +Agility, +Intelligence, +Endurance, +Accuracy (1), +Defense (1), +crit damage %, +school damage %, +Ward vs one school %, +move speed %.

## Trades and materials (optional system)

The three charters naturally give **gathering trades** that level by use, like the existing skills (lore: each charter's workers). These are optional for the first build but give the industry regions something to *do* besides fight.

| Trade | Region | Gather | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Woodcutting** | Hedgewood | Hedge logs, privet heartwood chips | Staffs, slingshots, barricades (Verge) |
| **Hunting** | Hedgewood | Cricket leather, wasp paper, spider silk | Jerkins, smocks |
| **Mining** | Rockery | Shard ore, slate, Tall-eye shards | Plate, weapons, charms |
| **Farming** | Cloverfield | Clover seed, wheat, herbs | Food, potions |
| **Ranching** | Cloverfield | Honeydew (from aphids), snail slime | Honeydew flasks, Glaze pots |

**War Supplies.** In chapter 7 the War Table at Last Tuft takes hand-ins of each region's goods. Every supply type you fill raises an ally's strength in the final battle (more archers, sturdier barricades, extra carts). That makes the trades matter to the story.
