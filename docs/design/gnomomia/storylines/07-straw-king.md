# The Straw King

**Zone:** Cloverfield · **Levels:** 13-16 · **Quests:** 6 · **Leans toward:** Tend it
**Unlocks after:** Chapter 4, "The Royal Warrant" (talk to Muckle) · **Ties to main story:** Old Gleaner at Crow's Perch, and one grain supply line at the War Table in chapter 7.

## Pitch
Straw is turning up in the ruts of the Sodbury road, and the great figure on Scarecrow Rise is coming apart. The player helps the Sheafkeepers, a family of farmers who dress the King each harvest with one particular knot, find out what their rite was for, and then decides how to keep him standing. It is worth doing because the answer is a quiet one about keeping something up when fewer and fewer people remember how.

## Roots in the three layers
- **Planting (3,000 years):** The songs say the Blade went round the King's knoll and never cut it, and that the King "keeps the wheat." Cloverfield is flat because the Mower flattened it. The knoll is the one bump it left alone.
- **Charters (300 years):** The Archive's Straw Record gives three accounts: a Gardener "who forgot to leave"; a Fieldwarden clerk's claim that the Charter's founders put him up to scare crows off the first wheat; and the Sheafkeepers' own rite-book, which says the knot is older than the Charter. Only the straw disagrees with all three.
- **Rumbling (30 years):** The First Rumble tipped the King's post a hand's width off true. Since the Quiet Years the Sheafkeepers have had too few hands to tie him properly, and rain has been getting into his straw from the top.

## Key scenes and dialogue
1. **The ruts, Sodbury road.** Clary: "Straw's in the ruts again. Rain's rotting it from the top, and nobody's been up to dress him."
2. **The rite-posts.** Comfrey: "Three posts, three knots. My mother tied the first. Count them, then tell me he's only straw."
3. **The Archive.** Inkcap: "There are three accounts of that. All wrong in interesting ways." Then, lower: "That straw isn't Charter straw. Nobody has made straw like it in three hundred years. A Sheafkeeper came up six years ago to copy the knot, and she never brought the copy back."
4. **After the defence.** Comfrey: "He's standing. Not for long, mind. Choose how."
5. **The choice.** Comfrey: "Tie him the old way and he stands another harvest. Or leave him to the crows. Gleaner has been circling since the straw started coming down. They'll keep him up after a fashion."
6. **After *Tend it*.** Clary: "Straw's stopped coming down the road. I walked it twice to be sure."

## The sad note
For forty years Comfrey and her sister Marjoram tied the knot together, one on each side of the King, because the knot needs two pairs of hands to hold. When the Quiet Years emptied the farms, Marjoram went to the Keep for the copy and did not come back. Comfrey has tied the knot alone since. It does not hold right with one pair of hands, and she knows it. She still sets a second cup out at the Hollow every evening. Play it straight.

## New canon

- Myth: the Mower's Last Mowing went round the knoll and did not cut it. Songs say the King "keeps the wheat."

## Lore checklist
1. **Garden object at gnome scale?** Yes. A huge straw figure with a sack head and button eyes. Gnomes call it the King and do not know what it is for.
2. **Two time layers?** Yes. Planting (the Blade went round the knoll) and Charters (three accounts, one of them the rite-book).
3. **Naming rule, one pun at most?** Yes. Hayfork, Rakehook, Stook Hollow, Damp-wight. No puns.
4. **What does each creature want?** Straw-wights guard the King. Damp-wights keep him standing while the rot takes him. Gleaner is hungry and wants the straw.
5. **Which idea does it lean toward?** Tend it. The roosted option is the "let it grow" save, and it costs the Sheafkeepers their rite.
6. **Loose thread given away?** No. The Archive says "a Gardener" and does not confirm one. Marjoram's fate and the King's origin stay open.
