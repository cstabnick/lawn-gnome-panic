# Finishing

**Zone:** The Rockery · **Levels:** 22-24 · **Quests:** 5 · **Leans toward:** none
**Unlocks after:** The Kiln Warden (Ch 6, main) · **Ties to main story:** Uses only the Kiln's three side mouths, never the main hearth, so Firing the Stone is unchanged.

## Pitch
Kilnkeeper Sedum has sat with the Unfired every evening for longer than anyone can count. She has never finished one. You help her haul fuel and Tall-eye glaze, then fire a few in the Kiln's side mouths with Sun magic. Some of them stand up as finished gnomes, one of them stays in Flintdelve, and one asks you for a choice. It is worth doing because the Rockery's sad note gets a few gnomes made whole.

## Roots in the three layers
- **Planting (3,000 years):** The Kiln was built to finish gnomes. The Unfired were shaped in the same age and left on the shelves when the Kiln went cold, so the Kiln's old job was never completed.
- **Charters (300 years):** The Charter of Stone gave the Delvers the Marble Pits. Their Tall-eyes are ground into the glaze that the Kiln needs.
- **Rumbling (30 years):** Sedum has been Kilnkeeper for thirty years and has visited the shelves every night of them. The Kiln lit itself four nights before you arrived, and she has told nobody what she saw.

## Key scenes and dialogue
1. **The lift at Flintdelve.** Sedum, at the top of the lift-baskets: *"Thirty years I've kept them. Tonight I'd like to finish one."*
2. **The glaze mill.** Sax Hammerclod, pushing a stone: *"Grind it fine. Sedum's particular, and I'm not paying for a second batch."*
3. **The back chambers.** Hyssop opens her eyes as the Sun catches her: *"Is it my turn? They've been calling me Shelf. I'd like my own name back."* She is named by you, through a dialogue choice, from a short list of plants. (Dialogue only, not a flag.)
4. **The walk up.** Hyssop, on the escort road: *"Don't tell me it's far. I've waited longer than your road has existed."*
5. **The last mouth.** Lupin, cracked down one side: *"The heat might take the rest of me. I'd rather know than sit."*
6. **The lift home.** Sedum, after the last firing: *"Four of them, and not one cracked for good. Better than the Kiln has done in my lifetime. Better than my mother's."*

## Choices and flags
One `choice` objective, in quest 5.
- **Flag `unfired=fired`** (*Fire her*): Lupin goes into the last side mouth. She comes out finished, with a crack down one side that Bloom glazes shut. In Flintdelve, Sedum keeps a bench beside Hyssop's. At the Battle of the Verge, Lupin hands out glaze pots at the Last Tuft Shrine. No mechanical effect.
- **Flag `unfired=kept`** (*Let her stay soft*): Lupin stays on her shelf, no longer hostile, and you may sit with her. Sedum says she is still Kilnkeeper. No mechanical effect in the finale.
- Both values give the same gold and close the quest. The choice is about her, not about a faction.

None. The Unfired (23-24) and the Kiln guardians already exist. A Sun-finished Unfired counts as finished, not defeated, and it does not slump.

### Places
- **Shard Mine ledges** (21-22): above the deep seam, where Dry rockrose grows in the warm air off the humming iron. Collect point, not a fight.
- **Flintdelve glaze mill**: a stone grinder at the foot of the lift-baskets, behind the Smith. Interact.
- **The Kiln's side mouths** (23-24): three small firing mouths beside the main hearth in the back chambers. Interact each with a glazed Unfired on the tray.
- **The Unfired shelves**: the back chambers, where Hyssop and Lupin stand. Hyssop's shelf is by the mouths, Lupin's is at the end.

## The sad note
Not every Unfired can be finished. The side mouths hold heat for three firings in a night, and the shelves hold more than that. Lupin is cracked, and the firing might take what is left of her. Some of the Unfired will stay soft after tonight, and Sedum will go on visiting them every evening, as she always has. The Rockery's sad note stays true. The player gets to decide what happens to one of its gnomes, and the answer is not always a happy one.

## New canon
- The Unfired were shaped in the Planting and set on the Kiln's shelves. The Kiln went cold before they were fired.
- The Kiln has three side mouths beside the main hearth, sized for small pieces.
- A Sun spell fires a glazed Unfired into a finished gnome. Finished gnomes can talk and no longer aggro. Heat is limited, so not every Unfired can be finished.
- Hyssop Mudpat (Flintdelve) and Lupin Crock (Kiln back chambers) are new gnomes.
- Kiln tongs (Sprouted trinket), Dry rockrose and Kiln glaze (materials).
- Flags: `unfired=fired` / `unfired=kept`.
- Lupin appears at the Last Tuft Shrine in the Verge chapter if fired.
- Hint, not answer: the side mouths are warm when you arrive, though Sedum has not lit them. Sedum says the warmth "came from the Spigot side". Nobody says who lit the Kiln.

## Lore checklist
1. **Garden object at gnome scale?** Yes. The Kiln, the rockrose, and the glaze mill are all garden-sized, and the names are plain gnome words.
2. **Two of three layers?** Yes. The Planting (the Kiln's purpose, the shaped Unfired) and the Rumbling (Sedum's thirty years, the Kiln lighting itself).
3. **Naming rule?** Yes. Lupin Crock and Hyssop Mudpat are plant first names with household and soil surnames, and neither has a pun.
4. **Does a creature want something?** Yes. The Unfired want to be finished, and Lupin wants the risk taken over sitting still.
5. **Which of the three ideas?** None. Nobody here argues how the lawn should grow. The choice is about one gnome.
6. **Any loose thread answered?** No. The warmth is a hint only, and the question of who lit the Kiln is left open.
