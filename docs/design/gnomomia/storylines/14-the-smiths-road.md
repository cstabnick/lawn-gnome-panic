# The Smith's Road

**Zone:** Thatchford Downs, Birdbath Keep (Drainpipe Gutters), the Rockery · **Levels:** 6-24 · **Quests:** 5 (four new, one existing and expanded) · **Leans toward:** Tend it
**Unlocks after:** Chapter 1, *What Moles Know* · **Ties to main story:** Shard iron is the Delver supply line and a War Supplies hand-in at the Muster (Ch 7), and Bramble's shop is where Mossbottom's share of it gets worked.

## Pitch
Bramble the Smith was thrown out of Flintdelve for ruining a Shard blade while drunk, and he has spent eleven years in Mossbottom making scraps. The player carries his old master's unopened letters down the Hose, fetches Shard ore from the Rockery, and takes a reply back to Smith Cobalt Anvilroot. It is worth doing because every step makes Bramble's shop better, and because forgiveness needs honest iron, not a kind word.

## Roots in the three layers
- **Planting (3,000 years):** The Shard is the Mower's broken blade, so every smith in Flintdelve works a piece of the monster. Gnome smiths quench Shard in Spigot water, because a song says the Spigot water "came from somewhere else." Nobody has checked.
- **Charters (300 years):** The Charter of Stone gave the Delvers the mines and the smelting. Their Pedestal Ward hall keeps a roll of every apprentice released from a Flintdelve forge, and Bramble is on it.
- **Rumbling (30 years):** The Shard has hummed louder since the First Rumble. Eleven years ago Bramble quenched a blade drunk and it cracked. Cobalt let him finish the quench, because the Keep's order was due.

Quest 4 is the "forgiveness" beat the Rockery already had. This chain makes it land: the player arrives with a reply Bramble actually wrote, a tonic he finally took, and a release roll that proves Cobalt wrote the cause himself. Quest 5 is what comes after the hug: the two smiths work one quench together.

## Key scenes and dialogue
1. **Dill's cellar, Thatchford.** Dill: "He paid for one pint and asked for a second. I gave him water. He said thank you, which was worse."
2. **Clementine's window.** Clementine: "Six letters, twelve grams. I kept them dry, which is more than anyone asked of me."
3. **Bramble's shop, Mossbottom.** Bramble, looking at the letters: "Not in the shop. The bellows have heard enough about them." Then: "Take my reply to Cobalt. Don't tell me what it says."
4. **The dry-locker, Gutters.** Cress: "Released, for cause. Cobalt wrote the cause in his own hand, then wrote *regret* underneath. He never writes that under anything."
5. **Cobalt's cold forge, Flintdelve.** Cobalt, reading the reply: "I should have taken the tongs off him. I didn't, and the order went out on time. Now let me look at your hands."
6. **The quench trough.** Cobalt, after the last quench: "That's his trowel-job for another day. Not until the Kiln Warden's finished with you. We don't touch that one yet."

## The sad note
Bramble did ruin the blade, and he has not forgiven himself for it. He has not forgiven Cobalt either, and he has never read the six letters Cobalt sent him, one in most years. They sit in a drawer above the bellows. Cobalt knows what the blade cost, because Cobalt was the one who let the quench go ahead. Neither smith says so out loud until quest 4, and then only once. Tansy has made the tonic three times, and nobody has said anything about that either. Play it straight. Nobody in the shop cries, and nobody makes a joke about the drawer.

## New canon
- Bramble was thrown out of Flintdelve eleven years ago, after a Shard blade he quenched drunk cracked. Cobalt let him finish the quench because the Keep's order was due.
- Bramble left Flintdelve by leaf-raft and walked south from Thatchford. He paid for one pint at the Tipsy Toadstool, asked for a second, and was given water.
- Cobalt sent six letters to Bramble over eleven years. They reached Thatchford's post, with 2 g postage each, and Clementine has kept them since.
- The Pedestal Ward Delvers' hall keeps a roll of released apprentices. Cress Hayrake (Roll-Weeder) is its keeper, and she has kept Bramble's release page in the Gutters' dry-locker for eleven years.
- Bramble drinks one half-pint on Sundays. He calls that a failure.
- Sax has hidden three mine stoppages from the Keep. Quest 3 gives the player the choice of whether that is known.
- The Muster's Delver sapper line needs one extra Shard-iron hand-in if `sax=reported`.
- Cobalt's Gauntlets are Sprouted, ilvl 22, hands, plate.
- The quench trough in Flintdelve is the place Spigot water is used for Shard. Nobody knows why it works better.

## Lore checklist
1. **Garden object at gnome scale?** Yes. The Shard is the Mower's blade, the Spigot is the tap, and the quench trough is a washtub. The names sound like gnome names.
2. **Two layers of history?** Yes. Planting (the Shard, the Spigot-water song) and Charters (the Delvers' roll and charter rights). Rumbling is the drunk quench, the letters and the hidden stoppages.
3. **Naming rule followed?** Yes. Cress Hayrake (plant first name, tool surname), Roll-Weeder (garden job). No puns.
4. **Every creature has a want?** Yes. Muck sprites want to splash, gutter rats want dry ledges, centipedes and rock-lice want the warm seam, and earwigs want crevices.
5. **Which idea does it lean toward?** Tend it. Sax's choice is between a balanced report and a quiet cut, and the chain says plainly that both have a cost.
6. **Loose thread given away?** No. The Spigot-water line is a hint, and the Kiln Warden's trowel is named only as a job for later.
