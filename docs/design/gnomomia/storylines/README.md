## Effects on the main story and the finale

These storylines add these hooks to the Battle of the Verge and the main chapters. They're accepted as canon and extend the ally table in [../quests.md](../quests.md) chapter 8.

| Source | Condition | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | `feral=turned_away` | Chapter 5 "The Mill Raid" gets one extra wave |
| 03 | `heap=truce` | **Heap diggers** ally: the phase 2 debris ramp forms 10 s sooner |
| 04 | `queen_outside` | One extra line when Marigold arrives at the Verge |
| 07 | `straw=tended` | Counts as one filled grain supply line at the War Table |
| 07 | `straw=roosted` | Old Gleaner's nest moves onto the Straw King (the deputy's hat is found there) |
| 11 | `unfired=fired` | Lupin Crock stands at the Last Tuft Shrine |

| 13 | `ironsod`, `cart` | Small finale dialogue and staging changes (see file) |
| 14 | `sax=reported` | The Delver sapper supply line needs one extra Shard-iron hand-in |

## Shared cast notes

- The Hobnails are one Sodbury family: Sergeant Bugle Hobnail (04), his father Samphire (04) and his uncle Bindweed (13).
- Teasel Mulchwell (09) and Old Lichen's daughter Thyme (05) died in the same falling-trunk accident at the Sawdust Mill, about 22 years ago.
- Rush Stoutpot, one of the claimed Kiln-born (05), shares a surname with Sergeant Briar Stoutpot. Thatchford folk assume she's descended from him, and she doesn't argue.
- The forty lost Pipewrights now have six names: Primrose Thistle, Speedwell Caulk, Madder Wringer, Woad Plumbline, Burnet Tallow (06) and Hob Mattock (02).
