A rusted Chapel of Mulch delivery van with a snapped rear axle, abandoned behind a fern bank off the `west` highway in Mossy Wood.

## Inside & Stories

Painted along the flaking side panel in faded gold lettering are the words *CHAPEL OF MULCH — QUOTA DUE FRIDAY*. Mulch-obsessed squatters use the cargo bay as a roadside shrine, burning pine incense and handing out dubious pamphlets. Inside the glovebox sits a stack of unpaid quota invoices addressed to Father Loam.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Scrawled in mud on the windshield: *"WE BRAKE FOR TRUFFLES."*
