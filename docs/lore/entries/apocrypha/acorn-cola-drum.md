The Acorn-Cola Drum of Turfton Park & Arts: a round tower in red-and-white candy panels on a square podium, crowned with a ring of red fins. It is the Acorn-Cola bottling offices, and nobody has ever been allowed to go up and see the bottles.

## What gnomes say

It stands on the x = -80, z = 30 block of Turfton Park & Arts, a red-and-white drum on its square podium, with the fin ring at the top. Its rounded silhouette, panel pattern and fin crown are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
