Turfton's classic crimson-and-white carbonated acorn soda, found in humming sidewalk vending machines and crushed cans across every gutter.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"Open A Cold One. Crack A Smile (Not Your Hat)."* Acorn-Cola sponsors the Hat Watch municipal softball team and lights up the Greenhouse Strip with giant red neon bottlecaps. Its syrupy, roasted-nut fizz has fueled city hall night-shifts and Union Local 404 meetings for decades.

## Consumer Reports

Best served cold from John Bodega's cooler—especially since Asphalt the cat insists on sleeping exclusively on the Acorn-Cola crates.
