Eight dishes, chalked on a slate above the pass at the Route South Acorn Diner: the same eight every day, except the last.

- Acorn-Flour Cake: dense, sweet, sold by the slab.
- Dew-Poached Clover: a morning dish; the dew must be that morning's.
- Grub-Free Root Stew: Sage's stew. The capitals are a reassurance.
- Toasted Acorn Toastie: served hot enough to argue with.
- Dandelion Fritters: golden, and gone before they cool.
- Mushroom Coffee: brown, earthy, said to wake the dead (nobody has tried).
- Mulch-Bean Chilli: ordered by gnomes who want to be left alone afterwards.
- Pie of the Day: whatever Sage decided that morning, never explained.

Rush hour comes twice a day: at noon, when the highway coach pulls in, and again after dusk. When the last plate of a rush goes out, Fence-Post Frank rings the end-of-rush bell from his stool.

## What gnomes say

"Eight dishes and not one of them a mistake."

"Frank's bell means you may breathe."

## In the games

The chef job at the diner (epic E24) cooks these dishes in the kitchen, one order ticket at a time, with a rush when the coach arrives.
