Arcing portable launcher firing toasted acorns that burst into bouncing shrapnel, knocking pursuing riders off mowers and shattering HOA headlights.

## Field Notes & Stories

Favorite weapon of door-gunners hanging out of The Whirligig or bouncing across Frostbite Fells' snow-squalls. Because the acorns are oven-toasted by Granny Gnertrude, they crack loudly on impact and smell faintly of roasted chestnuts across the battlefield.

## What gnomes say

"If you hear a dull *thump-pop* overhead, duck—it's raining toasted oak."

## Loose threads

Which recipe Granny uses to toast the mortar shells extra hard.
