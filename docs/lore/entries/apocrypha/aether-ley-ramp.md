A glowing blue crack in the snowpack atop Frostbite Fells' highest ridge that launches speeding mowers into brief, sparkling zero-gravity.

## Field Notes & Tales

Nobody knows why a shimmering Aether fissure opened on a mundane frozen slope, though Dandelion Fizz immediately tried to sponsor it. Hitting the blue glow at full throttle on a Push-Mower Bike triggers a faint, ethereal chime and sends the rider soaring high enough to wave at passing Crop Dusters.

## What gnomes say

"Hit the blue crack at thirty-four, pull back, and pray your wheels land before your hat does."

## Loose threads

Whether the blue fissure connects to the floating islands of the Aether, or is just leaking high-octane magic.
