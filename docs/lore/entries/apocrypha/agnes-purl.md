The knitter of Mittenholt: red hat, blue shirt, glasses, who knits only left mittens and has stopped asking why.

## What gnomes say

"Every mitten I knit is a left one. I have stopped asking why."

## The truth

Mittenholt's right mittens are knitted by nobody and arrive anyway, in the post, in autumn.
