The bolt-on parts Ammu-Gnation sells for its free guns, sorted into eight slots. A gun takes at most one part per slot, and a part only fits the guns it was made for. Each one is bolted or glued on like a Pay 'n' Glaze part, and each one costs acorns once per gun.

## Optics

The Dew-Drop Dot is a red dot set in a glass bead, steadying the aim a little and zooming it a touch. The Firefly Holo puts a firefly in a frame to draw the ring. The Long-Look Scope sees the far fence, and the far fence sees you back. The Jam-Jar Scope is a whole jam jar of zoom, and it still smells of plum.

## Muzzle devices

The Hush-Puppy Suppressor makes a *pfft* where the bang was, and it takes a little range and recoil with it. The Muzzle Brake is a slotted iron cup that kicks less and roars more. The Party Kazoo turns every shot into a small song. The Squeaky Duck is a rubber duck on the end of the muzzle, and it squeaks.

The kazoo and the duck are real, and very popular with nobody.

## Barrels

The Long Barrel reaches further and flies straighter. The Heavy Barrel is as thick as a fence post and hits like one. The Ported Barrel has holes along the top to let the hurry out.

## Underbarrel

The Twig Foregrip is a second handle for a second hand. The Firefly Laser is a firefly that always points where the gun points.

## Magazines

The Extended Mag holds half as many rounds again and is a bit slower to swap. The Drum Mag is a round tin of plenty and heavy to reload. The Speed-Loader Mag drops out on a pull-tab and reloads in a blink.

## Stocks

The Moss-Padded Stock is a cushion of moss against the shoulder. The Wire Stock is bent garden wire: light, quick and a little wobbly.

## Finishes

Finishes glaze the gun's own body and fit every weapon. Gilded is gold all over and shoots the same. Chrome is mirror-bright, and you can check your hat in it. Pay 'n' Glaze Pink is the same pink as the shop across town. Hedge Camo is green as a hedge and hides it in one. Terracotta is fired clay, like the gnome holding it. Firefly Glow lights up in the dark.

## Charms

Charms hang off any weapon and do nothing a gun needs. The Lucky Acorn is an acorn on a string, with luck not included. The Fuzzy Dice always roll a six and never get to use it. The Tiny Gnome is the size of a thumb and hangs on for dear life. The Brass Bell tinkles when you run.

## Loud, quiet and the Hat Watch

A suppressed shot is quieter, but it is not silent, and the Hat Watch still notices a cracked gnome. The mods change how a gun sounds and how it shoots. None of them change what a hit does: a shot gnome still cracks, and nothing bleeds.
