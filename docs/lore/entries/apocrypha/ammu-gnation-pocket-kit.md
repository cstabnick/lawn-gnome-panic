A fold-out canvas tool roll that Ammu-Gnation sells to nobody and gives to everybody. Unrolled, it is a sheet of olive canvas with a row of little pockets, each one holding a spanner, a spare screw or a bolt-on part, and a card tucked in the corner: the order card.

## How it works

With the roll on his back, a gnome can bolt gun mods onto his gun anywhere in the open world, not just in the shop. He flips the roll open, the shop sheet slides up, and he picks his mods the same way he would at the counter. The gun in his hand wears the draft, so he can see the new sight or barrel before he commits.

Mods the gnome already owns go on free. A refit is a refit, and the spanners come out of the pocket without a word.

New mods are ordered from the roll's order card. The card goes to the Pigeon-Drop Couriers, who drop the part from altitude onto the gnome's position for a 10-acorn delivery charge. The part lands, the gnome fits it, and the crater is somebody else's problem.

## The Hat Watch rule

Ammu-Gnation will not take an order from a gnome with the Hat Watch on its tail. The Pocket Kit stays shut at high heat, and the roll's little card says so in capitals. A gnome who wants his mods must lose the Watch first, and only then can he ask the Couriers to drop anything on him.
