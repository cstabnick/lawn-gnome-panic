An olive-and-orange walk-in gun store on the east edge of Turfton, on the road out to the Dust Flats, where every gun is free and the shiny bits cost acorns.

## Inside the store

The door chimes once and nobody comes out to see who it was. The counter runs the length of the room, and behind it a pegboard covers the whole back wall: every gun in Turfton, hung up by its handle, with a price tag that says FREE in friendly letters. The store sells the bolt-ons: scopes, barrels, mags, stocks, finishes and charms, laid out in glass trays by slot. Above the door the slogan is painted in the olive and orange house colours: *"The Gun Is Free. The Shiny Bits Are Not."*

Every gun in Turfton is free because it came from Fort Sandpit. The Spade Guard lost the list for the surplus crates, so nobody can say how many there were, and the store has never tried to count. The shop makes its money on the mods, and the fine print on the window says so: *Fort Sandpit surplus, bolt-ons by the acorn. Not liable for kazoo-related embarrassment.*

## The back room

Past the counter is a back room with a single lit plinth. A gun sits on it, bolted to its mount, and a gnome can try any mod on it before paying. The gun turns slowly on the plinth so every side can be seen. Nobody has ever been seen to fire in the back room. The plinth light is warm and the gun does not look like it has ever been loaded.

## Who is not served

The store will not serve a gnome with the Hat Watch on its tail. The counter gnome looks at the gnome, looks past it at the street, and shakes its head. There is no sign. There doesn't need to be one.

## What gnomes say

"Free gun, forty acorns for the scope. That's how Ammu-Gnation does it."

"You don't pay for the gun, you pay for the bit that makes it sound like a kazoo."
