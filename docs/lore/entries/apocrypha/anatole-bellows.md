The accordion player on the east quay, who knows three tunes and plays all of them in the sad key.

## What gnomes say

"Anatole plays for the river. The river has never put anything in his hat."

"He knows a fourth tune. He is saving it."

## The truth

Anatole is not sad. He is a happy gnome who happens to be good at the sad key, and the accordion does the rest. His three tunes are the music of the quay, along with the gramophone, and the town would not know what to do with a fourth.

## In the games

On the east quay and the Thymes Walk (near x -1584, z 200).
