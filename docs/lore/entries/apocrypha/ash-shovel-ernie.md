Compost Heap stoker standing beside a glowing mulch furnace roasting chestnuts on a trowel and humming old Fescue work-songs whose words he has long forgotten.

## Story: Songs of the Mulch

Ernie has worked the Compost Heap's acid-amber stills since before the highways were paved. His beard is permanently tinted soot-grey, and his trowel doubles as a spatula and a crowbar. When Vesper's lieutenants started welding armor plates onto heavy machinery nearby, Ernie didn't ask questions—he just kept shoveling clippings into the boiler.

Yet late at night, when the steam vents hiss, he still hums melodies from the Planting era without realizing where he learned them.
