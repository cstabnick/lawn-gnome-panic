## Story: Songs of the Mulch

Ernie has worked the Compost Heap's acid-amber stills since before the highways were paved. His beard is permanently tinted soot-grey, and his trowel doubles as a spatula and a crowbar. When Vesper's lieutenants started welding armor plates onto heavy machinery nearby, Ernie didn't ask questions—he just kept shoveling clippings into the boiler.

Yet late at night, when the steam vents hiss, he still hums melodies from the Planting era without realizing where he learned them.

## What gnomes say

"Ernie's roasted chestnuts taste slightly of 87-octane, and honestly, it's an improvement."

## The truth
Ernie stokes the Compost Heap furnace for whoever pays in clippings, which is the HOA now. He wants the furnace to burn and the old songs to come back, and fears remembering the words. Secret: where he learned them (Mossy Wood, a loose thread). Home: the furnace room. He sells dyed sawdust to Cedric without asking, Tansy brings him spare gaskets, Chard Levelright's crews eat his chestnuts and the Heap's welders ignore him. Voice: warm, gruff, trailing into humming mid-sentence, repeating the last word of a phrase; he roasts a chestnut as a full stop ("Plates. ...Plates, leaving. Hmm hmm, hm." / "Chestnut? Tastes of petrol. Better.").

Arc: side quests only in act I; raises the alarm about the plates leaving at night (#27); hums as the plates go by (III); calls the player as the Combine leaves the Heap (#69).
