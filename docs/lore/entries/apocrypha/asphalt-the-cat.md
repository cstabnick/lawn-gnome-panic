A small, completely unbothered clay-dust tabby cat who sleeps on the Acorn-Cola crates inside Bodega & Sons twenty-four hours a day.

## Field Notes: Countertop Majesty

Asphalt is roughly the size of a Turfton sedan's hood and possesses the emotional range of a brick. Sirens wail, Zero-Turns backfire, and pedestrians sprint past screaming about their glaze—Asphalt merely bats lazily at a stray bottlecap and goes back to sleep.

By unwritten Turfton law, shooting near John Bodega's counter is tolerated only until Asphalt wakes up. Even Red Caps and Blue Caps lower their pistols if the cat glares through the glass.

## What gnomes say

"If Asphalt ever leaves the counter, pack your hat and head for the Fence."

## Loose threads

How a real tabby cat ended up small enough—or Turfton big enough—to share a watering-can shop with fired-clay gnomes.

## In the games

Planned (E25). Visits Fennel's hammock under the Gutter Bowls' deck. On fire calls Asphalt is sometimes found up a lamp post, and comes down the Watering-Can Pumper's ladder, unimpressed. Chief Hydra Pumpwell keeps a saucer out for him.
