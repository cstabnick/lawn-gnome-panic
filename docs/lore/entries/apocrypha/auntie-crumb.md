Auntie to the whole of Hearth Hall. She makes the grub bread, the supper, and anyone's tea better.

## What gnomes say

"Do not ask what is in the bread. Eat the bread."

## The truth

It is grubs, flour and kindness, in that order. Grubs are what the moles eat, and also what eat the roots, so the moles do the lawn a favour at every meal. She is the head of the Crumbs, the family that keeps Hearth Hall, cooks for Loamdeep and digs (`loamdeep-clans`), and the aunt of DJ Moleman, whose place she keeps at the table ("COUSIN (DJ)") and warm. Her line: "Do not eat the root. The root is for the grubs, the grubs are for the bread, and the bread is for you. That is all the order there is."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: in Hearth Hall, Loamdeep, beside the dig faces where Dig a Nook is done.
