The head gardener of the Jardin, who clips the parterre with nail scissors.

## What gnomes say

"Monsieur Clippe does not cut the hedge. He persuades it."

"He has one pair of scissors, and they are older than his hat."

## The truth

Aurèle is gentle and exacting, and nothing escapes him. He regards a stray leaf as a personal failing. He is the reason the Jardin looks as it does, and the reason nobody is allowed to walk on it.

## In the games

The Jardin parterre (near x -1600, z -620).

Whether the hedge is ever finished, and whether he would know what to do if it were.
