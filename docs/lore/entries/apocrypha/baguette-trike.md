A three-wheeled bakery delivery trike; the bread in the box is always one loaf short of the order.

## Field Notes

The Baguette Trike turns up in the European yard's traffic, pedalling along at a top speed of 15 units an hour. It has a wooden box at the back, a bell that works most of the time, and a rider who is always just about to be late. The box is always one loaf short. Nobody knows where the loaf goes.

Burl's Forecourt does not sell bread, but it does sell trikes, and the forecourt staff are sure the missing loaf is hidden under a seat. They have looked. The seat is full of other things, none of them bread.

## What gnomes say

"Ordered twelve. Got eleven. The eleventh is always the one you wanted."

## Loose threads

Where the twelfth loaf goes, and whether it is the same one every time. The bakery that the trike belongs to has been closed for a strike since before anyone can remember, and the trike still turns up on the morning run.

Baguette Run is the achievement for this ride. Its reward is the Baguette Quiver.

Grand Tour is the achievement for driving all five European rides. Its reward is the Tour Beret.
