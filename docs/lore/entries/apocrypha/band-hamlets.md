Three small villages on the band roads, settled by gnomes who left Turfton for quieter lawns: **Espalier End**, **Mittenholt** and **Dunelow**. All three post the same sign, "Twinned with nowhere, by request" (R-204).

## What gnomes say

"Next bus: see previous bus."

## The truth

**Espalier End** (Trellisdown, off the North Wicket road, near (-1080, -420)) is a pear-and-topiary village. Notices: a topiary contest ("entries must not resemble the judges"), "LOST: one pear. Answers to nothing", and a feud over the hedge between No. 3 and No. 4, which is 1.2 m and also 1.4 m (mediation Tuesday). Timetable: "Buses: in principle." Residents: **Judge Quince**, who deducts points for symmetry ("Symmetry is for the HOA"), and **Pip Pearsley**, the pear picker ("Nine pears, one basket, and a hill with opinions").

**Mittenholt** (Snowcap Rise, near (550, -1375)) is a knitting village on the north band road. Its board: "FOUND: 41 left mittens. Right mittens need not apply. Hot cocoa on Thursdays. On other days, cold cocoa. Do not lick the pump." Bus: "8:15, 8:15 (later) and sometimes 8:15". Residents: **Agnes Purl**, who knits only left mittens and has stopped asking why, and **Olaf Splitt**, the woodcutter, whose winter came early.

**Dunelow** (Saltbucket hinterland, near (-560, 965)) is a seaside village on the south band road. Notices: "GULLS ARE NOT TO BE FED. GULLS ARE AWARE OF THIS AND DISAGREE", an ice cream van "Tuesdays and whenever it hears you", a lost flip-flop (left, possibly at sea), a snail coach ("Mon-Sat: sometimes. Sun: see Mon"), deckchairs for hire ("Falling over included") and a sandcastle contest judged by three gulls (the tallest wins; the bucket stays). Residents: **Old Wrack**, the net mender ("The fish have learned where the holes were"), and **Cornet Brindle**, the ice-cream seller ("Ninety-nine flavours. Ninety-eight of them are vanilla").

The band roads carry roadside milestones in oddly chosen units (furlongs, whistles, trowels, strolls, baguettes, knots).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: each hamlet has houses, notice boards and two ambient gnomes; visiting all three and the High Tarn earns Off the Beaten Lane.
