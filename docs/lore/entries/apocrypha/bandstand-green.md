A lawn in the south-west of Turfton built round an iron bandstand, the one the city was going to scrap until the townsgnomes
asked for it back. Saturdays bring the market stalls and the picnic rugs.

## What gnomes say

"Every good tune in Turfton has been played on that bandstand, and most of them were stopped by the Watch." The bandstand
roof is the only roof in town that a busker is allowed to stand under without a permit.

## Inside & Stories

- The bandstand itself: an octagonal stone platform, a cone roof with a brass finial, and three steps up from the
  +z side. The railing has a gap where the steps are.
- A pavilion for the picnic crowd, a grove of broadleaf trees on the south-west corner, and wide gravel paths that
  meet at the bandstand.
- The Saturday market row along the south edge: three stalls run by whoever got there first, and a stallholder who
  calls out prices all morning.
- Benches face the bandstand from four sides. The picnickers have worn the grass into the shape of their rugs.
- Lamps and noticeboards line the paths. The noticeboard by the north gate is where the market rota is pinned, and
  where somebody keeps pinning a second, different rota over it.

## In the games

Bandstand Green is the park at block centre (-30, -150) in Gnome Theft Auto. Players can sit on the benches, and a
busker plays the guitar at the bandstand. The market stalls and the townsgnomes are scenery; there are no shops to
rob.

## Loose threads

Who is entitled to the bandstand on a Saturday. The Long Grass League says the grass is; City Hall says the stone is.
