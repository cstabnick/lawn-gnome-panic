Feral lawn-chair baron ruling the Sovereign Republic of New Puddle from a nine-foot throne of zip-tied folding patio loungers and commanding a Push-Mower Bike gang.

## Story: The Leopard-Print Decree

Brenda used to live in a quiet cul-de-sac until the HOA fined her for "excessive patio leisure." Rather than pay, she dragged twelve plastic lawn chairs into the Southern Drainage Canal, zip-tied them into a fortress, and declared herself sovereign royalty.

Armed with a feather-duster scepter and a leopard-print poncho, she taxes passing smugglers in spare spark plugs and leads midnight raids to kink pressurized Tall-One garden hoses near Patio Heights.
