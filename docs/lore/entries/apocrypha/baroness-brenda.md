Feral lawn-chair baron ruling the Sovereign Republic of New Puddle from a nine-foot throne of zip-tied folding patio loungers and commanding a Push-Mower Bike gang.

## Story: The Leopard-Print Decree

Brenda used to live in a quiet cul-de-sac until the HOA fined her for "excessive patio leisure." Rather than pay, she dragged twelve plastic lawn chairs into the Southern Drainage Canal, zip-tied them into a fortress, and declared herself sovereign royalty.

Armed with a feather-duster scepter and a leopard-print poncho, she taxes passing smugglers in spare spark plugs and leads midnight raids to kink pressurized Tall-One garden hoses near Patio Heights.

## What gnomes say

"Don't laugh at the sun-visor. Her Push-Mower pack can strip a Ride-On to the chassis in ninety seconds."

## The truth
Brenda is sovereign of the Republic of New Puddle: independent, and she taxes everyone. She wants recognition as a nation, and fears an HOA fine for "excessive leisure" (the fine that started it). Secret: where she finds the vinyl webbing. Home: the Vinyl Lounge throne. Her pink push-bike gang adores her; Don Compost respects her toll; she has mutual admiration with Captain Bilge and dislikes the HOA with a legal passion. Voice: grand, bright, declamatory, with the occasional giggle; she begins sentences "By decree." ("By decree, one spark plug." / "The Republic notes your hat. Approvingly.").

Arc: takes a toll from the cigar run (#11); tolls Driftwood's raft (#32); absent in act III; opens New Puddle to the Bathtub Queen (#64 part 1). She rides a gold push-bike; the gang's are pink.
