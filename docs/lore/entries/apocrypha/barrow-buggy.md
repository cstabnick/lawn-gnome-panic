Pip Potsworth's old monastery wheelbarrow, fitted with pram wheels and now a two-stroke engine that has opinions about the hill.

## Field Notes

The Barrow Buggy turns up now and then in Turfton traffic. It is the best of the odd rides at being a car, which is to say it is slow, loud and cheerful about it. Its top speed is 11 units an hour, and it handles about as well as a barrow with an engine has any right to. Pip still says it is the first thing he ever built properly, and he will not hear a word against the pram wheels.

Side-Valve Salvage sells copies, which are not as good. The copies have the engine but not the barrow, so they are a two-stroke in a tin tray. Pip thinks this is an insult to the Elders.

## What gnomes say

"If you hear a cough and a squeak coming down Ring Road, get out of the way. It's Pip's."

## Loose threads

Whether Pip ever returns to the monastery, and whether the Elders know the barrow is now a two-stroke. The buggy's engine was never described in the Fescue records, and Pip says it is "not the Elders' business, it's mine".

Sunday Driver is the achievement for driving all four odd rides (Pot Rod, Lawn Roller, Teacup Trundler, Barrow Buggy). Its reward is the Sunday Driver's Cap.
