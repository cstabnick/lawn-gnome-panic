The club pro of Hedgeworth: lean, superstitious and certain that moles are to blame for every bad lie. He carries a lucky tee, a lucky glove and a grudge from a final he lost.

Home: the pro's cottage by the first tee. Work: Hedgeworth pro shop.

## What gnomes say

"Basil won't play the ninth without touching the flag twice. He won't play it with a mole in sight at all."

"Best swing in the garden. Worst temper at the ninth."

## The truth

In that final a mole moved his ball. It was saving it from a sprinkler. Basil has never been told, and has blamed moles for it since.

## In the games

Built (batch 3, E15): practising his swing on the first tee, with a spare ball in his sock, beside his cottage. He is the pro the player beats at one hole in quest 47 (`m47-hedgeworth-links`); he sets a trap for the moles in 48 (`m48-the-groundskeepers-secret`) and softens once he learns the truth; he is evacuated in 52 (`m52-the-test-cut`).
