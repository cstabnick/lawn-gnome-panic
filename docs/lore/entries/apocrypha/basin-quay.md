The harbour at the south end of Pardonlin, on the still water of the Basin, with a retired swan pedalo moored at the end.

## What gnomes say

"Basin Quay has a harbourmaster, a harbour and a swan. Two of those are in service."

"The ships come in one at a time. There is only the one ship."

## The truth

The Thymes ends in the Basin, and Basin Quay is where the town pretends it is a port. The swan pedalo has been retired, with honours, and is kept shipshape by the harbourmaster's refusal to admit that it has stopped.

## In the games

South of town, near x -1510, z 1020 (a screenshot spot), by the Harbour Wicket. Captain Anchois keeps the quay.

## Loose threads

Whether the swan pedalo has ever left the Basin, and whether it was ever meant to.
