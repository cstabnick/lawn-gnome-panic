The bookie at Pardonlin's race: a maroon-hatted, black-bearded gentleman for whom a wager is "a small wager, nothing so vulgar as a bet", taken in a little envelope.

## What gnomes say

"Le Pari. A small wager, nothing so vulgar as a bet. Acorns, please, in a little envelope."

## The truth

His envelopes are of the finest paper and are never opened. He pays out in a larger envelope when you win.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E26): the bookie at the Pardonlin race meet.
