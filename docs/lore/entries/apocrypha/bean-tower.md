A thirty-metre wigwam of Tall-One bean poles with runner beans climbing it, and the landmark of Pardonlin. Admission is one acorn, exit is two, and the queue for it has its own queue.

## What gnomes say

"You can see the Bean Tower from everywhere in Pardonlin. That is why nobody goes up it: you have seen it."

"One acorn to go in, two to come out. The second acorn is for the view, which you will not have had time to enjoy."

## The truth

The poles were left by a Tall One who meant to grow beans and then went indoors. The beans did the rest. The town charges for the climb because charging for things is how a landmark knows it is one. The queue for the Bean Tower has a queue of its own, which forms at the foot of the first queue; the Continentals regard this as good order, not a problem. Fifi Haricot guides the climb with a flag.

## In the games

In the Old Town, north of the Grand Platz (a screenshot spot looks at it from the Platz, near x -1520, z -392). Its district plan is `districts/oldTown.ts`; Fifi Haricot waits at its foot.
