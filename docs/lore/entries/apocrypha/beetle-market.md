A covered market hall and its stalls on the Left Bank, where cheese is aged by argument and the beetles are the best-dressed traders.

## What gnomes say

"Nothing in the Beetle Market is fresh, and everything in it is excellent."

"The cheese has been on that stall since before the dispute. The dispute has aged it."

## The truth

The market is named for a beetle that sold lettuce here once and was so charming nobody ever worked out how to stop. Stalls sell cheese, bread, pots and opinions. A crackling stall radio plays Radio Adagio, at a volume the stallholders call romantic.

## In the games

The Left Bank, around x -1733, z 120 to 205, with an annex to the north at z 55 to 100: a cobbled square of striped-canopy stalls along a crooked central aisle, each trade (cheese, beetles in jars, bric-a-brac, books, records, prints, old hats, bread, produce) in clusters with side alleys between. The stall radio is at z 170. Roquette Fromage keeps the cheese stall in the annex.

## Loose threads

Whether the beetle is a gnome's friend, or a trader's, or both.
