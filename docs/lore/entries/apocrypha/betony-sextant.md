An astronomer at Rootwell: a quiet, very exact gnome in a coat with too many pockets, who surveyed the stars for a perfectly level line and had it taken for the Grand Combine.

## What gnomes say

"The Professor counts stars as if they were owed to her."
"Do not ask her for the time. She will tell you, to the second, and then tell you the sky's version."

## The truth

Home: a staff room in the Anthill Observatory. Work: Rootwell University. She is not a rebel: she is a scientist whose result was turned into a weapon, and she wants it back the way a person wants back a borrowed book. She writes to the player in row 45 and is found in the library.

## In the games

Gives row 45 `m45-rootwell-admissions` (by letter, then in the library) and row 46 `m46-starfall-at-the-anthill`; calls in at row 70 (finale). Planned (batch 3).
