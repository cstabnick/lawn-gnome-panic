A sunbather on the coast: pink hat, cyan top, a deck chair, and four hours of looking at a sea that has not moved.

## What gnomes say

"Four hours on the deck chair. The sea has not moved. Neither have I."

## The truth

Bev is the other half of the beach: calm where Ned is red. She arrives at nine and leaves when the chair does. She is the only gnome on the coast who has timed the tide and found it punctual.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E02): reclines in a deck chair at about (-66, 1177) with the "lean-back" idle.

Whether it is the same four hours each day.
