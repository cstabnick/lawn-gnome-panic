The oldest of the three Carburettors who stayed with Lulu: orange hat, dark shirt, a grey beard and thirty years on the road, who has seen every charter the HOA ever drew up and filed none of them.

## What gnomes say

"Bertha. Thirty years on this road."

"If Bertha nods, the engine is fine. If Bertha does not nod, go home."

## The truth

She is the steady one. When Lulu reads out the HOA charter at the finish of the Capwood Run ("Clause two: the Carburettors shall be beige") Bertha puts her hand over her face; she has been waiting for the clause to be funny for thirty years and it finally is, by being torn up. She drives a long, low, heavy car that she calls by its first name.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto, quest 40 `m40-the-capwood-run`: introduced by Lulu in the intro scene on the start line with a nod, drives in the Run, and stands at the Ring beside the tent for the outro, where she facepalms at Clause one and Clause two. She wears an orange hat (0xff6a00), a dark shirt and a dress cut, with a pale grey beard.
