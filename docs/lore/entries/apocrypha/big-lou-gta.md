Immovable VIP bouncer standing outside The Gnomecino's velvet rope, famous for his ironclad rule against wet hats and his lifetime ban on Vinnie Delacroix.

## Story: The Wet-Hat Embargo

Big Lou crosses his arms, glares down his nose, and checks every single hat brim at The Gnomecino's door. Rainstorms, stray lawn sprinklers, or a splash from the Greenhouse Strip's puddles all earn an immediate polite shove back onto the sidewalk. When Red Cap hooligans once hijacked his shipment of microfiber hat-towels, Lou didn't call the Hat Watch—he simply locked the casino doors until someone brought them back dry.

## What gnomes say

"You can roll up in a bullet-riddled Zero-Turn, just make sure your felt is bone-dry before Lou sees you."

## The truth
Lou is the door of the Gnomecino; the Mulch Club lends him out as muscle when the Don taps, and he is neutral by trade. He wants every hat through his rope dry and in order, and fears a rainstorm on a Friday night. Secret: he still bowls ten-pin in his head and has never told anyone where (the Yard, a loose thread). Home: a flat behind the Gnomecino. Voice: very slow, very low, one or two words a sentence, long pauses, never a question; "Hat." is his greeting, verdict and farewell ("Hat. ...Dry." / "Don said yes. I say hat."). He serves the Don and says so, has a lifetime ban on Vinnie Delacroix and a soft spot for Neon-Moth Nina (who shoos the moth off his sign), and has never once let Brenda past his rope.

Arc: checks the player's hat at the Mulch Club back door (#11), a small test of trust; side quests only in act II; absent in III; stands behind the Don at the Four Taps and lets the whole coalition's hats in, dry or not (#63). He translates the Don's taps for the player, nervously (#3).
