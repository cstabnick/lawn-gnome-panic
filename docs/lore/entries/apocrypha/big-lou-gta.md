Immovable VIP bouncer standing outside The Gnomecino's velvet rope, famous for his ironclad rule against wet hats and his lifetime ban on Vinnie Delacroix.

## Story: The Wet-Hat Embargo

Big Lou crosses his arms, glares down his nose, and checks every single hat brim at The Gnomecino's door. Rainstorms, stray lawn sprinklers, or a splash from the Greenhouse Strip's puddles all earn an immediate polite shove back onto the sidewalk. When Red Cap hooligans once hijacked his shipment of microfiber hat-towels, Lou didn't call the Hat Watch—he simply locked the casino doors until someone brought them back dry.

## What gnomes say

"You can roll up in a bullet-riddled Zero-Turn, just make sure your felt is bone-dry before Lou sees you."

## Loose threads

Whether Lou still sneaks away on weekends to bowl ten-pin back in the Yard.

Lou's crate of forty microfibre hat-towels went missing from the rope; the Red Caps are the suspects. He keeps a spare microfibre bowler of his own, which he hands over only to a player who brings the crate back dry. He takes the VIP's Zero-Turn as the final test, and he lets the Mayor in through the rope, wet-hat rule notwithstanding, when the Mayor has a fresh coat.
