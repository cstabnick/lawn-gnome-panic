Head of Turfton Mechanics' & Dew-Haulers' Local 404, deciding single-handedly which highway potholes get patched and which stay deep enough to swallow an HOA van.

## Story: The 48-Hour Wrench

Big Wrench talks like a 1950s dockworker, drinks Acorn-Cola spiked with a rumored drop of Black Dew, and carries a pipe wrench heavier than most Push-Mower Bikes. He doesn't care about Red Caps, Blue Caps, or Fescue prophecies—only that Local 404's mechanics get paid in real acorns, not HOA store credit.

When Vesper's lawyers threatened to replace union haulers with automated drones, Grout parked four Combine Harvesters sideways across the `east` highway for three hours. The drones were quietly cancelled before lunch.

## What gnomes say
