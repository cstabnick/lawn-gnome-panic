Head of Turfton Mechanics' & Dew-Haulers' Local 404, deciding single-handedly which highway potholes get patched and which stay deep enough to swallow an HOA van.

## Story: The 48-Hour Wrench

Big Wrench talks like a 1950s dockworker, drinks Acorn-Cola spiked with a rumored drop of Black Dew, and carries a pipe wrench heavier than most Push-Mower Bikes. He doesn't care about Red Caps, Blue Caps, or Fescue prophecies—only that Local 404's mechanics get paid in real acorns, not HOA store credit.

When Vesper's lawyers threatened to replace union haulers with automated drones, Grout parked four Combine Harvesters sideways across the `east` highway for three hours. The drones were quietly cancelled before lunch.

## What gnomes say

"If Local 404 goes on strike, Turfton stalls in forty-eight hours flat."

## The truth
Grout heads Local 404 and is the enemy of drones. He wants his mechanics paid in real acorns, and fears a drone in the yard. Secret: the Black Dew in his cola is a rumour he started. Home: the back room of the Local 404 Union Hall; work: Local 404 and the Skyway. Dot's client; Burt owes him; Hogweed's citation lands on his desk; Tansy pays her dues; Don Compost does not cross him. Voice: a dockworker growl, slow, loose grammar, speaking in "we"; his tic is "Local 404 says" ("Local 404 says no. Forty-eight hours." / "Pay in acorns. Real ones.").

Arc: his union citation names the Underspanners' "unauthorised verticality" (#10); side quests only in every other act.
