The Turfton bin lorry: a cab-over lorry with a garden compost bin for a back and a yellow lift arm on the kerb side. The HOA fines spills, so neatness is pay.

## What gnomes say

"Lift slow, tip clean."

## In the games

Planned (E25). The garbage job vehicle. Spills are fined against the crew's pay (R-199).
