A forty-unit mud crater in Cloverfield Farms left behind when a giant dog named Biscuit wandered over from the Bare Patch for a single afternoon nap.

## Field Notes & Tales

Farmers initially panicked when the sky darkened and a furry mountain curled up in the clover. When Biscuit woke up, stretched, and trotted back over the fence, the compressed mud pit left behind proved miraculously fertile—yielding acorns twice the normal size. Local kids dare each other to jump Push-Mower Bikes across the toe-pad ridges.

## What gnomes say

"Best acorns in the south grow inside Biscuit's Nap, just pray he doesn't come back for a second snooze."
