Turfton's premier refined 87-octane petrol brand, drawn from Cloverfield Dew-Wells and distilled in the Compost Heap's acid-amber stills.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"87 Octane. 100% Unlicensed."* Amber-and-chrome Black Dew pump canopies line the `east` and `south` highways, surrounded by branded fuel barrels that erupt in dramatic soot-and-water chain reactions if clipped by stray gunfire. In Fescue, petrol is whispered of as dark earth-blood; in Turfton, it's sold by the jerrycan next to beef jerky.

## Consumer Reports

Burns hot, smells faintly of roasted grass clippings, and allegedly tastes terrible in Acorn-Cola despite Big Wrench Grout's claims.

How long Cloverfield's underground Dew-Wells can keep pumping before the turf sinks.
