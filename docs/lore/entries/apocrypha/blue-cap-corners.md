Cobalt-hatted Turfton street gang defending the eastern kerb of the Neutral Picnic Median against Red Cap acorn barrages.

## Story: East Kerb Honor

Blue Cap Corners pride themselves on sharper aim, cleaner Zero-Turn park-jobs, and never throwing acorns unless the wind is blowing east—which happens almost every afternoon. When Founder's Trim Day parades roll down `ROAD_LINES`, their accidental crossfire with Red Caps reliably turns downtown into a distraction for smarter thieves.
