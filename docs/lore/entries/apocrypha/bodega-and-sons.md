A 24-hour corner shop inside an overturned watering can at `x: -60, z: 60`, famous for lukewarm Acorn-Cola, spark plugs, and Asphalt the cat.

## Inside & Stories

The narrow counter is crammed with Dandelion Fizz crates, loose dandelion-silk cigarettes, and dusty jars of pickled clover. Neon tubes buzz softly in the front window all night long. Even when gunfire cracks flowerpots across the street, nobody breaks Bodega & Sons' glass—partly out of respect for John, and partly out of superstitious fear of waking Asphalt.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Hand-painted sign taped over the register: *"BODEGA & SONS (NO SONS, JUST JOHN). CASH OR ACORNS."*
