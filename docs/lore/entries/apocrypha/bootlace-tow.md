The rope tow up the east side of Snowdrop Slope: knotted bootlaces strung over cotton-reel pulleys, with a cotton-reel handle to hang on to. It pulls skiers and sledders alike.

## What gnomes say

"It is a bootlace. It is a very long bootlace. Do not ask whose."
"Hold the reel, not the rope. The rope has opinions."

## In the games

Built on Snowdrop Slope (E12). Grab a handle at the foot and it hauls you to the top; it works for skiers and for gnomes on tea trays (R-182).

## Loose threads

Whose boots the laces came from, and where they are anchored at the top.
