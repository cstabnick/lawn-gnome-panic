The sweeper of Capwood: a copper-hatted, grey-bearded gnome who sweeps the spores off the paths and has been told by Section Twelve that the spores coming back is not his fault.

## What gnomes say

"Sweeping spores. They come back. Section Twelve says that is not my fault."

## The truth

He is employed by the warden to sweep, and is very good at it; the paths are the cleanest in the toadstool forest for about eleven minutes after he finishes. He has made his peace with the spores, and they with him.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E08): sweeps with the "sweep" idle at about (322, -1224) in Capwood, a resident with a bubble line near the player.
