The landlord of The Rusty Tine on the north road out of Turfton: a broad, patient gnome in a stained apron, who has
repainted the rusted garden fork over his door every spring for as long as the sign has been there.

## What gnomes say

"Pour it slow and it'll forgive you the head." Bramwell says the Tine has never once run dry, and that the regulars
are the only thing in Chillyard that stays put in a frost.

## Inside & Stories

Bramwell keeps the barrels by the door on a stand of old tyres and will tell anyone who asks which tyre came with which
barrel. Nobody has ever taken him up on it.

## Loose threads

Whether he painted the fork rusty on purpose, or the frost did it and he simply never painted it back.
