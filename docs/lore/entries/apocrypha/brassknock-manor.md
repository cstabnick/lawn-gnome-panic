The Brassknock family house at Trellisdown: a long, pale terraced mansion with a brass knocker the size of a mower wheel, a terrace over the vines and a hall for tastings. It stands across vineyard-lane from the vineyard, x -1210..-1090, z -10..90.

## What gnomes say

"They say the knocker has not been used since the charter was signed. Everyone who comes in comes round the back."

"Seven chimneys, and the Countess only lights one."

## The truth

The house is far bigger than the family. Most of the rooms are shut, and one is a ledger room where the Brassknock accounts, including the cheques made out to the HOA, are kept in a single long book.

## In the games

Built by epic E16 (R-211): the gate is on the north wall at (-1150, -9), then a gravel drive and a fountain, then the house (46 x 18 m, centre (-1150, 31)) facing north so its raised terrace looks over the vines. The front door has the great brass knocker and nobody answers it; the way in is the back door on the south side, into the tasting hall, where Footman Pell stands. A row of rooms is shut, the ledger room is locked, and gardens lie behind. An enterable manor (interior-visit). Quest 17 (`m17-the-dew-buyer`) glimpses the Countess on its terrace; 50 (`m50-trellisdown-tasting`) is the waiter infiltration and the ledger photograph; 65 (`m65-the-countess-relents`) is her change of heart; 70 (`ch5-even-cut`) brings her back for the finale.
