Pardonlin's baker, and the keeper of the Baguette Round: a morning delivery of long loaves by hand, bike and basket.

## What gnomes say

"Madame Levain's baguette is eighty centimetres. Not seventy-nine. She measures."

"Bread is the town's clock. When Brigitte opens, it is morning. When she closes, it is her business."

## The truth

The baker has an opinion about every loaf and a firm view on yours. Her Baguette Round is a tradition older than anyone's memory of it: a route through the town, delivering to every door, in the right order. Getting the order wrong is not done.

## In the games

A bakery in the Old Town, off the Rue Haute (near x -1462, z -200). The Baguette Round is a town activity with its own route.

## Everyday

- **Shop signs:** "Bread. Opinions on bread. No refunds on opinions." "Bakery. Today: bread. Tomorrow: also bread." "Loaves, long and longer." (See [her bakery](brigitte-levains-bakery.md).)
- **Habit:** she measures each baguette with a brass rod, and sets a short one aside for the pigeons, who are not consulted.
- **Saying:** "Bread is the town's clock."

## Loose threads

The recipe. She says there is none; she does not say that the bread is made without one.
