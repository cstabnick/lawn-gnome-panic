The bakery off the Rue Haute in Pardonlin's Old Town (near x -1462, z -200), where Brigitte Levain bakes the long loaves that the Baguette Round delivers.

## What gnomes say

"Bread. Opinions on bread. No refunds on opinions."

"When Brigitte opens, it is morning. When she closes, it is her business."

## The truth

The shop is a corner of Rue Haute with a bell, a brass measuring rod and a long window of loaves in a row. Its sign reads one of three things, depending on the shopfront: "Bread. Opinions on bread. No refunds on opinions.", "Bakery. Today: bread. Tomorrow: also bread." or "Loaves, long and longer." The baguette is eighty centimetres, "not 79". Radio Adagio runs its advertisement between pieces.

## In the games

An Old Town shopfront on the Rue Haute (`euro-bakery`, `pardonlin/districts/oldTown.ts`) and the start of the Baguette Round activity.

## Everyday

- **Menu:** Baguette (80 cm), Long Baguette (96 cm, "by order"), Croissant (one per customer, per life).
- **Hours:** open at dawn, closed when the bread has finished, which is "when it is ready".
- **Notices:** "No mowers inside. Mowers includes you, Gerald." "The twelfth loaf is for the one who counts."
- **Habits:** a customer who asks for a short loaf is given a long look and a long loaf.
- **Sayings:** "Bread is the town's clock."
