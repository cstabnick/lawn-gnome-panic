The seven named bends of the Brim Gap Road, each christened for a part of a hat and numbered, on its own sign, in the order the HOA's Highways Sub-Committee argued about them. Drivers use the names; the sign uses the numbers; the two never agree.

## What gnomes say

"Hairpin four is the one that looks back at you."

"The Highways Sub-Committee numbered them by argument. You drive them by prayer."

## The truth

In driving order, name (number on the sign): **the Crease** (3), **the Cowlick** (1), **the Dent** (5), **the Glare** (4), **the Flip** (2), **the Scrunch** (7) and **the Brim Kink** (6). The numbers record the order the sub-committee argued them into the plan, not the order a car meets them, which is why the first bend on the road says 3 and the last says 6. The names were given by the first drivers and the sub-committee has never adopted them, on the grounds that "they are descriptive".

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): `mountainRoutes/brimGapPlan.ts` (`HAIRPIN_NAMES`): each hairpin carries a numbered sign and a name plate; the Top Hat café sits at the gap (R-232).
