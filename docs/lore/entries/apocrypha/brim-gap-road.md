The only road over the Hatbrim: it leaves the north band road east of Mittenholt, climbs seven or so numbered hairpins to Brim Gap, and drops down past the High Tarn. Cars use it both ways, and it has never been ploughed.

## What gnomes say

"Hairpin four is the one that looks back at you."
"The HOA built it to plough the Pass. It is still waiting for the snow to come and be ploughed."

## The truth

The Highways Sub-Committee built the road, and wrote "to plough it" on the first plan. It has never ploughed it. The hairpins are numbered in the order they were argued over, not the order they are driven. Velma Crown's café sits in the gap, so every car that climbs the road is counted (R-225, R-224).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): a two-way road from the north band road (`north-band-east`) east of Mittenholt, through Brim Gap (near (705, -1585)), past the High Tarn (`trail/high-tarn-path` runs alongside). The road is `mountainRoutes/defs/brimGapRoad.ts`, with its spots and dressing in `mountainRoutes/brimGap.ts`. The Top Hat is at the gap.
