A footpath over the Hatbrim, waymarked by cairns that somebody keeps stacking and nobody owns. It starts at the band hamlets and climbs past Pate's Bothy, a slate hut on Old Pate, to the Bobble's Summit Register: a book in a tin box, kept since before the HOA.

## What gnomes say

"Sign the book. You were here, and so was your hat."
"The cairns are not a map. They are a promise that someone came this way once and was not lost."

## The truth

Signing the Summit Register is a rite. Nobody agrees on who started it, and the entries go back past the first HOA charter. The bothy is kept dry, and its tally is not the Top Hat's (R-224). Walkers say the cairns are restacked each spring by a gnome who never signs the book.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): `mountainRoutes/defs/brimWalk.ts`, a footpath with its cairns and spots (Pate's Bothy and the Summit Register). Near the Bobble (700, -1700) and Old Pate (750, -1500). It links to the High Tarn path to the east.
