Keeps the Flock's washing, which is grey, because pink is for the Lady only. It is the dullest washing line in Loch Moss and Bulrush is proud of it.

## What gnomes say

"Brother Bulrush once found a pink sock in the grey load and stood out the whole of Tuesday."

## The truth

He wrings the washing on one leg. The line leans to the west. He says that is the wind, but it is the Lady.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: by the washing line at Wader's Rest, all of it grey.
