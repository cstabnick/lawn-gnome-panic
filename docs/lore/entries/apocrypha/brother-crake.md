The Flock's longest-standing debtor: a broad, patient member who stands at the west end of the ring before the Standing Lady and owes the tea urn nine cups, eleven if you count Thursday.

Home: a small grey tent on the ring at Wader's Rest. Work: the Flock (stands the west of the circle).

## What gnomes say

"Crake has owed the Flock nine cups since the thaw. They keep the count for him, kindly, on the rota."

"He stood a whole sermon once without wobbling. He was so surprised he fell over."

## The truth

Crake joined for the quiet and stayed for the rota. He wobbles on principle, on the grounds that a gnome who never has a thought is a gnome the Lady has stopped watching. The cups are real: eleven now, because of the one he spilled on Thursday, which he says does not count and the rota says does. Sedge has never once refused to pour him another.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: one of the five gnomes on the ring of the ritual circle at Wader's Rest (angle -80 degrees, the west end), facing the Lady, one-legged, grey-clad. His bubble: "Nine cups owed by me. Eleven if you count the one I spilled on Thursday." Named in the code (`societies/flock/life.ts`) but not in the E17 cast table.

## Loose threads

Whether the Thursday cup was spilled or poured out on purpose.
