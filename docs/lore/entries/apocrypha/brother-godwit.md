Always wobbling, always owing tea. In the rota his name is beside "had a thought" so often that Plover has stopped writing the rest.

## What gnomes say

"Godwit has had a thought. Godwit has had another."

## The truth

He is not bad at standing. He is a gnome who thinks a great deal, and it shows in the knees. The Flock are fond of him, so the tea is quietly free.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: wobbles at the edge of the circle in Wader's Rest.
