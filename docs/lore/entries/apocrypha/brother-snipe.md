The Flock's keeper of small distinctions: a quick, wiry brother on the ring who sorts every movement into prayer or shortcut and will tell you which yours was.

Home: a grey tent on the ring at Wader's Rest. Work: the Flock (stands the east side of the circle).

## What gnomes say

"Snipe says a hop is a prayer and a walk is a shortcut. He says it in the shade, where it is cooler to be right."

"Do not hop near him unless you mean it."

## The truth

Snipe's rule of thumb is not in Mother Saxifrage's five rules and has been quietly adopted anyway, which the Flock regard as the best way for a rule to start. He keeps to the shade because the Lady's side of the circle is in the sun. Of the ring five he is the one most likely to be found on two feet after dark, and the one least likely to admit it.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: on the ring of the ritual circle at Wader's Rest (angle 40 degrees, right of the Lady's line), one-legged and grey. His bubble: "If you hop, it is a prayer. If you walk, it is a shortcut. Walk in the shade."
