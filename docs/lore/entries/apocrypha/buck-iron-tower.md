Buck-Iron Tower is green glass in three gold-edged setbacks, with a gold spire. At about 77 metres it is the tallest tower in Turfton after Thorne Tower, and it does not let anybody forget it.

## What gnomes say

"Second tallest is the first loser. Buck-Iron has the spire to prove it is not losing."

"Thorne Tower looks down on Buck-Iron. Buck-Iron looks at the spire. Everybody else looks at the gold, which is the point."

## In the games

It stands on the x = 20, z = -90 block of Downtown Turfton, a green glass tower in three setbacks under a gold spire, second only to Thorne Tower in height. Its setbacks, gold edges and spire are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
