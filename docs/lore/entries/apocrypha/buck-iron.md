Heavy green-and-gold agricultural equipment manufacturer behind Turfton's Ride-On Mowers and Combine Harvesters, locked in a bitter feud with Razor-Back.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"Nothing Runs Like A Buck (Until You Take The Keys)."* Buck-Iron builds iron-framed beasts meant to flatten hedges and haul pumpkin trailers down the `south` highway. Their smug Turfton FM radio spots dismiss Razor-Back racers as *"over-caffeinated blenders on caster wheels,"* though Buck-Iron engineers still haven't figured out how to stop thieves from hotwiring their ignitions with a butter knife.

## Consumer Reports

Drivers in green Buck-Iron caps honk aggressively at orange Razor-Back riders at red lights, and Turfton mechanics charge a 20% "Emotional Damage Surcharge" to work on the rival brand.

## Loose threads

Why Buck-Iron refuses to install keyed ignitions that can't be turned with cutlery.
