Bucketworth & Sons, Collectors: the firm in the Annex, "collecting since before there was anything to collect". It is
hiring for one position, Collector. It has never produced a document.

## What gnomes say

"Collecting since before there was anything to collect."

"The position is Collector." (Primrose, at reception)

"Nobody's ever finished the interview. You just sort of... stop." (Sorrel Gluepot, at the water cooler)

## In the games

- `games/nothing-left-to-collect`: the company behind the interview. Every floor of the Annex is its office, and every
  collectible in the game (Acorns, Clips, Sticky Squares, Gold Buttons, Tokens of Appreciation, Stars of Recognition,
  Collector's Badges, Everything) is its work.
