Race director of the Lost & Found Speedway, quest giver at the race desk in the pit lane, and a former Hat Watch traffic
marshal who switched sides when the flag came with a better pension.

## What gnomes say

"Welcome to the Lost & Found Speedway!" He says it to everyone, including the Hat Watch, who have not yet worked out
whether he is joking.

"Wait for my flag. It is a tea towel, but it is a very serious tea towel." The chequered tea towel has been washed
more often than the track.

## History

Bellweather spent nine years directing traffic at the Turfton ring road for the Hat Watch, where he learnt to read a
convoy from a hundred metres and to hate lollipop-stick signals. He left after a dispute over a tea towel and set up
at the Speedway the week the pan was paved. He runs the Fizz Cup for Dandelion Fizz, who pay the prize money and send
their own man to check the rosettes.

He is the only gnome on the pan who calls the heap "the Spill" in public and "the Lost Property" in his notes.

## Quirks

- He wears a race director's headset that is not plugged into anything. He says it picks up the Tall Ones.
- He will not start a race until he has counted the grid twice, out loud.
- He has never taken the tea towel off his shoulder, not even in the rain.

## In the games

- The Fizz Cup's quest giver (`quests/races.ts`, id `speedway`). He introduces each of the four races at the race desk.

## Loose threads

Did he really leave the Hat Watch over the tea towel? He says so in the Rookie Mow-Down intro and never again.
