An old gnome who sits on the bench at Drainpipe Court, the bench that is older than the court. He keeps the chalk score on the scoreboard and has never played a point.

## What gnomes say

"The bench was here first. The court came later and went round it."

"Burdock has never played. He has an opinion on every shot."

## In the games

Sits on the old bench on the north side of the court, facing the court. Keeps the chalk score on the scoreboard during pickup games. Does not play; his stats are his own.

## Loose threads

Whether he was ever young enough to play, and what the bench was for before there was a court.
