Pedals the Underspan's generator, all day, on a bicycle bolted to the floor. The workshop lights, the welder and the kettle all depend on Burdock's calves.

## What gnomes say

"If the lights flicker, Burdock has stopped to think. Throw him a biscuit."

## The truth

He is the most important gnome in the Underspan and the only one who has never asked for credit. He pedals to a tune he hums, and the generator, loyally, hums back.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: pedals in the Underspan's yard; the `pedal-whirr` is the sound of the place.
