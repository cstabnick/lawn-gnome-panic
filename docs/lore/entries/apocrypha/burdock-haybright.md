The head ranger of the Unmown: white-bearded, olive-hatted, unhurried, keeper of the station book, the Last Blade and the checklist; he will not say "hedge trimmer".

## What gnomes say

"Welcome to the Unmown. Please sign the book, wipe your boots, and do not, under any circumstances, mention hedge trimmers."

"Twelve species before lunch. Thirteen if you count the HOA inspector hiding in the reeds."

## The truth

Rangers never fine anyone (R-203). He keeps the book and has read every line of every visitor's remark; the thirteenth species is the HOA inspector, whom he is fond of.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E15): at the station desk inside the Unmown Ranger Station (olive hat, white beard), and outdoors beside the door.
