A used-mower lot in Turfton, beside Side-Valve Salvage, hung with bunting and a red price-tag sign. Ground vehicles only.

## Story: Everything Must Go

Every mower on the lot has a price tag on its handle, and every price tag says the same thing: a little less than the last one. Honest Burl runs it from a folding table at the gate. The Sling-Lift delivers each sale, so a buyer never has to haul a mower home.

## What gnomes say

"Burl's got a mower for every budget. Tansy got the good ones first."

## Loose threads

Whether any mower on the lot has ever been sold twice.
