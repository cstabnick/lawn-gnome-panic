A used-mower lot in Turfton, beside Side-Valve Salvage, hung with bunting and a red price-tag sign. Ground vehicles only.

## Story: Everything Must Go

Every mower on the lot has a price tag on its handle, and every price tag says the same thing: a little less than the last one. Honest Burl runs it from a folding table at the gate. The Sling-Lift delivers each sale, so a buyer never has to haul a mower home.

## What gnomes say

"Burl's got a mower for every budget. Tansy got the good ones first."

## Everyday

- Price tags: every tag on a handle has the price crossed out, the new price written under it and "NOW" stamped over the lot. A mower that has not been marked down in a week is looked at with suspicion.
- Notices: "EVERYTHING MUST GO" (the sign has stood for eleven years); "TEST DRIVES: ONE LAP, BURL'S WATCH"; "NO REFUNDS. SOME LAUGHTER."
- House rule at the folding table: "You break it, I fix it. You break it twice, I know you."
- Habit: Burl walks every buyer to the gate and shakes their hand, then counts his fingers.
- Saying: "As honest as Burl" (said in jest by those who have paid for it, and in earnest by those who have not).
- Small history: the lot's bunting was bought as a job lot of party hats, and has been in the same colours ever since.

## Loose threads

Whether any mower on the lot has ever been sold twice.
