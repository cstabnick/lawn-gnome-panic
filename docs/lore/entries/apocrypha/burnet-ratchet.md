The mower mechanic of Pebblewick: oil to the elbows, a spanner in every pocket, and a tow rope always coiled by the door.

## What gnomes say

"Every racer stops here twice. Once on the way out, once on a tow."

## The truth

Burnet fixes the racers who break down on the Speedway run and tows the rest home, and he keeps every bent part in a box in case the next racer needs it.

## In the games

Keeps the mower bench on a Pebblewick side street. Ratchet's Thanks is the reward for 'The Hum at Three'.

## Loose threads

Where the bent parts go when the boxes are full.
