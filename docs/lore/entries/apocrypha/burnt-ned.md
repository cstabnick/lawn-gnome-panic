A sunbather on the coast: orange hat, white shirt, a very red face, and absolute confidence in his sun protection.

## What gnomes say

"Factor fifty. I am factor fifty. Nothing else is factor anything."

## The truth

He is the proof the coast works: he lies still for hours and comes back a deeper red each day. The gnomes of the coast say his name is a description, not a warning.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E02): lies back on a deck chair at about (-105, 1171) on the beach with the "lean-back" idle, beside Bev Nell.
