The walk-in café on Teacup Row where Hector Disdain waits and never takes an order.

## What gnomes say

"Café Pardon has a very good menu. You may admire it. You may not order from it."

"The coffee arrives when it is ready. It was ready on Tuesday."

## The truth

The café is named for the first word everybody says in it. A player can walk in and sit down; whether anything comes of that is up to Hector's mood, which is the same every day. The gramophone on the terrace table plays Radio Adagio to the whole lane.

## In the games

Teacup Row, near x -1525, z 143 (the terrace gramophone). A walk-in interior: stand at a table and wait, and Hector will pass, sigh and not take your order.
