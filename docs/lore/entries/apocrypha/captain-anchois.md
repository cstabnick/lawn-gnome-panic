The harbourmaster of Basin Quay, who commands one retired swan pedalo and a very large hat.

## What gnomes say

"The Captain has never lost a ship. He has only the one."

"He logs every arrival. The log is mostly about the swan."

## The truth

Anchois takes the harbour seriously because nobody else will, and he keeps it spotless for ships that do not come. The swan pedalo is his flagship and his oldest friend, and he salutes it on the hour.

## In the games

Basin Quay, at the south end of town (near x -1510, z 1020).
