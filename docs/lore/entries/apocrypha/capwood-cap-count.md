The six caps in Capwood that Warden Chanterelle Stook has not yet fined anyone for, each with a ticket-flag stuck in it, and the five little scenes the forest keeps: a bruised cap, an abandoned picnic, a half-dug hole, a photographer's stool and the Warden's fine board.

## What gnomes say

"Count the caps. Chanterelle counts them first, and she is always one higher."
"Don't lean on the Leaning Cap. It has a reputation and it is an earned one."

## The truth

The six caps (the Cap Count side quest): **the Leaning Cap** (120, -1250), **the Twin Stalks** (300, -1265), **the Dewbowl Cap** (200, -1090), **the Shelf Stump** (380, -1110), **the Puffball Hollow** (385, -1190) and **the Warden's Favourite** (130, -1180). Each carries a ticket-flag the Warden stuck in it and has not yet got round to using. The forest's scenes are told without anyone telling them: **The Bruised Cap** (301, -1128) is a zero-turn mower nose-first in the stem of a purple cap with a ticket under its wiper and a pen in the moss; **The Abandoned Picnic** (231, -1100) is laid out under a cap with an umbrella open over it, though it was not raining; **The Half-Dug Hole** (165, -1235) is by a stump, the spade standing, the "Tall-One wood: do not dig" sign knocked flat; **The Photographer's Spot** (171, -1105) is a tripod and a worn stool aimed at the Great Ring, its owner still without the photograph; and the Warden's fine board by her hut at (360, -1245) has been corrected twice, both times upward. The Great Ring itself is a fairy ring of glowing toadstools 14 m in radius at (150, -1125).

## In the games

Side quest "The Cap Count" (`quests/dirtRaces.ts`) walks the six spots as a loop out from the Warden's hut and back. The scenes are vignettes in `capwood/forestPlan.ts`. The Run's road keeps 25 m clear of the hut, the ring and each of the six caps.
