The rally route from Frostbite Fells over the mountain, through the toadstools of Capwood and across fords of Cap-water, which is thick and slow and never floods an engine. The Carburettors run it for pride, and the HOA charter has never been allowed to touch it.

## What gnomes say

"Start at the Fells, finish with mud to the axles and your hat still on. That is a good Run."
"Cap-water won't drown a mower. It will only slow you down, and tell the whole forest you passed."

## The truth

Stages, for the race build: (1) the start in the Frostbite Fells, by the Carburettors' ice; (2) the climb, a switchback up the mountain on packed snow; (3) Capwood, a weaving run between giant stalks, with the Warden's cap-bruise fines; (4) the Cap-water fords, shallow crossings that drag but never stall; (5) the finish in the clearing on the far side.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3): E14 builds the route as a rally or checkpoint race (E01 rally-route). Quest row 40 (m40-the-capwood-run): Lugnut Lulu will help the crew if the player wins it. The Carburettors get their own scene.
