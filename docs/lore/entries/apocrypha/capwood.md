A forest of giant toadstools on Snowcap Rise, tall enough that gnomes walk under the caps. The toadstools grow over a Tall-One woodpile, buried long ago and still rotting slowly. Warden Chanterelle Stook keeps it and fines anyone who bruises a cap.

## What gnomes say

"Don't brush the caps. The Warden will know."
"Pink under the cap, grey on top. Never eat one, and never ask what they grow on."

## The truth

The woodpile is the reason for the forest: the toadstools feed on it and have for longer than anyone has kept the wood. Why it was buried is a Tall One matter, and a Tall One matter stays unanswered.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3): E01 site toadstool-forest at (250, -1150), built by the area epic. Quest row 40 (m40-the-capwood-run) runs through it; Chanterelle Stook fines the racers for bruising the caps.
