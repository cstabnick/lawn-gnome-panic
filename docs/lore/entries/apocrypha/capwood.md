A forest of giant toadstools on Snowcap Rise, tall enough that gnomes walk under the caps. The toadstools grow over a Tall-One woodpile, buried long ago and still rotting slowly. Warden Chanterelle Stook keeps it and fines anyone who bruises a cap.

## What gnomes say

"Don't brush the caps. The Warden will know."
"Pink under the cap, grey on top. Never eat one, and never ask what they grow on."

## The truth

The woodpile is the reason for the forest: the toadstools feed on it and have for longer than anyone has kept the wood. Why it was buried is a Tall One matter, and a Tall One matter stays unanswered.

## In the games

Built (E14): natural ground at x 100..400, z -1280..-1080, about 2 m high. The Warden's hut is in a hollow toadstool stalk at (360, -1245), and the Great Ring, a fairy ring of toadstools round a clearing, sits at (150, -1125). The caps glow faintly after dark, the Great Ring brightest (R-192). Quest row 40 (m40-the-capwood-run) runs through it; Chanterelle Stook fines the racers for bruising the caps.

## Everyday

**Shops.** None. The Warden's hut at (360, -1245) takes fines, and two sacks of acorns beside her board are the proof that people pay.

**Notices.**
- The fine board: "FINES. Bruised cap: 11 acorns. Licked cap: 4 acorns. Loud spores: a warning." Corrected twice, both times upward.
- "CAPWOOD. Section Nine applies." and "BRUISE-FREE ZONE."
- "DO NOT LICK THE CAPS. (Section Four)"
- By the Great Ring: "THE GREAT RING. Looking is allowed (Section Eleven)."
- "MIND THE ROOTS. They were here first."
- Knocked flat by a stump: "TALL-ONE WOOD. DO NOT DIG."
- By the Warden's door: "FINES PAYABLE IN ACORNS OR A SINCERE APOLOGY."

**Sayings and habits.**
- "Section nine." Anyone who touches a cap hears its number before they have finished touching.
- A photographer sits by the Ring with a tripod and a worn stool, waiting for the caps to glow in one frame. He has not got it.
- Local food: none. Nobody eats a cap, and nobody asks what they grow on. See also the Great Ring.

**Small history.** The first fine was four acorns and a bad hat. The Warden has not forgotten the hat.
