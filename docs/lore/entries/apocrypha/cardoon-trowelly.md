A regular at Drainpipe Court and its unofficial champion. A hustler with a heart: he wins bets for a living and gives the stake back if you ask properly.

## What gnomes say

"Swish never loses a bet. He loses interest."

"He took Pip's toolbox. He's kept it dusted."

## In the games

Home: a flat over the laundrette by the court, Lather & Lint (hidden). Work: Drainpipe Court (unofficial), where he plays pickup games with Lintel Crossover. He wears the net the hoop never had, and that is why the court calls him Swish. His only main quest is 14 (`m14-drainpipe-court`).
