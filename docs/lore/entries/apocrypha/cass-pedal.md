A cyclist on the Hatbrim: red hat, yellow jersey, a ginger beard, who rode all the way up for a fence and says it is a good fence. Not Cass of Pay 'n' Glaze.

## What gnomes say

"Rode all the way up for this. It is a fence. A good fence, mind."

## The truth

She is the proof that the Hatbrim has a good fence; she tests the fence each time she passes. She is not Chrome-Knuckle Cass, who is a foreman, and has never ridden anything.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): on the Hatbrim, "look-around" idle, facing outward toward the fence.
