The ride each named stage character drives, parks or hides in. These are the decisions the stage cast table (`CAST` in `stages.ts`) commits to, and the vehicles a player sees on the stage.

## In the games
- Pip: a red primer Zero-Turn.
- Tansy: a smoking orange Zero-Turn.
- Vesper: a pearl-white Lowrider with gold spinners.
- Don Compost: a gloss-black Lowrider with whitewalls.
- Granny: a lilac Ride-On with a hay seat and a cowcatcher.
- Juniper: a navy pearl Ride-On with a flag.
- Mayor Mulch: a candy-red Ride-On.
- Reginald: an HOA Code-Enforcement Van.
- Diggs: an olive Zero-Turn with a roo-bar.
- Father Loam: a Ride-On "snowmobile" on spiked tyres with candle lamps and a bell.
- Wally: owns the Crop Duster.
- Driftwood: a walnut dinghy.
- John Bodega: a delivery Push-Mower Bike.
- Big Lou: a matte-black Lowrider.
- Brenda: a glitter-gold Push-Mower Bike.
- Clara: a shock-absorbed pearl Lowrider.
- Burt: a decommissioned primer HOA van.
- Cedric: a glitter Mulch-Spreader.
- Bramble: the Buck-Iron Combine.
- Shelldon: a retired snail-coach he lives in.
- Nina: a fairy-lit Push-Mower Bike.
- Turncoat Cam: a chameleon Zero-Turn, bought with his reward.
- Elder Bracken, Sorrel and the Unmowed ride nothing with an engine.
