Fast-talking Chapel of Mulch upline recruiter operating out of a rusted van, hawking "Artisanal Miracle Mulch" made of dyed sawdust and craft glitter.

## Story: Quota Due Friday

Cedric wears two matching lapel pins, smiles like a used-mower salesman, and promises every desperate rookie in Turfton that they can achieve "Total Soil Enlightenment" by recruiting three friends under them. His miracle mulch bags smell faintly of cedar and attract furious wasps within twenty minutes of opening.

When angry suburbanites call the Hat Watch, Cedric is already three blocks away in his van, blaming "negative turf energy."

## What gnomes say

"If Cedric offers you a ground-floor mulch opportunity, check your wallet and run."

## The truth
Cedric is the Chapel of Mulch's upline recruiter: a pyramid in a van. He wants three recruits by Friday, and fears the Chapel's quota actually being real. Secret: his own mulch gives him hives. Home: the rusted Chapel van. He owes the Chapel a quota, drinks coffee with Burt, and Ernie sells him the dyed sawdust. Voice: bright, salesman patter at double speed, laughing at his own lines; "ground-floor opportunity" in every sales sentence ("Ground-floor opportunity, friend! Soil Enlightenment, no wasps!" / "Three friends. That is all."). Father Loam is the chaplain his Chapel cast out; Cedric's scheme sold that Chapel its quota (R-139).

Arc: side quests only in all four acts; he stays himself while everything else changes.

## Loose threads

How much of Cedric's glitter-sawdust actually reaches the Chapel of Mulch's quota.

Is the Chapel of Mulch's quota a real sanction, or only a deadline Cedric made up? Tansy at Side-Valve Salvage says the
scheme is busted "all the way down", and nobody has said what happens to a recruiter who misses it.

## In the games

The Mulch Pyramid Scheme side quest (`sq-mulch-pyramid`): Cedric pitches "Artisanal Miracle Mulch" outside Bodega &
Sons and sends the player off in his glitter-purple Mulch-Spreader to a Patio Heights drop, where the wasps and the Hat
Watch arrive. The scheme is busted at Side-Valve Salvage, under Tansy's nose.
