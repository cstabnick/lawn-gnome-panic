Fast-talking Chapel of Mulch upline recruiter operating out of a rusted van, hawking "Artisanal Miracle Mulch" made of dyed sawdust and craft glitter.

## Story: Quota Due Friday

Cedric wears two matching lapel pins, smiles like a used-mower salesman, and promises every desperate rookie in Turfton that they can achieve "Total Soil Enlightenment" by recruiting three friends under them. His miracle mulch bags smell faintly of cedar and attract furious wasps within twenty minutes of opening.

When angry suburbanites call the Hat Watch, Cedric is already three blocks away in his van, blaming "negative turf energy."

## What gnomes say

"If Cedric offers you a ground-floor mulch opportunity, check your wallet and run."
