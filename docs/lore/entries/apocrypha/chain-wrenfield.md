The cyclist with the upside-down bike at Brim Gap: yellow hat, blue shirt, brown beard, and a firm opinion that it is not the hill, it is the chain, and also the hill.

## What gnomes say

"It is not the hill. It is the chain. It is also the hill."

## The truth

He has been fixing the chain since the foot of the hill; the bike is on its saddle so the hill cannot get at it.
