Warden of Capwood: stern, upright and always carrying a notebook, in which she writes down every cap that gets bruised.

## What gnomes say

"That is a bruised cap. That is four acorns. The next one is eight."
"I do not dislike racers. I dislike marks on the caps."

## In the games

Home: a hut in a hollow toadstool stalk in Capwood (E01 site toadstool-forest). Work: she keeps Capwood as its warden. Planned (batch 3): row 40 (m40-the-capwood-run), where she fines the crew and the Carburettors for bruising the caps. Her hat is a tall green felt with a cap-shaped brim.
