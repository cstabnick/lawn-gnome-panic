Warden of Capwood: stern, upright and always carrying a notebook, in which she writes down every cap that gets bruised.

## What gnomes say

"That is a bruised cap. That is eleven acorns. I will write it down."
"I do not dislike racers. I dislike marks on the caps."

## In the games

Home: a hut in a hollow toadstool stalk in Capwood, at (360, -1245). Work: she keeps Capwood as its warden. Built (E14): she gives The Cap Count (`sq-capwood-cap-count`) and keeps a clipboard of unfined caps. Row 40 (m40-the-capwood-run) is where she fines the crew and the Carburettors for bruising the caps, citing the rules by number: "That is a bruised cap. Section nine. Eleven acorns." (R-192). Her hat is a tall green felt with a cap-shaped brim.

## Everyday

**The fine board.** At her door (368, -1252), a notice board in her own hand: "Bruised cap: 11 acorns. Licked cap: 4 acorns. Loud spores: a warning." Two sacks of acorns beside it, and "FINES PAYABLE IN ACORNS OR A SINCERE APOLOGY." She prefers the acorns, but tallies the apologies.

**Habits.** She walks the forest every dusk with a clipboard, counting caps and sticking a ticket-flag in each one she has not fined anyone for. She cites rules by number, never by name.

**Sayings.** "Section Nine. Eleven acorns." "Looking is allowed. That is Section Eleven."
