Warden of Capwood: stern, upright and always carrying a notebook, in which she writes down every cap that gets bruised.

## What gnomes say

"That is a bruised cap. That is four acorns. The next one is eight."
"I do not dislike racers. I dislike marks on the caps."

## In the games

Home: a hut in a hollow toadstool stalk in Capwood, at (360, -1245). Work: she keeps Capwood as its warden. Built (E14): she gives The Cap Count (`sq-capwood-cap-count`) and keeps a clipboard of unfined caps. Row 40 (m40-the-capwood-run) is where she fines the crew and the Carburettors for bruising the caps, citing the rules by number: "That is a bruised cap. Section nine. Eleven acorns." (R-188). Her hat is a tall green felt with a cap-shaped brim.
