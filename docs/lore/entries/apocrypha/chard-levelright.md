Foreman of Levelright Construction: broad, patient, sure of his trade and fond of a good straight edge. He builds what the plans say and has stopped reading who signs them.

## What gnomes say

"Chard? Best bricklayer in the yard. Ask what he's building and he'll give you the dimensions."

"A level doesn't lie, he says. Nobody has the heart to ask who holds it."

## The truth

He is not cruel. He has kept the work going for years by looking at the bubble and nothing past it. In #69 he realises he built for nobody.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Home: a site caravan at the nearest Even-Estates site (site:construction-1, acknowledged). Work: Levelright Construction, Thorne Even-Estates. First main quest 7 (`m07-even-estates`), last 69 (`m69-the-combine-wakes`); also in #15 and #28.
