Head gardener of Trellisdown: wiry, brown-handed and loyal to the vines before the family. He burns frost-pots through the cold nights and has no time for anyone who walks on a row.

Home: the gardener's lodge at the vineyard gate. Work: Trellisdown Vineyard.

## What gnomes say

"Chicory talks to the vines and they answer, in the sense that they do what he says."

"Ask him about the Countess and he will tell you about the frost."

## The truth

Chicory likes the Countess and does not trust the money. He buys the stolen Dew at the gate because the frost-pots must be lit, and does not ask where it comes from, which has kept him honest in his own opinion.

## In the games

Built by epic E16 (R-204): his lodge stands just inside the vineyard gate at (-1172, -78), and he is "he" everywhere. The gardener who burns Granny's Dew in quest 17 (`m17-the-dew-buyer`); the one to slip past in 50 (`m50-trellisdown-tasting`); a quiet helper in 65 (`m65-the-countess-relents`).
