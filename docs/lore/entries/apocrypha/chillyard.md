The northern edge of the Fringe, where the north road climbs out of Turfton towards the Fells: stone sheds,
portacabins and a water tower, frost on every fence in the morning, and the only pub the Watch will not drive past.

## What gnomes say

"In Chillyard the fence is the only thing that stays warm, because it's the only thing that gets painted." The frost
is so regular that the townsgnomes plan their washing days around it.

## Inside & Stories

- Stone sheds with heavy doors, built low to keep the wind out, with the frost laid on the roofs like icing.
- Portacabins with steps and blocks underneath, and one window lit at all hours by someone who is not sleeping.
- A water tower on splayed legs, with a ladder that goes up to a tank nobody has checked since the last freeze.
- The Rusty Tine, its warm windows the first thing a traveller sees coming in from the Fells.

## In the games

Chillyard is the north edge of the Fringe, bordering Frostbite Fells. It is the coldest-looking part of the Fringe, and
the lots here take the Fells' look on the north side of the belt.
