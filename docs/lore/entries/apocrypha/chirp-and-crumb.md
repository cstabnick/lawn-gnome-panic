Turfton's warm-pink fast-food birdseed-and-pastry chain, locked in a bitter culinary cold war with Gnoma's Food Truck.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"Freshly Baked Since 4:00 AM. Hard Enough To Patch A Wall."* Chirp & Crumb delivery vans rattle down Turfton's grid before dawn, unloading trays of dense millet scones that double as emergency masonry in a pinch. Even washed-up surf-rockers The Velvet Toadstools argue over royalties in their corner booths.

## Consumer Reports

Cheap, filling, and indestructible—though Cress Caliper regularly mocks their delivery vans on 104.1 HOA Compliance Beats.
