Foreman of the Pay 'n' Glaze custom shop in Turfton. Her knuckles are chrome-plated, or very well polished; nobody has asked which. She hands out client work orders from a pink pegboard by the roll-up door, and judges every finished car by one measure: whether the client cried (happy).

## What gnomes say

"Cass doesn't ask if it's fast. Cass asks if they cried."

"Take a card off the pegboard. Don't take two."

## In the games

Planned (E24). Cass runs the work-order pegboard at the Turfton Pay 'n' Glaze lot, where the mechanic job starts.
