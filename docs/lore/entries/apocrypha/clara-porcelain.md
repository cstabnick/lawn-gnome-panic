A finely cracked porcelain gnome hiding inside a hollow log near Mossy Wood's boarded-up porch, organizing midnight escapes for fellow Tall House refugees.

## Journal: Lantern-Watch Notes

> *Earthenware gnomes don't understand what it's like to be brittle. They get clipped by a Zero-Turn, sweep themselves into an ambulance, and walk out chewing mints. If I hit a kerb too hard, I'm dust. Tonight we move four more newcomers to the Hollow Oak Stump—just need someone with soft suspension and Wax Lanterns before dark.*

Clara speaks softly, keeps a tiny velvet cushion in her coat pocket, and never looks directly at yellow porch-light.

## What gnomes say

"Drive slow over speed bumps when Clara's in the passenger seat, or you'll break her heart—and her ribs."
