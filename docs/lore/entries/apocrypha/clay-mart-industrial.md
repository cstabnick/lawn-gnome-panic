The south-west corner of Turfton: the warehouses, the returns yard, the Kilnworks behind them and the Local 404 Union Hall on the road that runs round the back. Everything that comes into town by the Snail-Coach line is sorted here, and most of what goes out again is sorted here too.

## Character

Hard-working and heavy. The air smells of firing clay and cheap oil, the roads are the union's and they are better than they need to be, and the gnomes are tired in a way that makes them very good at their jobs.

## Landmarks

- The Clay-Mart warehouse, (-150, 150): the big sorting shed. See `clay-mart-warehouse`.
- The returns yard and the HOA impound, (-130, 150): where repossessed mowers wait to be claimed, and where the impound clerks work.
- The Clay-Mart Kilnworks, behind the warehouse: lore only, see `clay-mart-kilnworks`.
- The Local 404 Union Hall, on the z = 180 road behind Clay-Mart, around (-150, 180): see `local-404-union-hall`.
- The canal culvert, (-195, 195): where the drainage canal goes under the district and into the Under-Deck. See `under-deck`.

## Factions

- Local 404: the union, which keeps the roads and the Dew-Haulers in work.
- Clay-Mart Collections: the company's billing office, which sends a bill for everything it touches.
- The HOA impound: the Patio Heights HOA's repossession crew, who are not welcome on the union's roads.

## What gnomes say

"Clay-Mart fires you, then bills you for the fire."

"The union's roads are the only roads in Turfton that don't have a sign telling you what they're for."

## Local slang

- "fired and billed": a newcomer, who has been fired and has the bill to show for it.
- "forty-eight hours": a strike threat. When somebody in Local 404 says it, the roads will be quiet by morning.
- "on the meter": in debt, usually to Clay-Mart.

## In the games

Clay-Mart Industrial is the south-west district. The impound run and the Skyway job both start here: the impound clerks work the returns yard, and the ledger is taken from the Union Hall. Its district sign is `industrial` in `src/districts.ts`, a block from x -215 to -115 and z 115 to 215.
