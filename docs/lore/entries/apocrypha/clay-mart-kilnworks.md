A brick kiln behind the Clay-Mart warehouse. It fires and glazes newcomers for the Snail-Coach line, and then bills them.

## What gnomes say

"You're born fired, glazed, and five grand in the hole." Newcomers say it with less humour than the old hands.

## The truth

Turfton has no kiln of its own for new gnomes. Everyone who steps off the Snail-Coach was fired here, and Clay-Mart
holds every Kiln-Debt that came out of it.

## In the games

Lore only. The Kilnworks is behind the Clay-Mart warehouse in Clay-Mart Industrial and is not a playable place.
