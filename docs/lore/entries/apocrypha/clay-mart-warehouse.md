A colossal blue-and-yellow warehouse on the edge of Turfton's grid housed beneath an upside-down Tall-One Tupperware container.

## Inside & Stories

Inside, fluorescent aisle lights hum over towering shelves of discount garden hats, pink plastic flamingos, and budget umbrellas. Rumor says brand-new gnomes with pre-approved Kiln-Debt occasionally walk straight out of the garden-centre crates into the parking lot. A smaller satellite branch across from Bodega & Sons sits permanently shuttered.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Banner over the automatic doors: *"EVERYTHING UNDER ONE GIANT UPSIDE-DOWN TUPPERWARE."*
