Blue-and-yellow big-box suburban retail giant and aggressive collections agency that buys up newcomers' Kiln-Debt before they even step off the snail-coach.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"Everything Under One Giant Upside-Down Tupperware."* Clay-Mart sells budget umbrellas, plastic flamingos, and bulk garden hats at prices no corner shop can match—except John Bodega, whose neighborhood mysteriously repelled Clay-Mart's expansion after a delivery snail vanished for three weeks.

## Consumer Reports

Great for cheap replacement hats; terrible when *Clay-Mart Collections* sends repo gnomes after your Push-Mower Bike.
