The formal garden on the east side of Turfton: a hedge maze in clipped boxwood, rose beds in straight rows, arches of
climbing roses over the paths, a wishing fountain at the heart of it all, and two statues of a pair of Clayfoots that
no two townsgnomes can agree on.

## What gnomes say

"The maze is easy. You just have to be lost in the right direction." The head gardener says the maze has been redrawn
eleven times, and that the entrance has never once been in the same place twice.

## Inside & Stories

- The hedge maze: seven by four cells of clipped boxwood, with the entrance on the south side and the exit on the north.
  The centre cell has a small gnome-statue, the only figure in the maze that does not turn out to be a dead end.
- The Clayfoot Twins: two gnome-statues either side of the fountain, each facing the other and each claimed by a
  different family. The gardeners say the left-hand one is Tansy Clayfoot and the right-hand one is "the other one".
- The wishing fountain, a stone basin with a jet, where an Acorn thrown in the right way is said to bring a fortune
  (a splash of Glaze, or a coin back, depending on who is asked).
- Rose arches over four paths and rose beds in the quadrants between them, red, pink and cream, with every fourth bush
  a gold one that nobody planted on purpose.
- Benches face the fountain. Lamps and a noticeboard keep the paths lit and the rules pinned up at the gate.

## In the games

Clayfoot Rose Garden is the park at block centre (150, 30). It has the one wishing fountain outside downtown: a
player can stand at the basin and make a wish with an Acorn. Players can sit on the benches, and the maze is a
walkable hedge, not a driveable one.

## Loose threads

Which Clayfoot is which. Nobody agrees, and the statues are not signed.
