A big yellow wooden clog with four seats, a tiller at the heel and a pair of mower-grade paddles bolted to the sides. It is the family boat of the New Puddle marina.

## Field Notes & Tales

A clog floats because it was made to keep a foot dry, which in Turfton means it is always a little too roomy and always a little too slow. Four gnomes fit comfortably, and the marina rents them by the hour to anybody who can still prove they own a hat.

## What gnomes say

"Get in the clog, Gran. The Republic has a schedule to keep and the schedule is a pun."

## In the games

Moored at the New Puddle marina on the Southern Drainage Canal. It carries the most gnomes of any boat and goes the slowest of the big ones.

Moored at New Puddle Marina. All five boats count toward the "Captain Clay" achievement: take the helm of every one and earn a Captain's Cap.
