Underground reservoirs beneath Cloverfield Farms where crude Black Dew is pumped to fuel Turfton's mowers, planes, and illicit stills.

## Field Notes & Tales

Before Thorne Lawn & Order LLC began buying up rural fuel contracts, Cloverfield farmers tapped the Dew-Wells freely to run their Buck-Iron Combines. Controlling these pumps is the spark behind Chapter 2's highway blockades—whoever owns the Dew-Wells decides whether Turfton drives or walks.

## What gnomes say

"Stick a hose in the right gopher hole down south and you'll pull up 87-octane."
