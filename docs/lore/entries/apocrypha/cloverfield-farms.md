Rolling southern pastures (`z > 215`) of giant pumpkin silos, rich clover soil, Dew-Wells, and stubborn farmers defending their harvests from HOA foreclosures.

## Field Notes & Tales

Cloverfield is the breadbasket—and chop-shop—of the entire yard. Early gnomes terraced the clover hills and bred slow, heavy snails to haul acorn harvests north to Turfton. Between the pumpkin patches sit hidden Black Dew wells that fuel half the city's illicit racers, making the `south` highway a constant battleground for convoy ambushes.

## What gnomes say

"Cloverfield feeds Turfton's stomachs—and fuels half its chop-shops."

## Look and motifs

Cloverfield is fields stitched together with hedgerows and drystone walls. Hay bales sit by the
gates, scarecrows watch the verges, and the farm gnomes live in thatched cottages with white walls and a wooden fence
round the yard.

- Motifs: hedgerows, drystone walls, wooden fences, hay bales, scarecrows, thatched cottages.
- Palette: white and cream walls, straw thatch, pink and red flowers.
- In the game: theme `cloverfield-farms` in `src/data/regionThemes.json`.
