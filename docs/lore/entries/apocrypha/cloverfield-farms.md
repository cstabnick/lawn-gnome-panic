Rolling southern pastures (`z > 215`) of giant pumpkin silos, rich clover soil, Dew-Wells, and stubborn farmers defending their harvests from HOA foreclosures.

## Field Notes & Tales

Cloverfield is the breadbasket—and chop-shop—of the entire yard. Early gnomes terraced the clover hills and bred slow, heavy snails to haul acorn harvests north to Turfton. Between the pumpkin patches sit hidden Black Dew wells that fuel half the city's illicit racers, making the `south` highway a constant battleground for convoy ambushes.

## What gnomes say
