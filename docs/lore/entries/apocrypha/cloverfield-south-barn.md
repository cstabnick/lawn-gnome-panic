A barn and a silo at the south end of the Cloverfield farmland. Granny Gnertrude's armoured silo is hidden here, in plain sight among the hay.

## What gnomes say
*"Nobody looks in a barn that's already full of hay."* (Granny, who has never been asked twice)

## In the games
Stage `south-barn`. Farmer Bramble haunts it, and it is where the armoured silo can be found.
