What the Dust Flats overlook becomes once `m64-all-hats` is done: seven tents in a ring, one per faction, round a long table made of fruit crates with one pie in the middle and a rail on which everybody's hat is hung. Nobody planned its layout. It looks as if a town had been invited and had brought its own furniture.

## What gnomes say

"There is a table up at the overlook with every hat in the yard on it. I put mine there. I did not know whose it would sit by."

"Nobody sleeps at the overlook. Everybody sleeps at the overlook."

## The truth

The camp is centred at (246, 82), on the rise north-west of the overlook safehouse (stage centre (276, 44), radius 18), with a ring of radius 9 m and seven doors facing the middle, one per faction: Carburettors (orange tent, a tow tube for a doormat), Underspanners (grey-blue, two crooked crates), Unmowed (green, a log bench and a lantern), Burrowfolk (brown, a lantern each side of the flap), Spade Guard (olive, an empty camp stool and a leaning flag: someone is on watch and has gone for tea), the Flock (pink, a pink flamingo at the flap as a doorbell) and Granny's red-check pie table. In the middle stands the long crate-table with four mismatched plates and one pie, a slice already gone; behind it the hat-rail (six caps: a mower cap, a pointed red hat, a miner's helmet, a ranger hat, a racing helmet, a straw hat); beside it the beacon, unlit; a campfire to the south-west; and the coalition flag on the rise. There is always a gnome asleep on a crate, two arguing over a map (orange against grey-blue) and one feeding the fire. At New Puddle Marina the Bathtub Queen lies moored, flying the crew's colours.

World change W16 (`worldChanges/rows/coalitionCamp.ts`), shown after `m64-all-hats`; the final part of that quest is played here, with Tansy counting the hats out loud.
