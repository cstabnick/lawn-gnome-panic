The coalition's patchwork flag: squares stitched from every faction's cap colour on a pole with a stone base, which the coalition chose "so the flag could never be carried off by anyone in a hurry". It is one flag, flown once at the overlook camp.

## What gnomes say

"It is the colours of every cap in the yard, sewn badly by somebody who cared."

"You cannot run off with it. The base is stone. That is the whole point of the base."

## The truth

The squares are orange (Carburettors), grey-blue (Underspanners), green (Unmowed), brown (Burrowfolk), olive (Spade Guard), pink (the Flock, with the Lady's permission) and red check (Granny). The Flock's square was sewn separately and hung a little to one side. W15 flies the same colours over Fort Sandpit's gate and as a small pennant on Pebblewick's water tower, once the Guard and the reeve have come over.

## In the games

Prefab `coalition-flag` (about 1.25 m tall; scale 2 at the camp). Stood on the rise at the camp (246, 96) (W16); also over Fort Sandpit's gate arch and as a pennant on Pebblewick's water tower (W15).
