The alliance of everyone Vesper Thorne's Even Cut would level: the Carburettors, the Underspanners, the Unmowed, the Burrowfolk, the Spade Guard, the Flock of the One Leg and Granny Gnertrude's pie table, with the Mulch Club's crews and money behind them. They do not agree on anything except that the Grand Combine must not cross the meridian. Tansy calls it "All hats", and the name stuck.

## What gnomes say

"They have a flag made of everybody's cap. It is the ugliest flag in the yard and it is not for sale."

"Every one of them has a reason to hate the others. They sat at one table anyway. Somebody brought pie."

## The truth

The coalition has no charter and no name on paper. Tansy Clayfoot says "All hats" when the last cap goes on the rail, and the camp, the quest and the achievement ("All Hats at One Table") all take it from her. It is the one thing the HOA's charter-writers could not foresee: that the outcasts, who had every reason to stay separate, would be asked and would come. Its members and the colour each carries in the flag and the tents:

- **The Carburettors** (orange): Lugnut Lulu's engines, the convoy from the Rutbottom Dirt Bowl (By Road).
- **The Underspanners** (grey-blue): Hogweed Sprocket and the Updraft's crew; the air side (By Air).
- **The Unmowed** (green): Elder Bracken's folk from Mossy Wood, the ones who arrive by root.
- **The Burrowfolk** (brown): Old Mother Tumblesoil and her diggers.
- **The Spade Guard** (olive): Commander Diggs's sentries, now allies and "the coalition's military arm".
- **The Flock** (pink, for the Lady only): the camp's one pink tent, and a flamingo at its flap.
- **Granny Gnertrude** (red check): her pie table, because she "brought pie".
- **By Water**: Captain Samphire Bilge's Bathtub Queen and Brenda's New Puddle.

The Mulch Club's crews and money (Four Taps, `m63-four-taps`), Hedgeworth's greenkeepers, Juniper Vance and the counter-order and the Underspanners' beacon on the Bobble are the coalition's hands; Sorrel's radio is its voice.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (act IV): formed in `m64-all-hats` (By Water, By Air, By Road, By Root) at the Dust Flats overlook, which becomes the coalition camp (W16, `coalition-camp`). It fights the finale (`ch5-even-cut`). Achievements: "All Hats at One Table" (the patchwork coalition cape).
