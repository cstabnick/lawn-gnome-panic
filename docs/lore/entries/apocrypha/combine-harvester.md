Colossal, slow-moving Buck-Iron heavy iron (`maxSpeed: 15`, `mass: 6`) capable of smashing through HOA roadblocks and towing whole rafts or silos.

## Field Notes & Specs

Climbing into a Combine Harvester's cab feels like boarding a moving apartment block. At `mass: 6`, nothing on Turfton's roads wins a head-on collision against it—Hat Watch cruisers bounce off its front reel like acorns. It turns like a barge, guzzles Black Dew, and shakes windows three blocks away.

## What gnomes say
