The glaze doctor at the Pebblewick glaze post: a dusty white coat, a pot of glaze on every shelf, and a steady hand.

## What gnomes say

"Hairline cracks, mostly. The dry wind does it."

## The truth

Doc Comfrey trained at the Turfton Glaze Clinic and came out to the Flats because he likes a patient that does not complain about the bill.

## In the games

Keeps the glaze post on a Pebblewick side street by the water tower.
