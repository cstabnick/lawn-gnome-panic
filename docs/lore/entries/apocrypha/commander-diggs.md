Olive-capped hardliner commanding Fort Sandpit's Spade Guard, who has spent fifteen sun-baked years guarding a flying mower nobody is legally cleared to start.

## Journal: Fort Sandpit Duty Log (Day 5,478)

> *05:00 — Perimeter check complete. Sandbox wall intact. Two scorpions evicted from the eastern bucket.*  
> *12:00 — Pad 1 inspection: The Whirligig remains chained. Privates asked again if they can hover it to grab lunch from Turfton. Denied. Verticality is a privilege, not a delivery service.*  
> *18:00 — HOA fuel invoice arrived. Double last month's rate. If "The Brass" doesn't stop letting civilians in beige vests audit my diesel drums, I'm going to start firing warning shots at clipboards.*

Diggs lives by the manual, sleeps with a tactical trowel bandolier, and secretly hates the retired colonels ("The Brass") who haven't left Turfton's air-conditioned bunkers in a decade.

## What gnomes say

"Diggs won't blink if you shoot at his gate, but mention an HOA fuel audit and his left eye twitches for an hour."

## The truth
Diggs commands the Spade Guard at Fort Sandpit and later becomes the coalition's military arm. He wants his chopper guarded and his Guard respected, and fears being the guard nobody guards. He lives in a tin bungalow in Pebblewick (his neighbour and friend is Reeve Yarrow) and works at the fort. Secret: why he stayed fifteen years (told at #61: the medal, the rosters). Voice: clipped military, numbers, "effective immediately"; no contractions, no adjectives ("Effective immediately: stand down. Fifteen years, and nobody asked.").

Arc: absent in acts I-III; runs the fort at the start of act IV, hears Vesper's phone order, turns ("Nobody fires the Guard", #59), salutes the mole at #60 and lets the player into his bungalow at #61; joins the coalition at #64. Tansy outranks him.
