The ice-cream seller of Dunelow: pink hat, glasses, a blue shirt, and a menu of ninety-nine flavours of which ninety-eight are vanilla.

## What gnomes say

"Ninety-nine flavours. Ninety-eight of them are vanilla."

## The truth

The ninety-ninth is not on the menu. He says it is "for regulars".
