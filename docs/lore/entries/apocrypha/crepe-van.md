A ribbed grey food van with a serving hatch; it smells of batter for ever.

## Field Notes

The Crepe Van turns up in the European yard's traffic, lumbering along at a top speed of 19 units an hour. Its hatch is always open and its queue is always there, even when the van is moving. The queue is for nothing in particular. It is just a good queue.

Burl's Forecourt has parked one by the gate for years, and nobody has ever seen it serve a crepe. The forecourt staff say the batter is still there, somewhere under the hatch, and that it will be ready as soon as the strike ends.

## What gnomes say

"No crepes today. Crepes tomorrow. Tomorrow is a holiday, so, the day after."

## Loose threads

Whether the batter is older than the van. Nobody has been allowed to lift the lid of the batter tub since the paperwork was filed in triplicate.
