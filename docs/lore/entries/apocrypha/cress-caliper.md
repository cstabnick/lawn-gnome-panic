The host of 104.1 HOA Compliance Beats: smooth jazz, sponsored by the HOA and read between the bylaws by a woman who used
to write those bylaws into fines.

## What gnomes say

"Section 12-C prohibits unlicensed lawn flamingos, unauthorised puddles and smiling without a municipal permit." Cress
reads it the way other presenters read the weather, and then she says "I wrote that section".

## History

Cress Caliper was a compliance clerk for the HOA for eleven years, in the office under the Thorne tower, issuing fines
for verges, flamingos and posture. Her supervisor was Reginald "Quarter-Inch" Vance, who measured the office carpet at
three in the morning. She was good at the job. She was also very good at the forms, which she designed, and which
still have a box for "delighted" that is crossed out with a single line.

She was fired after a live link to Compliance Beats in which she read out a fine for smiling, and then smiled, on air,
for the full length of a jazz standard. The HOA took the station as a penance. The station took Cress as a host, on
the grounds that the HOA could not object to a presenter who was already on its payroll. She is still on the payroll.
She says so on air.

## Quirks

- She wears the beige vest on Thursdays "to see whether it still fits". It does not.
- She reads the Pebble Lane grass heights every hour, and never mentions the other lanes.
- Before each bulletin she clicks a brass caliper against the desk. Nobody has asked her why.

## In the games

- Compliance Beats' banter, calls and adverts (`radio/content.ts`): the fines, the Clerk's number, the Chirp & Crumb
  vans, Juniper Vance's four hundred pages on dandelions (a filing the station has also read in full).
