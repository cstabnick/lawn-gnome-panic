Rusty fixed-wing biplane parked at the Desert Airfield (`maxSpeed: 55`, `stallSpeed: 22`, `ceiling: 300`), used for Dust Flats drops and daring bailouts.

## Field Notes & Specs

Painted in faded agricultural stripes and held together with baling wire, the Crop Duster is the fastest vehicle in the entire 1200×1200 yard. Pilots must build airspeed past `22` down the 170-unit runway before pulling up—a fact Wind-Sock Wally gleefully logs from his lawn chair.

## What gnomes say

"Great for crossing four biomes in twenty seconds; terrifying when you realize the landing gear squeaks."
