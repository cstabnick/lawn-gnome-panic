A snack delivery service. Its courier drops snacks and party tat straight into a gnome's bag, for a price. Nobody has ever seen the courier.

## Story: Nobody Saw the Drop

A bag on the seat, a receipt in the bag, and a bag of crisps that was not there a minute ago. Crumbs & Co. has never once been caught leaving a parcel anywhere but where it was paid for. Rival [Chirp & Crumb](chirp-and-crumb.md) says the courier is a crow. The crow has not commented.

## What gnomes say

"Crumbs & Co. dropped a party hat in my bag. I didn't order a party hat."

"Don't tip the courier. Nobody knows where to leave it."
