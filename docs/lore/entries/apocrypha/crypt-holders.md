A closed, rich faction that holds Mausoleum Hill: the crypts, the Great Crypt and the richest loot in the world, behind iron doors.

## What they are

Founded by Thistle Stonewort, who broke into the first crypt to hide when the moss came and never came out again. They
hold Crypt Row and the Great Crypt, a domed mausoleum with stained glass, and call the rest of the Hill their
own. Their hat is slate (`#4b4f6b`).

## How they live

- **Norms:** a tithe on everything taken from the Hill; strangers are turned away at the iron doors; the bitten are cast
  out; the founder's line leads, and their word is the law of the Hill.
- **Violence:** raiders who come up the Hill are met with force, and the Holders will not parley.
- **Relics:** they keep the T4 relics the Hill yields, and sell none of them.

## What they want

Walls that hold, and a hoard that never gets smaller. They would rather be a fortress than a market.

## Loose threads

Who Thistle Stonewort was before the crypt, and what is actually in the sealed crypts that nobody has opened.
