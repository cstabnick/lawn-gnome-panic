A house in the yard, entered from the yard, that holds cursed loot and a cultist. It is not the Tall House of the Paragnormal setting.

## In the games

- It is entered from the yard, at a spot labelled Enter Cursed House.
- Its loot includes the 666-Watt Cursed Toaster: "Only toasts bagels into pentagrams."
- Its enemies are giant spiders and an Evil Shadow Gnome Cultist.
- Its sermon: "THE HOUSE STINKS OF SIN. LOOT BOTH!"
