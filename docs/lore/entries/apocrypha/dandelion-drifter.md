A hot-air balloon whose envelope is a huge dandelion clock: a white ball of seed tufts over a wicker basket, with a burner on four struts. Moored at the Desert Airfield, and a Tallgrass Commons favourite.

## Field Notes & Specs

The Drifter is slow. Its burner lifts it gently and it cannot go far in a hurry, but it can hover a long time, and with the burner off it sinks softly rather than falling, the way a seed does. Tallgrass Commons gnomes take it up on calm mornings to look at the Dust Flats from above. It has never been known to land in the wrong place, which is more than can be said for the Crop Duster or a copter.

## What gnomes say

"Go up when the wind is quiet, come down wherever you like. That's the whole trick."

## Loose threads

Whether the seed tufts on the envelope are replaced each spring, and by whom.
