Electric-yellow extreme citrus-pollen soda sponsoring illegal Zero-Turn street races and Frostbite Fells' Aether-Ramp jumps.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"110% Daily Value Of Pollen & Bad Decisions."* While Acorn-Cola courts the Hat Watch, Dandelion Fizz embraces pure chaos—plastering neon yellow banners over frozen birdbath derbies and paying Vinnie "Turbo-Sod" Delacroix to jump flaming garden hoses on television.

## Consumer Reports

Guaranteed to make a gnome vibrate slightly faster than Midge on espresso, though side effects include yellow-stained beards and reckless cornering.
