Tactical seed-head grenade that detonates into a blinding white fluff-cloud, sending nearby Hat Watch officers into uncontrollable sneezing fits.

## Field Notes & Stories

Invented by Pure-Loam Co-op youths and perfected by Shelldon "No-Trail" Mucilage for alley getaways. One toss coats a cruiser windshield in stubborn white pappus fluff, dropping Watch aggro instantly while leaving the street smelling like May sunshine.
