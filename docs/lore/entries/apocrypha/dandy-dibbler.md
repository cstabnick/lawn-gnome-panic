Gold-hatted, bespectacled fugitive hiding in Mossy Wood (`-320, 40`) and the first target on Don Compost's First Contract.

## Journal: Moss-Stained Pocket Diary

> *Thought the pine needles would hide the glare off my gold hat. Turns out gold reflects moonlight like a lighthouse. Skipped town with three crates of Don Compost's best acorn-cigars and a pair of prescription spectacles. If I stay quiet behind this toadstool, nobody from Turfton will ever find me.*

Dandy Dibbler fancies himself a gentleman rogue, polishing his brass buttons with fern fronds while waiting for the heat to die down. Unfortunately, Don Compost's wanted posters have very clear coordinates.

## What gnomes say

"Never steal cigars from a silent mob boss and then wear a shiny yellow hat in a dark forest."
