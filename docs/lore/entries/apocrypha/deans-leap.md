A wedge ramp on the north rim of the Anthill's summit, aimed at the campus. Anything driven off it flies a good way before it lands.

## What gnomes say

"Dean's Leap. Nobody has seen the Dean leap. Plenty have seen his car."
"It points at the university. Make of that what you like."

## The truth

A ramp cut for a stunt nobody remembers. The Dean claims it was a wheelchair ramp for a very ambitious professor.

## In the games

Stunt ramp `deans-leap` on the north rim of the Anthill's summit, 8 m wide and about 4.5 m high; it throws a car north toward the campus over the mound's north slope. Landing it earns the achievement "Not Termites". It is built of old planks over packed earth, with a faded wheelchair symbol painted on the slope and a brass plate by the entry: "DONATED BY THE CLASS OF ——" (R-237).
