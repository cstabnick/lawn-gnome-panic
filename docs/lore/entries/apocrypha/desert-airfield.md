A 170-unit alkali airstrip in the Dust Flats (`x: 480, z: -110`) where the Spade Guard parks rusty Crop Dusters under Wind-Sock Wally's watchful binoculars.

## Field Notes & Tales

Shortly after fortifying Fort Sandpit, the Spade Guard dragged two battered Crop Dusters onto the flattest alkali pan in the east and painted white stripes down the dirt. Security is notoriously lax on the far apron—mostly because anyone foolish enough to steal a fixed-wing plane has to clear the dunes at `stallSpeed: 22` without clipping a bucket.

## What gnomes say

"The runway's plenty long enough, provided you don't panic and pull up at fifteen."

## The truth

The airfield's apron is at about (450, -96) (stage `airfield-apron`, radius 40), with the runway running west to east along z -110 from x 420 to 560, joined to the east highway by an airfield spur (via (374, 70), (450, 70) and (450, -70)). Wind-Sock Wally's caravan stands on the apron. After `ch4-runway-flares` (R-234) Wally signs Hangar Two over to the crew and the Crop Duster becomes the crew's plane.

## In the games

Act IV: the apron is the first stop of `m54-the-heist-board` (fetch Wally from his caravan) and `ch4-runway-flares` (clear two Spade Guard patrols and steal the Crop Duster); `m58-the-reeves-barn` collects the spray-pump impeller from Wally's hangar here; `m64-all-hats-p2` ("By Air") makes it the coalition's air base, with one Dewdrop on the apron.

## Everyday

- **Shops:** Wally's Wing Lot beside the charter desk (aircraft and hangar space), the charter desk itself, and a tin pail of roasted sunflower seeds by Wally's chair, "for customers, one each".
- **Notices:** painted on the hangar door: "Stall speed 22. Do not embarrass yourself." On Wally's clipboard: the day's wind, and the day's number of landings, "which is a number I prefer".
- **Habits:** at dawn Wally stands at the runway end and waits for the wind to say something. He logs every take-off, official or not. The windsock is checked twice, "once for the wind, once for the sock".
- **Sayings:** "Stall speed's twenty-two, kid." "Wind's good. That is all the news."
- **Small history:** the first stripes were painted by hand, with a mop; the second stripe leans, and the pilots call it "the Dune".
