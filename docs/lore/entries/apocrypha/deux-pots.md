A corrugated tin car with a roll-back canvas roof that leans in corners as if listening to gossip.

## Field Notes

The Deux-Pots turns up in the European yard's traffic, rattling along at a top speed of 20 units an hour. It was built to cross a ploughed field with a basket of eggs in the back and not break one. It has done this once, on paper, and the eggs were a drawing.

Burl's Forecourt sells them as "first cars", and the forecourt staff say the roof is the best feature. Lean into a corner and the canvas tilts after you, quietly, as if it has heard something worth hearing.

## What gnomes say

"Carries eggs, carries gossip, carries nothing else. Very reliable at the first two."

## Loose threads

Who the eggs were for, and whether the field was ever ploughed. The original drawing shows a basket and a tiny question mark where the car should be.

Grand Tour is the achievement for driving all five European rides. Its reward is the Tour Beret.
