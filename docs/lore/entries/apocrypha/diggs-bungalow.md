Commander Diggs' home: a tin Nissen hut at the south edge of Pebblewick, walk-in; the door is at (615, 206.5), just past the dry plots. The sign reads "FINDERS KEEPERS. LOSERS: HOA."

## What gnomes say

"He guards a mower all day and a tin hut all night."

"The hut is tin, the lock is brass and the sign is the law."

## In the games

Built by E10 (batch 3): Diggs' home, walk-in. Plate on the map once the player has stood at the door (R-180).

## The truth

Home in the homes table is "a tin bungalow in Pebblewick" (R-180; the walk-in interior is `diggs-bungalow`, plate DIGGS). The outline's "tin bungalow in Pebblewick" and the hut's position at the south edge are the same place: the town's last door before the open Flats. In act IV (`m61-diggs-medal`) it is where Diggs asks the player to come, "not the fort": fifteen years of guard rosters pinned to the wall and one empty nail, where he hangs the medal the player takes back from the HOA lost-property shed at the fort gate, and says why he stayed. Diggs sits down for the first time in the scene.
