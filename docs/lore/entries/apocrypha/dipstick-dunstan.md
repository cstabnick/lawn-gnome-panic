The second of the three Carburettors who stayed with Lulu: a yellow-hatted gnome in round spectacles who apologises for the mud in advance and is usually responsible for it.

## What gnomes say

"Dunstan! Sorry about the mud, in advance."

"Dunstan checks the oil. Dunstan does not check the sky."

## The truth

He is the cheerful one, a mechanic with a long dipstick of a name (nobody remembers his surname, if he had one). He apologises first so that nobody has to be cross later. He stands by the Ring at the finale of the Capwood Run and waves at the warden, who writes it down. Not to be confused with Dunstan Cairn, the walker on the Hatbrim summit, or with anyone else named Dunstan.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto, quest 40 `m40-the-capwood-run`: a wave in the intro scene, and part of the three-gnome line at the Ring in the outro. Yellow hat (0xe8c33a), navy shirt, brown beard, glasses.
