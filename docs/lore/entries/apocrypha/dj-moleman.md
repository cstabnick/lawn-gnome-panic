The host of 108.0 Subsoil Radio: a mole who plays one long record, from a burrow under Turfton, through speakers he has
dug into the ground. He is a mole in no costume (he wears a hat, which is for the antenna). He is the only presenter in
town who thinks the weather is somebody else's job.

## What gnomes say

"The floor you are standing on is the speaker." Drivers say a good kick drum in a mower seat is "Moleman, not the
engine". The Carburettors say he knows every one of them by the thump of their engine, and that he is right.

## History

Moleman has been digging under Turfton for longer than the HOA has been fining it, and nobody can say how long that is,
because nobody has been down. He learned the ground the way every mole does: by feel. Moles feel a footstep before a
gnome hears it, so he knew about the Hat Watch's patrols, the Carburettors' midnight runs and the Grand Combine's
engine before the radio did. He started to play records on the quiet because the town was so loud above him, and
somebody should hold a steady low note under all of it.

He holds that a tunnel has no gaps in it, so his set does not stop between tunes. It changes colour: a deep, slow
chord-and-echo room (the dub), a hard rolling floor of drums (the house), a wire of squealing acid. He says the colours
are the same tunnel at three depths. Each one hands over to the next the way a tunnel opens into a room.

## Quirks

- He tunes to the low hum of the Dew-Wells under Cloverfield, which he says is the only thing in Turfton that has never
  been fined.
- He will not say "mower" and "kick drum" in the same breath. He says "seat".
- He keeps a hat on the antenna and leaves the transmitter exactly where the Hat Watch will not look: under Cress
  Caliper's own tower. They keep finding hers.
- Cress Caliper has framed a card he sent her, and will not say why.

## In the games
