The mud-pie seller at the Rutbottom Dirt Bowl: a pink-hatted, freckled gnome who sells warm, brown pies and says they are exactly what you think.

## What gnomes say

"Mud pies! Warm, brown and exactly what you think!"

## The truth

They are mud, baked in the sun at the stand, and entirely ornamental; nobody has been seen eating one. Dolly sells about forty a day, mostly as ornaments, and says the trade is "all in the glaze".

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E07): the pie stand at about (1214, -524), "idle-jaunty" idle.
