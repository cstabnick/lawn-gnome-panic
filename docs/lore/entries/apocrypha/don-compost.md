Turfton's silent underworld boss, ruling his syndicate from a cream suit (white pinstripe on screen) and an armchair dais inside The Mulch Club without ever uttering a spoken word.

## Story: Three Taps on the Armrest

Nobody in Turfton has heard Don Compost speak since the Great Sprinkler Scare. In the back room of The Mulch Club, he conducts multi-biome racketeering entirely through eyebrow raises, red-inked wanted posters, and slow, deliberate taps on his chair's armrest. One tap means *fetch a mower*. Two taps mean *bring acorns*. Three taps mean *someone's hat needs cracking*.

Junior enforcers once hired a lip-reader from Cloverfield Farms to figure out if the Don was actually mute or just committed to the bit. The lip-reader stared for ten minutes, collected his fee, and quietly moved to Frostbite Fells the same night.

## What gnomes say

"He doesn't need a speech. When Don Compost slides a poster across the felt, you already know how much clay dust it's going to cost."

## The truth
The Don wants his Club's rackets left alone and the city crooked on his terms, and fears a beige Turfton with no use for a Don. He lives in rooms above the Mulch Club and works from the armchair in its back room (R-025: cream suit, black hat, two bodyguards; the bible adds a white pinstripe, a red hat band, a grey beard combed flat and an unlit cigar). He never speaks (R-025 stands; why is a loose thread). His taps: one a mower, two acorns, three crack a hat, and four, which nobody has seen, the war.

Arc: the first contract (#3), then the cigar boats at war with the HOA (#11); a presence at the edge in act II; his crews keep Turfton's back alleys alive while the crew is away (III); in act IV he taps four times (#63) and lends crews and money in exchange for the Mayor's signature. Big Lou is his hands and translates, nervously; the Mayor owes him money; Brenda's toll is tolerated.
