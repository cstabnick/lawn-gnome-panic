A spectator at the hairpin of the Capwood Run: green hat, grey beard, who knows that everybody brakes too early and that this is the best bit.

## What gnomes say

"Wait for the hairpin. Everyone brakes too early. It is the best bit."

## The truth

She has watched the hairpin from the same patch of verge for years, and has the only complete record of who braked early; she shares it with nobody.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E08): beside the hairpin on the Capwood Run, "cross-arms" idle.
