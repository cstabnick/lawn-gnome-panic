A picket-fenced pen between Thorne Tower and the z = 0 road, where the Espresso-Fed Slug syndicate takes its bets. The slugs are fed espresso grounds, and the odds are chalked on the fence.

## What gnomes say
*"Two to one on the fat one. Don't ask why it's fat."* (the bookie at the gate)

## In the games
Stage `snail-paddock` (night). Shelldon haunts the paddock, and the bets are taken on the z = 0 road side.
