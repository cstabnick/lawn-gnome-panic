The civic core of Turfton, around the x = 0 and z = 0 roads: City Hall at the north end of the main road, Thorne Tower over the middle, the Glaze Clinic and the Central Bus Stop on the way out. Everything that is official in Turfton happens here, at a walk.

## Character

Clipped and civic. Hedges are cut square, the benches are painted the council's cream, and every corner has a bin with a sticker telling you which one to use. Downtown is where gnomes go to be seen doing the right thing, and where they are seen doing the other thing by the people who were looking for them.

## Landmarks

- City Hall steps, (0, -72): the council's front door, with the Zoning Board's bench on the top step.
- Thorne Tower, (30, -30): 86 m, the tallest stacked-pot tower in Turfton, with Vesper's Penthouse Pot on top.
- The Mulch Club, (-30, -30): Don Compost's private club, behind a door with no handle.
- Glaze Clinic Hospital, (30, 30): where cracked gnomes are glued, glazed and sent home.
- Central Bus Stop, (-6, 14): the end of the Snail-Coach line and the taxi rank.
- Kerbside Exchange, (-9, 45): Dot Pennywort's board and folding desk, south of the Bus Stop.
- Gnoma's food truck, (13, 44): parked by the Exchange at lunch, and most nights after.
- The slug paddock, (24, -14): where the council keeps the slugs it has not yet decided about.
- The Watch House, on the x = 0 road north of City Hall: lore only, see `watch-house`.
- Gnorwin Clayfoot Park: the small green square at the Downtown edge, with a statue that everybody photographs and nobody can name the hat of.

## Factions

- City Hall: the council, the zoning board and the street-sweepers.
- The Hat Watch: City Hall's watch, working from the Watch House.
- Don Compost's Mulch Club: the club's members are never in the same place twice.
- Thorne Lawn & Order: the corner pots and the tower, run from the penthouse.

## What gnomes say

"Downtown is where the grid starts, so that's where you get told off first."

"Every fine in Turfton is stamped at City Hall, even the ones the HOA wrote."

## Local slang

- "on the grid": working a legal job, with a badge or a wage to show for it.
- "a two-coat": a politician who repaints after a scandal. The paint is still wet.
- "kerb-sitting": waiting at the Exchange for work to be pinned up.

## In the games

Downtown is the start of most city play. The Kerbside Exchange board and Dot's desk sit on its south side, the Central Bus Stop is where the taxi and the Snail-Coach drop you, and the Hat Watch patrols its streets. Its district sign is `downtown` in `src/districts.ts`; the border runs to |x| and |z| of 45, and out to 95 wherever another district does not take the block.

## Loose threads

Why the Watch House is on the x = 0 road and not in the building the Hat Watch was founded in. The answer is in a drawer at City Hall, and nobody has opened it.
