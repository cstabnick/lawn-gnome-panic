The professor of the Physics of Gnomes at Rootwell: a grey-bearded lecturer in a slate hat who gives the same lecture, on repeat, on why we fall over and how to do it better. He asks the room to sit, because sitting is the first step.

## What gnomes say

"The physics of gnomes: why we fall over, and how to do it better. Please sit. Sitting is the first step."

"He recorded it once. Now the recording gives the lecture and he sits in the back row."

## The truth

The lecture has a chalk board behind the lectern that nobody has ever seen wiped. He has been teaching the course for longer than the lecture hall has had a roof; the Physics of Gnomes is the one course nobody fails, because everybody has done the practical. The recording of him on repeat beside the back-row bench is, he says, "for the students who cannot attend", which is every student.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the lecture hall interior on the Rootwell campus (E13). He stands at the lectern in slate hat (0x37474f), brown shirt (0x8d6e63) and grey beard; walking up to the lectern spot ("Listen to the lecture") plays his next line. A sign on the back wall reads "LECTURE RECORDING: THE PROFESSOR, ON REPEAT".
