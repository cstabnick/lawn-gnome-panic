A lecturer on the Rootwell campus: purple hat, cream shirt, glasses, a pale beard, tapping one foot outside the lecture hall and doubting the Dean's grant out loud.

## What gnomes say

"The Dean says the grant is a gift. Gifts do not usually come with a survey."

## The truth

A lecturer, not the radio host: Dr Cress is not Cress Caliper of 104.1 Compliance Beats, and has been asked about it more often than is polite. Her doubt about the grant is the first on campus; it is the Dean's ledger of late rent to the ants that she has actually read.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E13): the campus life layer, a lecturer standing at (1234, -163) tapping her foot, facing west toward the lecture hall; walking near her shows her bubble.
