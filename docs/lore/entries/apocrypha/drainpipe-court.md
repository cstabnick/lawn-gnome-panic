The public basketball court in Downtown Turfton. The hoop is bolted to a drainpipe, the lines are chalk, and the bench has been there longer than the court.

## What gnomes say

"The ball goes through the hoop, then down the drainpipe. Bring a spare."

"Nobody owns the court. Swish Trowelly is the nearest thing."

## In the games

The court stands at (-90, 30), inside Founders' Green. Two drainpipe hoops (no net), a chalk scoreboard on its easel by the south sideline, a ball cage at the east end, and Old Burdock's bench on the north side. Pickup games are 2v2 to 11 with the regulars: Cardoon and Lintel against the player and Pansy (or Burdock keeps the score). A shootaround at the ball cage. Side quest `sq-court-regulars`. Story quest #14 (`m14-drainpipe-court`): Pip owes Cardoon Trowelly his toolbox; a jam-game to 7, then Pip on the bench says why he left Fescue.

## Everyday

- Notices: the chalk scoreboard ("I.O.U. ONE TOOLBOX - P." is written at its east end, untouched since Pip wrote it); a bench plaque reading only "Burdock was here first"; the ball cage's tally of balls lost down the drainpipe this season.
- Habit: pickup games run to 11, win by one, and the losers carry the ball cage's key to the next game.
- Habit: whoever sinks the last jam shot shouts "That's my boy!" at nobody in particular, and Burdock says "Mind the drainpipe."
- Saying: "Down the pipe is a point if you can say who it went to." / "Bring a spare, the drainpipe doesn't give them back."
- Small history: the hoop was bolted up in one afternoon by someone who has not been identified, using a spanner that was never returned to the toolshed it came from.
