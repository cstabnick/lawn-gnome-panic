A gang of squirrels who den in Cloverfield's hedgerows and an old silo, and raid gnomes for Acorns: they pick pockets, pelt you with acorns from cover and, now and then, mob a parked mower and drive off with it.

## What gnomes say

"The Dreys don't bluff. They want your Acorns, and they'll take the mower to carry them home."
"Shoot at them and they scatter. Wait a minute and they're back, and they've told their cousins."

## The truth

They are not evil, whatever the farmers say: they are filling the winter stores, and a gnome's pocket is the
fullest store in Cloverfield. A stolen mower is the best acorn cart they have ever had.

## In the games

Squirrels around their dens in Cloverfield Farms. They steal Acorns when close, throw acorns that chip the player's
clay, and sometimes steal a parked mower. Shooting them knocks them over in a puff of fur; they run off and regroup.
Turfton FM reports their raids.

The three dens are two hedgerows at (-260, 520) and (260, 640), and an old silo at (420, 820).
