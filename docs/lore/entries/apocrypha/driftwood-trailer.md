A walnut dinghy on a cart in the southern fields. Driftwood waited beside it for six months, sure that the lawn-ocean would come to him if he kept the boat ready.

## What gnomes say
*"The tide's coming in. I can feel it in my hat."* (Driftwood, to the empty field)

## In the games
Stage `driftwood-trailer`. Driftwood haunts the trailer, and the walnut dinghy is parked on its cart.
