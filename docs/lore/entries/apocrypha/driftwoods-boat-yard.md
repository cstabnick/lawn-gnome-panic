A boat yard on the Loch Moss shore, near the dock that [Driftwood](driftwood-castaway.md) keeps. It sells dinghies to steamers.

## Story: Built for the Big Puddle

The walnut dinghies are sanded smooth, painted in sea-foam, and each one is named after a puddle its builder has never seen. Driftwood says the yard is a waste of good walnut. He still signs every sale.

## What gnomes say

"Buy a dinghy at Driftwood's yard and you'll know the water before the water knows you."

## Loose threads

Whether any of the dinghies has ever been to the Big Puddle.
