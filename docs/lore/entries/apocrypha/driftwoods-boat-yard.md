A boat yard on the Loch Moss shore, near the dock that [Driftwood](driftwood-castaway.md) keeps. It sells dinghies to steamers.

## Story: Built for the Big Puddle

The walnut dinghies are sanded smooth, painted in sea-foam, and each one is named after a puddle its builder has never seen. Driftwood says the yard is a waste of good walnut. He still signs every sale.

## What gnomes say

"Buy a dinghy at Driftwood's yard and you'll know the water before the water knows you."

## Everyday

- Prices on the slipway board: walnut dinghy 350, gravy boat 1,100, clog cruiser 2,000, watering-can steamer 3,000. "Sea trials free. The sea is not."
- Notices: "NO LAUNCHING WITHOUT A NAME. NO NAME WITHOUT A PUDDLE." A bell on the shed to call Driftwood from the water.
- House rule: "Every boat goes out once with me first. I keep the stories."
- Habit: each new boat is named after a puddle the builder has not seen, written in chalk on the stern and left to wear off.
- Saying: "Walnut-hearted" (small, brave, a bit hollow).

## Loose threads

Whether any of the dinghies has ever been to the Big Puddle.
