A very large, very gentle grey shark of the far Puddle Sea, with a pointed snout, a tall dorsal fin, a crescent tail and a pale belly. It is the proof Driftwood has been waiting for, and it is mostly interested in cheese.

## What gnomes say

"Driftwood was right. Driftwood is always right, but he was right about the shark, and he has been insufferable about it ever since."
"It does not bite. It nibbles. There is a difference, and you will feel it, and then you will be late for supper."

## The truth

Driftwood has said for six months that the canal frogs are lawn sharks in disguise, and that the open-ocean lawn-boats are ridden by real sharks. The Puddle Sea shark is real, and it proves him right, but it is not what he imagined. It is a shark the way a sofa is a boat: large, grey and slow, with a smile line that never shows teeth. It is never hurt (R-146). A gnome who lands it writes its catch card, measures it and lets it go, and it sinks back into the Far Puddle, very pleased with the cheese.

## In the games

Only at the Far Puddle hot spot, out in the deep sea. It is found most on Neon Nights, less at Dusk and rarely at Noon, and it goes for moon jig and cheese rind over anything else. It needs the top rod (the Moonline). It is the third mythic fish of the game.
