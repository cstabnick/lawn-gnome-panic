A seaside village in the Saltbucket hinterland, near (-560, 965), on the south band road: one street, a lot of deckchairs and a flat-footed gull population that has read the notices. Its sign says "Twinned with nowhere, by request", and the gulls agree.

## What gnomes say

"Dunelow is where you go to be told, politely, that the sea is not for you."

"Gulls are not to be fed. The gulls are aware of this, and disagree."

## The truth

The hamlet lies within 75 m of (-560, 965), on `south-band` (the road along z about 1000 from x 0 to -950). Its notices: "GULLS ARE NOT TO BE FED. GULLS ARE AWARE OF THIS AND DISAGREE"; an ice cream van on "Tuesdays and whenever it hears you"; a lost flip-flop (left, possibly at sea); a snail coach ("Mon-Sat: sometimes. Sun: see Mon"); deckchairs for hire ("Falling over included"); and a sandcastle contest judged by three gulls (the tallest wins; the bucket stays). Residents: **Old Wrack**, the net mender ("The fish have learned where the holes were"), and **Cornet Brindle**, the ice-cream seller ("Ninety-nine flavours. Ninety-eight of them are vanilla").

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E32): houses, a notice board, a deckchair row and two ambient gnomes (`living/hamletDunelow.ts`). One of the places Off the Beaten Lane counts.

## Everyday

- Notice board (already posted in the village): gulls are not to be fed (the gulls disagree); ice-cream van Tuesdays and whenever it hears you; lost: one flip-flop, left, possibly at sea; the snail coach Mon-Sat "sometimes", Sun "see Mon"; deckchairs 50p, "falling over included"; sandcastle contest judged by three gulls, the tallest wins, the bucket stays.
- Cornet Brindle's menu: ninety-nine flavours, the first of which is vanilla, the second also vanilla, and so on to the ninety-eighth. Cone 2 Acorns, "double" 3, a flake 1, "extra vanilla" free.
- Habit: everybody on the street stops talking when the van's tune starts, and resumes when it fades, "out of respect".
- Local drink: warm lemonade in a deckchair-stripe mug.
- Saying: "Dunelow-low" (very quiet). / "Twinned with nowhere" for any place that is nobody's business.
- Small history: Old Wrack's net has been mended forty times; the fish keep a count of the patches.
