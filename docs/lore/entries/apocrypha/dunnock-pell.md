The keeper of the Reedwatch Hide on Loch Moss: an old, patient gnome who whispers everything and has never once raised his voice near the water.

## What gnomes say

"Shh. You'll have the heron off. Sit down and wait; it'll come to you."
"The phoenix is real. I've seen it over the Flats at Golden Hour. The heron is probably real."

## The truth

Dunnock has watched the birds of Loch Moss for longer than the Turfton lanes have had names. He keeps the life list because somebody has to, and he pays Acorns for milestones because a gnome who counts birds tends to keep coming back. He is sure about the phoenix and only "probably" sure about the moon heron, and he does not say why.

## In the games

Sitting inside the Reedwatch Hide on the south shore of Loch Moss: white beard, teal hat, round glasses. He sells Pocket Binoculars, Field Glasses and the Reedwatch Scope, pays Acorns for life-list milestones, and talks in a whisper.

## Loose threads

How long he has been on the shore, and what he was watching before the loch was a golf course plan.
