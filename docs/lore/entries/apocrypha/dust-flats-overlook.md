The crew's safehouse on the Dust Flats: an adobe hut, a command tent and a map table on a rise where the east highway bends, looking east across the flats to Fort Sandpit.

## What gnomes say
*"From up here you can watch Fort Sandpit sulk."* (Tansy, on the map table)

## The truth
The Overlook is the rise where the east highway bends, at about (276, 44) (stage `dust-safehouse`, radius 18), looking east across the flats to Fort Sandpit's north wall, main gate and pad. It is a safehouse in the way a lean-to is a house: an adobe hut, a command tent and a plank table with Tansy's plan pinned to it (`the-heist-board`), and a rail on the edge from which the gate, the wall and the pad can be photographed. The way in runs along the east highway from the west; the way out is north up the ring-NE road. It is not the same place as the Fence-Line Overlook, which is a ridge on the perimeter (`fence-line-overlook`).

## In the games
Stage `dust-safehouse`. From chapter 3, Tansy runs the crew from here, and the Whirligig lands beside the hut. Act IV rows 54 (`m54-the-heist-board`), 55 (`ch4-runway-flares`), 57 (`ch4-sandpit-drop`), 59 (`ch4-spade-truce`) and the opening of 70 (`ch5-even-cut`) are given from here. After `m64-all-hats` the rise north-west of the hut becomes the coalition camp (W16, `coalition-camp`): the safehouse stays Tansy's, and the camp fills the slope beside it.

## Everyday

- **Notices:** pinned to the map table: Tansy's plan (gate, wall, pad, photographs numbered one to nine), a rota for the tin kettle ("Pip: Mon, Tue, Wed. Pip: Thu to Sun") and a card that reads "Do not wave at the Fort. The Fort is watching, and it is sulking."
- **Habits:** the crew takes tea at the rail at dusk and rates the Fort's sulk out of ten. The hut door is kept shut for the heat, "and so the hut remains a hut".
- **Sayings:** "From up here you can watch Fort Sandpit sulk."
- **Small history:** the hut was the shed of a surveyor who never came back to check it. The crew has stopped asking who left the kettle.
