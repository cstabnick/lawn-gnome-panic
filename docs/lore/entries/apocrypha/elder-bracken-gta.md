Grumpy Unmowed scholar in Mossy Wood guarding ancient Fescue tablets and fuming that Vesper Thorne uses a Replica Stopping Stone as a doorstop.

## Story: History as a Doorstop

Elder Bracken spent forty years translating cracked earthenware tablets about the Planting and the Rumbling, only to learn that Turfton's wealthiest porcelain CEO props open his patio door with a sacred Stopping Stone replica. Bracken refuses to step onto paved asphalt, so he hires street drivers to liberate Fescue relics from downtown display cases.

When the Grand Combine finally rolls down `x = 0`, Bracken is the one who hands over the real Stopping Stone to jam its blades.
