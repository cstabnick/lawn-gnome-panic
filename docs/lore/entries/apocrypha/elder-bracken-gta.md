Grumpy Unmowed scholar in Mossy Wood guarding ancient Fescue tablets and fuming that Vesper Thorne uses a Replica Stopping Stone as a doorstop.

## Story: History as a Doorstop

Elder Bracken spent forty years translating cracked earthenware tablets about the Planting and the Rumbling, only to learn that Turfton's wealthiest porcelain CEO props open his patio door with a sacred Stopping Stone replica. Bracken refuses to step onto paved asphalt, so he hires street drivers to liberate Fescue relics from downtown display cases.

When the Grand Combine finally rolls down `x = 0`, Bracken lends the real Stopping Stone (#53) and does not give it (R-072: "lends and does not give"); at the finale he watches it go into the Combine's intake (#70).

## What gnomes say

"Bracken complains about noisy engines the whole time he's paying you to hotwire a truck."

## What Bracken says in The Fescue Fugitive

"A replica of something holy is not a doorstop. It is a question. Vesper is answering it wrong." He pays a fugitive six hundred and fifty acorns and an old man's blessing, and asks them to spend the blessing on nothing with an engine. When the replica comes home he sets it between the standing stones in Unmowed Hollow, where it is never mown. Granny Gnertrude bids for it through the gallery phone; Bracken warns that she always bids.

## The truth
Bracken leads the Unmowed in Mossy Wood and keeps the Cracked Tablet and the Stopping Stone. He wants to keep the Unmowed hidden and the old ways pure, and fears another Potsworth leading the Mower to them (Pip is the one who stung him). His root-house stands in Unmowed Hollow. Voice: archaic, heavy, low; accusations as statements; no jokes, no contractions ("You carry engines in your hands. Do not carry them in here."). Sorrel is his voice outside the wood; Clara is the older refugee he shelters.

Arc: absent in act I; the Cracked Tablet, a scene where nobody jokes (#23); lends the Stopping Stone ("the only thing that ever stopped one", #53) in act III; arrives at the overlook with the Unmowed (#64) and watches the stone go in at the finale.
