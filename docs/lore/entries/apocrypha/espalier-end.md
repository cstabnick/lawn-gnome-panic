A pear-and-topiary village in Trellisdown, off the North Wicket road, near (-1080, -420): twelve houses, a very long hedge and a topiary contest whose entries "must not resemble the judges". It posts the same sign as its sister hamlets: "Twinned with nowhere, by request."

## What gnomes say

"Espalier End has more pear trees than gnomes and more opinions than pears."

"Do not lean on the hedge. It is the boundary. It is also, depending on the day, 1.2 m or 1.4 m."

## The truth

The hamlet lies north of the road along z -500 (`wicket-north-approach`, from x -950 to -1400) within a radius of 75 m of (-1080, -420). Its notice board: the topiary contest, "LOST: one pear. Answers to nothing", and the hedge feud between No. 3 and No. 4 (mediation Tuesday). The bus timetable says "Buses: in principle." Residents: **Judge Quince**, who deducts points for symmetry ("Symmetry is for the HOA"), and **Pip Pearsley**, the pear picker ("Nine pears, one basket, and a hill with opinions"). The village was settled by gnomes who left Turfton for quieter lawns and found Pardonlin's gates too near for comfort; it keeps its hedges a little untidy to show they chose to.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E32): houses, a notice board and two ambient gnomes (`living/hamletEspalier.ts`); one of the four places Off the Beaten Lane asks you to visit.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** The pear stand by the pump: pears, "perry (the small kind)", pear chutney and a jar labelled "pear". Open "when picked". The honesty box is a pear.
- **Notices:** The board: "Topiary contest: entries must not resemble the judges." "Lost: one pear. Answers to nothing." "The hedge between No. 3 and No. 4 is 1.2 m. It is also 1.4 m. Mediation Tuesday." The bus stop: "Buses: in principle. Next bus: see previous bus."
- **Sayings and habits:** "A straight hedge is a nervous one." At noon the whole hamlet looks at its own hedge for one minute, "to check it is still there". Nobody trims on Sundays; the hedges are given a rest.
- **Small history:** The Pear Incident: one ladder, one basket, one hill. The pears got to the road before the picker did and are still counted as "in transit".

## Loose threads

Which hedge is the true boundary, and whether either of the houses wants to win.
