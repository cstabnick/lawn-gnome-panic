Five road bridges where Turfton's highways and ring roads cross the Everspring and its branches. Each one is sized for a mower and sits a step above the road, so the water runs under the traffic and the traffic never has to stop for it.

## In the games

Five bridges on the gnome's road map, each named for the water it crosses:

- **Fellsmelt Bridge** at (-64, -395): the upper river under Ring Road North-West, below the Frostbite Fells.
- **Ravine Bridge** at (-482, -30): the Mossy Wood ravine under Highway West.
- **Bracken Bridge** at (-243, 230): the ravine river under Ring Road South-West.
- **Culvert Bridge** at (0, 230): the Southern Drainage Canal under Highway South.
- **Clover Bridge** at (258, 327): the farm river under Ring Road South-East.

Turfton FM reads a traffic report for each of them, and some of those reports are about boats passing underneath.

## Loose threads

Who decided the bridges should be a step above the road, and whether a mower has ever asked for a higher one.
