A shadow gnome that stalks the gnome through the Great Deku Oak and the Cursed House, chanting as it comes.

## In the games

- It spawns in the Great Deku Oak and in the Cursed House.
- It stalks the player, and it makes its chant sound while it stalks.
- A bonk or the airhorn stuns it, the same as spiders and squirrels.
