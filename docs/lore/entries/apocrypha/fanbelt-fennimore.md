The youngest of the three Carburettors who stayed with Lulu: a freckled, beardless gnome in a teal hat who cheers loudest, drives fastest in a straight line and flinches when anyone says "clause".

## What gnomes say

"Fennimore! Youngest! Loudest!"

"He is not fast. He is early."

## The truth

The youngest of the crew by a distance, taken in at fifteen because he could hold a spanner and a tune at the same time. At the Ring he flinches at "Clause two: the Carburettors shall be beige", and honks his horn with the others when Lulu tears the charter in half. He drives the car at the back of the line, where Lulu can see him.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto, quest 40 `m40-the-capwood-run`: introduced with a cheer in the intro scene, honks at the end of the outro, and has a car mark of his own on the Ring line. Teal hat (0x3a7a6a), cream shirt, freckles, no beard.
