Trembling Cloverfield Farms grower who spends half his week hiding in a storm cellar whenever Biscuit's giant shadow crosses the southern fence.

## Story: Cellar Watch

Bramble grows prize clover and oversized acorns along the edge of Biscuit's Nap. He respects Granny Gnertrude, pays his Local 404 dues, and knows better than to leave a shiny tractor parked in the open. His worst nightmare came true when Biscuit dropped a city-bus-sized rubber squeaky hotdog straight into his irrigation ditch—forcing Bramble to hire a Combine driver before the giant dog came looking for it.

## What gnomes say

"Bramble's storm cellar has better canned peaches than Bodega & Sons."

## Side quest: Biscuit's Lost Squeaky Toy

Bramble drives a Combine down the south highway into Granny's yard and says the same thing every time anything big moves: "Don't look up. Just listen." He has been sleeping in the cellar since the hotdog went into his ditch, and he will not show the player the sky while Biscuit's thumps are on the wind. He calls the Central Bus Stop the only address Mabel Kerbstone knows.

## The truth
Bramble works Granny's fields and calls her Boss (R-139); he is her oldest friend and drinking partner, and pays his Local 404 dues. He wants his double acorns in the barn before Biscuit wakes, and fears the sky and what moves across it. Secret: he knows more of Granny's silo than he lets on. Home: the bunkhouse at Granny's barn, though since Biscuit's squeaky hotdog started squeaking he has slept in his cellar (R-070); work: Granny's fields. He owes Rooster for the barometer that saved his crop and distrusts the HOA's foreclosure men after Granny's chipping. Voice: low, almost whispering, flat farm vowels; "Don't look up. Just listen." before any big noise ("Don't look up. ...That's the combine, not the dog." / "Cellar's got peaches.").

Arc: absent in act I; lends the Buck-Iron to the Dew Run and the gauntlet (#16, #18) and carries Granny's farmhouse key when the bailiffs come (#31); absent in act III; stands with the Granny contingent at the overlook camp (#64).
