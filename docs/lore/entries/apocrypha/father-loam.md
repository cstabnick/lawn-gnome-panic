Excommunicated Chapel of Mulch chaplain who preaches that twin-turbo carburetors are a gift of the Kiln and blesses getaway mowers with artisanal compost tea.

## Journal: Oil-Spotted Homilies (Excerpt)

> *They cast me out of the Chapel because I asked a simple question: if the Kiln gave us clay and the Tall Ones gave us spark plugs, is a supercharged Zero-Turn not a prayer in motion? Now I minister from a snowmobile on the Frozen Birdbath Basin, mixing Kiln-Resin for cracked brothers and cheering when the Carburettors drift past the salt-plows.*

Loam wears a compost-stained robe, chrome-rimmed sunglasses, and a 14mm wrench on a rosary chain. He never turns away a chipped racer.

## What gnomes say

"A blessing from Father Loam won't stop a Hat Watch bullet, but it'll definitely make your idle sound meaner."

## The truth
Loam is the excommunicated chaplain of the Chapel of Mulch and keeps the Kiln-Resin shrine at the Frozen Birdbath Basin. He wants his shrine kept and the cold congregation fed, and fears the chapel ever calling him back. His journal gives his own account of why he was cast out (the carburettor question); the Chapel never confirms it, so the true reason stays a loose thread (the bible). Home: a snowmobile shed at the Basin; work: his chapel-van and the shrine. Theo is his closest neighbour; Cedric is the chapel that cast him out; Tansy and Pip are "a congregation of two". Voice: a preacher's cadence, "my child"; kind first, odd second ("Go with grace, my child, and a full tank of it."). He blesses with a mitten.

Arc: absent in acts I-II except a call at #35; shelters the crew at the Basin (#36) and blesses getaways and the drift run (#53); in act IV a voice in the coalition's prayers, absent in person.
