Excommunicated Chapel of Mulch chaplain who preaches that twin-turbo carburetors are a gift of the Kiln and blesses getaway mowers with artisanal compost tea.

## Journal: Oil-Spotted Homilies (Excerpt)

> *They cast me out of the Chapel because I asked a simple question: if the Kiln gave us clay and the Tall Ones gave us spark plugs, is a supercharged Zero-Turn not a prayer in motion? Now I minister from a snowmobile on the Frozen Birdbath Basin, mixing Kiln-Resin for cracked brothers and cheering when the Carburettors drift past the salt-plows.*

Loam wears a compost-stained robe, chrome-rimmed sunglasses, and a 14mm wrench on a rosary chain. He never turns away a chipped racer.

## What gnomes say

"A blessing from Father Loam won't stop a Hat Watch bullet, but it'll definitely make your idle sound meaner."
