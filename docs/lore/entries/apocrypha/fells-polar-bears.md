Huge white bears who lounge in deck chairs on the Fells' sunny slope, in sunglasses, and are the calmest creatures in Turfton: nothing makes them angry.

## What gnomes say

"Shoot at one and it shrugs. Ram one and it shrugs. I have never seen one stand up."
"They're not lazy. They're on holiday. They have been on holiday for as long as anyone remembers."

## In the games

A row of polar bears in deck chairs in Frostbite Fells. A shot or a ram gets a slow shrug, never a fight; they never
chase, never flee and never join a scrap. Turfton FM's weather reports quote them (Epic 8).

Five lounge on the Fells' sun deck, now signposted "BEAR DECK". Four more live on Snowcap Rise (batch 3, E12): one asleep on Pail Pond's ice, one turning slowly in an inner tube, one fishing a hole from a stool, and one on the Woolly Sock Lodge's porch bench with a mug of cocoa. Their call is a long contented yawn. They never speak and nothing upsets them. Standing beside all nine earns "Bear Hug". They are paid in sardines (an empty tin sits by the holes). See R-188.
