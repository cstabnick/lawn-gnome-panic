A windswept dirt ridge running along the outer wooden fence posts at `|x|, |z| = 600`, marking the absolute edge of Turfton's known universe.

## Field Notes & Tales

Fescue's oldest myths call the Fence the sacred boundary between lawns. To Turfton street racers, it is simply the wall you try not to hit at 34 units an hour. Near the southwest post, smugglers once pried a knot-hole wide enough to pass Fescue relics through—and on quiet nights, a muffled *thrum* echoes from the Neighbor's Lawn beyond.

## What gnomes say

"Don't drive past the Fence-Line. There's nothing out there but the Neighbor's Lawn."
