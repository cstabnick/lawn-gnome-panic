A skater at the Gutter Bowls who feeds strays and delivers parcels for Bodega & Sons between runs.

## What gnomes say

"Fennel's got no home but the deck. Asphalt visits every day."

"If a cat's missing, ask a skater."

## In the games

Home: a hammock slung under the Gutter Bowls' deck. Work: the Gutter Bowls, where he keeps the Board Library stocked ("take one, leave one"); delivers for Bodega between runs. His only main quest is 9 (`m09-where-asphalt-sleeps`).
