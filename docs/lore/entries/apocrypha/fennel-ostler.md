A forager in Capwood: olive hat, beardless, who knows which cap is bruised and which has not yet noticed her.

## What gnomes say

"Shh. Not that one, it is bruised. This one has not noticed me yet."

## The truth

She picks only the unbruised, so she is the one forager the warden writes nothing down about; it annoys the warden considerably. She is not related to Fennel Gripwax or to Little Fennel; Fennel is a very common name on the Trellisdown side.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E08): at about (212, -1160), "look-around" idle, with a bubble line.
