The Bean Tower's guide, who waves a small flag and knows every bean by its first name.

## What gnomes say

"Follow the flag! The flag knows the way. The flag has never been up."

"Fifi says there are four hundred steps. Fifi has never counted them. Fifi has the flag."

## The truth

Fifi is genuinely good at the job and enormously proud of it, and the one thing she does not do is go to the top. She has stayed at the foot for so long that she has forgotten whether she wants to. The flag does most of her talking.

## In the games

At the foot of the Bean Tower in the Old Town. She sells admission (one acorn in, two out) and shouts the history.

## Loose threads

Whether Fifi has been to the top. She changes the subject.
