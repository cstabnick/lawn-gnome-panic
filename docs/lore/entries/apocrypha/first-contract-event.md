The protagonist's inaugural syndicate job for Don Compost: tracking down and cracking three gold-hatted rogues across Mossy Wood, Frostbite Fells, and The Dust Flats.

## History & Accounts

Sent to The Mulch Club by Pip to pay down a mounting Kiln-Debt, the protagonist stood silently as Don Compost laid out three red-crossed posters—Dandy Dibbler, Gordie Gravelbottom, and Sandy Spadewell—and earned the coveted Wiseguy Hat upon completion.

## What gnomes say

"Three biomes, three gold hats, zero words spoken."
