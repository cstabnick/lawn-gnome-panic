The First Kiln Bank is the bank in Downtown Turfton, and the institution that holds Turfton's Kiln-Debt ledgers. Its tower is gold mirror glass tapering in three stages, crowned with a giant red gnome hat with a gold band. Clay-Mart sells the debt; the First Kiln Bank keeps the books.

## What gnomes say

"Clay-Mart fires you, glazes you and sends you the bill. The First Kiln Bank keeps the bill until it is paid, which is the part of the story that is always on the bank's side."

"The hat is on top so that you can see the bank from anywhere in the city. You will be able to see the bank from anywhere in the city until you have paid off the debt."

## In the games

It stands on the x = 100, z = 90 block of Downtown Turfton. Its three gold mirror stages rise to the giant red hat, and a gold-coin and hat mural sits at street level. Its taper, hat and mural are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
