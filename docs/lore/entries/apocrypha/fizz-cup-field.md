The rivals on the Fizz Cup grid, grouped by the team or crew they race for. Each one has a line for the radio and a
pace that makes them easy or hard to beat.

## What gnomes say

"Sorry! Sorry! Was that a corner?" Pebble Quickloam, every lap, to anyone behind.

"Eat my clippings, rookie." Marigold "Mulch-Mouth" Fennick, who races for Buck-Iron and says it with a smile.

"I do not brake. I negotiate." Two-Ton Teasel, who races for the Carburettors.

## The truth

**Rookie Mow-Down** (ride-on mowers)
- Pebble Quickloam, a rookie who races in a sky-blue hat and apologises on every corner.
- Marigold "Mulch-Mouth" Fennick, Buck-Iron's works rider, who wants the podium more than she wants the trophy.
- Tumbleweed Tom, an old pan racer who lost his hat in 1994 and has been driving bareheaded ever since.

**Low & Loud Derby** (lowriders, the Carburettors)
- Lugnut Lulu, the Carburettors' captain. Orange hat, black shirt, purple tint on her paint.
- Two-Ton Teasel, who will not brake, and says so.
- Chrome-Dome Delphine, polished every morning, and asks everyone not to touch the car.

**Snail-Coach Endurance** (snail-coaches)
- Shelldon "No-Trail" Mucilage, who races on the gravel run-off all lap. See [shelldon-mucilage](shelldon-mucilage.md).
- Auntie Periwinkle, who bred her snail Doris herself, and does not like being overtaken.
- Slow Moe, who paces himself, and says that is not the same as being slow.

**The Final** (zero-turns)
- Vinnie "Turbo-Sod" Delacroix, three-time Grand Zero-Turn champion and Dandelion Fizz's celebrity endorser. See [vinnie-turbo-sod](vinnie-turbo-sod.md).
- Dock "Full-Throttle" Hoewright, out of retirement. See [dock-hoewright](dock-hoewright.md).
- Sprocket Vane, Razor-Back's factory works driver, who says factory parts beat garage parts.
