A harmless camp of a dozen or so gnomes who stand on one leg before a giant pink flamingo and keep rules that are absurd and gentle. They are nothing to do with the Evil Shadow Gnome Cultist of the Cursed House. The worst the Flock can do to you is a sore ankle.

## What gnomes say

"They stand on one leg at sermons, never sit on Tuesdays and keep pink for the Lady only. That is the whole creed, and they will say it twice."

"A Flock gnome at a bar will not take a stool. They'll take the foot rail."

## The truth

Their rules, as kept: one leg at every sermon; no sitting on Tuesdays; pink is for the Lady only (so the camp's washing is grey); nobody turns their back on her to eat; a gnome who wobbles has "had a thought" and owes the Flock a cup of reed tea. They prophesy "the Even Cut, on the longest day", and that turns out to be a real date.

Members take wading-bird names when they join. Besides Mother Saxifrage, Sister Sedge, Brother Bulrush, Sister Plover, Brother Godwit and Novice Pipit, the circle is kept by Brother Crake (owes nine cups, eleven counting Thursday), Sister Avocet, Sister Spoonbill, Brother Snipe and Sister Rail.
