A harmless camp of a dozen or so gnomes who stand on one leg before a giant pink flamingo and keep rules that are absurd and gentle. They are nothing to do with the Evil Shadow Gnome Cultist of the Cursed House. The worst the Flock can do to you is a sore ankle.

## What gnomes say

"They stand on one leg at sermons, never sit on Tuesdays and keep pink for the Lady only. That is the whole creed, and they will say it twice."

"A Flock gnome at a bar will not take a stool. They'll take the foot rail."

## The truth

Their rules, as kept: one leg at every sermon; no sitting on Tuesdays; pink is for the Lady only (so the camp's washing is grey); nobody turns their back on her to eat; a gnome who wobbles has "had a thought" and owes the Flock a cup of reed tea. They prophesy "the Even Cut, on the longest day", and that turns out to be a real date.

The code's rules board (in the big tent) lists five, and a sixth is "under discussion since the spring": one leg at every sermon; no sitting on Tuesdays; pink is for the Lady only; never turn your back on her to eat; a wobble is a thought, and a thought is a cup of reed tea (`reed-tea`). A tick-box on the wall reads "TODAY IS: TUESDAY / NOT TUESDAY (tick one, carefully)". Their rota (Plover's chalk board): Mon tea (Sedge), Tue NO SITTING, Wed washing (Bulrush), Thu wobble watch (Plover), Fri and Sat Godwit owes tea, Sun stand. They dress in grey and wear nothing pink.

Members take wading-bird names when they join. The camp is eleven strong, in two groups. The **officers**, each with a job: Mother Saxifrage Wader (leads; `saxifrage-wader`), Sister Sedge (the reed tea), Brother Bulrush (the grey washing), Sister Plover (the rota board), Brother Godwit (always wobbling, always owes tea) and Novice Pipit (new, still learning which leg). The **ring five**, who stand on the ritual circle facing the Lady from west to east: Brother Crake (`brother-crake`; owes nine cups, eleven counting Thursday), Sister Avocet (`sister-avocet`; balance with feelings), Sister Spoonbill (`sister-spoonbill`; the sceptic), Brother Snipe (`brother-snipe`; a hop is a prayer) and Sister Rail (`sister-rail`; has the Tuesday rule backwards). The big tent (`the-big-tent`) holds the rules, the rota board and a small white model of the Lady. The Flock's debts are all in reed tea; the only way to leave is to sit down at the Long Vigil (`the-long-vigil`), and everyone is forgiven.

In act IV the Flock join the coalition (`m64-all-hats`): their tent in the ring at the overlook camp is pink (W16), the one pink thing the Flock allow themselves, because for the day it is the Lady's tent and "pink is for the Lady only" (R-NEW-3); they stand outside it. And Mother Saxifrage's prophecy is the reason the crew knows the date (R-233, `m52-the-test-cut`).

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** Reed tea, by the cup, for the debts above. The big tent sells nothing and accepts anything, in grey.
- **Notices:** The rules board in the big tent lists the five rules and "a sixth, under discussion since the spring". The tick-box for Tuesday. A small card on the model of the Lady: "She is not an ornament. She is standing."
- **Sayings and habits:** "Stand as you are able." "Yesterday's wobble is today's tea." A sermon opens with everyone saying "ah", and ends when the Lady's shadow leaves the tent pole, which has not yet been measured.
