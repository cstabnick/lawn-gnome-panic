Brassknock Manor's footman: polite, tired, and the keeper of the great brass knocker, which he guards by telling everyone to go round the back.

## What gnomes say

"Pell will tell you to go round the back before you've said hello. He is not being rude. He is being consistent."

## In the games

Stands in the tasting hall by the back door of Brassknock Manor (R-211).

Whether he has ever been asked to open the front door.
