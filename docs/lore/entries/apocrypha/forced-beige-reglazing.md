The HOA's ultimate civic punishment: stripping a delinquent gnome's custom hat and coat colours and baking them in regulation uniform beige.

## How It Works

Because nobody dies in Turfton, real terror comes from losing who you are. Repeat violators of Municipal Code §404-B face foreclosure liens—and if they can't pay, HOA enforcers threaten to haul them to a compliance kiln and paint over every personal stripe, freckle, and bright cap.

## What gnomes say

"Crack my arm, fine my porch, just keep that beige paintbrush away from my hat."

## Loose threads

Where the HOA sends gnomes who resist even after three coats of beige.
