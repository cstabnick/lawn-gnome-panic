A walled military base in the corner of Turfton, built inside a giant sandbox. Sandbag walls, watch towers, one gate,
and a landing pad with the Whirligig on it.

## What gnomes say

"Nothing goes in the Sandpit and nothing comes out." The Spade Guard say it more often than it is true.

## The truth

The fort stands in the Dust Flats, east of the highway bend: gate near (374, 44) in the north wall (the gate road runs off the east highway), the Whirligig's pad near (362, -2), a tall sandbag wall with guard posts and an armory behind the gate. It is run by Commander Diggs (`commander-diggs`) for the Spade Guard (`spade-guard`) and has been under the HOA's fuel audits for fifteen years.

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: the Dust Flats, east of the city (not the south-east corner: its gate is at about x 374, z 44). Entering raises the heat by two hats; the pad holds the Whirligig.
- Act IV: `ch4-sandpit-drop` (steal the Whirligig by parachute, over the wall), `ch4-spade-truce` (Vesper has fired the Guard and sealed the fort from Thorne Tower: "Nobody fires the Guard") and `m60-the-moles-dig` (the Burrowfolk dig under the wall and open the armory from below). After the truce the gate wears the coalition's patchwork flag and the Guard waves from the walls (W15).

## Everyday

- **Notices:** at the gate: "Nothing goes in. Nothing comes out. Except post." A chalk board lists the day's watchword (always "Spade"). Below the HOA's fuel-audit letter, in a Guard hand: "We have read it."
- **Habits:** the Guard salute the gate, the flag and, at dusk, the horizon. Reveille is at six; the bugle has been missing for fifteen years, so the sergeant hums it.
- **Food:** the mess serves a dull stew called "Sand and Gravy", with Dust Biscuits for afters (bought in Pebblewick).
- **Sayings:** "Nothing goes in the Sandpit." "A Guard is a spade with a hat."
- **Small history:** the HOA's audit of the fort's fuel has been open for fifteen years; the file is now thicker than the wall. The HOA's lost-property desk stands outside the gate, in [its own shed](lost-property-shed.md).
