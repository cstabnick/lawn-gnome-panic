The downtown park made from three blocks Vesper Thorne had condemned. Councilwoman Juniper Vance rezoned them after the Founder's Trim Day tribunal. It includes Gnorwin's statue.

## What gnomes say

"They condemned three blocks to make a point. Juniper gave the point a lawn."

"Bring a hat. The bandstand is for sitting, not for standing on."

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Opened at the end of #15 `ch1-founders-boot` (world change W03: ribbon, crowd, bandstand, condemned signs gone). The party for it is #34 `m34-forty-seven-wrenches` (W07, bunting). Epic E01 builds the park from the three downtown park blocks; Gnorwin Clayfoot Park becomes part of it.
