Turfton's annual civic parade down `ROAD_LINES`, featuring marching bands, acorn-pretzel carts, and the thirty-foot Gnorwin Clayfoot Boot float.

## History & Accounts

Normally a sleepy excuse for Mayor Mulch to wave from a convertible Ride-On, Founder's Trim Day turned legendary when Pip and the protagonist hijacked the Boot Float mid-route—scattering marching trombonists while Red Caps and Blue Caps accidentally turned the parade into a crossfire zone.

## What gnomes say
