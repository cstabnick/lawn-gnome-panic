Fence-Post Frank's shack behind the south welcome sign, where he sleeps when he is not leaning on it. The sign reads "NO SOLICITORS. NO SOLICITING EITHER."

## What gnomes say

"He leans on the sign by day and the wall by night."

"No solicitors, no soliciting either, and he means both."
