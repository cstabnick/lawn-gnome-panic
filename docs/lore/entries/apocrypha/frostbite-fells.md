Permafrost northern tundra (`z < -215`) born from a leaky outdoor spigot that froze solid, famous for black-ice hairpins, smuggling runs, and Carburettor drift derbies.

## Field Notes & Tales

Early explorer gnomes lost a dozen good hats to the permafrost before realizing frozen birdbaths make flawless drift arenas. Smugglers soon followed, discovering that ice-packed Black Dew stays volatile twice as long on long hauls. When winter squalls roll in, visibility drops to ten feet and clay limbs rattle like porcelain teacups.

## What gnomes say

"Nothing freezes clay faster than Frostbite Fells—chip a boot up there and you'll rattle all the way home."

## Look and motifs

The Fells are walled in stone that is never quite free of snow. Snow-capped stone walls follow the
roads, cairns mark the high ground and the passes, and the cottages are grey stone with steep slate roofs that shed the
drifts. The mountain at the north edge is the highest ground in the garden.

- Motifs: snow-capped stone walls, cairns, snow pines, steep-roofed stone cottages.
- Palette: grey stone walls, slate roofs, blue flowers where any grow.
- In the game: theme `frostbite-fells` in `src/data/regionThemes.json`; the mountain is `mountain/fells` in
  `src/data/landscape.json`.
