A wide, glassy basin of solid ice in Frostbite Fells that serves as The Carburettors' midnight figure-eight drift arena and demolition derby bowl.

## Field Notes & Tales

Every Friday at midnight, headlights ring the frozen rim while Zero-Turn Racers slide sideways inches from each other's fenders. Father Loam watches from an idling snowmobile, blessing radiators with compost tea, while Dandelion Fizz banners flap against the snowdrifts. When HOA salt-plows try to ruin the ice, the racers simply ram them into the snowbanks.

## What gnomes say

"If you can hold a drift across the Basin without kissing a bumper, you're a made Carburettor."
