The angler on Basin Quay, Pardonlin: tan hat, glasses, a white-grey beard, who catches nothing and admires the nothing for its commitment.

## What gnomes say

"Nothing today. Nothing yesterday. The nothing is getting bigger. I admire its commitment."

## The truth

The quay's patient man: he says every catch is a loan, and every nothing a deposit. He has been in the same spot long enough that the swan nods to him.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E30): at the end of the Basin Quay, "fish" idle; the "angler who never catches" vignette.
