The rickety shed that is the gnomes' base. It holds the shrine to Omnipresent Gnome Jesus, the tithe altar, the miracle shop and a departure lever.

## In the games

- The shrine gives a speed blessing for 14 seconds when prayed at.
- The tithe station takes held loot to Gnome Jesus.
- The miracle shop is opened from its own station.
- A lever labelled Pull Sacred Departure Lever sits beside them.
- Quota sermons say whether the quota was met, "HALLELUJAH! QUOTA MET!" or "YOU FAILED THE QUOTA!"
- The README title is "Chapel of Mulch". The code never uses that name.
