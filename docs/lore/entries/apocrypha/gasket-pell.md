The pit mechanic at the Rutbottom Dirt Bowl: black cap, grey beard, glasses, sawing something that is not leaking, only sweating.

## What gnomes say

"It is not leaking. It is sweating. Knobblies sweat."

## The truth

He has mended every bike that has ever fallen over in the Bowl and has a name for each of the leaks. He is a distant cousin of Bracken Pell of Capwood, which both deny.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E07): in the pits at about (1020, -692), "saw" idle, a bubble when you pass.
