The waterer of the Little Gardens, Pardonlin: yellow hat, brown shirt, tan beard, who waters at noon as the rules say and interprets how much.

## What gnomes say

"I water at noon, as the rules say. The rules do not say how much. I interpret."

## The truth

He waters next to the well sign that reads "The well is for water. Not for wishes. Wishes go in the compost." His allotment is the greenest; the others suspect the interpretation.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E30): the Little Gardens, at about the well by the allotments (z 760), "water" idle.

## Loose threads

How much he waters. Nobody has measured; the gardeners have rules about that too.
