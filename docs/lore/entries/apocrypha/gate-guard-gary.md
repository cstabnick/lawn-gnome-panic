Bored Patio Heights sentry standing inside a tiny guardhouse with a 900-page HOA binder, waving luxury Zero-Turns through while citing smoking Push-Mower Bikes.

## Story: The 900-Page Binder

Gary didn't join the HOA out of ideological zeal; he joined because the sentry box has a padded stool and zero rain leaks. He has read approximately four pages of his 900-page binder and uses the rest to press clover leaves and prop up his lukewarm tea.

His rule of thumb is brutally simple: if your mower shines, he waves you past without looking up. If your exhaust smokes or your hat is crooked, he sighs, reaches for his citation pad, and mutters about "curb appeal."
