Bored Patio Heights sentry standing inside a tiny guardhouse with a 900-page HOA binder, waving luxury Zero-Turns through while citing smoking Push-Mower Bikes.

## Story: The 900-Page Binder

Gary didn't join the HOA out of ideological zeal; he joined because the sentry box has a padded stool and zero rain leaks. He has read approximately four pages of his 900-page binder and uses the rest to press clover leaves and prop up his lukewarm tea.

His rule of thumb is brutally simple: if your mower shines, he waves you past without looking up. If your exhaust smokes or your hat is crooked, he sighs, reaches for his citation pad, and mutters about "curb appeal."

## What gnomes say

"Give Gary a shiny paint job at Pay 'n' Glaze and he'll salute you straight into Vesper's driveway."

## The truth
Gary is the HOA's unambitious sentry at the Patio Gate. He wants a dry stool and no incidents, and fears page 412. Secret: he has read four pages in total. Home: the gatehouse itself. He waves Reginald through every morning, barely tolerates Cam on the same gate, fears only Vesper's driveway and has nightmares about Brenda's gang. Voice: bored, nasal, bureaucratic, citing section numbers of a book he has not read, with a sigh before every sentence ("Sigh. Section nine. Hats must be level." / "Wave through. Do not smoke.").

Arc: the gate the player must pass to reach Reginald's door (#12); waves the player through to the Penthouse Viewing and sighs when Cam arrives in a vest (#21); absent in act III; side quests only in act IV.
