Keeper of the Woolly Sock Lodge and ski instructor on Snowdrop Slope: brisk, warm-handed and unimpressed by anything that falls over twice.

## What gnomes say

"Bend the knees. Look where you are going. If you are going into a tree, look at a different tree."
"There is a bunk. There is soup. The sock rack is not for sharing."

## In the games

Home: the Woolly Sock Lodge (E01 site ski-hill; interior if E05 allows). Work: ski instructor on Snowdrop Slope. Planned (batch 3): rows 37 (m37-snowdrop-slope), where she gives shelter and a bunk, and 38 (m38-pail-pond), where she sends the crew fishing. Her hat is a striped bobble cap.

## Loose threads

Where she learned to ski, with no snow to learn on.
