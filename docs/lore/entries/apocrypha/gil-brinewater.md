## What gnomes say

"Two acorns a cup. Three with a fork. The fork is the expensive part."

Gnome Theft Auto (FX11, rulings R-244 and R-249): behind the Plank & Pickle counter at (-97, 1109.5), calling his wares. Look: A white hat and a green shirt.
