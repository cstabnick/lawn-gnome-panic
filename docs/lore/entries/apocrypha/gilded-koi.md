A gold-and-white koi with long flowing fins that shines like a new acorn. It lives at the lock pool on the Southern Drainage Canal, and says it escaped from Baroness Brenda's ornamental pond.

## What gnomes say

"Came out of the lock pool with a fin like a bedsheet and a face like it owed somebody money."
"Brenda says she has never lost a koi in her life. The koi says it has never lost anything in its life either, and it is very sure about that."

## The truth

The Gilded Koi is a real fish, the sort of animal gnomes keep mistaking for an ornament. It is never hurt (R-146). A gnome who lands it measures it, writes a catch card and lets it go, and it swims off into the lock pool, shining and offended. Its story of escaping from the Baroness's pond is the koi's own, and nobody has checked it against the pond.

## In the games

Only at the Lock Pool hot spot on the canal, and only for a gnome with a Brass-Reel rod or better. It is one of the three mythic fish of the game.

## Loose threads

Whether the Gilded Koi really escaped from Baroness Brenda's pond, or was always in the canal and is only borrowing the pond's story. Where its gold comes from. Whether Brenda ever came looking for it.
