Vesper Thorne's hydraulic throne at the end of the Grand Combine's deck: gilded, upholstered in beige and fitted with a lamp that rises as it is raised. It is the one seat on the Combine, and it has been warm since the Combine woke.

## What gnomes say

"Everything on the Combine is a place for a Porcelain Guard to stand. The Throne is the only place to sit."

"The gold is paint. He knows. He says it is the idea of gold."

## The truth

The Lawn-Throne is hydraulic, set at the deck's far end, where the Golden Acorn's ignition core hums under the deck plate. It rises for the third part of the finale (`ch5-even-cut-p3`), where the Porcelain Guard fight across the deck to Vesper: the throne's lamp is lit when they are down. It is where Vesper, cracked under his top hat by a hairline Clara has known since a porch step, stops being a lawn-sized idea and becomes a gnome. The player is asked to take the hat, not to break him (Clara's plea).

## In the games

Planned: the deck's end in `ch5-even-cut-p3` ("The Gilded Lawn-Throne") and `ch5-even-cut`. A prop of the deck dressing (the deck itself is E-finale work).
