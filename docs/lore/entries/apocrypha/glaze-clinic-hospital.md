Turfton's ceramic trauma centre at `x: 30, z: 30`, where Glaze Clinic Ambulances haul cracked gnomes to be glued, baked, and billed.

## Inside & Stories

Nobody dies in Turfton—they crack, get swept into a dustpan by paramedics, and wake up on the Glaze Clinic's front steps (`x: 7, z: 30`) smelling faintly of kiln-cement and peppermint. Inside, Squeaky-Clean Midge and an army of caffeine-wired interns reassemble shattered hats at assembly-line speed while an automated stamp slams *CLAIM DENIED* onto every Kiln-Shield form.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Scratched into the waiting-room bench: *"Second crack this week. Ask for the cherry-scented glue."*
