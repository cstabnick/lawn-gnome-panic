Turfton's top glaze-model and lifestyle influencer, winking from every Kiln-Shield billboard while selling 400-Acorn jars of "Unfired Aesthetic" mud.

## Story: Pure Cloverfield Silt

Glenda's face adorns bus benches from Patio Heights to the Dust Flats. Her luxury skincare line—*Unfired Aesthetic*—retails for four hundred acorns a jar and consists entirely of scooped mud from Cloverfield Farms' drainage ditch, packaged in miniature terracotta pots with gold foil lids.

She never drives her own mower, preferring to be chauffeured in a spotless white Ride-On while recording whispered radio endorsements.
