A cheerfully painted mobile kitchen parked outside Glaze Clinic Hospital, serving warm Acorn-Flour Cake and locked in a cold war with Chirp & Crumb.

## Inside & Stories

Gnoma leans out the serving window with a wooden spoon, humming cheerfully while Hat Watch sirens wail two streets over. Her Acorn-Flour Cake restores cracked clay morale instantly—and even off-duty Spade Guard soldiers trade rations for slices through a loose sandbag at Fort Sandpit. Bump her fender with a mower, and she yells, *"Oops, a little wobbly! We learn from it!"*

## Ledger & Graffiti

Menu board chalked on the side: *"TODAY'S SPECIAL: ACORN-FLOUR CAKE (NOT BRICK-HARD LIKE SOME CHAINS)."*
