The flat-pack furniture firm that supplies Flat 3B. They deliver to Saucer Court, "stairs extra, free". Nothing they sell comes assembled, and the instructions are one diagram of a gnome looking worried.

## What gnomes say

"Stairs extra, free. Read it twice."

"It arrives in nine boxes and leaves as a bookcase."

## In the games

Built by E10 (batch 3): the player buys furniture here and has it delivered to Flat 3B.
