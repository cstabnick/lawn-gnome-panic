The gnome yard is the unit Hedgeworth measures in: half a Tall-One yard, 0.5 metres. Every distance on the card, the signs and the pro's advice is in gnome yards.

## What gnomes say

"A good drive goes about two hundred. Nobody at Hedgeworth has ever said it in metres."

## The truth

A gnome yard is a Tall One's yard cut in half, so a gnome can walk it. The whole yard, a garden of 360 by 420 metres, is 720 by 840 gnome yards, and it still feels small after a bad hole.

## In the games

Built (batch 3, E15): the HUD shows yards, and a driver carries about 205 gnome yards (about 102 m). The golf frame in `golf/course.ts` converts between yards and the world (R-193).
