Turfton's central downtown patch of grass west of Glaze Clinic Hospital, anchored by a weathered statue of Gnorwin Clayfoot wearing a traffic cone.

## Inside & Stories

In Fescue, Gnorwin Clayfoot is a solemn hero who jammed the great Mower 3,000 years ago. In Turfton, teenagers climb his bronze boot to balance orange traffic cones on his pointed hat and spray-paint *EVEN CUT WAS HERE* across the plinth. Pigeons roost on his beard while off-duty mechanics eat lunch on the benches below. The statue now stands on a stepped monument plinth facing the wishing fountain, and the fountain takes an Acorn for a wish: a fortune, a splash of Glaze, or the coin back. Benches ring the fountain, and the Watch has agreed not to move them.
