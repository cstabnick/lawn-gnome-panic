A thirty-foot papier-mâché parade float shaped like Gnorwin Clayfoot's mud-caked boot, built on a tractor chassis with a confetti-and-acorn horn cannon.

## Story: The Tribunal Crasher

Constructed annually for Turfton's Founder's Trim Day parade, the Boot Float was meant to symbolize humble Fescue heritage. Instead, Pip packed the toe with fireworks and helped hijack it straight down `ROAD_LINES`—drifting thirty feet of papier-mâché sideways through pretzel carts and smashing clean through the Turfton Amphitheater's cardboard arch.
