A humming, glowing Fescue Golden Acorn discovered inside a stolen Zero-Turn's grass-catcher bag—and the sole ignition core capable of powering The Grand Combine.

## Story: The Humming Crate

Stamped with the Fescue Crown and smuggled out through a knot-hole in the Fence, the Seed-Cell hums like a high-voltage battery in your palm. Vesper Thorne needed it to awaken Project Even Cut; Tansy and the Carburettors realized it could power Turfton's entire downtown grid forever.

## What gnomes say

"Hold it near your ear and you can hear three thousand years of grass growing."
