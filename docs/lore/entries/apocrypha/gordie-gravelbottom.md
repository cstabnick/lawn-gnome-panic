Brown-bearded, gold-hatted syndicate debtor hiding out in Frostbite Fells (`20, -330`) and the second target of Don Compost's First Contract.

## Story: Frozen Assets

Gordie figured nobody from Turfton would drive all the way up the `north` highway in a snow-squall just to collect an overdue acorn tab. He packed two thermos flasks, a heavy wool scarf, and a gold hat he won in a crooked Gnomecino dice game, then set up camp near Thermos Theo's bend.

He spends most of his exile complaining that his beard keeps freezing to his collar while scanning the horizon for headlights.

## What gnomes say

"Gordie's tough, right up until his boots freeze to the permafrost."
