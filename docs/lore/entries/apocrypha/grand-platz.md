Pardonlin's main square, in the Old Town: a fountain, a bandstand, the Unknown Pigeon on its plinth, a boules pitch and café terraces round every edge.

## What gnomes say

"The Grand Platz is the only square in the world with more chairs than gnomes. The chairs are there for the watching."

"Nothing happens in the Platz. It happens slowly, on purpose, and everybody turns up for it."

## The truth

The square is built for sitting and being seen, and everything in it is slightly too ornamental to use: the fountain is for looking at, the bandstand is for the gramophone, and the boules pitch is for arguing about. The Continentals queue here for nothing in particular and call it civic life.

## In the games

The Old Town, around x -1500, z -260 (the bandstand is at about z -235, where the town's gramophone plays Radio Adagio). Marcel Muet, the Gastons and the Unknown Pigeon all live here.

## Everyday

- **Notices:** "The fountain is for looking at." "Boules: not in the box hedge." A ticket board in the queue shows number 4 of 4,311, and nobody has called the next number.
- **Habits:** at noon everybody on the Platz turns to the bandstand gramophone and pretends they were not already facing it. The Unknown Pigeon is saluted by tilting the hat toward the plinth. Chairs are carried to wherever the sun is and carried back, which is called "the walk".
- **Sayings:** "Meet me on the Platz" means "I will be there, you will be late, and the chairs will wait."
- **Feud:** the boules players against the café terraces, over whose edge of the pitch is whose.
