Cloverfield Farms' sweetest-looking baker and the sharpest black-market acorn fence and Black Dew taproom operator south of Turfton.

## Story: Load-Bearing Pumpkins

To Hat Watch rookies, Granny Gnertrude is a harmless old earthenware lady in a floral apron who hands out warm acorn-flour biscuits. To anyone who has tried to park a stolen Combine near her porch, she is a one-gnome cartel who keeps a loaded pump-action shotgun behind the flour bin.

Her prize pumpkins aren't just for pie—she hollows out the biggest ones to hide 87-octane Black Dew drums from HOA inspectors. When Reginald Vance once tried to measure her porch squash for "excessive girth," Granny politely asked if he'd ever seen what buckshot does to brass calipers.

## What gnomes say

"Take her biscuits, pay your tab in full, and never—ever—drive over her pumpkin patch."

## The truth
Granny is Cloverfield's matriarch, the sharpest fence in three biomes, the crew's employer and proof that the old and crooked can be kind. She wants her silos and Dew-Wells kept, and a cut, and fears foreclosure. Secrets: her late husband's acorn silo (told at #31) and how much she knows about the Brassknocks' Dew trade (hinted at #17). Home and work: Granny Gnertrude's Farmhouse, her barn and "bake shop". Bramble is her right hand and calls her Boss (R-139); Rooster is her weather. Voice: warm, unhurried, a recipe's rhythm; "sugar" once a scene at most; pie is currency (the entry's biscuits are the everyday coin, the pie is the serious one). "Now we can cook. Sit down, sugar, and mind the crust."

Arc: named at #13 as "the only gnome Vesper can't buy"; the employer and debt-clearer (#16) who sends the player to Vesper knowing the trap (#20); chased by bailiffs and chipped (#31) and glued at the Glaze Clinic; absent in act III; at the overlook with pie (#64) and holding the south canal in the finale.
