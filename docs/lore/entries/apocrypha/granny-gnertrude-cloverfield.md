Cloverfield Farms' sweetest-looking baker and the sharpest black-market acorn fence and Black Dew taproom operator south of Turfton.

## Story: Load-Bearing Pumpkins

To Hat Watch rookies, Granny Gnertrude is a harmless old earthenware lady in a floral apron who hands out warm acorn-flour biscuits. To anyone who has tried to park a stolen Combine near her porch, she is a one-gnome cartel who keeps a loaded pump-action shotgun behind the flour bin.

Her prize pumpkins aren't just for pie—she hollows out the biggest ones to hide 87-octane Black Dew drums from HOA inspectors. When Reginald Vance once tried to measure her porch squash for "excessive girth," Granny politely asked if he'd ever seen what buckshot does to brass calipers.

## What gnomes say

"Take her biscuits, pay your tab in full, and never—ever—drive over her pumpkin patch."
