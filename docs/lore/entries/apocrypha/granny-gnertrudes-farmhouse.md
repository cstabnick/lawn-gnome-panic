A cozy, apron-curtained farmhouse on Cloverfield Farms' `south` highway that doubles as an illicit Black Dew taproom and heavy-weaponry fence.

## Inside & Stories

The kitchen smells of cinnamon and fresh acorn-flour cake; the root cellar smells of 87-octane petrol and gun oil. Granny Gnertrude serves tea to lost travelers on the front porch while her crew unloads hijacked Dew-Hauler drums behind the prize pumpkin patch. HOA inspectors who walk up the gravel drive rarely get past the screen door.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Needlepoint sampler hanging above the shotgun rack: *"HOME IS WHERE THE LOAD-BEARING PUMPKINS ARE."*

## Loose threads

How many barrels of Black Dew are actually buried beneath the barn floorboards.
