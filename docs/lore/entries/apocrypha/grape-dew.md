The sweet pressing of Trellisdown's vines, poured at weddings and tastings in thimble glasses. It is not Black Dew and runs no engine; a gnome who tries it in a tank gets a sticky mower and a lecture.

## What gnomes say

"A thimble of grape-dew for a wedding, two for a christening, none for a mower."

"Black Dew makes it go. Grape-dew makes you go slowly and say nice things."

## The truth

Chicory Snips presses it the old way, with bare feet in a clay trough, and swears the vine remembers the gardener. The brass-capped Brassknock bottles are the only part of it that is for show.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Quest 17 (`m17-the-dew-buyer`) starts as Granny's stolen Black Dew going west and ends at the vineyard, where it is burned in frost-pots; quest 50 (`m50-trellisdown-tasting`) is a grape-dew tasting. A bottle can be a pickup through the prefab library in the area epic.

Why Black Dew was being bought up at Trellisdown at all, when grape-dew is the one thing the vineyard has no need to buy.
