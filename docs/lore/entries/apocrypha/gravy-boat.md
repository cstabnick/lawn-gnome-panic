A white porcelain speedboat with a spout at the bow, the fastest thing on Turfton's water. It is a gravy boat that somebody decided was a boat.

## Field Notes & Tales

Porcelain is heavy, so the Gravy Boat sits low and fast and trails a little plume of foam from its spout whenever it is pushed. Nobody knows which dinner service it came from. Gnomes who ride it say the spout is the best place to stand, and nobody has ever stood there for long.

## What gnomes say

"Slow down, it's a gravy boat, not a Combine." Then they open the throttle anyway.

## In the games

Moored at the south canal wharf and at New Puddle Marina. It is the quickest way across deep water, and the one most likely to be taken without asking. All five boats count toward the "Captain Clay" achievement: take the helm of every one and earn a Captain's Cap.
