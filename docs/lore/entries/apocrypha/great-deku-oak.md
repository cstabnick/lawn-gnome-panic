A giant oak in the yard that gnomes crawl into for golden acorns, ancient sap and cursed twigs.

## In the games

- It is entered from the yard, at a spot labelled Enter Great Deku Oak. Its exit leads back to the yard.
- Its loot includes the Golden Deku Acorn, "Blessed nut of the Great Oak", and the Jar of Ancient Sap.
- The Blighted Deku Twig is cursed.
- Its enemies are giant spiders, an Evil Shadow Gnome Cultist and a rolling pillbug.
- Its sermon: "THE DEKU OAK GROANS WITH TREASURE."
