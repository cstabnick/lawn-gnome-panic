The historic afternoon when municipal sprinklers misfired across Turfton, driving the Spade Guard to fortify Fort Sandpit and silencing Don Compost for good.

## History & Accounts

Old-timers remember the sky suddenly erupting in cold, rhythmic arcs of water from unseen brass heads. Earthenware gnomes scattered indoors, Slip-and-Slide chaos gripped downtown, and the military marched straight east to wall off the dry sandbox—never to return their budget.
