A humid, glass-roofed downtown alleyway lit by buzzing grow-lamps, housing The Gnomecino's back doors, Pay 'n' Glaze paint bays, and 24-hour fern stalls.

## Field Notes & Tales

Built inside a cracked Tall-One cold-frame, the Greenhouse Strip is where Turfton's rules soften after midnight. Red Caps, Blue Caps, and off-duty Hat Watch officers share fermented dandelion wine under the pink sodium glow, adhering to an unspoken truce—mostly because Big Lou's bouncers throw troublemakers straight into the cactus planters.

## What gnomes say

"What happens under the grow-lamps stays under the grow-lamps—unless Big Lou throws you out."
