A humid, glass-roofed downtown alleyway lit by buzzing grow-lamps, housing The Gnomecino's back doors, Pay 'n' Glaze paint bays, and 24-hour fern stalls.

## Field Notes & Tales

Built inside a cracked Tall-One cold-frame, the Greenhouse Strip is where Turfton's rules soften after midnight. Red Caps, Blue Caps, and off-duty Hat Watch officers share fermented dandelion wine under the pink sodium glow, adhering to an unspoken truce—mostly because Big Lou's bouncers throw troublemakers straight into the cactus planters.

## What gnomes say

"What happens under the grow-lamps stays under the grow-lamps—unless Big Lou throws you out."

## Landmarks

- The Gnomecino's back doors, along the glass wall: the club's side entrance, guarded by Big Lou's bouncers.
- Neon-Moth Nina's stoop, (51, -41): the step where she sits between jobs. See `neon-moth-nina`.
- The fern stalls and the cactus planters, along the alley: open all night, and the planters are where the bouncers put people.
- Pay 'n' Glaze's paint bays: the shop itself stands south in the Ring Blocks (R-147), and its bays open onto the alley.

## Local slang

- "under the glass": out for the night, somewhere under the grow-lamps.
- "moth-bait": a show-off in neon. Nina's word, and she uses it on herself.
- "sugar": Nina's word for anyone, friend or stranger.
