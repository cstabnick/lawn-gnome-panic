The Left Bank's backyard laundress, who has been feuding with her neighbour over a garden wall for years.

## What gnomes say

"Greta's washing is always the whitest in the row. She makes sure the neighbour can see it."

"The wall was a foot higher on her side last spring. It is a foot higher on the other side now."

## The truth

The wall belongs to neither of them, and both of them keep adding to it. Greta hangs her washing along the top of it. The feud is the only thing she and her neighbour have in common and the reason they know each other so well.

## In the games

The Left Bank backyards (near x -1740, z 600), among the washing lines.
