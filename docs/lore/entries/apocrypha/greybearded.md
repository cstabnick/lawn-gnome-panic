A faction that lives with the moss rather than fighting it. They walk among the Hatless, and rarely get bitten.

## What they are

Founded by Lichen Ashbucket, who decided the moss is the garden's own will and must be lived with, not cured. Their home
is the Wormways under the Compost Heaps, and their hat is moss green (`#7d8b6a`). They let moss grow in their beards,
and the moss is theirs.

## How they live

- **Norms:** everything is shared; strangers are questioned at the tunnel mouths; the bitten are nursed, not cast out;
  the founder's line leads; they never strike first.
- **Among the Hatless:** they walk the herds and are rarely bitten. Why is a loose thread the Greybearded do not
  explain.

## What they want

To be left under the heaps, warm and alive. They will trade with anyone who does not bring fire.

## Loose threads

Why they are rarely bitten. The Greybearded say the moss knows them; the Holders say it is the beard; nobody has checked.
