A long corrugated-tin hangar standing east of the old airfield hangar on the same line, its roller door facing the runway. Wind-Sock Wally will sell a gnome the hangar for 1200 Acorns, and the roller door stays rolled up, so anyone passing can see straight in to the three bays.

## How it works

The hangar is 24 metres wide, 14 deep and 9 tall, with a concrete floor and a concrete apron out front and a windsock of its own on a pole by the door. It takes aircraft and nothing else: a Hedge-Hopper, a Crop Duster, a Sycamore Copter, a Dandelion Drifter or the Whirligig, one to a bay. A gnome who wants a fourth aircraft has to sell one first.

Wally keeps the clipboard. The paperwork is a seed-tin lid with a "2" scratched on it in the same hand as his flight logs, and he will tell you it is a perfectly good deed. Tansy's Sling-Lift will fetch an aircraft from here to a gnome's phone, for 80 Acorns. Rotor craft and the Dandelion Drifter are lowered onto clear flat ground near the gnome; fixed-wing aircraft are set down at the start of a clear strip about 60 metres long, facing down it, and if there is no strip nearby they are flown back to the Desert Airfield instead.

## What gnomes say

"Three bays, three aircraft, and one lid with a 2 on it. That's the whole deed."
