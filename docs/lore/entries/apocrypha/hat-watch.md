Turfton's watch: gnomes in navy caps and yellow coats who chase troublemakers and shoot back. The HUD counts how
interested they are in hats, up to three.

## What gnomes say

A gnome in trouble "has the Watch on their hat". Shoot enough officers and they crack apart and go off duty for good.

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: heat rises for cracking gnomes, stealing occupied mowers and trespassing; officers shoot the
  player until the player is Cracked (R-017) or the heat cools down out of sight.
