Turfton's watch: gnomes in navy caps and yellow coats who chase troublemakers and soak them back. The HUD counts how
interested they are in hats, up to three.

## What gnomes say

A gnome in trouble "has the Watch on their hat". Soak enough officers and they go off duty, hands up.

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: heat rises for soaking gnomes, stealing occupied mowers and trespassing; officers spray the
  player until the player is Washed Out (R-015) or the heat cools down out of sight.
