An old goat shelf that runs round the Bobble like the band round a hat. It is for bikes and buggies only, and it stays that way because of the bollards at either end, not because of the sign. At its high point is the World's Edge, a lookout over the north fence.

## What gnomes say

"Keep to the shelf and the shelf keeps to you."
"From the World's Edge you can see the fence. The fence cannot see you, which is the whole point of a fence."

## The truth

The shelf is older than the road. The goats made it, and the bollards were put in later by people who wanted a cart path. A car would fall off the Hatband; a buggy will manage it if it keeps its speed up. The World's Edge looks out past the perimeter fence, which is the nearest the yard comes to admitting it has an edge (R-231).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): the shelf is `mountainRoutes/defs/hatband.ts`, a bikes-and-buggies route round the Bobble (near (700, -1700)), with the World's Edge lookout at its high point. It joins the Brim Gap Road at the Hatbrim's foot.
