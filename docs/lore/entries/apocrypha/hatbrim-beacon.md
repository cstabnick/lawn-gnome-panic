The coalition's signal for the longest day: an iron fire basket on a tall tripod, stacked with kindling, set on the Bobble, the Hatbrim's summit. It is lit at dawn on the day of the Even Cut, and every gnome who sees it knows what it means.

## What gnomes say

"There is a fire on the Bobble. Nobody lights the Bobble. Somebody has."

"You can see it from Mittenholt. You cannot see Mittenholt from it. That is just what a beacon is."

## The truth

R-234 chose the Bobble because it is the highest ground in the yard (105 m, higher than Snowdrop Slope's mountain) and the only range the HOA never named, so no HOA map marks it as anywhere a fire might be lit. Wind-Sock Wally flies the Crop Duster up to it, because there is no road a fire basket can be carried up, and Hogweed Sprocket builds the fire. The beacon is an Underspanner design: three iron legs, a basket, a stack of kindling, "a warm glow at its heart". An unlit twin stands at the coalition camp by the hat-rail so the coalition can see what it is waiting for.

## In the games

Prefab `hatbrim-beacon`. Placed on the Bobble in `m64-all-hats-p2` ("By Air") and lit at dawn on the Even Cut. An unlit one at the camp (W16). The Bobble is at about (700, -1700); the Summit Register is on the same summit.
