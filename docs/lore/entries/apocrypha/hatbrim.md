A short, bald range on the north edge of the yard, between Mittenholt and the High Tarn. Seen from the hamlet it looks like a hat left on the fence: Old Pate is the near crown, Brim Gap the dent, and the Bobble the bobble on top. It is the highest ground in the yard, and snow lies on it all year above about 32 m (R-230).

## What gnomes say

"Wear two hats on the Hatbrim, and then ask whether the first one was a good idea."
"You can see the Bobble from Mittenholt. You can't see Mittenholt from the Bobble. That's the HOA for you."

## The truth

The Hatbrim is the Rise's high country, and the Rise has no peak until the Hatbrim is counted. The Bobble stands near (700, -1700) at about 105 m, a dome with a summit register. Old Pate, a bald dome near (750, -1500) at about 72 m, has nothing on it but wind. Brim Gap, near (705, -1585) at about 40 m, is the saddle between them, and the only place the roads can get over without a rope. The routes that climb it are described in their own entries: the Brim Gap Road, the Hatband, the Brim Walk and the Spine Cut (R-231).
