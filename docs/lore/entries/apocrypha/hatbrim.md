A short, bald range on the north edge of the yard, between Mittenholt and the High Tarn. Seen from the hamlet it looks like a hat left on the fence: Old Pate is the near crown, Brim Gap the dent, and the Bobble the bobble on top. It is the highest ground in the yard, and snow lies on it all year above about 32 m (R-230).

## What gnomes say

"Wear two hats on the Hatbrim, and then ask whether the first one was a good idea."
"You can see the Bobble from Mittenholt. You can't see Mittenholt from the Bobble. That's the HOA for you."

## The truth

The Hatbrim is the Rise's high country, and the Rise has no peak until the Hatbrim is counted. The Bobble stands near (700, -1700) at about 105 m, a dome with a summit register. Old Pate, a bald dome near (750, -1500) at about 72 m, has nothing on it but wind. Brim Gap, near (705, -1585) at about 40 m, is the saddle between them, and the only place the roads can get over without a rope. The routes that climb it are described in their own entries: the Brim Gap Road, the Hatband, the Brim Walk and the Spine Cut (R-231).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33, mountain routes): the range is in `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/data/landscape.json` as `mountain/the-bobble` (690, -1690) r 210 h 125, `mountain/old-pate` (760, -1500) r 150 h 80, and `ridge/hatbrim` (775, -1450) to (650, -1800). The route dressing and pads are in `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/mountainRoutes/`. Snow paint above 32 m; rock paint on slopes steeper than the rock slope.

Act IV (R-234, `m64-all-hats-p2` "By Air"): the coalition's signal for the longest day is a beacon on the Bobble (`hatbrim-beacon`), because it is the highest ground in the yard and the only range the HOA never named. Wind-Sock Wally flies the Crop Duster up to it, Hogweed Sprocket builds the fire, and it is lit at dawn on the day of the Even Cut; the coalition's camp keeps an unlit twin by its hat-rail (W16). The Summit Register (`summit-register`) sits on the same summit.

## Everyday

- **Notices:** the Highways Sub-Committee's "Hairpins are numbered in the order they were argued over, not the order they are driven" stands at the foot of the Brim Gap Road. On the slope: "Snow-plough markers. For use when it snows. (Not yet used. Not yet snowed.)"
- **Habits:** gnomes arrive at Brim Gap with two hats and take one off at the Top Hat. Everybody signs the register on the Bobble and then looks for the Fence, "to be cross at it".
- **Sayings:** "Wear two hats on the Hatbrim, and then ask whether the first was a good idea." "Rocks fall. Signs do not."
- **Food and drink:** Gap Tea and Hairpin Buns at the Top Hat; a tin flask of something hot, on the way down.
- **Small history:** the seven hairpins (the Crease, the Cowlick, the Dent, the Glare, the Flip, the Scrunch and the Brim Kink) were named by a committee that never agreed on their order; the numbers on the signs record the argument.
