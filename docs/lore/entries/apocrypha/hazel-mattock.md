The keeper of the Tuft & Tally Lodge in Mossy Wood: dry, plain and unhurried, she sells the Tuft License, buys the tufts and keeps the journal of species.

## What gnomes say

"Licence is a hundred and twenty acorns. Comb what you like. Put the tuft on the counter, not in your pocket."
"Go slowly near the western tree-line. Something older than me lives there, and it doesn't care for hurry."

## The truth

Hazel has kept the Lodge since the Wood was first shaded, and she has never once raised her voice at a gnome. She treats the quarry as neighbours with a long memory, which is why the journal is so careful and why she will not say the word for what a hunter might do to them.

## In the games

Behind the counter of the Tuft & Tally Lodge in Mossy Wood. Her cap is moss-green, her glasses are round, and her grey pigtails are tied with twine. She sells the Tuft License and buys tufts and keepsakes, and her journal lines are the hints and blurbs for each quarry.
