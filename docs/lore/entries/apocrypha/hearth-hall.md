The north-east hall of Loamdeep and the Crumbs' own: an oven, a long table and three bare faces of earth on the north wall that moles dig into for anyone who asks. It is where Loamdeep eats, and the only hall with a seat kept for someone who is not in it.

## What gnomes say

"Do not ask what is in the bread. Eat the bread."

"They dig you a room while you eat. I have been given two."

## The truth

Hearth is 20 by 16 metres of local floor (x 30 to 50, z -38 to -22). The sign by the oven reads "GRUBS, NOT ROOTS / SUPPER AT THE THIRD BELL. ALL WELCOME. EVEN GNOMES." The long table keeps one place marked "COUSIN (DJ)" for DJ Moleman, who is a Crumb and has not sat in it. The three dig faces on the north wall, at (34, -37), (40, -37) and (46, -37), each carry a sign: "DIG HERE, IF IT PLEASES YOU". Dig a Nook is done here; the Crumbs dig because they have the grubs and the grubs live where the digging is.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the north-east hall of the Loamdeep interior; Dig a Nook (`mole-jobs`) is played at the dig faces. Auntie Crumb keeps it.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** Supper at the third bell: grub bread, root broth, a "nook cake" with a hole in the middle for the next one. A bowl for a stranger is always laid. Seconds are called "the other half".
- **Notices:** "GRUBS, NOT ROOTS. Supper at the third bell. All welcome. Even gnomes." Over the three dig faces: "DIG HERE, IF IT PLEASES YOU." A card at the long table: "COUSIN (DJ)", with a clean spoon.
- **Sayings and habits:** "Do not ask what is in the bread." The Crumbs hum while they dig; the room hums back.

## Loose threads

Whether DJ Moleman will ever sit in his place. They keep it warm.
