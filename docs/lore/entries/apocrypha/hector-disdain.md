The waiter at Café Pardon, who never takes an order.

## What gnomes say

"Hector has seen you. This is the full extent of the service."

"He brings you the coffee you were going to order, three days later, and calls it intuition."

## The truth

Hector is a very fine waiter in every way except the one that matters. He glides, he sighs, he wipes a table that is clean, and he does not ask what you would like. Nobody has complained: it is understood to be part of the experience, and the experience is excellent.

## In the games

Inside and on the terrace of Café Pardon, Teacup Row (near x -1525, z 143).

## Loose threads

Whether he has ever taken an order, and whether it was that of the last customer to leave.
