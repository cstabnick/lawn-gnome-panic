The editor of the Turfton Tattler. She talks in headlines, drops verbs, and has never been photographed.

## What gnomes say

"Penthouse. Exclusive. Tonight." Her headlines are the only proof anyone has that she exists.

## The truth

The Tattler's masthead still shows nobody. Hedda has never printed a correction. She hands out her jobs at Dot's desk
on the Kerbside Exchange.

Hedda is press, loyal to the story. She wants the front page and fears a correction. Secret: nobody has seen her face on the masthead; she is never photographed. Home: a flat high in the Tattler Building; work: the Tattler, run from a street desk at the north gate of Founders' Green since the Green took the Building's block (R-229: "a desk you can see is harder to sue"). Juniper is her best contact in the press, Reginald her one unconfirmed source, Dot hands her jobs, and Vesper cannot buy her ink. Voice: clipped headlines, no verbs, telegram style, always present tense, naming the story like a banner ("Penthouse. Exclusive. Tonight." / "Mayor missing. Permits found. Reginald mum.").

Arc: gives the player the float keys' location in the parade office (#15 part 1); prints Juniper's permits only if Reginald confirms them (#28); absent in act III; holds the Tattler Building for the emergency assessment (#67).
