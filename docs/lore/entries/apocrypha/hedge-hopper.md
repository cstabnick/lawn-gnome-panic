A small red two-seat low-wing sport monoplane with a cream stripe, parked on the Desert Airfield apron beside the Crop Dusters. Wind-Sock Wally's own; he flies it when nobody is watching the windsock.

## Field Notes & Specs

Where the Crop Dusters are rusty and wired together, the Hedge-Hopper is the one the Spade Guard was never allowed to touch. It is quick and low, and it flies just over the hedge tops. It stalls near 18 m/s, so the runway needs a good run before it lifts. The pilot's rule, painted inside the cockpit, is short: it hops hedges, not fences.

## What gnomes say

"Twenty metres above the lawn and it still feels like you're in the grass."
