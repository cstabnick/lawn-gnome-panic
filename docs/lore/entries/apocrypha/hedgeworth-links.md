A four-hole course among hedges, tall grass and giant plants, in the neighbour's garden, on a card that says nine. It is not Vesper's Patio Country Club; Vesper only holds its mortgage. Run from the Hedgeworth Clubhouse.

## What gnomes say

"Nine holes, and each one is a hedge you cannot see over."

"They play it for a drink at the ninth. The ninth is a long way."

## The truth

Four holes, numbered 1, 2, 3 and 9 on the card, because "a nine-hole course" reads better on the mortgage papers (R-194). Course par 16. Distances are in gnome yards (R-193), and a good drive goes about 200 of them.

1. **The Mortgage**, par 4: from the tee by the clubhouse, along a long fairway past two bunkers, to the green at the west.
2. **Vesper's Patience**, par 3: a short hole north to south, with bunkers either side of the green.
3. **The Long Week**, par 5: down the length of the garden and round a dogleg, with Hosepipe Pond on the bend.
4. **The Ninth**, par 4: by Thrift's shed and the Kicked Board, up to a green over a mole run.

## In the games

Built (batch 3, E15), in the E01 site `golf-yard` (coordinates from `golf/types.ts`):

- Tees and greens: hole 1 tees at (-1080, 482) and putts to (-1250, 472); hole 2 (-1285, 503) to (-1295, 585); hole 3 (-1320, 622) to (-1170, 785); hole 9 (-1140, 760) to (-1065, 640).
- Hosepipe Pond on hole 3's dogleg at (-1250, 735), see `hosepipe-pond`.
- Solid hedge walls (3.2 m high) with three hedge arches to drive a golf cart through; the grass is tall and mowable, and giant plants are soft: you drive through them.
- Cart paths of gravel from the Members' Gate and round the loop; two golf carts sit in the cart shelter beside the clubhouse.
- Balls lost in the rough to pick up, and four molehills by the ninth green to pat flat.
