A nine-hole course among hedges, tall grass and giant plants, in the neighbour's garden, where the card was right all along. It is not Vesper's Patio Country Club; Vesper only holds its mortgage. Run from the Hedgeworth Clubhouse.

## What gnomes say

"Nine holes, and each one is a hedge you cannot see over."

"They play it for a drink at the ninth. The ninth is a long way."

## The truth

Nine holes, in card order, course par 34. For years the card said nine and the course had four (holes 1, 2, 3 and 9, par 16, R-194); Dame Peony promised the rest "next season", every season. Thrift laid out holes 4 to 8 by hand in the Bottom Garden and the promise is kept (R-226). Distances are in gnome yards (R-193), and a good drive goes about 200 of them.

1. **The Mortgage**, par 4: from the tee by the clubhouse, along a long fairway past two bunkers, to the green at the west.
2. **Vesper's Patience**, par 3: a short hole north to south, with bunkers either side of the green.
3. **The Long Week**, par 5: down the length of the garden and round a dogleg, with Hosepipe Pond on the bend.
4. **Peony's Promise**, par 4: the long-promised hole, with the opening ribbon still across the green.
5. **The Maze**, par 4: the old hedge maze clipped into a hole; hedges stop a ball, a high wedge can go over.
6. **The Drip**, par 3: a water carry over the overflow pool and its giant birdbath.
7. **The Potting Shed**, par 3: teed off the shed roof, downhill over the cabbages.
8. **The Rhubarb Patch**, par 4: a dogleg left round the vegetable patch.
9. **The Ninth**, par 4: by Thrift's shed and the Kicked Board, up to a green over a mole run. Played last, back through the Turn Gate.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** The pro shop sells "clubs, balls, luck (luck not sold)" and closes at "back in five strokes", which means tomorrow. The ball bucket by the Kicked Board is free and says so, then says not to.
- **Notices:** The Members' Cup is the first of the month, dress tweed. A sign at the course edge reads "Carts on paths. Gnomes on grass. Moles: we do not have moles." Another, at the pond: "No paddling. The mower is working."
- **Sayings and habits:** "A hole is a hedge you have not met yet." "Replace your divot, and if it was Basil's, replace Basil." Members tap the first tee marker twice before driving, for the card. Nobody has ever said why.
- **Small history:** The card said nine holes for years while the ground held four, and every season Dame Peony said "next season". The members still say it, as a toast, now the nine are real.
