A nine-hole course among hedges, tall grass and giant plants, in the neighbour's garden. It is not Vesper's Patio Country Club; Vesper only holds its mortgage. Run from the Hedgeworth Clubhouse.

## What gnomes say

"Nine holes, and each one is a hedge you cannot see over."

"They play it for a drink at the ninth. The ninth is a long way."

## The truth

The nine holes, with par (total 36):

1. **The Gate Hole**, par 3, over a garden hose.
2. **The Marrow Patch**, par 4, round a squash the size of a shed.
3. **Long Hedge**, par 5, down the whole length of one hedge.
4. **The Birdbath**, par 3, across the water.
5. **Dogleg Delphinium**, par 4, round a stand of tall flowers.
6. **The Compost Carry**, par 4, over a heap that smells of good intentions.
7. **Sundial**, par 3, with a stone dial in the middle of the green.
8. **The Long Border**, par 5, along a flower bed nobody putts across.
9. **The Ninth**, par 5, a green over a mole run: the reason Thrift hides moles there.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). A golf-hole objective in quest 47 (`m47-hedgeworth-links`), and the test site for the Combine's cutting deck in 52 (`m52-the-test-cut`). E15 builds the nine holes above with their par.
