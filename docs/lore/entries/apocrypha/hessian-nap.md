The deckchair attendant on Dunelow's strand: pale hat, teal shirt, glasses, one acorn an hour and a gull on every chair.

## What gnomes say

"One acorn an hour. The gulls pay nothing and sit longest."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (FX11, rulings R-244 and R-249): on the sand by the deckchair row at (-594, 1164.5), under the sign "DECKCHAIRS / HIRE 1 ACORN / (Chairs not responsible for gulls)" at (-619, 1160). Look: A sand-coloured hat, a teal shirt and cream trousers, a grey beard and glasses.

## Loose threads

Whether he has ever got a chair back from a gull.
