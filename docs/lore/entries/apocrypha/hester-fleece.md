The shepherd of the Spine: red hat, green dress, leaning on a rail among eighty-one sheep and eighty-two tags, and asking you not to ask. Sheep 41 is thinking about it.

## What gnomes say

"Eighty-one sheep and eighty-two tags. Do not ask. Sheep 41 is thinking about it."

## The truth

The extra tag is for the sheep she has not met. The flock goes down to the road each dusk and counts itself.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): on the Spine path, "lean-back" idle.
