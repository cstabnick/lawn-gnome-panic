The treacherous frozen highway (`[0, -180]` to `[-20, -570]`) climbing out of Turfton into Frostbite Fells' black-ice hairpins.

## Field Notes & Tales

Past `z: -180`, green grass surrenders to sleet and frost. The sweeping bend at `x: 40, z: -330` is marked by Thermos Theo's burning pine-cone barrel—a welcome beacon for fleeing smugglers whose tires have lost every ounce of grip on the ice.

## What gnomes say

"If you hit Route North in summer tires, Theo's already pouring your broth before you spin out."
