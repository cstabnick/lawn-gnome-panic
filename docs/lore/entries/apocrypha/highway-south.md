The bustling southern arterial (`[0, 180]` to `[40, 570]`) carrying acorn trailers, Black Dew convoys, and Buck-Iron tractors through Cloverfield Farms.

## Field Notes & Tales

Fence-Post Frank watches every rig rolling past the welcome sign at `x: 0, z: 260`. Further south, the road curves past Granny Gnertrude's farmhouse and wide clover pastures—making the bend at `[-40, 320]` Turfton's favorite ambush spot for hijacking HOA fuel convoys.

## What gnomes say

"Slow down for tractors, speed up for HOA vans."

## Loose threads

How many Dew-Hauler axles lie rusted in the ditch beside the `z: 320` bend.
