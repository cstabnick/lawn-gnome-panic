The shadowy western road (`[-180, 0]` to `[-570, -30]`) winding beneath Mossy Wood's pine canopy past Moss-Watcher Merle's tree root.

## Field Notes & Tales

Route West is the quietest highway in the yard, dappled in deep fern shade and littered with hollow acorns containing Unmowed Stopping Stone shards. Most city drivers turn back before `x: -470`, uneasy about the silence hanging near the boarded-up porch at the tree-line.
