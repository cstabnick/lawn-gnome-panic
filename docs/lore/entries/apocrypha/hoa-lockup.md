The HOA's cells under the Double Pot: a row of beige rooms with a bunk, a broom and a loudspeaker, entered by a door in the Double Pot Tunnel behind the tunnel watch. The paint is the standard HOA beige; the lock is the standard HOA lock; the broom is Cam's.

## What gnomes say

"Do not get arrested in Turfton. If you must, do not get sent to the Double Pot. It is warm, and everything is beige, and it is the only place in the yard where the coffee is free and nobody wants it."

"There is a penthouse above the tunnel. There is a lockup below it. They are the same building. I stopped asking."

## The truth

The Double Pot was built as the Even-Estates sales floor and the lockup is its other face (R-166, R-170). The cells are a row of narrow rooms off one passage; each holds a bunk and a beige wall; the speaker above each door is the building's voice and, in act IV, Vesper's. The tunnel watch changes at the hour. Turncoat Cam is held here in `m62-cams-cell` after Vesper cancels his charter: Cam sits on the bunk with his broom against the wall, nervous, talking fast, and gives the Combine's PA code and route. The player chooses to free him or leave him for the Watch; both give the code, and the epilogue line differs. Vesper's voice comes over the lockup speaker for one slow sentence.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (act IV): an interior entered through the Double Pot Tunnel (an `interior-visit` objective, E05); the story-row scenes are `cams-cell-intro`, `-mid` and `-outro`. The stub of `m62-cams-cell` places it as the row's writer chooses.
