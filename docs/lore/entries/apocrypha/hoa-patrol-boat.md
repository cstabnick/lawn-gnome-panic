The Hat Watch on water: a beige patrol boat with a light bar on the cabin roof and the HOA's crest painted over the
bow. It is sent out when a gnome on the water reaches three hats of heat.

## Field Notes & Tales

It is slower than the Gravy Boat and better armed, which is to say it carries two officers with spud-guns and a
loudhailer that only says "PLEASE REMAIN CALM AND STAY ON THE LAWN." The light bar flashes on the water the way it does
on the road, and gnomes on the canal can see it from the bridges.

## What gnomes say

"Three hats and the boat's coming. Hide in the reeds, the reeds don't have a permit."

## In the games

Launched when the Hat Watch reaches its top heat (three hats) on open water. It hunts the player's boat across the canal
and Loch Moss, and gives up when the heat cools down out of sight, the same as the road Watch (see
[hat-watch](hat-watch.md)).

Not moored anywhere; it only appears when the Hat Watch launches it. All five boats count toward the "Captain Clay" achievement: take the helm of every one and earn a Captain's Cap.
