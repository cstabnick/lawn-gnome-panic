The homeowners' association that Gnome Jesus blames, appeases and sends the gnomes to rob for.

## What they say

Gnome Jesus's sermons: "THE HOA DEMANDS SACRIFICE. STEAL FROM THE YARD!" and "THE HOA IS APPEASED! RECEIVE MY BLESSING AND A HIGHER QUOTA!"

## In the games

- The HOA exists only in sermons and one item description, the cursed truffle, which "whispers rude HOA complaints".
- No model or behaviour in the code belongs to the HOA.
