The Underspanners' tin-roofed workshop shed on the east side of the Underspan: one 22 by 16 metre room with a bench, a cradle that is empty for reasons nobody asks about, and a lift in the north-east corner that has been refused a permit six times and works every time.

## What gnomes say

"It is a shed on the outside and a conspiracy on the inside."

"Knock before you go in. Not for manners. There may be a test in progress."

## The truth

The shed stands at x 586 to 598, z -22 to -6 (5 m high), with its only door on the west face at (584.5, -14), toward the road. Inside, Hogweed's bench is at the middle-north; the Updraft (Mk. 7: "Mk. 1-6: ask Nettle") fills the north-east corner and a hole in the roof above it leads to the Roost; and the Dewdrop's cradle at the south-east corner carries a sign: "DEWDROP: PENDING (DO NOT ASK)". The walls are posters: "A RULE IS ONLY A PROTOTYPE NOBODY TESTED", "TODAY'S INVENTION: TOMORROW'S CITATION" and, on the west wall, a short list: "THINGS THAT STAYED UP: 1. the Span (barely) 2. Nettle (once)". The back door at the south opens onto the scrap heap.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: a walk-in interior (`underspan-workshop`) entered from the Underspan; Hogweed's bench takes the parts for `sq-underspan-scrap-run`; the Updraft starts `sq-underspan-verticality`. The Dewdrop cradle is empty until E18 puts it there.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** The price list by the door: "Fix a bell 2. Fix a hinge 3. Test something 0 (you pay in bruises)." A bell on the bench rings when the Updraft is honest.
- **Notices:** "Knock before you go in." "DEWDROP: PENDING (do not ask)." "Citations: please take one." A tally on the wall: "Days since the last test: a few."
- **Sayings and habits:** "Nothing stays up, but things stay up longer if you believe." Visitors are handed an umbrella before they are asked what they want. "Keep arms in umbrella" is the shop's only safety rule.
