A moss-padded hollow stump in Mossy Wood where Clara hosts midnight support-group meetings for porcelain gnomes escaped from the Tall House.

## Field Notes & Tales

Lined with soft dry ferns and spare velvet scraps to prevent accidental chips, the Hollow Oak Stump is a quiet haven far from Turfton's roaring engines. Inside, traumatized porcelain newcomers whisper over acorn tea, earnestly debating whether a kitchen toaster is a Tall-One altar.

## What gnomes say

"Knock twice, speak softly, and wipe your clay boots before stepping onto the moss."

## Loose threads

How many escaped porcelain gnomes are currently sheltered beneath its roots.
