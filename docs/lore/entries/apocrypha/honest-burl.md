Gnome salesman who runs [Burl's Forecourt](burls-forecourt.md) in Turfton. Dry, unhurried, and always closing a sale.

## Story: The Nickname

Burl is called Honest. Nobody can say why, and he will not explain it. Asked, he says "Ask me again after the sale," and then he sells you the mower anyway.

## What gnomes say

"Honest Burl never lies about a mower. He just won't tell you what it is."

"Ten more seconds and I'll have you signing for it."
