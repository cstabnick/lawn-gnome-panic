The water hazard of The Long Week at Hedgeworth Links: a pond on the dogleg, fished by a paddling pond mower.

## What gnomes say

"Nobody has ever swum in Hosepipe Pond. Nobody has ever been asked to."

"A ball in the pond is not lost. It is resting, and the mower will fetch it when it has finished its tea."

## The truth

The pond mower is Hedgeworth's cousin of the sea mower at Gnome Golf: it paddles out, finds the ball and brings it back to the shore. Basil Divot blames the moles for every splash.

## In the games

Built (batch 3, E15): a pond about 14 m across on The Long Week's dogleg, at about (-1250, 735). A ball that lands in it costs a stroke and is played again from where it was struck; the banner says a pond mower got it. The pond mower is a paddling mower (R-195).
