About 26 small camps in the empty country of every outer region, kept by Tuft & Tally licence holders: tents, shared camps round a fire, shacks, deer stands, duck blinds on loch and coast shores and tuft-drying racks. Each leaves a tin of acorns or a snack for the next walker.

## What gnomes say

"You leave the tin full for the next gnome. Nobody says so; everybody does."

"Never take the Thermos Stand's tea before noon. Teasel counts."

## The truth

The custom is older than the licence. Hazel Mattock's lodge only noticed the habit (R-207). Some camps have a hunter asleep by the fire; nobody wakes them, and the tin is refilled later. Examples: Teasel's Tent (one hunter, three kettles), The Thermos Stand (a shared camp round a fire, with one very good flask) and Damp Socks Camp (a duck blind, a washing line and no luck).

## In the games

Scattered through the outer regions of `games/gnome-theft-auto`.
