Husky-Blade Tower is a plain blue glass block with a blue glass pyramid on top, in Turfton Park & Arts. It stands across the block from Razor-Back Tower. On paper it was built one storey shorter than its rival; in fact the pyramid makes it one pyramid taller.

## What gnomes say

"Husky-Blade's storeys are all on the plans. The pyramid is not on the plans, which is why it is taller."

"The pyramid is not for the view. Razor-Back's boardroom cannot see the pyramid, and that was the point."

## In the games

It stands on the x = -20, z = -90 block of Turfton Park & Arts, a blue glass box with the pyramid crowning it. Its block shape and pyramid cap are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
