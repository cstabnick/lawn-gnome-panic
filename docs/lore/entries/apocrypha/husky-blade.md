Blue-and-silver two-stroke power equipment maker producing Push-Mower Bikes, hedge-trimmers, and illegal high-decibel exhaust kits.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"Loud Enough To Wake The Tall Ones."* Husky-Blade doesn't bother pretending to be refined. Their Push-Mower Bikes are cheap, light, rattle at idle like a tin can full of pebbles, and can squeeze through alleyways where a Ride-On would wedge tight.

## Consumer Reports

Every Husky-Blade bike sold in Turfton comes with an unofficial packet of illegal two-stroke jets tucked under the seat cushion.

## Loose threads

Whether a chorus of fifty Husky-Blade exhausts could actually wake someone inside the Tall House.
