The player's nosy neighbour in flat 3A at Saucer Court: a retired lollipop-crossing warden who now watches the stairwell and comments on everyone who uses it.

## What gnomes say

"Forty years she stopped the traffic. Now she stops the stairs."

"If Hyssop hasn't remarked on your boots, you haven't got any."

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Home: Saucer Court flat 3A (apartment, acknowledged). Work: retired crossing warden; watches the stairwell. Appears in #2 `m02-roof-on-credit` (first meeting), #39 `m39-couch-surfing` (suspicious of Pip; her "NO LODGERS" note, W09), #68 `m68-lights-out` and #69 `m69-the-combine-wakes`. Epic E10 places her: at her window by the stairwell door, under her posted rules (wipe feet, no running, no lodgers, I am watching).
