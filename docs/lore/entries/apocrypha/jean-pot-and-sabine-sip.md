Two café philosophers on Teacup Row who have shared a single espresso since Tuesday.

## What gnomes say

"The Sips are on their coffee. It was a double, once, and they have made it a lifestyle."

"Jean-Pot says life is a queue. Sabine says it is a table. They have been at the table for a week."

## The truth

They are married, and the espresso is not what they are sharing. They take turns to say something profound and then argue over whose it was. The espresso has long since stopped being hot and has become a principle.

## In the games

A terrace table on Teacup Row. They comment on the player's route and each other's logic.
