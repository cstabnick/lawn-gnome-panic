Unflappable 24-hour corner shopkeeper at `x: -60, z: 60`, wearing a faded paper-cup visor and selling lukewarm Acorn-Cola, spark plugs, and dandelion-silk cigarettes.

## Story: Sweeping Through the Sirens

John has zero interest in turf wars, HOA politics, or ancient Fescue prophecies. Whether a 3-Hat Watch shootout is shattering flowerpots outside his window or the Grand Combine is rumbling down `x = 0`, John calmly sweeps clay dust off his doormat and makes sure Asphalt's saucer is topped up.

When Clay-Mart tried to open a superstore across the street, John didn't hire lawyers. He just nodded once—and Clay-Mart's delivery snail mysteriously vanished into the Under-Deck for three weeks.
