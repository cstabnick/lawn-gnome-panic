Turfton Zoning Board's lone loud voice fighting HOA foreclosures, famous for burying Vesper Thorne's lawyers under 400-page Grass Height Impact Studies.

## Story: The Friday 4:59 PM Counter-Attack

Juniper is Reginald Vance's estranged sister, though they share the exact same stubborn jaw and affinity for heavy stationery. While Reginald weaponizes Municipal Code §404-B to fine flowerpots, Juniper spends her nights in City Hall's basement digging up century-old easement loopholes—including a clause stating any dandelion taller than three inches qualifies as "historic shade."

She drinks cold Acorn-Cola straight from the can, keeps a spare trowel in her briefcase, and isn't above hiring a gnormal street driver to tail the Mayor when paperwork isn't fast enough.
