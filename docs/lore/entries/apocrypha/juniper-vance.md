Turfton Zoning Board's lone loud voice fighting HOA foreclosures, famous for burying Vesper Thorne's lawyers under 400-page Grass Height Impact Studies.

## Story: The Friday 4:59 PM Counter-Attack

Juniper is Reginald Vance's sister and dearest enemy (they never look at each other while insulting each other), and they share the exact same stubborn jaw and affinity for heavy stationery. While Reginald weaponizes Municipal Code §404-B to fine flowerpots, Juniper spends her nights in City Hall's basement digging up century-old easement loopholes—including a clause stating any dandelion taller than three inches qualifies as "historic shade."

She drinks cold Acorn-Cola straight from the can, keeps a spare trowel in her briefcase, and isn't above hiring a gnormal street driver to tail the Mayor when paperwork isn't fast enough.

## What gnomes say

"The Trowel is the only reason half of downtown hasn't been paved over with beige astroturf."

## The truth
Juniper sits on the Zoning Board and is the paper-and-law side of the resistance. She wants to beat the HOA through paperwork, faster, and fears needing a street driver and being the sister who stayed. Home: a narrow terrace on Kerb Row (Bodega's street); work: City Hall. Voice: brisk, formal, full sentences, devastating one-liners; she insults her brother by agreeing with him ("I have never known my brother to be wrong. That is how I know."); she never hurries or runs. Props: briefcase and cold Acorn-Cola.

Arc: hires the player for the Even-Estates permits (#7) and the calipers (#12) and rezones Vesper's condemned blocks as Founders' Green (#15); brings the permits to the Tattler (#28) and is a party guest (#34); a voice on the phone at the tasting (#50); held at City Hall (#67), rescued, and driven home on the last night tour (#68). The photograph in townhouse 404 shows her and Reginald at ten and eight, signed "J. and R." (R-229); it is never a joke.
