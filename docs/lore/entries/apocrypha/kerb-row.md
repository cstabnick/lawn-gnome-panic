Juniper Vance's narrow terrace of identical houses in Turfton. Her home plate reads "J. VANCE"; outside stands a council bike with a parking ticket from her own council.

## What gnomes say

"She writes the tickets and gets them. Fair is fair."

"Kerb Row is the only street where the bins go out on time, out of fear."

## In the games

Built by E10 (batch 3): Juniper's home. A stop in `city-kerbside-rounds` and `ch1-two-coat-tail`. Plate on the map once the player has stood at the door (R-180).

Whether the ticket on the bike has ever been paid.
