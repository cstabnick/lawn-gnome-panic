A cork notice-board and a folding desk on the pavement of the x = 0 road, just south of the Central Bus Stop. Every side job in Turfton is pinned to the board, and Dot Pennywort punches a ticket for each one she hands out. The Exchange is the place a newcomer steps off the bus and first hears what there is to do.

## Character

Cheerful, scruffy and always a little bit in the way of the pavement. The board is crowded with cards in six hat colours, and the desk always has a cup of something that is still warm. Nobody is in charge of the Exchange except Dot, and Dot is not in charge of anything except the tickets.

## Landmarks

- The cork board, (-9, 45): every open job pinned to it, the newest on top.
- The folding desk, beside the board: Dot's ticket book, the stamp and a jar of acorns she does not touch.
- The Central Bus Stop, a short walk north at (-6, 14): where every gnome arrives, and where the taxi always comes back to.

## Factions

- Dot's clients, who hand their jobs out at her desk: Neon-Moth Nina, Hedda Galley, Big Wrench Grout and Pip Potsworth.
- Everybody else who pins a card: the board is open to any gnome who has a job and a ticket.

## What gnomes say

"Dot doesn't take a cut. She takes your name, so she knows where to send the next one."

"If it's not on Dot's board, it's not a job. It's gossip."

## Local slang

- "kerb-sitting": waiting at the Exchange for work to be pinned up. See `downtown-turfton`.

## In the games

The Exchange is the city's hub. In the game, giver `kerb-exchange` in `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/quests/defs.ts` is Dot, and her five contracts are in `src/quests/cityQuests.ts`: Kerbside Rounds, Impound Run, Glow-Band Express, Penthouse Exclusive and The Skyway Job. Intros are staged at the Exchange itself. The phone app "Kerbside Exchange" lists every open side job in and around Turfton, so the player reads the board from the phone as well as from the pavement.

The Exchange does not replace the other givers. Big Lou, Shelldon, Clara, Granny Gnertrude and the rest still give their own jobs where they live.

## Loose threads

Who pinned the very first card. The board has been full for as long as anyone has been looking, and Dot will not say.
