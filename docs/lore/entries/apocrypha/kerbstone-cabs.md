Mabel Kerbstone's cab company. The yellow Turfton Taxi is her own cab; Kerbstone Cabs also hires any gnome with a spare seat by the shift.

## Field Notes

The rank is a yellow sentry booth near the Central Bus Stop with a stool inside and a CABS light on the roof. A gnome signs on, gets a little checkered roof light for whatever they drive, and waits. Fares hail at the kerb with one arm up: plain ones, gnomes in a hurry, nervous ones who bruise like teacups, chatty ones, and now and then a celebrity in sunglasses who wants no photos. Mabel keeps the meter. The Acorns go into a biscuit tin at the rank and are paid out at the end of every fare, which answers the old question of where the taxi's money goes.

## What gnomes say

"Kerbstone, dear? Just put your arm up." A gnome who drives badly hears about it from Mabel before the fare has finished complaining.

## In the games

The Taxi Driver job (phone Jobs app, or talk to the dispatcher at the rank). Any car, cart or bike with a passenger seat. A fare waits at a kerb within about 200 m, rides to a named place, and pays a base fare plus a tip for a smooth, quick ride (one to five stars). Harsh braking, hard corners, crashes and running gnomes over cost stars. Online, another player can call a cab from their phone and ride as your fare; the company covers it and adds 25 Acorns. Levels 1 to 6 bring bigger tips, more fares and long hauls to Pebblewick.
