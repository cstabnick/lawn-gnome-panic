The crushing 5,000-Acorn firing-and-glazing balance slapped onto every newcomer stepping off the Snail-Coach—usually bought up immediately by Clay-Mart Collections.

## How It Works

Whether you arrived from Fescue or walked straight out of a Clay-Mart garden crate, somebody claims you owe five thousand acorns for the clay in your boots. Paying it down pushes half of Turfton's rookies into boosting Ride-Ons, hauling Dew, or knocking on The Mulch Club's door.

## What gnomes say

"You're born fired, glazed, and five grand in the hole."
