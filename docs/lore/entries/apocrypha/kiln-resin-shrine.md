An abandoned, mysteriously warm stone alcove tucked behind Frostbite Fells' northernmost snowbank where raw Kiln-Resin still drips slowly from the ceiling.

## Inside & Stories

Even in the dead of a tundra squall, snow melts three feet from the shrine's threshold. Early Fescue travelers built it to patch frost-cracked boots; today, Carburettors and outlaws use it to warm frozen carburetors and glue shattered clay arms back onto their friends.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Carved into the warm stone lintel: *"FIRE REMEMBERS WHAT ICE FORGETS."*
