Teal-and-white ceramic casualty insurer plastered across Turfton billboards, famous for covering almost nothing in its microscopic fine print.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"We Cover Chips, Cracks, And 'Acts Of Mower.'"* Glenda "Matte-Finish" Clay winks next to their logo on every bus-stop bench. Meanwhile, the billboard fine print explicitly excludes porcelain, Fort Sandpit trespassing, Crop Duster landings, and *"anything involving Pip Potsworth."*

## Consumer Reports

Every Glaze Clinic Hospital receipt arrives pre-stamped with *KILN-SHIELD: CLAIM DENIED* in cheerful teal ink.

## Loose threads

Whether anyone in Turfton's history has ever successfully collected a Kiln-Shield payout.
