The Kiln-Shield billboards' tower of Turfton Park & Arts: a cream art-deco block that steps in near the top, with teal ribs and a ring of pale fins on its crown. Kiln-Shield Insurance's billboards cover the rest of it, and a cage of scaffolding has climbed one side for years.

## What gnomes say

"The scaffolding is for the repairs. The repairs are for the claim. The claim is still pending." Kiln-Shield says it is a long process.

"You can see the Thorne billboard from the Plaza's top floor. You can also see the Plaza's billboards from the Thorne billboard. Nobody has been able to say which one is being looked at."

## In the games

It stands at the west edge of Turfton Park & Arts, across the block from the Thorne rooftop billboard that sits between it and its neighbour. Its look is set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`: the stepped cream silhouette, the teal ribs, the fin crown, and the scaffolding cage on one flank.
