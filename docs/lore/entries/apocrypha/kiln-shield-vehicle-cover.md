Vehicle cover sold by [Kiln-Shield Insurance](kiln-shield-insurance.md), the insurer famous for denying hospital claims. The fine print is still the same, but this cover pays out.

## Story: The Surprise Payout

Kiln-Shield sells the cover for a fee of 10 % of the vehicle's value. When a covered vehicle is wrecked, the company pays out, less a 25 % deductible. It pays once per vehicle per ten minutes, and the adjuster will not say why the window is ten minutes.
