A commune of healers and glazers who opened the Chapel Infirmary to anyone who is hurt, bitten or not, and who try to cure the moss.

## What they are

The Kiln Sisters hold the nave of the Bell Chapel, where the quarantine pews are. They retook the chapel with fire and patience and opened an infirmary there. Their founder is Old Mother Comfrey Kilnworth, a commune-minded gnome of empathy who believes the moss is a sickness and can be cured. Their hat colour is bone white (`#e8e2d0`), the colour of fired glaze.

## How they live

- **Sharing:** everything is shared. A gnome who has something gives it to the common kiln.
- **Strangers:** welcomed at the door of the infirmary, with no questions asked until they are fed.
- **The bitten:** nursed in the quarantine pews, never cast out.
- **Leadership:** a council of sisters.
- **Violence:** never first.

They glaze, scrape moss, make medicine, and are the only ones who try to cure the moss. Their firing is a grim trade: a gnome spread with grey moss can be fired for a deep crack, and that crack may end the infection. Not every gnome lives through it.

## What they want

A cure for the moss. The Kiln Sisters keep samples from every stage of the infection, glaze recipes and the books of the Vestry. A cure would change the rule for every gnome in the world, and the Sisters would be the ones who found it.

## Loose threads

Whether the cure can be made at all, or whether the firing only trades one kind of loss for another. Why the Sisters' founder believed the moss could be beaten when everyone else gave up.
