The recreation ground in the west of Turfton, named after the old kilns that once stood on the far side of the fence.
It is a five-a-side pitch, a playground and a skate corner, all inside the same railings, and the Hat Watch has agreed
not to notice the third one.

## What gnomes say

"The Rec is where the kids learn to lose properly." Kickabouts here end in arguments, arguments end in a game of
tag, and the tag ends at the skate corner, where nobody has been hurt badly enough to need the clinic.

## Inside & Stories

- The pitch, marked with painted lines and two goals. The goals are the only ones in Turfton with nets, which the
  kickabout gnomes insist is a sign of something.
- A playground: a swing set, a play slide with a ladder, a climbing frame and a see-saw on a pavement floor.
- The skate corner: two quarter-pipes and a grind rail on a pavement floor, with graffiti tags on the ground. The
  tags are not signed. Everybody knows who did them.
- Benches face the pitch from either side, and lamps light the paths after the Watch has gone home.
- A noticeboard by the north railings holds the pitch booking sheet, which has never once been filled in on time.

## In the games

Kilnside Rec is the park at block centre (-150, -30). Players can sit on the benches facing the pitch. The skate
corner is scenery only: the quarter-pipes are props, not a ramp, so nobody gets to drive off them.

## Loose threads

Who keeps moving the goals a metre closer together every week, and whether the kickabout gnomes know it.
