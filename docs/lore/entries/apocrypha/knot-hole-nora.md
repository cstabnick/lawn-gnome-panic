Eccentric lookout perched on an overturned thimble at the Fence-Line Overlook, peering through a knot-hole into the Neighbour's Lawn with a brass spyglass.

## Journal: Beyond the Boards (Scrap Paper)

> *Thursday: Saw a pink plastic bird taller than Thorne Tower. Still hasn't blinked.*  
> *Saturday: Something rolled past the knot-hole that made my teeth hum. Twice the size of a Combine, painted bright yellow. Thank the Kiln for oak planks.*  
> *Sunday: Kid from Turfton asked what's out there. Told him nothing. Let them worry about the HOA.*

Nora refuses to come back into town, surviving on rainwater and acorns tossed up by curious racers who make the pilgrimage to `x = -600`.

## What gnomes say

"They've got pink flamingos over there. Huge ones. Don't tell Turfton."

## The truth
Nora is the Fence-Line lookout who reports on the neighbour's yard and belongs to no one. She wants to keep Turfton from worrying, and fears the day something looks back. Secret: what she saw with the yellow thing, and whether it was a bicycle wheel (never answered). Home: the Knot-Hole; work: the lookout at the Fence-Line Overlook. Racers bring her acorns; Pip's one visit is her only company; Sorrel records the rain at her gap. Voice: whispered, clipped, reporting like a diary entry with a pause between observations; she opens with the day of the week ("Thursday. Pink bird. Taller than the tower." / "Nothing out there. ...Nothing.").

Arc: side quests only in every act; she is the lookout who never leaves her post. Her knot-hole is visited as a place in #26 (the knot-hole with Pip and Sorrel); she does not speak there. She has no CAST preset or intro card: she is a side-quest voice.
