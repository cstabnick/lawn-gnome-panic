Eccentric lookout perched on an overturned thimble at the Fence-Line Overlook, peering through a knot-hole into the Neighbour's Lawn with a brass spyglass.

## Journal: Beyond the Boards (Scrap Paper)

> *Thursday: Saw a pink plastic bird taller than Thorne Tower. Still hasn't blinked.*  
> *Saturday: Something rolled past the knot-hole that made my teeth hum. Twice the size of a Combine, painted bright yellow. Thank the Kiln for oak planks.*  
> *Sunday: Kid from Turfton asked what's out there. Told him nothing. Let them worry about the HOA.*

Nora refuses to come back into town, surviving on rainwater and acorns tossed up by curious racers who make the pilgrimage to `x = -600`.

## What gnomes say
