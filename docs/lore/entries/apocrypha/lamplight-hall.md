The east hall of Loamdeep, kept by the Delves: racks of lamps and wicks and tapers, and a wall where every lamp lit that week is counted in tally marks. It is the brightest hall and the one that smells of oil and thanks.

## What gnomes say

"They keep a hundred lamps and one sign: a lamp lit is a promise kept."

"Moles are afraid of light, they say. Ask Mistress Delve how many lamps she has."

## The truth

Lamplight is 20 by 16 metres of local floor (x 30 to 50, z 12 to 28), at the east end of the east gallery. Its signs read "LAMPLIGHT HALL / Keepers of the Lamps / Mistress Delve presiding", "A LAMP LIT IS A PROMISE KEPT. A LAMP UNLIT IS A NOTE TO SELF." and "LAMPS LIT THIS WEEK: |||| |||| ||". The taper for the Lamplighter job is taken at (46, 24). The Delves light three tunnels (the west rise, the east rise and the Mother's north tunnel), four lamps each, and when all twelve are burning the Burrowfolk let a mole come up from under the north hall and play.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the east hall of the Loamdeep interior; Lamplighter (`mole-jobs`) and `sq-loamdeep-lamps` start here. Mistress Delve keeps it.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** Tapers by the dozen, a lamp-oil dipper and a small jar of wicks. A lit lamp in the window is a free supper somewhere in the burrows; an unlit one is a "note to self".
- **Notices:** "A LAMP LIT IS A PROMISE KEPT. A LAMP UNLIT IS A NOTE TO SELF." A tally wall: "LAMPS LIT THIS WEEK: |||| |||| ||". A chalk rule: "Do not light a lamp you are not prepared to be seen by."
- **Sayings and habits:** "One lamp, then the next." Delves count lamps aloud, quietly, as other folk breathe.
