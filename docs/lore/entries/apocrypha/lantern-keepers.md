A council settlement of ordered, lawful gnomes who live in the niches of the Columbarium and keep the lanterns lit for the dead.

## What they are

The Lantern Keepers hold the High Niches (rows 3 to 6) of the Columbarium, a city cut into the wall of niches with rope ladders and pulleys. They are picky about who comes up, and the ladders are the only way in. Their founder is Sexton Yarrow Gravelpot, who is ordered, open and a builder, and whose line still keeps the Keeping Rolls. Their hat colour is gold (`#d9a441`), the colour of the lanterns.

## How they live

- **Sharing:** a tithe. Every gnome gives a share of what they find to the council, and the council keeps the stores.
- **Strangers:** questioned at the ladder foot before they are let up.
- **The bitten:** nursed and watched, never cast out at once.
- **Leadership:** a council, chosen by the gnomes who live in the niches.
- **Violence:** never first. They fight when they are attacked, and not before.

They rent storage niches, teach skills, and run the rose market. A gnome who wants safe sleep and a roof will pay the Keepers for both.

## What they want

To relight Lantern Night: the grave lanterns of the Old Rows, the Sexton's line, and a peace between three or more factions. A yearly truce that the chronicle remembers would be the Keepers' proudest thing.

## Loose threads

Whether the Sexton's line is still alive, and who lights the lanterns if it is not. Whether the rose market is the Keepers' or the Columbarium's.
