A busker who plays the guitar on the bandstand at Bandstand Green most afternoons: a thin, cheerful gnome with a
faded green hat and a guitar case with more stickers than strings.

## What gnomes say

"Every song's a bit of a lie if you play it right." Lark takes requests and then plays something else, and the
townsgnomes say it is the only time the bandstand has ever been listened to all the way through.

## Inside & Stories

Lark has played the same four chords in the same order for about nine years, and nobody has complained. The case is
open on the grass, and the Acorns in it are for the Watch, the stallholders and anyone who needs a tune more than a
coin.

## Loose threads

Whether Lark knows any other chords, and what happened to the fifth one.
