One dandelion, alone in a ring of stones in the Long Meadow at (-600, -1660), with a log bench five metres south of it facing it.

## What gnomes say

"Do not pick it. Do not even think about blowing on it."

## The truth

Every other dandelion in the yard was weeded, mown or sprayed long ago. The rangers guard this one, and gnomes sit on the bench and look at it for as long as it takes. It is the only plant on the Unmown checklist. A rusty ride-on mower stands nearby with a one-metre mown stripe behind it, as if it tried once and gave up.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the plant, bench and ring of stones in the Long Meadow; the checklist's plant entry (`dandelion`). Ticking every checklist entry earns the Unmown Checklist achievement.

## Loose threads

Whether it will ever go to seed, and who is allowed to blow.
