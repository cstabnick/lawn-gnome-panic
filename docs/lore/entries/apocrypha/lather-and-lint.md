The laundrette by Drainpipe Court, under Swish Trowelly's flat. The machines are loud, the drying line is always full, and the tumble-dryer hums the same note as the court's drainpipe.

## What gnomes say

"Swish does his washing at Lather & Lint, so he can say he's been to the laundrette."

"Put your whites in before the game. Put your shorts in after."

## In the games

A laundrette by Drainpipe Court, under Swish Trowelly's flat (hidden). It is where the court's regulars go for clean kit.
