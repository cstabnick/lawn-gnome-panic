The unseen morning voice of 99.3 The Blade. A name that has been passed from gnome to gnome, and never once been
shown to anyone.

## What gnomes say

"You can't see me. That's the deal." Lawn-Dart is the only presenter in Turfton who does not mind being asked who
they are, because the answer is always the same: "I'm the morning."

## History

Lawn-Dart broadcasts from a hollowed-out birdbath somewhere downtown, which the Blade's engineers have never located
and do not look for. The name was the on-air name of the first morning voice, and was kept when that voice left. It
has been kept each time since. Nobody agrees on how many people have been Lawn-Dart, or whether the name was ever a
person rather than a slot on the schedule.

The morning show is the one with the best news. A scanner in the birdbath listens to the Hat Watch's dispatch, so
the morning hears about a cruiser on the Greenhouse Strip three minutes before the Strip does. The Watch has asked
the Blade to stop. The Blade has asked the Watch to stop being so loud on its channel.

## Quirks

- Lawn-Dart always starts by saying that you cannot see them.
- Lawn-Dart's tea is acorn tea, and the tea is mentioned in every show.
- Lawn-Dart never gives a name for a Hat Watch officer, only a kerb.

## In the games

- The Blade's banter, in Lawn-Dart's own lines (`radio/content.ts`, speaker `LAWN-DART`). Lawn-Dart's lines are
  part of the Blade's mix, not a station of their own.
