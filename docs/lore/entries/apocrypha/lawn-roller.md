A garden roller made into a tiny steamroller by somebody who was told lawns must be kept to Code and took the instruction personally.

## Field Notes

The Lawn Roller is the slowest thing in Turfton that is allowed on the road. Its top speed is 7 units an hour, so it moves at walking pace and then slightly less, with a loud engine that sounds as if it is thinking about it. It turns up now and then in traffic, flattening anything that does not get out of its way: kerb stones, wheelie bins, the odd Hat Watch cone, and once, unfortunately, a Clay-Mart flamingo.

The HOA's own paperwork calls this "lawn maintenance equipment compliant with Covenant 4(b)". The HOA would like it back.

## What gnomes say

"Don't overtake the Lawn Roller. You will not overtake the Lawn Roller. Go round the block."
