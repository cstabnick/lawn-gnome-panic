A comedy cellar off the Grand Platz in Pardonlin: a brick wall, one mic, ten seats. The audience comes to judge, not to laugh, and the room has never had a laugh on record. The market provides tomatoes, for cultural reasons.

## What gnomes say

"Ten seats, ten verdicts."

"Not a laugh, but a very thoughtful silence."

## In the games

Planned (E24). The comedy job: the player takes the mic at Le Petit Groan. See R-195 for how the room reacts.
