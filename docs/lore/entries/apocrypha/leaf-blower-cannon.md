Civilian Razor-Back high-velocity air cannon modified to launch ragdoll pedestrians, Hat Watch cruisers, and loose acorns halfway down the block.

## Field Notes & Stories

Zero lethal gore, maximum aerodynamic comedy. A single blast from the Leaf-Blower Cannon sends Reginald Vance's brass calipers sailing into the upper bleachers and clears an entire sidewalk of pursuing officers without leaving a single scratch on their glaze—until they land in a hedge.
