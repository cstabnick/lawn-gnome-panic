The Leaning Fizz is a pale yellow tower of balconies on the Greenhouse Strip. Each floor sits a little further east than the one under it, so the whole block leans, and a yellow sail fin sits on top.

## What gnomes say

"Dandelion Fizz says the Fizz was built leaning. It was built leaning on purpose, and it has been leaning on purpose ever since."

"The Greenhouse Strip says the pots settled. The pots have settled in the same direction as the Fizz, which is either a coincidence or a very good sales pitch."

## In the games

It stands on the x = 100, z = 30 block of the Greenhouse Strip. Its balconies step east floor by floor and the yellow sail fin tops it off. The lean and the fin are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
