The building firm that raises Thorne Even-Estates. Beige hoardings, a spirit level in every hat band, a night watch that does not sleep, and a motto painted on the cranes: "Level Is Kind."

## What gnomes say

"Levelright. They'll level your garden and tell you it was always flat."

"Their watchmen keep the best-measured patrols in Turfton. You can set a clock by them."

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Foreman Chard Levelright. Appears at the fringe sites: #7 `m07-even-estates` (permits and night watch), #15 `ch1-founders-boot` p2 (night chase), #69 `m69-the-combine-wakes`. Hoardings and cranes: world change W02.
