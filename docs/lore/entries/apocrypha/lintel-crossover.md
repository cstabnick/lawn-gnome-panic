Cardoon Trowelly's partner on court. Lintel has more fakes than shots and is very good at both kinds of pass that never happen.

## What gnomes say

"Lintel's first move is a fake. His second move is also a fake. Nobody has seen the third."

"Don't guard the ball. Guard the fake."

## In the games

Plays on the other side in Drainpipe Court pickup games, with Cardoon, against the player and Pansy. Stands by the south sideline of the court (ratings: fast, poor three-point, good defence). Pickup games against the regulars put him on the opposing team with Swish.

## Loose threads

Whether Lintel has ever taken a shot that was not a fake.
