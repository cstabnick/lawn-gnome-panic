The sketcher on the Unmown trail: a child gnome in a golden hat who has drawn the same view eleven times and says it looks different every time, which her grandfather says is how you know it is real.

## What gnomes say

"I have drawn it eleven times. It looks different every time. Grandpa says that is how you know it is real."

## The truth

She sits on the ground with her pad at the edge of Reedmere. She is not Fennel Ostler of Capwood or Fennel Gripwax of Turfton, who are grown gnomes and do not draw.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E15): "sit-ground" idle near Reedmere.
