The child on the carousel: hot-pink hat, yellow shirt, freckles, no beard, and a firm grasp of the word "again". Not Pip Pearsley or Pip Tussock (R-235).

## What gnomes say

"Again. Again. Again. Again. Again."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (FX11, rulings R-244 and R-249): at the carousel at (30.4, 1121.2), standing on one leg. Look: A pink hat, a yellow shirt and freckles.

## Loose threads

Whether the carousel ever stops, from her side.
