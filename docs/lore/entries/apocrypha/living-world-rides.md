Three small civilian vehicles for the quiet corners of the map: the **Unmown Ranger Buggy**, the **Snail-Shell Camper** and the **Ninety-Nine Cone Van**. None of them is in a hurry.

## What gnomes say

"A weekend away is mostly the getting there, slowly."

## The truth

The **Unmown Ranger Buggy** is the rangers' open two-seat runabout (top speed 21), nippy enough for the trail loop. The **Snail-Shell Camper** is a camper built in a giant snail shell: two seats, slow (top speed 17), wide to turn. The **Ninety-Nine Cone Van** is the ice-cream van of Dunelow's trade: a driver's seat only, because the freezer fills the back, and a serving hatch; ninety-eight of its flavours are vanilla.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: vehicle kinds `ranger-buggy`, `shell-camper` and `cone-van`, parked in the quiet places they belong to; driving each earns Weekend Away.
