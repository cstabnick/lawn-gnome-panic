The Burrowfolk are five families under one roof, each keeping a hall and a trade: the Tumblesoils keep Loamdeep, the Tamps the roots, the Delves the lamps, the Sifts the post and the Crumbs the hearth, the grubs and the digging. The sixth hall, Under-Chorus, belongs to nobody and is kept by whoever is humming. They are not "the mole clans" of Fescue, and they would be polite about the question.

## What gnomes say

"Ask a mole who they are and you get a surname and a hall. Ask what they do and you get the hall again."

"Five families, six halls. The sixth one is for dancing. I did not ask who owns it."

## The truth

The families are named for what a mole does, as gnomes' surnames are for soil and tools. Each keeps one hall, a keeper, and a job gnomes can help with:

- **The Tumblesoils** (the Mother's Hall): keep Loamdeep itself. Old Mother Tumblesoil decides when the halls help, and every request gets her one answer, "we dig where we are asked, and nobody ever asks". They are the oldest family and the least in a hurry.
- **The Tamps** (Rootway Hall): store, sort by smell and send on every root. Old Tamp is the keeper. Their job is **Root Run**.
- **The Delves** (Lamplight Hall): keep the lamps along the three lamp tunnels and count the tapers back in. Mistress Delve is the keeper; the small twins Pim and Pod are Delve cousins, and the first to cheer a lit lamp. Their job is **Lamplighter**.
- **The Sifts** (Postern Hall): sort and carry the post. Mister Sift is the postmaster and young Nubbin, a Sift cousin and the post runner, is the one who goes up to the surface. Their job is **Post Sorting**.
- **The Crumbs** (Hearth Hall): bake the grub bread and cook the supper, and **dig**: the dig faces are on Hearth Hall's north wall, because a mole digs where the grubs are and the Crumbs have the grubs. Auntie Crumb is the keeper. Their job is **Dig a Nook**. DJ Moleman is a Crumb by birth, which is why Hearth Hall keeps a place at its table labelled "COUSIN (DJ)".
- **Under-Chorus Hall** has no clan. It was a hall for quiet and is now a hall for quiet that moves; the Burrowfolk say it is "everyone's, and mostly mine", and nobody argues.

The families marry across halls, trade favours without keeping count, and agree on three things: the surface is loud, the dark is only unpleasant alone, and a letter in the wrong pigeonhole is a small sorrow.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E17): the six halls and the four mole jobs (`mole-jobs`) are built in the Loamdeep interior (`societies/loamdeep/`), and the keepers say their lines in `folk.ts`. The clan names themselves are lore (R-NEW-2): the game only shows the halls, signs such as "LAMPLIGHT HALL: Keepers of the Lamps" and "COUSIN (DJ)", and the people.
