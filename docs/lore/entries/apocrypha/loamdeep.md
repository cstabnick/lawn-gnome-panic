The underground town of the Burrowfolk: halls and tunnels lit by lamps, reached through the Burrow Door. It is warm, tidy and quiet, and the ceilings are low for a gnome and just right for a mole.

## What gnomes say

"They live in halls. Real halls, with beams. They say the roots are the beams."

"Everyone who has been down says the same thing: it is the cleanest place they have been in."

## The truth

Loamdeep has six halls: the **Mother's Hall** (Old Mother Tumblesoil's), **Rootway Hall** (roots stored and sorted), **Lamplight Hall** (lamp-oil and the keepers), **Postern Hall** (the post), **Hearth Hall** (grubs, bread and supper) and **Under-Chorus Hall** (the hall with the humming floor, where DJ Moleman's cable comes up). Work is three kinds of mole job: carrying **roots** between the halls, lighting the **lamps** along the tunnels, and sorting and carrying the **post**.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). The mole halls are an interior cell (E05), reached from the Burrow Door. Quest 49 (`m49-loamdeep`) has the three jobs; 24 (`m24-the-burrow-door`) introduces it from outside; 60 (`m60-the-moles-dig`) sends diggers out of it. E17 builds the mole jobs from the three kinds above.
