The underground town of the Burrowfolk: halls and tunnels lit by lamps, reached through the Burrow Door. It is warm, tidy and quiet, and the ceilings are low for a gnome and just right for a mole.

## What gnomes say

"They live in halls. Real halls, with beams. They say the roots are the beams."

"Everyone who has been down says the same thing: it is the cleanest place they have been in."

## The truth

Loamdeep has six halls: the **Mother's Hall** (Old Mother Tumblesoil's), **Rootway Hall** (roots stored and sorted), **Lamplight Hall** (lamp-oil and the keepers), **Postern Hall** (the post), **Hearth Hall** (grubs, bread and supper) and **Under-Chorus Hall** (the hall with the humming floor, where DJ Moleman's cable comes up). Each hall belongs to a family (the Tumblesoils, Tamps, Delves, Sifts and Crumbs; Under-Chorus belongs to nobody): see `loamdeep-clans`, and the halls' own entries (`mothers-hall`, `rootway-hall`, `lamplight-hall`, `postern-hall`, `hearth-hall`, `under-chorus-hall`). The interior is 104 by 90 metres with a 4 m ceiling, entered by a tunnel on its south side, with the halls ranged either side of the entry and the Under-Chorus at the far north. Work is four kinds of mole job: carrying **roots** between the halls, lighting the **lamps** along the three lamp tunnels, sorting and carrying the **post**, and digging a **nook** at Hearth Hall's dig faces. Everything not a hall or a tunnel is packed earth.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** There are no shops. A hall gives what it keeps: roots by smell at Rootway, lamp oil at Lamplight, stamps at Postern, grub bread at Hearth. A mole pays an Acorn for a job well done and apologises for the amount.
- **Notices:** The mole jobs' board lists four: "ROOTS TO CARRY. LAMPS TO LIGHT. POST TO SORT. A NOOK TO DIG." Under it, in smaller writing: "All welcome. Even gnomes." Signs: "Wipe your paws (if it pleases you)", "Please mind the ceiling (it minds you)".
- **Sayings and habits:** "We dig where we are asked." "Nobody is behind in Loamdeep; they are simply further down." At the third bell everyone stops where they are for supper, and a gnome in the middle of a tunnel is handed a bowl.
- **Small history:** The first hall was dug as a cupboard for one jar. The jar is still there and the cupboard is the Mother's Hall.
