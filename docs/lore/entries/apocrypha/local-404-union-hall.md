A grease-stained brick hall near Turfton's industrial edge where Union Boss "Big Wrench" Grout holds court over mechanics and Dew-Haulers.

## Inside & Stories

Folding tables sag under cold Acorn-Cola cans, highway pothole maps, and strike pickets leaned against the radiator. When City Hall forgets who keeps the Black Dew flowing, Local 404 meets here to decide which roads stay paved and which ones swallow HOA enforcement vans.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Framed behind the podium: *"WE FIX THE MOWERS. WE HAUL THE DEW. DON'T TEST US."*

## In the games

- Location: behind Clay-Mart on the z = 180 road (R-152). Clay-Mart Industrial's Union Hall is where Local 404 meets.
- The story of The Skyway Job (R-152): the HOA took the strike-fund ledger from this hall after the Pour's strike. The
  player takes the Skyway, gets ahead of the HOA van at the east off-ramp, and brings the ledger home here.
