Riding fat orange tubes on ropes behind a boat on Loch Moss, for the fun of falling off.

## Field Notes

The tube rack stands on the shore by the Mossbank Boathouse: five Acorns a tube. A boat can tow up to three riders. Riders lean to swing wide of the wake and hop the waves for air; lean too hard at speed and the loch has you. The buoy run is six orange buoy gates in a ring on the open loch, a time trial for the boat with everyone on the ropes sharing the score. Tessie is said to watch.

## What gnomes say

"Hold on tight!" Shouted, mostly, by the gnome who just let go.

## In the games

The 🛟 Tubing phone app: driving a boat, it puts the tow line out; otherwise it hops you on behind a nearby boat (online). Alone, add NPC riders. Riders lean with the steering and hop with jump or the handbrake; points for distance, air time and big air.
