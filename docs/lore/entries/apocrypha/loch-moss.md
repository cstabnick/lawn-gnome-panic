A still highland loch in a glen at the north-west corner of Turfton's map, where the Everspring's upper river runs out of the Fells and the water lies quiet under the hills.

## What gnomes say

"Nothing in Loch Moss but the water and the heron. Mind the glen road; it turns to bog at the far end."

## The truth

The loch is the overgrown ornamental pond in the back corner of the garden, never named in game. Its rim is a straight pond-liner edge under the weed, and a stone heron lies tipped on its side in the reeds. Hints only: a gnome never says so.

## In the games

Reached by a spur road north from the far end of the west highway. A small dock sits on the shore, where the Watering-Can Steamer calls and where Driftwood camps on his raft. Out on the water, a low island with a nest in its reeds is where Tessie's eggs are kept, and an HOA dredger comes to drain the loch for a golf course. The Tessie side quest (`sq-tessie`) starts at the dock: follow the sightings by boat at dusk, drop the sonar buoys, escort the egg raft and ride Tessie back to the dock. See [tessie](tessie.md).

Rock glen walls close the loch on the north and west, a moor rises above it, and the shore is gravel. Tessie's island is at (-760, -720) and the dock at (-715, -564). A weir at the ravine outlet holds the loch level, so boats stay in the loch.

## Loose threads

What, if anything, lives at the bottom of the loch. Old gnomes in Turfton tell a story about something very large asleep under the water. Tessie is the answer the games give, but the rumour stays loose: whether she is the only one down there, and what else is asleep beside her, is never said.
