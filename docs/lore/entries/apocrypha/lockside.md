The southern edge of the Fringe, across the canal from the Cloverfield farms: polytunnels, barns and caravans, with
hedgerows and hay creeping up to the towpath and the old lock gates still rusting in the water.

## What gnomes say

"Lockside is where the canal stops being a canal and starts being a hobby." The lock-keepers say they have kept the
gates shut for so long that nobody remembers which way the water was meant to go.

## Inside & Stories

- Polytunnels, half-round and milky, with hoops along the top and a door frame at one end, and the tomatoes inside
  dressed in their own little cloths.
- Caravans on blocks by the towpath, with a striped side panel and a gas bottle at one end, parked nearer the water
  than the planners would like.
- Hay bales and a barn with its big doors open, and a tractor-shaped shadow somewhere in the back.
- Lean-to sheds with wheelbarrows, and a lock keeper's hut with the windows painted over.

## In the games

Lockside is the south edge of the Fringe, bordering Cloverfield Farms across the water. Its lots are the greenest in the
belt, so the hedgerows and long grass here are the Fringe's own green creeping in.

## Loose threads

Who opens the lock gates, and what they would do if they got the water going again.
