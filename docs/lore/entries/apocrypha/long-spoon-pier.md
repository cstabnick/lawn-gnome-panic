A pier made from a giant Tall-One spoon laid out into the sea, bowl end first. The Bathtub Queen moors at its far end, and Marram Teaspoon keeps a hut on it.

## What gnomes say

"Walk it slow. The bowl end sways."
"A spoon is a pier if you stand on it long enough."

## The truth

The spoon was found half-buried in the shore and hauled out by every gnome in Saltbucket on one tide. What the Tall One was stirring with it was never in question: nobody wants to know.

## In the games

Marram's hut stands on it (her home, spine section 6); Captain Bilge gives row 44 `m44-the-bathtub-queen` from the pier. Part of the boardwalk build in E01: planned (batch 3).

Built by E11 as a walkable deck from the boardwalk's east end (the spur, x 210 to 304) down the handle (x 296 to 304, z 1120 to 1236) to a bowl at z 1236..1254. The handle's rails and the bowl's ring are silver-white, to read as one spoon. At the head of the handle Captain Bilge stands at (300, 1168) by a chalk A-board of tour times (R-244); Reel Ruth fishes from the west rail at (297, 1176); Marram's hut is on the bowl's west side. Stop four of Pirate Tours is the bowl, at (300, 1250) (R-247). The Bathtub Queen moors south of the bowl at (300, 1268).

## Everyday

- Notices on the pier: "Walk it slow. The bowl end sways" at the spur; "PIRATE TOURS / Noon. Also noon. / Midnight is cargo. Not a tour." on the chalk board; "FISHING FROM THE HANDLE ONLY. THE BOWL IS NOT AVAILABLE."; "PLEASE DO NOT STIR".
- Habit: gnomes who walk to the end say "Stir it" at the rail, then look round to see who heard.
- Saying: "As long as the spoon" for anything that takes a while.
- Small history: when the spoon was hauled up the shore on one tide, the rope that held it was a tangle of every gnome's own washing line. Each line came back knotted to somebody else's.
- Marram's hut has a lamp that is lit at dusk; it is not a lighthouse and she asks nobody to treat it as one.
