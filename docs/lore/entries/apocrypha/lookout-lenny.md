The lifeguard on the coast: red hat, yellow shirt, glasses, no beard, on a chair above the sand with his eyes open mostly.

## What gnomes say

"Eyes open. Mostly. Wake me if something drowns."

## The truth

Lenny has never had to save anyone, because the water on the coast is the Puddle and the Puddle is calm; he is certain this is because he is there. The chair is the tallest object on the beach and he is very proud of it.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E02): sits in the lifeguard chair at about (56, 1172) on the coast, facing the sea, with a "guard-stand" idle; his bubble plays when you walk near.
