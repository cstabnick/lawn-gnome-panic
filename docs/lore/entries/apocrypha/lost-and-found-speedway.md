A 1.2 km circuit on the alkali pan north-east of the Desert Airfield, built from everything the Tall Ones lost on the night of the Great Spill. Free to drive any time, with a lap clock at the line.

## What gnomes say

"Everything out there fell out of the sky one night. We paved round it." Burdock Bellweather says this at the race desk, and most gnomes repeat it as if it were a fact about the weather.

"Why 'Lost & Found'? Because somebody very large lost all of it, and we found it." Nobody agrees on who the somebody was.

## The truth

The circuit runs clockwise. It climbs up the east side, takes a banked hairpin, and goes over two jumps: the Rake-Handle Hop and the Frisbee Crest. Grandstands are built from the Great Spill's heap: the Book Stack, the Sneaker, the Cooler, a watering can knocked on its side. A giant garden hose arches over the north straight. The pit wall is a ruler. The pit lane has its own Pay 'n' Glaze.

The circuit's surface is laid over the rubble, and its rises, banking and jumps are the heap's own lumps, smoothed over by Dandelion Fizz's paving crew.

## In the games

- Driven from the Fizz Cup race desk in the pit lane (`quests/races.ts`). Free practice runs any time from the grid.
- The circuit, its banking, jumps and lap timing are in `raceTrack/track.ts`. A lap clock sits at the start line.
- Rivalries are on the grid: Dandelion Fizz sponsors the series, and Acorn-Cola courts the Hat Watch with its own
  banners. The Carburettors and the Razor-Back and Buck-Iron works teams race their own machines in the Fizz Cup.
- The HOA wants the circuit shut for "unlicensed noise". It is the same rule Dock Hoewright was fined under.

## Loose threads

Who lost the sneaker, and whether they came back for it. The circuit's clockwise direction was never explained, and
the gnomes who ask about it are told that the pan is "just that way round".
