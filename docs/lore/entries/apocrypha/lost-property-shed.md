The HOA's lost-property desk, a beige shed outside Fort Sandpit's gate at about (398, 58), where two clerks turn a medal nobody lost into lost property.

## What gnomes say

"If it was never yours, it can't be lost. If it was never lost, it can't be found. But the shed will hold it for you."

"The shed has a tag for everything and a tag for the tags."

## The truth

Two clerks, Clerk Rubric and Clerk Docket, keep it in beige hats and beige shirts and no black. A tag rail runs along the back wall; each tag names an item, a date and "Reason: logged as lost". The tin star of Commander Diggs hangs there, claimed after fifteen years, from a tag that reads "Lost (by Diggs, which he never did)". The clerks run when approached, and leave the tags.

## In the games

Act IV, `m61-diggs-medal`: the player cracks the two clerks, takes the medal from the rail, brings it home in the clerks' own van. The Turfton FM bulletin after it reads "The Association lost-property desk by the fort gate reports one tin star claimed after fifteen years. The clerks ask the public to remain calm."

## Everyday

- **Shop:** none. Items go in; "claims" come out, with a form in triplicate, available in duplicate.
- **Notices:** "Lost property. Please do not find it here." "Claim tickets: one per item. Items: many." "This desk closes for lunch. Lunch has not been announced."
- **Habits:** the clerks stamp every tag twice, once for the entry and once for the stamp.
- **Sayings:** "We do not lose things. We receive them." "If it can be tagged, it can be kept."

## Loose threads

How long the shed has held the other tags on the rail, and what they are for.
