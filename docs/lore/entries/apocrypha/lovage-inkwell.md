Rootwell's dean, in Vesper Thorne's pocket: a smooth, well-meaning gnome with a gown, a ledger of late rent to the ants and a firm belief that the survey belongs to the university.

## What gnomes say

"The Dean is a very reasonable gnome. Ask him; he will tell you."
"Drove off the ramp once. Landed fine. Has not been the same about heights since."

## The truth

Home: the Dean's lodge on the quad. Work: Rootwell University. He signed the survey over to the HOA for a grant and has been explaining it ever since. He is not cruel; he is afraid of being found to have been wrong.

## In the games

Appears in row 45 `m45-rootwell-admissions` (the player avoids him) and row 46 `m46-starfall-at-the-anthill`. Planned (batch 3).
