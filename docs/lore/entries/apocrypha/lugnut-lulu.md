Captain of the Carburettors and a Fizz Cup racer: orange hat, black dress, purple tint on her paint. She laughs at losers and keeps the racing charter close.

## What gnomes say

"Carburettors run this town. Low and loud!"

"Nice hop. Shame about the rest."

## The truth

In the Fizz Cup's Low & Loud Derby she is the rival the player beats. In the story she is the loyal captain who refuses the HOA's racing charter.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Home: a converted car-wash bay at the Lost & Found Speedway (acknowledged). Work: the Carburettors; Rutbottom in summer. Wins a race before Cam in #5 `m05-sweepers-wages`; laughs at him in #30 `m30-cams-big-night`; gives #40 `m40-the-capwood-run` and #42 `m42-rutbottom-impound`; joins #64 `m64-all-hats` p3 "By Road"; the Carburettors ride with her in #41 `m41-pinwheel-ridge` and she drives support in the finale #70 (`ch5-even-cut`).
