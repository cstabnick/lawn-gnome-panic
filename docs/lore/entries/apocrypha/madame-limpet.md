Keeper of The Lucky Limpet: purple hat, mustard dress, glasses, and one fortune for one acorn, always the same one.

## What gnomes say

"Something will turn up. That will be one acorn."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (FX11, rulings R-244 and R-249): behind the Lucky Limpet counter at (93, 1109.5), looking about with great meaning. Look: A purple hat, a mustard-yellow dress and glasses.

## Loose threads

Whether she knows the fortune is always the same one, or has simply never been asked.
