Foreman of the Turfton Bin Depot. She has worn hi-vis since birth and knows every bin in Turfton by its lid. She takes her tea facing the Compost Heap and hands out the day's rounds from a whiteboard.

## What gnomes say

"Mags knows your lid."

"Never ask which one is her favourite bin."

## In the games

Planned (E25). Gives the player rounds at the Bin Depot.

## Loose threads

Her favourite lid, which she will not name.
