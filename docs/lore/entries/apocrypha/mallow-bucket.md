A youngster who watches the eastern dunes from the water tower, with a bucket for a hat.

## What gnomes say

"There's a combine in the dunes and it hums at three in the morning."

## The truth

Mallow has heard the hum at three every night for a year. She has not told the reeve, because she is not sure the reeve would believe her.

## In the games

Gives the player 'The Hum at Three' side quest from the water tower. The reward is Ratchet's Thanks.

## Loose threads

What answers when she knocks at night.
