The Grand Platz's mime, who is stuck in an invisible box and never speaks.

## What gnomes say

"He has been in that box since the fountain was a puddle. He is very good at it."

"Do not give him an acorn. He cannot get his hand out to take it."

## The truth

Marcel is not acting. Or he is, and the box is real to him, which comes to the same thing. He cannot be talked to; he answers in gestures, and the gestures are always clear and always slightly rude about your hat.

## In the games

On the Grand Platz, near the fountain. He never speaks: his lines are captions of what he is miming.

## Loose threads

What is outside the box. He is the only one who knows, and he is not saying.
