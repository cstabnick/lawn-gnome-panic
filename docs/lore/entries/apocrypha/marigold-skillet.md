The cook at The Dry Spoon: a sunburnt apron, a flour-dusted hat and a laugh you can hear across the square.

## What gnomes say

"Prickly pear pie, fresh from the oven. The oven is the sun."

## The truth

Marigold learned to cook in the open yard behind the Dry Spoon, and she thinks a pie that has been in the sun all afternoon tastes better than one from a real oven.

## In the games

Keeps the counter at The Dry Spoon, on the Pebblewick square.

## Loose threads

What she does on the days the sun does not come out.
