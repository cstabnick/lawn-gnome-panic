A birder on the Unmown trail: brown hat, green shirt, glasses, thrilled by any noise at all. She is not Marigold Skillet of the Dry Spoon.

## What gnomes say

"Shh. That is either a rare warbler or somebody's kettle. Either way, I am thrilled."

## The truth

She has a life list with 211 entries and four of them are kettles. Dunnock Pell of the Reedwatch Hide whispers hello when she passes.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E15): on the Unmown trail, near the Reedmere hide.
