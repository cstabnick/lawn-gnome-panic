Runs the Spokewheel and the boardwalk stalls: a brisk, warm gnome who counts everyone's change twice and everyone's mood once.

## What gnomes say

"Hat on, hands in, wait for the bell."
"Driftwood has a hut now. We do not mention the sharks."

## The truth

Home: a hut on Long Spoon Pier. Work: Saltbucket Boardwalk. She took the stalls over from a brother who left on a tide and did not come back, and does not discuss it. She runs the Spokewheel because someone has to remind everyone to come down.

## In the games

Appears in row 43 `m43-saltbucket-boardwalk` only: rides the Spokewheel with the player to spot HOA barges. Planned (batch 3). Her hut is on the coast site at Long Spoon Pier (E01).
