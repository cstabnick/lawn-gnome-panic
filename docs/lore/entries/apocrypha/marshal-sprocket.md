The marshal at the start of the Capwood Run: orange hat, all in black, arms crossed, with a clipboard that says everyone is a Carburettor today.

## What gnomes say

"Carburettors only. Everyone is a Carburettor today. Clipboard says so."

## The truth

Sprocket is not a Carburettor; he is the person the Carburettors hire when they want something to look official. His black outfit is borrowed from Lulu's spare kit, and he has never given it back.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E08): at the start marker of the Capwood Run, "cross-arms" idle.
