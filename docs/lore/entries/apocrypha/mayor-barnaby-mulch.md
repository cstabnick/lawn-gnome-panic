Turfton's grinning career politician, infamous for surviving three consecutive corruption scandals by getting a fresh coat of glossy paint and claiming it was a different glaze.

## Story: The Ribbon-Cutting Specialist

Barnaby Mulch has never built a road, tuned a mower, or settled a turf war, yet his smiling face appears on brass plaques across every block in Turfton. His political philosophy is simple: whichever faction brings the biggest ribbon and the shiniest scissors gets the photo-op.

Behind closed doors, he quietly leases downtown parking medians to Thorne Lawn & Order LLC in exchange for complimentary astroturf on City Hall's front steps—and keeps a standing appointment at Pay 'n' Glaze whenever an election poll dips.

## What gnomes say

"Never trust a mayor who changes hat colours faster than a stolen Zero-Turn."

## The truth
Mayor of Turfton, who wants to be on the winning side and fears being repainted by someone else. Secret: the signatures he has already sold to both sides (how many is a loose thread). Home: the Mayor's Residence, a glossy red villa in Patio Heights; work: City Hall. The Don owns his debts, Vesper owns his zoning, Juniper is the conscience he mutes, Gary admires him. Voice: campaign slogans, "Ding, ding!", a cheery rise at the end of every sentence ("Progress is a gloss, friends! Ding, ding!").

Arc: tailed through town (#6) and waving at cameras that are not there; drifting toward the HOA in act II; his villa is a closed gate in act III; brought to sign the emergency counter-order (#63), where he finally picks the side that is winning; an epilogue repaint.
