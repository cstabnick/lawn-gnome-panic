Turfton's grinning career politician, infamous for surviving three consecutive corruption scandals by getting a fresh coat of glossy paint and claiming it was a different glaze.

## Story: The Ribbon-Cutting Specialist

Barnaby Mulch has never built a road, tuned a mower, or settled a turf war, yet his smiling face appears on brass plaques across every block in Turfton. His political philosophy is simple: whichever faction brings the biggest ribbon and the shiniest scissors gets the photo-op.

Behind closed doors, he quietly leases downtown parking medians to Thorne Lawn & Order LLC in exchange for complimentary astroturf on City Hall's front steps—and keeps a standing appointment at Pay 'n' Glaze whenever an election poll dips.
