Holy plant food, which Gnome Jesus drinks from a chalice.

## In the games

- The Bioluminescent Chalice is the vessel Gnome Jesus drinks it from.
- A cursed sermon warns that you should "WASH YOUR TINY HANDS IN HOLY MIRACLE-GRO" after touching an evil relic.
- The shed's shop is named the Shed Miracle Shop.
