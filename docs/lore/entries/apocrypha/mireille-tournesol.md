A resident at Pardonlin's race: yellow hat, cream dress, measuring the gap between two cones with a ruler, twice: two metres and eight centimetres, when it is meant to be two and seven.

## What gnomes say

"Two metres and eight centimetres. It is meant to be two and seven."

## The truth

The extra centimetre has been present for four races. She has told the commissaire; the commissaire has made a form.

Gnome Theft Auto (E26): a resident at the Pardonlin meet, "look-around" idle.
