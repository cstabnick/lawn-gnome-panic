The postmaster of Postern Hall. Every letter in Loamdeep passes his desk, and he reads none of them, loudly.

## What gnomes say

"Mister Sift has never lost a letter. He once returned one that was from the wrong century."

## The truth

He is fussy, kind and a stickler for the pigeonholes. A letter in the wrong hole is, to him, a small unhappiness. He heads the Sifts, the family that keeps Postern Hall (`loamdeep-clans`); Nubbin, the post runner, is his cousin's son. His line: "You have post? Oh. It is addressed to a hole in a lawn. We will deliver it. We always deliver."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: in Postern Hall, Loamdeep, at the Post Sorting desk, and in the quest `sq-burrow-post`.

## Loose threads

Who the 412 letters to the surface are addressed to, and whether he has ever read one.
