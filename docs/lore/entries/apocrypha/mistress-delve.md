Keeper of the lamps in Lamplight Hall. She trims every wick and counts the tapers back in at the end of the shift.

## What gnomes say

"Moles are afraid of light, they say. Mistress Delve has a hundred lamps and a very good reply."

## The truth

Moles like a lamp as much as anyone; it is being alone in the dark they dislike. Delve lends a taper to anyone careful.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: in Lamplight Hall, Loamdeep. She sets Lamplighter and the quest `sq-loamdeep-lamps`.
