Keeper of the lamps in Lamplight Hall. She trims every wick and counts the tapers back in at the end of the shift.

## What gnomes say

"Moles are afraid of light, they say. Mistress Delve has a hundred lamps and a very good reply."

## The truth

Moles like a lamp as much as anyone; it is being alone in the dark they dislike. Delve lends a taper to anyone careful. She is head of the Delves, the family that keeps Lamplight Hall (`loamdeep-clans`), and Pim and Pod are her cousins' children. Her line: "The lamps are trimmed, the wicks are straight, and the oil is counted twice. If it pleases you, do not breathe on the big one."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: in Lamplight Hall, Loamdeep. She sets Lamplighter and the quest `sq-loamdeep-lamps` (the taper at local (46, 24)).
