The knitting village on Snowcap Rise, near (550, -1375), north of the north band road, at the foot of the Hatbrim. From its green the range looks like a hat left on the fence. Its notice board has a policy on mittens, and the policy is left.

## What gnomes say

"Mittenholt has forty-one left mittens and nobody to claim them. The right ones are elsewhere."

"Hot cocoa on Thursdays. On other days, cold cocoa. Do not lick the pump."

## The truth

The hamlet lies within 75 m of (550, -1375), north of `north-band-east` (the road along z about -1320 from x 60 to 1060). Its board reads "FOUND: 41 left mittens. Right mittens need not apply." Its bus: "8:15, 8:15 (later) and sometimes 8:15". Residents: **Agnes Purl**, who knits only left mittens and has stopped asking why, and **Olaf Splitt**, the woodcutter, whose winter came early. From Mittenholt the Hatbrim stands to the north-east: Old Pate the near crown, Brim Gap the dent and the Bobble the bobble on top (R-230). The Brim Gap Road leaves the band road east of the village. In act IV the beacon on the Bobble can be seen from here; the village does not know what it is for, and by dawn has made a guess.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E32): houses, a notice board and two ambient gnomes (`living/hamletMittenholt.ts`). One of the places Off the Beaten Lane counts. It is the nearest settlement to the mountain routes of E33.

## Everyday

**Shops.** None. The pump on the green gives cocoa on Thursdays, which is a rumour.

**Notices.**
- Village sign, both ends: "MITTENHOLT. Twinned with nowhere, by request."
- The rules board: "FOUND: 41 left mittens. Right mittens need not apply. Hot cocoa on Thursdays. On other days, cold cocoa. Do not lick the pump."
- Bus post by the shelter: "BUS: 8:15, 8:15 (later) and sometimes 8:15."

**Sayings and habits.**
- "Left is right in Mittenholt." Said when someone is wrong and will not hear it.
- Every autumn the Snowman Committee meets to build a snowman. Three carrots wait on the bench, numbered 1, 2 and 3. There has never been agreement on which goes first.
- The Odd Mitten Line on the west side pegs out every found mitten, with one matching pair in the middle. Nobody admits to bringing it.

**Small history.** The Sled That Got Away went through the village in the spring of the long thaw and ended nose-first in a drift at the east edge, its scarf trailing back up the hill. The sled is still there.
