The four jobs a Burrowfolk hall will give a polite stranger: Root Run (carry roots to the right hall), Lamplighter (light a tunnel, one lamp at a time), Post Sorting (put the letters in the right pigeonhole) and Dig a Nook (dig a small room for somebody).

## What gnomes say

"The moles pay in Acorns. Where would a mole get acorns? I did not ask."

## The truth

They pay in Acorns they find in the soil, which is more than the HOA has ever paid anyone. They count it a favour to be helped, and say so.

Each job belongs to a family and a hall (`loamdeep-clans`): **Root Run** to the Tamps (take a bundle at Rootway and drop it at the right hall), **Lamplighter** to the Delves (take a taper at Lamplight and light one of the three lamp tunnels), **Post Sorting** to the Sifts (the pigeonhole desk in Postern) and **Dig a Nook** to the Crumbs (the three dig faces in Hearth). A first finished shift is paid and counted in the societies save; best stars (0 to 5) are kept per job.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the four jobs in Loamdeep. A first shift earns "Honest Dirt" (`gta-honest-dirt`); all four earn "Every Hall" (`gta-every-hall`; the mole crown).


## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** The jobs' board by the Burrow Door: "ROOTS: carry to the right hall. LAMPS: light a tunnel, one at a time. POST: sort by who it is for. NOOKS: dig one for somebody." Pay is "an Acorn, give or take what the soil gave".
- **Notices:** "Please do not lose the roots; they are labelled by smell." "Lamps lit this week: |||| |||| ||".
- **Sayings and habits:** "A job done is a favour returned." Moles take their pay in two hands and give a bow, which is a nod with the whole body.
