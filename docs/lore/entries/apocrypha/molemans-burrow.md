DJ Moleman's own burrow: a snug round room under Turfton with the decks against the north wall and a hat on the antenna.

## What gnomes say

"It is lined with records. And one very good chair."

## The truth

The transmitter is nowhere in sight and nobody has been brave enough to ask. There is no door from the surface: the only way in is the Long Tunnel from Loamdeep, and he likes it that way.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: Moleman's hidden room, reached from Under-Chorus Hall. Standing at his decks counts as meeting him (probe `moleman-met`).
