A walker on the Hatbrim: green hat, rust shirt, white beard, sitting down to say the map says they are at the bottom and that either the map or the mountain is upside down.

## What gnomes say

"The map says we are at the bottom. Either the map is upside down or the mountain is."

## The truth

The map is the right way up. He has not been told. He sits at the bench by the bottom of the path and is wonderfully content.
