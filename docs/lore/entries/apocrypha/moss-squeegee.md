The gnome who dries the Gutter Bowls after rain. Moss works the bowl floor with a squeegee and a bucket, and thinks of the bowls as a roof that happens to be on the ground.

## What gnomes say

"Rain fills the bowls. Moss empties them. Nobody asked Moss to."

"The gutters were a roof once. Moss still treats them like one."

## In the games

Stands on the bowl floor of the Gutter Bowl with a bucket, by the Round the Kidney line. Squeegees the bowls dry after rain. Talks to skaters about the old roof.

## Loose threads

Which roof the gutters came from, and whether Moss ever got permission to dry it.
