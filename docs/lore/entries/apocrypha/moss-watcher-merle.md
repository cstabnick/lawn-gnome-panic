Serene Unmowed hermit sitting cross-legged on a giant tree root off the `west` highway, slowly painting tiny moss landscapes onto river pebbles.

## Story: The Unhurried Brush

Every fleeing thief, panicked courier, and pursuing Hat Watch cruiser tears past Merle's tree root at top throttle. Merle never flinches, never ducks, and never spills his green pigment. He lifts two fingers in a slow wave and offers every passerby the exact same observation: *"You're in a hurry. The moss isn't."*

Rumors say he used to be Turfton's fastest getaway driver before he drove straight into a fern bank, heard a cricket sing, and never turned an ignition key again.

## What gnomes say

"If you crash into Merle's root, he'll help push your mower out—then lecture you for twelve minutes about lichen."
