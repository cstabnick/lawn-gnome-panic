A wooden boathouse on the south shore of Loch Moss, about 25 metres east of the dock where Driftwood keeps the loch. Its back wall stands on the shore and its front is open to the water, so a boat can come in off the loch and sit in its slip out of the weather. Driftwood lets it for 1200 Acorns, and he will tell you it has been a boathouse for longer than the loch has been a loch.

## How it works

The boathouse has three slips, each a long channel of still water between two wooden walkways, with a back door onto the shore for gnomes who would rather walk in. It takes boats and nothing else: no aircraft, and no Tessie, who belongs to the loch and does not sleep in anybody's shed. The HOA patrol boat is not welcome either.

Tansy's Sling-Lift will lower a boat from here onto open water at least a gnome's height deep (1 metre), near the gnome, for 100 Acorns. If there is no open water nearby the Sling-Lift will not fly, which is the only time it refuses anything.

## What gnomes say

"Driftwood's boathouse. He says the loch comes up to the door every night and that is why the slips are wet."

"Three slips, three boats. Tessie stays out in the reeds and the patrol boat stays out on the golf course, and that is the end of the discussion."
