A great Tall One city cemetery, about 1.45 km across in Tall terms, seen at gnome height: one Tall metre is 11 gm. Its
headstones are cliffs, its urns are towers, and its railings are the Fence the gnomes live behind. Grey moss covers
most of it.

## What it is

- Mossgrave is one hand-made map, the same place for every player. Its walls, crypts and stones are the only
  geography that matters to a gnome.
- The Verge is everything beyond the railings. Traders come in from it through the Lychgate every season.

## The twelve districts

1. **The Lychgate**: south edge, the roofed gate, the forecourt, the noticeboard and the bins, where new gnomes first arrived.
2. **The Old Rows**: west centre, the oldest section, leaning sandstone headstones in tight rows, ivy and collapsed graves.
3. **Wreathwood**: north-west, where old wreaths are thrown by the compost, and where gnomes pick flowers.
4. **The Compost Heaps**: far north-west, grass-clipping heaps and compost bays, warm, wet and alive.
5. **The Gnome Garden**: south-west, a memorial garden of Tall One ornaments, and the Pile where the gnomes' own dead lie.
6. **The Columbarium**: north edge, a long brick wall of urn niches and the largest friendly haven.
7. **The Bell Chapel**: north centre, a stone chapel on a rise with one bell, a vestry and catacombs beneath.
8. **The Yew Walk and the Tap**: centre, the main avenue of clipped yews and the standpipe tap halfway up.
9. **The Lawn Section**: east centre, flat bronze plaques set in mown lawn, with no headstones and no cover.
10. **The Drowned Corner**: east, a lily pond with a stone edge, reeds, a bridge and a storm drain.
11. **Mausoleum Hill and the New Hill**: north-east, the rich families' crypts on a slope and an unfinished extension.
12. **The Shed and the Mower Yard**: south-east, the groundskeeper's brick shed, its tool shelves and the open ride-on bay.

## The eras

- **The Long Quiet**: before living memory. Gnomes kept the graves of the Tall Ones and nobody fought.
- **The Greying**: the first autumn to spring. The moss came, the Pile woke, and the Bell Night fell.
- **The Scatter**: every gnome for themselves, the Shed fire, and the first factions.
- **The Holding**: the havens, the Truce of the Tap, and the traders coming back through the Lychgate.
- **Now**: the day the game starts, in spring of the third year.
