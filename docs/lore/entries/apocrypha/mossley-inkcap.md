Paranoid Turfton surveyor armed with a chalkboard and plumb-line, convinced the city's 60-unit grid proves the Tall Ones are still watching.

## Journal: Grid-Truther Manifesto (Chalkboard Notes)

> *Sixty units north-south. Sixty units east-west. Natural clover doesn't grow in right angles! Either the Tall Ones laid Turfton out like a hobby-room train set, or we're living on graph paper. Need a driver to hit all four survey markers before the Watch arrests me for "vandalizing sidewalks with chalk."*

After uncovering the Buried Tall-One Pencil beneath `x: 0, z: 0`, Mossley calmly chipped off three pounds of giant graphite, sold it as artisan eyebrow pencil, and retired comfortably.

## What gnomes say

"Mossley used to sound crazy until he pulled a cedar pencil the size of a redwood out of a storm drain."
