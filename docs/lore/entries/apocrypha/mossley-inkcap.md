Paranoid Turfton surveyor armed with a chalkboard and plumb-line, convinced the city's 60-unit grid proves the Tall Ones are still watching.

## Journal: Grid-Truther Manifesto (Chalkboard Notes)

> *Sixty units north-south. Sixty units east-west. Natural clover doesn't grow in right angles! Either the Tall Ones laid Turfton out like a hobby-room train set, or we're living on graph paper. Need a driver to hit all four survey markers before the Watch arrests me for "vandalizing sidewalks with chalk."*

After uncovering the Buried Tall-One Pencil beneath `x: 0, z: 0`, Mossley calmly chipped off three pounds of giant graphite, sold it as artisan eyebrow pencil, and retired comfortably.

## What gnomes say

"Mossley used to sound crazy until he pulled a cedar pencil the size of a redwood out of a storm drain."

## The truth
Mossley is a lone prophet of right angles, with no faction. He wants every gridline proved, and fears the HOA "correcting" the maps. Secret: the pencil is only the start. Home: a chalk-marked basement on Kerb Row; work: surveyor, his markers. He warns Burt about the van routes, trades maps with Sal (both watch the culvert), uses Bodega's step, and Juniper takes his surveys seriously and tells nobody. Voice: fast lecturer, rising pitch, shows his work out loud and interrupts himself with measurements; ends claims with "sixty and sixty" ("Sixty and sixty! Nature does not do that." / "The grid is a ruler. Who holds it?").

Arc: side quests only except act III, when he stands in the Turfton street watching the Even-Estates hoardings go up, talking to himself. No spine row names him.

## In the games

In the Grid Truthers side quest (`sq-grid-truthers-*`), Mossley rides up to Gamma (120, -120) on his survey bike and sights the three markers toward the centre datum. At the datum he spots the eraser end worn down. He chips off the graphite and retires on the proceeds. He predicts Vesper's HOA will "correct" the maps by Friday.
