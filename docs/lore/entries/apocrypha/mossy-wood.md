Dense western pine-needle forest (`x < -215`) where the Unmowed Fescue exiles shelter beneath towering toadstools and swore never to touch an engine.

## Field Notes & Tales

When Fescue traditionalists slipped through a knot-hole in the Fence centuries ago, they chose the deepest shade where no mower had ever rolled. They built moss-bridges and hid Stopping Stone shards inside hollow acorns along the `west` highway. Lately, orange "DEMOLITION PENDING" tape from Vesper Thorne's surveyors has begun appearing on old-growth toadstools—pushing peaceful elders toward open sabotage.

## What gnomes say

"Mossy Wood is where gnomes go when they can't stand the sound of a two-stroke engine."
