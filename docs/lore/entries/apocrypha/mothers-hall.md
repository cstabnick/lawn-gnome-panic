The first hall inside the entry tunnel of Loamdeep and the Tumblesoils' own: a warm, low, moss-rugged room with one chair in it, facing the door. Everything else in Loamdeep is arranged around where the chair looks.

## What gnomes say

"You go in, and she is there, and she has been expecting you for some time. It is not a trick. She is simply old and punctual."

"There is a table laid for gnomes. It says so on the table."

## The truth

The hall is 24 by 20 metres of local floor (x -12 to 12, z 6 to 26), centred just north of the entry tunnel, with Old Mother Tumblesoil's chair at its north end facing south toward the door. The moss rug is moss and "listening". At the table, beside two mole stools and a grub loaf, stands one gnome-sized chair laid with a reed tea cup and a tray, under a small sign reading "FOR WHEN THE GNOMES VISIT" (the place has been laid for as long as anyone remembers, and kept hopefully); two bunk nooks and four lit lamps fill out the corners; a plaque says "OUR MOTHER (she asked for no portrait)"; another reads "WE DIG WHERE WE ARE ASKED". The hall opens north up the mothers' tunnel, the third of the lamp tunnels, which the Delves light. Roots are delivered here during Root Run, and the Mother's Hall is the warmer for every lamp lit.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the middle of the Loamdeep interior. Old Mother Tumblesoil sits here and gives `sq-loamdeep-lamps` and `sq-loamdeep-under-chorus`; one of the five Root Run drops (local (8, 22)). Introduced in `m49-loamdeep`.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** The table laid for gnomes: a reed-tea cup, a small tray, a grub loaf. Nothing for sale; guests are fed.
- **Notices:** "OUR MOTHER (she asked for no portrait)." "WE DIG WHERE WE ARE ASKED." "FOR WHEN THE GNOMES VISIT."
- **Sayings and habits:** "Ask the Mother and then wait. She has answered; you are still asking." At every bell the family stand once, for a breath, facing the chair.
