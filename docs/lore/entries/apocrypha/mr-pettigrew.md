An HOA inspector who comes to the Unmown off duty: navy hat, grey beard, glasses, and a personal ruler that he is not measuring anything with.

## What gnomes say

"I am not measuring anything. This is a personal ruler."

## The truth

He is the thirteenth species on Burdock's list. The rangers do not fine him, and he does not fine them, and they are both a little disappointed by it.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E15): on the Unmown trail as "Mr. Pettigrew, off duty".
