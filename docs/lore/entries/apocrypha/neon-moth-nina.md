Greenhouse Strip night-owl sitting on a flowerpot stoop selling glow-stick hat-bands to street racers while perpetually shooing a giant moth away from The Gnomecino's sign.

## Story: Glow Under the Glass

Nina knows every racer, bouncer, and off-duty Hat Watch officer who wanders down the humid, glass-roofed alley after midnight. Her glow-stick bands are practically mandatory attire for illegal Zero-Turn runs, and she doubles as an unofficial lookout: whenever the *Pay 'n' Glaze* neon starts flickering in Morse code, Nina casually taps her broom to warn the block.

Her mortal enemy is a furry, dinner-plate-sized luna moth named Barnaby Jr. that keeps trying to headbutt the casino lights.

## What gnomes say

"Buy a glow-band from Nina and she'll tell you which alley the Watch isn't checking tonight."

## The truth
Nina is the Greenhouse Strip's night vendor and street lookout, the racers' informant. She wants the Strip glowing and the moth off the sign, and fears the lamps going out. Secret: what the Morse in the neon really spells. Home: the Greenhouse Strip; work: the Gnomecino floor (and her stoop). Big Lou keeps her stoop dry, Dot hands her client jobs, the Carburettors buy bands before every race, and Barnaby Jr. the moth is her enemy. Voice: a conspiratorial stage-whisper, quick and sing-song, dropping gossip and then winking; she ends tips with "you didn't hear it from me" ("Alley three. Watch is not checking. You didn't hear it from me." / "Glow-band, one acorn. Barnaby Jr. approves.").

Arc: side quests only in every act; she sells glow-bands for the finale's coalition torches.
