The Underspan's reader. Nettle lies in a hammock slung from the scaffold, upside down, with a book, and says it is the only way the sentences stay in.

## What gnomes say

"Nettle has read everything and will tell you, from the wrong way up, what it meant."

## The truth

The hammock is rated for one gnome and a small dog. Nettle has no dog. The book is usually the manual for something Hogweed built last week.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: hangs in the scaffold of the Underspan, upside down, reading.
