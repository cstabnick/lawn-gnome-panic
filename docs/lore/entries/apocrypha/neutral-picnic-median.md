A grassy traffic island at `x: 60, z: -60` legally designated as neutral picnic ground between rival Red Cap and Blue Cap street corners.

## Field Notes & Tales

On paper, City Hall declared the median a demilitarized zone where gnomes of any hat colour can eat an acorn-pretzel in peace. In practice, Red Caps stand on the west kerb and Blue Caps stand on the east kerb, pelting each other with acorns the moment the wind blows east. Three HOA benches have been cracked in half since spring.

## What gnomes say

"It's neutral ground right up until someone's hat shadow crosses the painted white line."
