A small marina on Cloverfield's coast, where two wooden docks reach south into the Puddle Sea. It rents boats by the hour to anybody who can still prove they own a hat.

## In the games

Two docks reach south from the coast into the Puddle Sea, and three boats are moored at them: the Clog Cruiser, a Gravy Boat and a Walnut Dinghy. Walnut Dinghy rentals are advertised on Turfton FM. The docks and boats sit at the south end of the Everspring, where the farm river ends in the sea (see [the-puddle-sea](the-puddle-sea.md)).
