A cream punch-card Mabel Kerbstone hands every newcomer at the Central Bus Stop: one stamp per hobby club or trade in
and around Turfton, each punched by the gnome who runs it (R-156).

## What gnomes say

"Get your card punched, dear. Nobody is from Turfton until they've fallen off something here."

"Half the card is hobbies, half is jobs. Mabel says that's Turfton in a nutshell, and the nutshell is on the meter."

## In the games

Giver `newcomers-club` (Mabel, by the Central Bus Stop) in `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/quests/newcomerQuests.ts`.
Each stamp is a short side job that teaches one thing and leaves a small perk:

- Good Neighbour (Dot Pennywort): help at a street event.
- Strike a Pose (Neon-Moth Nina): emotes, and a pair emote with a townsgnome.
- The Wheel and the Kit (Pip Potsworth): the weapon wheel, the Pocket Kit, Ammu-Gnation.
- Store It, Call It (Tansy Clayfoot): the Side-Valve Lock-Up and the Sling-Lift on the phone.
- Mind the Verges (Juniper Vance): mowing plants, the Country Club's putting-green cart.
- The Bent Pin (Wenna Gudgeon): rod, cast, bite, reel, catch cards.
- Up on the Skyway (Big Wrench Grout): the Skyway deck and the Unfinished Span jump.
- Odd Jobs (Mabel): Kerbstone Cabs and the other sim jobs.
- Wings Over the Flats (Wind-Sock Wally): flying, and the Wingwright's Slipway.
- Sky Spotting (Hedda Galley): the banner plane.
- Tuft & Tally (Hazel Mattock): the license, a comb, a sale at the lodge.
- Last Stop, Pebblewick (Mabel): Pebblewick and the Hopper.
- Reedwatch (Dunnock Pell): a sighting, a call, a photograph.
- Hangar and Boathouse (Tansy Clayfoot): Hangar Two, the Mossbank Boathouse, a boat.
- Throw a Party (Neon-Moth Nina): online only, and not needed for a full card.

A full card unlocks the achievement `gta-newcomer-card` (the Newcomers' Card Lanyard).

## Loose threads

The card has one more space than there are stamps. Mabel says that one is "for later, dear".
