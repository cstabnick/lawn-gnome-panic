The newest of the Flock, still learning which leg. Pipit has tried both and holds that the left is more spiritual.

## What gnomes say

"Pipit stood a whole sermon, once. Then the sermon ended and Pipit kept going."

## The truth

She joined after a long walk and liked that nobody asked her anything. She is the gnome the camp is most anxious to keep, and the one most likely to sit.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the novice at Wader's Rest; a good first gnome to ask about the rules.
