A small young mole with a satchel too big for him: the post runner of Loamdeep, the player's friend among the Burrowfolk, and the one who first got lost above ground.

Home: his family's burrow in Loamdeep, a Sift household (the post family; `loamdeep-clans`). Work: runner for Loamdeep's post, out of Postern Hall.

## What gnomes say

"Seen a little one with a bag? Nearly stepped on him twice. He said sorry both times."

"He talks like he's reading from a card. I think he is."

## The truth

Nubbin goes up to see the sky, which the Burrowfolk consider a bad habit. He has no idea that gnomes with spades are afraid of him, only that they are loud.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Chased through Mossy Wood and driven home in quest 24 (`m24-the-burrow-door`); in 48 (`m48-the-groundskeepers-secret`) he is the mole the player shows Basil Divot, the one who moved the ball; a digger in 60 (`m60-the-moles-dig`). E17: waits by the Burrow Door and gives the first errand, `sq-burrow-post`.
