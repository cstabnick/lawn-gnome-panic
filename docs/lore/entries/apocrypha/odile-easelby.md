A Left Bank painter who paints everything, including you, as a croissant.

## What gnomes say

"She painted the Bean Tower as a croissant. She painted the river as a croissant. She will paint you as a croissant, and you will look wonderful."

"Madame Easelby says it is not what you look like, it is what you are. We all are croissants, apparently."

## The truth

Odile has painted exactly one thing, forty thousand times, and refuses to see it. Her portraits are famous and nobody has asked her to stop. Sitting for her takes a long time and ends in a croissant.

## In the games

On an artists' terrace on the Left Bank. Sitting for her portrait is one of the town's side activities.

## Loose threads

What a croissant would look like if she painted one, and not a gnome.
