The oldest streets in Turfton, south-west of Downtown: narrow lanes, low brick shopfronts and the shops that were here before the grid was. Its footpaths are the lanes the Flat Patch wore into the clover, and they still bend where the clover did.

## Character

Worn and honest, or at least it says so on the sign. Old Brick is where a gnome goes for a loaf, a repair or a favour, and where the favour is usually paid back the same afternoon. Nothing here is new, and the people who have been here longest like it that way.

## Landmarks

- Bodega & Sons, (-90, 90), shopfront at (-76, 90): John Bodega's corner shop. Bread, bolts and a bench for waiting.
- Side-Valve Salvage, (-150, 90), bay at (-150, 72): the yard where old mowers come to be made into new ones.
- Shelldon's dumpster alley, (-80, 72): where Shelldon gives out his jobs between the bins.
- Mossley's survey table, outside Bodega & Sons: a folding table, a map of the grid and a pencil on a string.

## Factions

- The Red Caps: the corner crews, who keep the lanes in order by standing in them.
- The Side-Valve crew: Pip and Tansy's salvage team, who will fix nearly anything and charge for the parts.
- Shelldon's couriers: quick, cheap and not always about the parcel.

## What gnomes say

"Bodega's is open when it's open. Ask the bread."

"If the lane bends, it's because the clover did. Don't straighten it."

## Local slang

- "no sons": honest, after the Bodega & Sons sign. "Is it fair?" "No sons."
- "the paving's listening": keep your voice down. The lanes carry.
- "pulls left": a plan, or a mower, that is a little off and needs watching.

## In the games

Old Brick is the south-west side of the city. Bodega & Sons, Side-Valve Salvage and Shelldon's alley are the places the city's errands start from, and the Side-Valve pair's jobs are given here. Its district sign is `old-brick` in `src/districts.ts`, a block from x -185 to -45 and z 45 to 125.

## Loose threads

Whether the lanes were ever a straight road before the clover moved. Nobody who knows is saying, and the lanes have never once been asked.
