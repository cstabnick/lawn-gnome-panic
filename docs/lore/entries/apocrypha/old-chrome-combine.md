A half-buried chrome combine in the eastern dunes beyond Pebblewick. It hums at three in the morning and answers a knock at night.

## What gnomes say

"There's a combine in the dunes and it hums at three in the morning."

## The truth

Why it hums stays a loose thread. The pilots' story that it sits out past the eastern dunes is true.

## In the games

Half buried in the eastern dunes. Mallow Bucket, a youngster who watches the dunes, gives the player 'The Hum at Three' side quest, and Ratchet's Thanks is the reward. The HOA is interested in it.

## Loose threads

Why it hums at three in the morning, what answers the knock at night, and what the HOA wants with it.
