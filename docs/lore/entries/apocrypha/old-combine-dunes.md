A half-buried, chrome-plated Combine Harvester resting in the eastern Dust Flats dunes that reportedly hums to itself at three in the morning.

## Field Notes & Tales

Desert pilots flying low out of the Desert Airfield swear they've seen moonlight glint off its spotless chrome grille miles from any road. Conspiracy callers on 99.3 The Blade insist it is a relic from the Panic Yard—or an escaped test unit that wandered into the sand and simply refused to shut off.

## What gnomes say

"Don't park near the chrome grille after midnight unless you want your radio to start picking up static from another lawn."
