The old regular at the Punch booth: brown hat, green shirt, white beard, glasses, who has seen the show forty times.

## What gnomes say

"Forty times I have seen this. He still does not duck."

Gnome Theft Auto (FX11, rulings R-243 and R-248): at the Punch booth at (-91.6, 1121.4), hands on hips. Look: A brown hat, a moss-green shirt, a white beard and glasses.
