The barman at the Hedgeworth Clubhouse: a brown-hatted, grey-bearded old gnome behind the bar counter, who offers the usual because there is only the usual.

## What gnomes say

"The usual, member? There's only the usual."

## The truth

He has tended the bar since before the hedges grew, and the only other thing on the shelf is the bottle that Dame Peony keeps for Sundays. He knows the score of every round played here and gives it away to nobody.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E12): the east end of the clubhouse interior, beside the bar counter and two bookshelves; he is a standing NPC with one line.
