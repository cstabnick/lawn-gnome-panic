A very old grey badger who walks the far western tree-line of Mossy Wood after dark, older than the Wood itself and grey to the tips of his coat.

## What gnomes say

"Go at night, go slowly, and don't stand in his path. He'll let you comb a tuft, but he'll remember your face."
"Grizzle's the only quarry the Lodge keeps a page for by itself."

## The truth

Grizzle is an animal, the oldest anyone at the Lodge has heard of. He is never hurt and never dies (R-021, R-049): a shot knocks him over and he grumbles his way back to his feet. Whether his walking is why the birds go silent along the western trees is the question the Wood has not answered.

## In the games

Appears only after dark, along the far western tree-line of Mossy Wood, and is the legendary quarry with its own achievement. He is big and slow but hears a gnome from far off, and has a silver bristle tuft and a rarer white stripe lock, both bought at the Lodge like any other tuft.

## Loose threads

Whether Grizzle is why the birds go quiet along the far western tree-line. Stays unanswered on purpose: do not explain it in game.
