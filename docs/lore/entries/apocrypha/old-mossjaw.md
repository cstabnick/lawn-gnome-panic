The ancient, moss-covered fish of the Loch Moss deeps: a heavy carp-like giant with a long whiskery jaw and green moss growing along its back. Tessie's neighbour, and the largest fish in the loch by a long way.

## What gnomes say

"Older than the Steamer, mossier than the hills. Tessie nods to it. It nods back."
"You will know the bite when it comes. Nothing else on the loch takes its time about it."

## The truth

Old Mossjaw is an animal, very old and very slow, that has lived in the deepest water of the loch for longer than the Steamer has run on it. It is never hurt. A gnome who lands it measures it, writes a catch card and lets it go, and it sinks back into the moss. Like Tessie (R-051), it is larger than R-049's sizes for animals, and it is the only fish in Turfton that is.

## In the games

Only in the Loch Deeps, at the hot spot of that name, and only for a gnome with the top rod (the Moonline). It is the hardest fish to land on the loch and the mythic fish of the loch. Landing it earns the Old Mossjaw achievement and the Golden Gudgeon reward.

## Loose threads

Whether it is older than Tessie, or she is older than it. Whether anyone else on the loch has ever seen it come up on its own.
