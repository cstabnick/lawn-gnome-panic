The matriarch of the Burrowfolk: a stout, pale-whiskered mole in a shawl, who keeps Loamdeep and talks about everyone as if they were visiting. She is very old and very polite, and she knows exactly what you owe.

Home: the Mother's Hall in Loamdeep. Work: keeps Loamdeep.

## What gnomes say

"I thanked her once. It took an hour. At the end of it I had been thanked back."

"She does not raise her voice. The tunnel does it for her."

## The truth

Tumblesoil decides when Loamdeep helps. Her answer to every request is the same ("we dig where we are asked, and nobody ever asks"), and she has been waiting a long while for someone to prove her wrong.
