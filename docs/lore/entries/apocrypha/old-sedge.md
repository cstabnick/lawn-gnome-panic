A busker on the Pebblewick square with a battered guitar and one string that still works.

## What gnomes say

"I played the Speedway once. They lapped me."

## The truth

Sedge raced the Lost & Found Speedway as a young gnome, was lapped twice in one afternoon, and has played the square ever since to hide the fact that he still thinks about it.

## In the games

Plays on the Pebblewick square, near the bell.

## Loose threads

Whether the one string is the one he lost on the track.
