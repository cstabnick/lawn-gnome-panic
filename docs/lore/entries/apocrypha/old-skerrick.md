The fisherman on the western strand: navy hat, rust shirt, a winch and a rowing boat that comes up when it comes up.

## What gnomes say

"The boat comes up when it comes up. I only push the winch."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (FX11, rulings R-244 and R-249): at the fishing winch at (-936, 1159.5); the rowing boat on the sand, the drying net, the oars and a gull on the bow are the scene 'winch-and-boat'. Look: A navy hat, a rust-red shirt and a sandy beard.

## Loose threads

What the boat was hauled up from, and who owns the gull.
