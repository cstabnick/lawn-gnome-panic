Rootway Hall's keeper. Tamp sorts roots by smell, with his eyes shut, and has never once been wrong or been asked to explain.

## What gnomes say

"Gnomes say the moles eat roots. Old Tamp would like a word, if it pleases you."

## The truth

He stores them, sorts them and sends each where it is needed, as carefully as a letter. He is grave, small and very old.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: in Rootway Hall, Loamdeep, where Root Run begins.
