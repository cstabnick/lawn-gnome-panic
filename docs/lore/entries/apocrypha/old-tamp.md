Rootway Hall's keeper. Tamp sorts roots by smell, with his eyes shut, and has never once been wrong or been asked to explain.

## What gnomes say

"Gnomes say the moles eat roots. Old Tamp would like a word, if it pleases you."

## The truth

He stores them, sorts them and sends each where it is needed, as carefully as a letter. He is grave, small and very old, and head of the Tamps, the family that keeps Rootway Hall (`loamdeep-clans`). He says "if it pleases you" before every sentence and after most of them, and talks to the roots when he thinks nobody is listening.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: in Rootway Hall, Loamdeep, where Root Run begins (the pickup at local (-40, 24)). His lines: "Gnomes say we eat roots. We store them. For the grubs. Who eat them. It is a long story and nobody stays for the end." and "A root sorted by smell is a root sorted properly. I have never once been thanked for it, and that is perfectly fine."
