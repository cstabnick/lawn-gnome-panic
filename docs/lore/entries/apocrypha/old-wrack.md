The net-mender of Dunelow: blue hat, grey beard, sitting on the green and saying he has mended this net forty times and the fish have learned where the holes were.

## What gnomes say

"Mended this net forty times. The fish have learned where the holes were."

## The truth

He has never caught anything; the net is for sitting beside. The hamlet considers him part of the green.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E23): Dunelow, at about (-634, 957), "sit-ground" idle facing the green.
