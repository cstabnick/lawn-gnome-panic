The old trees at the west end of the Unmown, north of Wood Hide: big trunks, logs, moss and ferns, and a ring of ten toadstools around a chair that fits a gnome. It is the Unmown's quietest corner and the only place in the preserve that is thought to be listening.

## What gnomes say

"The fox was here first. Knock before entering the hide that looks at it."

"There is a ring of toadstools with a chair in it. Sit if you like. It was built for sitting. I do not say by whom."

## The truth

The wood lies north-north-west of Wood Hide (-1700, -1330), around (-1690, -1500). The trees are old (small trunk colliders), with logs, moss and ferns between. The fairy ring is ten toadstools around a tiny gnome-size chair: the rangers insist it was there when they came, and the HOA's survey team insist they were not asked to count it. The wood keeps the Unmown's foxes and hedgehogs; the name is the rangers' own, for the preserve's oldest roots.
