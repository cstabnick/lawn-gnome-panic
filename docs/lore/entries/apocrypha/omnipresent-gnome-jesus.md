A satirical gnome god whose statue stands on the shed shrine and who preaches in capital letters. He blesses the gnomes who pray to him, and takes their loot as tithes.

## What they say

Day sermons come in capitals: "MY CHILDREN OF CERAMIC! THE HOA DEMANDS SACRIFICE. STEAL FROM THE YARD!" When the quota is missed: "NO TOASTERS?! NO ACORNS?! GNOME JESUS HATH NO MERCY! *FLUSH*"

## In the games

- The statue on the shrine bobs and sways in the shed.
- Prayer at the shrine gives a speed blessing for 14 seconds.
- Loot brought to the tithe altar counts toward the quota.
- He likes holy Miracle-Gro, and the Bioluminescent Chalice is the item he drinks from.
