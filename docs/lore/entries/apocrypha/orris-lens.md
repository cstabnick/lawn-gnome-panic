The watcher of Capwood's glowing cap: navy hat, grey beard, spectacles, pointing at the ring where the glow is supposed to be and waiting for dark.

## What gnomes say

"It glows at night. I am told it glows tonight. I have been told that for six years."

## The truth

He has never seen it glow; the one night it did, he was asleep in the hide. The warden has recorded the glow nine times and Orris has been present at none of them. He says that is how you know it is a good spot.
