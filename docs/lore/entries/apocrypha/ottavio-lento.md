Maestro Ottavio Lento, conductor and host of 96.1 Radio Adagio, a pompous, kind and deeply slow Continental who runs the station from the Opera Hall on the Left Bank.

## What gnomes say

"He introduces a piece for ten minutes and then plays it for eight." Said fondly, and with a seat cushion.

## The truth

He believes everything should be played slower, including traffic, bread and the evening news. He adores the Thymes Quartet, calls the Blade "the loud one", and says "Turfton" as "Terfin" on purpose. He keeps a running feud with the pigeons on the Opera Hall roof, who sit in during the rests, and reads out the brass section's strikes as if they were the weather. He reads the weather too, and is pleased when it drizzles.

## In the games

The voice of Radio Adagio, label MAESTRO: idents, banter, ads and calls in `src/radio/adagioStation.ts`. He never appears on foot.

## Loose threads

His age, and whether he has ever once finished a piece on the day he began it.
