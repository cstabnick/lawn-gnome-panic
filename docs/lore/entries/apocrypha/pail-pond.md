A frozen lake on Snowcap Rise, round and too regular to be natural: a Tall-One pail sunk to its rim and frozen over. Ice-fishing huts dot the surface, and the polar bears who wander past are very relaxed.

## What gnomes say

"It is a pail. Everyone knows it is a pail. Nobody has found the handle."
"The bears let you fish. They do not let you hurry."

## The truth

The bears here are cousins of the ones on the Fells' sunny slope (see Fells Polar Bears): the same calm, the same shrug. Anything dropped through the ice stays down until someone thinks to look.

## In the games

The ice is a disc of radius 62 m centred on (-640, -1060); Thermos Theo's shanty stands on it at (-612, -1040). Four holes at most stay open at once. 
Built (batch 3, E12): flat ice around (-640, -1060) (E01 site ice-lake). Anyone may drill a hole with a hand auger; a hole refreezes when a fifth is drilled, and the ice never cracks. Fish come up, are measured, carded and slipped back: Pail Perch, Woolly Minnow, Lantern Burbot (at night) and Frostjaw Char (in falling snow, with a Split-Cane Rod or better). Thermos Theo keeps a red shanty here and lends a Bent-Pin Twig Rod. Four polar bears are about: one asleep, one in an inner tube, one fishing from a stool. The pail's handle sticks out of the ice at the edge; gnomes found it and still do not know what it is for (R-187). Quest row 38 (m38-pail-pond): the crew fishes with Theo past the bears for an HOA radio, which names the four parts of the Grand Combine and where they are.

## Everyday

**Shops.** Thermos Theo's red shanty at (-612, -1040) lends a Bent-Pin Twig Rod and pours broth. He takes no payment; a spare pine-cone by his boots earns a nod.

**Notices.**
- Pond sign: "PAIL POND. THIN ICE: NONE. THICK ICE: ALL OF IT."
- HOA notice: "ICE HOLES MUST BE ROUND. SQUARE HOLES WILL BE FINED." (Nobody has ever drilled a square one. The HOA fines for it anyway, on principle.)
- By the bears' corner: "DO NOT FEED THE BEARS. (THEY HAVE SARDINES.)"
- On Theo's door: "THEO'S BACK IN FIVE (SINCE TUESDAY)." On the blue shanty: "GONE FISHING. BACK NEVER."

**Sayings and habits.**
- "Measure it, card it, put it back." Every fish gets a card, and every card goes in the biscuit tin on a shanty step.
- Nobody speaks above a murmur on the ice. The bears did not ask; it just seems the polite thing.
- Local feud: the blue shanty against the green one, over a hole that is now exactly between them.

**Small history.** The first hole was drilled by accident, with a walking stick. It is still open, and the pond calls it Hole One.
