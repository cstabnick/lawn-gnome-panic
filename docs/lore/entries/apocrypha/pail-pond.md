A frozen lake on Snowcap Rise, round and too regular to be natural: a Tall-One pail sunk to its rim and frozen over. Ice-fishing huts dot the surface, and the polar bears who wander past are very relaxed.

## What gnomes say

"It is a pail. Everyone knows it is a pail. Nobody has found the handle."
"The bears let you fish. They do not let you hurry."

## The truth

The bears here are cousins of the ones on the Fells' sunny slope (see Fells Polar Bears): the same calm, the same shrug. Anything dropped through the ice stays down until someone thinks to look.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3): E01 site ice-lake at (-640, -1030), with ice-fishing huts built by the area epic. Quest row 38 (m38-pail-pond): the crew fishes with Theo past the bears for an HOA radio, which names the four parts of the Grand Combine and where they are.
