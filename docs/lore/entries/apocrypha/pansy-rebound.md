A regular at Drainpipe Court who rebounds everything that comes off the drainpipe hoop, and most things that do not. Wears a sweatband that has its own sweatband.

## What gnomes say

"Pansy gets the ball back before the ball knows it missed."

"Pass to Pansy. Don't ask her to pass back, she's busy stretching."

## In the games

The regular who partners the player in pickup games, on the player's side. A fierce rebounder with good defence and a good dunk. Stretches on the north side of the court before a game starts.

## Loose threads

Whether the sweatband is for sweat or for holding the rest of her together.
