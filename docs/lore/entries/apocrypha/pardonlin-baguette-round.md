Brigitte Levain's morning delivery of long loaves round Pardonlin, in the right order.

## What gnomes say

"The Baguette Round starts at dawn, in the Old Town, with a basket and a bell."

"Deliver them out of order and the town will know by lunch."

## The truth

The order of the Round is not written down. It is a map of who is speaking to whom this season, and Brigitte alone knows how to read it.

## In the games

A town activity starting at Brigitte Levain's bakery in the Old Town.

## Loose threads

The map, and the season.
