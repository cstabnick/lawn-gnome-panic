Pardonlin's sanctioned road race: the roads closed, cones counted twice, geraniums on every sill, and marshals who came
by bicycle. The only street-racing meet in Fescue's gardens that has a licence.

## Inside & Stories

- **Organiser:** Commissaire Aurelie Fonce. **Bookie:** Monsieur Bastien Pipette, who calls a wager "a small envelope".
  **Flag-dropper:** Odette Marchand, who lowers the flag once, politely.
- **Residents:** Gaspard Lorry sweeps the cobbles before every car starts; Mireille Tournesol measures the gap between
  two cones with a ruler, a second time.
- **The regulars:** Hugo Sablier, Colette Frisson and Anselme Brie, in Deux-Pots.

## What gnomes say

"Please do not overtake. You will anyway."
