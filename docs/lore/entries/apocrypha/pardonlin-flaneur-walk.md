A Pardonlin activity: a long aimless stroll round the town's best views, and the only one in which nobody is trying to get anywhere.

## What gnomes say

"The flâneur does not arrive. The flâneur is already there, and moves on."

"You walk. You look. You stop. You look again. That is the whole of it."

## The truth

The walk takes in the three screenshot spots of each district, and anyone who completes it has learnt that the town is meant to be seen at walking pace. It cannot be done in a car, which the Continentals find a relief.

## In the games

A town activity round the four districts, on foot.
