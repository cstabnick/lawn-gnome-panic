Pardonlin's artists' quarter on the west bank of the Thymes: the Opera Hall and its radio station, the Beetle Market, a gallery with three stops, a poet on a diet of irony and a great many backyards. It runs the town's culture slowly and has never needed to hurry.

## What gnomes say

"On the Left Bank everyone is in the middle of something. Do not interrupt. They will be a while."

"There is a strike on, against the strike scheduled for yesterday. The coffee is still excellent."

## The truth

The district is the strip x -1795 to -1626, z -460 to 400, with an arm south to z 900 for the backyards. Three bridges tie it to the east bank: the Pont Neuf ("newer than Pont Nord, probably"), the Pont Nord ("still being finished") and the Pont Sud ("north of the other one"). The Boulevard of Forms (Triplicate) runs the length, with Windows 1 and 2 (each says to queue at the other), the Opera Hall and Radio Adagio, a poetry slam ("bring your own silence"), a Gallery Walk (stop 1: a chair; stop 2: the other chair; stop 3: lunch), the Terrace of Doubt and the Fountain of Indecision. South of the Boulevard is the Beetle Market's covered hall.

Gnome Theft Auto (E30): the Left Bank plan (`districts/leftBank.ts`). Radio Adagio's studio and the Opera Hall; the Beetle Market; the artists' terraces; Odile's portrait stand; the backyards. People: Odile Easelby, Roquette Fromage, Sartorius Gloom, Greta Clothespeg.
