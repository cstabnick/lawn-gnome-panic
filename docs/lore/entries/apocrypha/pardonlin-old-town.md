Pardonlin's oldest quarter, on the east bank of the Thymes north of the Hatwheel: the Bean Tower, the Grand Platz and the Rue Haute, all within a bicycle's earshot of one another and all queued for. It is where a Continental takes a visitor first, and where the visitor first hears that something is not done.

## What gnomes say

"The Old Town is old the way a joke is old: everyone has heard it and nobody will say so."

"Come early. The queue for the Bean Tower opens before the Bean Tower does."

## The truth

The district is the rectangle x -1595 to -1400, z -460 to -52. It is built in bone and paper walls, slate roofs and window boxes, in three strips: the Grand Platz in the middle (fountain, bandstand, the Unknown Pigeon on its plinth, a boules ground), the Bean Tower to the north on its own lawn, and the Rue Haute running along the east side with shopfronts, bistros and a bakery. Behind the Rue Haute the courtyards are shared: washing lines, benches, a laundry quarrel nobody is winning. Street signs take a position and then withdraw it ("Rue A: it was first; it has never forgiven the others"; "Rue Nord (North, mostly) (ask a bird)"). The Old Town keeps three classical vignettes: the queue for nothing on the Platz, the queue for the queue, and the pigeons, who have standing.
