One of Pardonlin's town activities: a gnome sits still on a terrace while Odile Easelby paints them as a croissant.

## What gnomes say

"You will sit for three hours. Madame Easelby will paint for four seconds. The rest is atmosphere."

"Every portrait is a croissant. Nobody has been disappointed."

## The truth

The sitting is the point. What is painted does not matter, because it is the same every time, and everyone leaves flattered and slightly flaky.

## In the games

A town activity at Odile's terrace on the Left Bank. Not a journal quest.
