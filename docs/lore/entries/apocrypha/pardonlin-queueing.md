A Pardonlin pastime: joining the queue that forms on the Grand Platz for no reason that anyone will give.

## What gnomes say

"The queue is for the queue. The queue is also for something. It has not been announced."

"You will know what it was for when you get to the front. You will not be told."

## The truth

The Continentals regard queueing as the highest form of civic cooperation. The queue on the Platz began as a line for a bench, then for a bench for a queue, and has since become institutional.

## In the games

A town activity on the Grand Platz. The player joins the end and waits.
