Another Tall One's garden west of Trellisdown, laid out as a continental formal garden: gravel walks, box-hedge parterres, clipped limes, terracotta pots crammed onto every sill and a long ornamental rill. Its gnomes are the Continentals, and their town is called "pardon", which is also its first word.

## What gnomes say

"Pardon. Pardon? Pardon, it is not done."

"In Pardonlin you do not cross the road. You queue to cross it, and then you cycle round the queue."

## The truth

The Continentals are polite to a fault. They take long lunches, have strong opinions on bread, queue for everything, cycle everywhere and have a dozen ways to say that something is not done. The satire is gentle: a Paris, London and Berlin mash with no real city and no nationality named. Their town is older than Trellisdown's walls and wants nothing from Turfton but a fair price for the roundabouts it has already sold.

## In the games

The Pardonlin region (code region `pardonlin`, biome continental) runs x -1800 to -1400, z -900 to 1100, built by E28. The river the Thymes runs north to south through it into Pardonlin Basin (Basin Quay to the south). Districts: the old town on the east bank, the Left Bank on the west, the Jardin (the formal garden north of town), the Little Gardens (allotments) and Pardonlin Basin, a still-water basin centred at (-1600, 1040). Two roundabouts stand in town: the Hatwheel (centre (-1555, -30), ring radius 20) and Place du Pot (centre (-1500, 450), radius 14). The town is joined to Trellisdown at three gates, the Wickets. Lupin Pardoux directs traffic on Pardonlin High Street, on loan from the Continentals.

Reserved for later, named here but not yet built: the Bean Tower (a giant Tall-One bean-pole wigwam, the town's landmark), the Grand Platz (main square), Teacup Row (cafe street), Radio Adagio (the classical-music station) and the Beetle Market.
