Loan officer at the First Kiln Bank. Polite, precise and never loud: adds 1,500 Acorns to the player's Kiln-Debt for a flat and thanks the player for the business.

## What gnomes say

"Inkpot will see you now. Inkpot has always seen you."

"The word is never 'debt'. The word is 'the balance of your arrangement'."

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Home: a bedsit off the bank's back stair (hidden). Work: First Kiln Bank. Gives the loan in #2 `m02-roof-on-credit`; that is the first and last main quest (side quests later). Mabel Kerbstone's cab takes the player to the bank. The bank keeps the ledger; Clay-Mart only sold the original debt (R-166).
