The stallholder at the Pure-Loam produce row on the allotment block: a cheerful, muddy gnome in a sun hat, who sells
cabbages from a handcart and insists the price is fair.

## What gnomes say

"No chemicals, no engines, just dirt. Two Acorns a cabbage." Parsnip says this to every customer, including the ones who
have just asked for a fertiliser the Co-op does not sell.

## Inside & Stories

Parsnip runs the produce stall on the east path with a ledger written in soil, and gives a free carrot to any child who
can name three kinds of compost. Nobody has ever named three, and the carrots keep coming.

## Loose threads

Who keeps slipping Stink-Gro leaflets into the Pure-Loam ledger, and whether Parsnip has been reading them.
