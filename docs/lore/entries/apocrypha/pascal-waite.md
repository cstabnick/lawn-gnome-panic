The first in the queue on the Left Bank, Pardonlin: grey hat, brown shirt, grey beard, who has been first since the last strike and says whether it moves is not his department.

## What gnomes say

"I have been first in the queue since the last strike. Whether it moves is not my department."

## The truth

The queue is for nothing yet; it formed because there was a gap and Pascal stood in it. He is gently proud of being first, and gentler about being alone.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E30): the Left Bank at about (-1736, -223), "wait-bus" idle; part of the "queue for nothing" vignette.
