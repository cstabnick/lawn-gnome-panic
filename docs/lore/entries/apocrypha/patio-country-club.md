A spotless, manicured astroturf country club atop Patio Heights where Vesper Thorne and HOA lawyers sip tea beside silent electric golf carts.

## Field Notes & Tales

Every blade of synthetic grass is brushed to identical height each dawn. Membership requires porcelain credentials or an HOA board seat—making it Baroness Brenda's absolute favorite target when she leads Push-Mower Bike raids to kink Tall-One garden hoses and flood the eighteenth hole.

## What gnomes say

"Best turf in the yard, worst company."

## In the games

The Putting-Green Country Club is the north-east corner block beyond the Patio Heights roads: a walled close-cut putting green with flagpole pins and a clubhouse terrace (stage `putting-green`).

## Loose threads

What Vesper keeps locked inside the club's trophy cabinet.

When Brenda's raid kinks the club's sprinkler hose, Vesper measures the slope with brass calipers and blames a caddie named Gerald. The eighteenth hole floods, the members flee, and Vesper stands in the mud with his monocle unmoved.
