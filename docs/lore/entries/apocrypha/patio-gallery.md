An open-air row of plinths along the north wall of Patio Heights: a porcelain cat, a golden remote, and a Replica Stopping Stone propping up a painting. Everything on display is for sale, and nothing is for sale to anybody who has not been invited.

## What gnomes say
*"Nothing on this lawn is for sale, except everything."* (the gallery placard)

## In the games
Stage `patio-gallery` (dawn). Vesper inspects the astroturf here at 06:00 with her calipers, and Mayor Mulch's kickback handover happens on the green.
