The gated flagstone terrace's homeowners' association, chaired by Vesper Thorne through Thorne Lawn & Order LLC. It
passes covenants, not laws, and its fines end in foreclosure at the Glaze & Tax Office.

## What gnomes say

"Curb appeal is not a suggestion." Reginald Vance is the one who comes to say so in person.

## The truth

This is Gnome Theft Auto's HOA. It is not the unseen HOA of Gnome Company's sermons (`hoa`): the two only share a name.
Reginald Vance is its enforcer.

## In the games

- The HOA's courier vans run the ground road and never the Skyway (The Skyway Job, R-152).
- The HOA seized Local 404's strike-fund ledger and keeps it at Fort Sandpit's vault.
- Pip Potsworth's Zero-Turn was impounded by the HOA (Impound Run).
