An elevated, impeccably swept flagstone terrace overlooking Turfton's northeast corner, walled by wrought-iron fencing and patrolled by silent electric carts.

## Field Notes & Tales

Long ago, Patio Heights was a Tall-One sun terrace where dropped sunglasses were revered as tinted skylights. Vesper Thorne bought the entire slab, evicted the pigeons, and laid spotless astroturf across every inch. Every flagstone is pressure-washed at 6:00 AM sharp, and porcelain residents file noise complaints if a Push-Mower Bike idles within two blocks.

## What gnomes say
