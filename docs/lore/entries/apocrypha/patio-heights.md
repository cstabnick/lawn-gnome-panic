An elevated, impeccably swept flagstone terrace overlooking Turfton's northeast corner, walled by wrought-iron fencing and patrolled by silent electric carts.

## Field Notes & Tales

Long ago, Patio Heights was a Tall-One sun terrace where dropped sunglasses were revered as tinted skylights. Vesper Thorne bought the entire slab, evicted the pigeons, and laid spotless astroturf across every inch. Every flagstone is pressure-washed at 6:00 AM sharp, and porcelain residents file noise complaints if a Push-Mower Bike idles within two blocks.

## What gnomes say

"Porcelain gnomes and HOA lawyers live up there; earthenware gnomes only go up to mow it."

## In the games

Vesper's Patio Heights gallery is the open-air row of plinths along the compound's north wall (stage `patio-gallery`, see `patio-gallery`). Mayor Mulch's kickback handover happens on the green there.

## Landmarks

- The compound's north wall: the row of gallery plinths (see `patio-gallery`).
- The covered garden pergola at the far north-east corner: locked, and the only building in the terrace nobody has been inside.
- The Glaze & Tax Office: where HOA fines end, in foreclosure (R-149). See `turfton-glaze-tax-office`.

## Local slang

- "curb appeal": said like a threat, usually by somebody from the HOA who has just looked at your lawn.
- "a quarter-inch": any petty fine, after Reginald Vance's measuring ruler.
- "flagstoned": foreclosed. The house is gone and the terrace is swept.
