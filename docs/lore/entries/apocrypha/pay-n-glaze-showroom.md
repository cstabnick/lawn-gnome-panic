The Custom Shop's front room: a great clay pot tipped on its side in a patch of dirt at the edge of nowhere, with a lit round doorway, a flower and a spill of pink glaze under the lip.

## Inside the pot

The doorway glows. Through it, the shop is dressed for a showroom: a rack of spray cans with the finish names on their caps, a giant wrench leaning on the wall, a tyre stack, a row of buckets for the rinse, and a sign over the counter that buzzes in dots and dashes. Neon Moth Nina reads the sign for anyone who asks. Nobody else seems to need to.

## Driving in

When a gnome drives in, the pot takes the mower out the back. The mower goes in at the doorway and the pot takes it, with the gnome still at the wheel, to the workshop yard. Nobody sees how. The mower comes back later at the same spot, with the same loyalty number and no VIN, looking exactly as it did, or a little better.

## What gnomes say

"Go in the front and you come out the back. That's the Custom Shop for you."

"The pot's not the shop. The shop's the bit out the back, and you don't ask about the bit out the back."

## Loose threads
