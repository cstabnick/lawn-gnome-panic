The seven finishes and ten sprays the shop lays on a mower's body, from a flat primer grey to a sheen that changes as you walk round it.

## Finishes

- **Kiln Gloss**: the factory shine. Fired twice, polished once.
- **Dust-Bowl Matte**: no shine at all. Draws no attention, which is the point.
- **Mica-Flake Metallic**: crushed mica that winks at the sun.
- **Snail-Trail Pearl**: a slow, pearly sheen, harvested at dawn.
- **Candy-Apple Lacquer**: deep, wet colour. Looks edible. Do not lick it.
- **Chameleon Beetle-Shift**: changes colour as you walk round it. Gnomes have lost bets over the real colour.
- **Primer Grey, VIN Off**: a flat undercoat. Nobody can say what the number was.

## Sprays

- **Flame-Job Aerosol**: licking flames along both flanks and across the hood.
- **Twin Racing Stripes**: two bold bars from nose to tail.
- **Chequered Chaser**: a band of black-and-white squares. The Hat Watch dislike it on principle.
- **Starry Mulch**: a scatter of stars, like a very small night sky.
- **Mulch Camo**: blotches of bark and leaf. Vanishes into any hedge.
- **Thunder-Bolt Stencil**: a jagged bolt down each side. Smells of rain.
- **Lucky Number**: a big racing number on each flank. Pick your own.
- **Hat Repeat**: rows of little pointed hats, all the way round.
- **Daisy Chain**: a cheerful chain of daisies. Entirely harmless-looking.
- **Glaze Drip**: paint that appears to be sliding down the doors. It is not. Probably.

## What gnomes say

The "aerosol" is a bellows can of pressurised enamel, pumped by hand in the bay. The pearl is harvested from snail
trails at dawn, and the snails are not asked.

"Shine is just clay that has been told to behave."
