The small parts the Custom Shop bolts onto a mower: lamps and their beams, underglow, spoilers, seats, steering wheels, hood ornaments and extras.

## Headlights

- **Bug-Eye Rounds**: big round lamps that look permanently surprised.
- **Square Dealers**: boxy lamps with a stern look.
- **Halo Rings**: a glowing ring around each lamp. Very angelic.
- **Pop-Up Peepers**: lamps that sit in little hoods, peeking out.
- **Barn Lanterns**: glass lanterns on little brackets. Warm and old-fashioned.
- **Light-Bar Blaster**: one long bar of light across the nose.
- **Acorn Lamps**: lamps shaped like acorns. Nobody asked for these.

Beam colours: **Candle Yellow** (the usual warm glow), **Ice-Blue Xenon** (oncoming gnomes squint), **Fog Amber**
(for the foggiest mornings), **Rose Blush** (surprisingly intimidating), **Firefly Green** (a summer evening) and
**Dusk Violet** (for the dramatic).

## Underglow

The glow comes from glow-worms kept in jars under the sills. The shop sells six colours: **Glow-Worm Green**, **Moth
Blue**, **Bramble Pink**, **Marigold**, **Violet Hour** and **Ember Red**. The jars are not topped up by the shop.

## Spoilers

- **Duck-Tail**: a little upturned lip on the tail. Understated.
- **Picket Wing**: a section of white picket fence, bolted on as a wing.
- **Barn-Door Wing**: a big flat door on two posts. Holds you down, or up.
- **Trellis GT**: a lattice wing for the sweet peas to climb.
- **Biplane Wing**: two wings, stacked. Because one is not enough.
- **Dorsal Fin**: a single tall fin. Makes everything feel faster, and a bit shark.
- **Giant Leaf Lip**: a broad green leaf. Smells fresh in the rain.

## Seats

- **Quilted Bench**: a patchwork cushion stitched from three old aprons.
- **Racing Bucket**: a deep bucket that hugs the gnome tight.
- **Cowhide Saddle**: stitched leather with a high back. Yeehaw.
- **Bean-Bag Lounger**: a squashy lounger. Lovely, if you do not steer hard.
- **Garden Throne**: carved back, gold knobs. Fit for an unelected mayor.
- **Tractor Springer**: a metal pan seat on a big coil spring. It bounces.
- **Tea-Cosy Cushion**: woolly, bobbled and warm.

## Steering wheels

- **Racing Dish**: a small, flat-bottomed wheel with a leather rim.
- **Banjo Sprig**: three thin spokes and a wooden rim. Very old money.
- **Chunky Donut**: a fat, squashy rim. Gnomes can hold it with their whole arm.
- **Daisy Wheel**: a wheel with petals. It steers just the same.
- **Pretzel Wheel**: twisted into a knot. Nobody knows which way is up.

## Hood ornaments

- **Mini Gnome Hat**: a tiny pointed hat on the bonnet. A hat for the hat.
- **Golden Acorn**: a solid gold acorn. Very hard to steal, very easy to lose.
- **Chrome Snail**: a shiny snail, forever on its way somewhere.
- **Lawn Flamingo**: a pink flamingo on one leg. Dignified.
- **Dragonfly Pilot**: a dragonfly with a goggled head, leaning into the wind.
- **Trowel Standard**: a little trowel, pointing the way.
- **Weathervane Rooster**: a rooster that points where the wind came from.
- **Fly-Agaric Knob**: a red, white-spotted toadstool. Do not eat.
- **Tiny Hood Gnome**: a little gnome riding the bonnet with both fists in the air.

## Extras

- **Fuzzy Dice**: two huge fluffy dice swinging by the seat.
- **Whip-Antenna Flag**: a tall whippy aerial with a little pennant.
- **Stovepipe Exhausts**: two shiny chimneys that pop and crackle.
- **Mudflap Gnomes**: a shiny flap behind each rear wheel, each with a gnome silhouette.
- **Parade Bunting**: a string of little triangle flags across the top.
- **Vanity Plate**: a plate that reads GNOME 1. Obviously.
- **Hedge-Bar Bumper**: a tough bar to plough through hedges.
- **Bicycle Bell**: ring ring! Mounted on the handlebar or dash.
