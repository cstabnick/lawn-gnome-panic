The small parts the Custom Shop bolts onto a mower: lamps and their beams, underglow, spoilers, seats, hood ornaments, extras, grilles, bumpers, canopies, mirrors, exhausts and horns.

## Headlights

- **Bug-Eye Rounds**: big round lamps that look permanently surprised.
- **Square Dealers**: boxy lamps with a stern look.
- **Halo Rings**: a glowing ring around each lamp. Very angelic.
- **Pop-Up Peepers**: lamps that sit in little hoods, peeking out.
- **Barn Lanterns**: glass lanterns on little brackets. Warm and old-fashioned.
- **Light-Bar Blaster**: one long bar of light across the nose.
- **Acorn Lamps**: lamps shaped like acorns. Nobody asked for these.
- **Cat-Eye Slits**: slanted slit lamps that look sly, and slightly bored.
- **Goggle Lamps**: two big round lenses in brass frames. Very aviator.
- **Candle Lamps**: flickering candle-flame lamps. Fire regulations ignored.
- **Spotlight Cans**: big spotlights in tin cans, aimed at the hedge.

Beam colours: **Candle Yellow** (the usual warm glow), **Ice-Blue Xenon** (oncoming gnomes squint), **Fog Amber**
(for the foggiest mornings), **Rose Blush** (surprisingly intimidating), **Firefly Green** (a summer evening),
**Dusk Violet** (for the dramatic), **Mint Frost** (a cool glow that smells faintly of sweets), **Crimson Menace** (a
red glow that says do not overtake), **Sunset Blush** (for the golden hour) and **Sky Blue** (cheerful and clear).

## Underglow

The glow comes from glow-worms kept in jars under the sills. The shop sells ten colours: **Glow-Worm Green**, **Moth
Blue**, **Bramble Pink**, **Marigold**, **Violet Hour**, **Ember Red**, **Sunset Smoke**, **Frost Cyan**, **Lilac Fog**
and **Lemon Zing**. The jars are not topped up by the shop.

## Spoilers

- **Duck-Tail**: a little upturned lip on the tail. Understated.
- **Picket Wing**: a section of white picket fence, bolted on as a wing.
- **Barn-Door Wing**: a big flat door on two posts. Holds you down, or up.
- **Trellis GT**: a lattice wing for the sweet peas to climb.
- **Biplane Wing**: two wings, stacked. Because one is not enough.
- **Dorsal Fin**: a single tall fin. Makes everything feel faster, and a bit shark.
- **Giant Leaf Lip**: a broad green leaf. Smells fresh in the rain.
- **Sail Wing**: a canvas sail that catches the breeze, and occasionally the gnome.
- **Rake-Tine Wing**: wooden rake tines fanned out as a spoiler. Mind the leaves.
- **Watering-Can Wing**: a tin watering can tipped over the back. Sprinkles the rear.
- **Butterfly Wings**: two painted butterfly wings on the tail. Delicate, and very fast-looking.

## Seats

- **Quilted Bench**: a patchwork cushion stitched from three old aprons.
- **Racing Bucket**: a deep bucket that hugs the gnome tight.
- **Cowhide Saddle**: stitched leather with a high back. Yeehaw.
- **Bean-Bag Lounger**: a squashy lounger. Lovely, if you do not steer hard.
- **Garden Throne**: carved back, gold knobs. Fit for an unelected mayor.
- **Tractor Springer**: a metal pan seat on a big coil spring. It bounces.
- **Tea-Cosy Cushion**: woolly, bobbled and warm.
- **Toadstool Stool**: a fat red-capped toadstool to perch on. Very squishy.
- **Barrel Seat**: an old wine barrel cut in half. Rolls with every bend.
- **Teacup Seat**: a china teacup, pastel and round. Mind the saucer.
- **Hay-Bale Seat**: a bale of hay with a cushion on top. Smells like summer.

## Hood ornaments

- **Mini Gnome Hat**: a tiny pointed hat on the bonnet. A hat for the hat.
- **Golden Acorn**: a solid gold acorn. Very hard to steal, very easy to lose.
- **Chrome Snail**: a shiny snail, forever on its way somewhere.
- **Lawn Flamingo**: a pink flamingo on one leg. Dignified.
- **Dragonfly Pilot**: a dragonfly with a goggled head, leaning into the wind.
- **Trowel Standard**: a little trowel, pointing the way.
- **Weathervane Rooster**: a rooster that points where the wind came from.
- **Fly-Agaric Knob**: a red, white-spotted toadstool. Do not eat.
- **Tiny Hood Gnome**: a little gnome riding the bonnet with both fists in the air.
- **Brass Bee**: a brass bee on a spring that bobs on every bump.
- **Ladybird**: a spotted ladybird on the bonnet, waiting for aphids.
- **Dandelion Clock**: loses a seed every time you brake. Make a wish.
- **Chrome Spray Can**: a chrome spray can on a stick. Mostly decorative. Mostly.

## Extras

- **Fuzzy Dice**: two huge fluffy dice swinging by the seat.
- **Whip-Antenna Flag**: a tall whippy aerial with a little pennant.
- **Stovepipe Exhausts**: two shiny chimneys that pop and crackle.
- **Mudflap Gnomes**: a shiny flap behind each rear wheel, each with a gnome silhouette.
- **Parade Bunting**: a string of little triangle flags across the top.
- **Vanity Plate**: a plate that reads GNOME 1. Obviously.

- **Fairy Lights**: strings of tiny lights round the windows. Fairy-approved.
- **Little Toolbox**: a tin toolbox clipped to the rear. It rattles.
- **Picnic Cooler**: strapped on the back. Lemonade included.
- **Windsock**: a striped windsock on a pole. Shows which way the gnome is going.

## Grilles

- **Picket Grille**: a white picket fence across the nose. Keeps the hedgehogs out.
- **Chicken-Wire Grille**: fine wire mesh across the nose. Keeps the bees out.
- **Toothy Grin**: a big toothy grin in chrome. Looks hungry.
- **Letterbox Slot**: a thin horizontal slot in the nose. Post, anyone?

## Bumpers

- **Chrome Bar**: a shiny chrome bar across the front. Shiny is safety.
- **Cowcatcher**: a curved iron cowcatcher to shove cows, leaves and rivals aside.
- **Log Bumper**: a chunky log bolted to the front. Very rustic.
- **Tyre Fender**: old tyres bolted to the front. Squishy and loud.

## Canopies

- **Garden Umbrella**: a striped umbrella over the seat. Rain? What rain.
- **Striped Awning**: a striped awning on little poles. Shade for the whole car.
- **Glass Bubble**: a glass dome over the cabin. Lovely view, slightly warm.
- **Leaf Parasol**: a big green leaf on a stalk. Smells of rain and compost.
- **Toadstool Roof**: a red spotted toadstool cap over the driver. Do not eat the roof.

## Mirrors

- **Wing Mirrors**: classic wing mirrors, big and sensible.
- **Spoon Mirrors**: curved spoon mirrors. Distorts everything very cheerfully.
- **Snail-Eye Stalks**: mirrors on wobbly stalks that look all round the lawn.
- **Periscope**: a periscope that sees over the hedge. Very submarine.

## Exhausts

- **Smoke Stack**: a tall smoke stack out of the roof. Puffs on request.
- **Twin Tailpipes**: two chrome tailpipes side by side. Loud, obviously.
- **Tin Chimney**: a little tin chimney with a tiny curl of smoke.
- **Trumpet Pipe**: a flared trumpet exhaust. Gives the engine a brass section.

## Horns

A gnome's horn is a little brass bellows and a reed. Squeaks, moos and kazoos are reeds from the Verge, and the shop
will not say which reeds it buys from whom.

- **Rubber Duck**: a squeezy rubber-duck horn. Quack, quack, outrage.
- **Cow Moo**: a long, deep moo. Every cow on the lane answers.
- **Party Trumpet**: a cheerful party-trumpet blast. Invites everyone round.
- **Sheriff Siren**: a wailing siren. Not actually the sheriff. Yet.
- **Bicycle Ding**: a brisk ding-ding on a bell. Polite, mostly.
- **Kazoo**: a kazoo hum of pure menace. Hard to take seriously.
