How the custom shop takes work. A client drops their mower at the Turfton lot with a brief ("make it look like my nan's hat") and a parts budget. The mechanic glazes the mower to the brief, and the client rates the result on the spot, in front of everyone.

## What gnomes say

"The brief is the brief. The budget is also the brief."

## In the games

Planned (E24). The mechanic job: take a card from Cass's pegboard, fit parts within the budget, and hand the mower back for a rating. See R-194 for who pays what.
