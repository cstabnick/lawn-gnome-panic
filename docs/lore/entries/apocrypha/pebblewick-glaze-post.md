The glaze post in Pebblewick, where cracked gnomes go to be re-glazed. The dry wind causes most of the cracks.

## What gnomes say

"Hairline cracks, mostly. The dry wind does it."

## The truth

Doc Comfrey Mortar can fix most cracks in an afternoon, but the wind comes back every day, so the work never really ends.

## In the games

On a Pebblewick side street by the water tower. Doc Comfrey Mortar keeps the post.

## Loose threads

Whether the wind is a gnome.
