The open sand square at the middle of Pebblewick, x 662 to 700, z 72 to 112, where the town has a bell, a bus and a reeve, and holds its news. There is a wishing well in the middle, two benches that face each other, and a palm that has been there as long as the town.

## What gnomes say

"The square is where Pebblewick decides things. It decides them at about walking pace."

"Toss an acorn in the well. If it comes back, it was not yours."

## The truth

The square is unpaved sand, swept every morning by Reeve Yarrow Potsherd. The wishing well stands at (681, 92) and takes acorns from the square side; the benches are at (681, 77) and (681, 107); the Dry Spoon, Twine's General and the post office face it from Main Street. The town hall (bell rope on its front step at (662.3, 141)) is on the Weeping Lane side; the Green with the founder's statue is south at (689, 168). In act IV the bell rings once for the water when the pump is humming (`m58-the-reeves-barn`).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E10): Pebblewick's marketplace and the base for its townsgnomes; the Reeve keeps it. `m58-the-reeves-barn` begins here (Yarrow sweeping and telling the player the town has "a bell, a bus and a reeve, and now an HOA letter").

## Everyday

- **Notices:** the town hall door carries the week's notices, among them the reeve's minutes (see [the reeve's minutes](reeves-minutes.md)); Pebblewick's notice board stands by the square.
- **Habits:** at noon the benches face each other and nobody sits on the left one. The well takes one Acorn at a time, "which is how you tell a wish from a donation". Meetings begin at dusk on the Green and end when the argument does.
- **Sayings:** "Toss an acorn in the well. If it comes back, it was not yours." "The square decides at about walking pace."
