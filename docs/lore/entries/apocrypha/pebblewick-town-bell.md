The hall bell on Pebblewick's town hall, rung by a rope on the front step at (662, 141), once a day for the news and once for any water that arrives.

## What gnomes say

"One ring is a notice. Two is a meeting. None is a normal day."

"The bell rings once for the water, and once more if the water is good."

## The truth

The bell is tin, as is the town. It was cast from the lids of a dozen seed tins, which is why it sounds a little like a seed tin. A sight in the town lets the player ring it (`outskirts/sights.ts`).

## In the games

Act IV, `m58-the-reeves-barn`: the bell rings once at dusk when the pump is humming.

## Everyday

- **Notices:** a card on the rope: "Please pull once. We heard you."
- **Habits:** the reeve rings it for any news; children ring it for no news, and are asked kindly to stop.
- **Sayings:** "A bell, a bus and a reeve."
