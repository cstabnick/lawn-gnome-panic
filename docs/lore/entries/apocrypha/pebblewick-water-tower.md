Pebblewick's landmark: a tin tank on four legs at (720, 176) over the town's well and hen pen, with a valve wheel at the foot of one leg. It is the town's pump, its clock (the shadow) and its argument with the HOA's water office.

## What gnomes say

"You can see it from the highway. That is how you know you are nearly there, and how the HOA knows."

"The tank is tin. The water is gold. The HOA would like both."

## The truth

The tower stands over the well that Pebblewick grew up around, and over the hen pen at (716, 158). Mallow Bucket's map of arrows is pinned to one leg (it is where she gives "The Hum at Three"). In `m58-the-reeves-barn` the HOA cuts the town's water at sunset unless the pump runs, and the crew's fix, with a spray-pump impeller fetched from Wally's hangar, is flown in by the Whirligig and landed beside it. After that, the tower flies a small coalition pennant (W15).

## In the games

Prefab `pebblewick-water-tower` (a landmark, solid); the valve wheel is an interact spot; the stage `pebblewick-tower` is at its foot. A pennant is added by W15.

## Everyday

- **Habits:** the shadow of the tank is the town clock: "tower on the well" means lunch, "tower on the bus stop" means dusk. The valve wheel is turned by the reeve, one turn each morning.
- **Notices:** painted in white on a leg: "Water: for Pebblewick. Letters: for the HOA." Mallow Bucket's map of arrows is pinned beside it.
- **Sayings:** "You can see it from the highway." "The tank is tin. The water is gold."
