A small adobe town of about thirty gnomes at the far east of the Dust Flats, where Route East runs out towards the fence. Dry pepper plots line the Weeping Line, and it is the last stop before the Lost & Found Speedway.

## What gnomes say

"Pebblewick is a town, not a stop. We have a bell, a bus and a reeve."

"Every racer stops here twice. Once on the way out, once on a tow."

## The truth

Pebblewick grew up around a well dug in the dunes before anyone mapped the Flats. The adobe walls keep the day's heat out and let it back in at night, which is why the doors stay shut until the sun goes down.

## In the games

The town sits at the end of Route East's extension, at the junction near (650, 20), on the eastern edge of the Dust Flats (x 580 to 730, z 10 to 200). A square with a palm on the Green, the Dry Spoon and Twine's General facing it, Ratchet's mower shed and the Glaze Post on the side streets, the tin water tower over the well, and the Pebblewick Hopper stop by the road. The look is adobe, wattle fences, palms and the tin water tower. Reeve Yarrow Potsherd keeps the square.

Act IV: Pebblewick is the crew's hiding place and the Guard's home. In `m58-the-reeves-barn` Yarrow offers his hay barn (`pebblewick-hay-barn`) for the Whirligig if the crew fixes the town's pump before the HOA cuts its water at sunset; the Whirligig is flown to the tin water tower (`pebblewick-water-tower`) and landed in the hay under a tarp, and the town bell rings once. In `m61-diggs-medal` Commander Diggs asks the player to his tin bungalow (`diggs-bungalow`) at the south edge of town. After the two rows the water tower flies a small coalition pennant (W15, `coalition-flag`). The reeve lives on the square (`reeves-house`).

## Everyday

- **Shops and menus:** Twine's General: Dust Biscuits 4 Acorns, Cactus Water 8, Pebblewick Postcard 5, Water Tower Globe 15, and the "Pop. 31" Mug 10 (the town has never corrected the number). The Dry Spoon: Bottomless Dust Coffee 3, Prickly Pear Pie 9, The Spoon Special 18, "bring your own spoon". Ratchet's Mower and Dew: Full Repair 35, "no questions, some answers".
- **The notice board:** "Lost: one hum. Last heard at three in the morning in the eastern dunes. - M. Bucket." "The Pebblewick Hopper leaves for Turfton whenever you ask it to. Fare at the stop." "Fizz Cup weekend: the Dry Spoon opens early. Bring your own spoon." "Somebody is tapping the Weeping Line. Reward. See Purslane at the plots." "Town meeting every dusk on the Green. Topic: still the highway."
- **Habits:** doors shut in the day and open at dusk; the bell rings once for any water that arrives; the reeve sweeps the square at dawn, always the same way. The town counts itself every New Year for the "Pop. 31" mug, and arrives at 31.
- **Sayings:** "Pebblewick is a town, not a stop. We have a bell, a bus and a reeve." "Every racer stops here twice. Once on the way out, once on a tow." "Water is a promise. The HOA would like it in writing."
- **Food:** prickly pear pie, baked in the sun; Dust Biscuits; Cactus Water.
- **Feud:** the town against the HOA's water office, and the bus against everyone.
- **Small history:** the well was dug before the Flats were mapped; the reeve's staff passed to Yarrow when his predecessor "went to Turfton for a week". See [the bell](pebblewick-town-bell.md) and [the reeve's minutes](reeves-minutes.md).
