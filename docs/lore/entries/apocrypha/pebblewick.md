A small adobe town of about thirty gnomes at the far east of the Dust Flats, where Route East runs out towards the fence. Dry pepper plots line the Weeping Line, and it is the last stop before the Lost & Found Speedway.

## What gnomes say

"Pebblewick is a town, not a stop. We have a bell, a bus and a reeve."

"Every racer stops here twice. Once on the way out, once on a tow."

## The truth

Pebblewick grew up around a well dug in the dunes before anyone mapped the Flats. The adobe walls keep the day's heat out and let it back in at night, which is why the doors stay shut until the sun goes down.

## In the games

The town sits at the end of Route East's extension, at the junction near (650, 20), on the eastern edge of the Dust Flats (x 580 to 730, z 10 to 200). A square with a palm on the Green, the Dry Spoon and Twine's General facing it, Ratchet's mower shed and the Glaze Post on the side streets, the tin water tower over the well, and the Pebblewick Hopper stop by the road. The look is adobe, wattle fences, palms and the tin water tower. Reeve Yarrow Potsherd keeps the square.
