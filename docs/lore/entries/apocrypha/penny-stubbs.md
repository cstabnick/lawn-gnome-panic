Keeper of Marram's Office: slate hat, cream shirt, glasses, who counts the change and has read every stub on the shelf twice.

## What gnomes say

"Every fare since the bucket sank. I have read them all twice."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (FX11, rulings R-244 and R-249): behind the counter of Marram's Office at (148, 1109.5), by the stub shelf and the pile of lost hats. Look: A slate-grey hat, a cream shirt and glasses.
