Owner of Hedgeworth Links: a straight-backed old gnome in a tweed cap, who has run the course since before the hedges grew and keeps the club's mortgage in a drawer she does not open.

Home: rooms above the Hedgeworth Clubhouse. Work: Hedgeworth Links.

## What gnomes say

"Dame Peony has never missed a tea or a putt. She will tell you which she minds more."

"She is polite to everyone. It is a method."

## The truth

Peony trusts Vesper because he is the one gnome who has never asked her to change a rule. She will not hear a word against him until she is beaten fairly at her own game, and then she hears all of them, in order.

## In the games

Built (batch 3, E15): standing on the clubhouse terrace over the first tee, looking it over with her driver for a cane. Quest 29 (`m29-the-kicked-board`) glimpses her; she gives 47 (`m47-hedgeworth-links`), and lets the crew in once the player beats Basil at a hole; she is evacuated in 52 (`m52-the-test-cut`) and present in 70 (`ch5-even-cut`).
