Fast, reckless aerial parcel service that delivers packages by dropping them straight from altitude—regularly flattening mailboxes across Cloverfield Farms.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"Dropped From Altitude, Good Luck."* Pigeon-Drop prides itself on thirty-minute delivery anywhere within the Fence, provided you don't mind your fragile ceramics arriving inside a crater three feet from your barn.

## Consumer Reports

Great for anvil-grade spare Combine parts; catastrophic for Acorn-Cola bottles and porcelain ornaments.

## Loose threads

Who trained the pigeons to aim specifically for Cloverfield's red tin mailboxes.
