The Pigeon-Drop Depot is an H-shaped concrete block with ribbon windows and a helipad on the roof, where the couriers' pigeons come in to land. It is lower than its neighbours, because every newcomer's welcome flight over Turfton passes low over it.

## What gnomes say

"The Depot is short because the pigeons need the headroom. That is the official line, and it is also the true line, if you ask a pigeon."

"If you see the helipad, you have arrived. If you do not see the helipad, you are being flown over it for the first time."

## In the games

It stands on the x = 20, z = 90 block of Downtown Turfton, a low H-shaped concrete block with ribbon windows and a helipad. It is kept lower than its neighbours, and the intro tour's camera passes low over it. Its shape and helipad are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
