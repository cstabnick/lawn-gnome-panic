The Tunnel twins: two very small moles, very fast, who are never where they were told to be and always where a snack is.

## What gnomes say

"I saw one of them. Or the other. It is the same mole, twice."

## The truth

They are not the same mole. Pim says "if it pleases you" the loudest, Pod the quietest, and neither has ever been told off for long.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: run about the tunnels of Loamdeep, and are first to cheer a lit lamp.
