The Tunnel twins: two very small moles, very fast, who are never where they were told to be and always where a snack is.

## What gnomes say

"I saw one of them. Or the other. It is the same mole, twice."

## The truth

They are not the same mole. Pim says "if it pleases you" the loudest, Pod the quietest, and neither has ever been told off for long. They are Delves, cousins' children to Mistress Delve (`loamdeep-clans`), which is why they are first to a lit lamp. Pod counts the moss, and reports a hundred and twelve; it was a hundred and twelve yesterday.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: run about the tunnels of Loamdeep, and are first to cheer a lit lamp.
