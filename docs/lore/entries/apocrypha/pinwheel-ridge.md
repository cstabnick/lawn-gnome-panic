A high ridge of giant garden pinwheels, red, yellow and blue, turning in the steady wind. They make the electricity for Turfton's lights and neon. Black Dew still runs the engines.

## What gnomes say

"Every neon sign in Turfton is a pinwheel up on the Ridge, spinning."
"Wind is free. The cable down to town is not."

## The truth

The pinwheels run lamps, signs and the Combine's cells, never an engine; that is why Black Dew has not gone out of business. The HOA re-cabled the Ridge to charge the Combine, and the power part of the Grand Combine is built here.

## In the games

Site wind-farm (R-202): ten giant pinwheels (`pinwheel-1` to `pinwheel-10`, red, yellow and blue in turn), each on a 24 m white-and-red mast with a ladder, 30 m apart along the crest from x 715 to 985 at z about -1202, all facing south and always turning. Ragwort Coilspring's control hut (860, -1234) and a substation (905, -1236) stand on the north flank below the crest; a gravel service track runs up from the north-band-east road. Flying along the ridge earns "Windfall". Quest row 41 (m41-pinwheel-ridge): Ragwort Coilspring shows the player three pinwheels to free; the lights come back in Turfton (world change W10).

## Everyday

**Shops.** None. Ragwort's hut is closed to visitors and open to wind.

**Notices.**
- Substation door: "HIGH VOLTAGE. HIGHER WIND."
- Hut door: "KNOCK TWICE. I CANNOT HEAR ONCE."
- HOA cable tag on the first mast: "PROPERTY OF THE ASSOCIATION", with "(IT IS MY CABLE. - R.C.)" scratched under it.

**Sayings and habits.**
- "Count the colours." Red, yellow, blue: Ragwort greets the dawn by naming the three in turn, and the pinwheels answer by starting.
- Every pinwheel has a name known only to her. She says a stuck one is "thinking".
- Turfton's neon blinks when the wind drops. Turfton calls it atmosphere. The Ridge calls it a Tuesday.

**Small history.** The Ridge began as one pinwheel left in the ground by a Tall One. It turned all summer and did not stop in autumn. More were added.
