A high ridge of giant garden pinwheels, red, yellow and blue, turning in the steady wind. They make the electricity for Turfton's lights and neon. Black Dew still runs the engines.

## What gnomes say

"Every neon sign in Turfton is a pinwheel up on the Ridge, spinning."
"Wind is free. The cable down to town is not."

## The truth

The pinwheels run lamps, signs and the Combine's cells, never an engine; that is why Black Dew has not gone out of business. The HOA re-cabled the Ridge to charge the Combine, and the power part of the Grand Combine is built here.

## In the games

Site wind-farm (R-192): ten giant pinwheels (`pinwheel-1` to `pinwheel-10`, red, yellow and blue in turn), each on a 24 m white-and-red mast with a ladder, 30 m apart along the crest from x 715 to 985 at z about -1202, all facing south and always turning. Ragwort Coilspring's control hut (860, -1234) and a substation (905, -1236) stand on the north flank below the crest; a gravel service track runs up from the north-band-east road. Flying along the ridge earns "Windfall". Quest row 41 (m41-pinwheel-ridge): Ragwort Coilspring shows the player three pinwheels to free; the lights come back in Turfton (world change W10).
