The pear picker of Espalier End: gold hat, olive shirt, with nine pears, one basket, and a hill with opinions. Not Pip Tussock of the Unmown.

## What gnomes say

"Nine pears, one basket, and a hill with opinions."

## The truth

The hill is steep and has views about pickers. Pip is on his third trip up and has never carried more than nine at a time.

Gnome Theft Auto (E23): Espalier End orchard at about (-1031, -464), "scratch-head" idle.
