Disgraced Fescue exile, self-taught two-stroke mechanic, and the protagonist's perpetually sweating best friend in Turfton.

## Journal: Oil-Stained Notebook (Page 14)

> *Tuesday. Tried to explain to Tansy that a Push-Mower Bike doesn't need brakes if you aim for soft bushes. She threw a 12mm wrench at my hat. Missed, hit a flowerpot, and a Hat Watch officer wrote ME a ticket for "unlicensed ceramics." Cousin Wendel never had to deal with this back in Fescue. Then again, Wendel never felt a twin-carb Zero-Turn hit 34 units an hour down `x = 0` with the wind in his beard. Worth it. Probably.*

Pip fled Fescue after bolting salvaged pram wheels onto a monastery wheelbarrow—an act the Elders called "Mower-sympathy." He arrived in Turfton with three bent screwdrivers, a crooked red cap, and an unshakeable belief that every problem can be solved by either hotwiring a mower or hiding behind a bigger gnome.

## What gnomes say

"If Pip offers you a 'legitimate import-export gig,' check whether the crate is humming before you lift it."

## The truth
Pip is the crew's mechanic and liability: an exiled Fescue gnome and a Potsworth, the regent's cousin, which is why Elder Bracken fears him ("another Potsworth leading the Mower to them"). He wants to be useful, now, loudly, and fears being the family embarrassment. He loves Tansy as a boss and a sister he would not say it to. Home: a hammock in the back office of Side-Valve Salvage, then the player's couch at Saucer Court from #39. Voice: fast, run-on, engine names, exclamation marks; his tic "Okay, hear me out" is allowed once an act; a second sentence undoes the first; bad at numbers; never cruel.

Arc: the liability who drags the player in (I), then a first sincere beat on the Drainpipe Court bench; shamed by Bracken and looking through the knot-hole at Fescue (II); the arm he loses in the Salvage Raid, one sleeve pinned empty from then on, and a joke through the pain (II-III); in act IV the plan-man at last ("Okay, hear me out", and Tansy lets him), who rigs the Whirligig and says the line that sends them in. Dodges every question about Fescue; the vial of Fescue soil in his toolbox is opened at #26 and not poured.
