The junior ranger of the Unmown: an olive-hatted, beardless gnome counting the reeds of Reedmere by hand and losing count whenever a visitor walks past.

## What gnomes say

"Four thousand and six reeds. Four thousand and seven. Please stop walking past, you keep moving them."

## The truth

Not Pip Pearsley of Espalier End; two different gnomes with the same first name. Pip has been counting since spring and says the total is a mood.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E15): on the trail near Reedmere, "look-around" idle.

## Loose threads

Whether the number was ever going to hold still.
