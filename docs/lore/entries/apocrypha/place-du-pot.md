The second roundabout of Pardonlin, at the south end of the Quays, with a ring of cafes and townhouses facing in and a cobbled belt round it. Its sign is the town's best joke and it does not explain it.

## What gnomes say

"Place du Pot (Pot Not Included). They mean it."

"If you are looking for the pot, it is not here. Also not there."

## The truth

Place du Pot is at (-1500, 450), radius 14 m, smaller than the Hatwheel and proud of it. A cobbled belt runs six metres clear of the ring, and the market square lies before the hall to the north, where the Rue Haute meets the quay road. The roundabout is the Continentals' last stop before the Little Gardens and the Basin; they hold that anything with a pot is a different town.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E30): the south hub of the east bank, with the Potager (allotments and a bean for scale) east of the Rue Haute and the Square of Perpetual Argument north of it.
