The surveyor at the Keyhole. He has measured the arch over the Spine Cut every day for years, with a plumb line, a tape and a notebook of figures, and he has never been sure of the answer.

## What gnomes say

"Not yet. I'm nearly sure. Ask me on Thursday."
"Bob's been measuring that arch since before the road. He says it's got two sides, and he's only checked one."

## The truth

The surveyor's job was to finish the measurements before the Spine was cut. He never did, because each day the arch looked slightly different: the plumb line swings in the wind off the Ridge (R-232). The Highways Sub-Committee has never asked him for a figure, which he takes as a compliment.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): he works at the Keyhole, the rock arch over the Spine Cut near (520, -1170), between the north and east band roads. The Pinwheel Ridge works are to the east.
