The north-west hall of Loamdeep and the Sifts' own: a wall of pigeonholes, a high counter and a postmaster who has never lost a letter. A great deal comes in; nothing, so far, has been answered.

## What gnomes say

"They sort a letter by who it is for. Then they sort it again by whether they would like it."

"Four hundred and twelve letters to the surface. Not one reply. They keep sending."

## The truth

Postern is 20 by 16 metres of local floor (x -50 to -30, z -38 to -22). The sorting desk where Post Sorting is played stands at (-40, -34). The signs read "POSTERN HALL / All post sorted by Mister Sift (eventually)", "LETTERS TO THE SURFACE: 0 answered, 412 sent" and, on a stamped returned letter on the round counter, "NO SUCH BURROW". One whole pigeonhole is stuffed with letters addressed upward and labelled "TO: THE SURFACE". Post for the Mother's Hall goes in the top holes and everyone else's in the lower ones. Nubbin carries the surface post out and brings back what little there is.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the north-west hall of the Loamdeep interior; Post Sorting (a sorting minigame at the desk) and `sq-burrow-post`. Mister Sift keeps it.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** Stamps: one is an Acorn, three are a hug. A pigeonhole for every burrow and one labelled "TO: THE SURFACE", always full.
- **Notices:** "POSTERN HALL. All post sorted by Mister Sift (eventually)." "LETTERS TO THE SURFACE: 0 answered, 412 sent." A stamped returned letter: "NO SUCH BURROW".
- **Sayings and habits:** "Sorted by who it is for, then by whether they would like it." Every letter gets a small bow before it is filed.
