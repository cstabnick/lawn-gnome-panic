A cracked Clay-Mart terracotta flowerpot bolted to a plank chassis, painted with flames, and fitted with wheels that have never once been asked to go fast.

## Field Notes

The Pot Rod turns up now and then in Turfton traffic, usually wobbling along the ring road at the speed of a sulking snail with a large amount of paint on it. Its top speed is 13 units an hour, about the pace of a determined walk and roughly a third of a Push-Mower Bike. The flames are hand-painted and very confident. The pot is not.

Somebody at Clay-Mart sold the pot as a seconds lot, which is why it has a crack down one side and a sticker that says "Ask us about our patio". The wheels came off a lawn trolley. The seat is a folded doormat.

## What gnomes say

"Looks like it's on fire. Isn't. Would be quicker if it were."
