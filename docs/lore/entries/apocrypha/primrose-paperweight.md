The secretary at the Annex's reception desk. She says "Hello." to every applicant; nobody says it back. She is the
first gnome the player meets and the last one they talk to.

## What gnomes say

"Hello."

"I collect nothing. That's my secret."

"Oh. Nobody ever says that." (when the player says they are not taking the job)

## The truth

Her nameplate reads "Primrose Paperweight, Reception". On the fourth reading it reads "Second Class". She asks, at the
end, "Was it the interview? Or did you nod off in the chair? It's a very comfortable chair." She does not answer it.

## In the games

- `games/nothing-left-to-collect`, prologue: "Hello.", the choice to say it back (achievement Hello.), and the lift to
  Floor 1. Examining her nameplate four times is the easter egg Still Primrose.
- The ending: "Hello." again, a new line for each "Hello." back, and then "I'm not taking the job." (achievement I'm
  Not Taking the Job). "Mind the step. And don't collect anything on your way out."
- The credits: "Primrose Paperweight as Herself".
