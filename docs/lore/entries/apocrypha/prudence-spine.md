The riverside bookseller, who sells books out of green boxes on the quay wall and will not sell the one you asked for.

## What gnomes say

"Madame Spine has every book. She will not tell you which are in the boxes."

"She sold a man a book once. He has not been the same since."

## The truth

The boxes are never quite open and the padlocks are always on. Prudence reads each book before she sells it and withholds the ones she thinks you are not ready for, which is most of them. She is almost always right.

## In the games

The riverside quay wall beside the Thymes Walk.
