The allotment block on the south-east edge of Turfton: raised timber beds of dark soil, cold frames, water butts and
little sheds, all rented out by the Pure-Loam Co-op to any townsgnome who promises not to bring an engine onto the
plots. A produce stall row runs along the east side.

## What gnomes say

"The soil does the work. You just have to stand there looking patient." Plot holders talk to their carrots, and the
carrots, the plot holders say, talk back in a whisper that only the very patient can hear.

## Inside & Stories

- The beds: rows of raised, timber-edged plots with cabbages, carrot tops, and bean canes up to knee height. Every
  plot has a little sign with the holder's name on it, and every sign is in a different hand.
- Cold frames with sloped glass lids, left slightly open, with seedlings inside.
- Water butts on brick stands, and a bee skep or two on the east side, which the Co-op insists are not for honey.
- Garden sheds and a compost bin, both with padlocks; the padlocks are not for keeping anybody out.
- The produce stalls: three stalls along the east path, run by the Co-op's stallholders, who sell vegetables for
  Acorns and refuse to take anything with a motor in it.
- Lamps and a noticeboard at the north gate, where the plot waiting list is pinned up and nobody has been on it for
  years.

## In the games

Pure-Loam Allotments is the park at block centre (150, 90). The allotment beds are scenery: they can be walked round
and between, not sat on. The stall row and the gnomes working the plots are part of the block's life.
