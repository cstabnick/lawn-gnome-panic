Mossy Wood's earthy-brown artisanal organic compost collective, operated by Unmowed elders who despise synthetic chemicals and two-stroke engines.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"No Chemicals. No Engines. Just Dirt."* Pure-Loam packs hand-turned leaf mold into hemp sacks and refuses to ship on motorized vehicles, relying instead on slow snail-wagons. To counter Stink-Gro's mocking billboards, co-op youths quietly sabotage Stink-Gro delivery trucks with dandelion bombs.

## Consumer Reports

Twice the price of Stink-Gro pellets, zero chemical burn, and smells like a damp forest after rain.
