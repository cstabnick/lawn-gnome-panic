The Pure-Loam Glasshouse is a sandstone tower in setbacks, with planted balconies and a roof garden of planters, little trees and a pergola. A flower mural covers the street side. The Co-op's members grow vegetables on every ledge that will hold a pot.

## What gnomes say

"The Glasshouse does not have a garden. The Glasshouse is a garden that happens to have a tower under it."

"Pure-Loam sells the vegetables from the balconies. The balconies are not for sale, and the members are very clear about that."

## In the games

It stands on the x = 80, z = 30 block of the Greenhouse Strip, a stepped sandstone tower with planted balconies and a roof garden with a pergola. Its setbacks, balcony planters and flower mural are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
