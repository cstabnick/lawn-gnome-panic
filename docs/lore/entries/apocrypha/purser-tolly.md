The Steamer's purser: the ferryman's sister, who sells the tickets he counts twice from a green tin booth at the Loch Moss dock.

## Field Notes

Tolly runs the Loch Moss Steamer Co. from the booth. She banks the acorns her brother counts, which answers what he does with them at the far shore: he gives them to her. She hires boat drivers for an 80-Acorn licence, for the water taxi between docks and for the Steamer loop: the dock, the reeds, the north loch, Tessie's isle and Mossbank.

## What gnomes say

"Mind the waves. Passengers are not ballast."

## In the games

The Boat Driver job, in any boat. Water taxi fares go from dock to dock; the Steamer loop (from level 2) has a time at each stop. Rough water at speed makes passengers seasick and costs stars.
