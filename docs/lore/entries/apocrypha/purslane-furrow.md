A dry farmer on the Weeping Line: a wide-brim hat, a dusty watering can and a field of peppers that never gets enough rain.

## What gnomes say

"The Weeping Line gives a drop every span."

## The truth

Purslane has watched the tube weep for thirty years and waters her peppers from the drops, which is why her peppers are the hottest in the Flats.

## In the games

Gives the player 'The Weeping Line' side quest. The reward is Weeping Line Discount.

## Loose threads

Whether the peppers would grow without the drops.
