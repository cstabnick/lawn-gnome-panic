Lightweight, nimble Husky-Blade two-stroke bike (`maxSpeed: 20`, `mass: 1`) favored by alley runners, Pip Potsworth, and Baroness Brenda's gang.

## Field Notes & Specs

Cheap to insure (though Kiln-Shield still denies claims), narrow enough to thread between downtown flowerpots, and loud enough to wake three blocks at idle. Its single-unit mass means a speeding Combine will send it tumbling like a tin can, yet riders swear by its `2.8` turn rate for dodging Hat Watch roadblocks.

## What gnomes say

"If you hear a tinny *braaap-braaap* behind your hedge, check your bike rack."
