Pardonlin's classical station, 96.1 on the dial, broadcast from the Opera Hall on the Left Bank. It plays classical music and nothing else, only slowly, and it is the bonus station of Turfton FM: Drive-Time Regular does not ask the player to hear it.

## What gnomes say

"Please lower your engine." Maestro Ottavio Lento says it at every station ident, and the Continentals do.

## The truth

The station is one gnome, a microphone and a very long record shelf. It reaches the town through the horn speaker in the Opera Hall forecourt and the gramophones on Teacup Row and in the cafés, and it reaches the road through the radio of any mower that crosses the Wickets. Nobody has ever heard it end on time.

## Programmes

- **The Slow Hour**, which is ninety minutes. Maestro Lento introduces one piece at enormous length, then plays it.
- **Traffic, Slowly**: driving advice (indicate, then wait a bar).
- **Weather on the Left Bank**: a light drizzle, which the Maestro finds perfect.
- **The Brass Bulletin**: the latest news of the brass section's strike.
- Calls, taken between pieces: a Turfton caller who wants it faster, Fifi Haricot of the Bean Tower, and Rooster, who is on every station.

## In the games

Station `adagio` in `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/radio/adagioStation.ts`, listed in `STATIONS` in `content.ts`. The N key turns to it like the others. Its ads are Café Pardon, Brigitte Levain's bakery and Opera Hall season tickets ("seats not provided").
