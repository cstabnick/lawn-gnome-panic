Engineer of Pinwheel Ridge: lean, oil-stained and a little deaf from the wind, with a coil of cable over one shoulder.

## What gnomes say

"The pinwheels keep Turfton lit. The cable keeps the HOA greedy. I would rather have one without the other."
"Free these three and the rest will do the thinking."

## In the games

Home: the control hut on the ridge (E01 site wind-farm). Work: engineer of Pinwheel Ridge. Planned (batch 3): row 41 (m41-pinwheel-ridge), where she shows the player which three pinwheels to free from the HOA cabling. Her hat is a dented tin pot with wire round the brim.
