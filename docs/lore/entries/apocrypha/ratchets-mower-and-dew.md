The mower shed on a side street of Pebblewick, where Burnet Ratchet fixes mowers, tows racers and sells Black Dew fuel from a drum.

## What gnomes say

"Every racer stops here twice. Once on the way out, once on a tow."

## The truth

Burnet takes every breakdown in Pebblewick as a personal challenge. The fuel drum was left behind by someone, and he has never asked who.

## In the games

On a side street of Pebblewick. Burnet Ratchet keeps the mower bench, and Ratchet's Thanks is the reward for 'The Hum at Three'.
