Razor-Back Tower's black glass slab stands across the block from Husky-Blade Tower in Turfton Park & Arts. Orange ribs run up its face, and an orange sail-shaped fin sits on the roof. Each company built its tower to be looked at from the other's boardroom.

## What gnomes say

"Razor-Back's boardroom faces Husky-Blade. Husky-Blade's boardroom faces Razor-Back. Neither boardroom has a window that faces anything else."

"The fin is not a sail. It is a sign that says the sail is somewhere else."

## In the games

It stands on the x = -40, z = -90 block of Turfton Park & Arts, across the street from Husky-Blade Tower. Its black glass slab, orange ribs and sail fin are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
