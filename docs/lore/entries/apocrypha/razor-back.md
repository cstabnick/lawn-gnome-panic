Safety-orange and matte-black high-RPM manufacturer of Zero-Turn Racers and civilian Leaf-Blower Cannons, favored by The Carburettors street crew.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"Zero Turning Radius. Zero Regrets."* Razor-Back thrives on midnight birdbath derbies, neon orange decals, and aggressive billboard trolling. Their favourite highway board along Cloverfield shows a Zero-Turn doing donuts around a bogged-down Buck-Iron Combine with the caption: *"Still Turning Since Tuesday."*

## Consumer Reports

Unmatched cornering speed at 34 units an hour, though warranty coverage is voided the moment a driver mentions "ice," "stairs," or "VIP escalators."
