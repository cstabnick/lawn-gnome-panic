Petty crimson-hatted Turfton street gang locked in an eternal, pointless turf war with the Blue Cap Corners over a single grassy median.

## Story: West Kerb Pride

Nobody in Red Cap Corners remembers what started the feud with the Blue Caps—only that crimson felt is objectively superior and anyone wearing cobalt near `x: 60, z: -60` deserves an acorn to the forehead. They hang out outside Bodega & Sons, trade bootleg Black Dew, and occasionally hijack Big Lou's towel shipments just to prove a point.

## What gnomes say
