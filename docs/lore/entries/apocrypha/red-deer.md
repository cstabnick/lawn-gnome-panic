Turfton's red deer and hill goats: herds that graze the empty country and bolt at the sight of a gnome, a mower or a gunshot. The deer are also a Tuft & Tally quarry.

## What gnomes say

"A stag looks at you like you owe him rent."

"Hill goats don't run. They relocate, and make a point of it."

## The truth

Ambient herd and hunting quarry are one animal (R-205). The herds are a stag, hinds and a fawn, found around Trellisdown, Snowcap Rise, Loch Moss, Cloverfield and Rootwell; the goats keep to Snowcap Rise and the Fells' north flank. A knocked-over deer gets up offended and leaves. Nothing dies (R-021, R-049).

## In the games

Herds in the country of `games/gnome-theft-auto`. The Trellisdown Red Deer is a quarry west of the vineyard: tuft is the red-deer winter tuft, keepsake a shed antler tine.
