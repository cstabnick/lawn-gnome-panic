The Flock's only drink and its only fine: a pale, faintly green tea steeped from the camp's reeds, poured by Sister Sedge from the urn, and owed by any gnome who wobbles. It tastes of a pond that has made an effort.

## What gnomes say

"A wobble is a thought. A thought is a cup. You look thoughtful." (Sister Sedge)

"It tastes of nothing and then of the loch. Ask for a second one and you owe it."

## The truth

The tea is the Flock's whole penal code. A wobble ("a thought") costs one cup, owed to the urn; a spilt cup costs another; a gnome who sits down at the Long Vigil is forgiven with a cup, handed over kindly. The debts are kept on Plover's rota, nobody has ever been asked to pay, and Brother Godwit and Brother Crake are each at nine cups and counting.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the reed tea urn at Wader's Rest (-1166, -616) and inside the big tent; the sign "A WOBBLE IS A THOUGHT. A THOUGHT IS A CUP." The Reed Tea Cup is the hand reward for "Sat Down" (`gta-sat-down`, the end of `sq-flock-vigil`).
