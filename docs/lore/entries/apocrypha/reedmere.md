The reed marsh of the Unmown, about 0.7 m deep (x -1260 to -1040, z -1705 to -1605), crossed by a raised boardwalk to a viewing platform. It is where the bittern lives, and where a gnome can keep dry feet.

## What gnomes say

"Whisper. The bittern is listening. It is always listening."

## The truth

The **boardwalk** leaves the loop trail on the marsh's north shore and runs in four straight legs to a platform at the far end, its deck 0.6 m above the water. Frogs live underneath, dragonflies patrol, and nobody has ever seen the bittern, only heard it. **The three hides** are the Unmown's bird huts: **Reedmere Hide** (the quote above), **Meadow Hide** ("Please do not count the grass. We tried. We ran out of numbers.") and **Wood Hide** ("The fox was here first. Knock before entering."). On the north shore two birdwatchers' chairs stand back to back with a flask each: a quarrel over whether it was a bittern or a very tall reed. **The Reedmere Bittern** is the marsh's rare bird, a reed-coloured wader at dusk that booms "like a very large gnome blowing across a very large bottle".

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: water `reedmere`, the boardwalk and platform (`BOARDWALK`, `PLATFORM`), the three hides (`UNMOWN_HIDES`), the birdwatchers' vignette and the bittern (`reedmere-bittern`). The achievements Dry Feet (walk the boardwalk end to end) and Hide and Seek (stand in all three hides) belong here.
