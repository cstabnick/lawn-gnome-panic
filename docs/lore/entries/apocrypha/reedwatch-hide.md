A low wooden hide on the south shore of Loch Moss, with a slit window over the reeds, where gnomes sit quietly with binoculars and watch the birds of Turfton.

## What gnomes say

"Sit down, keep your voice low, and the loch will show you everything. Don't rush it."
"Dunnock's hide is the only place on the loch where the birds don't mind you being there."

## The truth

The hide is the one quiet corner of Loch Moss that the birds have agreed to share. Dunnock Pell keeps it, runs the life list, and sells the optics that make the watching worthwhile. Nothing is taken from the birds here: a gnome leaves with a list, a photograph or a feather that fell off by itself.

## In the games

A wooden hide on the south shore of Loch Moss, reached along the reed path from the dock. The birding system places the Turfton birds around the loch and the reeds, and the hide's window is where a gnome looks through binoculars. Dunnock Pell sits inside, sells Pocket Binoculars, Field Glasses and the Reedwatch Scope, and pays Acorns for life-list milestones.

## Loose threads

Why the hide's window faces the Dust Flats side of the loch on Golden Hour evenings. Dunnock has not said, and the phoenix has not yet been counted.
