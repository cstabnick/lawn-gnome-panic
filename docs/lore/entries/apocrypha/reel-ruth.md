The angler on the Long Spoon Pier's handle: a mustard oilskin sou'wester, a navy jumper, glasses, a grey beard, and a long story about the one time something bit the pier.

## What gnomes say

"Nothing bites the pier. Something bit the pier once. Long story."

## The truth

She has fished the pier for longer than the pier has had its name and has never caught anything but the one thing that was not a fish. She will tell it if you stay; she does not tell it all the way through.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E02, moved by R-238): stands on the Long Spoon Pier's handle at the west rail, about (297, 1176), facing out over the side with the "fish" idle.
