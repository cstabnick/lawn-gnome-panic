Reeve Yarrow Potsherd's house on Pebblewick's square: a flat-roofed adobe, swept to the step, with the reeve's staff by the door and, on the lintel, a notice that says the reeve is in. He generally is.

## What gnomes say

"The reeve's house is the first on the square and the last with a light."

"A town with a bell, a bus and a reeve has a house for him. It is not a mayor's house. He is quite clear."

## The truth

The house stands on the square (the code places Yarrow at "the reeve's house, Pebblewick square" and works him out of Pebblewick). It is adobe like the rest, with the town's news pinned to the door. Inside there is a stove, a desk of town business and an empty hook for a staff that was never elected. In act IV it is where Pip's call finds the reeve before the water cut-off (`m58-the-reeves-barn`).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: Yarrow's home in the homes table (`atStage('yarrow', "the reeve's house, Pebblewick square", ...)`), acknowledged once the player has stood at the door (R-180); the base for `m58-the-reeves-barn`.
