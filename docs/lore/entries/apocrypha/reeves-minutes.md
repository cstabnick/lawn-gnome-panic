The ledger Reeve Yarrow Potsherd keeps of every meeting in Pebblewick: formal, patient and always ending in "Noted."

## What gnomes say

"If it is not in the minutes, it was a rumour. If it is in the minutes, it was a rumour with a seal."

"Yarrow writes it down. Then he writes down that he wrote it down."

## The truth

The minutes run to forty-one volumes in a tin chest. Each sitting is headed "Present: the town." The ledger is thinner than the council knows (see Yarrow's own entry): a few of the early pages record only the weather.

## In the games

Carried by Yarrow in the cutscenes (`cutscene/cast.ts`, preset `yarrow`); "Pebblewick thanks you. In writing." is his sign-off.

## Everyday

- **Notices:** the week's minutes are pinned to the town hall door: "Item 1: the water. Item 2: the water, further."
- **Habits:** minutes are read aloud before the meeting, so that the meeting may begin by agreeing.
- **Sayings:** "Noted." "So minuted."
