Obsessive HOA Chief Turf-Auditor who patrols Turfton at three in the morning with a laser level, brass pocket-calipers, and a laminated copy of Municipal Code §404-B.

## Journal: Turf-Audit Field Notes (03:14 AM)

> *Block (-60, 60): Corner bodega awning projects 0.4 inches past the curb easement. Noted. Tabby cat on counter lacks municipal collar-tag. Noted. Will issue dual citations once the owner stops staring at me with that broom.*  
> *Side note: My sister Juniper filed another 400-page dandelion brief today. Unbelievable. We grew up on the same potting bench and she still defends weeds.*

Reginald genuinely believes civilization hangs by a quarter-inch of blade height. He carries a silver whistle tuned specifically to summon HOA Code-Enforcement Vans.

## What gnomes say

"If you hear a tiny *click-click* in the bushes at midnight, tuck your blades in—Quarter-Inch is measuring."

## The truth
Reginald is the HOA audit officer, Vesper's most precise employee and Juniper's brother. He wants a quarter-inch of order and fears that order is not enough. Secret: the photograph of himself and Juniper as kids (seen at #12), and that he signs what he doubts. Home: townhouse 404, Patio Heights; work: the HOA audit office at Thorne Tower. Voice: municipal code, numbers, "four-oh-four dash B"; monotone, exact, never swears ("That is a quarter-inch over. Regulation allows none.").

Arc: signs the Even-Estates permits (#7), meets the player in his own townhouse (#12) and plants a doubt; does not confirm Juniper's story but corrects the spelling (#28); absent in act III (he audits at Thorne Tower); lets the player in by the side door (#67) and stays behind to "measure something".
