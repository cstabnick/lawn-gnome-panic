Custom-painted Ride-On Mower fitted with working hydraulic suspension to hop kerbs and cushion fragile porcelain passengers over potholes.

## Story: Zero-Chip Suspension

Built in the back bays of Side-Valve Salvage and Pay 'n' Glaze, the Lowrider swaps stiff tractor springs for pressurized air-bags. Drivers tap `Space` to bounce cleanly over HOA tire-spikes—or cruise gently at five miles an hour while transporting Clara's porcelain refugees through Mossy Wood without rattling a single teacup.
