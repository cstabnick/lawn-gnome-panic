Turfton's ubiquitous Buck-Iron commuter vehicle (`maxSpeed: 25`, `mass: 2`), famously left idling at the kerb with keys in the ignition.

## Field Notes & Specs

Every townsgnome owns one, nobody locks them, and half of them can be hotwired with a butter knife in under two seconds. Solid acceleration, dependable handling, and wide enough to flatten a row of parking cones without losing a hubcap.

## What gnomes say

"Mowers are for borrowing—just don't complain when somebody borrows yours back."
