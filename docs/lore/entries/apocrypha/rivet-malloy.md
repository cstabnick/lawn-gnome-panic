The owner of The Boneyard on the eastern edge of Turfton: a short, dusty gnome with a pencil behind each ear and a
spanner always in hand, who takes wrecked mowers apart and sells the parts on, and will not say where the parts go.

## What gnomes say

"Nothing's ever scrap. It's stock waiting for its next job." Rivet says this to customers, and to the gnomes who
bring in mowers that have already been pronounced dead by somebody else.

## Inside & Stories

Rivet keeps every mower's name on a card, the price on the card crossed out and written over again. The cards are
kept in a biscuit tin in the lean-to, and the tin has never been opened in front of a customer.

## Loose threads

Where the parts go after they leave the yard, and who has been buying the good ones.
