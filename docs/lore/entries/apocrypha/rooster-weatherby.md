The prophet on the windmill, who calls the Blade from a tinfoil-lined cap and a tin funnel and will not come down.

## What gnomes say

"THE BAROMETER IS DROPPING! VESPERS IN THE CLOUDS!" Shouted at the Greenhouse Strip, and at Cloverfield, and once, by
accident, at the right moment.

## History

Rooster lived in the Cloverfield windmill for thirty years, and says he has never been down since he saw the
barometer change. He was a weather-vane gnome until the vane broke and he took it as a sign. He has built a station
on the top of the mill, with a tin funnel for a microphone and a car battery for the power, and rings the Blade's
request line to tell Dock what the sprinklers are spelling.

He believes that the Tall Ones talk in oscillating sprinkler patterns and that the patterns currently spell GRASS.
Turfton's sprinklers are not known to spell anything, except, once, a short word that a Hat Watch officer swore was
"COOL".

## Quirks

- He always calls Dock "Dock" in an unnaturally formal way, as if reading it off a card.
- He insists the HOA is a barometer. Cress Caliper has told him that it is a clipboard. He has not been persuaded.
- He will not confirm his surname.

## In the games

- The call-in segments on The Blade and on Compliance Beats (`radio/content.ts`). He calls whichever station will have him.

He lives up the Squall-Box Windmill, a rusted red-bladed windmill in the Cloverfield fields with a lookout platform where he shouts his prophecies (stage `rooster-windmill`, see `squall-box-windmill`).
