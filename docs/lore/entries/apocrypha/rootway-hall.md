The west hall of Loamdeep, where the Tamps store, sort and send on every root in the halls. Roots hang in bundles from the beams, each labelled by smell, and the whole room smells of rain on a very good lawn.

## What gnomes say

"They say the moles eat the roots. Go and look at Rootway. Nobody has so much as nibbled."

"Sorted by smell. I put my nose to one and it was extremely clear."

## The truth

Rootway is 20 by 16 metres of local floor (x -50 to -30, z 12 to 28), at the west end of the west gallery. The signs read "ROOTS: SORTED BY SMELL. Ask Old Tamp, do not sniff yourself" and "LOANED, NOT EATEN (we eat grubs)", and a gnome's lost spade, cleaned and hung on the north wall, carries the card "FOUND IN OUR CEILING. RETURN TO OWNER, GENTLY." (the Tamps keep what falls through from the surface). Old Tamp keeps the hall, and Root Run begins at the pickup spot at (-40, 24). Roots are sent on to the Mother's, Lamplight, Postern, Hearth and Under-Chorus halls.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the west hall of the Loamdeep interior; Root Run (`mole-jobs`) takes a bundle here and drops it at the right hall. Old Tamp stands in it.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** Roots by the bundle, labelled by smell: "after rain", "before rain", "a good lawn", "someone has been digging". Old Tamp loans, never sells.
- **Notices:** "ROOTS: SORTED BY SMELL. Ask Old Tamp, do not sniff yourself." "LOANED, NOT EATEN (we eat grubs)." On a hung spade: "FOUND IN OUR CEILING. RETURN TO OWNER, GENTLY."
- **Sayings and habits:** "Nose before hand." Tamps greet each other by smelling the sleeve, a custom that has cost them two friendships.

## Loose threads

What the roots are for, once they have been sorted. The Tamps say "for the grubs", and the grubs say nothing.
