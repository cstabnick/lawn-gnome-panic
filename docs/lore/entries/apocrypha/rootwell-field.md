The university's airstrip in the far east, kept by the aeronautics department: a long flat run beside the campus, with a hangar, a windsock and a very small tower.

## What gnomes say

"The Field is flatter than the Flats. The professors keep it that way on purpose."
"Wally says the Field's wind is the best he has flown in. He does not say it to the professors."

## The truth

The aeronautics department has one working plane and four students. They are glad of any pilot who turns up, even a visiting one.

## In the games

Row 46 `m46-starfall-at-the-anthill` ends with the player reaching it after Dean's Leap and flying out, with Wind-Sock Wally on the radio. Site runway-2 (R-191): a 420 m north-south runway at x 1300 (centre (1300, 600)) with edge lights lit at night; an apron to the west with two Crop Dusters, a Hedge-Hopper and a Sycamore Copter, a pad, a hangar, the "very small tower" (a brick stalk with a glazed cab) and an orange-and-white windsock. The Hedge-Hopper flies circuits ("Rootwell Aeronautics Circuit"); Wally's Charters sometimes fly a visiting professor in. Landing on it earns "Touch and Go".
