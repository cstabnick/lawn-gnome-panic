Turfton's research university in the far east, past the Dust Flats: twelve buildings round a quad, an observatory on a giant ant mound, and an airstrip. It teaches soil, stars and the physics of gnomes, and pays its landlords in sugar.

## What gnomes say

"Rootwell men do not dig. They study digging."
"Four years and a hat. Some of them leave with the hat."

## The truth

The twelve buildings, round the quad: **The Long Shelf** (library), **the Crucible Block** (lab block), **Tilth Hall** (lecture hall), **Bedstraw House** (dorm), **the Trencher** (refectory; the university's sugar is kept under it), **the Cold Frame Club** (faculty club), **the Cloche** (glasshouse), **the Heap Barn** (mulch-science barn), **Bodger's Shed** (engineering shed), **Wicket Lodge** (gate lodge), **the Clod** (stadium) and **Plumbob Hall** (gnome-physics hall). The Dean's lodge stands on the quad. Dean Lovage Inkwell runs the place for Vesper Thorne.
