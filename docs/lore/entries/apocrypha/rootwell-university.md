Turfton's research university in the far east, past the Dust Flats: twelve buildings round a quad, an observatory on a giant ant mound, and an airstrip. It teaches soil, stars and the physics of gnomes, and pays its landlords in sugar.

## What gnomes say

"Rootwell men do not dig. They study digging."
"Four years and a hat. Some of them leave with the hat."

## The truth

The twelve buildings, round the quad: **The Long Shelf** (library), **the Crucible Block** (lab block), **Tilth Hall** (lecture hall), **Bedstraw House** (dorm), **the Trencher** (refectory; the university's sugar is kept under it), **the Cold Frame Club** (faculty club), **the Cloche** (glasshouse), **the Heap Barn** (mulch-science barn), **Bodger's Shed** (engineering shed), **Wicket Lodge** (gate lodge), **the Clod** (stadium) and **Plumbob Hall** (gnome-physics hall). The Dean's lodge stands on the quad. Dean Lovage Inkwell runs the place for Vesper Thorne.

## In the games

The east band of the map (code region `east-campus`, x 900 to 1400, z -900 to 900), built by E01, called Rootwell by the gnomes. Roads: `east-band` runs on from Route East at (870, 50) and north along x 950, `east-band-south` runs south, and `runway-spur` runs to the field. Rootwell University's ground is 320 x 300 m at (1140, -200). The Anthill mound stands at (1240, 150), about 45 m high. Rootwell Field's runway runs north to south at (1300, 600). The Rutbottom Dirt Bowl is 300 x 200 m at (1150, -650), and an aircraft dealership stands beside the runway at (1150, 640).

Rows 45 `m45-rootwell-admissions`, 46 `m46-starfall-at-the-anthill`, 70 (finale call) use the region tag `rootwell`. E13 builds the twelve buildings from this list: planned (batch 3).

Since the second expansion the band reaches the east edge, where Rootwell Field Station stands (R-175).
