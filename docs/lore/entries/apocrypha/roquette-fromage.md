The cheesemonger of the Beetle Market, whose cheese is aged by argument.

## What gnomes say

"Roquette's cheese is not old. It is experienced."

"Do not ask which smell is the cheese. They are all the cheese."

## The truth

Roquette believes cheese ripens through being disagreed with, and she has a customer for every disagreement. The strongest on the stall has been in dispute for three seasons and is very nearly ready.

## In the games

A stall in the Beetle Market, Left Bank (near x -1733, z 120).

## Loose threads

What the strongest cheese is arguing about.
