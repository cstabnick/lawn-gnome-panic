The head gardener of the Clayfoot Rose Garden: a brisk, sun-browned gnome with secateurs on a lanyard and a watering
can she has never been seen without.

## What gnomes say

"A rose doesn't need your advice. It needs you to stop talking and water it." Rosa has redrawn the maze eleven times
and says every version was the right one at the time.

## Inside & Stories

Rosa keeps the rose beds in colour rows, red, pink and cream, and insists the gold bushes are a happy accident. She
has been seen arguing with the Clayfoot Twins about which of them is taller, and losing.

## Loose threads

Which of the two statues Rosa thinks is the real Tansy Clayfoot, and whether she has ever told anyone.
