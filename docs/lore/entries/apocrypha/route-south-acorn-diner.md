A roadside diner on the east side of the South Highway, just south of the canal bridge in Cloverfield. Fence-Post Frank eats every meal at its counter, and his hand-painted sign stands across the road where nobody can miss it.

## What gnomes say
*"Eat Here Or Be Driven Past."* (the hand-painted sign, read aloud by everyone who stops)

## In the games
Stage `south-highway-diner` (chapter 2, "Order Over Chaos"). Players see Frank at the counter, his sign across the road, and Vesper Thorne's outdoor booth, where she holds her "casual" meetings.

The kitchen is a walk-in interior (`acorn-diner-kitchen`), entered from the west front; the chef job (E24) works there. The menu is in `acorn-diner-menu`.
