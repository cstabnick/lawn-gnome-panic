Pardonlin's high street, the spine of the east bank: it runs from the Old Town south through the Hatwheel to Place du Pot, lined with plane trees, bistros, bakeries and a lamp at every doorway. It has two names on one sign and has not chosen between them.

## What gnomes say

"The Rue Haute is the main street. Ask the sign. It says it is the high street, and then it says the other thing."

"Walk down the middle of the Rue Haute and you will be asked, politely, to be a cyclist."

## The truth

The street's sign in the Old Town reads "Rue Haute (High Street) (Main Street) (Please Choose One)", and in Teacup Row "Rue Haute (Please Be Brief)". Its pavements carry sandwich boards, a newspaper box and a basket of baguettes at every third door; plane trees and lamps run both sides, with benches under the trees. It passes through the Hatwheel by the Avenue ("Allow Several Laps") and ends at the north square of Place du Pot, where it meets the quay road. Brigitte Levain's bakery is just off it near (-1462, -200); a Continental "Traffic-Hat Warden" directs on loan from the Continentals (Lupin Pardoux).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the main north-south road of Pardonlin's east bank (E30); the Baguette Round route starts off it.
