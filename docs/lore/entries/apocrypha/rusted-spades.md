A band of raiders who work the Yew Walk and take what they can find, and who set tolls on the roads they hold.

## What they are

The Rusted Spades live in the Yews, the clipped hedges of the Yew Walk, in branch towns hung between the yews. They are raiders, not builders. Their founder is Nettle Rustspade, who believes the strongest rules and who leads by strength. Their hat colour is rust (`#8b3a2b`), the colour of the spades they dig up from the dead.

## How they live

- **Sharing:** each keeps their own. A gnome who finds something keeps it.
- **Strangers:** robbed as they pass, unless they have something the Spades want more.
- **The bitten:** shattered at once. The Spades do not nurse the sick.
- **Leadership:** the strongest. A leader is the gnome who can hold the Yews.
- **Violence:** anyone outside the Yews is fair game.

They took the Footbridge in Year 3 and set a toll of two acorns, and they hold Yew Edge. The toll is their lifeblood, and the Tap, the one neutral market, is the only place they trade face to face with their enemies.

## What they want

The Yew Walk, and every road that runs through it. A Rusted Spade does not want peace. They want the tolls, the bounties and the land, and they will take what the Keepers and the Sisters will not share.

## Loose threads

Whether any of the Spades still remembers the strongest rule from before Nettle, or whether Nettle invented it. Who the Spades' toll-keepers answer to when Nettle is not in the Yews.
