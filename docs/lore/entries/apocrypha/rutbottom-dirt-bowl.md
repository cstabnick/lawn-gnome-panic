A bumpy dirt oval with big banked turns in the north-east of the map: the Carburettors' summer home, and the place the HOA impounds their rides.

## What gnomes say

"Rutbottom is where the Speedway goes to get its knees scraped."
"Dust in the eyes, mud in the hat. We are fine, thank you."

## The truth

The Bowl was dug out by whoever got tired of the Speedway's tarmac. It is rough on purpose, and the Carburettors prefer it that way.

## In the games

Region tag `rutbottom`, the dirt-track site (1150, -620). Row 42 `m42-rutbottom-impound`: race for the impounded rides under Marshal Clary Mudguard, on a dirt bike (race, E14); row 64 `m64-all-hats` (By Road). Built by E01 and the area epic: planned (batch 3).
