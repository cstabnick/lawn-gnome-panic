A bumpy dirt oval with big banked turns in the north-east of the map: the Carburettors' summer home, and the place the HOA impounds their rides.

## What gnomes say

"Rutbottom is where the Speedway goes to get its knees scraped."
"Dust in the eyes, mud in the hat. We are fine, thank you."

## The truth

The Bowl was dug out by whoever got tired of the Speedway's tarmac. It is rough on purpose, and the Carburettors prefer it that way. The corners have names, all given by the people who left skin on them: Hay Fever (the first right-hander), the Berm (the biggest bank; the berm remembers), Gravel Hat, Mudguard Dip (down into the infield), Cowpat Corner and Last Orders (onto the home straight). Two jumps, Clary's Hump and Teasel's Table, are named for the marshal and the rival who built them. The site is 400 x 260 m, x 1000 to 1400, z -780 to -520, and the start line is at (1124, -543).

## Everyday

**Shops and stalls.**
- The mud-pie stall between the Sneaker Stands: Mud Pie, 3 Acorns (it is only called mud), tea from the Marshal's flask if you ask nicely.
- The pit garage by the gate: one bench, two tyre piles, a bucket for the bike wash. "Pits: Knobblies only. Mowers by apology."
- Hours: Saturday heats, Sunday hosing down. Gate board: "RUTBOTTOM DIRT BOWL, est. the year it rained."

**Notices.**
- Spectator board: "Spectators WILL get muddy. That is the ticket."
- Sponsors: "RAZOR-BACK: Mud not included." "DANDELION FIZZ: Refreshing. Mostly."
- Before each rough section: "WHOOPS (not an apology)."
- At the Berm: "THE BERM REMEMBERS", with a tally, "IIII IIII II", which is still short.
- At the bike wash: "Clean boots. Not ours. Explain yourself."

**Sayings and habits.**
- "Mind the berm. The berm remembers."
- The start is counted off hand-lettered cards: "3", "2", "GO ON THEN".
- Umbrellas stand against the stands, left by spectators who came to stay dry.
- Local feud: the Bowl's regulars against the Speedway's tarmac, over whether a track must be smooth to be fair.

**Small history.** The Bowl was dug the year it rained, by someone who had given up on the Speedway. Four pairs of boots stand outside the bike wash and none has been collected. For the pens where the HOA keeps taped-up rides, see the Rutbottom Impound.
