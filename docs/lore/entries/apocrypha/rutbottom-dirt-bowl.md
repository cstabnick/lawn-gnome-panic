A bumpy dirt oval with big banked turns in the north-east of the map: the Carburettors' summer home, and the place the HOA impounds their rides.

## What gnomes say

"Rutbottom is where the Speedway goes to get its knees scraped."
"Dust in the eyes, mud in the hat. We are fine, thank you."

## The truth

The Bowl was dug out by whoever got tired of the Speedway's tarmac. It is rough on purpose, and the Carburettors prefer it that way.

## In the games

Region tag `rutbottom`. Built (E14): the Dirt Bowl sits at (1200, -650), a banked dirt circuit with whoops and jumps, entered by the gate at (1000, -650) on the east-band-north road. Row 42 `m42-rutbottom-impound`: race for the impounded rides under Marshal Clary Mudguard, on a dirt bike (race, E14); row 64 `m64-all-hats` (By Road). Side quests "Rutbottom: Knobbly Heat" (`race-rutbottom-knobbly`, the Knobbly, R-191) and "Rutbottom Heats" (`sq-rutbottom-heats`, Teasel's rematch, R-190).
