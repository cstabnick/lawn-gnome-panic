The pits of the Rutbottom Dirt Bowl, where the HOA has taped six Carburettor rides in place with its beige tape and left them to think about it. Marshal Clary Mudguard will release them only to a gnome who beats the HOA's entry on her oval.

## What gnomes say

"They taped the lowriders. Lowriders. At a dirt track. That is cruelty."
"Nothing leaves the pits without Clary's flag. The tape is the HOA's. The flag is the law."

## The truth

An impound in name only: the HOA has no pound here and no right to one, so it taped each ride where it stood and wrote "IMPOUNDED" on the tape in a very neat hand. The pits are by the gate at about (1012, -694), with a garage, a bench, tyre piles and a bike wash outside, where four pairs of muddy boots stand and one clean pair has been asked to explain itself. The tape has never been cut by anyone but the Marshal.

## In the games

Quest row 42 (m42-rutbottom-impound, src/quests/story/42-rutbottom-impound.ts): the player wins three laps on a dirt bike against three HOA riders, then drives an impounded ride out of the pits to the gate. The Carburettors roll out in a line under a green flag. The pits and signs are in src/rutbottom/plan.ts.

## Everyday

- The tape reads "IMPOUNDED. DO NOT TOUCH. THIS MEANS YOU." The Carburettors say it also means the tape.
- Hours: the pits open when Clary's flag is up, which is also when they shut.
- "Pits: Knobblies only. Mowers by apology."
- Local custom: whoever wins a race is allowed to take the first strip of tape home, as a scarf.
