A street racer with a grudge: purple hat and paint, a dark dress, freckles. Her brother held the Ring Road record until
the Hat Watch impounded his lowrider, and she is looking after the record until he is let out.

## What gnomes say

"The Ring Road record is my brother's. I am only looking after it."

## The truth

It is a very short reason. She is not cruel, only cross; she wins quietly and loses louder.

## In the games

The rival of the street-racing questline (E26): she races in `sr-skyway-midnight` (the Skyway at night) and in the
finale `sr-ring-road`, where she hangs her brother's number on the player's mirror.
