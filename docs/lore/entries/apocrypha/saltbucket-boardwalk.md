The plank promenade of Saltbucket Shore: stalls, candyfloss, tube and ski rental, and Driftwood's beach hut, with Long Spoon Pier running out to sea from the middle.

## What gnomes say

"Eight acorns for a tube. Eleven if you want it to come back with you."
"Everything here is sticky and nothing is lying. Go and look at the sea, then buy a candyfloss."

## The truth

The stalls are run by a handful of families who have not left in years. Marram Teaspoon keeps the boardwalk's books and its mood. The stalls: **Spun Sugar Sal's** (candyfloss), **The Last Tube** (Driftwood's tube and ski hut from row 43), **Plank & Pickle** (pickled things in cups), **Tin Shy** (a knock-down game with chipped acorns), **Scoop & Spade** (cones, served in a bucket) and **The Lucky Limpet** (fortunes, mostly the same fortune).

## In the games

Centred on (0, 1140). Rows 43 `m43-saltbucket-boardwalk` (Driftwood's hut, Marram, the Spokewheel) and 44 `m44-the-bathtub-queen`. Built by E01 and the south-shore area epic: planned (batch 3).

Built by E11 at (0, 1115), 420 x 46 m. The six stalls stand in a row on its north edge, and the boardwalk office is Marram's. Driftwood's tube and ski hut is [the-last-tube](the-last-tube.md) on the sand at (150, 1150).
