The plank promenade of Saltbucket Shore: stalls, candyfloss, tube and ski rental, and Driftwood's beach hut, with Long Spoon Pier running out to sea from its east end.

## What gnomes say

"Eight acorns for a tube. Eleven if you want it to come back with you."
"Everything here is sticky and nothing is lying. Go and look at the sea, then buy a candyfloss."

## The truth

The stalls are run by a handful of families who have not left in years. Marram Teaspoon keeps the boardwalk's books and its mood. The six stalls along the north edge, west to east: **Spun Sugar Sal's** (candyfloss, with a candyfloss cart beside it), **Plank & Pickle** (pickled things in cups), **Tin Shy** (a knock-down game with chipped acorns), **Scoop & Spade** (cones, served in a bucket) **The Lucky Limpet** (fortunes, mostly the same fortune) and **Marram's Office**, the boardwalk office, where the books are kept. Driftwood's tube and ski hut, [the-last-tube](the-last-tube.md), is not a stall: it stands on the sand below. The boardwalk's own signs read "Saltbucket Boardwalk: Est. the day the bucket sank", "No gulls (they cannot read, but we live in hope)" and "The Saltwater: It is real. Stop asking."

## Everyday

- Stall menus and prices are in [the-boardwalk-stalls](the-boardwalk-stalls.md).
- Notices (the signs players read): "No gulls (they cannot read, but we live in hope)"; "Please do not feed the sea. It is full."; "Mind the stairs. There are three. We counted."; "Lost property: hats, mostly"; "Candyfloss is a vegetable (Sal says so)"; "Lawn Sharks: smaller, meaner, mostly grass" on the beach below.
- Habit: at noon, when the Spokewheel's bell goes, everybody on the boardwalk turns and looks at the sea for a count of three, "in case".
- Saying: "Eight acorns for a tube, eleven if you want it back."
- Small history: in a gale a whole row of deckchairs went out to sea in order, and Marram said it was the neatest thing the boardwalk had ever done.
