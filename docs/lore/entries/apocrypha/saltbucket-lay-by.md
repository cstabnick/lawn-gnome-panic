A roadside meet on the band road out to the coast: sand on the tarmac, gulls on the roof and a snack van that does
chips for racers who have not yet finished. The Saltbucket Dash runs from here to the sea.

## Inside & Stories

- **Organiser:** Dune Gritstone. **Bookie:** Shingle Nan Trimm, who takes wagers from a deckchair into a bucket.
  **Flag-dropper:** Spade Ormerod, who drops the flag and leaves the rest to the gulls.
- **Residents:** Haddock Hobbs fries chips in the van; Limpet Larkin builds a sandcastle copy of the racing line.
- **The regulars:** Dinghy Dolores, Rip-Tide Rufus and Winkle Pickering, on Wespas.

## What gnomes say

"I drop the flag. The gulls drop everything else."
