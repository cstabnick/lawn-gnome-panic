The seaside south of the Puddle Sea, past a line of dunes: a bucket-and-spade town built round a giant sunk Tall-One bucket, with a boardwalk, a pier, a wheel, a pirate ship and the open sea.

## What gnomes say

"Past the dunes? There is nothing past the dunes. My cousin went to look and came back with sand in his hat."
"It is a town built in a bucket. I do not know who thought of that, and I do not want to meet them."

## The truth

The bucket and spade were left by a Tall One long ago and sank to the rim. The town grew in the bucket's shelter and the shore grew round the town. Most of Turfton has never been, which is why the place keeps its own manners.
