Captain of the Bathtub Queen: a loud, salt-cured gnome who gives pirate tours by day and does real smuggling by night, and sees no difference between the two.

## What gnomes say

"Mind the rail, matey. It is not a real rail, but it is mine."
"Every cargo is honest. Some of it was just not yet yours."

## The truth

Home: the captain's cabin on the Bathtub Queen. Work: the Bathtub Queen. She keeps the tours cheerful so nobody looks in the hold. She joins the crew at row 44 because the HOA's barges carry what she has been taking from them for years, and because the crew pays better.

## In the games

Gives row 44 `m44-the-bathtub-queen`; appears in row 64 `m64-all-hats` (p1 By Water) and in the finale. Planned (batch 3). Her cabin is the pirate-ship site (E01).
