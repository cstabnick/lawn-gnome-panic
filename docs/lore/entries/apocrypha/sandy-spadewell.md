Freckled, gold-hatted outlaw loitering in The Dust Flats (`330, 110`) and the third target on Don Compost's First Contract.

## Story: Sunburn & Shards

Sandy fled east into the Dust Flats hoping the Spade Guard's perimeter patrols would scare off any syndicate collectors. He wears a bright gold hat to keep the desert sun off his freckles and passes the time skipping flat pebbles across the alkali pan.

Whenever a Crop Duster flies overhead, Sandy ducks behind a cactus—convinced Don Compost finally bought an air force.
