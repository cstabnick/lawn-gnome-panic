An existential poet on the Left Bank who finds everything meaningless, and rhymes about it.

## What gnomes say

"Sartorius has written a poem about the pointlessness of poems. It is his best."

"He says the sun rises for no reason. He gets up for it."

## The truth

Gloom is a cheerful gnome with a black scarf. The sadness is professional and the poems are very good, which he finds the most depressing thing of all. He listens to Radio Adagio and declares it the only honest voice in the town.

## In the games

A Left Bank café terrace. His lines are short, rhyming and low in spirits.

## Loose threads

Whether he has ever written a happy poem. He says no. There is a rumour of one.
