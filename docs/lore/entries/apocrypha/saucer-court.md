A block of flats in Downtown Turfton built round a giant Tall-One saucer set into the ground as a courtyard. The player's loaned home is flat 3B, paid for by a 1,500-Acorn loan from the First Kiln Bank that is added to the Kiln-Debt.

## What gnomes say

"Saucer Court? Lovely courtyard. Nobody owns the courtyard, so nobody sweeps it."

"The bank lent you a roof. The roof is the bank's until you stop owing for it."

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). #2 `m02-roof-on-credit` moves the player in (world change W01: name on the buzzer, flat 3B's window lit at night). #39 `m39-couch-surfing` has Pip move onto the couch (W09). #68 `m68-lights-out` ends on the roof with Pip. Flat 3A belongs to Hyssop Doormat. Epic E10 builds the apartment block and its interior.

Flat 3B is walk-in (R-170, `docs/lore/rulings.md#r-170`): empty on move-in, with a bedroll, moving crates and the bank's notice on the wall. The player furnishes it.

Built by E10: three low wings of flats round the saucer courtyard, open to the street on the south. The stairwell door to 3A and 3B is on the north wing, with a buzzer panel ("3A H. DOORMAT / 3B (YOU)") and Hyssop's posted rules (wipe feet, no running, no lodgers, I am watching). Nobody sweeps the courtyard, and the saucer is dry. Flat 3B is signed for in quest #2 at the First Kiln Bank counter with Parsley Inkpot (1,500 Acorns on the ledger beside the Kiln-Debt, R-169). The player buys furniture from Gnome & Garden Furnishings (R-181), paints the walls and sleeps in their own bed: time passes and the game is saved. Each player's Flat 3B is their own (R-180).
