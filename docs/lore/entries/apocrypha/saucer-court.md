A block of flats in Downtown Turfton built round a giant Tall-One saucer set into the ground as a courtyard. The player's loaned home is flat 3B, paid for by a 1,500-Acorn loan from the First Kiln Bank that is added to the Kiln-Debt.

## What gnomes say

"Saucer Court? Lovely courtyard. Nobody owns the courtyard, so nobody sweeps it."

"The bank lent you a roof. The roof is the bank's until you stop owing for it."

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). #2 `m02-roof-on-credit` moves the player in (world change W01: name on the buzzer, flat 3B's window lit at night). #39 `m39-couch-surfing` has Pip move onto the couch (W09). #68 `m68-lights-out` ends on the roof with Pip. Flat 3A belongs to Hyssop Doormat. Epic E10 builds the apartment block and its interior.
