Leader of the Flock of the One Leg: tall, thin and calm, balanced on one leg through whole sermons, with a long grey beard braided with reeds. She is serious about every rule and has never raised her voice.

Home: the big tent at Wader's Rest. Work: the Flock.

## What gnomes say

"Mother Saxifrage has not been seen to put a second foot down since the thaw. She is still the most reasonable gnome I know."

"Ask her something and she answers the next day. It is the leg."

## The truth

Saxifrage keeps the Flock because someone has to, and prefers a rule to a mess. She believes the prophecy and did not make it up: it came to her as a plain fact in the reeds, like a timetable. She is happy to be wrong about everything else.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). The giver of quest 51 (`m51-the-flock-stands`) and the voice in 25 (`m25-one-leg-to-stand-on`), where her sermon names "the Even Cut, on the longest day". She gives the test date. E17: stands beside the circle at Wader's Rest and gives `sq-flock-errands` and `sq-flock-vigil`.
