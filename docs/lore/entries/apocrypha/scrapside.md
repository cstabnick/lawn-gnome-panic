The eastern edge of the Fringe, where Turfton runs out into the Dust Flats: scrap yards, tyre piles, container yards and
lock-ups baking in the sun, and the shadow of the Skyway falling across all of it at noon.

## What gnomes say

"Scrapside is where the wind comes from, and it brings the bits with it." The dust settles on everything, and the
Scrapside folk say you can tell how long someone has lived out there by how much of them is beige.

## Inside & Stories

- Container yards with rusty blue boxes stacked two high, the doors chained and the ribs streaked orange.
- Lock-ups with roller doors, half open, and dark inside. One of them has been a lock-up for a tractor for longer than
  anyone remembers.
- Tyre piles and oil drums, and dust tracks peeling off the highway in every direction.
- Power poles marching out along the road, carrying wires nobody has ever been seen to clip on.

## In the games

Scrapside is the east edge of the Fringe in Gnome Theft Auto, bordering the Dust Flats. The lots here are rougher and
emptier the further out they go; the nearest ones are the densest.

## Loose threads

Whether the Skyway shadow is shade or a lease, and who has been paying for it.
