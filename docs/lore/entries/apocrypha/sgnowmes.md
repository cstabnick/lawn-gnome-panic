The tundra gnomes of Frostbite Fells' far north: ordinary fired-clay gnomes glazed frost-white, who call themselves sgnowmes (the s is silent, and they will tell you so).

## What gnomes say

"A sgnowme is a gnome who stayed out in the cold long enough to be proud of it."
"The glaze is frost, not snow. They don't melt. Stop asking them if they melt."

## In the games

A sgnowme village in the outer north of Frostbite Fells. Townsgnomes in a frosty look (white and ice-blue glaze, pale
beards, an icicle scarf), built with the same gnome model as every other townsgnome. They put their hands up when shot
at, like anyone sensible.

Their village sits in the Fells' far north, and the sgnowmes wander it.

## Loose threads

Who first glazed them, and whether the frost glaze was a choice or an accident at the kiln.
