A guild of builders and crafters living on the shed shelves, the Shelves haven of DayG's Mossgrave.

## What they are

The Shelf Wrights live on shelves two to four of the groundskeeper's shed, among paint tins, seed packets and coils of string. They build, mend and craft, and they will hire out their tools to anyone who can pay in parts. Their hat colour is paint red (`#b5523b`), the colour of the tins on the top shelf.

## How they live

- **Work:** crafting stations on the shelves, repairs for hire, and tools lent by the day.
- **Strangers:** met at the string ladder, and asked what they need before anything is done.
- **Payment:** parts first, promises never.
- **Leadership:** the master who holds the glue pot; a new master is chosen by the guild when one falls.
- **Violence:** a warning, then a thrown-out gnome. Blows are not part of the guild's work.

## What they want

To keep the shelves standing and the tools sharp, and to be the first call when a crafting job goes wrong. They keep a shelf empty for whatever comes next.

## Loose threads

Who first put the paint tins up on the top shelf, and why the top shelf is never used. Whether the guild's string ladder was made by the Shelf Wrights or found by them.
