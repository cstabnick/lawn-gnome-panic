A grass strip behind Bodega & Sons: two green dumpsters, a lettuce bed, a wicker basket and a retired snail-coach that Shelldon lives in. The coach has not left the alley in years, and the snails have not asked it to.

## What gnomes say
*"Slow is a lifestyle, and the snails are my staff."* (Shelldon, to anyone who asks why he never gets anywhere)

## In the games
Stage `shelldon-alley` (dawn). Shelldon kneels at the lettuce bed feeding his courier snails, and the snail-coach stays parked.
