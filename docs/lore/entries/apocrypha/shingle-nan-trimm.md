The bookie at the Saltbucket Lay-by meet: pink hat, cream dress, taking wagers from a deckchair into a bucket that is for acorns.

## What gnomes say

"Wagers taken from a deckchair. Acorns in the bucket, please. The bucket is for acorns."

"The sea takes, the bucket keeps."

## The truth

Everyone calls her Nan. The stakes go in the bucket, and the gulls have never seen them.
