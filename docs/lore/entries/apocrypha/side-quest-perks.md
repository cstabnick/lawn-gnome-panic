The small favours Turfton's strangers do for the player after a job. None of them is much, and that is the point:
nobody in Turfton gives away anything big.

## Inside & Stories

- **Datum Map** (Mossley, The Grid Truthers): a survey map that shows where trouble is brewing anywhere in town.
- **Alley Clearance** (Shelldon, Snail-Racing Rigging): the Watch looks the other way a little sooner in Turfton.
- **Velvet Rope Tab** (Big Lou): snacks and drinks at a quarter off.
- **Mulch Rebate** (The Mulch Pyramid Scheme): Pay 'n' Glaze knocks a bit off.
- **Biscuit's Nose** (Biscuit's Lost Squeaky Toy): the giant dog sniffs out the nearest Golden Acorn.
- **Castaway's Float** (Driftwood): cracks mend while swimming.
- **Porcelain-Glaze Charm** (Clara, Porcelain Blues): the Glaze Clinic refunds half its bill.
- **Bracken's Blessing** (Elder Bracken, The Fescue Fugitive): cracks mend slowly out in the wild.
- **Sonar Trinket** (the Tessie quest): pings the nearest Stopping Shard.
- **Brenda's Pension** (Baroness Brenda, The Great Hose-Pipe Tax Revolt): a few Acorns every few minutes.
- **Audit Paperwork** (The HOA Audit): a single hat of heat fades fast.
- **Thick Glaze** (The Porcelain Support Group): a slightly tougher coat.
- Fizz Cup: **Rookie Plate** (speed-trap payouts up), **Carburettor Contacts** (cheaper taxis), **Shelldon's Odds**
  (event rewards up), **Fizz Sponsor** (free Dandelion Fizz).

- **Weeping Line Discount** (Purslane Furrow, The Weeping Line): a small cut off the Pebblewick shops.
- **Ratchet's Thanks** (Mallow Bucket, The Hum at Three; Burnet Ratchet's shed): a free tow.
