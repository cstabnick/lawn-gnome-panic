Five numbered roller doors in the back lot of Side-Valve Salvage, where Tansy Clayfoot keeps a gnome's own mowers out of the rain and out of the Hat Watch's sight. A gnome drives in, Tansy pulls the door down, and the ride waits there with its paint, its dents and its odd engine note exactly as it was left.

## How it works

The Lock-Up has five doors and no more. A gnome who wants a sixth mower must scrap one first, and Tansy pays a little in Acorns for the parts, more for a mower that still runs well. Anything that is a wreck she will not take at all: a burnt-out mower is not stored, it is mourned.

Every mower in the Lock-Up has its own chalk number on its door, and Tansy never lets the same mower be in two places at once. If its owner asks for it while it is parked somewhere out in Turfton, she goes and fetches that one rather than handing over a copy.

## The Sling-Lift

Tansy also runs the Sling-Lift, an old Fort Sandpit whirligig bought at a surplus sale and fitted with a winch and a rope. A gnome rings her from any phone, pays the fee, and the Sling-Lift flies out with the mower hanging underneath, lowers it onto dry flat ground nearby, unhooks and flies off without landing. It needs time to refuel between trips. It will fetch a gnome's mower to a beach, a hilltop or a sandbar in the loch, which is why stranded gnomes ring it more than anybody else. It also fetches from Hangar Two and the Mossbank Boathouse: aircraft from the hangar, boats from the boathouse, lowered onto open water when there is no dry ground to set them down.

## What gnomes say

"Five doors, five mowers. Tansy says a gnome with six mowers is a gnome with a problem."

"The Sling-Lift never lands. The pilot says the ground is where the paperwork is."

## Everyday

- Doors: each of the five has a chalk number, and under it a chalk note in Tansy's hand: "1 Dented. 2 Honest. 3 Owes me. 4 Shy. 5 Ask."
- Notices: "NO SIXTH DOOR"; "ENGINES OFF, KEYS ON THE HOOK, HAT ON THE PEG"; a rota of who has been paid for scrap this week, and who has not.
- House rule: "A mower is parked once. If you want it twice you want another."
- Habit: Tansy slaps each door twice when she pulls it down and says its number to it.
- Saying: "Five doors, six excuses."
- Small history: the first door was a hen-house door. It is still door 1.
