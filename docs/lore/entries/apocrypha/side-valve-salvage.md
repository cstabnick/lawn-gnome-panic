Tansy Clayfoot's gritty downtown chop-shop and illegal two-stroke tuning garage on the western edge of Turfton's grid (`x: -120, z: 60`).

## Inside & Stories

Side-Valve Salvage smells of burnt 87-octane, chicory coffee, and freshly cut steel. Every wall is lined with salvaged Husky-Blade mufflers, hotwired ignition barrels, and spare Zero-Turn tires. It is the crew's home base—until Vesper Thorne's HOA lawyers slap a 48-hour foreclosure lien on the roll-up door and turn a grease-monkey garage into ground zero for a citywide rebellion.
