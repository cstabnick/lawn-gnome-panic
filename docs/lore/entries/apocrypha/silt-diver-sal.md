Rubber-wader-wearing Under-Deck scavenger in a colander helmet who fishes lost acorns and dropped pistol magazines out of storm grates with a magnet on a string.

## Journal: Storm-Drain Salvage Log

> *Grate 14 (Downtown): Two Acorn-Cola tabs, one Hat Watch badge (scrubbed clean), three 9mm casings. Quiet down here today. Heard an HOA van roll overhead and stop right over the pipe. Held my breath for four minutes. Found a soggy blueprint scrap floating toward the Tall-One Pencil.*

Sal knows every culvert shortcut beneath Turfton's 60-unit grid and trades dry matches and drain maps for clean socks.

## What gnomes say

"Drop an acorn down a grate and Sal owns it before it hits the mud."

## The truth
Sal is the Under-Deck scavenger and drain guide, trading in maps. He wants clean socks and dry matches, and fears Grate 22. Secret: what came out of the deepest pipe under Thorne Tower. Home: the Under-Deck culvert. Mossley watches the same culvert, Tansy owes him dry socks, Hogweed buys his scrap and Grout trusts his drain maps. Voice: raspy, a trader's patter that lists items as he names them and bargains in every sentence; "for a pair of clean socks" is his tic ("Two cola tabs, one badge. For clean socks, the map." / "Grate fourteen. Quiet. Too quiet.").

Arc: side quests only in acts I-II and IV; in act III he hides Tansy in the culvert and helps regroup the crew (#39).
