Pretentious Fescue-born theatre actor who moved to Turfton to star in gritty indie dramas like *Cracked in the Rain* while paying rent with Stink-Gro voiceovers.

## Story: Art vs. Fertilizer

Sir Reginald insists on being addressed by his full theatrical title at Chirp & Crumb, where he rehearses dramatic monologues about existential clay brittleness. Unfortunately, indie stage plays at Turfton Amphitheater rarely cover rent, forcing him into late-night recording booths to boom *"GROW TWICE AS TALL BY THURSDAY!"* for Stink-Gro radio spots.

He vehemently denies any relation to Tansy Clayfoot, though both share the exact same stubborn eyebrow tilt.

## What gnomes say
