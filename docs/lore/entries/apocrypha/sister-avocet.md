The Flock's steadiest standing gnome: a thin, upright sister on the ring who treats balance as a feeling and says so to anyone within earshot, and a few who are not.

Home: a grey tent on the ring at Wader's Rest. Work: the Flock (stands, and explains standing).

## What gnomes say

"Avocet has never been seen to wobble. Ask her and she will tell you it is because she wobbles on the inside."

"She says it kindly. That is the worst of it."

## The truth

Avocet came to the Flock from a tidy household and found that the Flock's rules were the first she had liked. Her creed is the shortest in the camp: balance is not stillness, it is stillness with feelings. She is Saxifrage's quiet favourite and the nearest thing the Flock has to a second opinion.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: on the ring of the ritual circle at Wader's Rest (angle -40 degrees, left of the Lady's line), one-legged and grey. Her bubble: "Balance is not standing still. It is standing still with feelings."
