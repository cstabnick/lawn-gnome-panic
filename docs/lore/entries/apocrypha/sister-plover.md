Keeps the rota board: who stands, who fetches reeds, who owes tea. The board is chalk and the writing is small and certain.

## What gnomes say

"If Plover has chalked it, it has already happened."

## The truth

Plover is the one gnome in the camp who sits down on a Wednesday, and she is careful about Tuesdays. Her board has a column called "Thoughts had" and it is the longest.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: at the rota board in Wader's Rest. Quest `sq-flock-errands` runs on her rota.
