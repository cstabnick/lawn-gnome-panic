The Flock's contrary: a thin, grim sister on the ring who has the Tuesday rule the wrong way round and states it with total conviction. Nobody corrects her, because she stands anyway.

Home: a grey tent on the ring at Wader's Rest. Work: the Flock (stands the far east of the circle).

## What gnomes say

"Rail says two legs are for sitting, and sitting is for Tuesday. It is the rule, backwards, and she keeps it perfectly."

"Ask her what day it is. She will say it is not Tuesday, and she will be standing when she says it."

## The truth

The rule is **no sitting on Tuesdays** (the rules board, the rota and Sister Plover all say so). Rail has always said sitting is *for* Tuesday, and the Flock settled long ago that this is Rail's way of keeping the rule: she never sits, because it is never, in her mind, quite Tuesday. The Flock count her as the most reliable stander in the camp for that reason, and have asked Plover to leave her line on the rota alone.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: on the ring of the ritual circle at Wader's Rest (angle 80 degrees, the east end), one-legged and grey. Her bubble: "Two legs are for sitting, and sitting is for Tuesday, and it is not Tuesday." This reads as the opposite of the rules board in the big tent; the lore keeps it as Rail's own inversion (proposed ruling R-NEW-1), so no game text needs to change.

## Loose threads

Whether she has ever been caught sitting, and what day the Flock decided it was.
