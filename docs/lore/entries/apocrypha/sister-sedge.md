Keeper of the reed tea at Wader's Rest. She tends the urn, knows who owes a cup, and pours it with great gentleness and no comment.

## What gnomes say

"Sister Sedge has never asked anyone to wobble. She just keeps the cups clean."

## The truth

The tea is very weak and tastes of pond. Sedge keeps a tally on the urn in pencil and forgives every entry. She is the Flock's real treasurer.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: at the tea urn in Wader's Rest. Her cup is the reward for leaving the Flock gracefully (`gta-sat-down`).
