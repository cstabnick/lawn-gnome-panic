The Flock's sceptic in good standing: she stands directly before the Standing Lady, believes in everything the Flock says except one thing, and has not told Mother Saxifrage which.

Home: a grey tent on the ring at Wader's Rest. Work: the Flock (stands the middle of the circle).

## What gnomes say

"Spoonbill was told the Lady is a flamingo. She was told it by a flamingo. She says it did not look at her."

"She stands nearest the Lady and trusts her least. The Lady has never minded."

## The truth

Spoonbill knows the Lady is a plastic lawn ornament, as every gnome in the camp does, and holds that this is exactly the right size of mystery for one leg. She stands in the middle of the ring because someone has to be the centre, and it might as well be someone who asks. She is the first to wobble in a thunderstorm and the last to leave a sermon.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: on the ring of the ritual circle at Wader's Rest (angle 0 degrees, due north of the circle's centre, in line with the Lady), one-legged and grey. Her bubble: "I was told the Lady is a flamingo. I was told that by a flamingo. It did not look at me."
