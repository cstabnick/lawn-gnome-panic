A Crop Duster with a long rope behind it and a giant advert banner dragging at the end, flying slow laps over Turfton and the ring highways. Its pilot is Dorrie Throttle, who has never once been seen out of the cockpit and sends the rental invoices in a tin.

## Story: the lap rate

The banner is rented by the lap. Each of Turfton's sixteen brands (`brands.ts`) may buy space on it, and nobody else may: a brand that is not from Turfton is not allowed to advertise in the sky over the town. The rates go up with the Hat Watch heat, because a rival brand's banner is the first thing the Watch looks for. Each lap carries two adverts, one a brand's own message and the other, now and then, a line that nobody paid for.

## What gnomes say

"Read the banner, then look away. It's the only way to keep your hat on."

"Dorrie's banner has the best slogans in town, and nobody can prove who writes them."

## The Easter-egg banners

Every so often the banner says something no brand asked for. A proposal to someone called Bernice, signed by the pilot, circles loop three over the Turfton ring. A line from Wind-Sock Wally reads "STALL SPEED'S 22, KID", which Wally will not explain. The eggs are rare on purpose, so a gnome who sees one tells the others.

## The truth

A torn banner is a known hazard. Gusts over the ring highways snap the rope now and then, and the sign comes down in a heap of cloth on whatever is below it. The brands write this into their rental fine print and nobody reads that. Crop Dusters and their banners are not insured by Kiln-Shield.

## In the games

The Sky-Sign Banner Tow is an NPC aircraft in the sky traffic of Gnome Theft Auto (`games/gnome-theft-auto/src/skyTraffic/`). It flies a slow loop over the town and the ring highways, trailing the banner, and it is shot down like any other vehicle: it crashes and burns like a mower, and the operator sends another one later. The banner's adverts come from `ads.ts`.
