The Carburettors' night meet at the west foot of the Turfton Skyway: tyre stacks, a lamp on a plank and a boombox on a
bonnet. Quiet by day, with a few regulars; full of lowriders after dark.

## Inside & Stories

- **Organiser:** Ratchet Gaskin. **Bookie:** Tuppence Pomfret, who takes wagers on the Skyway Sprint and does not do IOUs.
  **Flag-dropper:** Wick "Chequers" Lisle, who has never once got the flag wrong.
- **Residents:** Kipper Bodkin polishes a hubcap that is not attached to anything; Verbena Plimsoll checks tyre pressures
  with a thumb and a very serious face.
- **The regulars racing pickups:** Bolt-Hole Barney, Flo Camshaft and Gasket Greaves, on lowriders.
- **Cameo:** Lugnut Lulu, captain of the Carburettors, turns up here; the night of #5 (Cam's part) is staged at the meet.

## What gnomes say
