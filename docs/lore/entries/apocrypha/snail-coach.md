Turfton's boxy, slow-rolling public transit bus fitted with overhead garden-twine hand straps for commuters stepping in from beyond the Fence.

## Field Notes & Stories

Every newcomer's first sight of Turfton is through the Snail-Coach's smudged plastic windshield as it rumbles up to `SPAWN` (`-6.25, 14`). Its suspension sways like a hammock in a gale, yet commuters calmly hold onto the twine straps even when a reckless hijacker barrel-rolls it around a downtown corner.

## What gnomes say

"Always thank the Snail-Coach driver—even if you're about to steal his bus."
