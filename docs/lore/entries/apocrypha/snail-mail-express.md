Turfton's slow, unionized, remarkably reliable parcel service powered by racing snails, locked in a delivery war with Pigeon-Drop Couriers.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"Eventually Is Still On Time."* Backed by Union Local 404 and favored by paranoid couriers like Shelldon "No-Trail" Mucilage, Snail-Mail Express guarantees your letter won't be dropped from two hundred feet onto a porch roof—even if it takes three days to cross two city blocks.

## Consumer Reports

Zero crushed mailboxes, encrypted micro-scroll options available, and couriers who refuse to step on paved asphalt.

## Loose threads

How Shelldon's racing snails consistently outrun Pigeon-Drop birds during alley-races.
