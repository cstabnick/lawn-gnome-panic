The Snail-Mail Express sorting tower of Turfton Park & Arts: a thin red-brick wedding cake, with a post-office clock on top that runs slow on purpose. Shops sit at street level under the sorting floors.

## What gnomes say

"Our deliveries are never late. The clock is late. That is how we know the deliveries are on time."

"Do not ask the clock the time. Ask the postman, and then wait for him."

## In the games

It stands on the x = -80, z = -30 block of Turfton Park & Arts, a narrow red-brick stack that rises above the galleries. Its silhouette, tiered facade and clock crown are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
