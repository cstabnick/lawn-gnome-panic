The photographer at Brim Gap: teal hat, grey beard, who has taken forty photos of the same tarn under different clouds.

## What gnomes say

"Forty photos of the tarn. Same tarn. Different clouds."

## The truth

He has never shown anybody the photographs; he keeps them in order of cloud. He eats at the Top Hat, where Velma Crown counts him twice.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): at the viewpoint at Brim Gap, facing away from the road, "look-around" idle.

## Loose threads

Whether he is photographing the tarn or waiting for something above it.
