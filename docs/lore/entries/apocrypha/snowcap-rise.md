The high country north of Frostbite Fells, where the road gives up being a road and the snow stops melting at the edges. It holds the ski hill, a frozen pond, a toadstool forest and a ridge of pinwheels, and very few gnomes who mind being there.

## What gnomes say

"Past the last cairn it's all Rise. Wear two hats."
"The HOA sends its plows up the Rise. It has never sent a gnome who wanted to stay."

## The truth

The Rise is the Fells' older, quieter neighbour. The wind is steady there, the ground is high and the HOA's patrols thin out, which is why the crew's friends at the edges are scattered across it and why the Combine's welded plates head this way at night.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3): the north-highlands band (z -1400 to -900), built by E01 and dressed by the area epics. Quest rows 37 (m37-snowdrop-slope) and 38 (m38-pail-pond) use the region tag `snowcap-rise`; row 27 (m27-heap-of-trouble) ends with a convoy vanishing toward it. It is act III's northern base.
