The high country north of Frostbite Fells, where the road gives up being a road and the snow stops melting at the edges. It holds the ski hill, a frozen pond, a toadstool forest and a ridge of pinwheels, and very few gnomes who mind being there.

## What gnomes say

"Past the last cairn it's all Rise. Wear two hats."
"The HOA sends its plows up the Rise. It has never sent a gnome who wanted to stay."

## The truth

The Rise is the Fells' older, quieter neighbour. The wind is steady there, the ground is high and the HOA's patrols thin out, which is why the crew's friends at the edges are scattered across it and why the Combine's welded plates head this way at night.

## In the games

The north band of the map (code region `north-highlands`, x -1400 to 1800, z -1800 to -900 since the second expansion; west of x -200 it stops at z -1400, where the Unmown begins), built by E01. Roads: `north-band` runs on from the north highway at (20, -870) west to (-950, -950), and `north-band-east` runs east along the top edge to Pinwheel Ridge's end. Snowdrop Slope (-350, -1175) is 220 x 350 m on a mountain peaking about 65 m near (-350, -1340). The Wool Run starts near the summit and the old short course below it is the Nursery (R-250). Pail Pond (-640, -1060) is 180 x 140 m. Capwood (250, -1180) is 300 x 200 m on the Fells mountain's north side. Pinwheel Ridge (850, -1200) is 300 x 90 m on a ridge. The Capwood Run starts at a paddock (90, -760) in Frostbite Fells and finishes near (150, -1300).

Quest rows 37 (m37-snowdrop-slope) and 38 (m38-pail-pond) use the region tag `snowcap-rise`; row 27 (m27-heap-of-trouble) ends with a convoy vanishing toward it. It is act III's northern base.

The Rise is under snow (R-250): white everywhere above the band road, with a patchy frost edge where it meets greener ground, a snow apron round Pail Pond and the slope, Capwood's hollow thawed green and Mittenholt's green kept clear.

Since the second expansion the band reaches the north edge, where the High Tarn sits (R-177). The Hatbrim (R-230) stands on that north edge with the Brim Gap Road, the Hatband, the Brim Walk and the Spine Cut (batch 3, E33).

## Look and motifs

- Motifs: bare high ground above the Fells; drystone walls; cairns; wind-bent pines; slate bothies.
- Palette: snow, stone and slate walls, slate roofs; sapphire flowers only on the thawed ground (Capwood, Mittenholt's green).
- In the game: theme `north-highlands` in `src/data/regionThemes.json`.

## Everyday

**Shops.** Almost none: the Lodge at Snowdrop Slope sells the cocoa, Mittenholt sells nothing, and Velma Crown's Top Hat at Brim Gap sells tea, buns and the view. Everything else is carried up in a pocket.

**Notices.**
- A cairn at every road bend carries a flat stone with one number chalked on it: the hats advised.
- The HOA plow schedule, pinned to the Lodge porch: "Plowing: when it snows. Not plowing: when it does not. Complaints: see Plowing."

**Sayings and habits.**
- "Two hats, one spare." Every Rise gnome carries a spare hat and lends it without being asked.
- At noon the whole Rise stops and listens for the pinwheels on the Ridge. If they are quiet, so is everyone else.
- Local drink: cocoa. Local feud: the Lodge against Mittenholt, about whose mittens the lost mittens are.

**Small history.** The band road was an HOA project to reach the Ridge's cable. It reached the Ridge. It did not reach anywhere else, and the Rise has used it ever since.
