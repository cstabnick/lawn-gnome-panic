The ski hill of Snowcap Rise: a long white slope with a rope tow, a sledding run and a row of striped flags. Gentian Bootlace teaches there, and the Woolly Sock Lodge sits at the foot.

## What gnomes say

"The rope tow has pulled every gnome up this hill at least once, and dropped most of them."
"Ski down fast and keep your hat low. The slope forgives a fall. It does not forgive a salt-plow."

## In the games

The Bootlace Tow runs from its base at (-300, -1028) to near the top at (-325, -1270), 8 m/s, on seven pylons with 24 handles on the rope loop. The slope's ski hill site is x -460 to -240, z -1300 to -1000. 
Built (batch 3, E12): a snow slope from about 60 m at its north top down to its foot beside the Woolly Sock Lodge (E01 site ski-hill). The Bootlace Tow pulls riders up the east side, the Wool Run is the ski slalom, and the Tea-Tray Run is the sled lane down the west side. A piste map at the foot shows both runs. Falls are tumbles: nobody cracks on snow (R-186). Quest row 37 (m37-snowdrop-slope): Thermos Theo sends the crew up, and they ski or sled down the slope to lose the HOA salt-plows.

## Everyday

**Shops.** The Lodge's porch hatch and rack serve the slope: Hot Cocoa 6 Acorns, skis 8, tea trays 4 (see the Woolly Sock Lodge). Open from first snow until Gentian says it is spring.

**Notices.** The signs on the slope are its own jokes, and every one is true.
- Foot of the hill: "SNOWDROP SLOPE. GRADIENT: YES."
- The Bootlace Tow: "HOLD ON. LET GO. IN THAT ORDER."
- The Wool Run hut: "SIX GATES. NO EXCUSES."
- The Tea-Tray Run: "ROSE SIDE UP."
- Beside the tow: "LOST & FOUND (MOSTLY LEFT)", holding one mitten, two scarves and a ski.

**Sayings and habits.**
- "Snowplough, not snow-plow." Gentian's first lesson, and the only one learners remember.
- Everyone who reaches the top turns round and waves at the Lodge. The Lodge waves back with a sock.
- A flattened LEARNERS plate lies beside a snowman with one ski. Nobody moves either.

**Small history.** The tow was strung one night by someone nobody saw, and it ran the next morning. The first rider was a sled.
