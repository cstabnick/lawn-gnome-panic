Sorrel Tillbrook's broadcast booth beside Unmowed Hollow: a wall of crates, a tape bank and a dish pointed at the sky. It is the only station in Turfton that runs without a motor.

## What gnomes say
*"Stay off the paved bits, friends, and keep your hats on."* (Sorrel, on air)

## In the games
Stage `sorrel-radio-booth`. Sorrel broadcasts from here, and she rides nothing with an engine.
