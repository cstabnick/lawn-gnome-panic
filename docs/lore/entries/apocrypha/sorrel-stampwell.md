The postmistress of Pebblewick: quick-eyed, with a stamp pad always inked and a pile of postcards she does not read.

## What gnomes say

"I never read the postcards. I glance. For the stamps."

## The truth

Sorrel reads more than she admits. She keeps every postcard from the Hopper in a drawer, sorted by the stamp.

## In the games

Keeps the post desk in Pebblewick. The Hopper's mail goes through her.
