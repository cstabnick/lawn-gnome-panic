The foreman of the Pigeon-Drop Couriers depot in Clay-Mart Industrial, who runs the company's ground crew.

## Field Notes

Pigeon-Drop drops its parcels from altitude. The parcels that must arrive in one piece (hot food, fragile ornaments, sealed VIP envelopes, crates too heavy for a pigeon, and the mail round) go out from Sid's orange shed by bike, cart and van instead. Sid sorts with a clipboard and a pencil behind each ear. He hates Snail-Mail Express on principle and admires their record in private.

## What gnomes say

"Fragile means fragile. If it rattles, it is yours." Sid, every morning.

## In the games

The Courier job. Any ground vehicle (a push-bike carries two parcels). Load at the depot, then drop each parcel at its address round town (and the outskirts from level 4) before its time. Bumps and crashes dent the parcels; a dented fragile parcel pays much less.
