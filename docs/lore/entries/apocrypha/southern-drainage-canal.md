A concrete-lined runoff channel dividing Turfton's southern suburbs from Cloverfield Farms, claimed by Baroness Brenda as the Sovereign Republic of New Puddle. It is a channel with a shallow, wadeable floor: waded at the edges and in the fords, floated in the deep middle.

## Field Notes & Tales

The canal floor is shallow enough to wade, which makes it a nightmare for low-slung racers and a paradise for heckling frogs. It steps down twice, through two sponsored locks (see [turfton-waterworks](turfton-waterworks.md)). Two wharves serve it: Old Brick at the west end and the Industrial wharf at the east, where the boats are moored. A road bridge crosses at the south highway, sized for a mower. A castaway gnome named Driftwood once washed ashore here on a popsicle-stick raft, babbling about "lawn sharks" and open-ocean lawn-boats. Today, Brenda's Push-Mower Bike gang controls the bridges.
