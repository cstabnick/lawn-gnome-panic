A concrete-lined runoff channel dividing Turfton's southern suburbs from Cloverfield Farms, claimed by Baroness Brenda as the Sovereign Republic of New Puddle.

## Field Notes & Tales

Shallow rainwater pools along the canal floor, making it a nightmare for low-slung racers and a paradise for heckling frogs. A castaway gnome named Driftwood once washed ashore here on a popsicle-stick raft, babbling about "lawn sharks" and open-ocean lawn-boats. Today, Brenda's Push-Mower Bike gang controls the bridges.

## What gnomes say

"Pay Brenda's toll in spark plugs, or take the long way around through the clover."
