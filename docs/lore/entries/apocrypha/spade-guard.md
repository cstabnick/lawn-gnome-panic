Olive-capped sentries who guard Fort Sandpit and its flying mower. Stricter than the Hat Watch, and better cracked.

## The truth

Fifteen years of guard rosters, a manual and one commander: the Guard are the soldiers of a base that has never had a war, and an armoury locked by the HOA's own remote. They wear olive caps, carry a spade each on their parade belts and salute at nothing. Under Diggs they do not choose sides until Vesper fires them by phone; after that they are the coalition's military arm, and its olive tent stands in the ring at the overlook camp.

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: sentries at Fort Sandpit's posts. Walking through the gate makes them spray on sight.
- Act IV: hostile until `ch4-spade-truce` (row 59), where Diggs says "Nobody fires the Guard" and they join the crew; allies from then, in W15's patrols on the walls and W16's olive tent. "Salute to a Mole" (row 60, with a pin for the neck) rewards a dig with no mole cracked.

## Everyday

- **Rosters:** fifteen years of guard rosters hang on the wall of Diggs's bungalow, one sheet a year, each with the same four towers, the same four names and, on the last Thursday of every month, the same note: "Relief: none." One nail is empty, where the medal hung.
- **Habits:** each Guard carries one spade on the parade belt and one on duty. Salutes are given to the commander, the flag and any hat of rank. The pay is Dust Biscuits and a day off in Pebblewick.
- **Sayings:** "Nobody fires the Guard." "A spade is a promise, with a handle."
- **Feud:** the Guard against the HOA's clerks at the lost-property shed, over whether a medal can be lost if it was never held.
