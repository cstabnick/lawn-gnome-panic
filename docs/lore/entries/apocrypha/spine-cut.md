A road cut through the Highland Spine near (520, -1170), blasted out for the Pinwheel Ridge works, running from the north band road to the east band road. Halfway through, a rock arch called the Keyhole stands over the road, and it was left there.

## What gnomes say

"Go through the Keyhole slowly. The surveyor is still measuring."
"They cut the Spine in two and left the one bit that matters standing over the road."

## The truth

The Keyhole was left because the surveyor "had not finished measuring it". Plumb Bob has measured it every day since, waiting to be sure (R-232). The cut is the shortest way between the two band roads, which is why the convoys use it and why the Hat Watch has never managed to park a van under the arch.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): `mountainRoutes/defs/spineCut.ts` gives the road and its spots, `mountainRoutes/spine.ts` the cut's shape and pads. The Keyhole is near (520, -1170), above the road, with the Pinwheel Ridge site (x 700..1000, z -1245..-1155) to the east and the Capwood site to the west.
