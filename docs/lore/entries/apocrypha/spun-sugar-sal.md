The candyfloss seller on the boardwalk: pink hat, pale shirt, brown pigtails, and a firm view that candyfloss is a vegetable.

## What gnomes say

"Candyfloss is a vegetable. Sal says so. Do not argue with the cart."

## The truth

Sal's cart is on the boardwalk near the Spokewheel, at about (-152, 1112). She has the only permit on the coast for "sugar, spun" (granted by Marram, in pencil) and treats it like a charter.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E02): a vendor with the "vendor-call" idle at the cart; her stall is the Spun Sugar Sal's in the coast layout.
