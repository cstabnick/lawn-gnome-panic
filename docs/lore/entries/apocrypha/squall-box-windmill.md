Rooster Weatherby's rusted windmill in the Cloverfield fields, beside the silo-haul route. Its red blades turn over the crops, and a lookout platform at the top is where he shouts his prophecies down at the road.

## What gnomes say
*"I'm not coming down, and neither is the sky."* (Rooster, from the platform)

## In the games
Stage `rooster-windmill`, built from the new `gta-windmill` prefab. Rooster lives up here and does not come down.
