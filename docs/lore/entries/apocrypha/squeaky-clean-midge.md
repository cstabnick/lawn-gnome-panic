Hyper-caffeinated Glaze Clinic intern vibrating at 140 BPM who treats a minor hat chip like five-alarm trauma and can re-glaze a shattered bumper in four seconds.

## Story: Espresso-Acorn Protocol

Midge wears a dual-cup espresso-acorn helmet with twin straws routed directly to her mouth and hasn't blinked since Tuesday. Whenever a Glaze Clinic Ambulance drops a cracked gnome outside the hospital, Midge is already waiting with a bucket of quick-set slip and a heat gun.

Patients wake up fully intact, though occasionally with a slight peppermint scent and a receipt stamped *KILN-SHIELD: CLAIM DENIED*.

## What gnomes say

"Don't ask what's in Midge's glaze bucket. Just thank her that your ears are glued back on straight."
