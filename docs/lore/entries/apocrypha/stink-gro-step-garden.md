The Stink-Gro Step Garden is a terracotta stepped pyramid in Downtown Turfton, with a balcony on every step, a brew tank on the top and a racing-slug mural down one face. It smells exactly as its name says.

## What gnomes say

"Step up the Garden and you will be on a step. Step up again and you will be on a smaller step. Keep going and you will reach the tank, and then you will know why it smells."

"Stink-Gro grows everything on the balconies. Nobody has ever asked what it grows for."

## In the games

It stands on the x = -30, z = 90 block of Downtown Turfton, a stepped terracotta pyramid with its balconies and brew tank on top. Its stepped silhouette, balcony rhythm and mural are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
