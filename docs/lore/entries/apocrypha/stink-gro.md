Neon-lime and purple synthetic plant-food corporation selling hyper-concentrated fertilizer pellets and glowing green lawn paint to the HOA.

## Corporate Lore & Ads

Slogan: *"Grow Twice As Tall By Thursday. Don't Ask How."* Stink-Gro billboards mock Pure-Loam Co-op as *"Expensive Mud For Gnomes With Beards,"* while Sir Reginald Clayfoot quietly pays his rent recording deep-voiced radio ads for their pressurized fertilizer canisters.

## Consumer Reports

HOA inspectors love Stink-Gro's neon-green lawn paint for faking healthy grass before audits; gardeners hate the purple sludge it leaves in rain gutters.

## Loose threads

Persistent rumors that Stink-Gro's secret ingredient is distilled from the Chapel of Mulch's leftover runoff.
