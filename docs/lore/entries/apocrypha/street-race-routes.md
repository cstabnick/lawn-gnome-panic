Five races laid over roads that already exist, so nothing is built for them but a start gate and some barriers: the Kerb Row Circuit downtown, the Skyway Sprint over the bridge, the Ring Road Run round the ring highways, the Saltbucket Dash down to the sea and La Boucle de Pardonlin round the European town.

## What gnomes say

"They don't close the road for the Ring Road Run. They just hope nobody has an appointment."
"The Dash starts at the anthill and ends in the sea air. That's the whole plan."

## The truth

Each route follows real road centre lines. **The Kerb Row Circuit** is closed and 10 m wide: east along the z -120 road, down x 120, west on z 60, then a jog north, west and south that makes the chicane, and back up x -120; ten gates. **The Skyway Sprint** is open and 11 m wide, from the West Road at the Skyway's foot up the ramp, east along the deck and down the east ramp to (550, 24). **The Ring Road Run** is the Carburettors' illicit time trial, closed and 10 m wide, twelve gates round the ring highways: the West Road link, the South Road bend, the North Road bend and the ring again. **The Saltbucket Dash** is open and 10 m wide, from the anthill roundabout's south side down the East Band to (950, 935) and west along the South Band to (150, 975). **La Boucle de Pardonlin** is the tight one, 7 m wide and closed: pont-nord west to the Boulevard, south, pont-sud east to Rue D, north up Rue Haute and back along Rue A.

## In the games

Defined in `raceTrack/defs/streets.ts` and run from the street-race meets in `streetRace/`. The Saltbucket Lay-By, the Skyway Foot and the Pardonlin cafe grandstand have their own entries.
