The western edge of the Fringe, where Turfton's woodpiles and sawing yards meet the pines of Mossy Wood, and the pines
have been quietly taking the yards back for years.

## What gnomes say

"Stumpend is the end of the stump. Past that, the wood starts." The woodcutters say there are as many stumps out here
as there are trees, and that the stumps have been waiting longer.

## Inside & Stories

- Woodpiles stacked square and then not square, with an axe stuck in a block by every pile.
- Lean-to sheds with planks for walls and a wheelbarrow inside, tools hanging off the back wall in the order the owner
  last used them.
- Sawing yards where the logs wait to be cut, with a saw bench under a corrugated roof.
- Pine needles in the gutters, and a lone sapling growing up through the middle of a half-built house.

## In the games

Stumpend is the west edge of the Fringe, bordering Mossy Wood. Its lots are woodpiles, lean-to sheds and half-built
houses, thinning out into the trees.

## Loose threads

Whether the trees are advancing, or the woodcutters are retreating so slowly that it only looks like advancing.
