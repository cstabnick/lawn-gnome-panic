108.0 Subsoil Radio: the fourth station on Turfton FM's dial, above the other three. Underground techno, played as one
long set that never restarts, by a mole called DJ Moleman.

## What gnomes say

"Nobody puts it there." It is on every mower's dial anyway, after the Beats and before the radio goes quiet. Gnomes who
have driven all night say it is the only station that does not feel like a station.

## The set

One set, one key, one tempo, and no gaps. The set changes colour three ways and always travels through the drums:

- **Dub** (deep, echoing chord stabs, a soft round kick and a long sub).
- **Drum** (heavy, housey percussion: claps, toms tuned to the key, shakers, rim ticks, a few stabs).
- **Acid** (a squealing, filter-swept bass line, with a high ping answering it on its own beat cycle).

Dub and acid never touch. The night runs dub, drum, acid, drum, acid, drum, dub and starts again, a little different
each lap. The drums arrive first when one colour hands over to the next, the harmony follows, and the low end swaps
over in a single bar. In the acid cuts the filter opens over the whole cut, swells through each phrase and sweeps wide
open after a breakdown.

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: station `under` in `radio/content.ts`. It is a bonus station: "Drive-Time Regular" asks for a song
  on the three main stations and does not count it. The set is `buildUndergroundSet` in `@lawn/music`, and the song
  credit on screen follows the part of the set that is playing. E17: once the lamps are lit, the station plays live in Loamdeep's Under-Chorus Hall, the floor humming along (`sq-loamdeep-under-chorus`).

## Everyday

- **Notices:** On the Loamdeep wall by the decks: "Not the transmitter." "The floor you are standing on is the speaker." "Please do not say 'mower' and 'kick drum' in the same breath. Say 'seat'."
- **Sayings and habits:** "Turn it up until the floor agrees." Moles tune by pressing an ear to the ground; gnomes tune by pressing the Beats button four times and holding. A listener who drives off the dial the long way "has gone to Subsoil".
- **Small history:** The first night it went out, the only complaint was from a badger, who said it was the most restful noise he had been given since the thaw.

## Loose threads

The station is not on the HOA's list of licensed frequencies. Neither is the end of the dial.
