A book in a tin box on the summit of the Bobble, kept since before the HOA. Signing it is a rite: "you were here, and so was your hat". It is the only document on the Hatbrim that nobody has tried to amend.

## What gnomes say

"Sign the book. It does not ask your name. It asks if you meant it."

"Page one says we were here, and so were our hats. Everyone since has agreed."

## The truth

The register stands at the Bobble's top (105 m), on the path at the summit spot, beside the summit cairn. The entries the game reads back are short and hat-minded: "Reached the top. Hat blew off. Worth it. - B."; "Came for the view. Got the wind. Fair trade. - P.T."; "Wore two hats. Needed three. - M."; "You can see the Fence from here. Wish I could not. - anon"; "Restacked a cairn on the way up. Do not tell the others. - ?". The sixth begins with page one, and its second page repeats the first. Dunstan Cairn, a walker, sits reading it.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): an interactable on the Bobble ("Sign the Summit Register"), which unlocks an achievement and reads back one earlier entry. `mountainRoutes/hatbrimPlan.ts` (`REGISTER_LINES`). The beacon of act IV stands on the same summit (`hatbrim-beacon`).

## Everyday

- **Habits:** walkers sign, read an earlier line, and tell the next person which one. By long custom the first gnome up in spring turns to page one, "to keep it honest".
- **Sayings:** "Sign the book. It does not ask your name. It asks if you meant it."
- **Notices:** a tin lid, scratched, reads "Close the box. The wind reads too."
