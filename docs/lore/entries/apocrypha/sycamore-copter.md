A two-seat helicopter whose cabin is a giant sycamore seed pod on two skids, and whose rotor is the pod's own pair of seed wings. Parked north of the airstrip, on the way to the Dust Flats.

## Field Notes & Specs

The Sycamore Copter is a proper hovering machine. It climbs when the collective is pulled, holds still in a hover and turns on its pedals, and it can drift sideways once it is off the ground. It does not need a runway. Gnomes who have flown the Whirligig say it is a mower that wanted to be a bird; the Sycamore Copter is the bird it was trying to be.

## What gnomes say

"It doesn't flap. It doesn't roll. It just goes where it's asked, and then it hangs there looking pleased about it."

## Loose threads

Who grew the sycamore that became the pod, and whether it ever dropped its seed wings on purpose.
