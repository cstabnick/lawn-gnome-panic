The poet of the Jardin, Pardonlin: plum hat, cream shirt, no beard, reading on a bench and composing an ode to that same bench, which so far rhymes with nothing, which is perfect.

## What gnomes say

"I am composing an ode to a bench. So far it rhymes with nothing. Perfect."

## The truth

He has been working on the ode for three seasons and is delighted that there is nothing to rhyme with "bench". A Continental, gentle and not mean: he never writes anything unkind about the bench.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E30): a resident of the Jardin at about (-1586, -735), "read" idle.

## Loose threads

Whether the ode has an ending, or only a bench.
