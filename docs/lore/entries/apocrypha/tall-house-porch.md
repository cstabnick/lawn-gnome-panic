A towering, weathered wooden porch at the far western edge of Mossy Wood, sealed with crossed planks where terrified porcelain gnomes occasionally stumble out.

## Field Notes & Tales

Even when the sun is high, ambient birdsong drops off fifty yards from the porch steps. Earthenware street toughs who mock ghosts in Turfton refuse to park near the planks after dusk. Inside the cracks between boards, faint yellow lantern-light sometimes drifts past—and never casts a shadow in the right direction.

## What gnomes say

"Don't look at the lantern, and if a porcelain gnome asks for a ride, just open the door."
