A redwood-sized yellow cedar pencil buried horizontally directly beneath `x: 0, z: 0` in the Under-Deck, its pink eraser wedged into the bedrock.

## Field Notes & Tales

Discovered by paranoid surveyor Mossley "Plumb-Line" Inkcap and storm-drain diver Sal, the colossal pencil aligns suspiciously well with Turfton's 60-unit street grid. Mossley promptly chipped off three pounds of graphite to sell as artisan eyebrow pencil, funding his retirement before anyone could ask why the Tall Ones used a giant pencil to lay out a town.

## What gnomes say

"Look down a drain at dead-centre downtown and you can still see yellow paint flaking off the concrete."
