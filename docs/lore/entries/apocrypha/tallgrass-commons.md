A parkland of grass at the south-west corner of Turfton's map, grown so tall that a gnome drives through it as through a forest.

## What gnomes say

"The grass on the Commons is taller than the Fence was. Keep to the tracks and you'll be fine."

## In the games

Reached by a spur road south from the far end of the west highway, the same junction as Loch Moss. Stands of giant
grass hide a gnome on foot from the Hat Watch and slow a mower to a crawl; a mower cuts a path that grows back. Bees
work the flowers around their hives, and a knocked hive sends a swarm after you that chips your clay until you reach
water or outrun it.

Four straw hives sit in flower rings in the grass, and the flat-topped Hive Hill at (-750, 720) has a trail up its side. On foot inside the grass the Hat Watch cannot see you.

## Loose threads

Who keeps the Commons, if anyone does. The grass has not been cut in living memory, and no gnome will say why.
