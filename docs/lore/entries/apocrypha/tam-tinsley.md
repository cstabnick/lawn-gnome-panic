Keeper of Tin Shy: blue hat, grey shirt, three chipped acorns a go and a pyramid of tins, and sulks whenever somebody wins.

## What gnomes say

"Nobody has won today. I am in a very good mood."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (FX11, rulings R-243 and R-248): behind the Tin Shy counter at (-45, 1109.5), looking about; the tin pyramid stands in front of the stall. Look: A blue hat and a slate-grey shirt.

## Loose threads

How long a win lasts before he is cheerful again.
