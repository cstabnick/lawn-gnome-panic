Turfton's sharpest chop-shop fixer and the proprietor of Side-Valve Salvage, claiming direct descent from Fescue's legendary Gnorwin Clayfoot.

## Story: The Wrenches on the Pegboard

Tansy keeps forty-seven wrenches hung in size order above her workbench, and a forty-eighth one—cast in tarnished brass—locked in a cigar box. She tells rookies she bought it at a flea market. Only Pip has ever seen her polish it before a big heist, muttering old Fescue proverbs she swears she doesn't believe in.

When Vesper Thorne's HOA served her garage a 48-hour foreclosure lien, Tansy didn't yell. She simply brewed a pot of chicory coffee, welded a steel bumper onto a Ride-On Mower, and asked the protagonist who owned the nicest flowerpots in Patio Heights.

## What gnomes say

"Tansy can tell the year, make, and stolen status of a Ride-On Mower just by listening to it idle from three blocks away."

## The truth
Tansy owns Side-Valve Salvage and heads the crew; she is a reluctant heist planner. She wants to keep the bay open and stay out of everybody's war, and fears losing the place she built (and being found out as no Clayfoot at all). Home: the Loft above Side-Valve, then the Under-Deck culvert in act III. Voice: dry, low, short; garden proverbs twisted into street slang; orders in three short clauses; she does not raise her voice until act III; chicory coffee is her stress tell, twice in the whole game. She touches no one until the act III hug.

Arc: "not my bay, not my problem" while her bay is the target (I); the warm high point at the party (#34) with the forty-eighth wrench story, then the bay is seized (#35); underground in the culvert, surfacing for the crew and the ridge call (III); in act IV she calls a heist big enough for the brass wrench, and hangs the forty-eighth wrench back up on the wall (forty-eight, R-071). Her surname beyond Clayfoot is never given, and "allegedly" Gnorwin's descendant (R-139) stays open.
