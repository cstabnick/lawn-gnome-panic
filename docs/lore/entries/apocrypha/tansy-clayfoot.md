Turfton's sharpest chop-shop fixer and the proprietor of Side-Valve Salvage, claiming direct descent from Fescue's legendary Gnorwin Clayfoot.

## Story: The Wrenches on the Pegboard

Tansy keeps forty-seven wrenches hung in size order above her workbench, and a forty-eighth one—cast in tarnished brass—locked in a cigar box. She tells rookies she bought it at a flea market. Only Pip has ever seen her polish it before a big heist, muttering old Fescue proverbs she swears she doesn't believe in.

When Vesper Thorne's HOA served her garage a 48-hour foreclosure lien, Tansy didn't yell. She simply brewed a pot of chicory coffee, welded a steel bumper onto a Ride-On Mower, and asked the protagonist who owned the nicest flowerpots in Patio Heights.

## What gnomes say

"Tansy can tell the year, make, and stolen status of a Ride-On Mower just by listening to it idle from three blocks away."
