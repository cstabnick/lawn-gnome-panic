The Turfton Tattler's offices: a square block of stained, rain-streaked dark brick with a timber water tank on the roof. Hedda Galley's newsroom takes the top floor. The side wall carries a mural of a gnome on a mower, under the words "MOWERS ARE FOR BORROWING".

## What gnomes say

"MOWERS ARE FOR BORROWING. The Tattler did not paint that. The Tattler does not paint things. It only prints them afterwards."

"The water tank has never been filled. The Tattler says it is for emergencies, and the emergencies are the stories."

## In the games

It stands on the x = -100, z = 30 block of Turfton Park & Arts, a square dark-brick block with the tank on its roof and the mower mural on the side wall facing the street. Its shape, tank and mural are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
