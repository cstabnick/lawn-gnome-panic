The straight sled lane down the west side of Snowdrop Slope. The sleds are round Tall-One tin tea trays with a painted rose.

## What gnomes say

"Sit in the rose. Hold the rim. Scream if it helps."
"Gnomes call them rose shields. Nobody has used one as a shield."

## In the games

Built on Snowdrop Slope (E12). The Lodge rents a tray for 4 Acorns and sells one to keep for 30 (R-182). The Bootlace Tow pulls sledders up.

Which Tall-One tea service they came from, and whether anyone is missing it.
