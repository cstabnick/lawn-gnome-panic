A pedestrian lane of cafés south of the Hatwheel, every one with its tables out in the road and no road left.

## What gnomes say

"On Teacup Row you do not walk. You are served on the way past."

"Every café has the best coffee on the Row. They have all printed it on a board."

## The truth

The lane is called Teacup Row because the chairs stand in a line and have never been sat in all the way. It has no cars, because there is no room, and no hurry, because there is no reason. A gramophone on a terrace table gives the lane its music.

## In the games

The east bank south of the Hatwheel, around x -1522, z 120 (a screenshot spot). Café Pardon is its best-known café; the Sips are its longest customers.

## Loose threads

Whether any café on the Row has ever closed, or whether they only rotate.
