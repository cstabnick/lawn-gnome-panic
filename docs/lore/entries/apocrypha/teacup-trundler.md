A porcelain teacup on a saucer, fitted with four casters, a seat made of a doily, and a steering wheel that is mostly for show.

## Field Notes

The Teacup Trundler turns up now and then in Turfton traffic, usually spinning. It turns on the spot, quicker than most things on the road (turn rate 3.0), so it can rotate in its own length. It cannot go anywhere quickly: its top speed is 9 units an hour. A pair of porcelain passengers can ride inside without a drop of tea spilling, which is the only reason anyone bothers.

Clara Porcelain's crowd is responsible for most of the Trundlers on the road. They are fond of them, and they do not drive them far.

## What gnomes say

"It can turn round in a bin lid. It cannot get out of a bin lid."

## Loose threads

Whether the saucer was ever a real saucer, and where the teacup was before it was a car. Clara will say only "it was a good cup" and change the subject.

Sunday Driver is the achievement for driving all four odd rides (Pot Rod, Lawn Roller, Teacup Trundler, Barrow Buggy). Its reward is the Sunday Driver's Cap.
