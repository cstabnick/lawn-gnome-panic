The Underspan's welder. Nobody has seen Teasel's face: the visor stays down at meals, in the rain and, it is said, in bed.

## What gnomes say

"Teasel Gimbal has never lifted the visor. I asked. The answer was a small shower of sparks."

## The truth

There is a face under there, and it is quite ordinary. Teasel just likes that nobody has checked. The welds are neat, the sparks are cheerful, and the HOA has cited the visor twice for "concealment of the head".

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: welds beside Hogweed's workshop in the Underspan, with a `weld-crackle` that carries across the yard.
