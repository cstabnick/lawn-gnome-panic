A huge, ancient turtle with a mossy shell who lives in Loch Moss. Most of Turfton thinks she is made up; a few old gnomes and one castaway are sure she is not.

## What gnomes say

"Old gnomes in Turfton say something very large is asleep under the loch. Nobody who says so has been down to look."
"Driftwood swears it sings through the hull of his raft at night. Driftwood swears a lot of things."

## The truth

For writers only. Never said in game. To the Tall Ones, Loch Moss is the overgrown ornamental pond in the back corner of the garden, and Tessie is the pet pond turtle they left behind, still living there years later. To the gnomes she is a giant, ancient monster. She is very calm around the stone heron lying tipped in the reeds, and she seems to wait for someone at the shore at feeding time.

## In the games

The Loch Moss side quest, given by Driftwood on the loch shore (`sq-tessie`). The player follows three sightings around the loch by boat at dusk, drops four sonar buoys while the HOA patrol boat circles, escorts a raft of turtle eggs to her island at (-760, -720) and rams the HOA dredger off three times. Tessie then surfaces beside the island; the player climbs aboard and rides her back to the dock. The last choice is to sell the photo to the Turfton Tattler (+1500 Acorns) or keep her secret (a turtle-shell backpack). Afterwards she surfaces in the loch now and then and can be ridden again.

## Loose threads

How old she is, and whether anyone else lives under the loch with her. Who she waits for at the shore at feeding time. Whether the Tall Ones ever came back for her. Leave all of these open.
