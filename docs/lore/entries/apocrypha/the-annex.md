An office building said to stand at the edge of Club Gnome's square, home of Bucketworth & Sons, Collectors. It has a
reception, Floor 1 (Orientation), Floor 2 (Onboarding), Floor 2½ (Synergy), Floor ∞ (Everything), and a lift whose
panel also has a B. Every clock in it says nine.

## What gnomes say

"Interviews are always at nine."

"Help yourself to anything that glows. That's sort of the job." (Primrose, at reception)

"Don't wake them. They're on their break. Since nine." (of the coworkers asleep at their desks on Floor 2½)

## The truth

Club Gnome does not show the Annex and need not agree that it is there (R-040). Nothing says whether the player rode
the lift through four floors or nodded off in the reception chair. The game never settles it.

## In the games

- `games/final-collectathon`: every chapter is one floor. The prologue and the ending are in reception; Chapter 1 is
  Floor 1, Chapter 2 Floor 2, Chapter 3 Floor 2½, Chapter 4 Floor ∞. Each chapter ends with a ride in the lift.
- Pressing B on the lift panel is the easter egg Floor B.

## Loose threads

Why the floors go 1, 2, 2½, ∞. What is on Floor B. Why every clock, on every floor, still says nine at the end.
