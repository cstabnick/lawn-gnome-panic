A giant ant mound in the far east, with the observatory on its flat summit and a drive-off ramp on its side, Dean's Leap. The ants still live inside and are Rootwell's landlords, paid in sugar.

## What gnomes say

"Not termites. Do not call them termites. They know."
"The university pays rent in sugar. The ants close the doors when it is late. That is the whole arrangement."

## The truth

The ants are neighbours, not pests: they want sugar and quiet, and say so the only way they can, by shutting the door. The university forgot it was a tenant long ago, and the Dean has been paying late for years. Dean's Leap was a ramp cut for a stunt nobody remembers.

## In the games

Row 46 `m46-starfall-at-the-anthill`: p1 Night Class (cross campus to the base), p2 The Sugar Toll (steal sugar from the Trencher and pay the ants), p3 The Lens, final Dean's Leap (drive off the ramp). Site observatory-mound (1240, 140), built by E01: planned (batch 3).
