A Tall-One toy pirate ship, sea-going now, crewed by Captain Samphire Bilge. She runs pirate tours for the boardwalk crowd and, quietly, real smuggling.

## What gnomes say

"The Queen? Oh, she is a ship. She says so on the side."
"Tours at noon. Cargo at midnight. Do not mix them up."

## The truth

She began life as a bath toy, with a bath. How she got from the bath to the sea is not a story anyone aboard tells twice the same way. She floats, she sails, and the HOA's barges have never caught her.

## In the games

Row 44 `m44-the-bathtub-queen` (board an HOA barge, deliver its cargo, the Combine's spare blades, to her), W11 (she flies the crew's colours) and row 64 `m64-all-hats` (p1 By Water: brought up the canal). The pirate-ship site (E01): planned (batch 3).

How a Tall-One bath toy went to sea. Samphire gives three versions and they do not match.
