A bait and tackle shack on the canal's south bank, by Old Brick wharf. It sells rods and bait, buys catch cards for the Turfton catch register, and runs the Bent Pin Derby.

## What gnomes say

"Rods up on the rack, bait in the cooler. Cards go on the counter. Fish go back in the water."
"Sign says the Bent Pin. Nobody has ever bent a pin in there. That is rather the point of it."

## The truth

The shack is named for its clip. A Turfton rod ends in a nibble-clip, a bent pin with no point, so a fish that bites the bait holds on without being hurt. It can be reeled in, measured and let go. The Bent Pin is where the rods come from, from a bent-pin Twig up to the silver Moonline, and where the bait is sold: dough (free with every rod), cheese rind, acorn crumb, sock-fluff flies and lures. Catch cards are bought here for the Turfton register, which is why every landed fish is measured before it goes back.

## In the games

On the south bank of the canal by Old Brick wharf, in Cloverfield Farms. Wenna Gudgeon keeps the counter, with the rod rack and the bait cooler beside it. The shop buys catch cards for acorns, and the Bent Pin Derby (three minutes, land 150 cm of fish) is entered at the counter.

The hot spots near it are the Lock Pool and the Industrial Reach on the canal, and further off the Reedwatch Shallows, the Loch Deeps and Steamer Bay on Loch Moss, the Ravine Pool, the Frostfin Run and the Sluice Bend on the rivers, and the Puddle Shallows and the Far Puddle at sea. Fish bite faster at a hot spot, and a few are only found there.
