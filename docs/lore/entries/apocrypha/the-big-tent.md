Mother Saxifrage Wader's big grey tent at the heart of Wader's Rest, where the Flock keep their rules, their rota and a small white model of the Lady. It has one door, on the north side, facing the real one.

## What gnomes say

"They keep the rules on the wall in case anyone forgets which leg."

"Inside it is just a tent. Outside it is the tent. They would like that said twice."

## The truth

The tent stands at (-1130, -608), radius 9, with its door on the north face looking straight at the Standing Lady (so a gnome coming out always faces her). Inside it is 16 by 16 metres, grey canvas, with a ring of mats, the reed tea urn on one wall, the rota board and two Flock banners. The rules hang on the west wall: *One leg at every sermon. No sitting on Tuesdays. Pink is for the Lady only. Never turn your back on her to eat. A wobble is a thought; a thought is a cup of reed tea.* A tick-box on the north wall reads "TODAY IS: [ ] TUESDAY [ ] NOT TUESDAY (tick one, carefully)". The prophecy board on the south wall says "PROPHECIES THIS WEEK: the Even Cut, on the longest day. Also rain." Against the south wall stands the shrine: a small model of the Lady in white, because pink is for the Lady only and the model is not her.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: a walk-in interior (`flock-tent`, `SOCIETY_INTERIORS.tent`) entered from Wader's Rest. Sister Plover stands by the rules board ("Rule six is under discussion. It has been under discussion since the spring.") and Novice Pipit on a mat ("Which leg is it? Mother says either. I keep picking the wrong either.").
