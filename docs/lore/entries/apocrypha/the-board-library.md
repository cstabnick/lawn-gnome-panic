The rack of spare skateboards at the north entrance of the Gutter Bowls. Take one, leave one. Fennel Gripwax keeps it stocked.

## What gnomes say

"Take one, leave one. That's the whole rule. Fennel enforces it with a look."

"The rack is never empty. Somebody always leaves one."

## In the games

A rack of spare boards by the north entrance of the park. Walking up and pressing the interact key takes a board for the bag. Fennel restocks it. A board is ridable anywhere paved, and grass stops it (R-211).

## Loose threads

Who made the first board, and whether the rack has ever been emptied.
