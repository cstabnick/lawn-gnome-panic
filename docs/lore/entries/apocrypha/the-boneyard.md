The junkyard on the eastern edge of the city, where wrecked mowers go to be taken apart by people who will not admit
they are taking them apart. Stacks of tyres, a scrap car on blocks, oil drums, woodpiles and a container or two, all
under a sky that never quite decides whether it is raining.

## What gnomes say

"Nothing in the Boneyard is broken. It's just waiting to be a different thing." Rivet Malloy says this to everyone who
asks, and to some who don't.

## Inside & Stories

- Mower carcasses in rows, each tagged with a name on a piece of card and a price that is always crossed out.
- A tyre pile higher than the fence, and a scrap car tilted on blocks, its hood propped up with a broom handle.
- Oil drums stacked in a pyramid, a woodpile with an axe stuck in a block, and a pallet stack that the gnomes
  say has been the same height for years.
- A lean-to shed where the parts are sorted, and a power pole on the road outside with wires that nobody has
  ever seen anyone climb to fix.

## In the games

The Boneyard is part of the Scrapside edge of the Fringe: rough lots, scrap and stacked junk along the dirt tracks
that peel off the highway. It is scenery to drive past, and a good place for a tow truck to think about its next job.

## Loose threads

Where the mowers go after the Boneyard. Rivet says the parts are sold on; the Long Grass League says the mowers are
never really dead.
