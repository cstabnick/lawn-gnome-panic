The Tall One's old bottom garden, south of Hedgeworth's old fence: a wild strip of meadow, a potting shed, a hedge maze and a vegetable patch, now holes 4 to 8 of Hedgeworth Links.

## What gnomes say

"Holes four to eight are next season. They have been next season since before the hedges."

"Go through the Turn Gate and mind the rhubarb. It minds you."

## The truth

Thrift Hosepipe laid out five holes here by hand over three seasons, and Dame Peony named them. The garden was the Tall One's, and still has their potting shed, their maze and their vegetable patch; nobody has told the cabbages (R-226).

## In the games

Built (batch 3, E32): the south strip of the yard, x -1360..-1000, z 829..974, with holes 4 to 8 of Hedgeworth Links. The yard is now 360 by 564 m. It is entered through the Turn Gate at (-1140, 829).
