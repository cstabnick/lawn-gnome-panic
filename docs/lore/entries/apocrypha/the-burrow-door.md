A mound with a round door in it, just across the west road from Mossy Wood's western edge, at (-1000, 300). It is the way down to Loamdeep, and it is always a little open.

## What gnomes say

"There's a door in a mound by the last pines. Don't knock. They don't like it."

"Every gnome knows where it is. Nobody has been through."

## The truth

The door is a round stone, hung so that a mole's shoulder opens it and a spade does not. Once the town knows the Burrowfolk, a lantern burns beside it.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). The mole-entrance site in the Mossy Wood area epic, with the interior in E05's cell. Quest 24 (`m24-the-burrow-door`) ends here. It gets a lantern and Sorrel's "Moles are neighbours" sign (W06).
