Small, gentle, formal moles who live in Loamdeep. Gnomes call them root-eaters and Tall Ones blame them for every lump in a lawn. Both are wrong. They do not wear hats.

## What gnomes say

"Moles eat the roots. Everyone knows it. Put a spade by the hole and be done."

"There is a mole under every bad lawn. That is not a thing I would say if I had been to a good one."

## The truth

The Burrowfolk eat grubs, and the grubs eat the roots; their tunnels keep a lawn aerated and a garden alive. They are shy, say "if it pleases you" and mean it, and almost nothing the surface says about them is true. `dj-moleman` is one of them by birth and lives apart in his own burrow under Turfton; the family loves him and wishes he would visit. They say their great-greats came "from under the Fence, running", which may or may not tie them to the Fescue `mole-clans`. Moles wear no hats, except Moleman's, on his antenna.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Met in quests 24 (`m24-the-burrow-door`) and 49 (`m49-loamdeep`); sheltered in 48 (`m48-the-groundskeepers-secret`); diggers in 60 (`m60-the-moles-dig`). W13 (after quest 49) puts moles in Turfton.
