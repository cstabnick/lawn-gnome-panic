Small, gentle, formal moles who live in Loamdeep. Gnomes call them root-eaters and Tall Ones blame them for every lump in a lawn. Both are wrong. They do not wear hats.

## What gnomes say

"Moles eat the roots. Everyone knows it. Put a spade by the hole and be done."

"There is a mole under every bad lawn. That is not a thing I would say if I had been to a good one."

## The truth

The Burrowfolk eat grubs, and the grubs eat the roots; their tunnels keep a lawn aerated and a garden alive. They are shy, say "if it pleases you" and mean it, and almost nothing the surface says about them is true. `dj-moleman` is one of them by birth and lives apart in his own burrow under Turfton; the family loves him and wishes he would visit. They say their great-greats came "from under the Fence, running", which may or may not tie them to the Fescue `mole-clans`. Moles wear no hats, except Moleman's, on his antenna.

They are five families, not one people: the Tumblesoils (who keep Loamdeep), the Tamps (roots), the Delves (lamps), the Sifts (post) and the Crumbs (grubs, supper and digging), with a sixth hall, Under-Chorus, that no family owns (`loamdeep-clans`).

## In the games

In act IV the Burrowfolk join the coalition: in `m60-the-moles-dig` Old Mother Tumblesoil sends diggers (Nubbin leads them) under Fort Sandpit's wall to open the armory from below, and in `m64-all-hats` the Burrowfolk's brown tent is one of the seven round the long table at the overlook camp (W16). They arrive having said "we dig where we are asked", and been asked.

E17 builds their halls and jobs (`loamdeep`, `mole-jobs`). Met in quests 24 (`m24-the-burrow-door`) and 49 (`m49-loamdeep`); sheltered in 48 (`m48-the-groundskeepers-secret`); diggers in 60 (`m60-the-moles-dig`). W13 (after quest 49) puts moles in Turfton.
