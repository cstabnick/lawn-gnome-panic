Turfton's underground street-racing and chop-shop crew, living for high-RPM Zero-Turns, custom paint tints, and midnight drift derbies in Frostbite Fells.

## Story: Two-Stroke Brotherhood

Born in downtown alleys and bonded by grease-stained beards, The Carburettors keep Turfton's roads wild against Vesper Thorne's quiet electric carts. They run illicit time trials along the Ring Road, tune Razor-Back engines until the exhaust glows cherry-red, and treat a clean getaway from 3-Hat Watch as a rite of passage.

Even after a bribed lieutenant betrayed Side-Valve Salvage in Chapter 3, the loyal core rallied on the Frozen Birdbath Basin to help rebuild the crew.
