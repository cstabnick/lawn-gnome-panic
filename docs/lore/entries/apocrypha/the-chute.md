A tin slide from the Roost to the ground: the Underspanners' answer to the question "and how do I get down?", asked by every gnome who has ever ridden the Updraft. It ends with a clang.

## What gnomes say

"Up is a lift. Down is a slide. Neither is approved."

"It is faster than the stairs, and the stairs do not exist."

## The truth

The Chute leaves the south-east corner of the Roost deck at (583, -34), six metres above the ground, and lands at (588, -30), just beside the workshop door. It stays inside the site: nothing the Underspanners build may go under the Span's deck, which begins at x 605.5. The clang is the tin. Nettle says the clang is also the signature.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: an interact spot on the Roost deck ("Slide down the Chute"); the only way down for a gnome who went up by the Updraft. Riding the Updraft and then the Chute is the whole of `sq-underspan-verticality`.

## Loose threads

Whether the Chute was built to be a way down or the Updraft was built to need one.
