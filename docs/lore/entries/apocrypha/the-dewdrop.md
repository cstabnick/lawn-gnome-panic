A clear rolling ball built by the Underspanners. The gnome rolls it from inside, hamster style, strapped to a little
seat-and-harness frame that stays the right way up while everything else does not. Hogweed Sprocket built it under the
Unfinished Span from a clear panel off the Greenhouse Strip, a coil spring from the Compost Heap furnace and a brass
seal-ring from his own scrap heap.

## What gnomes say

"The Dewdrop goes where you run. It stops where you stop. It does not stop where you'd like."

"It's clear. That's the point. You see everything and so does it."

## The truth

The first one got away with nobody in it, rolled down the Skyway verge and stopped against the canal bank. Hogweed says
nobody was inside it on purpose. It is slow to turn and bounces off kerbs; the HOA has no form for it, which Hogweed
counts as a licence. Every Dewdrop comes with a handwritten ownership form, underlined (R-226).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the vehicle kind `bubble-mobile`, unlocked by Hogweed's three jobs (`sq-dewdrop-1` The Rolling
Prototype, `sq-dewdrop-2` Hogweed Rolls Out, `sq-dewdrop-3` Dewdrop). Parked by Hogweed's workshop afterwards; a
replacement costs 400 Acorns there and nowhere else. "Roll With It" (the Bubble Visor) and "Hamster Hour" (a minute
inside without climbing out). A prototype rolls past in #10 `m10-under-the-span` once E20 builds it.
