A clear rolling ball built by the Underspanners. The gnome runs inside it like a hamster; the ball goes wherever the gnome's feet take it.

## What gnomes say

"The Dewdrop goes where you run. It stops where you stop. It does not stop where you'd like."

"It's clear. That's the point. You see everything and so does it."

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). A prototype rolls past in #10 `m10-under-the-span`. The drivable bubble vehicle is built by epic E18. Made in the Underspan.
