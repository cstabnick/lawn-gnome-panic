Thorne Lawn & Order's tower over the street, built from two giant flower pots side by side, with the Double Pot Tunnel running beneath. It is the Even-Estates sales floor and, later, the HOA lockup. The roof can be walked.

## What gnomes say

"Two pots, one hand on the lever. Thorne never builds anything that grows."

"They say there's a penthouse in the top pot. They say a lot of things in the tunnel."

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). The mega block at (90,-90) and (150,-90) with its tunnel, built by epic E01. #21 `m21-penthouse-viewing` (the viewing is held on a beige roof garden on the tunnel slab, R-240; it tops out, W05: crane gone, banner, sales floor lit); #62 `m62-cams-cell` (the HOA lockup, entered through the tunnel; see `hoa-lockup`).

Walk-in (R-170, `docs/lore/rulings.md#r-170`): the Thorne Even-Estates sales floor sits inside the Double Pot, entered by the door in the Double Pot Tunnel. The roof is the Thorne Helipad (R-171, `docs/lore/rulings.md#r-171`), reached by copter or by the sales floor's lift; gnomes do not climb.

## Everyday

- Sales floor (Thorne Even-Estates): a price board of three house types, each "Even", "Evener" and "Evenest", which are the same house with a different number on the door. Viewings by appointment, shoes in a bag, hats on a peg.
- Notices: "NO HEIGHT ABOVE THE BEIGE LINE" on the lift door; "VIEWINGS ARE NOT OFFERS. OFFERS ARE NOT VIEWINGS" at the desk; the tunnel watch's rota, pinned at the tunnel mouth and always one name short.
- Habit: the tunnel watch hums the same four bars every shift change, and the echo hums the first two back.
- Saying: "Two pots, one lever" (someone is always in charge, and it is never the person you spoke to).
- Small history: the tower topped out a day early, which is the only recorded time anything of Thorne's was early. The banner announcing it was up before the paint on the pots had dried.
