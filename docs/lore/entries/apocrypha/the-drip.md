The overflow pool below Hosepipe Pond, fed by a leaking hosepipe, with a giant stone birdbath in the middle: the water carry of hole 6.

## What gnomes say

"The Drip is not a hazard. It is the pond thinking out loud."

"The birds bathe in the birdbath and watch you play. They are not impressed."

## The truth

The hosepipe Thrift has been meaning to fix for three seasons leaks into the low ground below Hosepipe Pond, and the Drip is what collected. The pond mower does not go here: a ball in the Drip costs a stroke, as in the pond (R-195, R-218).

## In the games

Built (batch 3, E32): a pool about 14 m in radius at (-1248, 934), with the giant birdbath in the middle. Hole 6, The Drip (par 3), carries it from (-1282, 932) to the green at (-1212, 936).
