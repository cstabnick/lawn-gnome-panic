The cook's shop on the Pebblewick square, where prickly pear pie comes out of the oven.

## What gnomes say

"Prickly pear pie, fresh from the oven. The oven is the sun."

## The truth

Marigold Skillet bakes in the open yard behind the shop, and only lights a fire on the days the sun is not hot enough.

## In the games

On the Pebblewick square, facing Twine's General. Marigold Skillet keeps the counter.

## Loose threads

Whether the pie has ever been baked in a real oven.
