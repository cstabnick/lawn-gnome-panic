Vesper Thorne's secret master plan to level Turfton's messy grid and four surrounding biomes into a silent, regulation-height putting green.

## History & Accounts

Discovered on rolled blueprints inside the Glaze & Tax Office vault, Project Even Cut united sworn enemies—The Carburettors, The Unmowed, Granny Gnertrude, and Commander Diggs' Spade Guard—in a desperate air-and-deck assault to jam the Grand Combine before it reached downtown.
