The Grand Combine's guidance: a single level line, computed from the stars at the Anthill Observatory, that tells the machine where to cut. Everything on one side of it is to be one height. It runs through Turfton's meridian, and through the Countess Brassknock's first vine.

## What gnomes say

"The Combine does not steer. It follows a line. The line was drawn by someone who has never had to live on it."

"A level line is a thing you can only draw from a very long way off."

## The truth

Of the Combine's four parts (deck, guidance, power, money) this is the guidance, the one that makes the machine cut straight (R-167). It was computed in the dome of the Anthill, using Professor Betony Sextant's lens, which Dean Lovage Inkwell took under an HOA work order, chalked "B. Sextant" on its ring (R-233). Betony's survey of a level line was meant for the stars; Vesper's people use it to cut the world to one height. The Line runs from the Anthill to Turfton and enters it on the meridian, where the Combine turns onto it at dawn in `m69-the-combine-wakes`; in Trellisdown it crosses the Countess's vineyard, which is why she gives up her first vine and the key to the shield grates (`m65-the-countess-relents`). Betony calls with the line's last coordinate in the finale (`ch5-even-cut-p2`).

## In the games

Planned in rows 45, 46 (computed, Rootwell), 65 (the Countess), 69 (the meridian) and 70 (the finale). No world object marks it; it is the route the Combine takes.
