The one river that links Turfton's wild edges. It starts in the Frostbite Fells, runs west into Loch Moss, drops through Mossy Wood and ends in the Puddle Sea to the south. Each stretch has its own name and its own garden reading (see [rulings.md](../../rulings.md) R-048).

## What gnomes say

"The Everspring never stops. Fells gnomes say it has never once run dry, and nobody in Turfton has asked them how they know."

## The stretches

Named from the source down. The Tall-One reading is a hint for the writer only; a gnome never gives it.

- **The Source.** The thaw outlet of the Frozen Birdbath Basin, high in the Fells. Fells gnomes call the whole river the Everspring after it. *Tall-One hint: the birdbath overflowing, fed by a hose left running up by the house.*
- **The Upper River.** A fast, rocky stream running west through Frostbite Fells into Loch Moss. *Hint: the garden's runoff swale.*
- **Loch Moss.** A still highland loch in the north-west glen, where the stream slows. *Hint: the overgrown ornamental pond in the back corner, with a pond-liner rim and a tipped stone heron.*
- **The Ravine.** The overflow from the loch drops into Mossy Wood's ravine and runs south-east into Turfton's south-west corner. *Hint: the pond's overflow trench through the shrubbery bed.*
- **The Canal.** Past Turfton's south-west, the water runs into the Southern Drainage Canal (see [southern-drainage-canal](southern-drainage-canal.md)). *Hint: the concrete storm gutter along the patio.*
- **The Farm River.** Cloverfield's river, running south-east down to the coast. *Hint: the vegetable patch's irrigation channel.*
- **The Puddle Sea.** Where the canal and the farm river end, in the south. See [the-puddle-sea](the-puddle-sea.md).

Every stretch runs downhill from the house in the north to the Puddle Sea in the south (R-048).

## Loose threads

Where the Everspring's water comes from in the first place. The Fells gnomes will not say, and the house that the water runs down from is never shown.
