A ruin built on purpose in the Jardin's north-west corner: a post booth, two stretches of ivy wall and a statue with no head. A plaque reads "FOLLY (Romantic Ruin). Built last Tuesday. Ruined Wednesday."

## What gnomes say

"It is the only ruin in Pardonlin that was finished on time."

"The statue lost its head in the spring. It was never meant to have one."

## The truth

The folly stands at about (-1718, -698), a deliberate ruin laid out by Aurèle Clippe's committee in a corner where nobody will mistake it for a building. The ivy is real; the age is not. The headless statue is the Continentals' idea of a monument to the unmown, which is to say, to the unfinished.

Gnome Theft Auto: a vignette in the Jardin (`gd-english-folly`), with the plaque; no quest.
