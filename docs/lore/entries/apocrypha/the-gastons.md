Gaston Plonque and the other Gaston, two boules players on the Grand Platz who have been measuring the same throw for years.

## What gnomes say

"The Gastons are measuring. They will be measuring after you have gone to bed."

"Which Gaston won? That is the thing: someone has to measure."

## The truth

Both are called Gaston and both are right. The throw they are measuring was close, and it has been close ever since. They are the best of friends and will not speak to each other until it is settled, which is how they have kept the friendship.

## In the games

On the boules pitch of the Grand Platz. They make small remarks about the player's footwork and refuse to move for it.

## Loose threads

Which of them threw it. They have each forgotten.
