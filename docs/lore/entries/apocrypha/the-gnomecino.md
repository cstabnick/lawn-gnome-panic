Turfton's neon-lit downtown casino and high-roller lounge on the Greenhouse Strip, guarded at the velvet rope by Big Lou and glowing Acorn-Cola signs.

## Inside & Stories

Inside The Gnomecino, blackjack tables run twenty-four hours a day, pool cues clack in the back room, and off-duty Hat Watch officers pretend not to recognize wanted street racers across the felt. Big Lou enforces one iron rule at the door: nobody enters wearing a wet hat, no matter how many acorns they're carrying.

The VIP escalator currently has two scuffed tire tracks down the brass rail courtesy of Vinnie "Turbo-Sod" Delacroix.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Neon marquee slogan: *"LEAVE YOUR MOWER AT THE KERB. LEAVE YOUR ACORNS WITH US."*

## Everyday

- Chip prices at the cage: a red chip is 5 Acorns, a black one 25, a gold one 100. A chip dropped in the fountain by the door is handed back "as found", at a discount.
- House rules posted at the velvet rope: no mowers on the felt, no counting cards out loud, no counting cards in a rhyme (and Big Lou's one iron rule about hats).
- Menu in the back room: dandelion tea, a plate of "high-roller pretzels" (the same pretzels), Acorn-Cola in a glass with the cap on the side.
- Habit: the dealers shuffle in time with the neon sign's flicker, and a losing streak is blamed on the sign.
- Saying: "The house always wins; the Gnomecino just has more of them."
- Small history: the neon sign's second N once failed for a week and the place was open all week as "THE GNOMECIO". It was the best week of the year.

## Loose threads

Who actually owns the casino's deed—rumors point to a quiet partnership between Don Compost and a retired bowler.

Mayor Barnaby "Two-Coat" Mulch is the VIP on the night of the towel embargo, arriving in a fresh crimson coat. A VIP's show-car Zero-Turn (white pearl paint, whitewall tyres) is parked downtown at the west end, and it has to reach the VIP entrance without a smudge.
