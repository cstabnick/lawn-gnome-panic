Vesper Thorne's skyscraper-sized, autonomous harvester powered by a Fescue Golden Acorn, designed to scalp the entire 1200×1200 map into a silent putting green.

## Story: The Rolling Monolith

Patterned secretly after Fescue's ancient Mower and armored with plates welded at the Compost Heap, the Grand Combine boasts forty-foot PA horns, three rolling deck tiers guarded by Porcelain Guards, and Vesper's gilded hydraulic Lawn-Throne on the bridge. It met its end when the protagonist jammed a Fescue Stopping Stone into its intake—tipping it sideways into the Southern Drainage Canal.
