Vesper Thorne's skyscraper-sized, autonomous harvester powered by a Fescue Golden Acorn, designed to scalp the entire 1200×1200 map into a silent putting green.

## Story: The Rolling Monolith

Patterned secretly after Fescue's ancient Mower and armored with plates welded at the Compost Heap, the Grand Combine boasts forty-foot PA horns, three rolling deck tiers guarded by Porcelain Guards, and Vesper's gilded hydraulic Lawn-Throne on the bridge. It met its end when the protagonist jammed a Fescue Stopping Stone into its intake—tipping it sideways into the Southern Drainage Canal.

## In the story (act IV)

Built in four parts at the edges (`the-grand-combine-parts`): the cutting deck (tested on Hedgeworth Links), the guidance (`the-even-line`), the power (Pinwheel Ridge) and the money (the Countess Brassknock), with the Gilded Lawn-Throne on the deck (`gilded-lawn-throne`) and shield grates the Countess's key opens (`the-shield-grates`). It wakes at dawn on the longest day in `m69-the-combine-wakes` and turns onto the meridian; the coalition (`coalition-of-all-hats`) answers with a beacon on the Bobble (`hatbrim-beacon`) and the finale in `ch5-even-cut`: air support, a parachute drop onto the deck, the throne, and the jam. Vesper is stopped, not shattered.
