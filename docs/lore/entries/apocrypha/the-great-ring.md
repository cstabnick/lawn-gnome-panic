The fairy ring of toadstools round a clearing in the heart of Capwood, near (150, -1125), 14 m across. The caps glow faintly after dark, and brightest here. The Warden allows looking and nothing else.

## What gnomes say

"Look as long as you like. Looking is Section Eleven. Everything else is Section Nine."
"It is a ring. It is round. Do not ask what it is round of."

## The truth

The ring is the oldest part of the forest and sits above the thickest part of the buried Tall-One woodpile, which is the likeliest reason it glows. The clearing in the middle is bare, with no moss, no mushroom and no footprint. Gnomes who have stepped in it tell the same story: nothing happened, and they were very glad to have left.

## In the games

Built (E14): a ring at (150, -1125) of radius 14 in src/capwood/spots.ts, with the glow brightest here at night (R-192). A tripod and a worn folding stool are aimed at it from (171, -1105), the Photographer's Spot of the Cap Count. A plaque reads "THE GREAT RING. Looking is allowed (Section Eleven)." The Capwood Run passes it.

## Everyday

- Notices: the plaque above, and nothing else. The Warden thinks one sign is enough.
- Habit: visitors walk the ring clockwise once, at dusk, without going in. Nobody can say who began it.
- A photographer has sat here many nights and has not yet got the picture. He says it is the caps, who will not hold still.
- Local custom: a pine-cone is left on the nearest root as a thank-you for the quiet. The Warden counts them.
