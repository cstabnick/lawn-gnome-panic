The night a heap of giant Tall-One things landed on the alkali pan: a sneaker, a cooler, a stack of books, a garden
hose, a frisbee, a rake, a ruler and a watering can. Nobody saw them fall.

## What gnomes say

"It was a Tuesday, and then the pan was full of stuff." The most common account. Nobody can say which Tuesday.

"Something was thrown away." Some gnomes say the Tall Ones threw it all out, and that the Speedway is a dump with
ambitions. Others say the things were lost on a picnic, and that someone is still looking for the cooler.

"It was a sign." The Hat Watch's Sergeant once said this on the record, then asked for the tape to be stopped.

## The truth

No gnome, Tall One or otherwise, was on the pan when the heap arrived. The things were there at dawn and nobody has
found a fall line, a crater or a dropped label. The contradictory theories are the point: the gnomes have each kept
the version that suits them.

The Carburettors raced round the heap first, before anyone had decided where the track went. Dandelion Fizz paid to
pave it.

## In the games

- The Speedway's history, quoted by Burdock Bellweather at the race desk (`quests/races.ts`, the Rookie Mow-Down intro).
- The heap is the circuit's grandstands, jumps and pit wall in `raceTrack/track.ts`.

## Loose threads

Where the Tall Ones went, and whether they noticed the cooler was gone. The contradictory accounts stay contradictory on
purpose, and the Hat Watch's version stays on tape.
