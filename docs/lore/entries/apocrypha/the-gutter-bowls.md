The public skate park in Downtown Turfton, made from old Tall-One roof gutters: long half-pipes, curved bowls and a few downspouts to jump.

## What gnomes say

"It's only a gutter. It's the best gutter in the yard."

"If it rains the bowls fill. Fennel skates the water."

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). #9 `m09-where-asphalt-sleeps`: Asphalt the cat is found at the Bowls; skate one line to get the bottlecap back. Fennel Gripwax lives under its deck. The park stands at about (-150,-90); epic E01 builds it.

Whether the gutters came off one roof or several.
