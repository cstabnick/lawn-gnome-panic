A cracked gnome that grey moss has grown into. It gets up again, and it walks toward warmth, noise and other gnomes.

## What they are

- They are gnomes. The moss wants warm, wet clay to grow in, and it grows through the cracks.
- They lose their hats first. A gnome without a hat is hiding something, and the Hatless have nothing left to hide.
- They are slow and they do not think. They come in numbers, and they are not evil: every pest wants something.
- They cannot climb. A grave kerb stops them, and so does anything higher, so a single Hatless cannot reach a high place.
- They crumble over seasons, and in the end the moss takes the rest.

## The moss stages

1. **Exposed**: the grey moss shows at a crack, and the gnome still looks like a gnome under the hat.
2. **Rooting**: the moss has gone into the clay, the face is grey, and the gnome stands up without its hat.
3. **Spreading**: the moss runs down the coat, and the gnome walks toward whatever it hears.
4. **Taking**: the moss covers the whole gnome. It no longer stands on its own, and what is left crumbles.

## Speech and gore

- Nothing gory happens. The Hatless crack and shatter, like any fired clay.
- Gnomes say "put down" for a Hatless, and "shattered" for a gnome. See the ruling "DayG speech".

## In the games

- The Hatless are the enemy of DayG: a horde that wanders, hears and hunts.
- A gnome that is shattered can rise again as a Hatless, with its gear.
