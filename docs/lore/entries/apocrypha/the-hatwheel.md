Pardonlin's great roundabout, centre of the east bank, between the Old Town and Teacup Row: a ring of gravel twenty metres in radius, round which Continental cyclists go once, twice and often three times before leaving by the exit they wanted. It is the only roundabout in the yard that has been told it is too small.

## What gnomes say

"Allow several laps. It is on the sign. It is also in the contract."

"The Hatwheel is not a junction. It is a mood."

## The truth

The Hatwheel is at (-1555, -30), ring radius 20 m; the Rue Haute and the Avenue to the Hatwheel meet at it. Continentals count a lap as "the polite minimum". The Hatwheel is one of the two roundabouts in town (the other is Place du Pot).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the road ring and the hub where Pardonlin's traffic and its bicycles are densest; a traffic-hat warden on loan from the Continentals directs it.

Whether anyone has ever left it by accident.
