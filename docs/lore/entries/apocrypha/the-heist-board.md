Tansy Clayfoot's plan, pinned to a plank board on two trestle legs at the overlook safehouse: a paper map of Fort Sandpit with red string between brass tacks and three photographs along the top. It is a map of a fort, a to-do list for a mad idea, and the first thing the crew sees when it comes down from the cold.

## What gnomes say

"It is a board with string on it. When she is standing in front of it, it is a plan."

"Do not touch the tacks. Do not touch the string. Tansy touches the string."

## The truth

It begins as the plan for the Whirligig ("air superiority means the Whirligig, and the Whirligig is in Fort Sandpit") and ends as a map of the whole story: after `m54-the-heist-board` it is a pinned map in the safehouse that shows the quest line. The three photographs are the north wall, the main gate and the pad, taken from the overlook rail by the player.

## In the games

Prefab `heist-board`; the safehouse stage `dust-safehouse` (centre (276, 44), r 18). `m54-the-heist-board` (the scouting) and the reward "the Heist Board" (the quest-line map).
