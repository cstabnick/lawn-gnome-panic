A voice in the ceiling of the Annex who runs the interview, counts what the player collects, and praises it. He is
never seen. His name drifts each floor: The Interviewer, The Interviewer (probably), The Narrator, You, possibly, and
back to The Interviewer at the end.

## What gnomes say

"I used to have a script. It's in a filing cabinet. I collected it."

"That's not me. That's the economy." (when the music speeds up on Floor 2½)

"...The game was the collecting. I'm sorry. I thought you knew."

## In the games

- `games/nothing-left-to-collect`: the speaker of most lines in every chapter. Chapter 1 has him count acorns with growing
  praise; in Chapter 3 he tires; in Chapter 4 he announces the Pointlessness. The credits list him as "The Interviewer
  as the Ceiling".

## Loose threads

Whether he exists (R-040). Whether he is the player: his label on Floor ∞ is "You, possibly".
