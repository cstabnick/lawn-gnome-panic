The formal garden north of Pardonlin: a box-hedge parterre, clipped with nail scissors, that no gnome is allowed to stand in.

## What gnomes say

"The Jardin is looked at from the path. The path is the correct place to be moved along from."

"Every leaf is the same height. If one is not, Aurèle will know by tea."

## The truth

The parterre is a pattern, and the pattern is the point. The head gardener clips it with nail scissors, one blade at a time, which is why it takes all year and why it is never quite done. It is the HOA's Even Cut done by someone who has read about it, and it will not be mowed.

## In the games

North of town, around x -1600, z -620 (a screenshot spot of the parterre). Aurèle Clippe works here. Gravel walks run out of the parterre's gaps in the four directions: the main axis is a double avenue of pleached limes (clipped flat on stilts) between urns of geraniums, a rill court with benches lies to the east, a round with a stone urn to the west. Hubert Secateur clips the limes, under a sign asking that the pruners not be pruned.
