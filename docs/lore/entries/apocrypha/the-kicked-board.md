A fence board, loose at the bottom, on the neighbour's east fence at about (-1001, 640): the way from Tallgrass Commons into the garden. A gnome can push it aside and a mower can just about fit.

## What gnomes say

"Somebody kicked it once. Somebody has been kicking it ever since."

"There's a sign that says 'no'. It's been wrong for years."

## The truth

The board was loosened by a golf ball, not a foot; the name is only what the gnomes called it afterwards, because it sounded braver. Thrift Hosepipe has been propping it shut from the other side.

## In the games

Built (batch 3, E15): the loose board in the east fence, across the road from Tallgrass Commons. A vehicle at speed or a kick on foot breaks through it, and it stays broken. Breaking it earns "The Kicked Board" achievement. It is the only public way into Hedgeworth (R-195); Quest 29 (`m29-the-kicked-board`) has the player drive the fence line to it.

## Everyday

- **Notices:** Painted on the fence by the board: "Private course. If you can read this you are on the wrong side of it." A lost-ball bucket stands near: "Balls from over the fence. Help yourself. Don't." An arrow post points the way: "This way to the Kicked Board".
- **Sayings and habits:** "Go through the board, not over the fence." Gnomes passing from Tallgrass Commons give it a polite tap with the toe, for luck, and are not counted as having kicked it. Thrift props it shut each dusk, and each dawn someone has unpropped it.
- **Small history:** The first gnome to push through it was a golf ball. The first gnome to push through it on purpose took a picnic.
