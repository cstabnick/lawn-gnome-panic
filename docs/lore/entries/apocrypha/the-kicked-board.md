A fence board, loose at the bottom, at about (-895, 640): the way from Tallgrass Commons into the neighbour's garden. A gnome can push it aside and a mower can just about fit.

## What gnomes say

"Somebody kicked it once. Somebody has been kicking it ever since."

"There's a sign that says 'no'. It's been wrong for years."

## The truth

The board was loosened by a golf ball, not a foot; the name is only what the gnomes called it afterwards, because it sounded braver. Thrift Hosepipe has been propping it shut from the other side.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). The breach marker on the fence line. Quest 29 (`m29-the-kicked-board`) has the player drive the fence line to it; it is the first reach into Hedgeworth.

## Loose threads

Whether the Tall One in the neighbouring garden knows there is a hole in the fence.
