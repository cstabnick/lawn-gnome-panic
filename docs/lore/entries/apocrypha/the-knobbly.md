The Knobbly Dirt-Mower is a push-mower bike rebuilt for dirt by Rutbottom's mechanics: knobbly tyres, long forks and a number plate. It is sold at the dealer, and parked at the Dirt Bowl gate for anyone who signs Clary's clipboard. Nobody checks the clipboard.

## In the games

Built (E14): the Knobbly is the bike for the side quest "Rutbottom: Knobbly Heat" (`race-rutbottom-knobbly`), which Marshal Clary Mudguard gives once the player has run the impound (row 42).
