Driftwood's tube and ski hut on the beach: tubes to ride, skis to hire, and the only boat for gnomes who have none.

## What gnomes say

"Eight acorns for a tube. Eleven for skis. Driftwood does not give refunds, only stories."
"If you fall off the wake ramp, the Last Tube takes no responsibility, but it does come and look."

## The truth

The hut stands on the sand at (150, 1150), with the tube rack at (150, 1172). Tubes cost 8 acorns and skis 11. The water-skiing course is a slalom of six buoy gates heading east along z 1312 from x -380 to x -30, then a floating wake ramp at (40, 1312); the tow boat waits at the launch (150, 1224). Driftwood's tow boat takes anyone who pays, and tows gnomes with no boat of their own out onto the Saltwater.

## In the games

The hut stands at (150, 1150) with its door to the sea; the price board, the refunds note and the ski rack (at (150, 1172)) face the sea too, where customers come back from the water, and the tow boat waits at the launch (150, 1224) (R-246). Driftwood's side quest is "The Buoy Run" (`coast-boardwalk-tubing`, R-245). The hut is the rental point for the tube run and the water-skiing slalom (E11, row 43 `m43-saltbucket-boardwalk` and the side quest `coast-boardwalk-tubing`). The tow boat is the free ride for gnomes without a boat.

## Everyday

- Price board: tube 8 Acorns; skis 11; "wake ramp: yours to find". Under it: "Refunds: stories only."
- Price board: its back reads "Driftwood tows. Driftwood is not sorry."
- Notices: "NO TUBES IN THE BOWL" (back: "The bowl is east. We checked."); "IF YOU LOSE YOUR HAT, I'LL LOOK FIRST"; the rack's tags are written on bits of driftwood, with an owner and a date.
- House rule: "I tow. You hold on."
- Habit: Driftwood raises one hand to every boat that goes past. They are rarely his.
- Saying: "The last tube out" for a late decision that works.
