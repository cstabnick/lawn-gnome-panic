The keeper of the Long Shelf, Rootwell's library: a hushed, exact gnome who says the quiet part is over, that everything is overdue and that the ants may borrow anything and return it in sugar.

## What gnomes say

"Shh. That was the quiet part."

"Fines are paid in silence. You owe me four minutes."

## The truth

She has no other name on the staff list; the building has a name and she is the other half of it. She has never been seen leaving. She says the building itself is overdue, and nobody has been able to find who it was borrowed from.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the Long Shelf interior (E13); the "Ask the Librarian" spot near the door cycles four lines (Shh, Overdue, The ants, Fines).

## Everyday

**Shop.** The Long Shelf has a returns slot ("RETURNS (SINCE 1903)") and a shelf "RESERVED FOR LANDLORDS", which the ants use.

**Notices.** The sign over the desk: "SILENCE (EXCEPT THE ANTS)." The lintel: "THE LONG SHELF. EST. BEFORE THE ALPHABET."

**Habits.**
- Fines are paid in silence, a minute for each day overdue. The ledger is in minutes, and the Librarian is owed several years.
- The ants may borrow anything. They return it in sugar, and she logs the sugar under "Returns".
- Opening hours: "When it is quiet. If it is not quiet, it is the quiet part, and that is over."
