Pardonlin's allotments on the east edge: a patchwork of little plots, sheds and scarecrows, and the front line of the marrow war.

## What gnomes say

"The Little Gardens are very small and very serious."

"Every shed has a lock, a flag and a grievance."

## The truth

Every plot is the size of a doormat and defended like a country. The allotment holders compete for the largest marrow, and the contest has gone on so long that the marrows are now cited in other disputes. It is the only place in Pardonlin where raised voices are allowed.

## In the games

East of town, near x -1451, z 640 (a screenshot spot). Wilhelmina Marrow, the champion, has the biggest plot.

## Loose threads

Who started the marrow war. Both sides say the other did.
