The great waist-high grass of the Unmown, east of Reedmere and north of Meadow Hide: a meadow no mower has entered in years, kept in good order by nothing in particular. It has the Last Dandelion in it, and the things the grass has taken.

## What gnomes say

"Do not count the grass. Meadow Hide tried. They ran out of numbers."

"The Long Meadow is not lost. It is just not found yet, and it likes it that way."

## The truth

The meadow lies between about z -1760 and -1520 (north of Meadow Hide at (-560, -1490)), with the Last Dandelion in its ring of stones at (-600, -1660). The grass is waist high; wildflowers and tall grass fill it and nothing is solid. In it, as story vignettes: the HOA survey that never came back (a tripod and tape swallowed by grass, a hat on top and a clipboard), the mower that tried (a rusting ride-on with a one-metre stripe behind it and tall grass in front), and the picnic the ants won, at its edge by the trail (a blanket, a tipped basket, crumbs into the grass). The rangers walk it on a loop trail and sign nothing.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E31): the Unmown's meadow region (`unmown-meadow`, with `unmown/vignettes.ts` and the Last Dandelion); Meadow Hide looks north over it. The checklist's skylark, cuckoo and hare are found here.
