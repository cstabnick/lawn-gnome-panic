The passage from Under-Chorus Hall down to DJ Moleman's own burrow under Turfton. It runs a long way, and nobody agrees on how long.

## What gnomes say

"Three minutes. A week. It depends who is carrying the lamp."

## The truth

It is the length it needs to be, which for a mole who likes to be alone is quite long. The floor is smooth from use. Moleman comes up it when asked nicely and, once, without being asked.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: a stair on the west side of Under-Chorus Hall down to the burrow. Moleman walks up it when the lamps are lit.
