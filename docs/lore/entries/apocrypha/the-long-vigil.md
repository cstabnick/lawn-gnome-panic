The Flock's night vigil, stood on one leg before the Standing Lady from dusk to the first bird. It is not a hard rule, only a long one.

## What gnomes say

"Nobody sits at the Long Vigil. That is the point of it."

## The truth

A gnome who sits down at it has left the Flock. Everyone knows it and nobody minds. They are forgiven with a cup of reed tea, handed over with great kindness, and are still welcome to visit.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the end of `sq-flock-vigil`. Sitting down earns "Sat Down" (`gta-sat-down`; the reed tea cup). Finishing Errands for the Flock (`sq-flock-errands`) earns "Birds of a Feather" (`gta-birds-of-a-feather`).
