The wrought-iron gate in the east fence of Hedgeworth, beside the cart path from the road. It is always locked, and its plaque says MEMBERS ONLY.

## What gnomes say

"Nobody has ever said who the members are."

"It is a very handsome gate for a thing that does not open."

## The truth

The fence was built first and the gate was hung afterwards, to look like a way in. The key went missing before either was finished.

## In the games

Built (batch 3, E15): a locked wrought-iron gate at about (-1001, 500) in the east fence, 7 m wide, with the cart path running in from it toward the clubhouse. It is not a way in; the Kicked Board is the only public way (R-195).
