About every three seasons the ground shakes in one district for one night, and in the morning the grass is cut flat.

## What happens

The ground shakes as if a giant machine is running somewhere. In one district, in one night, the grass is cut flat. For a season the cover is gone, the herds scatter, and grave goods are strewn across the paths. Nobody sees it happen: no gnome has ever seen the machine, and no gnome has ever seen the one who runs it (core fact 3).

## What gnomes say

Every faction has its own idea of which district is next. The Shelf Wrights keep a tally on the shed wall, the Willow Folk watch the pond for the ripple, and nobody agrees. The Mowing is the one thing the factions all treat with the same care, because whoever guesses the district right gets the first pick of the scattered goods.

## Loose threads

Which district is next. Whether the schedule is a pattern or a seeded accident, and whether anyone has ever been right twice in a row.
