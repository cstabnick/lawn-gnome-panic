Don Compost's silent, members-only syndicate headquarters at `x: -30, z: -30`, with its heavy timber door facing `x = 0`.

## Inside & Stories

From the sidewalk, The Mulch Club looks like an ordinary dark-stained garden shed with drawn curtains. Inside, velvet-lined booths smell of damp cedar and expensive cigar-moss. In the back room, Don Compost sits on a raised dais flanked by two pistol-packing bodyguards, sliding red-crossed wanted posters across the felt without uttering a syllable.

Rookies who try to make small talk in the back room are politely escorted to the kerb by the collar.

## Ledger & Graffiti

A brass plaque by the coat rack reads: *"NO SIRENS. NO WET HATS. NO TALKING."*

## In the games

The lobby is walk-in (R-170, `docs/lore/rulings.md#r-170`), entered by the members' side door on the south face; the front door on the east face still leads to Don Compost's back room for quests. Inside are the velvet booths, the cloakroom with its brass plaque, a hat-check gnome, a silent bodyguard at the back-room door, and a card game that stops the moment somebody speaks.
