The ruin of the blasting hut beside the Spine Cut: two walls and a stack of crates, with a site barrier that reads "DANGER: OLD BANGS". It is accurate, and it is also what the neighbours said at the time.

## What gnomes say

"They blasted the Spine for the Pinwheel Ridge works. The Old Bangs is where they kept the bangs."

"The barrier says OLD BANGS. I walked round it. I advise you to walk round it."

## The truth

The hut stood where the cut began and was left when the Pinwheel Ridge works ended, with its barrel and crates and the barrier the surveyor put round it. The barrier is a joke the HOA has never found funny; the crates are empty.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): a roadside vignette at the Spine Cut (`spine-blasting-hut`).
