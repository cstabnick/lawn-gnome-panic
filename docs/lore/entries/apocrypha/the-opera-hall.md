The great hall of the Left Bank, where Maestro Ottavio Lento rehearses, Radio Adagio broadcasts and the Fifth Symphony is performed very slowly (Act 1 of 3, of 3). Its stage door has a notice asking you to queue in the other direction.

## What gnomes say

"They are playing the Fifth tonight. They have been playing it since Tuesday."

"Interval snacks: crackers (loud), crackers (quiet). The quiet ones are the expensive ones."

## The truth

The Hall's marquee reads "THE FIFTH SYMPHONY, very slowly" and, for Sunday, "the Overture to an Overture, bring a cushion and a day". The foyer bar sells crackers; the stage door is at (-1667, -112); an artist is at lunch behind it ("back some time before the light changes"). The Radio Adagio studio is on the Boulevard side ("ON AIR (slowly)"), so the station and the orchestra share a wall and, nobody says, a score.

Gnome Theft Auto: the Opera Hall forecourt and stage door on the Left Bank, the home of Radio Adagio (96.1) and Ottavio Lento.
