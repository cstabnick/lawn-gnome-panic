The gnome the player is: a hired hand on Tansy's crew, borrower of flat 3B and the driver everyone leans on, freshly fired from the Kiln with a debt and no name anyone has used yet.

## What gnomes say
"Your bikes?" "Why me?" The straight man's two lines, as Pip reports them to anyone who will listen.

"New kiln, new debt, same old driver." (Dot, at the Exchange, to nobody.)

## The truth
The player is the audience's seat, so the lore says little on purpose. Facts that hold: they arrive fresh out of the Kiln owing the Kiln-Debt to Clay-Mart (later held as a ledger by the First Kiln Bank, loan officer Parsley Inkpot), and take a 1,500-Acorn loan on flat 3B, Saucer Court (R-166); the neighbour at 3A, Hyssop Doormat, logs their stairwell comings and goings. They belong to no faction until the coalition. They drive for Pip and Tansy as family, and every ally has a mention of them in a last scene.

Voice (for lines, not for the game's voice engine): the straight man. Flat, two to six words, mostly questions, at most one line in three of any exchange, never tops a quip.

Look: always the player's own gnome from `@lawn/gnome`, never a cast preset. They nod, shrug and cross their arms at Vesper, and let others move round them. Vehicle: whatever they steal; they keep the dirt bike from #42 and get the Crop Duster and the Whirligig in act IV.

Arc: errands for the debt, a flat of their own and a first reason to stay (I); the debt paid, Vesper's offer at the diner refused, the crew chosen over comfort (II); the driver who takes Pip and the crew to the edges and gathers four allies (III); the one every plan depends on (the drop, the tunnel, the convoy), then the hand on the jam and the city's choice (IV).
