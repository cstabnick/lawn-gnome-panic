The moment on Floor ∞ when everything, the furniture included, has been collected. The counters reach ∞ ("Infinity
and one. Infinity and two."), then everything stops: the music cuts and the counters empty to one line, "Collected:
everything. Left to collect: nothing."

## What gnomes say

"So. You're free. You are finally free to play the game."

"...The game was the collecting. I'm sorry. I thought you knew."

## In the games

- `games/final-collectathon`, Chapter 4 (Floor ∞): the turning point and the achievement The Point. Afterwards the
  lift only goes to L, and Shellshore shows in the window.
