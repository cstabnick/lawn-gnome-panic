The big shallow water at the southern end of Turfton's map, where the Everspring ends, claimed by Baroness Brenda as the Sovereign Republic of New Puddle.

## What gnomes say

"Brenda says the Sea is hers and so is the tide. The tide has never been asked."

## The truth

The Sea is the low corner of the lawn where the runoff pools. Hint only: a gnome never names it that. The water arrives from the north by way of the canal and the farm river, and nothing leaves it.

## In the games

The southern end of the map, where the Southern Drainage Canal and Cloverfield's river both empty out. The Sovereign Republic's flags sit along the shore.

## Loose threads

Whether the Sea has an edge, and where the tide comes from when nothing runs into it fast enough.
