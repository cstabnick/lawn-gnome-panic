A neat stack of snow-plough marker poles by the Brim Gap Road, with a pink cone beside them and a notice from the Highways Sub-Committee: "For use when it snows. (Not yet used. Not yet snowed.)" They have been ready since the road was built. The snow has not yet been informed.

## What gnomes say

"The Highways Sub-Committee built the road to plough it. These are the proof that they meant to."

"The poles are ready. The snow is not. It is a stand-off."

## The truth

The poles lean in a pile at the roadside (six posts at an angle), with the cone beside. They exist for the HOA's one operational promise, to plough the Pass, which was written on the first plan and has never been tried because the snow lies on the Hatbrim above 32 m, not on the road. The notice is signed by the Sub-Committee.
