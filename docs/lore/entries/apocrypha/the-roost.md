A scaffold deck above the Underspan, high enough to see the Skyway and low enough to deny it. Chalk, hammocks, a kettle, and a fine view of HOA inspectors, who never look up.

## What gnomes say

"There is a deck above the Underspan. Do not go up. I went up. It was lovely."

## The truth

The Roost is the only way "up" in the Underspan, and it is not allowed. The way back down is the Chute, a tin slide that ends with a clang beside the workshop. It stays inside the site and never goes under the Span's deck.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the walkable deck over the Underspan, reached only by the Updraft. Quest `sq-underspan-scrap-run` is the scrap that goes up and down it.
