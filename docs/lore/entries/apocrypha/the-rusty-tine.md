The last pub before the Fells, on the north road out of Turfton: a two-storey brick pub with a pitched slate roof, a
crooked chimney and a hanging sign on a bracket by the door. The sign shows a rusted garden fork, three tines and all.

## What gnomes say

"You don't drink at the Tine for the beer. You drink there so the beer knows you came." The Tine's regulars say the
fork is the only thing in town that has been properly mowed since it was put up.

## Inside & Stories

- Warm light in the front windows, which is the only warm light on the Chillyard side of the Fringe.
- Beer barrels stacked by the door, on a stand made from old tyres. Nobody has ever been sure whether the tyres came
  with the barrels or the other way round.
- The sign: a rusted garden fork painted on a board, which the landlord repaints every spring, and which rusts again
  every autumn, to the same shade.
- A yard out the back where the lorries turn round. The turning circle is just big enough for a snail coach if it
  reverses carefully.

## In the games

The Rusty Tine is a rough pub on the north edge of the Fringe, the last building on the road before the Fells. It is a
scenery building: the player can see it from the road and pass it, and it stands among the other Chillyard lots.

## Loose threads

Whether the fork is over the door because of the pub, or whether the pub was built to hold the fork up.
