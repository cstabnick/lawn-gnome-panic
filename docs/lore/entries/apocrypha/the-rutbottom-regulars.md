The riders who line up in the dirt at the Rutbottom Dirt Bowl and on the Capwood Run: three regulars of the bowl, and three Carburettors who run the rally. Quests: race-rutbottom-knobbly, sq-rutbottom-heats, race-capwood-open.

## The six

- Skid Thistlewaite: an old-timer who has raced Rutbottom since it was a puddle, and says it was a better puddle.
- Polly Pebbledash: young and fearless, and has never touched the brakes.
- Brakeless Bramble: has no brakes to touch. It is on his entry form.
- Gravel Gertie: a Carburettor who treats the Capwood Run as an argument and gravel as seasoning.
- Fenwick "Flat-Out" Fogg: a Carburettor who has never lifted, and calls a ford a wet corner.
- Spanner Moss: a Carburettor mechanic who built his hatchback and knows exactly where it will break.

Two-Ton Teasel Mudguard, Clary's older brother, rides the Heats with Polly and Bramble (R-186).

## What gnomes say

"Mind the berm. The berm remembers."
