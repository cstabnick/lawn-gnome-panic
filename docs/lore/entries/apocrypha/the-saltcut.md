A channel the tide dug through the dunes, joining the fresh Puddle Sea to the salt Saltwater. Boats go through it from the loch to the sea.

## What gnomes say

"Driftwood dug it with a teaspoon. Ask him and he will show you the teaspoon."
"The water goes salt at the bend. You can taste the line with your hat off."

## The truth

The tide did the digging over a very long time, and Driftwood was there for the end of it. The channel runs from the Puddle Sea's south shore, about (700, 880), through the dunes to the Saltwater, about (745, 1205). It is 14 m wide and passes under the south-band-east road bridge at (728, 1000).

## In the games

The Saltcut is the water between the Puddle Sea and the south shore band (E11). Boats pass through it; the road bridge at (728, 1000) carries traffic over it.

Whether the tide dug the Saltcut alone, and whether Driftwood's teaspoon is still in it (R-180).
