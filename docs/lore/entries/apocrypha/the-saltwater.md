The sea at the south of the map: real salt, real swell, and the far edge of anything a Turfton gnome has been told. Gnomes who have not seen it do not believe in it, and say "the Big Puddle" instead.

## What gnomes say

"The Big Puddle is a story told to frogs."
"It tastes of pennies. It moves when nothing is pushing it. I have decided not to think about it."

## The truth

It is a sea, in every way that matters to a gnome the size of a hat. Whether it is a Tall One's sea, or a bath or a basin or something that was there first, nobody who has crossed it has come back to say.

## In the games

The water of the south-shore band, reached from Saltbucket Shore. Used for rows 43 (tubing and water-skiing, new kinds `tow-ski` and `ride-passenger`, E11), 44 (boarding an HOA barge from a boat) and 64. Planned (batch 3).

E11 build (Saltbucket Shore): still water `water/saltwater` runs along the whole south edge, its surface at about y -8.9, with waves 1.6 times the loch's. The Saltcut ([the-saltcut](the-saltcut.md)) joins it to the Puddle Sea. A slalom of six buoy gates and a wake ramp sit off the boardwalk, and boat traffic (HOA barges, the Bathtub Queen) moves across it.
