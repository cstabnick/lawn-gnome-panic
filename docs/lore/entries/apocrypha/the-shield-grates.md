The outer grates that keep anyone from the Grand Combine's deck, and the brass key, as long as a gnome's arm, that the Countess Brassknock gives the crew to open them. The key is stamped with her family's crest and was made by the same hands as the Combine's money.

## What gnomes say

"The Combine has a lock. The Countess has the key. She gave it up for a vine. That is the whole of her price."

"It is a key as long as your arm. It does not fit your pocket. It fits the Combine."

## The truth

The Countess's money (the fourth part of the Combine) bought the shield grates as well, and the key is hers by contract. She trades it, for the first time in her life, for something that is not paid for: a vine, carried to safety (`m65-the-countess-relents`). The grates open in `ch5-even-cut-p2` ("Deck Drop"): the Countess calls to confirm the key works, and the player parachutes onto a moving beige deck.

## In the games

Prefab `shield-grate-key` (a brass key with an ornate bow and a Brassknock crest). Row 65 gives it; row 70 uses it.
