A Tall-One bicycle wheel stood on end and fitted with teacup gondolas: the ferris wheel of Saltbucket Boardwalk, and the tallest thing for miles. From the top you can see the sea and everything on it.

## What gnomes say

"It goes round. That is the whole of it, and it is wonderful."
"Marram lets you stay at the top if you ask nicely. Do not ask nicely when the barges are in."

## The truth

The wheel turns on its own hub and is pushed round by a gnome in a treadmill under the boardwalk who has never been on it. Marram says he prefers it that way.

## In the games

Ridden in row 43 `m43-saltbucket-boardwalk` as the new kind `ride-passenger` (E11) to spot HOA barges at sea. Built by E01 and the south-shore area epic: planned (batch 3).

It stands at (-260, 1100), its hub 21 m up and its rim 18 m in radius, boarded from a platform at (-260, 1106). Rideable as a passenger in E11: one turn every 96 seconds, 12 teacup gondolas, fare 3 acorns. Side quest `coast-spokewheel-gondola` ("The Wobbly Gondola"). A brass bell stands on the boarding platform at (-253.5, 1110), rung at boarding and at the end of a turn, with Marram beside it; once the quest is done the wheel is free to ride ("Ride the Spokewheel (free)": "Marram waves you through. Within reason.") (R-246).

## Everyday

- Fare board: 3 Acorns a turn, "hat on, hands in, wait for the bell". Under it, in smaller paint: "Children free. Gulls not."
- Notices: "DO NOT RISE FROM YOUR TEACUP"; "THE TOP IS FOR LOOKING. THE BOTTOM IS FOR PAYING"; "MARRAM'S WHEEL, MARRAM'S RULES".
- Habit: the bell is rung once at a full gondola and twice at a full wheel, and three times when Marram has spotted a barge.
- Saying: "Teacup-high" (cheerful, wobbly).
- Small history: one summer the wheel stuck at the top for a whole afternoon with Sal's candyfloss on board. The gulls arrived in numbers, and the story is told with the number getting larger.
