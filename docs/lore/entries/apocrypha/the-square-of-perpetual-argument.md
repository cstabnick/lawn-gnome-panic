A small square between the Avenue to the Hatwheel and the Rue Haute, where the benches face each other in pairs and a boules game has been disputed since before the benches. Nobody wins. That is what the square is for.

## What gnomes say

"The Square of Perpetual Argument has one rule: you may sit, but you may not agree."

"I went in to settle it. I came out with a position."

## The truth

Benches stand in facing pairs, the boules lie where the last measurement left them, and the committee that settles measurements never meets (a poster says so). A visitor who sits on either side is asked, in a low and pleasant voice, to take a view. It is the town's one open-air parliament and it has never passed a motion.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: a square in the Teacup Row and Quays district; boules and benches, no quest.

Whether the argument has a subject, and whether anyone has been told.
