A giant pink plastic lawn flamingo, tall as a barn door, on one thin leg at the middle of Wader's Rest. A Tall One put her in a garden and left; the Flock found her, and have not sat down since.

## What gnomes say

"She has stood on that leg longer than the Flock have had a rule against sitting. That is the argument for the rule."

"Pink is for the Lady only. She does not seem to mind who else is pink, to be fair."

## The truth

A moulded plastic bird on a metal spike, sun-faded at the beak, with a crack at the knee that the Flock call her "wise line". She is a lawn ornament. The Flock know it and say it does not matter.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). The centrepiece of Wader's Rest. In quest 51 (`m51-the-flock-stands`) she is the fragile cargo: the player drives her to safety on a flatbed while the Flock follow on one leg. E17: stands on her sand mound at Wader's Rest facing west (R-221); the shrine inside the big tent shows her in white.

## Loose threads

Whether she has ever moved on her own. The Flock say the beak has turned in the night. Nobody has watched.
