The Tall One's garden rill: a slow, green river running north to south through Pardonlin into the Basin. It is narrow enough to step over in places and wide enough, at its middle, for the Continentals to row on it on Sundays.

## What gnomes say

"The Thymes is not a river. It is a long opinion about where the water should go."

"Cross it by the bridge. Do not cross it by the stones. The stones are for the Jardin."

## The truth

The Thymes is fed from a garden tap at the north end of the Jardin, so it has a clear start and a clear end. Gnomes disagree about whether anything lives in it apart from weed, and the Continentals say the question is not done.

## In the games

A 16 m water path (id `thymes`) running north to south at about x -1600 to -1625, from z -880 to Pardonlin Basin (still water centred at (-1600, 1040), 90 x 60 m). The river is named after the herb and is crossed by four bridges, north to south: Pont Nord, Pont Neuf, Pont Sud and the Basin Bridge. The Source is the garden tap at the north end of the Jardin.

## Look and motifs

- Motifs: clipped willows along the bank, a stone lip at each bridge, reeds in the shallows.
- Palette: slate-green water, bone stone, pale limes.
- In the game: the `thymes` water path in `src/data/levels/pardonlin.level.json`, with the `pardonlin` theme in `src/data/regionThemes.json`.
