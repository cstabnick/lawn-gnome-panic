An open garden gate in Hedgeworth's old south fence, between the third green, the ninth tee and the Bottom Garden.

## What gnomes say

"You go through the Turn Gate at the turn. That is why it is called that."

"Its board says HOLES 4 TO 8 on one side. Peony would like you to read it twice."

## The truth

The old south fence stayed standing when the garden grew; Thrift left the gate open and Dame Peony had the arch board painted. It says HOLES 4 TO 8 on the course side and THE TURN on the garden side (R-218).

## In the games

Built (batch 3, E32): an open gap in the old fence at (-1140, 829), 3 m either side of centre, with an arch board. Two cart paths run through it into the Bottom Garden. Play goes through it to hole 4, and back through it to hole 9.
