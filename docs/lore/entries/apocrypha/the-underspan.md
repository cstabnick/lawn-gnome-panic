The shade under the Skyway's Unfinished Span, where the Underspanners live and work. Hammocks, welding benches and half-built machines hang from the underside of the concrete.

## What gnomes say

"Under the Span there's weather nowhere else has."

"They don't pay rent. The bridge isn't finished, so nobody can say it's theirs."

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Region tag `underspan`. #5 `m05-sweepers-wages` (the night meet on the Skyway above), #10 `m10-under-the-span`, #33 `m33-the-bench-lock`, #56 `m56-mortar-for-a-mower`. Home of Hogweed Sprocket and the Dewdrop's workshop. Epic E01 builds it as the skyway-society site.
