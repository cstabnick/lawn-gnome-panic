The shade beside the Skyway's Unfinished Span, where the Underspanners live and work. Hammocks, welding benches and half-built machines hang from the underside of the concrete.

## What gnomes say

"Under the Span there's weather nowhere else has."

"They don't pay rent. The bridge isn't finished, so nobody can say it's theirs."

## The truth

The Underspan is the plot at x 560 to 600, z -51 to -1, flat ground under the west end of the Skyway's Unfinished Span, whose deck begins at x 605.5; nothing the Underspanners build leaves it. On it: the workshop shed (`hogweeds-workshop`), the Roost on scaffolding to the north (`the-roost`) with its tin slide down (`the-chute`), the manifesto wall along the road (`underspan-manifesto-wall`) and the scrap heap behind the shed (`underspan-scrap-heap`). In act IV it is where the crew fits the door mortar and parachute rig to the Crop Duster (`m56-mortar-for-a-mower`) and the Underspanners' grey-blue tent in the coalition camp (`m64-all-hats`) is theirs.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** Hogweed's price list is chalked inside the shed door: "Fix a bell: 2 Acorns. Fix a bell that was never broken: 4. Opinion: free, but you will be asked to test something. Invention: price on delivery (delivery not guaranteed)." The kettle on the Roost is "at the discretion of the kettle".
- **Notices:** The manifesto wall carries the hoped-for and the scrawled-under: "A RULE IS ONLY A PROTOTYPE NOBODY TESTED" and, under an HOA letter, "NOTED. (Thanks.)" A tin box by the shed: "Citations: please take one."
- **Sayings and habits:** "Under the Span, the weather is yours." "If it holds, it is a bridge; if it does not, it is a lesson." Everyone lowers a hammer, tool or voice when the Skyway traffic passes overhead, out of courtesy to the bridge.
- **Small history:** The Span was meant to be finished the year the Underspanners moved in, and "finished" has been scheduled for next year ever since.
