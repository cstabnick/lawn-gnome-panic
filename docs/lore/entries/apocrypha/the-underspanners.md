A commune of brilliant, slightly unhinged inventors living under the Skyway's Unfinished Span. They hold that anything built on the ground is already obsolete, refuse permits on principle and will build anything for scrap. Local 404's estranged cousins.

## What gnomes say

"Ask them for a ladder and you'll get a lift. Ask for a lift and you'll get a ladder."

"They don't believe in the ground. Politely, they don't believe in you."

## The truth

The union poured the Span; the Underspanners moved in when it stopped. They bear Local 404 no ill will, only no conversation.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Leader Hogweed Sprocket. #10 `m10-under-the-span`, #33 `m33-the-bench-lock`, #56 `m56-mortar-for-a-mower`, #64 `m64-all-hats` p2 "By Air". They build the Dewdrop.
