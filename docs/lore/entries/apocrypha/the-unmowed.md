Traditionalist Fescue exiles living deep inside Mossy Wood who despise engines and preserve the ancient knowledge of how to jam a Mower.

## Story: Keepers of the Stopping Stone

Centuries ago, The Unmowed slipped through a knot-hole in the Fence to escape the noise of the world. They built moss-bridges, founded Pure-Loam Co-op, and hid Stopping Stone shards inside hollow acorns. To them, Turfton's obsession with hotwiring Ride-Ons is madness—until Vesper Thorne's surveyors start tagging toadstools for demolition and building the Grand Combine.

Reluctantly, they ally with Turfton's street crews to stop Project Even Cut.
