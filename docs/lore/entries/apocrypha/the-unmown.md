The north-west corner the Tall One stopped mowing years ago. It is a nature preserve now, kept by rangers, and it is the opposite of the Even Cut.

## What gnomes say

"Nothing mows the Unmown. Something mows the Unmown the way a sea mows a beach."

"It is wonderful. It is also where the grass takes things. Keep to the path."

## The truth

Gnomes find the Unmown frightening and wonderful at once. It is a place no mower goes, and the HOA cannot bear it. The rangers are patient, the birds are plenty, and the rusting mower at the entrance is the grass's own trophy. Why the Tall One stopped mowing is not known.

## In the games

The Unmown (code region `the-unmown`, biome preserve) lies at x -1800 to -200, z -1800 to -1400, plus x -1800 to -1400, z -1400 to -900. It is reached by `unmown-road` from (-950, -950) to (-1400, -1020) and then `unmown-lane` to (-1540, -1120), ending at the Unmown Ranger Station (centre (-1560, -1150)). Its features are the Long Meadow (waist-high grass), Reedmere (reed beds), Oldroot Wood (old trees) and three bird hides. A rusting mower the grass swallowed sits at the entrance.

Since E31 it is a place to walk: the [Unmown Ranger Station](unmown-ranger-station.md) with its Last Blade and lost-hat box, the [rangers](unmown-rangers.md), the [Reedmere](reedmere.md) boardwalk and the three bird hides, story vignettes (the HOA survey that never came back, the mower that tried, the ants' picnic, the birders' quarrel, the ranger's lost hat, the fairy ring in Oldroot Wood), [the Last Dandelion](last-dandelion.md), and the Unmown checklist (six birds, five animals, one plant) at the station. New wildlife: hare, hedgehog, fox, frog and dragonfly, which follow R-021 (R-206). The Unmown Ranger Buggy ([rides](living-world-rides.md)) is parked there. Its rules are kept by politeness alone (R-203).

Act IV (R-234): the Countess Wisteria's first vine is brought to the Ranger Station and entered in the rangers' book (`m65-the-countess-relents`): the corner no mower goes is the only place the Even Line cannot reach.

## Look and motifs

- Motifs: waist-high meadow, reed beds, bramble, old trees, bird hides, the swallowed mower.
- Palette: paper walls, slate roofs, pink flowers.
- In the game: theme `the-unmown` in `src/data/regionThemes.json`.

## Everyday

- **Notices:** the preserve rules (no mowing, no thinking about mowing, hats off to the herons, the HOA may visit on Tuesdays but may not enjoy it) are nailed on the Ranger Station door; each hide has its own board.
- **Habits:** gnomes walk the loop trail with their hats in their hands at the heron beds. Walkers stop at the Last Dandelion bench for as long as it takes. Nobody carries scissors, and anybody who does says they are for string.
- **Sayings:** "Keep to the path. The path will keep to you." "Nothing mows the Unmown."
- **Food:** the rangers' tea corner serves one kind of tea, in one kind of cup, from a kettle that is "never quite off".
