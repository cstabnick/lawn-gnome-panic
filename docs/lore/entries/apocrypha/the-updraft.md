A lift in the corner of Hogweed's workshop made of a hose, a fan and a large umbrella. A gnome steps onto the plate, the fan roars, and up they go through the roof onto the Roost.

## What gnomes say

"It is not a lift. It is a controlled argument with the ceiling."

## The truth

It works every time and has never once been approved. The HOA cites each ride as "unauthorised verticality"; Hogweed has framed the citations and hung them, naturally, from the ceiling. The landing is soft because the umbrella is stubborn.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the corner of Hogweed's Workshop. Riding it earns "Unauthorised Verticality" (`gta-unauthorised-verticality`; goggles for the head) and is the quest `sq-underspan-verticality`.
