Washed-up four-piece surf-rock band from the Greenhouse Strip whose lone hit, *"Don't Mow My Baby Down,"* plays endlessly on classic mower rock radio.

## Story: Royalty Disputes Over Scones

Thirty years ago, The Velvet Toadstools packed the Turfton Amphitheater for one glorious summer. Today, the four aging earthenware rockers spend every afternoon in a corner booth at Chirp & Crumb, aggressively splitting a single millet scone while arguing over who wrote the iconic three-chord mower-blade riff.

Dock "Full-Throttle" Hoewright still spins their track twice a shift on 99.3 The Blade, mostly to annoy them.

## What gnomes say
