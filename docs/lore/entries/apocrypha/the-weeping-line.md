A buried Tall One tube under the dry farms of Pebblewick. It weeps a single drop every span.

## What gnomes say

"The Weeping Line gives a drop every span."

## The truth

The tube runs under the pepper plots at a shallow depth. Nobody in Pebblewick has dug far enough to see where it goes, and the gnomes never say what it is.

## In the games

Purslane Furrow, the dry farmer, gives the player 'The Weeping Line' side quest. Black Dew bootleggers tap the tube in the 'sq-weeping-line' quest, and the drops are what they sell.
