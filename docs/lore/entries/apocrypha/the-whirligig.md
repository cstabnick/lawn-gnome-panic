The Spade Guard's flying mower: a mower deck with its blades turned to the sky, spinning fast enough to lift the
whole thing and the gnome on it.

## What gnomes say

"A mower that cuts the air instead of the grass." Most townsgnomes do not believe it flies until it flies over them.

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: parked on Fort Sandpit's pad. Stealing it sets the heat to three hats at once.
