The three gates in the old perimeter fence between Trellisdown and Pardonlin. The Continentals took out the fence panels where the roads cross and hung proper gates, each with a customs hut.

## What gnomes say

"A Wicket is a gate that has decided to be polite about it."

"Show your hat at the Wicket. They will stamp it. It will be stamped again on the way back, and you will not be asked why."

## The truth

Turfton gnomes used to think the perimeter fence was the end of the world. The Continentals think the same of theirs, and the Wickets are where the two ideas shake hands. The customs huts are staffed by the Continentals; the gates are painted white and cost a fortune in paint.

## In the games

The perimeter fence line x = -1397 separates Trellisdown from Pardonlin. Three roads pass through the Wickets:

- **The Grand Wicket** on Vineyard Lane, at z -30.
- **The North Wicket** at z -500.
- **The Harbour Wicket** at z 1000, to the harbour gate.

## Look and motifs

- Motifs: white-painted gates, customs huts, stamps, fence panels laid flat in the grass.
- Palette: white paint, bone walls, slate roofs.
- In the game: the gates are part of the Pardonlin level, `src/data/levels/pardonlin.level.json`.
