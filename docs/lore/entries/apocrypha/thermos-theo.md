Quiet Frostbite Fells roadside fixture standing beside a burning barrel of pine-cones on the `north` highway bend, pouring endless steaming acorn-broth.

## Story: Broth on Black Ice

Theo's barrel has burned through three winters without going out, and his battered tin thermos never seems to run dry. Whenever a reckless Zero-Turn racer or fleeing smuggler spins out on the black ice at `x: 40, z: -330`, Theo walks over calmly with a steaming cup.

*"Roads are slick, friend,"* he says, handing it over. *"Clay's brittle in the cold."* Nobody has ever seen him accept an acorn in payment, though leaving a spare pine-cone by his boots earns a rare nod.

## What gnomes say

"Theo's acorn-broth is the only thing keeping half the Carburettors from shattering before midnight."

## The truth
Theo is an unofficial innkeeper to the wrecked, with no faction. He wants every spun-out racer back on their feet with tea in hand, and fears the day the barrel goes out. Secret: how the thermos stays full (a loose thread). Home: Theo's camp at the bend; work: the barrel fire and the post. He keeps Shelldon's scrolls, is Father Loam's closest neighbour, vouches for the crew to Gentian Bootlace, and the Carburettors owe him for half their cracked hats. Voice: gentle, slow, rhymy; short weather-proverbs, tea offered before anything else ("Roads are slick, friend. Clay is brittle." / "Drink. Then tell me.").

Arc: absent in acts I-II (side quests only); in act III sends the crew up to the Woolly Sock Lodge (#37) and fishes on Pail Pond (#38); absent in act IV.

## Everyday

**Shop.** At the Fells bend: acorn-broth from the thermos, free, in a tin cup. On Pail Pond: a Bent-Pin Twig Rod to lend, the same broth, and a door plate that reads "THEO'S BACK IN FIVE (SINCE TUESDAY)."

**Habits.**
- Tea before questions, always. A question asked before the cup is empty is answered with weather.
- He stirs the barrel three times clockwise at noon, and once back the other way "for luck, and balance".
- He keeps a list of everyone who has spun out at the bend, on the inside of the barrel lid. It is long.

**Sayings.** "Hat on. Hands warm. Everything else is weather."

**Small history.** The barrel was lit on the first night of the first winter and has had a pine-cone every day since. Theo says it is the same fire. The cones disagree.
