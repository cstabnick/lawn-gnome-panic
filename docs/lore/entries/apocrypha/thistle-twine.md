Shopkeeper at Twine's General: tall, patient, with a pencil behind each ear.

## What gnomes say

"If Twine's doesn't sell it, you don't need it."

## The truth

Thistle keeps the back shelf stocked for the one customer who will come in one day asking for something nobody has ever asked for before.

## In the games
