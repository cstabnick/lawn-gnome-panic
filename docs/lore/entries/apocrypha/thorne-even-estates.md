Vesper Thorne's HOA-approved beige housing, going up on three sites at the edge of Turfton: the north-west fringe, the south-east fringe and the south fringe. Every house has the same height, colour and number of windows. The Double Pot is its sales floor.

## What gnomes say

"Even-Estates: a home where nothing is out of place, including you."

"They finish a house a day. I've never seen anybody move in."

## The truth

The estates are built mainly to be seen: rows of houses with nobody inside, a way of saying the Combine's world already exists. Chard Levelright builds them and does not ask who for.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). One site per E01 construction slot (construction-1 north-west, construction-2 south-east, construction-3 south), built by epic E01. #7 `m07-even-estates` (permits, night watch), #15 `ch1-founders-boot` p2 "Dress Rehearsal" (the float drives through a site), #28 `m28-sibling-rivalry`, #69 `m69-the-combine-wakes` (evacuation). World change W02 (hoardings, cranes, show-homes) after #7; W17 tears the hoardings down.
