Vesper Thorne's HOA-approved beige housing, going up on three sites: Plot 1 on the west fringe by Mossy Wood, Plot 2 on the south-east fringe by Route South and Plot 3 out in Rootwell. Every house has the same height, colour and number of windows. The Double Pot is its sales floor.

## What gnomes say

"Even-Estates: a home where nothing is out of place, including you."

"They finish a house a day. I've never seen anybody move in."

## The truth

The estates are built mainly to be seen: rows of houses with nobody inside, a way of saying the Combine's world already exists. Chard Levelright builds them and does not ask who for.

## In the games

Built by epic E16 (R-203): Plot 1 at (-295, -120), Plot 2 at (195, 325), Plot 3 at (1050, 250). Each has pale-blue-and-beige hoardings with the gate facing the nearest road, identical empty beige show-homes (three, four at Plot 3), a half-built house of mushroom-stem girders in scaffolding, a bucket crane painted "Level Is Kind", a materials yard, a site caravan and crews in acorn-cap hard hats walking a loop. The night-shift side quest starts at Plot 1's gate. #7 `m07-even-estates` (permits, night watch), #15 `ch1-founders-boot` p2 "Dress Rehearsal" (the float drives through a site), #28 `m28-sibling-rivalry`, #69 `m69-the-combine-wakes` (evacuation). World change W02 (hoardings, cranes, show-homes) after #7; W17 tears the hoardings down.
