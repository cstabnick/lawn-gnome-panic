Turfton's tallest downtown terracotta-pot skyscraper, housing Thorne Lawn & Order LLC's offices, the HOA compliance desk, and Vesper Thorne's spotless penthouse.

## Inside & Stories

While lower floors are stacked with beige filing cabinets and lawyers drafting 400-page easement notices, the top floor is dead silent. Vesper's penthouse features zero grass, mirrored porcelain floors, and a priceless tea set. Down in the basement compliance office, an unlabelled telephone—the Clerk's number printed on Compliance Beats bulletins—rings endlessly with nobody picking up.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Brass elevator plaque: *"PLEASE WIPE CLAY DUST FROM BOOTS BEFORE ASCENDING."*
