Turfton's escalating notoriety scale measuring how hard the Hat Watch—and eventually the HOA—is trying to crack your glaze.

## Escalation Tiers

At **1 Hat**, a lone officer logs a noise complaint or chases a stolen Ride-On. At **2 Hats**, cruisers swarm the grid and Spade Guard turrets lock onto intruders. At **3 Hats**, 99.3 The Blade breaks into live news bulletins as officers open fire across downtown. Rumor speaks of a mythical **4th Hat**, where black-and-gold HOA vans arrive blasting municipal bylaws over loudspeakers to slow your tires.

## What gnomes say

"Three Hats is a good time; four Hats means somebody's bringing out the brass calipers."

Why ducking into Pay 'n' Glaze for five seconds wipes the Hat Watch's memory completely clean.
