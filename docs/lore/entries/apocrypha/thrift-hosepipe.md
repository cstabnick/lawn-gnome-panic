The groundskeeper of Hedgeworth: patient, bent and wet to the knee, who talks to the grass, props the Kicked Board shut and hides moles under the ninth green.

Home: the groundskeeper's shed by the ninth green. Work: Hedgeworth grounds.

## What gnomes say

"Thrift says 'good morning' to the fairway. It is the only fairway in the garden that answers."

"Don't walk on the wet bits. He knows which bits."

## The truth

Thrift keeps the grass because the grass is alive, and he keeps the moles because they are why it is. He has sheltered a family under the ninth for three seasons and hopes Basil never asks.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Chases the player off in quest 29 (`m29-the-kicked-board`) for "walking on wet fairway"; the giver of 48 (`m48-the-groundskeepers-secret`); evacuated ahead of the Combine's line in 52 (`m52-the-test-cut`).
