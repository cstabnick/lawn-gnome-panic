Reginald Vance's beige townhouse in Patio Heights, walk-in. A dining table is laid for two, with one chair knocked over. The carpet has been measured. The mat reads "SHOES OFF. SOCKS: BEIGE."

## What gnomes say

"Number 404: the one house the audit cannot find."

"He measures the carpet nightly. It has never been wrong, which bothers him."

## In the games

Built by E10 (batch 3): Reginald's home, walk-in. The table laid for two and the knocked-over chair are the vignette; nobody explains them. Plate on the map once the player has stood at the door (R-180).
