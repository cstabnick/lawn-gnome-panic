HOA wardens in tall striped hats who stand on a podium in the middle of a busy crossing and wave one road through while holding the other with a stop/go paddle.

## What gnomes say

"Go when the hat says go. The hat is taller than you, so it can see further."

"Those hats are too short, says Pardonlin. Pardonlin has never been wrong about a hat."

## The truth

The wardens are HOA staff, not police. They never chase anyone and they never fine on the spot; their whole job is to keep the traffic moving in one direction at a time. They are the reason the HOA's crossings look so tidy and so slow.

## In the games

Four named wardens, each on a podium at a busy crossing:

- **Sedge Pennyroyal**, Turfton, the hospital crossing.
- **Burnet Gatepost**, by the Mulch Club.
- **Agrimony Kerb**, the south crossing.
- **Lupin Pardoux**, Pardonlin High Street, on loan from the Continentals, who think the HOA's hats are too short.

Hat Watch is a different HOA thing (see the Hat Watch entry): the wardens direct traffic, and the Hat Watch chases gnomes who have built up heat.

## Look and motifs

- Motifs: tall striped hats, podiums, stop/go paddles, painted crossing lines.
- Palette: HOA beige with stripes, the paddles in red and green.

## Loose threads

Whether the hats are taller in Pardonlin, and whether any warden has ever been allowed to go home.
