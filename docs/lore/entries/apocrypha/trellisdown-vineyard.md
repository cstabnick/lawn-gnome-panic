Rows of old vines on a trellis hill in Trellisdown, kept in line by Chicory Snips and pressed each autumn into grape-dew. It covers the hill at (-1140, -170), with Brassknock Manor across vineyard-lane to the south.

## What gnomes say

"The vines have been trained so long they grow straight on their own. Don't tell Chicory; he'd stop pruning."

"If a vine leans, somebody has been rude to it."

## The truth

The oldest vine, the Countess's first, grows by the press-house wall and is never harvested. Everything else in the vineyard is a cutting of it. The row Chicory looks after most grows crooked on purpose.

## In the games

Built by epic E16 (R-204): x -1290..-990, z -280..-60, rows running east-west with a path up the middle. The gate is on the south edge at (-1140, -61) facing vineyard-lane, Chicory's lodge just inside at (-1172, -78), and the press-house at (-1105, -95) with the first vine on its west wall. Frost-pots stand among the rows and the crooked row is at z -205, east of the path. Red deer graze nearby. Quest 17 (`m17-the-dew-buyer`) ends here; quest 65 (`m65-the-countess-relents`) carries the first vine away from it. Part of the Grand Combine's line runs through these rows.
