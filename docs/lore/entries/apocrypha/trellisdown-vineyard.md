Rows of old vines on a trellis hill in Trellisdown, kept in line by Chicory Snips and pressed each autumn into grape-dew. It covers the hill at (-1140, -170), with Brassknock Manor across vineyard-lane to the south.

## What gnomes say

"The vines have been trained so long they grow straight on their own. Don't tell Chicory; he'd stop pruning."

"If a vine leans, somebody has been rude to it."

## The truth

The oldest vine, the Countess's first, grows by the press-house wall and is never harvested. Everything else in the vineyard is a cutting of it. The row Chicory looks after most grows crooked on purpose.

## In the games

Built by epic E16 (R-211): x -1290..-990, z -280..-60, rows running east-west with a path up the middle. The gate is on the south edge at (-1140, -61) facing vineyard-lane, Chicory's lodge just inside at (-1172, -78), and the press-house at (-1105, -95) with the first vine on its west wall. Frost-pots stand among the rows and the crooked row is at z -205, east of the path. Red deer graze nearby. Quest 17 (`m17-the-dew-buyer`) ends here; quest 65 (`m65-the-countess-relents`) carries the first vine away from it. Part of the Grand Combine's line runs through these rows.

## Everyday

- **Shops and stalls:** The press-house sells grape-dew by the thimble (never the bottle) at 4 Acorns a thimble, and by the basket on the day the pickers are paid. The tasting menu, such as it is, goes: "the First Row (young, hopeful), the Middle Rows (everyone agrees), the Crooked Row (no notes)".
- **Notices:** At the gate: "Tastings by invitation." "Pickers: one grape for the basket, none for the mouth." "Frost-pots lit by the gardener only." Beside the oldest vine: "Do not touch, prune, admire or lean."
- **Sayings and habits:** "Talk to the vine, but not about the neighbours." Pickers hum one note while they work, so the vines know where they are. The harvest ends with a single glass poured on the roots of the First Vine.
- **Small history:** One autumn a whole row was picked in the dark by mistake and pressed with a mole's hat inside. It was the best the vineyard has made, and nobody will say which row.
