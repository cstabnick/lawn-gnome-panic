Rows of old vines on a trellis hill in Trellisdown, kept in line by Chicory Snips and pressed each autumn into grape-dew. It sits beside Brassknock Manor, at about (-1150, -120).

## What gnomes say

"The vines have been trained so long they grow straight on their own. Don't tell Chicory; she'd stop pruning."

"If a vine leans, somebody has been rude to it."

## The truth

The oldest vine, the Countess's first, grows by the press-house wall and is never harvested. Everything else in the vineyard is a cutting of it. The row Chicory looks after most grows crooked on purpose.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). The vineyard site in the west-estates band, with the head gardener's lodge at its gate and frost-pots among the rows. Quest 17 (`m17-the-dew-buyer`) ends here; quest 65 (`m65-the-countess-relents`) carries the first vine away from it. Part of the Grand Combine's line runs through these rows.
