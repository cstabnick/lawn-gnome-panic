Old-money hill country west of Mossy Wood: terraced slopes, stone walls, white gravel drives and one family, the Brassknocks, who hold Turfton's oldest charter and own most of what can be seen from the road.

## What gnomes say

"Trellisdown doesn't have neighbours. It has tenants, and tenants who have not yet been told."

"You can tell you have crossed in by the walls. They get lower and the gates get taller."

## The truth

The hills were terraced long ago for the vines and never un-terraced for anything else. The gentility is real, and so is the money; the two have kept each other warm for generations, and the money has been quietly paying for the HOA's war without the gentility ever having to hear the word.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). The west-estates band of the map (x -1400 to -900), built by an area epic after E01. Quest 17 (`m17-the-dew-buyer`) is the first reach; 50 (`m50-trellisdown-tasting`) and 65 (`m65-the-countess-relents`) play out at the manor; Act III sends the crew back for the money part of the Grand Combine.
