Old-money hill country west of Mossy Wood: terraced slopes, stone walls, white gravel drives and one family, the Brassknocks, who hold Turfton's oldest charter and own most of what can be seen from the road.

## What gnomes say

"Trellisdown doesn't have neighbours. It has tenants, and tenants who have not yet been told."

"You can tell you have crossed in by the walls. They get lower and the gates get taller."

## The truth

The hills were terraced long ago for the vines and never un-terraced for anything else. The gentility is real, and so is the money; the two have kept each other warm for generations, and the money has been quietly paying for the HOA's war without the gentility ever having to hear the word.

## In the games

The west band of the map (code region `west-estates`, x -1400 to -900, z -900 to 900), built by E01. Roads: `west-band` runs on from the west highway at (-870, -30) and north along x -950, and `west-band-south` runs south. Trellisdown Vineyard is 300 x 220 m on a gentle hill at (-1140, -170). Brassknock Manor stands at (-1150, 40). Wader's Rest is 180 x 180 m at (-1130, -640), about 400 m west of Loch Moss and not on its shore. The Burrow Door sits at (-1000, 300), just across the west road from Mossy Wood's western edge. Hedgeworth Links is 360 x 420 m at (-1180, 620), entered through the Kicked Board at (-895, 640).

Quest 17 (`m17-the-dew-buyer`) is the first reach; 50 (`m50-trellisdown-tasting`) and 65 (`m65-the-countess-relents`) play out at the manor; Act III sends the crew back for the money part of the Grand Combine.

Pardonlin, the Continental garden town, lies beyond the old fence to the west: the Grand Wicket (z -30), the North Wicket (z -500) and the Harbour Wicket (z 1000) are the three gates between the two (R-172, R-177). Pardonlin itself has its own entry.
