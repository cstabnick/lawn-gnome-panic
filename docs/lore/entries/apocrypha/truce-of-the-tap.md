A council of every faction that signed the peace, and the holder of the Tap, the one neutral water source.

## What they are

No founder: a council of every faction that signed, which is why it has no single line to lead. It holds the Tap, the
standpipe on the Yew Walk, and runs the biggest market in the garden there. Their hat is slate blue (`#5b7a8c`).

## How they live

- **Norms:** a tithe on every trade; strangers are welcome; the bitten are cast out to the Infirmary; decisions go to the
  council; nobody strikes first on the Tap.
- **The Tap:** the one place enemies trade face to face, the notice board and the bounty office.

## What they want

Water, trade and the Yew Walk kept open. The Truce is worth more to them than any one faction's victory.

## Loose threads

Whether the Truce would hold if the Tap ran dry, and who gets to decide when the council disagrees.
