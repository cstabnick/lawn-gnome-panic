A log cabin by the west road in Mossy Wood where a gnome buys a Tuft License, sells the tufts combed from quarry, and reads the journal of every species in the Wood, the Flats, the Fells, the Loch, the Commons and the farms.

## What gnomes say

"Buy your license at the Lodge, comb a tuft, and let the animal go on about its business. Nobody's hurt. Nobody's wearing it."
"The Lodge pays for tufts, not for anything you'd have to explain at the Clinic."

## The truth

The Lodge is the only place in Turfton where a hunt is legal, and the only place that buys what a hunt brings home. Its tufts go on to Turfton's hat makers (plumes), upholsterers (cushion stuffing) and brush makers (bristles). Nothing from a hunt is skinned or eaten, and the Lodge's journal is the only record of what lives where.

## In the games

A cabin in Mossy Wood at the west road, with Hazel Mattock behind the counter. The Tuft License costs 120 acorns and is sold here. The counter buys tufts and keepsakes for acorns, and the journal lists each of the eight quarry with a hint before the first comb and a blurb after it.

## Loose threads

Why the lodge keeps its journal in pencil. Nobody has asked, and Hazel has not said.
