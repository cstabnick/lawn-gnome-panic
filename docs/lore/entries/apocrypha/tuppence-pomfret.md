The bookie at Skyway Foot: green hat, beige-grey beard, who takes acorns only, does IDKs instead of IOUs and keeps the counter on a bonnet.

## What gnomes say

"Wagers on the Skyway Sprint. Acorns only. I do not do IOUs, I do IDKs."

## The truth

Her counter is the bonnet of a scrap car; she pays out in cash, double when you win, with a look.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E26): the bookie at the Skyway Foot meet; her take/win/lose lines drive the wager panel.
