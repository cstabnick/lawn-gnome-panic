An open-air stepped garden bowl downtown normally used for Founder's Trim Day speeches, infamous for Vesper Thorne's emergency HOA kangaroo-court tribunal.

## Inside & Stories

Carved out of terraced flagstones with a cardboard archway at the entrance, the Amphitheater seats two hundred gnomes—or one thirty-foot Gnorwin Clayfoot parade float if driven through the jury box at full speed. Its acoustics are so sharp that Reginald Vance's brass calipers can be heard clicking from the back row.

## Ledger & Graffiti
