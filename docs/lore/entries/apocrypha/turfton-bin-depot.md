The depot beside the Turfton Compost Heap on the Mossy Wood edge, where the bin crews start every shift. Rounds go out every day, because in Turfton every day is bin day. Each load is tipped into the Heap, which is why the Heap is so warm. A sign over the gate reads "DAYS WITHOUT A SPILL: 0", and nobody has ever changed the number.

## What gnomes say

"Every day is bin day."

"The sign is optimistic about tomorrow."

## In the games

Planned (E25). The garbage job: take a round from the whiteboard, collect bins with the Bin Lorry, tip at the depot. See R-199.
