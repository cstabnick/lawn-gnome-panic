The humble concrete curb at `x: -6.25, z: 14` where every newcomer steps off the Snail-Coach clutching a battered suitcase and a fresh Kiln-Debt.

## Field Notes & Tales

Look up from the Bus Stop bench and Turfton's terracotta-pot towers loom against the sky while an unlocked Ride-On Mower idles innocently across the street. It is the exact spot where Pip Potsworth greets the protagonist with a sweating grin and a 1-Hat Watch siren already wailing two blocks away.
