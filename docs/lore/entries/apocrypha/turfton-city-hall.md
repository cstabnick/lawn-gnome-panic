An upside-down ceramic planter downtown with complimentary astroturf on the steps, hosting Mayor Mulch's ribbon-cuttings and Councilwoman Vance's zoning battles.

## Inside & Stories

The main rotunda is immaculate—swept hourly so Mayor Barnaby "Two-Coat" Mulch's glossy red paint reflects nicely in press photos. Two floors down in the cramped sub-basement, Councilwoman Juniper Vance wages guerrilla warfare with 400-page Grass Height Impact Studies, fortified behind towers of empty Acorn-Cola cans and century-old deed books.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Etched onto the front podium: *"THIS RIBBON CUT BY MAYOR MULCH (THIRD COAT)."*

## In the games

Stage `city-hall-steps` (day). The forecourt on the z = -60 road, in front of the building, has a press podium, two flagpoles and a CITY HALL plaque. Councilwoman Juniper Vance gives her noon statement at the podium, and Mayor Mulch's candy-red Ride-On is parked on the forecourt.
