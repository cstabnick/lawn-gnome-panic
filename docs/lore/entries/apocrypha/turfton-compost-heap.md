Steaming industrial badlands between Turfton and Mossy Wood, packed with rusted wheelbarrow cranes, mulch boilers, and acid-amber Black Dew refineries.

## Field Notes & Tales

When Turfton's yard waste needed somewhere to rot, entrepreneurial gnomes realized fermenting grass clippings generate intense heat—and heat distills crude Black Dew. Steam vents erupt unpredictably from the mulch piles, capable of launching a lightweight Push-Mower Bike twenty feet skyward if hit at full throttle.

## What gnomes say

"You can smell the Compost Heap from Frostbite Fells when the wind shifts."

## Loose threads

Whose armored Combine chassis is half-buried under the easternmost mulch mound.
