Turfton's elected government: Mayor Barnaby "Two-Coat" Mulch and a five-seat Zoning Board, meeting in City Hall.

## What gnomes say

"The board votes four to one. Nobody asks which one." The one is Councilwoman Juniper Vance.

## The truth

The board votes four to one for whatever the HOA files. City Hall sets the Hat Watch budget, parade permits and
street-sweeping, and nothing else. The mayor's ribbon-cuttings are the council's public face.

## In the games

- Stage `city-hall-steps`: Councilwoman Vance gives her noon statements at the City Hall podium.
- The `city-hall` giver in `src/city/cityCast.ts` is where Councilwoman Vance's jobs come from.
