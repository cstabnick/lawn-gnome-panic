Two white towers stand on a shared podium in Downtown Turfton, one a little shorter than the other. Their roofs bristle with masts and dishes. Turfton FM broadcasts from between them, and the shorter tower is the one that gets the calls.

## What gnomes say

"Tune in to Turfton FM and you will hear the masts. Tune in to the other mast and you will hear the first mast apologising."

"The two towers are the same height on the plans. The shorter one is the one with the presenters."

## In the games

It stands on the x = 90, z = -90 block of Downtown Turfton: two white towers on one podium, one shorter, both with masts and dishes on their roofs. Their silhouettes and roof rigs are set in `src/towers/landmarks.ts`.
