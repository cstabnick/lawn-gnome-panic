The radio of Turfton: three stations on one dial (and a fourth at the end of it), each with its own host, its own music and its own idea of what the
town needs to hear. The radio sits in every mower and plane the gnome drives. Press N to turn the dial.

## What gnomes say

"Keep your keys out of your flowerpots." The Hat Watch says it, the Blade says it, and the Blade's callers say it
back, which is how everyone in Turfton knows the bulletins are about them.

## The stations

- **99.3 The Blade** (*the Blade*). Loud, quick and fond of the word "machine". Dock Hoewright hosts the days; Lawn-Dart
  has the mornings from a birdbath. Breaking news is its strength. Themes: big beat.
- **88.7 Fescue Public Radio** (*Fescue Public*). Slow, careful and a little sad. Sorrel Tillbrook reads the lessons
  of Kiln & Culture and plays forty-five minutes of rain on terracotta. Themes: lullaby.
- **104.1 HOA Compliance Beats** (*Compliance Beats*). Smooth jazz to trim your edges to, sponsored by the HOA and
  hosted by a woman who used to write its fines. Cress Caliper reads the bylaws between the tunes. Themes: jazz.
- **108.0 Subsoil Radio** (*Subsoil*). The bonus station at the end of the dial: underground techno, one long set that
  never restarts, from a burrow under the town. DJ Moleman hosts. Themes: acid, dub and drum techno that blend.
  See [subsoil-radio](subsoil-radio.md) and [dj-moleman](dj-moleman.md).

The music is made in code (`@lawn/music`), so there are no recordings to get wrong. Song titles and artists are
invented for the stations; the Velvet Toadstools are the only band with a hit ("Don't Mow My Baby Down").

## The programme

Each station runs a sign-on, a song, then a break: banter, a commercial, a caller, a traffic report about somewhere
the gnome can actually drive to. Breaks rotate so that a long drive does not loop. Bulletins cut in over the top:
the Hat Watch level (1 to 3, the Watch's limit) and things the player has done (borrowing a mower, cracking up, the
Whirligig, a crop-duster take-off). Music is ducked under talk. U mutes the radio with everything else (M is the map).

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: the radio in every vehicle the gnome drives or flies. Off is a station too. The dial is kept per
  browser. The radio is local: nobody else hears your station.
- The stations also read wildlife bulletins (cow tipping, the Drey Gang, smashed snowmen, the polar bear "weather report", hives and swarms), boat adverts (Walnut Dinghy rentals at New Puddle Marina) and bridge traffic reports for the five Everspring Bridges.

## Loose threads

Who writes the traffic reports? Traffic is said to be "a Ride-On doing a stately pace", and nobody has seen the
desk. Who is the first Lawn-Dart? Is the knock on Sorrel's tape real? Nobody on the air will say.
