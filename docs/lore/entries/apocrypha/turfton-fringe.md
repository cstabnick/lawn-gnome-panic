The rough belt round Turfton where the clipped city gives up: workshops, lock-ups, scrap yards, caravans and half-built
houses, thinning out over a few hundred metres into the regions' own fields, woods, snow and sand.

## The four edges

| Edge | Side | Borders | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scrapside | east (+x) | Dust Flats | Scrap yards, tyre piles, container yards and lock-ups baking in the dust; the Skyway's shadow. |
| Stumpend | west (-x) | Mossy Wood | Woodpiles, lean-to sheds and sawing yards that the pines are slowly taking back. |
| Chillyard | north (-z) | Frostbite Fells | Stone sheds, portacabins and a water tower, frost on every fence; home of The Rusty Tine. |
| Lockside | south (+z) | Cloverfield Farms | Past the canal: polytunnels, barns and caravans, hedgerows and hay creeping up to the towpath. |

## What gnomes say

"Turfton ends where the mowing stops." The townsgnomes call it the Fringe and say nothing out there is finished: half
the houses have no roof, every yard has a mower in pieces, and nobody out there has ever paid a Glaze Tax bill on time.

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: a seeded belt of rough lots round the city (`src/fringe/`), densest by the city edge and fading out,
  each lot dressed in the neighbouring region's theme so the city blends into the countryside. Dirt tracks peel off the
  highways to the lots; power poles follow the roads out.
- The Rusty Tine: a rough pub on the north road out of town, the last stop before the Fells.

## Loose threads

Who owns the land. Thorne Lawn & Order has bought half of it on paper; the gnomes living on it have not been told.
