A dreary bureaucratic brick annex downtown where property deeds, kiln-debt liens, and HOA foreclosure paperwork are stamped in triplicate.

## Inside & Stories

Every desk has a brass stamp, a stack of beige carbon-copy forms, and a security guard who falls asleep after lunch. Beneath the public tax ledgers sits a locked vault containing Tansy Clayfoot's garage deed—alongside rolled-up blueprints for a colossal, autonomous harvesting machine stamped *PROJECT EVEN CUT*.

## Ledger & Graffiti
