The green side of Turfton, west of Downtown: a park with benches, galleries with no admission, and a stepped amphitheatre where somebody is always rehearsing. Busking pitches line the paths, and the back alleys behind the galleries are the Red Caps' short cut to everywhere.

## Character

Loud when it is pretending to be quiet and quiet when it is being loud. Gnomes here dress for an audience they have not met yet. Half the district is waiting for a review and the other half is writing one.

## Landmarks

- Turfton Amphitheater, (-90, -90): the stepped bowl where Founder's Trim Day speeches are made and HOA tribunals are held. See `turfton-amphitheater`.
- The back alleys the Red Caps use, around (-88, -34): narrow, shaded, and never the same way out twice.
- Busking pitches along the park paths: a flat stone, a hat and a good spot by the drinking tap.
- The Velvet Toadstools' rehearsal room: lore only, behind the gallery on the north path.

## Factions

- The Red Caps, who use the back alleys for their own business and do not take requests.
- The Velvet Toadstools, a band that has been about to play its first real gig for a long time.
- The gallery set, who review each other and very rarely anyone else.

## What gnomes say

"The Amphitheater never had a bad night. It had a bad critic."

"Busk where you can hear the tap. If you can't hear the tap, you're not in the Arts."

## Local slang

- "a toadstool encore": something that goes on too long, after the Velvet Toadstools' famous one.
- "cracked in the rain": melodrama, after Sir Reginald Clayfoot's play, which had a rain scene.
- "a tier-sitter": a critic, from the back rows of the Amphitheater where the critics sit.

## In the games

Park & Arts is the west side of Downtown: the Amphitheater's jury box and its stage are the scene of the city's courtroom beats, and the Red Caps' alleys run through it. Its district sign is `park-arts` in `src/districts.ts`, a block from x -135 to -5 and z -125 to 65.

## Loose threads

Whether the Velvet Toadstools ever played the rehearsal room's last show, or whether it is still going on somewhere under the gallery floor.
