Turfton's six public greens: Gnorwin Clayfoot Park downtown, and five garden blocks the city gave back to the
townsgnomes as parks, each with its own name, its own crowd and its own quarrel with City Hall.

## The six parks

| Park | Block centre (x, z) | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [Gnorwin Clayfoot Park](gnorwin-statue-park.md) | (-30, 30) | The downtown green: the wishing fountain, the coned statue of Gnorwin, picnic tables, benches. |
| Bandstand Green | (-30, -150) | A lawn round an iron bandstand: Saturday market stalls, picnic lawns, a tree grove, a pavilion, a busker. |
| Kilnside Rec | (-150, -30) | The recreation ground: a five-a-side pitch, a playground and a skate corner the Hat Watch pretends not to see. |
| Mallard Mere | (-30, 150) | A duck pond with reeds and a duck house, ringed by the dog-walk loop. |
| Clayfoot Rose Garden | (150, 30) | A formal garden: a hedge maze, rose beds under rose arches, a wishing fountain, the Clayfoot Twins statues. |
| Pure-Loam Allotments | (150, 90) | Raised beds, cold frames and sheds rented out by the Pure-Loam Co-op, with a produce stall row. |

## What gnomes say

"A park is a block that won the argument." Every green was once a garden block like the others; each one was handed to
the townsgnomes after a fight with City Hall that both sides remember differently.

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: the five newer parks replace the garden layout of their blocks (`src/parks/blocks.ts`); Gnorwin
  Clayfoot Park keeps its fountain and picnic tables and gains the rest. Benches can be sat on, the two fountains take an
  Acorn for a wish, and a few townsgnomes keep their habits in each park.

## Loose threads

Who decides which block becomes a park next. The Long Grass League has a list; City Hall says there is no list.
