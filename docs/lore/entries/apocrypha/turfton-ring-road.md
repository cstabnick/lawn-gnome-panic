The four-leg outer highway loop (`ring-ne`, `ring-nw`, `ring-sw`, `ring-se`) linking every outer biome without ever passing through Turfton's downtown grid.

## Field Notes & Tales

Built—or uncovered—so Dew-Haulers and Carburettor racers could bypass Hat Watch roadblocks in the city centre, the Ring Road is a lawless ribbon of gravel and asphalt. Local 404 refuses to patch potholes along `ring-nw`, leaving craters deep enough to swallow an HOA enforcement van whole.

## What gnomes say

"You want to lose a 3-Hat tail? Hit the Ring Road and don't lift off the gas till you smell pine."
