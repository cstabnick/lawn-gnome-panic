The sky over Turfton is busy. Aircraft fly the same regular routes day after day, mostly on errands for the town's businesses and its gossip. Nobody controls the traffic, and nobody has tried to.

## What gnomes say

"Look up before you cross the Skyway. Something is always coming."

"Wind-Sock Wally keeps the count. If you see a plane he hasn't logged, it's a new one."

## The flights

- **Sky-Sign Banner Tow.** A Crop Duster towing the giant advert banner in a slow loop over Turfton and the ring highways. Pilot Dorrie Throttle. See `sky-sign-banner-tow`.
- **Flight School Circuit.** Trainee pilots doing takeoff-and-landing circuits in a Hedge-Hopper at the Desert Airfield, under Wind-Sock Wally's stopwatch. Wally logs every landing and says nothing about the crashes.
- **Cloverfield Crop Duster.** A Crop Duster spraying the Cloverfield fields in long, low passes.
- **Turfton Sightseeing Copter.** A Sycamore Copter tour of the landmarks, with the pilot's commentary on the loudspeaker.
- **Sky-Writer.** A Hedge-Hopper writing rings and loops in smoke east of town. The smoke is the advert for nothing in particular, and the pilot will not say who asked.
- **Lawn-Dart Eye-in-the-Sky.** The news copter of Turfton FM's morning show, covering events and chases on the air for the Lawn-Dart voice. See `lawn-dart` and `turfton-fm`.
- **Pigeon-Drop Air Mail.** The Pigeon-Drop Couriers dropping parcels from altitude. A parcel that lands on the ground is the finder's to keep if they walk into it, and the courier pays a finder's fee in Acorns. See `pigeon-drop-couriers`.
- **Dandelion Drifter Balloon Ride.** A slow balloon over Tallgrass Commons. See `dandelion-drifter`.
- **Hat Watch Air Unit.** A Sycamore Copter that circles a gnome with two hats or more. See `hat-watch`.
- **Glaze Clinic Air Ambulance.** Flies to the scene of a bad glaze and lifts the gnome out. See `glaze-clinic-hospital`.

## In the games

These flights are the NPC sky traffic of Gnome Theft Auto (`games/gnome-theft-auto/src/skyTraffic/`). They are ordinary vehicles, so they can be shot down: a shot-down aircraft crashes and burns like a mower, and the operator sends another one later. A flight only starts when a gnome is near its anchor point in town.
