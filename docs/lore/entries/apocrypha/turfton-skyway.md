A raised road Local 404 poured over Turfton's east side, after a mower finally asked for a bridge higher than a step. It
answers the Everspring Bridges' loose thread: the bridges were kept a step above the road, and the Skyway is the one place
the town went higher.

## In the games

Two decks on the gnome's road map, both on the east side:

- **The Turfton Skyway** leaves the dead end of the z = -60 street at Turfton's east edge, climbs onto a viaduct, passes
  about 7.5 m over Ring Road North-East and the airfield spur, and comes down onto the east highway in the Dust Flats.
  Ground traffic keeps flowing underneath it. The skyway's map badge sits over Ring Road North-East.
- **The Unfinished Span** is a second stub that climbs north of the east highway and stops in mid-air, 6.5 m up, pointed
  over it. Its open end is marked by red and white boards. Drivers jump it.

## What gnomes say

Local 404 says the Skyway is the only road in Turfton that goes somewhere a mower can't. The Dust Flats crews say it is
the only road that comes down on them.

The Unfinished Span has no official name. Most gnomes call it the Span, and nobody calls it finished.

## The truth

The Skyway was poured in a hurry, and a strike stopped the crew before the far end was done. The Span is the part that
was never finished: the crew walked off the job during the strike and left the last stretch standing where it stopped.
