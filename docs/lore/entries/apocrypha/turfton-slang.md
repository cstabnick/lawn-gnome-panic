Every district of Turfton has its own sayings. Outsiders are often caught out by them.

## What gnomes say

- **Downtown:** "on the grid" (working a legal job); "a two-coat" (a politician who repaints after a scandal);
  "kerb-sitting" (waiting at the Exchange for work).
- **Greenhouse Strip:** "under the glass" (out for the night); "moth-bait" (a show-off in neon); "sugar" (anyone; Nina's word).
- **Patio Heights:** "curb appeal" (said like a threat); "a quarter-inch" (any petty fine); "flagstoned" (foreclosed).
- **Old Brick Quarter:** "no sons" (honest); "the paving's listening" (keep your voice down); "pulls left" (a bit off).
- **Park & Arts:** "a toadstool encore" (something that goes on too long); "cracked in the rain" (melodrama); "a tier-sitter" (a critic).
- **Clay-Mart Industrial:** "fired and billed" (a newcomer); "forty-eight hours" (a strike threat); "on the meter" (in debt).
- **Ring Blocks:** "pot-street" (an ordinary home); "a ring-blocker" (someone from no district).

## The truth

Most of the slang comes from the district's own work: the Exchange, the Strip's nights, the HOA's paperwork, the
Quarter's shops, the Park's critics and the Industrial's bills.

## In the games

Lore only. The slang is for writers and dialogue, and the district signs are in `src/districts.ts`.
