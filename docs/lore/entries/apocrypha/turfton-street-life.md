What happens on Turfton's streets and verges while nobody is paying the player for anything. Small scenes start a
little way off, run their course whether or not the player joins in, and end.

## Inside & Stories

- **The Hat Watch at work.** Red Cap getaway lowriders chased by Watch ride-ons; traffic stops where Officer Yarrow
  Ladle measures a driver's grass height and hat angle; foot chases after hatless thieves; barricaded checkpoints
  that wave clean gnomes through and remember the ones with heat.
- **Crime in progress.** Red Cap stick-ups outside the shops; Nettle Grubb and his kind snatching acorn purses;
  carjackings; Clover Thimblerig's shell game, which is honest about one time in three; HOA crooks gnome-napping
  townsfolk into a black van.
- **Quiet scenes.** Fennel Washboard busking on the upright bass; clay weddings riding a boot float through town
  (lately Juniper Kettle and Rue Ladle); picnics that always have room for one more; Parsley Gumboot's hat, which
  the wind keeps taking; Aunt Hyssop Crockery's yard sales; the Long Grass League's mow-in protests, led by Sedge
  Trugg and chanting "Longer grass! Fewer blades!" past an HOA inspector who writes it all down.
- **The road.** Fender benders with two drivers blaming each other; Chicory Sprocket's ride-on, which breaks down
  more often than it runs; Basil Tiller's runaway zero-turn; a snail coach holding up a queue of honking mowers;
  street races that come and go faster than the Watch.
- **Creatures.** Farmer Bramble's livestock loose on the verge; squirrels making off with acorn-flour sacks
  (hungry, not wicked); bee swarms that beekeeper Linden Skep would very much like back in their hive.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Long Grass League placard, abandoned on a kerb: *"WHAT DO WE WANT? SEED HEADS!"*
