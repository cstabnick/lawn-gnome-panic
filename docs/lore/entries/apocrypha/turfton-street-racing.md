Racing on real roads, for acorns and a name on a wall. Three meets keep it going, each with its own manners: the
Skyway Foot after dark, the closed-road Boucle at Pardonlin, and the lay-by at Saltbucket.

## Inside & Stories

- **The routes.** Kerb Row Circuit (downtown, in live traffic), the Skyway Sprint (up the Turfton Skyway), the Ring Road
  Run (the Carburettors' time trial, the one with the record on the wall), La Boucle de Pardonlin (closed road) and the
  Saltbucket Dash (the band road out to the coast).
- **Wagers.** Each meet has a bookie who takes stakes in Acorns for a pickup race: 25, 100 or 250, or a friendly for
  nothing. A winner is paid twice the stake. Nobody takes an IOU.
- **The ladder.** Newcomers begin on Kerb Row at the Skyway Foot, graduate to the Skyway at night, go abroad to Pardonlin
  and the coast, and end on the Ring Road against Sable Throttlewick.
- **The Watch.** Hat Watch knows about the Skyway Foot and has been told it is a car boot sale. They notice and rarely
  chase; a race through live traffic still draws a wanted star if the driver is careless. The HOA files it all under
  "Verge Activity, miscellaneous".

## What gnomes say

"Low and loud, and be home by the fourth star."

"It is not racing if there is a form. It is a Grand Prix."
