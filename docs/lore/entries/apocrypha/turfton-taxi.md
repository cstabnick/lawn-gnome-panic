The yellow checker cab a gnome can call from anywhere in Turfton. It only knows one address: the Central Bus Stop.

## Field Notes & Tales

Stand anywhere in Turfton, raise a hand and the cab comes. It pulls up beside you with its roof sign lit, Mabel Kerbstone at the wheel, and the meter already running. The fare is 10 Acorns to start and rises with the distance, up to 60. Nobody has ever asked where the cab goes when it is not carrying a gnome, and the cab has never said.

## What gnomes say

"Turfton Taxi, on my way!" Mabel says it, but every gnome in Turfton says it when they want a lift home.

## In the games

The yellow button with a 🚕 (44 px, top right of the map), the Y key, or D-pad left on a gamepad calls the cab. It will not come while a gnome is wanted by the Hat Watch, on a quest, in a vehicle, swimming, airborne or dead. Once the gnome is seated it drives off, fades to black, and comes back up to the kerb of the Central Bus Stop, where the gnome is let out and the cab drives away. Three rides earn the achievement Meter's Running, which gives the Cabbie Cap.
