The five eras of Turfton before the game starts, oldest first, and then Now. The order agrees with the world gazetteer
and the Skyway entry (R-148).

## What gnomes say

"Turfton was always a grid." Old gnomes say the paths were always there. Nobody under forty remembers them being anything else.

## The truth

**The Flat Patch.** A suspiciously level square of clover where the first earthenware gnomes stacked cast-off pots to
sleep out of the dew. The Old Brick Quarter's lanes are its footpaths.

**Grid Night.** One night the chalk-and-gravel grid appeared, perfectly straight, 60 paces to a block. Nobody saw who laid
it. Turfton answered by inventing parallel parking and the property deed by morning.

**The Pot Rush.** Gnomes claimed blocks, stacked pots into towers and sold the tops. Clay-Mart opened its Kilnworks and
began billing every newcomer for their firing (the Kiln-Debt). The first Homeowners' Association was founded to keep the
new towers looking alike.

**The Great Sprinkler Scare.** The municipal sprinklers misfired across Downtown. The Spade Guard withdrew to Fort Sandpit,
and Don Compost stopped speaking.

**The Pour.** Local 404 poured the Skyway over the east side, then struck over pay before the far end was done, leaving
the Unfinished Span. The Hat Watch unionised, the Gnomecino opened, and Thorne Lawn & Order began buying corner pots.

**Now** is the story of Gnome Theft Auto.

## In the games

Not shown as text. The eras are the background to the city's quests: the Skyway (The Skyway Job), the HOA's fines, the
Kiln-Debt on newcomers and the Hat Watch's union.
