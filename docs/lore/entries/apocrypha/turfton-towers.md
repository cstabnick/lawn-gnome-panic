The stacked-pot towers of Downtown: the buildings the Pot Rush made, each one a pile of pots that somebody sold the top of.

## What gnomes say

"Every tower in Turfton is a pot on a pot." Nobody argues. The argument is about who owns the top one.

## The truth

The towers were stacked during the Pot Rush, when gnomes claimed blocks and sold the tops. The notable ones:

- **Thorne Tower**, Thorne Lawn & Order's offices and Vesper's penthouse pot. It is 86 m tall, the tallest of them.
- **City Hall**, the council's building, which is an upside-down planter with astroturf on the steps.
- **The Kiln-Shield billboards' tower**, which is wrapped in advertising.
- **The Gnomecino's glasshouse block**, on the Greenhouse Strip.
- **Clay-Mart's warehouse**, the biggest box in Clay-Mart Industrial.

## In the games

The towers are the city's landmarks. Their looks are not fixed here: they are shaped by the game's own models.
