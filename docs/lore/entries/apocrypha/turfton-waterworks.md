The gnome waterworks: every lock, weir, sluice, wharf and water-powered thing that Turfton's water passes through on its
way downhill from the house to the Puddle Sea. Nobody runs it. It is simply maintained by people who were there first.

## What gnomes say

"Turfton's water goes where the sponsors say." Sponsored locks carry a plate with a brand name, and the brand gets to
name the lock.

## The works

- **The canal locks.** Two concrete locks where the Southern Drainage Canal steps down, named for sponsors:
  *Pay-n-Glaze Lock No. 1* at the upper step and *Black Dew Lock No. 2* at the lower one. Each has a hand-cranked gate
  and a plaque. The gates are opened by whoever gets to the crank first.
- **The Loch Moss weir.** A low stone weir at the loch's outflow that holds the loch level. It is why the
  [Watering-Can Steamer](watering-can-steamer.md) never leaves the loch.
- **The Cloverfield field sluices.** Wooden gates on the farm river that send water to the vegetable plots and let the
  rest run to the Puddle Sea.
- **The Mossy Wood waterwheel sawmill.** A big wheel on the ravine, turning a saw that cuts the timber for the
  boardwalks and the bridges.
- **The docks and wharves.** Old Brick at the west end of the canal and the Industrial wharf at the east end, where the
  boats are moored and the cargo is loaded.
- **The road bridges.** Each bridge is sized for a mower, and the rails are painted to show the width. A bridge that is
  too narrow for a Combine is a bridge the Combine goes around.

## The quiet oddities

Nobody in Turfton says what these are for. Gnomes have theories and keep them to themselves.

- The **Brass Horn** at the Everspring: the great brass nozzle on the source pipe, which only ever sprays a little mist.
- The **black rubber rim** of Loch Moss, where the loch meets the reeds.
- The **concrete lips** along the canal, which stick out above the water at exactly the height of a gnome's chin.
- The **toppled stone heron** in the Loch Moss reeds, lying on its side.

## In the games

The canal locks and wharves on the Southern Drainage Canal, the weir at Loch Moss, the sluices on Cloverfield's river,
and the sawmill wheel in Mossy Wood. Water in shallow reaches (the canal floor, river edges, fords) is waded; deep water
floats boats and gnomes (see [rulings.md](../../rulings.md) R-050).

## Loose threads

Who built the waterworks, who maintains the sponsors' plaques, and what the Brass Horn sprays for. Gnomes have
theories; the game never gives an answer.
