The gnome town of Gnome Theft Auto: a grid of paths through a lawn, lined with giant flower pots, sheds and
birdbaths that the gnomes live in, and busy with ride-on mowers that nobody locks.

## What gnomes say

"Mowers are for borrowing." Townsgnomes leave their mowers running at the kerb and are surprised every time.

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: the whole map. Pedestrians wander the sidewalks; shot ones crack apart into clay shards.

## Loose threads

Who laid the paths in a perfect grid. The townsgnomes say the paths were always there.
