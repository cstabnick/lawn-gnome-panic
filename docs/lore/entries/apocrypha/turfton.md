The gnome town of Gnome Theft Auto: a grid of paths through a lawn, lined with giant flower pots, sheds and
birdbaths that the gnomes live in, and busy with ride-on mowers that nobody locks.

## What gnomes say

"Mowers are for borrowing." Townsgnomes leave their mowers running at the kerb and are surprised every time.

## In the games

- Gnome Theft Auto: the whole map. Pedestrians wander the sidewalks; shot ones crack apart into clay shards. Drivers are on errands (R-026).

## Look and motifs

Turfton is the only place in the garden where the green is told what shape to be. Its blocks are civic
gardens: hedgerows clipped square with a gap at each side for walking through, topiary cones and boxwood balls at the
corners, flower beds in straight rows, and on some streets black iron railings on a stone kerb instead of a hedge.
At most one big Tall-One object stands in a block now, as a centrepiece; the rest was cleared and planted.

- Motifs: clipped topiary, iron railings on stone kerbs, flower beds, garden borders, stone planters.
- Palette: cream and clay walls, slate roofs, pink and red flowers.
- In the game: `src/landscaping.ts` lays the blocks out; the theme is `turfton` in `src/data/regionThemes.json`.

## Districts

Turfton has six named districts and the Ring Blocks around them. The full index is the [Turfton Gazetteer](turfton-gazetteer.md).

- [Downtown Turfton](downtown-turfton.md): the civic core around the x = 0 and z = 0 roads.
- [Greenhouse Strip](greenhouse-strip.md): glasshouse nightlife east of Downtown.
- [Patio Heights](patio-heights.md): the HOA's gated terrace in the north-east corner.
- [Old Brick Quarter](old-brick-quarter.md): the oldest streets, south-west of Downtown.
- [Turfton Park & Arts](turfton-park-and-arts.md): the park and galleries, west of Downtown.
- [Clay-Mart Industrial](clay-mart-industrial.md): the warehouses and the Union Hall in the south-west corner.
- [The Ring Blocks](ring-blocks.md): every block outside the six named districts.
