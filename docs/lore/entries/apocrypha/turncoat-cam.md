Side-Valve Salvage's sweeper, who is also the HOA's informant: a thin gnome with a push-broom who wants a Patio Heights racing charter more than he wants to be liked, and who sells the crew out for it.

## What gnomes say
"Cam? Sweeps like he is counting." Everyone at the bay says some version of it, fondly, and nobody has worked out what he was counting.

"Tansy trusts him. Pip likes him. Neither of them looks at his hands." (Lulu, laughing, not unkindly.)

## The truth
Cam's real name is Cameron (R-071). He sweeps the Side-Valve apron from the prologue on, watches the bay too often, and phones Vesper on a tin-can phone at the end of chapter 2 ("Tuesday. The charter's mine."), then hands him the Golden Acorn at the Salvage Raid, in exchange for a Patio Heights racing charter. He wants the charter and to be someone in Turfton; he fears sweeping someone else's apron for ever. Secret: he is selling the crew out (the plant: his stare at Lulu's charter plate at #5, the envelope at #30).

Voice: near silent until the betrayal, then short, flat, transactional, ending sentences on the price ("Noted." / "That is fine. It is what it costs.").

Look: the game's preset is a pale cap and a tan shirt, a push-broom in hand (the HOA vest replaces the apron after #35). The bible's first draft said "drab olive cap"; the preset is what players see, so it is pale. Hunched, eyes on the bay and never on the person talking.

Home: a hidden cot in Side-Valve's storeroom; an HOA rental in Patio Heights after #35; the HOA lockup in the Double Pot at #62. Work: sweeper at Side-Valve; HOA valet at the Patio Gate.

Arc: the broom in the background of every Side-Valve shot, a part run for the Carburettors at #5 and envy of Lulu (I); a Speedway big night (#30) that he loses, the envelope, the bench code watched at #33, the call at #34, and the betrayal at #35 (II); valet in an HOA vest at the Patio Gate, seen from #21 and absent otherwise (III); in act IV Vesper cancels his charter and locks him in the Double Pot lockup (#62), the player frees him or leaves him, and either way he gives the code (IV).

Tansy trusts him and he is her blind spot; Pip likes him and does not notice; Lulu laughs at him without meaning harm; Vesper uses him and drops him; Midge once had him in her waiting room.
