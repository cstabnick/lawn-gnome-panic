The general store on the Pebblewick square. If Twine's does not sell it, nobody in town needs it.

## What gnomes say

"If Twine's doesn't sell it, you don't need it."

## The truth

Thistle Twine stocks everything anyone in Pebblewick has ever asked for, and nothing they have not.

## In the games

On the Pebblewick square, facing the Dry Spoon. Thistle Twine keeps the counter.
