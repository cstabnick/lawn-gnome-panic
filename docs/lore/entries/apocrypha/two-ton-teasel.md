Clary Mudguard's older brother and the Rutbottom Heats rider with the orange hat and the black beard: heavy on the bike, light on the throttle, and unwilling to be told he has never won a start.

## What gnomes say

"Clary has never let me win a start. Today she has no say."

## The truth

Teasel rides the Heats with Polly and Bramble (R-190). He is called Two-Ton for the bike and not the rider; his sister marshals the start and has never once lifted the flag early. He waits by his bike in the pits, where the Rutbottom Heats giver stands.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E07): waits at about (1024, -630) in the pits, "cross-arms" idle; he gives the Rutbottom Heats questline. Six of his practice cones lie knocked over in a line in the Bowl's story vignette, with a seventh on a hay bale.

## Loose threads

Whether he has ever won a start; the official answer is "not yet".
