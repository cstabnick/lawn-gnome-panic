Loamdeep's sixth hall: a wide, quiet room with a humming floor. DJ Moleman's cable comes up here, and the moles dance with great seriousness and no hats.

## What gnomes say

"The floor hums. I put my ear to it. It put its ear to me."

## The truth

Before the cable it was a hall for quiet. Now it is a hall for quiet that moves: two speakers dug into the walls and a space in the middle for a mole to stand.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the northern hall of Loamdeep. Once the lamps are lit (`sq-loamdeep-lamps`), Moleman comes up and plays here (`sq-loamdeep-under-chorus`); being there earns "Seat" (`gta-seat`).
