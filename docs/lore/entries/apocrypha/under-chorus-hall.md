Loamdeep's sixth hall: a wide, quiet room with a humming floor. DJ Moleman's cable comes up here, and the moles dance with great seriousness and no hats.

## What gnomes say

"The floor hums. I put my ear to it. It put its ear to me."

## The truth

Before the cable it was a hall for quiet. Now it is a hall for quiet that moves: two speakers dug into the walls and a space in the middle for a mole to stand. It is the one hall no family owns (`loamdeep-clans`): the signs read "UNDER-CHORUS HALL / feel the floor" and, for the one who comes up the tunnel, "WELCOME HOME, COUSIN". The hall is 28 by 24 metres of local floor (x -14 to 14, z -42 to -18), with the decks at (0, -39), the dance floor at (0, -29) and the Long Tunnel stair at (-12, -40).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the northern hall of Loamdeep. Once the lamps are lit (`sq-loamdeep-lamps`), Moleman comes up and plays here (`sq-loamdeep-under-chorus`); being there earns "Seat" (`gta-seat`).


## Everyday

- **Notices:** "UNDER-CHORUS HALL. Feel the floor." "WELCOME HOME, COUSIN." A small plate by the decks: "TO CRESS, FROM BELOW."
- **Sayings and habits:** "No hats, no words, one nod." The moles dance with their eyes shut so as not to compare. A visitor is allowed to stay as long as they do not clap on the wrong beat, which nobody can name.
