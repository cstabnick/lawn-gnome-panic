A damp, echoing labyrinth of corrugated storm drains and brick culverts running directly beneath Turfton's 60-unit street grid.

## Field Notes & Tales

Built by the Tall Ones for rainwater runoff and colonized by Carburettor smugglers dodging Hat Watch patrols, the Under-Deck is Turfton's literal underworld. Recently, Vesper's crews welded iron bars across the main grates to hide thick power cables snaking toward unknown construction sites. Damp air makes clay feel twice as heavy, so nobody lingers without a lantern.
