A long board of chalked posters along the Underspan's west edge, facing the road, where the Underspanners write out what they believe for the benefit of everybody who walks past without asking. It is the only part of the Underspan that is finished.

## What gnomes say

"They put their beliefs out on the road for anyone to read. Most days the beliefs are better than the road."

"Vetch writes on the columns in chalk. The Wall is where she keeps the clean copies."

## The truth

The Wall stands at (561.5, -20), facing west, with the posters in rotating pairs: "A RULE IS ONLY A PROTOTYPE NOBODY TESTED", "PERMITS ARE FOR PEOPLE WHO ASK", "THE GROUND IS ALREADY OBSOLETE", "UP IS A DIRECTION, NOT A CRIME", "WE BUILT A LIFT. THEY BUILT A FORM." and "CITATIONS ARE A FORM OF PRAISE". Beside the workshop door a tray of HOA citations hangs under the sign "CITATIONS: PLEASE TAKE ONE", and at the road a stake sign reads "THE UNDERSPAN / pop. varies / est. when the Span stopped". The Wall is not a petition: the Underspanners have no request.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: signs and posters on the west edge of the Underspan site, read in passing from the road. No quest hangs on it.

## Loose threads

Whether the posters are ever changed, or the road changes to suit them.
