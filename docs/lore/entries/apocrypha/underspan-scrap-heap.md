The pile of scrap by the workshop's back door, where the Underspanners keep everything they have not yet built: hoses, umbrellas, a bicycle with the wrong number of wheels and a kettle on a frame. Nothing in it is rubbish; it is "pre-invention".

## What gnomes say

"The Underspanners will build anything for scrap. That is why there is so much of it."

"Do not take a piece. Everything in there is the one thing they were saving."

## The truth

The heap is at (596, -3), against the workshop's south wall, inside the Underspan site. It is the Underspanners' currency, supply and research budget: they trade a part for a part, and a gnome who brings one in gets a favour back at the bench. Burdock Flywheel's generator eats the oldest.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: the destination of `sq-underspan-scrap-run`: gather scrap and carry it up the Updraft and down the Chute.
