A statue of a pigeon on the Grand Platz that nobody can identify. The real pigeons hold their committee meetings on its plinth.

## What gnomes say

"It is a very good pigeon. Whose, no one can say."

"The plaque has worn off. The committee has not."

## The truth

Nobody remembers who the pigeon is, who made it, or what it is the unknown pigeon of. The living pigeons treat the plinth as a chamber: they sit in rows, coo in turn, and adjourn without a vote. The Continentals respect the committee and walk round it.

## In the games

On a plinth in the middle of the Grand Platz (Old Town). The pigeons are scenery that perches; they never attack.

## Loose threads

Whose pigeon it is. The committee's minutes, if there are any.
