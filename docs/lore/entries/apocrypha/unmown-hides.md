The Unmown's three bird huts: **Reedmere Hide**, looking south over the marsh; **Meadow Hide**, looking north over the Long Meadow; and **Wood Hide**, looking north into Oldroot Wood. Each has a rules board by the door, a feeder and a slot to look through, and each is kept, in the rangers' way, by nobody.

## What gnomes say

"Three huts, three habitats, three boards. Read the board before the birds."

"Standing in all three is called Hide and Seek. The hides are the ones doing the hiding."

## The truth

**Reedmere Hide** at (-1050, -1550): "Whisper. The bittern is listening. It is always listening." **Meadow Hide** at (-560, -1490): "Please do not count the grass. We tried. We ran out of numbers." **Wood Hide** at (-1700, -1330): "The fox was here first. Knock before entering." Each is a 12 by 12 m site, a hide on the prefab `bird-hide`. Standing within 4.5 m of a hide's centre counts as a visit. They are not the Reedwatch Hide on Loch Moss (`reedwatch-hide`), which is Dunnock Pell's.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E31): `unmown/hides.ts` and `living/config.ts` (`UNMOWN_HIDES`). Visiting all three earns "Hide and Seek".

## Everyday

- **Notices:** one board per hide: Reedmere Hide, "Whisper. The bittern is listening. It is always listening." Meadow Hide, "Please do not count the grass. We tried. We ran out of numbers." Wood Hide, "The fox was here first. Knock before entering."
- **Habits:** hide-goers bring a flask and a notebook. The rangers' tally of birds seen is kept in pencil; the count for "bittern" is a question mark.
- **Sayings:** "Read the board before the birds."
- **Feud:** the birders' quarrel over whether the heron by Reedmere Hide is the same heron. The rangers say it is "a heron".
