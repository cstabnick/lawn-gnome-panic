The rangers' hut at the head of the Unmown Lane, centre (-1560, -1150): a place to sign the book, wipe your boots and read the rules. It is where the Unmown's one law is written down, and where nobody enforces it.

## What gnomes say

"Sign the book. Wipe your boots. Do not mention hedge trimmers."

## The truth

The rules on the door read: **1. No mowing. 2. No thinking about mowing. 3. Hats off to the herons. 4. The HOA may visit on Tuesdays, but may not enjoy it.** The only enforcement is politeness (R-203). Inside: **the Last Blade**, the final mower blade, in a display case marked "Please do not sharpen"; a specimen shelf of jars; a wall sign "WHERE THE GRASS IS (EVERYWHERE)"; a tea corner with a ranger chair and its hat; and **the lost-hat box**, a bin marked "LOST PROPERTY (HOA HATS)", full of hats the HOA's inspectors have left behind. Outside, a rack of "CONFISCATED MOWERS" and the sign "UNMOWN RANGER STATION: Please do not mow".

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: a walk-in interior (`unmown-ranger-station`) at the end of the Unmown Lane, with Head Ranger Burdock Haybright at the desk, the trailhead of the loop trail on its north side, and the Unmown checklist (`src/unmown/checklist.ts`).

Act IV (R-234, `m65-the-countess-relents`): the Countess Wisteria's first vine, taken out of her vineyard to safety before the Grand Combine's Even Line reaches it, is delivered here, to the one corner no mower goes (R-174), not to Granny's greenhouse. Head Ranger Burdock Haybright takes it in at the station door, writes it in the book (in the column for "arrivals, unmown") and finds it a place on the north side by the trailhead. Chicory Snips still loads it and waters it. The Countess calls to say thank you in a way that costs her.

## Everyday

- **The book:** every visitor signs the rangers' book at the desk, with columns for name, hat and "arrivals, unmown". Head Ranger Burdock Haybright checks the boots, not the names. Most entries end "left the grass as found".
- **The rules (on the door):** 1. No mowing. 2. No thinking about mowing. 3. Hats off to the herons. 4. The HOA may visit on Tuesdays, but may not enjoy it.
- **Notices:** "Unmown Ranger Station: please do not mow." "Where the grass is (everywhere)." "The Last Blade: please do not sharpen." On the mower rack: "Confiscated mowers."
- **The lost-hat box:** "Lost property (HOA hats)." The rangers keep it tidy and do not send hats back; nobody has come to collect one.
- **Habits:** tea at four, in the corner, by the ranger's hat. The kettle has not been unplugged since the Tall One stopped mowing.
- **Sayings:** "Sign the book. Wipe your boots. Do not mention hedge trimmers."
