The rangers' hut at the head of the Unmown Lane, centre (-1560, -1150): a place to sign the book, wipe your boots and read the rules. It is where the Unmown's one law is written down, and where nobody enforces it.

## What gnomes say

"Sign the book. Wipe your boots. Do not mention hedge trimmers."

## The truth

The rules on the door read: **1. No mowing. 2. No thinking about mowing. 3. Hats off to the herons. 4. The HOA may visit on Tuesdays, but may not enjoy it.** The only enforcement is politeness (R-199). Inside: **the Last Blade**, the final mower blade, in a display case marked "Please do not sharpen"; a specimen shelf of jars; a wall sign "WHERE THE GRASS IS (EVERYWHERE)"; a tea corner with a ranger chair and its hat; and **the lost-hat box**, a bin marked "LOST PROPERTY (HOA HATS)", full of hats the HOA's inspectors have left behind. Outside, a rack of "CONFISCATED MOWERS" and the sign "UNMOWN RANGER STATION: Please do not mow".

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: a walk-in interior (`unmown-ranger-station`) at the end of the Unmown Lane, with Head Ranger Burdock Haybright at the desk, the trailhead of the loop trail on its north side, and the Unmown checklist (`src/unmown/checklist.ts`).
