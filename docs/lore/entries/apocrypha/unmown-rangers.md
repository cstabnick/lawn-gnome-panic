The patient keepers of the Unmown: **Head Ranger Burdock Haybright** and **Junior Ranger Pip Tussock**. They count, watch and tidy nothing.

## What gnomes say

Burdock, at the desk: "Twelve species before lunch. Thirteen if you count the HOA inspector hiding in the reeds."

Pip, on the trail: "Four thousand and six reeds. Four thousand and seven. Please stop walking past, you keep moving them."

## The truth

**Burdock Haybright** is old, white-bearded and unhurried; he keeps the book at the station, the Last Blade and the checklist of what is out there. He will not say the words "hedge trimmer". **Pip Tussock** is the junior, counting the reeds of Reedmere by hand and losing count whenever a visitor walks by. Both wear the olive ranger hat; the ranger's hat on a bramble at Oldroot Wood's edge, with a ladder and a stake reading "Back in 5 min", is one of theirs. Other regulars on the trail: **Mr. Pettigrew, off duty** (an HOA inspector with "a personal ruler") and **Marigold the birder**, thrilled by any noise at all. Rangers never fine anyone (R-199).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: Burdock at the station desk and outside it; Pip, Mr. Pettigrew and Marigold on the trail (`unmownPeople()`), each with a bubble line when the player walks near. Name notes: Pip Tussock and Pip Pearsley (Espalier End) are two different gnomes; Marigold the birder is not Marigold Skillet of the Dry Spoon.
