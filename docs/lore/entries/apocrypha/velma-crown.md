The keeper of the Top Hat, the café at Brim Gap. She serves Gap Tea and Hairpin Buns, and she counts every car that stops at her door.

## What gnomes say

"Cars today: 3. Buns: 41."
"Sit down, love. The Pass will still be there when you've had your tea. It always is."

## The truth

Velma has kept the Top Hat since the road was built. She counts the cars because somebody has to, and she is the only one who does. The count is written on a card by the till, and it is always right (R-232).

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E33): she serves at the Top Hat in Brim Gap (near (705, -1585)), beside the Brim Gap Road. Her tally is in `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/mountainRoutes/brimGap.ts`.
