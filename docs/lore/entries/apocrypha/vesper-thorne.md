Porcelain-bodied CEO of Thorne Lawn & Order LLC and secret Chair of the HOA, determined to mow Turfton's chaotic charm into a silent, regulation-height estate.

## Journal: Private Compliance Ledger (Entry 091)

> *06:00 — Inspected Patio Heights astroturf. One stray clover leaf detected near Sector 4. Dismissed the night-sweeper.*  
> *11:30 — Visited that dismal tin-shed garage on the west side. The earthenware woman has grease on her apron and a Fescue Seed-Cell in a mower bag. Charming. Gave her forty-eight hours to vacate.*  
> *21:00 — Dusted my porcelain cuffs. Earthenware gnomes think cracks are "character." They are wrong. Cracks are a failure of discipline.*

Unlike Turfton's earthenware locals, Vesper is fired from fine white porcelain—a material that does not forgive a fall. He speaks softly, tips impeccably, and genuinely believes that foreclosing on a family farmhouse is an act of civic kindness.
