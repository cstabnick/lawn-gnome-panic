Porcelain-bodied CEO of Thorne Lawn & Order LLC and secret Chair of the HOA, determined to mow Turfton's chaotic charm into a silent, regulation-height estate.

## Journal: Private Compliance Ledger (Entry 091)

> *06:00 — Inspected Patio Heights astroturf. One stray clover leaf detected near Sector 4. Dismissed the night-sweeper.*  
> *11:30 — Visited that dismal tin-shed garage on the west side. The earthenware woman has grease on her apron and a Fescue Seed-Cell in a mower bag. Charming. Gave her forty-eight hours to vacate.*  
> *21:00 — Dusted my porcelain cuffs. Earthenware gnomes think cracks are "character." They are wrong. Cracks are a failure of discipline.*

Unlike Turfton's earthenware locals, Vesper is fired from fine white porcelain—a material that does not forgive a fall. He speaks softly, tips impeccably, and genuinely believes that foreclosing on a family farmhouse is an act of civic kindness.

## What gnomes say

"Don't let the polite voice fool you. He'll compliment your hat while his lawyers are already measuring your porch for beige paint."

## The truth

Pronouns: Vesper is "he" everywhere (the bible, characters.md, and R-071's "He keeps one"). An older line of this entry said "she"; that was a slip (proposed R-NEW-1).

Vesper is the porcelain HOA chair, head of Thorne Lawn & Order, and builder of the Grand Combine. He wants a silent, even, beige Turfton and the Golden Acorn as the Combine's ignition, and fears cracking (porcelain does not glue back). Home: the penthouse of Thorne Tower; work: Thorne Lawn & Order, the HOA chair, the Double Pot sales floor. Voice: soft, slow, complete sentences, perfect grammar, no contractions, no exclamation marks until the finale PA; he calls people "friend" or by their full name, and his threats are compliments ("Nice hat. It would be a shame if it were to crack.").

Arc: a polite rumour with a lien, named before he is seen (I); the reasonable man with an offer at the diner (#20), then the penthouse viewing (#21) and the raid (#35); the Combine built in four parts, the tasting (#50, the first frame he shares with the player) and the test cut with its broadcast date (#52); in act IV he seizes City Hall (#67), cancels Cam, unravels on the PA and loses his top hat in the finale (stopped, not shattered). A hairline crack at the temple shows from act IV; Clara's photograph at #66 shows only the crack.
