Writes the Underspan's manifesto in chalk on the Unfinished Span's columns, one slogan at a time, and rubs out any that have been shown to be wrong.

## What gnomes say

"The best-read wall in Turfton, and the only one that corrects itself."

## The truth

Vetch writes "A rule is only a prototype nobody tested" at the top of every column and adds a footnote whenever someone tests one. The footnotes now outnumber the slogans.

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto: chalks the manifesto wall along the Underspan's west edge, facing the road.
