Three-time Grand Zero-Turn racing champion, Dandelion Fizz celebrity endorser, and recent lifetime banee of The Gnomecino after driving a mower up the VIP escalator.

## Story: The Escalator Incident

Vinnie lives in a penthouse flowerpot, wears sunglasses indoors, and signs every autograph with a tire skid mark if you let him. His Dandelion Fizz commercials air twice an hour on Turfton FM, usually ending with him jumping a flaming garden hose while screaming about pollen.

Last Tuesday, after Big Lou told him his hat was too damp for the high-roller lounge, Vinnie revved a Razor-Back Zero-Turn straight through the lobby and jammed it halfway up the brass escalator. He still insists the valet parked it there.
