A nine-foot fortress of zip-tied folding patio loungers straddling the Southern Drainage Canal, serving as Baroness Brenda's biker headquarters.

## Inside & Stories

Striped vinyl webbing forms both the throne room walls and the makeshift awning shading thirty parked Push-Mower Bikes. Visitors must surrender one spark plug or recite a formal insult about the HOA before crossing the plank bridge. Below the throne, canal frogs heckle anyone who stalls their engine.

## Ledger & Graffiti

Spray-painted on a lawn-chair armrest: *"NO HOAS. NO LEASHES. JUST LOUNGING."*

## Loose threads

Whether Brenda's zip-ties can survive a direct hit from a Combine Harvester.
