The ford marshal on the Capwood Run: yellow hat, waders to the chest, and a firm statement that cap-water is thick, slow and always fine, and that he is not.

## What gnomes say

"Waders on. Cap-water is thick, slow and always fine. I am not."

## The truth

He stands at the ford in all weathers; the water is a runoff of spore and rain, harmless and slow, and he has never entered it without a wader. The Carburettors refer to him as "the lock keeper".

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (E08): at the ford marker, "cross-arms" idle.
