A reedy camp in Trellisdown, about 400 m west of Loch Moss (not on its shore), at about (-1130, -640), where the Flock of the One Leg keep their tents, their rota and their pink plastic flamingo. It smells of mud and good intentions.

## What gnomes say

"They are very friendly, provided you stand the way they stand."

"You will know it by the pink. Nothing else in the reeds is that pink."

## The truth

The camp sits on a firm gravel spit, which is why the reeds there are tall and the ground is the only dry patch for a mile. The Flock think this is an omen. It is gravel.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). The cult-camp site in the Loch Moss area, with the big tent of Mother Saxifrage Wader at its centre and the Standing Lady on a sand mound before it. Quests 25 (`m25-one-leg-to-stand-on`) and 51 (`m51-the-flock-stands`) are set here. E17 builds the camp: the ritual circle, the big tent, grey washing, the tea urn and the rota board. Quests `sq-flock-errands` and `sq-flock-vigil`; achievements "Birds of a Feather" and "Sat Down" (`gta-birds-of-a-feather`, `gta-sat-down`).
