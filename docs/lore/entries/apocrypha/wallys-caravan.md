A tin caravan on the Desert Airfield apron at (466, -76), its door turned to face the apron, where Wind-Sock Wally keeps his folding chair, his logs, his seeds and his clipboard.

## What gnomes say

"The caravan is not for living in. It is for the logs to live in. Wally is allowed in as the keeper."

"If the kettle is on, the wind is wrong. If the kettle is off, the wind is good."

## The truth

Inside there are three shelves of flight logs, one stool, a pail of sunflower seeds and a kettle, and a stopwatch on a hook. A key hangs round Wally's neck, though nothing in the caravan locks. The caravan was a tour bus once, and is now the one place on the airfield that has never been audited.

## In the games

Act IV, `m54-the-heist-board`: the player drives to the caravan (stage `m54-wally-caravan`, apron arrival (466, -85)) and brings Wally back to Tansy's board. His intro scene is `heist-board-caravan`.

## Everyday

- **Shop:** none. Wally gives away one sunflower seed to anyone who asks for the stall speed.
- **Notices:** a card on the door, "Back in a gust". A second: "Wind: good."
- **Habits:** the chair faces the runway, the binoculars face the dunes, and the clipboard faces nobody.
- **Sayings:** "Wind's nor'-nor'-east. Good."

## Loose threads

What is on the third shelf, behind the logs, and why it is the only one with a lid.
