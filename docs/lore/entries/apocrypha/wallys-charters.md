Wind-Sock Wally's charter business: a plank desk under a striped awning at the Desert Airfield, with a clipboard and a little orange windsock.

## Field Notes

Wally sells a pilot's licence for 150 Acorns, written on a seed-tin lid in the same hand as his flight logs. Then he hands out work: charters to landing pads round the map, scenic loops over Loch Moss, Thorne Tower and Pebblewick, and cargo crates dropped on the Dust Flats. He grades every landing out loud.

## What gnomes say

"Stall speed's 22, kid. Passengers notice."

## In the games

The Pilot job, in any aircraft. Pick passengers up at the airfield stand, fly through the sky gates, land in the zone. A soft, accurate landing scores up to 100; stalls and hard climbs cost comfort. Cargo drops are released with the job action (I, the pad's right stick, or the on-screen button). A blustery sea means storm pay.
