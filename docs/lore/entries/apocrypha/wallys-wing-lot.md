A lot beside the charter desk at the Desert Airfield. [Wind-Sock Wally](wind-sock-wally.md) sells aircraft here, and hangar space.

## Story: Stall Speed, Paid Up

Wally checks every sale against his clipboard: stall speed, fuel, and whether the buyer can say the number out loud. He will sell you a Crop Duster and a hangar in the same afternoon, but never a plane he has not logged.

## What gnomes say

"Wally's lot. Ask for the stall speed before you ask for the price."

## Loose threads

Which of the planes on the lot Wally has flown himself.
