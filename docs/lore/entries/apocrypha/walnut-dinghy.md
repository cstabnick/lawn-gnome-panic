Half a walnut shell with two seats and a pair of oars whittled from lolly sticks. The slowest boat on the water and the first one any gnome learns to row.

## Field Notes & Tales

Every walnut from the Loch Moss glen ends up as a dinghy eventually. The shell is hard enough to float a gnome and a bag of acorns, and light enough to drag over a shallow bit of canal floor. Rowing is two gnomes, one oar each, and they argue about the rhythm the whole way across.

## What gnomes say

"Row it yourself and you'll get there. Let the other gnome row and you'll get there with a sore shoulder and an opinion."

## In the games

Hidden on the shore of Loch Moss and at the canal's wharves. Rowed in deep water at the gnome's own pace; a gnome in a dinghy cannot aim (both hands are on the oars). A gnome who falls in bobs upright beside the dinghy like a fishing float, hollow clay being lighter than it looks (see [rulings.md](../../rulings.md) R-050).

Moored at the Loch Moss dock, the south canal wharf and New Puddle Marina. All five boats count toward the "Captain Clay" achievement: take the helm of every one and earn a Captain's Cap.
