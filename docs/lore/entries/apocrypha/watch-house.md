The Hat Watch station: a plain brick building on the x = 0 road, north of City Hall, where the navy caps come on shift.

## What gnomes say

"Go past the Watch House with a hat on and you'll get a second look. Go past it without one and you'll get a first."

## The truth

The Watch has been unionised since the Pour. Its budget comes from City Hall; its duty roster is kept on a pinboard
in the front hall.

## In the games

Lore only. The Watch House is not built in the game; the Hat Watch's officers come from the police system.
