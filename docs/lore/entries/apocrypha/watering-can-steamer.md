The Loch Moss ferry: a green tin watering can with a funnel for a chimney, a paddle wheel on each side and a bench
across the middle for the passengers. It has run the loch for longer than anyone in the glen can remember.

## Field Notes & Tales

The Steamer goes from the boathouse at the loch's east shore to the reed beds and back again, and it never leaves the loch. Past the weir the current is too strong for a tin can, and the ferryman will not discuss it. Tickets are paid in acorns, handed to the ferryman in the stern, and he counts them twice.

## What gnomes say

"The Steamer doesn't go past the weir. Ask the weir why." Nobody asks the weir.

## In the games

On Loch Moss only. It runs a loop between the boathouse and the reed beds and is the only boat that carries a passenger from one side of the loch to the other without asking.

Moored at the Loch Moss dock. All five boats count toward the "Captain Clay" achievement: take the helm of every one and earn a Captain's Cap.

What the funnel is for, and what the ferryman does with the acorns at the far shore.
