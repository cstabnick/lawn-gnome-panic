The keeper of The Bent Pin: cheerful, quick, exact about centimetres, and firmly of the view that every fish goes back in the water.

## What gnomes say

"Card on the counter, love, and mind the cheese. The fish are very keen on the cheese."
"A hundred and twenty-eight centimetres. That is a Derby prize and a half. Let it go, go on, it has had quite enough excitement."
"Nobody is hurt. The clip is only a bent pin. I have never once seen a fish come out of the canal worse for the day."

## The truth

Wenna wears an oilskin-yellow coat over her blue waders and ties her auburn pigtails back with cord. She measures every fish to the centimetre and writes each card herself, because she holds that a gnome who lands a fish ought to know exactly how long it was, and ought to know that it has gone home.

## In the games

Behind the counter of The Bent Pin, with the rod rack on one side and the bait cooler on the other. She sells rods and bait, buys catch cards for acorns, and runs the Bent Pin Derby. Her lines, one of three picked at random, are always about clips, cards or letting fish go.

## Loose threads

Where she learned to measure to the centimetre. Whether she has ever kept a catch she did not let go.
