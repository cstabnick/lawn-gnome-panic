A wasp-striped scooter that is mostly noise, and the noise is the best part of it.

## Field Notes

The Wespa turns up in the European yard's traffic, buzzing along at a top speed of 26 units an hour. It is two up and one argument: the rider, the passenger, and whichever of them is steering. Nobody has yet agreed which of the three is in charge.

Burl's Forecourt keeps one in the window with a sign that says "Ask about the sound". The sound is very good. The scooter is less so, but it gets there, loudly, and the neighbours always know it has arrived.

## What gnomes say

"It is not fast. It is just loud in a hurry."

## Loose threads

Whether the stripes are there to warn people off, or to make the Wespa look busier than it is. The stripes are painted on by hand and nobody has been brave enough to ask who did them.

Grand Tour is the achievement for driving all five European rides. Its reward is the Tour Beret.
