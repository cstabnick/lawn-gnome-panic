A spectator at the Rutbottom Dirt Bowl: a pale-hatted, brown-bearded regular who has been there since the rain and was told that was the first lap.

## What gnomes say

"I have been here since the rain. I am told that was the first lap."

## The truth

The rain is the only weather the Bowl has. Wilf has not gone home since the first heat, and the heats have run without a break since; the stewards leave him a stool.
