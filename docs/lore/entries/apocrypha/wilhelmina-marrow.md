The allotment champion of the Little Gardens, and the owner of the marrow that won it all.

## What gnomes say

"Wilhelmina's marrow is the size of a barrow. She has the barrow to prove it."

"She does not grow vegetables. She campaigns with them."

## The truth

Her marrow has won so many shows that the committee has started a second prize for everybody else. She is ferocious and fair, and she is the reason the marrow war is never settled: if it were, she would have nothing to win.

## In the games

The largest plot of the Little Gardens (near x -1451, z 640).
