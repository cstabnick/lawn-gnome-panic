A small settlement of fishers living in the weeping willow over the Lily Pond, the Willow haven of DayG's Mossgrave.

## What they are

The Willow Folk hold the high branches of the willow over the water, reached by raft and by the ferry across the pond. They are small and slow to trust, and they know the pond better than anyone. Their hat colour is reed green (`#6f8f5a`), the colour of the reeds at the water's edge.

## How they live

- **Food:** fish from the pond, dried on the branches. Rafts carry it to the shore.
- **Strangers:** watched from the water before anyone is let onto the ferry.
- **Trade:** rafts and the ferry, for a price in whatever a gnome has found.
- **Leadership:** the eldest fisher who still has a steady hand on a pole.
- **Violence:** only on the water, and only when a raft is threatened.

## What they want

To keep the pond theirs: the fish, the reeds and the ferry landing, and a way across that does not need the Drain Mouth's smugglers.

## Loose threads

Where the first fisher came from, and whether the willow was planted or grew there. Who else knows the pond bed is not all silt.
