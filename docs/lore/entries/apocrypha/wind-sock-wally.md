Ancient, sun-bleached gnome in a leather aviator cap sitting in a folding lawn chair at the end of the Desert Airfield runway with binoculars and a clipboard.

Also sells aircraft and hangar space from [Wally's Wing Lot](wallys-wing-lot.md), beside the charter desk.

## Story: Stall Speed's 22

Wally has watched three generations of Spade Guard recruits and brazen city thieves sprint across the alkali pan to steal parked Crop Dusters. He has never once tried to stop anybody. He simply clicks his stopwatch, logs your takeoff speed on his weathered clipboard, and mutters, *"Stall speed's 22, kid, don't embarrass yourself."*

He eats roasted sunflower seeds from a tin pail and claims he can tell the wind direction just by how loudly his beard whistles.

## What gnomes say

"Wally's clipboard has better flight records than Fort Sandpit's command bunker."

## The truth
Wally keeps the Desert Airfield and flies charters; he is the Spade Guard's neighbour and answers to nobody. He wants to log one more perfect takeoff, and fears his knees finally going. Secret: which plane he used to fly. Home: a caravan at the airfield apron; work: Wally's Charters. He is an old friend of Commander Diggs, though neither says so; he gives Pip pilot patter; Hogweed builds the mortar rig for his plane. Voice: dry, gravelly, sing-song numbers, "kid" for everyone; he quotes a stall speed or wind reading as comfort ("Stall speed's twenty-two, kid. Don't embarrass yourself." / "Wind's nor'-nor'-east. Good.").

Arc: absent in acts I-II; the radio voice that talks the player off Rootwell Field (#46); in act IV he is fetched from the apron (#54), lights the runway flares (#55), becomes the coalition's air force (#64 part 2) and gives the finale's air cover.

## Everyday

- **Habits:** a folding lawn chair, a tin pail of roasted sunflower seeds, binoculars and a stopwatch. He logs every take-off he sees and says the stall speed at each one. His caravan is [on the apron](wallys-caravan.md).
- **Sayings:** "Stall speed's twenty-two, kid. Don't embarrass yourself." "Wind's nor'-nor'-east. Good."
- **Food:** sunflower seeds, always roasted, "by the sun, which is not fussy".
