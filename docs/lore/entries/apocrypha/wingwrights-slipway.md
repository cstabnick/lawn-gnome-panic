A travelling paint-and-tune crew for boats and aircraft. The Slipway does not have a garage: it comes to wherever a boat or aircraft is stopped, sets up a sheet of canvas and a tin of paint, and gets to work.

## Field Notes & Tales

Pay 'n' Glaze only takes things with wheels, so the Wingwrights were founded to fill the gap: a hull that floats, a wing that flies. Their work is in three kinds. Engine, Top Speed and Handling are for boats and aircraft alike, and Lift is for aircraft only. Each takes three steps, and each step costs more Acorns than the last. Paint is a single coat at a flat price, in any colour from the Slipway's card.

## What gnomes say

"Stop it on the water, stop it on the apron, stop it in the middle of the Flats. The Wingwrights will find you."
