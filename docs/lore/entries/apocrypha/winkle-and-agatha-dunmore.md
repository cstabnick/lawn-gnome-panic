The couple at the coin telescope: he in a blue hat and glasses who has never been this close to the sea he lives next to, she in a gold hat who has counted the waves.

## What gnomes say

Winkle: "Two acorns to see the sea. I live next to it. I have never been this close." Agatha: "He has been looking at the same wave since Tuesday. It is not a good wave."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (FX11, rulings R-243 and R-248): at the coin telescope on the south rail at (-55.8, 1129.2) and (-53.4, 1129.4), facing the sea; Winkle in a blue hat, cream shirt, grey beard and glasses, Agatha in a gold hat and a purple dress with grey pigtails. Not Winkle Pennant of the beach (R-248). Look: See above.
