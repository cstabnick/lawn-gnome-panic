The child-sized naturalist at the rock pool on the western strand: yellow hat, pink shirt, who has named every crab and been refused by every crab.

## What gnomes say

"I have named all the crabs. They have not agreed to the names."

## In the games

Gnome Theft Auto (FX11, rulings R-243 and R-248): at the rock pool at (-852, 1165), by two crabs and a bucket and spade. Not Winkle Dunmore of the promenade (R-248). Look: A yellow hat, a pink shirt and blue trousers.

## Loose threads

The crabs' own names for themselves, if they have any.
