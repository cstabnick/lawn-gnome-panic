Old money in a wide hat: head of the family whose charter is Turfton's oldest, owner of Trellisdown and secret backer of the HOA's war. She pays for what she has never asked to see.

Home: Brassknock Manor (interior). Work: Trellisdown Vineyard (grape-dew).

## What gnomes say

"The Countess has never been in a shop. The shops come to her and stand on the gravel."

"She smiles at everyone the same distance."

## The truth

Wisteria thinks the Combine is a tidy way to settle a lawn, and has funded it as she would fund a pruning. She has never walked its line. When she learns it runs through her own vines, her manners hold and her opinion changes.

## In the games

Planned (batch 3). Glimpsed on the terrace in quest 17 (`m17-the-dew-buyer`); the host of 50 (`m50-trellisdown-tasting`); the changed ally of 65 (`m65-the-countess-relents`), who gives the crew the key to the Combine's shield grates; at Vesper's side on the terrace in 52 (`m52-the-test-cut`); in the finale of 70 (`ch5-even-cut`).
