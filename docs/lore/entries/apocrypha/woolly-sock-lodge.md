A low timber lodge at the foot of Snowdrop Slope, with a stove, a rack of drying socks and more bunks than guests. Gentian Bootlace keeps it, and it is the crew's base in act III.

## What gnomes say

"Everything at the Lodge is wool and everything is damp. You get used to it by the second night."
"Leave your boots on the rack. Leave your troubles in the stove."

## In the games

The lodge stands at (-449, -1020), front door facing east, 14 m across; the sled stack, the cocoa hatch and the ski rack are on its porch at x -437 (z -1013, -1020, -1027). A polar bear with a mug sits on the porch bench at (-438, -1035). 
Built (batch 3, E12): a log lodge at the foot of the slope with a giant knitted sock over the door (E01 site ski-hill). It rents skis (8 Acorns) and tea trays (4 Acorns), sells warming Hot Cocoa (6 Acorns) and sells a tea tray to keep (30 Acorns). Outside: a sock-drying rack with one enormous Tall-One sock, a heap of lost mittens, a polar bear on the porch bench with a mug of cocoa, and Gentian Bootlace teaching two learners the snowplough. Quest row 37 (m37-snowdrop-slope) ends with Gentian giving the crew a bunk; row 38 (m38-pail-pond) starts here.

## Everyday

**The hatch and rack (porch, x -437).**
- Hot Cocoa, 6 Acorns. "Hot" is a promise, not a rule.
- Skis, 8 Acorns. Tea trays, 4. A tea tray to keep, 30.
- Boots on the rack, free. Troubles in the stove, free. Both are enforced.

**Notices.**
- The door plate: "THE WOOLLY SOCK LODGE. BOOTS ON THE RACK. TROUBLES IN THE STOVE."
- Under it, in pencil: "Bunks: 14. Guests: 3. Socks: more."
- The board by the tow: "LOST & FOUND (MOSTLY LEFT)". Gentian keeps a second board for the right ones. It is bare.

**Sayings and habits.**
- At dusk Gentian counts the socks on the rack aloud, and is never once out.
- House rule: nobody stands with their back to the stove. "It is a stove, not a throne."
- The polar bear on the porch bench gets the first cup of every pot, and has never been asked to pay.

**Small history.** The Lodge was a warming hut for the ski hill's builders, who left in spring and did not take the stove with them. Gentian came for the stove.
