The town reeve of Pebblewick: proud, tidy-bearded, and firm that a town with a bell and a bus does not need a mayor.

## What gnomes say

"Pebblewick is a town, not a stop. We have a bell, a bus and a reeve."

## The truth

Yarrow took the reeve's staff when the last one left for Turfton, and has kept the square swept ever since. He has never once asked Turfton for anything.

Yarrow is the reeve of Pebblewick: town council, neutral until the HOA cuts the water. He wants the pump fixed and the minutes kept, and fears the HOA's cut-off notice. Secret: the town's ledger is thinner than the council knows. Home: the reeve's house on Pebblewick square; work: Pebblewick. Commander Diggs is his old neighbour; Hogweed's pump part earns the town's water; the Weeping Line is his town's sorrow. Voice: formal, patient, council-minutes diction, careful clauses and a thank-you for your patience; his tic is "Noted." ("Noted. The barn is yours, with conditions." / "Pebblewick thanks you. In writing.").

Arc: absent in acts I-III; in act IV offers the hay barn for the Whirligig (#58) and hosts Diggs's medal run (#61).

## In the games

Keeps the square by the bell tower. Gives the player the town's news and sends them to the shops.

## Everyday

- **Habits:** he sweeps the square at dawn and rings the bell once a day. He keeps the minutes in the ledger he carries, and reads the last line aloud before he adds a new one.
- **Sayings:** "Noted." "Pebblewick thanks you. In writing." "A town with a bell and a bus does not need a mayor."
