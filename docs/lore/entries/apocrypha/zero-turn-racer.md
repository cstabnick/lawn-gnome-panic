High-speed Razor-Back street machine (`maxSpeed: 34`, `turnRate: 3.4`) built for drifting frozen birdbaths and outrunning 3-Hat Watch patrols.

## Field Notes & Specs

With twin-lever steering and a screaming orange chassis, the Zero-Turn Racer is the undisputed apex predator of Turfton's asphalt grid. It pivots on a dime, accelerates like a startled squirrel, and turns black ice into a ballet floor—provided the driver doesn't clip a kerb and launch into a ragdoll cartwheel.
