A gnome's hat is who they are. Teams are hat colours, and a gnome without a hat is hiding something.

## What gnomes say

In Fescue the one survivor of the King's Fall says only that the attackers wore *no hats*. Going bareheaded is what
Wildlings do, so the ambush was blamed on them.

## In the games

- Gnome Front: Red Caps against Blue Caps.
- Online games put each player's match colour on their gnome's hat (`{ ...look, hat: player.color }`, `packages/gnome`).
- Gnome Creator: the hat is part of the look every game shows.
