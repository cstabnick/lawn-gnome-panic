Gnomes are small garden folk of fired clay. They are made, not born; they crack instead of bleeding, get glued and
glazed when chipped, and shatter only when it is truly over.

## What gnomes say

In Fescue: the Tall Ones shaped the first gnomes from Rockery clay and fired them in the Kiln. New gnomes are made by
kilnwrights in small town kilns, never as well as the old Kiln made them. A good glaze is a point of pride. Gnomes live
about 150 to 200 years.

## In the games

- Gnomes of Gnomomia: earthenware with a glaze; respawning is being glued back together at a Glaze Shrine.
- Paragnormal: a porcelain shell; "A giant Gnome-Soul shattered your porcelain shell" ends a run.
- The Yard: gnomes crack, tumble and respawn, and never bleed.
- Games draw the shared gnome model from `packages/gnome` (`createGnomeModel`), so gnomes look alike everywhere.
