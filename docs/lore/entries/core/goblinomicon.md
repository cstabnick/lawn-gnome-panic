An old, bark-bound entity and ledger that turns up wherever goblins gather—damp with peat-ink, smelling faintly of cellar-moss, and seeming to remember debts and names that nobody wrote down.

## What goblins and gnomes say

- **In Fescue:** Goblins speak of the Goblinomicon in low voices near the Compost Heap and in the chalk-tunnels of the Stubble-Sappers. Some say it is merely a book of soil-tallies and root-knots; others say it listens when a bargain is struck in the dark and adds a page on its own.
- **In the Yard:** Older goblins behind the Gnomecino claim a battered copy sits under the bar's floorboards, keeping score of every pull-tab debt and wheelbarrow race since the garden was young.
- Gnomes who peek at it see only muddy smudges and pressed fern-fronds; goblins insist the smudges are perfectly clear if you read them by glow-worm light.

## Loose threads

Whether the Goblinomicon is a book written by the first goblins, a living root-entity wearing a bark cover, or something older left behind before the Leaving. More remains to be uncovered.
