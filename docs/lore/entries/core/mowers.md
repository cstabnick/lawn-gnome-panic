Mowers are the great machine of gnome life. Every setting has them, as the terror that cuts the world flat, as a
ride, or both. How tame they are is what sets one setting apart from another.

## What gnomes say

In Fescue there is only one, the Mower (the Turning Blade, Old Rumble), a servant-beast of the Tall Ones that drinks
Black Dew and breathes stink. Nobody there knows what an engine is. In the Yard, a mower is a mower: a danger when it
runs loose, and a kart, a warship or a pet when a gnome holds the wheel.

## In the games

- Lawn Gnome Panic (`sample-3d`): runaway mowers chase the gnome across the lawn.
- Lawn Prix: gnomes race ride-on carts and push-mower bikes.
- Gnome Front: riding mowers and THE COMBINE as vehicles.
- Faster Than Lawn: a mower as a crewed ship with rooms.
- Gnaft: a lawn-raft you mow to grow; better mower tech is found on islands.
- Gnome Golf: a sea mower leaps out of the water like a fish.
- Mower Chain: a growing chain of mowers. Gnomylpics: the Mower Toss.
- Gnomes of Gnomomia: the final boss.

## Loose threads

Why a mower runs with nobody on it. Fescue's answer is a secret kept from its gnomes; other settings don't say.
