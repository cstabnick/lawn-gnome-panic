The giants who made the gardens and the gnomes. They are never seen, never heard and never explained; no human appears
on screen in any game.

## What gnomes say

In Fescue they are also called the Gardeners. They planted the lawn, fired the first gnomes in the Kiln, gave the
Charge ("Keep the lawn"), and one day stopped coming. Every prayer starts "Tall Ones, who sowed us." Their footprints
became lakes.

## In the games

- Gnomes of Gnomomia: only traces (footprint lakes, a trowel the size of a bridge, the Shed).
- Paragnormal: the whole house is theirs, with towering furniture and a pet-door, and nobody is home.

## Loose threads

Who they were, why they left, and whether they will come back. Never answer any of these.
