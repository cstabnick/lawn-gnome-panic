Every gnome world is a garden seen from gnome height: ordinary yard objects are its castles, rivers and mountains,
and gnomes call them by their own names.

## What gnomes say

Each setting has its own words. In Fescue a birdbath is Birdbath Keep, a hose is the Hose (a great green river), the
picket fence is the Fence at the edge of the world, and the garden shed is the Dark House. Nobody there can say what
any of it was for.

## In the games

- Fescue (Gnomes of Gnomomia): the full table of renamed objects is in `docs/design/gnomomia/lore-rules.md` rule 1.
- The Yard: a lawn, a golf island, race courses and a battlefield side by side; gnomes here know a mower is a mower.
- The Tall House (Paragnormal): the garden's other side, inside the house, where furniture towers like cliffs.

## Loose threads

What lies beyond the edge of a garden (the Fence, the sea) is never shown, only set up as the next world.
