The gnome a player makes in the Gnome Creator is the same gnome in every game and every setting. How one gnome turns
up in Fescue, the Yard and the Tall House is a loose thread, never answered.

## What gnomes say

In Fescue the player washed up in Mossbottom's brook three days ago, holding an old trowel with the Clayfoot mark
under the rust, and nobody knows where from.

## In the games

Games load the player's look with `loadMyGnome()` from `packages/gnome`, saved under `gnome.profile.v1`.

## Loose threads

Who the player is and how they travel between settings. Hint at it, never explain it.
