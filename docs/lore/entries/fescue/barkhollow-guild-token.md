An oak token with the Charter of Bough burned into it, given by the hunters' lodge at Barkhollow. Every Hedgewright town honours it, and Barkhollow gives it only to a gnome both sides of the Hedgewood will vouch for.

## What gnomes say

- Its examine line: "An oak token with the Charter of Bough burned in. Every Hedgewright town honours it."
- Barkhollow is neutral ground, so the token is too: the lodge hands one over only when a Hedgewright and a Wildling would both speak for the bearer.

## In the games

- `c5-barkhollow-token`, a blooming trinket sold by Pelt Ashwick, the Barkhollow tanner (Chapter 5).
- It shows in the shop from the start but cannot be bought until the main quest "Fen's Two Families" (`c5-fens-two-families`) is done: Fen's Da at the Clippings and Mum in the hedge are the two sides who vouch (R-045).

## Loose threads

- Why a neutral lodge burns the Hedgewrights' own charter into its token. Old Lichen says the oak was there first, and will not say more.
