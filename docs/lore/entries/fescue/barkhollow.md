Barkhollow is the hunters' lodge in a hollow root of the Hedgewood. It is neutral ground, where hunters deal with both the Crown and the Wildlings.

## What gnomes say

- Boss Alder Splitwell, the Hedgewright foreman, says: "Trees grow back. Gnomes don't."
- Juniper Quickstring, the Huntmaster, says: "I hunt what's hungry before it hunts what's mine."
- Old Lichen, a half-moss Wildling elder, says: "Slow down, sapling. The hedge has been here longer than your worry."
- Old Lichen remembers one of the forty Pipewrights who fled to the Hedgewood after the Backflow, and hid him. The Pipewright lived. A side quest finds his grave.

## In the games

- Services: Shrine, Trainer (Slinger advanced, and beast taming), Inn (The Antler & Acorn) and Tanner.
- Barkhollow is a safe town, with no combat: it is inside the Bounds, and no enemy fights a gnome here outside a called fight (R-041, see `the-bounds`).
