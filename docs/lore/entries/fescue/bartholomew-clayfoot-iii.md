King Bartholomew Clayfoot III ruled after the Dry Summer. He exiled the schismatics, signed the three charters and turned Birdbath Keep into a palace.

## What gnomes say
- After the Dry Summer he signed three charters, each giving a guild a region to work forever: Cloverfield to the Fieldwardens, the Hedgewood to the Hedgewrights, and the Rockery to the Delvers.
- Some gnomes said the drought was the lawn punishing them for cutting it. They let their plots go to dandelion and clover. King Bartholomew Clayfoot III exiled them. They went to the Hedgewood and became the Wildlings.
- He expanded Birdbath Keep into a palace and dug the Drainpipe Deeps beneath it.

## In the games
- The Pedestal of Birdbath Keep is the palace he built. The Archive keeps the charters he signed.
