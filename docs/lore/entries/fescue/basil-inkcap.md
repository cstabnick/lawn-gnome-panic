Archivist Basil Inkcap keeps the records of the Royal Archive. He wants someone to read them.

## Want
Someone to read the records.

## Secret
He owes Tansy fifty-two replies he never got. The post office held her letters. He is delighted when the player fixes it.

## What gnomes say
- The Archive holds the written records of the Charters. Basil will tell you three versions of each event.

## In the games
- He keeps the Archive in the Pedestal of Birdbath Keep.

> "There are three accounts of that. All wrong in interesting ways."
