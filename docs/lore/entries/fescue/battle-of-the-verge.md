The final battle at the Verge. The Mower rolls out of the Shed, bogs in mud, is jammed by a new Stopping Stone, and falls silent when its Heartspark breaks.

## In the games

- Chapter 8, "The Turning Blade" (level 30), is one long quest in three phases.
- Phase 1, Mud: Ida's crew opens the Great Valve, and the Mower bogs in mud (-50% speed for the rest of the fight).
- Phase 2, Jam: at 50% deck HP, Mabel's snail-cart brings the new Stopping Stone. Blade Sweep stops for the rest of the fight.
- Phase 3, Heartspark: strike the Heartspark, with the Heartwood Stake as the special hit every 20 s. The Mower starts to Pull the Rope by itself. When the Heartspark breaks, the Mower falls silent.
- Allies depend on earlier choices: Wildling archers need the peace route, Old Gleaner pins the Rope (+15 s), and Potsworth funds the army if he confessed or was exiled.

## Ending

- The Mower stops. It doesn't explode. It sits there, quiet, for the first time in 3,000 years.
- Fernwick: "My knees have stopped hurting. First time in a month. Thought you should know."
- The title is Bladebreaker.
- The hook: the Mower's Belly tore in the fight, and something that isn't grass is inside. (See the-belly.)

## Loose threads

- After the battle the Mower is stopped, not destroyed.
