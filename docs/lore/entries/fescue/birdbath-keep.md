Birdbath Keep is the capital of Fescue: a royal castle carved into the stone column of a birdbath, with a royal lake on top and a town at its base.

## What gnomes say

- Planting: the Birdbath is the tallest stone in Fescue. Gnorwin Clayfoot built his watchtower on it to watch the Shed.
- Charters: Bartholomew III expanded it into a palace and dug the Drainpipe Deeps beneath it.
- Rumbling: the King died, and the Chancellor has ruled through a child-queen for 30 years.
- Birdbath is an old name nobody can explain.

## The truth

It is a stone birdbath. Its bowl is the Basin, where robins land.

## In the games

- The Pedestal is the castle itself: throne hall, Royal Archive, barracks and the Chancellor's counting room.
- The Basin is the royal lake on the bowl. The Robin Roost is on its rim.
- Pedestal Ward is the town around the base: guild halls of all three charters, the bank, and the stairs to the Drainpipe Deeps.
- Services: Shrine, Smith (the royal armoury), Herbalist, General, three advanced Trainers, Bank, Inn (The Basin Rim), Board, Cart, and Robin flight to Last Tuft late in the game.
- The Keep is a hub with no combat.
- Pedestal Ward is inside the Bounds: no enemy fights a gnome here outside a called fight (R-041, see `the-bounds`).

## Sad note

Queen Marigold has never been allowed to leave the Keep since her father's death. She has never seen the Verge, the Hedgewood or a real farm. She watches the robins land in the Basin and asks visitors what outside smells like.
