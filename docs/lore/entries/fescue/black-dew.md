The gnome word for the drink the Turning Blade needs. Gnome songs say the Mower drinks Black Dew and breathes stink.

## What gnomes say

- The Turning Blade drinks Black Dew and breathes stink. This is in the Planting songs and describes the Mower as an animal.
- Nobody in Fescue knows what Black Dew is for.

## In the games

- No quest, item or zone in the design docs names Black Dew yet.
- For writers: lore-rules rule 1 maps the garden word to petrol. No gnome may say "petrol".
