The broken piece of the Turning Blade. It was knocked off on a rock in the Rockery and is buried there. All good gnome iron comes from it.

## What gnomes say

- The Blade broke a piece off its own blade when it struck a rock in the Rockery.
- Gnomes mine and smelt the piece of the monster. All good gnome iron comes from it.
- Shard iron is the good iron. The Crown wanted valve seals made of it, not clay.
- The Rockery's iron hums. It hums when the Mower moves.

## In the games

- Chapter 6, "The Humming Seam": in the Shard Mine deep seam, you see your reflection in a piece of the Mower. Grout says "Thump. Turn. It's closer."
- Shard-iron gear: the Shard-iron trowel and Shard-iron plate in the Rockery (ilvl 19-24), a Shard-tipped sling, and Gromwell's Last Seal, which is "Shard iron, not clay".
- The Delvers mine the Shard ore, and Shard-iron supply lines help the final battle.
