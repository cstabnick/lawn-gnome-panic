Bramble is the smith of Mossbottom. He only gets scraps of metal, and he dreams of the Rockery's Shard iron.

## Want
Real Shard iron. He only gets scraps.

## Secret
He did his apprenticeship in Flintdelve and was thrown out for drinking. He'd love to go back.

## In the games
- He is the Smith in Mossbottom.
- His old master, Smith Cobalt Anvilroot in Flintdelve, kicked him out and regrets it. A side quest reunites them, and Bramble's shop in Mossbottom upgrades.

> "Bronze? Bronze is for buttons. Bring me Rockery iron and I'll show you a blade."
