The player's rusty trowel. It carries the Clayfoot mark, a footprint in clay, under the rust. Once reforged in Kiln fire, it does +50% damage to the Mower.

## What gnomes say

- Fernwick, on the statue at the well: "Moss was one of the seven, so they say. You've the same trowel. Odd, that."
- Flavour text, once reforged: "It has dug a thousand flowerbeds, and one grave, and it isn't done."

## In the games

- Mossbottom side quest "A Trowel Like Yours": the well statue of the Moss Kiln-Daughter, then talk to Fernwick.
- Chapter 6 side quest "Gnorwin's Trowel": after the Kiln Warden kneels, Smith Cobalt sees the Clayfoot mark and reforges the trowel in Kiln fire.
- The reforged trowel scales with your level and is a Slash weapon, +50% vs the Mower.
