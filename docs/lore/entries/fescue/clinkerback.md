Clinkerback is a centipede as long as a cart, grown fat on the warm rock of the deep seam under the Shard Mine. It is the boss of the Rockery's horn call, the Warm Seam.

## What it wants
The warm deep seam, and to stay near it. Every new Delver cut in the Shard Mine lets the warmth out, and Clinkerback comes to sit in the gap.

## In the games
- It is a horn-call boss at level 24, below the Shard Mine in the Rockery. It is won when Clinkerback dies, after its brood has been cleared.
- Its moves are Coil, a ring that staggers anyone close, and Grit Spray, a cone that chips. It also broods, calling up to four seam lice.
- The seam lice are its adds. They are small, and they want warmth from anything warm.
- Its drops are the Seam pick, the Clinker sling, the Warm-seam wand, the Grit-felt hood and the Centipede-leg charm, each one in five. The Last Tuft anvil reforges them.
