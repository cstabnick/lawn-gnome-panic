Clippings is the Crown's lumber camp on the edge of the Hedgewood. It is named for the huge heaps of cut branches.

## What gnomes say

- The Charter of Bough gave the Hedgewrights the right to log in the Hedgewood, and this camp is their Crown side.
- After the King's Fall, the Crown let the Hedgewrights log twice as hard. Wildlings raid the lumber camps in return.

## In the games

- The camp has log cabins, a sawpit and piles of clippings.
- Services: Shrine, Smith, General, Board and Cart.
- It is a safe town. Hunters at Barkhollow deal with both sides, and so can the player.
- Clippings is inside the Bounds: no enemy fights a gnome here outside a called fight (R-041, see `the-bounds`).
