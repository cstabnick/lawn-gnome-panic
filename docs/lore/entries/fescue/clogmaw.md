Clogmaw is a living blockage of hair, roots and slime. It is the boss of the Gutters, the upper wing of the Drainpipe Deeps.

## What it wants
To flow. It is a blockage that wants to move.

## In the games
- It is a boss at level 12 in the Gutters, the upper wing of the Drainpipe Deeps.
- Its moves are Suction, which pulls the player in, and Hairball, which roots them. It also spawns slime blobs.
- It is weak to Sun (x1.5), like all soft things.
