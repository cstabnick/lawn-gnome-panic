Cloverfield is the flat farm country, levels 11 to 16, where aphid ranches and snail-carts feed Fescue.

## What gnomes say

- Planting: the Last Mowing flattened the plains. Cloverfield is the only flat land in Fescue.
- Charters: the Charter of Root and Seed made it Fescue's breadbasket, run by the Fieldwardens.
- Rumbling: during the Quiet Years the young left for the Keep. Half the ranches are empty, and the Red Ants moved into them.
- Ma Honeydew Haybale says: "An aphid ain't smart, but it's loyal. More than I can say for some."
- Fieldwarden Clover Muckle says: "The Crown wants a tenth. The Crown doesn't plough."
- Mabel Snailwright says: "Slow is safe. Fast is how you end up on the Shorn."

## In the games

- Theme: a gnome Western. Ranchers herd aphids, milked for honeydew (the gnome staple), and snails, which pull the snail-carts. Red ants are rival ranchers, rustlers and squatters. A great crow hunts from above.
- Sub-areas: Honeydew Ranch (11-12), Snail Pens (12, runaway snails to lasso), the Red Anthills (13-15), Scarecrow Rise (14-16) and Crow's Perch (15-16, world boss).
- Sodbury is the farm town here, and the main cart depot.

## Sad note

Sodbury's schoolhouse has one pupil left. The teacher still rings the bell every morning.
