A steaming mound in the rat country of Thatchford Downs, and the one object gnomes name correctly. It is proposed as Snap's next ride, a spiral up the heap.

## What gnomes say

- Fescue's lore rules: "The Compost Heap (gnomes got this one right)".
- Rotgut, Rat of the Heap, rules it from a throne of peelings. Giant earthworms turn it and heap rats call it their kingdom.
- In the cold season gnomes quietly leave a bowl of peelings at the foot of the heap for the rats, though they are at war with them.

## The truth

A heap of kitchen scraps, the only place in Fescue that smells *warm*.

## In the games

A sub-area of Thatchford Downs in Gnomomia (levels 9 to 11). In Snap it is not built; see `docs/design/gnomeokemon/regions.md`.

## Loose threads

Whether the heap is warm because of the rot or because of the rats.
