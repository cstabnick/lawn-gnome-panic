Gnomes are fired clay, and they do not die easily. They crack. A badly cracked gnome is shattered, which is death. Small cracks are glued and re-glazed.

## What gnomes say

- Small cracks are glued and re-glazed. That is the Bloom school of healing: Mend glazes a crack shut.
- Gnomes live about 150 to 200 years. Elder Fernwick, at 212, is the oldest gnome alive.
- Nothing bleeds red. A bleed effect is sap or clay dust.

## In the games

- Respawn: when the player falls, they are carried to the nearest Glaze Shrine (in Mossbottom, the well) and glued back together. NPCs comment: "Cracked again? You're more glue than gnome."
- Side quest "Cracked Pots": collect six clay chunks from the Hose banks for the kilnwright. That unlocks Glaze pots in the shops.
- Glaze pot (item): absorb 10% of max HP for 10 s.
- The Kiln Warden's Heart: once per fight, when you would crack, you are re-fired at 30% HP instead.
