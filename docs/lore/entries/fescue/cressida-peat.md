A Charters-era goblin matriarch of the Sodbury marshes who, with her gnome partner **Kilnwright Alder Burlap**, moulded and cured **Bramble-Grub Burlap-Toe**—the first gnoblin recorded in the Royal Archive.

## What gnomes say

- During **the Dry Summer** (~310 years ago), Sodbury's town kiln cracked in the drought-heat and no new clay could be fired. Cressida taught Alder Burlap to pack wet clay around living reed-roots and cure it in a steaming compost-bed, founding **the Warm Mounds**.
- Archivist **Basil Inkcap** keeps three conflicting ledger-entries of that first Mound, each listing a different week and a different number of reeds.

## Loose threads

Whether Bramble-Grub Burlap-Toe was truly the first gnoblin in Fescue, or simply the first one a Keep clerk bothered to write down.
