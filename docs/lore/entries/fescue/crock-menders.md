A mixed craft guild of gnomes, goblins, and gnoblins based in Thatchford's damp cellar-wards and Sodbury's brick culvert. They lean toward *Tend it*, mending cracked clay with living root-twine and patching dry goblins with thin earthenware splints.

## What gnomes say

- **Rumbling:** Founded in the days after **the Backflow** (31 years ago), when forty Pipewrights were trapped in the flooded lower Deeps and no Glaze Shrine could reach them. Culvert-goblins hauled out survivors and packed their cracks with river-loam until proper glazers arrived.
- Their craft, **root-riveting**, binds shattered edges with willow-twine and potter's slip—uglier than a Shrine glaze, but fast and tough enough to walk home on.
- **Vetch Clayroot** leads the guild and quietly supplies Master Pipewright **Ida Gromwell** with waterproof peat-seals for the upper Deeps.

## Sad note

The Royal Archive still refuses to grant the Crock-Menders a Fourth Charter, so gnoblin apprentices may not wear a guild badge inside Birdbath Keep.
