About 305 years ago, during the Dry Summer, the Crown's gnomes split three ways. The three ideas come from this split.

## What gnomes say

- Some gnomes said the drought was the lawn punishing gnomes for cutting it. They let their plots go to dandelion and clover and refused the Crown's tending laws.
- King Bartholomew Clayfoot III exiled them. They went to the Hedgewood and became the Wildlings ("let it grow").
- A splinter of the Crown's own Groundskeepers decided the Dry Summer came from an uneven lawn, and that the Mower had been holy, a cleanser. They became the Order of the Even Cut ("cut it all").
- The Crown tolerates the Even Cut as cranks. They keep a chapel in Thatchford and a fort on the Shorn, the stubble strip by the Verge.
- The Crown, the Wildlings and the Even Cut all date their founding to the same terrible summer.

## In the games

- Chapter 1 side quest "The Feral Totem": Pip says the Wildlings carve dandelions, but they don't steal.
- Chapter 2 "Brother Sedge's Pamphlets": the first contact with the Even Cut. Do not cut grass at the Neat Chapel.
- Chapter 5 "The Mill Raid": the attackers are Ferals, not Wildlings, a first sign the Wildlings get blamed for others' work.
