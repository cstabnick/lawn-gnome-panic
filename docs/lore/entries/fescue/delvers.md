The Delvers hold the Charter of Stone. They mine the Rockery, and they have smelted the Blade Shard into gnome iron for 300 years.

## The ideas
The sources do not name one of the three ideas for the Delvers. Their charter comes from the Crown, which holds tend it.

## Founding
Bartholomew Clayfoot III signed the Charter of Stone after the Dry Summer, about 300 years ago. It gave the Delvers the mines in the Rockery. Their duty to the Crown is iron, slate and Tall-eyes.

## What gnomes say
- The Delvers found the Blade Shard, and it hums. Miners know they are mining a monster's tooth.
- Forewoman Sax Hammerclod says: "Iron's iron. If it sings while I dig, I dig louder."
- The Shard hummed at the First Rumble. Lately it hums every few nights.

## In the games
- Flintdelve is their mining town, cut into the rock face. Its smiths are the best in Fescue.
- Forewoman Hammerclod stopped the mine three times this year and hid it from the Keep, which would have had to stop the iron tithe.
- The Delvers' sappers join the war camp at Last Tuft.
