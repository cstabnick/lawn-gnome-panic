Dowager Mudtooth is the ancient mole matriarch of the Mudtooth Deeps, Grubthorn's great-grandmother. She remembers the Pipewrights stealing the moles' damp.

## What it wants
Her clan safe from the shaking. She will help if the player brings news of Grubthorn honestly.

## What gnomes say
- The Pipewrights stole the moles' damp tunnels during the Dry Summer, about 310 years ago. The mole grudge against gnomes dates from then. Moles live long too.

## In the games
- She is a mini-boss in the Rockery, at level 23. She is fought only if the player lies to her.
- She is in the Mudtooth Deeps, where the mole clan tunnels are.

> "My great-grandson ran south. You killed him. Tell me true and I'll show you the deep way."
