The Drainpipe Deeps are a dungeon under Birdbath Keep: a maze of pipes, cisterns and valves that the Pipewrights dug to find water.

## What gnomes say

- The Dry Summer: the Crown founded the Guild of Pipewrights to find hidden water. They dug the Deeps and tapped the Tall Ones' old buried pipes. The Deeps can push water anywhere in Fescue.
- The Backflow: the Deeps flooded backward when the seals failed. Forty Pipewrights were trapped below. The lower Deeps were sealed, and they have been full of pests since.
- Ida Gromwell, now Master Pipewright, says: "Water goes where you tell it. Gnomes don't."

## The truth

The Great Valve at the bottom can open the old buried Tall One pipe wide. That is what makes the Valve matter in the final battle. Who turned the Dry Summer tap down is a loose thread.

## In the games

- The Gutters (levels 10-12): opened in Chapter 3 by finding Gnorwin's Ledger. Upper pipes, ankle-deep water, rats and leeches. The boss is Clogmaw, a living blockage of hair, roots and slime.
- The Cistern (levels 18-20): opened in Chapter 5 by finding the old Valve maps. A huge drowned hall with pillars and a sunken Pipewright barracks. The boss is Mother Vesh, a giant leech queen.
- The Backflow (levels 24-25): opened in Chapter 6 by opening the Great Valve. The flooded bottom, full of the forty lost Pipewrights' tools. The boss is Gromwell.
- The player comes back three times and goes deeper each time.
