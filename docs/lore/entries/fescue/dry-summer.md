About 310 years ago the Spigot dropped to a trickle for a whole summer. It is the worst famine in written history.

## What gnomes say

- The lawn browned. Crops failed in the plains, and the moles went deep after the damp.
- The Crown founded the Guild of Pipewrights to find hidden water. They dug the Drainpipe Deeps under Birdbath Keep, a maze of pipes and valves, with the Great Valve at the bottom.
- The moles lost their damp tunnels to the Pipewrights' digging. The mole clans' grudge dates from this. Dowager Mudtooth still remembers it.
- Some gnomes said the drought was the lawn punishing gnomes for cutting it. That idea became the Dandelion Schism.

## The truth

The Dry Summer was not the lawn's anger. A Tall One tap was turned almost off. The Great Valve can open that old buried pipe wide, which is why the Valve matters in the final battle. (Designer's truth in history.md.)

## In the games

- Chapter 5 main quest "The Valve Maps": Ida Gromwell sends you to the Drainpipe Cistern for the maps. She says the Great Valve can bog the whole Shorn in mud.
- Chapter 8, phase 1 of the Battle of the Verge: the Valve opens and the Mower bogs in mud.
