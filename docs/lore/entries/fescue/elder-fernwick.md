Elder Fernwick is the village elder of Mossbottom and the oldest gnome alive, at 212. He sends the player north.

## Want
For Mossbottom to stay small and quiet.

## Secret
He is the last gnome alive who met Bartholomew III's grandson, and he has always believed the Mower is real.

## In the games
- He is the quest hub in Mossbottom. He sends the player north because he knows what Grubthorn's flight means.

> "My knees hurt when the ground shakes. My knees have been hurting a great deal."
