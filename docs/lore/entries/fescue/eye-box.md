A Tall One's camera, found in the Shed's crates, which takes a roll of 24 frames a ride. Nobody in Fescue knows how to make more film.

## What gnomes say

- "Hand me the camera. Gently. It has seen things."
- Gnome Jesus says the crate is deep and that he has stopped counting. He counts at night.
- The camera is carried on the shoulders of an unnamed gnome, who asks for nothing (R-019), and rides in Tag's saddle on a safari.

## In the games

24 frames per ride; Snap, Bait, Pester, Flute, Zoom and Speed are the tools.

## Loose threads

How many rolls are left. Whether the Tall Ones are the ones who will want them back.
