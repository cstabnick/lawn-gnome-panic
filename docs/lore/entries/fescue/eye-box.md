A Tall One's camera, found with its film in a drawer of the Glasshouse's potting bench when the lot came with the glasshouse. It takes a roll of 24 frames a ride, and it is the one Tall One object the Lab has worked out. Nobody in Fescue knows how to make more film.

## What gnomes say

- "Hand me the camera. Gently. It has seen things."
- Gnome Jesus says the drawer is deep and that he has stopped counting. He counts at night (proposed line).
- The camera is carried by an unnamed gnome who asks for nothing (R-019); the photographer stands on that gnome's shoulders, and on a safari the pair ride in Tag's saddle.

## In the games

24 frames per ride; Snap, Bait, Pester, Flute, Zoom and Speed are the tools.

## Loose threads

How many rolls are left. Whether the Tall Ones will want it back.
