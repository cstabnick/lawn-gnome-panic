The Ferals are the gnomes who took the Wildlings' idea too far. They want everything you own.

## The ideas
Let it grow, taken too far. The Ferals took "let it grow" to "take everything".

## Founding
The Wildlings were so bitter after the King's Fall feud that even Mother Burdock threw the Ferals out. She exiled them herself.

## What gnomes say
- Ferals camp outside Mossbottom, with stolen garden furniture and a crude idol of a dandelion.

## In the games
- Feral Camp is a sub-area of Mossbottom at levels 3-4.
- Feral gnomes want everything you own. They drop a red potion, a pot lid and a mallet.
- Feral brutes are rare elites. Some carry a Feral totem, which is part of a quest.
