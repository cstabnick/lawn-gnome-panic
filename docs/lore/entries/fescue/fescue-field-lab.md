Gnome Jesus's two-storey house on a big, well-kept lot at the edge of Fescue, where the garden meets the Yard's town. It is the hub of every Snap ride, and the one place with doors to both the garden and the town road.

## What gnomes say

- It has a red roof, gold trim and a chimney, with a street of parked cars in front and neighbours shoulder to shoulder (R-020).
- Downstairs: the Safari Map Room, the Lobby (Tag on the rug) and the Gnomokedex Archive. Upstairs: the Dressing Room, the Grading Office and the Darkroom, where the player's best prints hang on a clothesline (R-019).
- Out the back is the Glasshouse, his own greenhouse.

## The truth

A Tall One's house stood here. The lot came with a glasshouse.

## In the games

`games/gnomeokemon/src/lab/`. The map table's pins are the rides; the Lobby's door opens onto Tag's Walk.

## Loose threads

Where the street leads. The Fence is not crossed, and the Lab is on the town side of Fescue's edge.
