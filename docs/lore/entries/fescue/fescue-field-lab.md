Gnome Jesus's two-storey house on a big, well-kept lot at the edge of Fescue, where the garden meets the Yard's town. It is the hub of every Snap ride, and the one place with doors to both the garden and the town road.

## What gnomes say

- It has a red roof, gold trim and a chimney. On the town side a street of parked cars runs in front and neighbours stand shoulder to shoulder (R-020); the cars stay on the street, and nobody drives one into the garden.
- Downstairs: the Safari Map Room, the Lobby (Tag on the rug) and the Gnomokedex Archive. Upstairs: the Dressing Room, the Grading Office and the Darkroom, where the player's best prints hang on a clothesline (R-019).
- Out the back is the Glasshouse, his own greenhouse.

## The truth

The lot came with a glasshouse, and in its potting bench the Lab found a camera.

## In the games

`games/gnomeokemon/src/lab/`. The map table's pins are the rides, and one small wobbly clay figure on it always points at the Rockery (the Unfired compass).

## Loose threads

Where the street leads, and how Tag's walk gets from the Lab's door into the walled garden. Snap never shows the way in, and nobody asks.
