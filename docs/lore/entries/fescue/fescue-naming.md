How names work in Fescue. Worlds are named after grasses. Places join two plain garden words. Gnome first names are plants, surnames are soil and tools, and pests get grubby names.

## In the games

- Worlds: Fescue (this one). Later worlds beyond the Fence are named after other grasses, such as Bluegrass, Ryegrass, Zoysia and Bermuda.
- Places: Mossbottom, Thatchford, Sodbury, Flintdelve, Barkhollow, Clippings, Last Tuft. Real English endings (-ford, -bury, -ton, -wick, -delve, -hollow) are good.
- Gnome first names are plants: Fernwick, Tansy, Bramble, Thistle, Marigold, Sorrel, Yarrow, Sedum, Burdock.
- Gnome surnames are soil and household things: Clayfoot, Potsworth, Burlap, Stoutpot, Hammerclod, Splitwell, Haybale.
- Pests and beasts get grubby, simple names: Grubthorn, Mudtooth, Old Gleaner, Clogmaw.
- Titles are garden jobs: Reeve, Edger, Kilnkeeper, Pipewright, Weeder, Groundskeeper.
- A name gets at most one pun. Zero is fine. "Thatchford" is right, and "Mowtown" is too much.
