Fescue is the first world of Gnomomia: one walled lawn, seen from a gnome's height, with the Fence around its edge.

## What gnomes say

- The Tall Ones sowed the lawn from bare dirt. Every gnome prayer starts with "Tall Ones, who sowed us".
- The Tall Ones are never seen. Their traces are huge: footprints that became lakes, and a trowel the size of a bridge.
- The Leaving songs give a dozen reasons for the Tall Ones going. One is that they went to plant another lawn beyond the Fence. Nobody knows.
- Gnomes call the lawn's edge the edge of the world.

## In the games

- The map is seven zones in play order, levels 1 to 30: Mossbottom Vale (1-6), Thatchford Downs (6-11), Birdbath Keep (the hub, no combat), Cloverfield (11-16), Hedgewood (15-20), The Rockery (19-24) and The Verge (25-30).
- The Drainpipe Deeps lie under Birdbath Keep, in three wings.
- The Hose runs from the Spigot in the north-west, down past the Keep, to Thatchford and Mossbottom.
- Cloverfield and the Hedgewood overlap in level, so the player can do them in either order.

## Loose threads

What is beyond the Fence is never answered. The next world is a set-up, not an answer.
