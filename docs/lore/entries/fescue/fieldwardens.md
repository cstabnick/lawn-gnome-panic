The Fieldwardens hold the Charter of Root and Seed. They run farming and ranching in Cloverfield, the breadbasket of Fescue.

## The ideas
The sources do not name one of the three ideas for the Fieldwardens. Their charter comes from the Crown, which holds tend it.

## Founding
Bartholomew Clayfoot III signed the Charter of Root and Seed after the Dry Summer, about 300 years ago. It gave the Fieldwardens Cloverfield to work forever. Their duty to the Crown is grain, honeydew and snail-carts.

## What gnomes say
- The Last Mowing flattened Cloverfield, which is why it is the only flat land in Fescue.
- Ma Honeydew Haybale, the Fieldwarden elder, says an aphid is loyal, which is more than some gnomes are.
- Fieldwarden Clover Muckle says: "The Crown wants a tenth. The Crown doesn't plough." He has been skimming grain to feed the empty farms' old folk.
- During the Quiet Years the young left for the Keep, and the Red Ants moved into the empty ranches.

## In the games
- Sodbury, the farm town of Cloverfield, has a granary tower and a snail-pen corral.
- Muckle's hidden grain store is the peaceful way to feed Old Gleaner.
- Fieldwarden snail-carts join the war camp at Last Tuft.
