About 32 years ago, on a still night, all of Fescue heard a sound like a giant clearing its throat, once, from the north. The next morning the Shed had a new crack in its door.

## What gnomes say

- Cups fell off shelves in Thatchford.
- Miners in the Shard Mine said the iron hummed.
- The Even Cut calls it the Saint Stirring, and doubled its numbers in a year.
- The Crown sent King Gnorbert Clayfoot to the Verge to inspect the Shed. He found the door cracked but holding, and ordered it re-barred with Rockery iron.

## In the games

- Chapter 6 "The Humming Seam": reach the Shard Mine deep seam and interact with the mirror-bright Shard wall. Grout says "Thump. Turn. It's closer."
- Chapter 6 "A New Stopping Stone": the front gate collapsed in the First Rumble, so the only other way in is the moles' deep way.
