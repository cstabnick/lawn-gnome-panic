Flintdelve is the mining town cut into the Rockery's rock face. It has the Delvers' charter-hall and the best smiths in Fescue.

## What gnomes say

- Forewoman Saxifraga "Sax" Hammerclod, head of the Delvers, says: "Iron's iron. If it sings while I dig, I dig louder."
- Grout, a blind old miner, hears the Mower from the deep seam. He says: "Thump. Turn. Thump. It's further along than last month."
- Smith Cobalt Anvilroot, a master smith, says of his former apprentice: "He had good hands and a bad thirst." Bramble the Smith trained here, and was thrown out for drinking.
- Kilnkeeper Sedum says: "It's never been lit in my lifetime. Or my mother's. Or hers."

## In the games

- A mining town with lift-baskets on pulleys, slate roofs and the Delvers' charter-hall.
- Services: Shrine, Smith (Shard-iron gear, the best in Fescue), General, Trainer (Warden advanced), Bank, Inn (The Hot Seam), Board, Cart, and a leaf-raft dock.
- The leaf-raft is a one-way ride down the Hose to Thatchford.
- A side quest reunites Bramble with Cobalt. Bramble's shop in Mossbottom then upgrades.
