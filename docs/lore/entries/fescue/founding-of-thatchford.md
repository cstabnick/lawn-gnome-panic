About 290 years ago a market grew where the Hose leaks across the road. It became Thatchford, the only ford between the south villages and the Keep, and it lives by trade.

## What gnomes say

- Grain from Cloverfield, timber from the Hedgewood and iron from the Rockery all crossed the ford.
- Thatchford is a crossroads town and lives by trade.

## In the games

- Thatchford Downs, Chapter 2: the Reeve, Burlap, brushes off the warning with "Carts. It's always carts."
- The Tremor Tally: Rye Grindwell's mill wheel, where water beetles nest, and 31 years of marks.
- "Pressure's Wrong": the Spinning Fountains spray at random because the Deeps' pipes have leaked pressure since the Backflow.
