A Glaze Shrine is where a cracked gnome is carried and glued back together. It is the lore reason the player respawns after a fall.

## What gnomes say

- Gnomes do not die easily. They crack. A small crack is glued and re-glazed. A badly cracked gnome is "shattered", which is death.
- The Bloom school of healing is glazing. To Mend is to glaze a crack shut.
- Gnomes say: "Cracked again? You're more glue than gnome."

## In the games

- When the player falls, they are carried to the nearest Glaze Shrine and glued back together.
- Every town has a Shrine service. In Mossbottom it is the well. In Thatchford it is the fountain in the square.
- The Shrine in Birdbath Keep is also a respawn point.
