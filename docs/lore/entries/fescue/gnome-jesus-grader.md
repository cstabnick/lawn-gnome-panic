The robed, haloed, bearded owner of the Fescue Field Lab, who grades every photograph and is also Gnomokedex #036. He is warm, witty and vain, and owns the Lab's lot outright. Not a god, and not Gnome Company's Omnipresent Gnome Jesus (R-005, R-030).

## What gnomes say

- "His eyes follow you round a room." "He knows your score before you have framed the shot." "He says he is three thousand years old." "The halo is a Tall-eye."
- Mossbottom gnomes remember a bearded gnome in a white robe sketching in the vale the week after the First Rumble. He asked every slug what it wanted.
- He says of himself: "I look at everyone. It is a courtesy, and also an audit."
- His name is a maker's mark stamped on his foot, like every gnome's. He did not choose it. He says the Kiln "was having a day".

## The truth

He stares at the player for three reasons. They hold the Eye-Box, the only thing in Fescue that makes pictures like a Tall One's. Every Gnomokedex page has a photograph except his. And he is posing: same spot every night in his own Glasshouse, facing the lens, afraid of Bronze. His surprise at his own photograph is theatre.

## In the games

- Grades photos in the Grading Office with the muttering voice of the Blackjack gnomes (`@lawn/mutter`, 84 Hz). His lines are in `games/gnomeokemon/src/snap/jesus.ts`.
- Gnomokedex #036, a Mythic at the end of the Starlight Greenhouse ride (the Glasshouse). He drives a gold car with a halo over the roof (R-020).

## Loose threads

How old he is: "three thousand years" is in his lines. The songs say there were seven Kiln-born and agree on two. Nobody says he is the eighth. Who made him. Whether the halo is a Tall-eye.
