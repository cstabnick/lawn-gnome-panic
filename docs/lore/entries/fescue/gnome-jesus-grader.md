The robed, haloed, bearded owner of the Fescue Field Lab, who grades every photograph and is also Gnomokedex #036. His given name is Hyssop; "Gnome Jesus" is the street name for the robe, the beard and the halo. He is warm, witty and vain, and owns the Lab's lot outright. Not a god, and not Gnome Company's Omnipresent Gnome Jesus (R-005, R-030).

## What gnomes say

- "His eyes follow you round a room." "He knows your score before you have framed the shot." "He says he is three thousand years old." "The halo is a Tall-eye."
- Gnomes at the edge of the lawn remember a bearded gnome in a white robe sketching, the week after the First Rumble. He asked every slug what it wanted.
- Proposed lines (not in `jesus.ts` yet): "I look at everyone. It is a courtesy, and also an audit."

## The truth

He stares at the player for three reasons. They hold the Eye-Box, the only thing in Fescue that makes pictures like a Tall One's. Every Gnomokedex page has a photograph except page thirty-six, his. And he is posing: same spot every night in his own Glasshouse, facing the lens, afraid of Bronze. His surprise at his own photograph is theatre.

## In the games

- Sits behind the desk in the Grading Office and faces the player in the grading panel; stands in the Lobby. His voice is the muttering one of the Blackjack gnomes (`@lawn/mutter`, 84 Hz). His lines are in `games/gnomeokemon/src/snap/jesus.ts`.
- Gnomokedex #036, a Boss (the dex calls him a Mythic), at the end of the Starlight Greenhouse ride (the Glasshouse). He owns a gold car with a halo over the roof, parked on the street on the town side (R-020).
