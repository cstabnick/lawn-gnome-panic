Gnome Jesus's life's work: a book of every Gnomokemon he has framed, one page each, with what it wants. Every page has a photograph except the last.

## What gnomes say

- Gnome Jesus says: "A record is a promise to look again."
- Players add their own lines to a page ("Gnome Jesus says"), kept in the player's browser and mixed in with his.

## In the games

- The Archive (downstairs in the Lab) holds the shelves, the cases and the book; 42 pages in dex order.
- Each page has a rank, an element, a "family", a "wants" line and (proposed) a one-word temperament with a two-line hint in Jesus's voice.
- Page 036 is his own, and has no photograph until the player takes one (see `gnome-jesus-grader`).
