King Gnorbert Clayfoot went to the Verge to check the Shed's iron bars. His escort was ambushed on the Shorn on the way home, and the King was shattered past mending.

## What gnomes say
- After the First Rumble, the Crown sent King Gnorbert to the Verge to inspect the Shed. He found the door cracked but holding, and he ordered it re-barred with Rockery iron.
- The Crown says Wildlings did the ambush. The Wildlings say the Even Cut did it.
- The one survivor, Rowan Ironsod (then a squire), says the attackers wore no hats, and he won't say more.
- The snail-cart he rode to the Verge was built by Mabel Snailwright. It came back empty.

## The truth
The ambush was the Even Cut, led by a young Edger named Sedgewick. They went bareheaded so the attack would be blamed on Wildlings. See [Sedgewick](sedgewick.md).

## In the games
- The player can earn the proof in the Verge chapter, in the quest "No Hats".
- The King's cart is still on the Shorn where he fell. The Even Cut keep the grass around it mowed perfectly, as a trophy.
