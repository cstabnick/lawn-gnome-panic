Gnorwin Clayfoot is the first king of Fescue, the best-known of the seven Kiln-born. He threw the Stopping Stone into the Mower's blade and raised a Keep on the Birdbath to watch the Shed.

## What gnomes say
- The songs call him the first king. The songs never agree on the other six Kiln-born.
- He rode out with a stone carried up from the Kiln, the Stopping Stone, and threw it into the Blade's mouth. The Blade jammed and fell silent.
- The Kiln-born rolled the Mower back into the Shed and barred the door. Gnorwin raised a Keep on the tallest stone in Fescue, the Birdbath, to watch the Shed forever.

## In the games
- Birdbath Keep is his watchtower. The Pedestal, the Basin and the Robin Roost all stand on it.
- Chapter 3 of the main story sends the player to find Gnorwin's Ledger, which opens the Gutters of the Drainpipe Deeps.
- The trowel the player washes up with bears the Clayfoot mark (a footprint in clay) under the rust.

## Loose threads
- Whether the player is a lost Clayfoot, or a Kiln-born gnome fired when the Mower stirred, is left as a hint only.
