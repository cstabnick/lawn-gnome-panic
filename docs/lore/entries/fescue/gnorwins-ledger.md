Gnorwin Clayfoot's own ledger. It was kept in the old vault, the vault flooded in the Backflow, and it now lies in the Drainpipe Gutters, "under everything". Its key line sets out the plan to save the lawn.

## What gnomes say

- Archivist Inkcap: the ledger was moved to the old vault, which flooded in the Backflow. "It's in the Gutters now. Under everything."
- The ledger's key line, which the finale quest is built on: "Water to bog its feet. Stone to stop its mouth. Wood to still its heart. And carts and hands enough to bring them."

## In the games

- Chapter 3 "Three Accounts": interact with three records in the Archive, then talk to Inkcap.
- Chapter 3 "Into the Gutters": reach the Drainpipe Gutters, kill Clogmaw, and collect Gnorwin's Ledger.
- Chapter 3 "What the Ledger Says" (finale): the key line sets up the rest of the game. It is the plan the player carries out, with water from the Pipewrights, timber from the Hedgewrights, stone from the Delvers and carts from the Fieldwardens.
