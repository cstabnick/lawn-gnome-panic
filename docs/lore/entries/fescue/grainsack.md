Grainsack is a field mouse who got into the ranch silos at Honeydew Ranch, grew to the size of a hay bale, and named himself king of the granary. He wears a bottle cap for a crown. Nobody in Cloverfield has been brave enough to say it is not a crown.

## What it wants
Winter stores. The farms are bare, and the silos are the last full grain in the valley. He calls his harvest mice when he needs carrying, and they carry everything they can reach.

## In the games
- The Honeydew Silo Raid is a horn call (see [horn calls](horn-calls.md)) open once Chapter 2 is done. The horn post stands on the ground south of the silos.
- Grainsack is the boss, spawned at the centre of the arena. Harvest mice come in three waves while he fights.
- His moves are Belly Flop (a ring that staggers whoever is close), Chaff Cloud (a slowing pool) and Call the Granary (he summons three more harvest mice).
- A win drops one of five pieces of horn-marked gear, one time in five each. The Clippings smith can reforge any of them.
