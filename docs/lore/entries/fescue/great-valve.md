The Great Valve is the Pipewrights' main valve, at the bottom of the Drainpipe Deeps under Birdbath Keep.

## What gnomes say

- The Dry Summer: the Pipewrights dug the Deeps, and the Great Valve sits at the bottom.
- The Backflow: the Crown ordered the Pipewrights to flood the Shorn through the Great Valve. Lord Chancellor Wendel Potsworth had the valve seals made of clay instead of Shard iron, and they failed.
- Master Pipewright Gromwell stayed at the Great Valve to close it by hand, so the water would not reach the Keep. He was never seen again.
- Ida Gromwell, his daughter, says: "Water goes where you tell it. Gnomes don't."

## The truth

The Valve can open the old buried Tall One pipe wide. That is what makes it matter in the final battle.

## In the games

- The Valve is opened in Chapter 6, which unlocks The Backflow wing of the Deeps.
- Gromwell, the cracked Pipewright still at his post, does not attack until the player touches the Valve.
- Defeat him, or let Ida speak to him if she came along. Either way he lets go and says: "Is it dry up there? Good. Good."
