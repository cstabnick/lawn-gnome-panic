Grubthorn is the Mole King who tunnels under Mossbottom. He is not attacking the village. His clan is running south from the shaking ground.

## What it wants
To lead his clan south, away from the shaking.

His last words are: "We weren't attacking. We were running."

## What gnomes say
- Moles feel the ground before gnomes do. The mole clans are fleeing south, and Mossbottom is in the way.
- Elder Fernwick sends the player north because he knows what Grubthorn's flight means.

## In the games
- Grubthorn's Burrow is a level 6 fight in Mossbottom. The places sheet calls it an elite. The bestiary ranks him a boss. Both are in the sources.
- His moves are Burrow (he vanishes for 3 seconds and erupts under the player, with a ring telegraph), Mudsling (ranged, Slow), and Call the Clan at 50% health, which brings two moles.
- He drops the Mole crown and the Mole King claw.
- The fight collapses his burrow after it ends, and that opens the north road.
