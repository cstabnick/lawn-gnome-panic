The gnome word for the Mower's spark plug, which is the heart that keeps it running. In the final battle it is the last thing to break.

## In the games

- Battle of the Verge, phase 3: strike the Heartspark (Rain x2, and Gnorwin's Trowel +50%). The Heartwood Stake's special Drive the Stake hit comes every 20 s.
- The Heartwood Stake breaks the Heartspark. Its quest item comes from Chapter 5, Hedgewood.
- When the Heartspark breaks, the Mower falls silent.
- The Heartspark Sling (Slinger's Kiln-fired legendary) fires rust-acorns that chain to a second target. It takes the name, not the meaning.
