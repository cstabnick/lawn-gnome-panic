The Heartwood is the core of the Elder Privet, the oldest hedge in the Hedgewood. The Wildlings hold it sacred.

## What gnomes say

- The Hedgewood is the only uncut land in Fescue, and the Wildlings call it holy.
- Boss Alder Splitwell wants the Heartwood felled.
- Mother Burdock wants the logging stopped forever.

## In the games

- The Heartwood is in the core of the Elder Privet.
- The player needs one branch of it for the great stake that breaks the Mower's Heartspark.
- Chapter 5 offers two ways to get it. The player can help Splitwell fell it: this is fast, angers the Wildlings, and there are no Wildling archers in the final battle. Or the player can kill Queen Vespa for Burdock, broker the peace and be given a branch: this is slower and gives better rewards, and Wildling archers join the final battle.
- The main story suggests peace but does not force it.
