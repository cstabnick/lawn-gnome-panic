The Hedgewood is a hedge forest in the north-east, levels 15 to 20, where Hedgewrights log and Wildlings hold the only uncut land in Fescue.

## What gnomes say

- Planting: the Mower could not cut the hedges, so they are the only uncut land in Fescue. Wildlings call them holy for it.
- Charters: the Charter of Bough gave the Hedgewrights the right to log here, on top of the Wildlings' land. That fight has gone on for 300 years.
- Rumbling: after the King's Fall was blamed on the Wildlings, the Crown let the Hedgewrights log twice as hard. It is an open war now.
- Boss Alder Splitwell says: "Trees grow back. Gnomes don't."
- Mother Burdock, the Wildling chieftain, says: "You cut, you call it tending. We grow, you call it wild."

## In the games

- Theme: gnome lumberjacks against forest folk, with hunters caught between. Dark hedge tunnels, spiders and a wasp tower.
- Sub-areas: Clippings and Barkhollow (safe towns), Sawdust Mill (15-16), Webhollow (16-18, the Weaver Below), Burdock's Tangle (neutral or hostile), the Paper Spire (18-20, a wasp mini-dungeon) and the Elder Privet, the oldest hedge.
- The zone's question is whether the player cuts the Heartwood or is given it.

## Sad note

Wildling children carve their names into the Elder Privet's bark. The oldest names are 300 years old. The newest belong to the children the Hedgewrights' falling trees killed.
