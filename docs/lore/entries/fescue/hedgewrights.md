The Hedgewrights hold the Charter of Bough. They log the Hedgewood, in a war with the Wildlings that has gone on for 300 years.

## The ideas
The sources do not name one of the three ideas for the Hedgewrights. Their charter comes from the Crown, which holds tend it.

## Founding
Bartholomew Clayfoot III signed the Charter of Bough after the Dry Summer, about 300 years ago. It gave the Hedgewrights the right to log the Hedgewood, and the forest was where the Wildlings had just settled. Their duty to the Crown is timber, game and bowstring.

## What gnomes say
- The Hedgewood charter is the sore one.
- After the King's Fall was blamed on the Wildlings, the Crown let the Hedgewrights log twice as hard. It is an open war now.
- Hedgewright stamps are still visible on the logs in the Beetle Barrows. The log-dumping 30 years ago filled the barrows with rotting wood.
- Boss Alder Splitwell, the Hedgewright foreman, says: "Trees grow back. Gnomes don't."

## In the games
- Clippings is their lumber camp on the Crown side of the Hedgewood.
- Hedgewright thugs are hostile only on the Wildling route.
- The Paper Spire is a wasp tower that both Wildlings and Hedgewrights want gone.
- Splitwell's route to the Heartwood is the fast option. It angers the Wildlings and removes them from the final battle.
