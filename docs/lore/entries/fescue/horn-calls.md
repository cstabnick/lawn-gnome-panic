A horn call is a fight too big for one gnome. A Reeve hangs a horn on a post where a pest has grown too big or too many; whoever blows it gets every Wayfarer in earshot.

## What gnomes say

- A Keep sergeant says: "One blast and they come running. Two blasts and they go home."
- A Thatchford carter grumbles that the Wayfarers come for the horn-marked gear, not the town: "Nobody ever blew a horn for the potatoes."
- A Reeve's clerk says: "The horn is for the beast. The gear is for whoever is still standing."

## The truth

Horn posts are left by the Reeves where a pest has settled and will not be moved by one gnome's patience. The horn itself is old and nobody remembers who first carved it, but it works for anyone who can lift it.

## In the games

Raids in Gnomes of Gnomomia (`src/raids.ts`), open once Chapter 2 is done (A Letter for the Queen, Thatchford). There are five horn posts, one per later zone: Birdbath Keep (below the Archive), Cloverfield (Honeydew Ranch), the Hedgewood (under the Elder Privet), the Rockery (below the Shard Mine) and the Verge (the western Shorn). Up to three Wayfarers answer a blown horn: a Warden holds the beast, a Hedge-Mage mends, a Slinger shoots (`src/raidHelpers.ts`). A gnome's own party takes their places once parties exist. Each piece of horn-marked gear falls one time in five (see R-138).
