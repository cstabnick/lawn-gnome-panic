Ida Gromwell is the Master Pipewright and keeper of the Drainpipe Deeps. Her father was lost in the Backflow.

## Want
Her father found.

## Secret
She has never forgiven the Crown for the Backflow.

## The truth
She does not know that the valve seals were cheap. When she learns, she wants Potsworth shattered. The player's choice can calm her, or not.

## In the games
- She is keeper of the Drainpipe Deeps, reached by the stairs in the Pedestal Ward.
- If she comes with the player to the Backflow, she can speak to her father. The talk-down option with her present skips the second phase of the Gromwell fight.

> "Water goes where you tell it. Gnomes don't."
