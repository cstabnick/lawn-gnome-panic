Ironhorn is an old stag beetle who holds a rutting ground under the Elder Privet in the Hedgewood, and who will not give it up to the Hedgewrights' timber crews.

## What gnomes say

- A Hedgewright axeman says: "He's been down there since before my grandmother's grandmother. Every spring we clear the lawn and every spring he's back, with more sons."
- A Wayfarer who has fought him says: "He doesn't want the hedge. He wants the hollow. Give him the hollow and he'll go to sleep."
- A Clippings clerk says: "The Charter says we may log the Hedgewood. It doesn't say what to do about the stag. Nobody asked the stag."

## The truth

Ironhorn is a stag beetle grown very large on the warm sap under the Elder Privet, and he is old enough to remember when the hollow was his alone. The young stags he calls up are his sons, sent out to prove themselves against the Hedgewrights' axes. He is not evil. He is defending a patch of ground he had first.

## In the games

The boss of the Hedgewood horn call (`raid-hedge`, src/data/packs/raid-hedge.ts), open once Chapter 2 is done. He charges down a lane, sweeps the front with his horns, and calls two young stags out of the leaf litter with a Rut Call. The arena sits under the Elder Privet, next to the horn post. Each piece of gear dropped from his arena is horn-marked (see `horn-calls`).
