The Kiln-born are the seven gnomes the Kiln fired first, in the Planting. They were said to be stronger than gnomes made since. None are known to be alive.

## What gnomes say
- The Gardeners shaped the first gnomes from Rockery clay and fired them in the Kiln. Seven came out first.
- The best-known is Gnorwin Clayfoot, the first king. Mossbottom claims one, Moss, and every village claims one of the seven. The songs never agree on the other six.
- The Kiln-born rolled the Mower back into the Shed and barred the door.

## The ideas
The sources do not give the Kiln-born one of the three ideas. The Planting songs only say they were the first gnomes.

## In the games
- The Kiln guardians in the Kiln are fired-clay golems left by the Kiln-born. They guard the Kiln, as the Kiln-born ordered.
- Mossbottom's well holds the statue of Moss.
