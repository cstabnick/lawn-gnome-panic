Sedum is the Kilnkeeper of Flintdelve, a hereditary job that is mostly ceremonial. The Kiln lit itself four nights before the player arrived, and she is terrified.

## Want
To know who lit the Kiln.

## The truth
She saw the Kiln burning, and she saw a shape walk out of it toward the Spigot. She has told nobody. She tells the player only near the end.

## In the games
- She visits the Unfired, the clay gnomes in the Kiln's back chambers, every evening.
- Beating the Kiln Warden lets her fire the new Stopping Stone.

> "It's never been lit in my lifetime. Or my mother's. Or hers."
