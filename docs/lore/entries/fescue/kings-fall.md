About 30 years ago King Gnorbert's escort was ambushed on the Shorn, on the way home from the Verge. The King never recovered, and the Crown and the Wildlings have been open enemies ever since.

## What gnomes say

- The King went back to the Verge to check his iron bars. On the way home his escort was ambushed on the Shorn.
- The Crown says Wildlings did it. The Wildlings say the Even Cut did it.
- The one survivor, Knight-Commander Rowan Ironsod (then a squire), says the attackers wore no hats. He won't say more.
- The King was shattered past mending.
- His daughter Marigold was crowned at eight. Chancellor Potsworth ruled as regent for 20 years and still runs most of the realm.
- The Crown and Wildling feud became open hatred. Hedgewrights log harder, and Wildlings raid lumber camps.

## The truth

The ambush was the Even Cut, led by a young Edger named Sedgewick. He wanted the Crown's iron off the Shed door. They went bareheaded so the blame would fall on Wildlings, who often go bareheaded. Sedgewick is now High Edger and an adviser at court. Rowan suspects, but has no proof. (Designer's truth in history.md.)

## In the games

- Chapter 7 "No Hats": the King's cart on the Shorn, the King's Signet, and Brother Sedge. Ironsod admits "I hid under the cart, and I knew."
- Chapter 7 "The Level": you kill High Edger Sedgewick.
