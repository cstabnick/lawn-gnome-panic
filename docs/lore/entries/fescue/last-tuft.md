Last Tuft is the forward war camp on the Verge. It is the last tuft of uncut grass before the Shorn, turned into a camp for the final muster.

## What gnomes say

- Knight-Commander Rowan Ironsod says: "This time I don't get under the cart."
- Queen Marigold, when she arrives, says: "So this is what outside smells like. Stubble and fear."
- Brother Sedge, a defector from the Even Cut, says: "I wanted an even lawn. I didn't want an empty one."

## In the games

- The camp fills up as the player recruits each region: Delver sappers, Fieldwarden snail-carts, Wildling archers (or Hedgewright axes), Pipewrights at the Valve pipes, Keep soldiers, and the Ant Thousand if the player made peace.
- Services: Shrine, Smith, Herbalist, General, Robin flight, and a War Table that shows which allies have joined.
- Queen Marigold arrives in Chapter 7 to lead the final muster.
- If the player spared Brother Sedge in the "No Hats" quest, he defects and gives the player the layout of the Level.
