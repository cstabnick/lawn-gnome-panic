Master Pipewright Gromwell stayed at the Great Valve during the Backflow to close it by hand, so the water would not reach the Keep. He is Ida Gromwell's father.

## What gnomes say
- He was never seen again. The lower Deeps were sealed, and they have been full of pests ever since.
- The Keep's sheet calls him the cracked, rust-bound Pipewright still at his post after 30 years.

## Want
To keep the flood from the Keep, still, 30 years on.

## The truth
He isn't evil. He has held the Valve shut for 30 years and thinks the player is the flood. The Backflow sheet says he finally lets go if the player defeats him, or if Ida speaks to him. He says one last line: "Is it dry up there? Good. Good."

## In the games
- He is the boss of the Backflow, the bottom wing of the Drainpipe Deeps (levels 24-25). He does not attack until the player touches the Valve.
- His boss moves are the Valve Wrench, Pressure Burst and Seal the Room at 30% health.

The history says he was never seen again, while the game puts him back at his post in the Backflow. Both are in the sources.
