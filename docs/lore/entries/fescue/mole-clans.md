The mole clans dig the tunnels under Fescue. Right now they are fleeing south from the shaking ground, and their path runs through Mossbottom.

## The ideas
The sources do not give the mole clans one of the three ideas. Moles want to be safe from the shaking, and to get away.

## What gnomes say
- Moles feel the ground before gnomes do. The clans are fleeing south away from the Mower's growing shudder.
- The Pipewrights stole the moles' damp tunnels during the Dry Summer, and the grudge dates from then.
- Grubthorn's dying line was: "We weren't attacking. We were running."

## In the games
- The player fights Grubthorn in Grubthorn's Burrow, in Mossbottom.
- The Mudtooth Deeps are the clan tunnels in the Rockery. Deep moles are neutral if Mudtooth trusts the player, and hostile if not.
- Dowager Mudtooth, Grubthorn's great-grandmother, will show the player the deep way if the player is honest.
