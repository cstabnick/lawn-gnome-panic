Moss is the carved figure on Mossbottom's well, Mossbottom's claim to one of the seven Kiln-born. Every village claims one.

## What gnomes say
- Mossbottom's song, sung at every harvest, names a gnome called Moss. The songs never agree on the other six Kiln-born.
- Every village claims a Kiln-born gnome of its own. Mossbottom's claim is the statue on the well.

## In the games
- Her carved figure is the well in Mossbottom, the start of the game.
- The player may Examine it: "Her face is worn smooth. She is holding a trowel just like yours."
