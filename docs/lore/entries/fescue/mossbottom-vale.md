Mossbottom Vale is the starter zone, levels 1 to 6: a footprint hollow where the first village sits.

## What gnomes say

- Planting: a Tall One knelt in the vale to plant the first mushrooms. That is why Mossbottom's houses are mushrooms.
- Mossbottom's song names a gnome called Moss, one of the seven Kiln-born. Every village claims one.
- Charters: the vale was never chartered and never taxed much. It is a backwater that grows its own food.
- Rumbling: the vale was barely touched by the Rumbling, until now.
- Feral gnomes camp outside Mossbottom. Mother Burdock threw them out of the Wildlings after the King's Fall feud.

## In the games

- Sub-areas: Dewdrop Meadow (levels 1-2, slugs and moles), Puddlemere (2), Feral Camp (3-4), Beetle Barrows (4-5) and Grubthorn's Burrow (6, elite boss).
- Mossbottom is the start, and the town where the player is classless until Thatchford.
- Grubthorn's collapse after the fight opens the north road to Thatchford Downs.

## Sad note

Old Thistle has swept the path for forty years. He started when his wife Primrose took work in the Keep's waterworks, and she was lost in the Backflow nine years later. He never stopped sweeping, and he never says why. Tansy will tell you if you ask her about him.
