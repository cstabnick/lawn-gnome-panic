Mossbottom is the starting village of Fescue: five mushroom houses around a well, in the Mossbottom Vale.

## What gnomes say

- Elder Fernwick, the village elder, says: "My knees hurt when the ground shakes. My knees have been hurting a great deal."
- Bramble the Smith says: "Bronze? Bronze is for buttons. Bring me Rockery iron and I'll show you a blade."
- Tansy the Herbalist says: "If it's green, I can boil it. If it's purple, I won't."
- Captain Pip, the guard, says: "Halt! ...Oh. Carry on. Halt *politely*, I meant."
- Mossbottom's carved well figure, the Moss Kiln-Daughter, is one of the seven Kiln-born. Her face is worn smooth, and she holds a trowel just like the player's.

## In the games

- Services: Shrine (the well), Smith (Bramble), Herbalist (Tansy) and a Board, added after the Mole King. There is no trainer.
- The player washes up in the brook nearby with an old trowel. The brook is fed by the Hose.
- Elder Fernwick sends the player north, because he knows what Grubthorn's flight means.
- Mossbottom is inside the Bounds: no enemy fights a gnome here outside a called fight (R-041, see `the-bounds`).
