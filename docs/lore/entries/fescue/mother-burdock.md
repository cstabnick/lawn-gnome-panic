Mother Burdock is the Wildling chieftain of Burdock's Tangle in the Hedgewood. She wants the logging stopped forever, and she is bitter that nobody asked her about the King's Fall.

## Want
The logging stopped forever.

## Secret
She exiled the Ferals herself. She knows the King's Fall was not the Wildlings' doing, and she is bitter that nobody asked.

## In the games
- Burdock's Tangle is her home. It is neutral, or hostile if the player sided with the Hedgewrights.
- Her adopted son, Fen Splitwell, will be the go-between in the peace route.
- Brokering the peace means killing Queen Vespa in the Paper Spire for her, and then being given a branch of the Heartwood. Wildling archers then join the final battle.

> "You cut, you call it tending. We grow, you call it wild."
