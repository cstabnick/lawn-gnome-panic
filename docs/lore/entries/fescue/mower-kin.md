Creatures that took the Mower's shape: the Sea Mower in the Cistern and the Minimower at the Shed. They have no engine, no fuel, no Heartspark and no blade, and they do not stink.

## What gnomes say

- The Rockery's iron hums when the Mower moves (Blade Shard).
- The Even Cut build iron effigies of the Turning Blade. One got up one morning and wandered off; the acolytes call it a miracle (the Minimower).

## The truth

They are Grown: creatures that grew the shape of the thing every gnome fears, the way a cloud grows a face. They move like animals (paddling, rolling), so rule 8, that the Mower is the only machine, holds. Only the Mower has no nameable want (rule 6); its kin want to be near it: the Minimower wants to stand by the Shed door and hum along. The Sea Mower is Rain, the Minimower Iron.

## In the games

Sea Mower (dex 021, Cistern Reef) and Minimower (dex 012, the Verge). The Yard's `sea-mower` is a separate thing in a separate setting (R-032).
