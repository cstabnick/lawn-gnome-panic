Creatures that grew the Mower's shape: the Sea Mower in the Cistern and the Minimower at the Shed. They have no engine and no fuel and they do not stink.

## What gnomes say

- The Rockery's iron hums when the Mower moves (Blade Shard). Things near the Shed and the iron start to hum too.
- The Minimower wants "nothing anyone can name". Gnomes say that is the Mower's own want.

## The truth

They are Iron Gnomokemon: the Blade Shard's hum waking metal near the Mower. They move like animals (paddling, rolling), so rule 8, that the Mower is the only machine, holds.

## In the games

Sea Mower (dex 021, Cistern Reef) and Minimower (dex 012, the Verge). The Yard's `sea-mower` is a separate thing in a separate setting (R-032).
