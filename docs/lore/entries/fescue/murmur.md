Murmur is the starling mother who roosts over the west lawn below the Archive at Birdbath Keep. Her flock is loud, quick and everywhere at once, and she is the only one of them who seems to know where the flock is going.

## What it wants

Seed for the winter. The Keep's west lawn is where the seed-carts spill, and the flock has learned that a cart means a full crop. Nobody has told her the carts are the Keep's.

## In the games

- Horn call at level 12, from the horn post on the Archive lawn. The arena is the west lawn; the starlings come in three waves, and the boss comes down at the pull.
- Her moves are Swoop (a lane charge across the lawn), Gust (a cone that staggers whoever is in front of her) and Murmuration (she calls three starlings into the fight).
- The starlings are small, glossy and loud. They pay nothing, so the horn's reward is the five horn-marked pieces: a beak pick, a feather sling, a seed-store wand, a down hood and an egg charm. Each falls one time in five, and the Sodbury anvil reforges them (see horn-calls).
- Win and the flock lifts all at once and scatters over the Basin.
