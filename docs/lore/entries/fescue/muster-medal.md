A medal struck from a bottle cap at Last Tuft for gnomes who stand at the final muster. Smith Hobnail Ferrule strikes it, but only on Knight-Commander Rowan Ironsod's word.

## What gnomes say

- Its examine line: "Struck from a bottle cap for gnomes who stood at the muster."
- Ironsod gives his word for no one until someone has searched the King's cart on the Shorn and found what he would not say: the level-cut hat brims.

## In the games

- `c7-muster-medal`, a blooming trinket in Hobnail Ferrule's war stock at Last Tuft (Chapter 7).
- It shows in the shop from the start but cannot be bought until the main quest "No Hats" (`c7-no-hats`) is done (R-045).

## Loose threads

- Whether Ironsod wears one himself. He was under the cart, not at a muster, and nobody asks him.
