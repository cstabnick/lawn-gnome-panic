Grub-Master of **the Stubble-Sappers**: a lean, flint-toothed chalk-goblin who wears an inverted, rusted Tall One thimble on his head as a helmet.

## What gnomes say

- Canny and unsentimental, Nibs counts debts in river-pebbles and iron filings and speaks in short, clicking sentences.
- He takes coin from **High Edger Sedgewick** to dig tunnels under the Shorn, yet quietly slips rusted Shed-bar fragments to **Knight-Commander Rowan Ironsod**—convinced that when the Mower finally wakes, the only safe place is whoever owes you a favour.
- He has never once been seen near a Glaze Shrine or a Warm Mound, and claims rain makes his thimble itch.
