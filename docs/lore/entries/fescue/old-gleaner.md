Old Gleaner is the great crow who roosts on Crow's Perch above Cloverfield. She is hungry, not evil.

## What it wants
Food. The farms are empty, so there is no spilled grain for her.

## In the games
- Crow's Perch is a world boss, at level 16, on a fallen fence post.
- Her moves are Swoop (a lane charge), Gust (a knockback cone) and Snatch (she lifts the player and drops them for fall damage).
- At 50% health she flies out of reach. Slingers are best at shooting her down.
- The peaceful option is to feed her from Fieldwarden Muckle's hidden grain store. She then leaves, and the Sheriff finds his deputy's hat in her nest. The deputy was taken by the crow last spring.
