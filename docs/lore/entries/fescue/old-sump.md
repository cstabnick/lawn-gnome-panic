Old Sump is a rust-rat the size of a wheelbarrow, and the biggest of the rats that crawled out of the Shed. He dragged an oil drum out with him and has sat on it ever since. Anyone who comes near the drum is, in his view, coming to take it back.

## What gnomes say

- A Shorn hound-keeper says: "He was never a pet. He was a rat with a drum, and the drum was the part that mattered."
- A Hobnail smith says the drum is the last full barrel of oil from the Shed, and that Old Sump is the only one who knows where the rest went.
- Edgers on the western Shorn say he sings when he is angry, and that the song sounds like a lid being struck.

## What it wants

The drum. It wants the drum more than it wants food, gold or a fight. Its sump rats climb out of the drum when it calls them, and they go back in when it is done.

## The truth

The oil in the drum is Shed oil, the same black dew that slicks the Verge. Old Sump has been drinking it, which is why it has grown and why it has learned to shout. Nobody has told it the drum is empty.

## In the games

- The raid boss of the Oil Drum, a horn call on the western Shorn of the Verge (`raid-verge`, level 29). Blow the horn at the post, and he comes to the centre of the arena with three sump rats, then more at 60 s and 100 s.
- Its moves are Black Dew Slick (a pool that slows), Drum Roll (a lane that staggers), Pack Call (summons sump rats) and Oil Rage (a buff below 40% health).
- Its last words are "Mine. Was mine."
- Beating it drops one of five horn-marked pieces at ilvl 30, kiln-fired: a drum wrench, an oil-drum sling, a drum-lid wand, a rust-rat hood or a drum-bung charm. See `horn-calls` and R-138.
