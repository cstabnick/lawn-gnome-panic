Old Thistle is the sweeper of Mossbottom's path. He has swept it for forty years, and he has never said why.

## Want
To keep the path clean.

## Secret
His wife Primrose took work in the Keep's waterworks, and the path was the one she walked home on every season. She was lost in the Backflow nine years after he started sweeping. He never stopped sweeping, and he never says why.

## In the games
- He is the tutorial voice in Mossbottom.
- Tansy will tell you about him if you ask her.

> "In my day we fought slugs bare-handed. Uphill."
