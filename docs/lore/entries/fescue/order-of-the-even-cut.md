The Order of the Even Cut believes that only a perfectly level lawn is holy. They hold that the Mower is a saint, not a monster.

## The ideas
Cut it all: only a perfectly level lawn is holy. The Mower is a saint, not a monster.

## Founding
A splinter of the Crown's own Groundskeepers started the Order during the Dandelion Schism, about 305 years ago. They decided the Dry Summer happened because the lawn had grown uneven, and that the Mower had been holy, a cleanser.

## What gnomes say
- The Crown tolerates them as cranks.
- The Saint Stirring was their name for the First Rumble. They doubled their numbers in a year.
- Brother Sedge, a Thatchford preacher, says: "Peace is a lawn where no blade stands taller than another."

## In the games
- They keep a chapel in Thatchford, the Neat Chapel, with a perfectly trimmed lawn. Cutting a blade of their grass turns them hostile in that area only.
- They keep a fort on the Shorn, the stubble strip on the Verge, where they mow by hand as worship.
- High Edger Sedgewick leads them.
