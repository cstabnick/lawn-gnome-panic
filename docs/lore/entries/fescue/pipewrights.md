The Pipewrights are the Guild of Pipewrights. They find hidden water and move it wherever the Crown needs it, by pressure through the Drainpipe Deeps.

## The ideas
The sources do not name one of the three ideas for the Pipewrights. They work for the Crown, which holds tend it.

## Founding
The Crown founded the Guild during the Dry Summer, about 310 years ago, to find hidden water. The Pipewrights dug the Drainpipe Deeps under Birdbath Keep. The maze taps the Tall Ones' old buried pipes and can push water anywhere in Fescue. At the bottom is the Great Valve.

## What gnomes say
- The moles lost their damp tunnels to the Pipewrights' digging. The mole grudge dates from the Dry Summer.
- Forty Pipewrights were trapped below during the Backflow, 31 years ago. Many were Thatchford gnomes.
- The Pipewrights' pressure has been wrong since the Backflow. The Spinning Fountains spray at random because of it.

## In the games
- A table in the Puddleby inn in Thatchford is laid for the forty lost Pipewrights. A toast is made to it every night.
- The Backflow is the bottom wing of the Drainpipe Deeps, where the Pipewrights' tools lie.
