Puddlemere is a footprint lake in Mossbottom Vale, one of the marks the Tall Ones left when they walked the lawn.

## What gnomes say

- Footprint lakes are everywhere in Fescue, and gnomes say "The Tall Ones stepped here".
- The lake had no name until the map was built. Its name is Puddlemere.

## In the games

- A sub-area of Mossbottom Vale for players at level 2.
- Frogs live on the shore. They are passive.
- A drowned old cart holds a hidden chest.
