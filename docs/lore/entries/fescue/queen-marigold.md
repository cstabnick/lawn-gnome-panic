Queen Marigold Clayfoot is the child-queen of the Keep, crowned at eight after her father's death. She has never left the Keep, and she believes the old legends.

## Want
To be a real queen, and to see her kingdom.

## Secret
She has secretly been reading Gnorwin's legends and believes every word. She is the player's only ally at court at first.

## Sad note
She has never been allowed to leave the Keep since her father's death. She has never seen the Verge, the Hedgewood or a real farm. She watches the robins land in the Basin and asks visitors what outside smells like.

## In the games
- She is the Queen at Birdbath Keep. She arrives at Last Tuft in chapter 7 to lead the final muster.

> "Tell me: does Cloverfield really smell of honey?"
