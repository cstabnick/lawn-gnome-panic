The thirty years since the King's Fall. Nothing happened, and that was the problem. The Rumble did not come back, and the young left for the Keep.

## What gnomes say

- The Rumble didn't come back, so most gnomes stopped believing in it.
- The young went to the Keep for work. Sodbury's farms emptied.
- The Shed's iron bars rusted.
- The Even Cut quietly took the Shorn.

## In the games

- Cloverfield is half-empty. The young left, and red ants moved onto abandoned ranches and rustle aphids.
- Chapter 4 side quest "The Last Pupil": Sodbury's one pupil has a teacher who rings the bell. Two children come back from the Keep the next day. It is a small happy ending to the sad note.
- Chapter 4 side quest "Haybale Letters": Ma Haybale's three children are scattered in the Keep.
