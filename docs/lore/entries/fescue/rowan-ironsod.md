Knight-Commander Rowan Ironsod heads the Keep's army. He is the lone survivor of the King's Fall, and he has been hiding ever since.

## Want
Justice for the King.

## Secret
He was a squire at the ambush and hid under the King's cart. He has been hiding ever since.

## The truth
He knows the attackers wore no hats, and he suspects Sedgewick. He has no proof. The player can earn the proof in the Verge chapter, in the quest "No Hats".

## In the games
- He is at Birdbath Keep as the head of the army.
- At the front in the Verge he commands the war camp at Last Tuft.
