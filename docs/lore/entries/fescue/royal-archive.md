The Royal Archive is the records room in Birdbath Keep, where the written history of Fescue is kept.

## What gnomes say

- Written records start in the Charters era, and the Archive holds them.
- Archivist Basil Inkcap will tell you three versions of each event. He says: "There are three accounts of that. All wrong in interesting ways."
- The Archive says rust "tames" the Mower. That is why the Crown flooded the Shorn in the Backflow.
- The Archive calls the Tall One on Scarecrow Rise "a Gardener who forgot to leave".

## In the games

- The Archive is part of the Pedestal, in Birdbath Keep.
- Tansy the Herbalist writes to the Archivist every week, and nobody writes back. The letters were held at the post office.
- Basil owes Tansy fifty-two replies he never got. He is delighted when the player fixes it.
