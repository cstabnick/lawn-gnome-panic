High Edger Sedgewick leads the Order of the Even Cut and sits at court as an adviser. He is the main villain on the gnome side.

## Want
The Mower freed, to make Fescue perfectly even.

## The truth
He led the King's Fall ambush on the Shorn, when he was a young Edger. He wanted the Crown's iron off the Shed door. His men went bareheaded so the attack would be blamed on Wildlings, who often go bareheaded. Rowan Ironsod suspects him but has no proof. He believes the Mower is a saint that will make Fescue perfect.

## In the games
- Brother Sedge, the Even Cut preacher in Thatchford, reports everything to him.
- He is the boss of the Level, a perfectly square Even Cut fort on the Verge (levels 27-28). His moves are Edging Line, Level Ground and Call the Faithful.
- His defeat line is: "You can't stop it. You can only be cut sooner or later."

> "When it comes, it will not hate you. It will simply make you even."
