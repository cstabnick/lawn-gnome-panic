The Shard Mine is the deep seam of the Rockery, where the iron hums in the walls and the edge of the Blade Shard is exposed.

## What gnomes say

- Miners in the Shard Mine said the iron hummed on the night of the First Rumble.
- The Delvers have mined the Shard for 300 years and smelted it into gnome iron.
- Forewoman Sax Hammerclod has stopped the mine three times this year and hid it from the Keep, which would have had to stop the iron tithe.

## The truth

The iron in the walls is the Mower's blade. The deep seam is where its broken edge shows.

## In the games

- A sub-area of the Rockery at levels 21-22.
- Iron hums in the walls. Centipedes and rock-lice live here.
- The deep seam is a mirror-bright surface the size of a hall.
