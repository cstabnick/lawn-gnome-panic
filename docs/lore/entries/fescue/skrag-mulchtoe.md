Bog-Warden of **the Peat-Pack**: a broad-shouldered moss-goblin who smokes dried clover-stems in a dandelion pipe and wears a tarnished brass button as a belt buckle.

## What gnomes say

- Gruff, slow-spoken, and unexpectedly learned in soil layers, Skrag can tell which season a leaf fell just by chewing its stem.
- He keeps a truce with **Mother Burdock** of the Wildlings, trading weather-warnings from the deep roots for salt-free gourd rinds—though he still chases Wildling scouts off his best compost beds.
- *"A lawn takes one summer to wreck,"* he tells impatient young goblins, *"and ten to rot proper. Sit down."*
