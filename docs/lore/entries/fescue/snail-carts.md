Carts pulled by snails, run by the Fieldwardens of Cloverfield. The Charter of Root and Seed makes them a duty to the Crown.

## What gnomes say

- Duty to the Crown under the Charter of Root and Seed: grain, honeydew and snail-carts.
- Fieldwarden Muckle: "Haul for the Crown? With what?"
- Reeve Burlap, in Thatchford: "Carts. It's always carts."

## In the games

- Chapter 4 "Runaway Snails": lasso five runaway snails. The reward is a riding snail mount (+30% walk speed).
- Chapter 4 "The Great Haul" (finale): escort Mabel's snail-cart from Sodbury to Birdbath Keep, and get the Snail-cart Writ. Fast-travel carts unlock.
- Snail-cart ticket (items): fast travel between visited towns, sold at the Sodbury depot for 5 gold.
- Battle of the Verge, phase 2: Mabel's snail-cart rolls in with the new Stopping Stone.
