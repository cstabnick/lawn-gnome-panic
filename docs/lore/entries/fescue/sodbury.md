Sodbury is the farm town of Cloverfield, with a granary tower, a snail-pen corral and wide streets.

## What gnomes say

- Ma Honeydew Haybale, head of the biggest aphid ranch, says: "An aphid ain't smart, but it's loyal. More than I can say for some."
- Sheriff Timothy Fallow, the law in Cloverfield, says: "Ants don't sleep. So I don't either."
- Fieldwarden Clover Muckle says: "The Crown wants a tenth. The Crown doesn't plough."

## In the games

- The granary tower is a stacked flowerpot.
- Services: Shrine, Smith, General, Inn (The Full Trough), Board, Cart (the main cart depot, which unlocks fast travel), and Stables.
- At the Stables the player can get a riding snail mount, which gives +30% walk speed. It is unlocked by a quest.
- The schoolhouse, with its one pupil left, is in the town. See the Cloverfield sad note.
- Sodbury is inside the Bounds: no enemy fights a gnome here outside a called fight (R-041, see `the-bounds`).
