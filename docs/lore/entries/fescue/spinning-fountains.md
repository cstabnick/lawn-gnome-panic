The Spinning Fountains are a field of spraying heads on the Thatchford Downs. They fire at random and keep the low ground soggy.

## What gnomes say

- After the Backflow, the fountains started firing at random. Half the downs stay soggy because of them.
- Nettle the Pipewright apprentice says the pressure is wrong, and has been wrong since before she was glazed.

## The truth

They are sprinkler heads. The Deeps' pipes have been leaking pressure since the Backflow, so the heads fire on their own.

## In the games

- A sub-area of Thatchford Downs at levels 7-8.
- Environmental hazard: any one fountain may burst and knock the player back. The burst is a telegraphed ring.
- Muck sprites (sentient puddles) and rats live in the field.
