The road the Kiln-born rolled the Mower along, from the Kiln in the Rockery to the barred door of the Shed on the Verge. It is Snap's fourth ride.

## What gnomes say

- Planting: after the Stopping Stone jammed the blade, the Kiln-born rolled the Mower back to the Shed and barred the door.
- Seven waystones line the road, one for each Kiln-born. Two are carved with names (Gnorwin Clayfoot and Moss). The other five are worn blank, because the songs never agree on who they were.

## The truth

It is a worn path between a stone hearth and a shed, with petrol on it. The Mower's silhouette behind the door is the Shed's own shadow.

## In the games

The course called "The Kiln & Shed" today: glowing furnace arch, Stopping Stone obelisks, a Mower silhouette and a scorched Verge trail. Snap never shows the Mower move or the Stopping Stone fired.

## Loose threads

Who the other five were. The Kiln lit itself four nights before Gnomomia begins; Snap does not show the lighting.
