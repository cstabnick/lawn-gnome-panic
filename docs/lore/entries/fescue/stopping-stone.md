A stone carried up from the Kiln. Gnorwin Clayfoot threw it into the Turning Blade's mouth and jammed the Mower. Fescue now has to fire a new one to stop it again.

## What gnomes say

- Gnorwin rode out with the stone and threw it into the Blade's mouth. The Blade jammed and fell silent.
- The Kiln-born rolled the Mower back into the Shed and barred the door.
- Kilnkeeper Sedum on the new stone: "Fire a new Stone? In the Kiln? The Kiln hasn't burned in my lifetime... except four nights before you came."

## The truth

The Stone did not kill the Mower. It jams the blade, and over three millennia it has been ground down. (Designer's truth in history.md.)

## In the games

- Chapter 6, "A New Stopping Stone" (Sedum): the Kiln's gate has collapsed, so the only other way in is the moles' deep way.
- Chapter 6, "Firing the Stone": defend the Kiln for 120 s while Sedum fires the stone. The reward is The New Stopping Stone.
- Battle of the Verge, phase 2: Mabel's snail-cart brings the stone along the lane. You interact with it as the blade slows.
- Now: the Stone is nearly ground to dust, and the Mower is starting to turn again.
