Fescue has three magic schools, each something a garden needs to grow: Sun, Rain and Bloom. There is no fourth school.

## In the games

- Sun is heat and light: fire, burning and bursts. It was the old Destruction school.
- Rain is water and cold: frost, slows, storms and control. A storm is Rain, and lightning is a Rain trick at most.
- Bloom is soil and growth: healing, glazing, roots and shields. It was the old Restoration school.
- Hedge-Mage is the class that casts all three. The Spigot staff of the First Rain is a Rain weapon.
- Sun spells finish the Unfired in the Kiln, so they harden and sigh, and that counts double.
- The Heartspark takes Rain x2 in the final battle.
- Necromancy, demons, arcane runes and lightning from nowhere do not exist in Fescue.
