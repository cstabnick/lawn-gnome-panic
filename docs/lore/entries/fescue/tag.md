The Tall Ones' giant dog, left in Fescue, who carries the photographer and the camera along his old daily walk. He is not the Yard's Biscuit and not Gnome Company's Fetch Dog; he only shares their model.

## What gnomes say

- The name: his collar has a tag the size of a cart wheel, scratched with Tall One writing nobody can read. Nobody has asked what it is short for.
- He walks a round every day, from the Lab's door. If you sit on him you are taken round his route. The ride's rail is his walk.
- The saddle is an old garden glove ("an Empty Hand") stitched to a rope harness.
- In the Lobby he lies on the rug facing the front door, thumps his tail when it opens, and puts his head down again.

## The truth

Dogs are traces of the Tall Ones, not sightings of them (R-012). He carries the camera; every Gnomokemon tolerates him (to a mole he is weather); his tail sets the beat the Flute plays (anything within a few dog-lengths dances); and he goes at his own pace, so the Speed control is a pat on the neck.

## In the games

- The ride: the camera sits in his saddle and the rail is his route. The 12-unit Flute radius is measured from him.
- The Lobby: he sleeps on the rug (R-019).
