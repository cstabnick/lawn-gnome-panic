A Tall One's giant dog from the town side, who carries the camera, the photographer and a carrier gnome along his walk. He is not Fescue's dog, not the Yard's Biscuit and not Gnome Company's Fetch Dog; he only shares their model.

## What gnomes say

- The name: his collar has a tag the size of a cart wheel, scratched with Tall One writing nobody can read. Nobody has asked what it is short for.
- He walks a round, starting from the Lab's door. If you sit on him you are taken round his route. The ride's rail is his walk.
- The saddle is an old garden glove ("an Empty Hand") stitched to a rope harness. The carrier gnome sits in the thumb-pocket and the photographer stands on the carrier's shoulders (R-019).
- In the Lobby he lies on the rug facing the front door, thumps his tail when it opens, and puts his head down again.

## The truth

A dog is a trace of the Tall Ones, not a sighting of one (R-012), and on the town side the Tall Ones are simply never home. He carries the camera; he is the one big thing nobody runs far from (to a mole he is weather; a skittish creature bolts from the gnomes on his back); his tail sets the beat the Flute plays (anything within a few dog-lengths dances); and he goes at his own pace, so the Speed control is a pat on the neck.

## In the games

- The ride: the camera sits in his saddle and the rail is his route. The 12-unit Flute radius is measured from him.
- The Lobby: he sleeps on the rug (R-019).
