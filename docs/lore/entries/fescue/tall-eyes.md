Glass marbles, which gnomes call Tall-eyes. They are the most precious gems in Fescue.

## What gnomes say

- Glass marbles are called Tall-eyes, the most precious gems.
- The Charter of Stone makes the Delvers pay Tall-eyes as a duty to the Crown.

## In the games

- Tall-eye shards are mined in the Rockery, and the Tall-eye charm is a Rockery Hedge-Mage item.
- Chapter 7 side quest "Moths to Flame": a grass-bag moth carries a Tall-eye with a scratch that looks like writing. It is a hook.
