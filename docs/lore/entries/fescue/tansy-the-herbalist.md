Tansy is the herbalist of Mossbottom. She wants to study in the Royal Archive, and she writes to the Archivist every week.

## Want
To study in the Royal Archive.

## Secret
She writes letters to the Archivist every week, and nobody writes back.

## What gnomes say
- Tansy believes nobody writes back.
- The Thatchford postmistress, Clementine Wick, holds Tansy's letters. They were never sent, because postage is owed.

## In the games
- She is the Herbalist in Mossbottom.
- The Royal Archivist, Basil Inkcap, owes her fifty-two replies he never got. Fixing the post is a tiny side quest in Thatchford.

> "If it's green, I can boil it. If it's purple, I won't."
