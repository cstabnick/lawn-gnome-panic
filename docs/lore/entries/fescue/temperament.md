How a Gnomokemon behaves around Tag and the tools: Docile, Curious, Skittish, Shy, Territorial, Thief, Sentinel or Aloof. It is proposed, not built.

## What gnomes say

- Gnome Jesus says of the Shy: "It will come out if you stop talking."
- Only Territorial ones act against the player, and for a reason on their Gnomokedex line. Nothing bleeds: a warning, a shove or a smear on the lens.
