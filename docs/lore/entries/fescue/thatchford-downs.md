Thatchford Downs are the rolling market country, levels 6 to 11, where the Hose leaks across the low ground and the town of Thatchford stands.

## What gnomes say

- Planting: the Hose runs here. Where it leaks across the low ground it makes the only ford for miles.
- Charters: the downs were founded about 290 years ago as the crossroads for the three charters' goods.
- Rumbling: the First Rumble cracked the old mill wheel. After the Backflow the Spinning Fountains started firing at random, and they keep half the downs soggy.
- Gnomes in the Puddleby inn toast a table nobody sits at, laid for the forty Pipewrights lost in the Backflow.

## In the games

- Sub-areas: the Hose banks (6-7, water beetles, frogs, leeches), the Spinning Fountains (7-8), Crowfoot Hill (8-9, magpie thieves), the Compost Heap (9-11, a rat mini-dungeon with the boss Rotgut) and the Neat Chapel (Even Cut, neutral).
- Thatchford is the town here, and the Calling trainers sit in its guildhall.

## Sad note

The Puddleby inn has a table nobody sits at. It is laid for the forty Pipewrights lost in the Backflow, many of them Thatchford gnomes. A toast is made to it every night. If the player sits there, the room goes quiet.
