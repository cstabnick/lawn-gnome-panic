Thatchford is a crossroads market town of about forty buildings, on both banks of the Hose, joined by a stone ford.

## What gnomes say

- Thatchford was founded about 290 years ago, where a market grew at the Hose's leak across the road. It was the only ford between the south villages and the Keep.
- Grain from Cloverfield, timber from the Hedgewood and iron from the Rockery all crossed the ford. "Thatchford is a crossroads town and lives by trade."
- Reeve Yarrow Burlap says: "A rumble? Carts. It's always carts."
- Rye Grindwell the miller has kept a tally of every tremor since the First Rumble cracked his wheel. He says: "Thirty-one years of tallies. Count them. Then tell me it's carts."
- Nettle, a Pipewright apprentice, says: "Pressure's wrong. Pressure's been wrong since before I was glazed."
- Brother Sedge, an Even Cut preacher in the square, says: "Peace is a lawn where no blade stands taller than another."

## In the games

- The town has thatched toadstool roofs, a market square, a mill and a guildhall.
- Services: Shrine (the fountain in the square), Smith, Herbalist, General, three Trainers in the guildhall, Bank, Inn (The Tipsy Toadstool), Board, and a Cart after Cloverfield.
- Clementine Wick the postmistress holds Tansy's letters to the Archive, which were never sent because postage is owed. It is a small side quest.
- Thatchford is inside the Bounds: no enemy fights a gnome here outside a called fight (R-041, see `the-bounds`).
