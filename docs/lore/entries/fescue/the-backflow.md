About 31 years ago the Pipewrights flooded the Shorn through the Great Valve. The valve seals were made of clay, they failed, and the Deeps flooded backward, trapping forty Pipewrights.

## What gnomes say

- The Crown ordered the flood to keep the Mower's damp at bay. The Archive says rust "tames" it.
- The Deeps flooded backward, and forty Pipewrights were trapped below.
- Master Pipewright Gromwell stayed at the Great Valve to close it by hand, so the water wouldn't reach the Keep. He was never seen again.
- The lower Deeps were sealed and have been full of pests ever since.
- Ida Gromwell, his daughter, is now Master Pipewright. She has never forgiven the Crown.

## The truth

Lord Chancellor Wendel Potsworth, then a junior treasurer, cut the cost by having the valve seals made of clay instead of Shard iron. Ida does not know this. Potsworth knows exactly what he did, and that guilt is why he insists the Mower is a fairy tale. (From the history body, not a designer box, but a spoiler.)

## In the games

- Chapter 3 side quest "Ida's Father": Gromwell's notes say "The seals are clay. I said Shard iron. Someone said cheaper."
- Chapter 6 "Down the Drains" and "Clay Seals": you find three old valve seals, which are clay, then confront Potsworth.
