The gnome name for the Mower's grass bag. In the final battle it tears open, and something that isn't grass is found inside.

## In the games

- Chapter 7 side quest "Moths to Flame": kill 12 grass-bag moths. Something glittered in one moth's wings. It is a Tall-eye with a scratch that looks like writing.
- Battle of the Verge: Belly moths press in, and Wildling archers shoot them down automatically on the peace route.
- The hook at the end of the battle: the Belly tore open, and among the grass is a key the size of a gnome, with a tag in Tall One script that nobody can read.

## Loose threads

- The key's tag is in a script nobody can read.
- The design docs do not say what the key opens.
