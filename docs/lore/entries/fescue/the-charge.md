The Gardeners gave the gnomes one job: "Keep the lawn." Every faction in Fescue still argues about what "keep" means.

## What gnomes say

- Gnomes take the Charge to mean tend the lawn, guard it and keep pests from it.
- The Crown reads it as the Gardeners' old way: cut a little, water a little, keep the balance.
- The Wildlings read it as a call to let the lawn grow back, because cutting is cruelty.
- The Order of the Even Cut reads it as a call for a perfectly level lawn, and the Mower as a saint.

## In the games

- The three readings are the three ideas in three-ideas. Every faction, NPC and quest in Fescue leans toward one of them.
- The final battle, the Battle of the Verge, is the answer to the question the Charge left open.
