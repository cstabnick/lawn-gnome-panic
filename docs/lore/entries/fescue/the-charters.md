The history age, about 300 years ago. Written records start here. The Dry Summer, the Three Charters and the founding of Thatchford shaped the guilds and towns of Fescue.

## What gnomes say

- The Royal Archive in Birdbath Keep holds the written records. Archivist Basil Inkcap will give you three versions of each.
- The Dry Summer: the Spigot dropped to a trickle for a whole summer, and crops failed. It is the worst famine in written history.
- The Dandelion Schism split the Crown's own gnomes. King Bartholomew Clayfoot III exiled the dissenters.
- Bartholomew III then signed the Three Charters, each giving a guild a region to work forever.
- Thatchford grew where the Hose leaks across the road, the only ford between the south villages and the Keep.
- The factions agree on what happened, but not on why. That is the point of this layer.

## Kind of truth

History. Written records exist, but the factions disagree on why things happened. Players hear it in books, the Royal Archive, statues, place names and old laws.

## The truth

The Dry Summer was not the lawn's anger. A Tall One tap was turned almost off. (Designer's truth in history.md. See dry-summer.)

## In the games

- Birdbath Keep, Chapter 3 "Three Accounts": three records in the Archive, *The Long Lawn*, *Bartholomew's Charter* and *Gromwell's Pressure Notes*.
- Thatchford Downs, Chapter 2 "Pressure's Wrong": the Spinning Fountains, whose pressure is coming from the Deeps.
