The Crown rules Fescue from Birdbath Keep, through a child-queen and her Chancellor. It holds the idea to tend it, the Gardeners' old way.

## The ideas
Tend it: cut a little, water a little, keep the balance. The Gardeners' old way. The Crown and most towns hold this idea.

## Founding
Gnorwin Clayfoot was the first king. After the Dry Summer, Bartholomew Clayfoot III signed the three charters to feed Fescue. The Crown founded the Guild of Pipewrights to find hidden water in the same drought.

## What gnomes say
- The Crown's guilds hold their charters from the Keep, and each pays a tithe to it.
- The Crown tolerates the Even Cut as cranks.
- The Crown says the King's Fall was done by Wildlings. See [King Gnorbert Clayfoot](gnorbert-clayfoot.md).

## In the games
- The Keep is Birdbath Keep, the hub of the game.
