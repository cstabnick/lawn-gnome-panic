The Fence is the edge of Fescue. It is a wall nobody can cross, and gnomes call it the edge of the world.

## What gnomes say

- The Fence runs around the whole lawn. Nothing gets past it.
- Between the lawn and the Fence lies the Verge, the strip where the Mower comes from.
- The Leaving songs say the Tall Ones may have gone to plant another lawn beyond the Fence.

## The truth

It is a picket fence, the edge of the back garden. Gnomes call it the edge of the world.

## In the games

- The Fence is impassable. On the map it runs along the north edge of Fescue.
- The Shed sits by the Fence, at the north edge.
- The Fence line zone (levels 28-29) is the strip where things that fled the Shed roam: rust-rats, oil-slugs and grass-bag moths.

## Loose threads

What is beyond the Fence is left open on purpose.
