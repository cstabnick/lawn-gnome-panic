Gnome Jesus's greenhouse on the back lot of the Fescue Field Lab, a Tall One's glasshouse that came with the lot. It is the one place nobody has ever cut anything. It is a ride out of the Lab's glass back door, round the potting benches and back to the door, ending on Gnome Jesus.

## What gnomes say

- The panes are "Tall-eye walls", because glass is the Tall-eye's big cousin.
- A teapot and a cup on the potting bench, always cold.
- An empty picture frame, Page Thirty-Six, stands on the bench facing the door. He never mentions it.
- The camera and its film were found in a drawer of the potting bench.

## The truth

He stands in the same spot every night facing the door, in front of the frame, because page thirty-six of the Gnomokedex is his.

## In the games

"Starlight Greenhouse" today: night flagstones, glass arches, bioluminescent spores and a moonlit shrine. Species: Mooncap, Starbeetle, Dewghost, Glowmoth, Clockwork Frog, Bonsai Titan and Gnome Jesus.
