The Hose is the great green river of Fescue. It runs from the Spigot in the Rockery, past Birdbath Keep, down to Thatchford and Mossbottom.

## What gnomes say

- The Hose carries all the water in Fescue down from the Spigot.
- Where the Hose leaks across the low ground it makes the only ford for miles. Thatchford grew up at that leak.
- During the Dry Summer the Spigot dropped to a trickle, and the Hose failed with it. It was the worst famine in written history.

## The truth

It is a garden hose. Where it leaks across low ground, it makes fords and brooks.

## In the games

- The Hose banks are a sub-area of Thatchford Downs at levels 6-7, with water beetles, frogs and leeches.
- The Hose can be ridden downstream on a leaf-raft from Flintdelve to Thatchford. It is a one-way shortcut.
- It is also how the player first arrived. The brook at Mossbottom is fed by the Hose.
