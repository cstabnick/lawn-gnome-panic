The Kiln is a great hollow in the Rockery where the Tall Ones fired the first gnomes.

## What gnomes say

- The Gardeners shaped the first gnomes from Rockery clay and fired them in the Kiln. Seven gnomes came out first, the Kiln-born.
- The song says: "The Hand made the Kiln and the Kiln made the clay, and the clay stood up and was gnome."
- Kilnkeeper Sedum says: "It's never been lit in my lifetime. Or my mother's. Or hers."
- Gnomes today are made by kilnwrights in small town kilns. They are never as good as the old Kiln's work.
- Four nights before the player arrived, the Kiln lit itself. It went out again the next morning.

## In the games

- The Kiln is a dungeon at levels 23-24.
- Its guardians are fired-clay golems left by the Kiln-born. The back chambers hold the Unfired.
- The boss is the Kiln Warden, a giant fired-clay gnome statue that tests anyone who wants to use the Kiln. Its lines suggest it recognises the player's trowel.
- Beating the Warden lets Sedum fire the new Stopping Stone.
