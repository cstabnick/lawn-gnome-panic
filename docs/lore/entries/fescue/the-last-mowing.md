After the Leaving, the Turning Blade woke with no hand on the Rope. It mowed alone for one summer, until Gnorwin Clayfoot jammed it with the Stopping Stone.

## What gnomes say

- The Blade mowed the plains, which is why Cloverfield is flat.
- It struck a rock in the Rockery and broke a piece off its own blade. (See blade-shard.)
- It went toward the hedges, which were too thick to cut, so the Hedgewood still stands.
- Gnorwin Clayfoot rode out with a stone carried up from the Kiln and threw it into the Blade's mouth. The Blade jammed and fell silent.
- The Kiln-born rolled the Mower back into the Shed and barred the door.
- Gnorwin raised a Keep on the tallest stone in Fescue, the Birdbath, to watch the Shed forever.

## The truth

The Stopping Stone did not kill the Mower. It jammed the blade, a rock wedged under a mower deck. (Designer's truth in history.md. See the-mower and stopping-stone.)

## In the games

- Cloverfield is the flat farmland the Mower left behind. Its Charter duty is grain, honeydew and snail-carts.
- Birdbath Keep is the watchtower over the Shed, and the castle the player visits in Chapter 3.
- The Hedgewood is the forest the Mower could not cut. Wildlings call it holy for that reason.
