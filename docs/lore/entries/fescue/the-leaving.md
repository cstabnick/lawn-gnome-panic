One day, about 3,000 years ago, the Tall Ones stopped coming. The songs give a dozen reasons, and nobody knows which one is true.

## What gnomes say

- The songs say the Gardeners were angry, or sad, or went to plant another lawn beyond the Fence, or are only sleeping.
- Nobody knows.
- The Tall Ones are never seen. Their traces are huge: footprints that became lakes, such as Puddlemere and the Basin, and a dropped trowel the size of a bridge.
- The Turning Blade was still in the Shed. After the Leaving it woke with no hand on the Rope (see the-last-mowing).

## In the games

- The Tall Ones never appear, are never heard and are never explained. No human appears on screen.
- No trace ever settles who the Gardeners were or whether they will come back (lore-rules rule 2).

## Loose threads

- Why did the Tall Ones leave, and are they coming back?
- The songs say there is another lawn beyond the Fence. Nothing in the docs confirms it.
