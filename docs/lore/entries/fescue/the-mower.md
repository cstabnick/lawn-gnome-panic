The Tall Ones' great servant beast. Gnorwin Clayfoot jammed it with a Stopping Stone and locked it in the Shed about 3,000 years ago. It is the only machine in Fescue that runs by itself.

## What gnomes say

- The Turning Blade drinks Black Dew and breathes stink. It rolls across Fescue and cuts everything to one height.
- It lives in the Dark House (the Shed), and it wakes when the Waking Rope is pulled.
- Gnomes describe it as an animal and as holy. Nobody in Fescue knows what an engine is.
- The Even Cut calls it a saint, a cleanser, and not a monster.
- Its old names are the Turning Blade and Old Rumble.

## The truth

It is a machine left running with a stone in its blade. The Stopping Stone jammed it, and the Mower has been straining in the dark for 3,000 years, slowly grinding the Stone down. (Designer's truth in history.md.)

## In the games

- The Shed's door cracks in the First Rumble, and splits open at Chapter 7's "The Level". "The Mower is awake."
- The final battle is the Battle of the Verge. Gnorwin's Trowel does +50% damage to it.
- Every pest in Fescue has a want. The Mower is the only thing in Fescue with no want anyone can name.

## Loose threads

- After the Battle of the Verge the Mower is stopped, not destroyed. Its Belly is torn open. (Sequel hook.)
