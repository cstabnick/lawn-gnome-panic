A confederation of bog-goblins and mossy gnoblins living in the damp hollows under the Hedgewood roots, behind the Compost Heap, and along the leaky verges of the Hose. They lean toward *Let it grow*, holding that rot and leaf-litter are how the garden makes new soil.

## What gnomes say

- **Planting:** Wildling songs say the first Peat-Packers hid under the Shed floorboards during the Last Mowing while the Kiln-born fought outside.
- **Charters:** During the Dry Summer, the Pack survived by chewing wet moss-roots and quietly traded damp peat to starving Cloverfield farmers for gourd-seeds.
- **Rumbling:** Peat-Packers felt the First Rumble through the root-mats beneath Thatchford Downs three days before any gnome heard it in the air.
- They get on tolerably with **Mother Burdock** and the Wildlings, though they think Wildling gnomes still prune too much and talk too loud.

## Sad note

Every dry summer shrinks their borders. Old Peat-Packers who dry out past mending turn into silent, brittle turf-clods that sit beside the roots and cannot be woken.
