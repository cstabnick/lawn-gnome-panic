The myth age, about 3,000 years ago. The Tall Ones sowed the lawn, fired the first gnomes in the Kiln, gave them the Charge and set the Mower to work. Nobody can say which parts are true.

## What gnomes say

- "First the Lamp, then the Rain, then the Hand in the soil." (Opening of "The Long Lawn", sung at every Mossbottom harvest.)
- The Tall Ones planted the lawn. Fescue was bare dirt until the Gardeners seeded it. Every prayer starts "Tall Ones, who sowed us".
- The Kiln made the first gnomes from Rockery clay. Seven came out first, the Kiln-born. Gnorwin Clayfoot was the first king.
- The songs never agree on the other six. Mossbottom's song names one called Moss, and every village claims one.
- The Gardeners' one job for gnomes was "Keep the lawn". Gnomes take it to mean tend it, guard it and keep pests from it.
- A servant beast lived in the Dark House. When the lawn grew too long, the Waking Rope woke it, and it cut everything to one height. Gnomes hid underground. This was called a Mowing.
- The songs give a dozen reasons for the Leaving, and nobody knows which one is true.

## Kind of truth

Myth. The songs and sayings contradict each other, and they are probably half wrong. Players hear it in songs, carvings, prayers, children's rhymes and item flavour text.

## The truth

The Stopping Stone did not kill the Mower. It jammed the blade, like a rock wedged under a mower deck. Over 3,000 years the Mower has strained in the dark, and the Stone has been ground down slowly ever since. (Designer's truth in history.md.)

## In the games

- The footprint lakes (Puddlemere, the Basin) are "where the Tall Ones stepped". Cloverfield is flat because the Mower flattened it.
- Mossbottom side quest "A Trowel Like Yours": the Moss Kiln-Daughter statue at the well, and Fernwick's line about the trowel.
