The Rockery is the old, deep rock-garden mountain range, levels 19 to 24. The Kiln, the Spigot and a buried piece of the Mower's blade are here.

## What gnomes say

- Planting: the first gnomes were shaped from Rockery clay and fired in the Kiln, a great hollow in the rocks.
- Planting: the Mower broke its blade on these rocks, and the Blade Shard is buried in them.
- Charters: the Charter of Stone gave the Delvers the mines. They found the Shard and have smelted it into gnome iron for 300 years.
- Rumbling: the Shard hummed at the First Rumble, and lately it hums every few nights.
- Four nights before the player arrived in Mossbottom, the Kiln lit itself. Nobody lit it.

## The truth

The Blade Shard is a piece of the Mower's own blade. The Rockery's iron hums when the Mower moves.

## In the games

- Theme: deep, old, holy and a bit frightening. Miners know they are mining a monster's tooth.
- Sub-areas: Flintdelve (safe town), the Spigot (19-20), Marble Pits (20-21, where Tall-eyes are dug), Shard Mine (21-22), Mudtooth Deeps (22-23) and the Kiln (23-24, a dungeon).
- The game's quiet mystery, where the player came from, lives here.

## Sad note

The Unfired. In the Kiln's back chambers stand rows of clay gnomes that were shaped but never fired: soft, half-finished, with eyes painted on. Some move. They are not evil. They want to be finished. When the player defeats one, it slumps back into clay with a sigh. The Kilnkeeper visits them every evening.

## Loose threads
