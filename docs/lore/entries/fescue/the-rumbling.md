The memory age, about 30 years ago. The First Rumble, the Backflow and the King's Fall left living gnomes with grudges, guilt and no shared story about what happened.

## What gnomes say

- The First Rumble (32 years ago): a sound like a giant clearing its throat came from the north. The Shed's door cracked.
- The Backflow (31 years ago): the Deeps flooded backward, and forty Pipewrights were trapped below. Master Pipewright Gromwell closed the Great Valve by hand and was never seen again.
- The King's Fall (30 years ago): King Gnorbert's escort was ambushed on the Shorn. The Crown blames Wildlings, and the Wildlings blame the Even Cut.
- The Quiet Years (30 years ago to now): the Rumble stopped, most gnomes stopped believing in it, and the young left for the Keep.

## Kind of truth

Memory. Living gnomes were there, and they have personal grudges and guilt. Players hear it in NPC dialogue, first-hand stories and things people won't talk about.

## The truth

The King's Fall was the Even Cut, led by a young Edger named Sedgewick. (Designer's truth in history.md. See kings-fall.)

## In the games

- Chapter 6 "The Humming Seam": the Shard Mine wall hums, and Grout says "Thump. Turn. It's closer."
- Chapter 6 "A New Stopping Stone": the Kiln's front gate collapsed in the First Rumble.
- Chapter 7 "No Hats": Ironsod proves the King's Fall was an Even Cut job.
