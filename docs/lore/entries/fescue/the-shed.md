The Shed is the Dark House at the north edge of Fescue, where the Mower sleeps behind a barred door. The gnomes call it the Dark House.

## What gnomes say

- Planting: the Gardeners kept their servant beast in the Dark House. When the lawn grew too long, they pulled the Waking Rope and it rolled across Fescue, drinking Black Dew and breathing stink.
- Stopping: after the Leaving, the Kiln-born rolled the Mower back into the Shed and barred the door.
- Rumbling: after the First Rumble there was a new crack in the Shed's door. King Gnorbert ordered it re-barred with Rockery iron.
- Quiet Years: the Shed's iron bars rusted, and the Even Cut quietly took the Shorn.

## The truth

It is a garden shed. The Mower inside is a machine left running. The Stopping Stone did not kill it. It jammed the blade, like a rock wedged under a mower deck, and over 3,000 years the Stone has been ground down.

## In the games

- The Shed is the final battle at level 30. See the Battle of the Verge.
- Now the Shed door is splitting, and the Mower's shudder grows louder.
- Things that fled the Shed roam the Fence line below it.
