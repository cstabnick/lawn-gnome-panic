The Shorn is a stubble desert on the Verge. The Order of the Even Cut keeps it cut by hand, as worship.

## What gnomes say

- The Crown's watch-line was abandoned in the Quiet Years. The Even Cut moved in and mowed their Shorn.
- The Even Cut split from the Crown after the Dry Summer. They decided the lawn had grown uneven, and that the Mower had been holy, a cleanser. Their motto is "cut it all".
- The Crown says Wildlings ambushed the King on the Shorn. The Wildlings say the Even Cut did it. The one survivor, Knight-Commander Rowan Ironsod, says the attackers wore no hats, and he won't say more.

## The truth

The ambush was the Even Cut, led by a young Edger named Sedgewick. They went bareheaded so the attack would be blamed on Wildlings, who often go bareheaded. Rowan suspects this, but he has no proof. The player can earn the proof in the Verge chapter.

## In the games

- Sub-area of the Verge at levels 25-27.
- Enemies: Even Cut patrols, clippers (zealots with shears) and hand-mower acolytes.
- The Level, the Even Cut fort, is a perfectly square stronghold nearby.
