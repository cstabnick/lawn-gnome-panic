The Spigot is the source of the Hose: a huge metal tap rising from the rocks of the Rockery. All the water in Fescue comes from here.

## What gnomes say

- All water comes from the Spigot.
- During the Dry Summer, about 310 years ago, the Spigot dropped to a trickle for a whole summer. The lawn browned and crops failed.

## The truth

It is the outdoor tap.

## In the games

- A sub-area of the Rockery at levels 19-20. Earwigs live here.
- The Spigot is a huge metal tap rising from the rocks.
- Lore hint: the water comes from somewhere else entirely.
