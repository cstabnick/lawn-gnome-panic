The Verge is the bleak, mowed strip between the lawn and the Fence, levels 25 to 30. It is the war front, and the Mower waits in the Shed here.

## What gnomes say

- Planting: the Verge is the strip between the lawn and the Fence, where the Mower comes from.
- Charters: the Crown kept a watch-line here. It was abandoned in the Quiet Years, and the Even Cut moved in and mowed the Shorn.
- Rumbling: King Gnorbert went to the Verge to inspect the Shed after the First Rumble. He found the door cracked but holding, and ordered it re-barred with Rockery iron.
- Rumbling: King Gnorbert was ambushed on the Shorn on his way home, and the King's Fall followed.

## In the games

- Theme: open, bleak and windy. Everything is cut short, so there is nowhere to hide. Even Cut zealots, things that fled the Shed, and the Mower's shudder getting louder every day.
- This is where every thread meets.
- Sub-areas: Last Tuft (safe, the forward camp), the Shorn (25-27), the Level (27-28, the Even Cut fort and its boss, High Edger Sedgewick), the Fence line (28-29) and the Shed (30, the final battle).

## Sad note

The King's cart is still on the Shorn where he fell. The Even Cut keep the grass around it mowed perfectly, as a trophy.
