Sheltered, steaming beds of leaf-mould and compost along Sodbury's brick culvert and behind Thatchford's ford, where gnome-and-goblin households cure their gnoblins and where goblins gather in winter to keep from freezing stiff.

## What gnomes say

- **Charters:** The first Warm Mound was heaped by **Mire-Mother Cressida Peat** and **Kilnwright Alder Burlap** during the Dry Summer when Sodbury's kiln cracked.
- **Rumbling:** During the Backflow, the Mounds served as field-hospitals for waterlogged goblins and cracked Pipewrights alike.
- **Now:** **The Crock-Menders** tend the Thatchford Mounds and turn away Even Cut inspectors with polite, muddy smiles.

Why a Warm Mound's gentle compost heat sets clay hard enough to walk when no kiln flame ever touches it.
