About 300 years ago, after the Dry Summer, Bartholomew III signed three charters. Each gave one guild a region to work forever, to feed Fescue.

| Charter | Guild | Region | Duty to the Crown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Root and Seed | Fieldwardens | Cloverfield | Grain, honeydew, snail-carts |
| Bough | Hedgewrights | Hedgewood | Timber, game, bowstring |
| Stone | Delvers | The Rockery | Iron, slate, Tall-eyes |

## What gnomes say

- Each charter region has a charter-hall, a guild boss and a tithe to the Keep. That is why each industry region exists.
- The Hedgewood charter is the sore one. It gave the Hedgewrights the right to log the forest where the Wildlings had just settled, and the fight has gone on for 300 years.
- The Archive keeps a record called *Bartholomew's Charter*.

## In the games

- Chapter 4, Cloverfield: the Fieldwardens have no carts and no hands until you fix the ranch and the snails.
- Chapter 5, Hedgewood: the Hedgewrights log the Privet, and the Wildlings fight back.
- Chapter 6, the Rockery: the Delvers' iron and Shard-iron gear come from the Stone charter.
