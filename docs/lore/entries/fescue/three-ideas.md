Every faction in Fescue answers one question: how should the lawn grow? The three answers are tend it, let it grow and cut it all.

## What gnomes say

| Idea | Who | They believe |
|---|---|---|
| Tend it | The Crown, most towns | Cut a little, water a little, keep the balance. The Gardeners' old way. |
| Let it grow | The Wildlings, and the Ferals who took it too far | Cutting is cruelty. The wild should take the lawn back. |
| Cut it all | The Order of the Even Cut | Only a perfectly level lawn is holy. The Mower is a saint, not a monster. |

## In the games

- The split behind the three ideas is the Dandelion Schism. All three date their founding to the Dry Summer.
- The Battle of the Verge feels like the answer to the question the world has been asking all along.
- Writing rule: a new faction, NPC or quest should lean toward one of these three. That gives every conflict in Fescue the same backbone.
