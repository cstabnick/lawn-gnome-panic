Master of **the Crock-Menders**: a brisk, practical gnoblin with terracotta skin, pale lichen freckles across the bridge of their nose, and a half-glazed Thatchford cap.

## What gnomes say

- Raised in **the Warm Mounds** behind Thatchford's leaky ford by a Delver potter and a culvert-goblin, Vetch carries a satchel of potter's slip, willow-twine, and reed-splints everywhere.
- Gnomes who scoff at "muck-mending" in Birdbath Keep still knock on Vetch's cellar door after dark when a favourite teacup or a cracked kneecap needs fixing before morning.
- Vetch quietly supplies **Ida Gromwell** with waterproof peat-seals for the Drainpipe Deeps—far sturdier than the cheap clay seals that failed in the Backflow, though neither of them mentions it at court.
