The gnome name for the pull cord. The Tall Ones pulled it to wake the Mower. After the Leaving the Mower woke with no hand on the Rope.

## What gnomes say

- When the lawn grew too long, the Gardeners pulled the Waking Rope, and the Blade rolled out to cut.
- After the Leaving, the Turning Blade woke with no hand on the Rope.

## In the games

- Battle of the Verge, phase 3: the Mower starts to Pull the Rope by itself. It is the creepiest moment in the game. Cut the Rope within 30 s, or it restarts and heals.
- Old Gleaner (if you fed her, the gleaner=fed route) drops out of the sky and pins the Rope, which gives +15 s on the timer.
