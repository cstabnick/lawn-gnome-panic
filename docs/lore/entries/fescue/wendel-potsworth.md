Lord Chancellor Wendel Potsworth runs most of the realm in all but name. He insists the Mower is a fairy tale.

## Want
For the Mower to stay a myth.

## The truth
He cut the costs that caused the Backflow. Then a junior treasurer, he had the Great Valve seals made of clay instead of Shard iron. The seals failed, and the Deeps flooded backward. He knows exactly what he did, and that guilt is why he insists the Mower is a fairy tale: if the Mower is real, the Backflow was a real failure of a real duty, and it was his. He is not evil. He is a coward who has spent 30 years hiding one mistake. He can be redeemed at the end.

## In the games
- He runs the Chancellor's counting room in the Pedestal of Birdbath Keep.
- His cut-price seals are the cause of the Backflow that the player meets in the Drainpipe Deeps.

The sources disagree on how long he has ruled. History says he was regent for 20 years. The Birdbath Keep sheet says the Chancellor has ruled through a child-queen for 30 years.

> "Myths are expensive. The treasury isn't."
