The Wildlings let the lawn grow. They hold that cutting is cruelty, and that the wild should take the lawn back.

## The ideas
Let it grow: cutting is cruelty. The wild should take the lawn back.

## Founding
Some gnomes said the Dry Summer was the lawn punishing them for cutting it. They let their plots go to dandelion and clover, and King Bartholomew Clayfoot III exiled them, about 305 years ago. They went to the Hedgewood and became the Wildlings.

## What gnomes say
- The Hedgewood is holy to them because the Mower could not cut it.
- The Charter of Bough gave the Hedgewrights the right to log the forest where the Wildlings had just settled. That fight has gone on for 300 years.
- The Crown blamed the King's Fall on them, and the Crown let the Hedgewrights log twice as hard after that.
- Wildling children carve their names into the Elder Privet's bark. The oldest names are 300 years old.

## In the games
- Burdock's Tangle is their home in the Hedgewood.
- Wildling archers join the final battle on the peace route.
- Wildling scouts are hostile only on the Hedgewright route.
