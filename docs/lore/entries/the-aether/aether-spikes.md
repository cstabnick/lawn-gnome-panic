Clusters of glowing spikes that stand in the line of a Descent hill, and down the free-ride race course. Touch one below its top and the rider bails, so the spikes have to be jumped.

## In the games

- A cluster has 5 to 7 spikes leaning outward inside a yellow warning ring.
- Hills with a high hazard slider get them. The hill card tags them SPIKES at hazard 3 or more.
- A spike cluster is sized from 1.6 to 2.4 units tall, and placed to keep clear of rails.
- Free ride's race course has them too, with boulders, on every run (never at the start, on a kicker's run-up or where a jump lands).
