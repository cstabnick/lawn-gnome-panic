Clusters of glowing spikes that stand in the line of a Descent hill. Touch one below its top and the rider bails, so the spikes have to be jumped.

## In the games

- A cluster has 5 to 7 spikes leaning outward inside a yellow warning ring.
- Hills with a high hazard slider get them. The hill card tags them SPIKES at hazard 3 or more.
- A spike cluster is sized from 1.6 to 2.4 units tall, and placed to keep clear of rails.
