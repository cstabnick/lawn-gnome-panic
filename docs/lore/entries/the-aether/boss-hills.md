Every fourth Descent hill is a boss hill. It is offered alone, with all three sliders high and bigger rewards than an ordinary hill.

## In the games

- The first boss hill is The Shard Throne, the second is The Sky Wall, and the third and every later one is The Aether Maw.
- Boss hills use the same snowy pine look as other Descent hills.
- Their rewards are bigger than an ordinary hill's.
