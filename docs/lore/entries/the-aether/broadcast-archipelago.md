The upper stratum of the Aether, above Aether Board's floating snowboard islands, where glowing ley-lines braid into vertical plazas, highway overpasses, and rooftop spires saturated with leaked pirate radio signals.

## In the games

- Skaters on inline Aether Rollerblades carve between rooftops, grind glowing ley-line rails, and tag gang billboards across eight known districts (plus a ninth secret frequency revealed only after the eighth).
- **Velvet Keys Plaza** (Area 1) and **Bongo Basin Overpass** (Area 2) are the first two districts.

## Loose threads

How Turfton's pirate broadcasts climb into the ethereal plane is left unexplained.
