Padded pink walls with a glowing cyan pad along the top, set down both sides of every run of the free-ride mountain, and the
neon pinball bumpers that stand on the runs between them. A rider who hits one bounces off instead of bailing.

## In the games

- The walls run down both sides of each run, with gaps where a rail crosses, at kickers and where two runs meet. They keep
  a rider on the mountain: a glancing hit hands most of the speed back.
- Short bumper bars stand across the fall line on the runs, and pinball bumpers kick a rider away hard.
- Jump high enough and you fly over either.
- Nobody on the Aether says who put them there. Riders say the mountain grew them after too many gnomes went over the edge.
