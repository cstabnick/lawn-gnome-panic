A jazz-jungle gang of Woken instruments holding **Velvet Keys Plaza** (Area 1 of Gnom Set Radio), led by **Maestro Upright** (a lacquered upright piano) and **Pluck** (a carved upright bass).

## In the games

- Their turf is built around floating conservatory rooftops, piano-key plaza walkways, and rooftop Turfton FM relay dishes.
- Their sound is funky jungle-jazz fusion (162 BPM, D Dorian).
