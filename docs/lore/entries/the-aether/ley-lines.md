Glowing grind rails that float through the sky and arc between islands. Unlike the plain rails that sit on the islands, a ley-line pushes the rider along.

## In the games

- A ley-line arcs from the lower half of one run over to the next. Every other run has one.
- Grinding a ley-line gives the combo the word "Ley-line" on its first grind.
- On Descent, tricky hills bring more ley-lines.
