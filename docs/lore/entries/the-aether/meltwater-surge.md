A wall of snowmelt that comes down the Aether's mountain every half minute or so, runs the length of the slope and is gone again. Experimental: it exists only in Flood Run (`?mode=flood`).

## In the games

- Every run has a spring at its top: a steady stream, and every 24 to 38 seconds a surge with a sharp front and a long ebb.
- The water follows the real ground: it pools in dips, spills over rims into the sky and hands what is left to the next run.
- Ride with a surge and it carries you faster than you can push yourself. Ride against it and it drags you back.
