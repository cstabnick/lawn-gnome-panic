A percussion-driven gang of Woken drums and rhythm-runners holding **Bongo Basin Overpass** (Area 2 of Gnom Set Radio), led by **Conga King Kora** and her patrolling living conga sentries.

## In the games

- Their multi-tiered highway overpass uses suspension-cable ley-lines, Black Dew booster relays, and glowing bongo bounce pads to link four vertical decks.
- Their sound is a full-percussion bongo rip mix (132 BPM, A Mixolydian).
