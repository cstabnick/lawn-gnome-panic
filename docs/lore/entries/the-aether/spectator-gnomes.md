The gnomes along the island rims who cheer as the rider flies past, and go wild when the rider lands near them.

## In the games

- They stand along the rims beside the exit kicker and down the sides, facing the riding line.
- Giant spotted toadstools share the islands with them.
- Only a small pool of gnome models is drawn: the island the rider is on and the next two. The crowd stays a fixed size however long the world is.
