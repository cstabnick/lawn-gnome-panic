A 170 BPM liquid jazz-DnB gang of Woken neon signs and glowing birdbath basins holding **Neon Birdbath Spire** (Area 4 of Gnom Set Radio), led by **Fountainhead Finn**—a tiered living neon birdbath ringed by orbiting glass-tube halos.

## In the games

- Neon Birdbath Spire is DJ Broadcast's ancestral broadcast tower and the fourth rung of the Frequency Ladder (Dispersion / Pirate Beacon), scattering pirate signals across the upper Archipelago.
- Skaters spiral up mist-bowl tiers (4m, 8m, 12.5m, 16.5m Birdbath Apex) via glass-tube ley-line rails and mist updraft vents, guided by local helper gnome **Drizzle Dewdrop**.
