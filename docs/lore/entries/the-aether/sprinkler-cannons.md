Brass garden sprinklers on swivels, bolted to the banks of the Aether's runs and aimed across them. Each glows blue at the nozzle a moment before it fires. The Sprinkler Guild says they only water the slopes. A geyser vent is a hole in the run that does the same job from underneath. Experimental: Flood Run only.

## In the games

- Two cannons and two geyser vents on every run after the first, placed from the world seed.
- A cannon cycles idle, windup (about a second of glow) and a jet of about a second. A rider in the jet is shoved along it.
- A second blast soon after the first washes the rider out (a bail). A geyser launches the rider upward and never bails by itself.
