The ethereal plane: floating islands under a pink aether sky, where a gnome rides a snowboard from island to island. Fall off an island and you drop onto the next one.

## In the games

- Free ride is the candy aether plane: a pink sky, a low aether sun, and islands.
- Islands are linked by kickers, rails, ley-lines and halfpipe chutes.
- Past the last island, an aether gate warps the rider back to the first.
- Descent mode runs the same riding on snowy pine mountains. Those hills are a departure from the aether look, not the aether itself.

## Loose threads

How a gnome gets onto the plane is not explained. The setting list calls it a loose thread.
