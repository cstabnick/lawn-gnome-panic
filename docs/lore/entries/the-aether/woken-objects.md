Inanimate instruments and street objects—upright pianos, upright basses, conga drums, metronomes, fire hydrants, and cassette decks—that have woken up where Aether ley-lines knot around strong radio broadcasts.

## In the games

- Woken Objects sprout clay-glaze eyes and musical mutter-voices, adopting the genre of the frequency that woke them.
- Following core gnome canon, they never bleed: when tagged out or defeated, they detune harmlessly or pop into clay-glaze sparks.
