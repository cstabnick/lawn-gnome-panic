The secret ninth district at the apex of the Broadcast Archipelago, revealed only after all eight lower districts of the **Frequency Ladder** are retuned and converged at **Subsoil Relay Tower** (Area 8).

## The Frequency Ladder

1. **Velvet Keys Plaza** (Base Carrier / Melody)
2. **Bongo Basin Overpass** (Master Clock / Rhythm)
3. **Chrome Kiln Foundry** (Thermal Wattage / Amplification)
4. **Neon Birdbath Spire** (Dispersion / Pirate Beacon)
5. **Mulch Alley Arcade** (Archive / Bootleg Data)
6. **Tupperware Sky-Mall** (Acoustic Dome / Shielding)
7. **HOA Citadel Terrace** (Bylaw Override / Authority)
8. **Subsoil Relay Tower** (108.0 Convergence — revealed to be a giant tuning fork keeping the 09th frequency dormant)
9. **Zero-Hz Zenith** (The Silent Frequency — ruled by **The Original Needle** and **The Unfired Choir**, where only all eight regional styles together can break the silence).
