The gnome's only light: a ceramic lantern that it carries in front of itself. It is a comfort and a beacon, and it is not dependable.

## In the games

- `F` switches it on or off. Lit, it shows the gnome's surroundings and makes the gnome easy to see from far off. Dark, it is hard to see.
- It gutters. Now and then, while lit, the flame goes out for something between half a second and about two seconds, then catches again on its own, with a fizzle and a pop. The more the house is on edge, the more often it happens.
- Each gutter wears the wick. A worn wick gutters more often, and it flickers even when it is not gutting. The wick dries out slowly while the lantern is off.
- Lighting it again after switching it on takes a moment.
- The house's seed decides when it gutters, so the same house gutters at the same moments every time.
- After the third gutter in one house, the banner says the wick is tired and the lantern will keep doing this.

## Loose threads

The games never say who made the wick, or why the flame needs catching again.
