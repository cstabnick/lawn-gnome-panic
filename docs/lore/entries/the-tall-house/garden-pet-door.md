The way out of the house: a moonlit pet-door in the far wall of the last room. Going through it is the escape.

## In the games

- The game unlocks it once all three Soul-Bells are rung.
- Interacting with it (E) ends the run as an escape. The win text reads "Escaped into the Garden", with the time taken.
- Look: a giant pet-door with a wooden frame, a dark flap and a cool blue light.
- The escape banner reads "You escaped into the moonlit garden!"
