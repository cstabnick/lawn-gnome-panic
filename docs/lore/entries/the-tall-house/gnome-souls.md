Giant monsters that stalk the Tall House. Each is a towering, tattered, cloaked figure with a hollow porcelain face, a crooked gnome hat and glowing eyes, though the kinds differ in shape and colour.

## What they say

The monsters mutter in spatial audio from where they stand. Each kind has its own lines: "under the table... i hear tiny boots" (the Hollow Gardener), "three bells... do not touch the bells" (the Porcelain Watcher), "pretty flame... let it gutter" (the Lamplighter), "drip... drip... under the bed" (the Dripping Nanny), "hush... hush... no one is here" (the Hush).

## In the games

- Every house has two of them, the Hollow Gardener and the Porcelain Watcher, and one more drawn from the house's seed: the [Lamplighter](the-lamplighter.md), the [Dripping Nanny](the-dripping-nanny.md) or [the Hush](the-hush.md). Three Gnome-Souls in a house, five kinds across the games.
- Each kind hunts a different way. Some see the gnome by its lantern, some by the dark, some by its footsteps, some come under the furniture. Each has its own sound in the house.
- If one reaches the gnome, the gnome takes a hit. When the hits run out, the porcelain shell shatters and the run ends.
- Look: a dark cloak with a void core, a porcelain face, cyan soul-eyes (amber for the Lamplighter, pale for the Hush) and orbiting soul motes under an eerie glow. The Dripping Nanny crawls low on four legs.

## Loose threads

The games never say what a Gnome-Soul is, or whether it has anything to do with the Tall Ones.
