Giant monsters that stalk the Tall House. Each is a towering, tattered, cloaked figure, about 2.4 units tall, with a hollow porcelain face, a crooked gnome hat and glowing eyes.

## What they say

The monsters mutter in spatial audio from where they stand. Their lines include "under the table... i hear tiny boots", "cold clay... come out and play" and "three bells... do not touch the bells".

## In the games

- Two of them, the Hollow Gardener and the Porcelain Watcher, patrol the house.
- Look: a dark cloak with a void core, a porcelain face with beard wisps, cyan soul-eyes and orbiting soul motes under an eerie glow.
- If one reaches the gnome, the gnome takes a hit. When the hits run out, the porcelain shell shatters and the run ends.

## Loose threads

The games never say what a Gnome-Soul is, or whether it has anything to do with the Tall Ones.
