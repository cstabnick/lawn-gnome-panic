A heavy acorn on the floor of the third or fourth room. The gnome picks it up and tosses it onto the Brass Snare-Trap.

## In the games

- It lies on a free spot of floor, not under furniture.
- Picking it up readies the gnome. The HUD changes from "Find Acorn" to "Acorn Ready".
- The banner says to toss it onto the Giant Brass Snare-Trap.
- Look: a brown acorn with a dark cap, lit by a warm light.
