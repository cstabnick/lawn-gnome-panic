A ward across the doorway between the second and third rooms. It blocks the way until the gnome shatters it with both Soul-Glaze Shards in hand.

## In the games

- It blocks passage through that doorway until it is shattered.
- Shattering it alerts the Gnome-Souls near it.
- Look: a translucent blue frame with a glowing ring of light.
- The banner after shattering reads "Kiln Ward shattered! Watch for the Brass Snare-Trap ahead."
