Three bells, all of which must be rung to unlock the Garden Pet-Door. Ringing one wakes the house's monsters nearby.

## In the games

- Two bells stand in the fifth room, and one in the Moonlit Conservatory.
- Each ring alerts the Gnome-Souls within range. The banner says "hide or douse your lantern!"
- Ringing all three unlocks the exit: "All 3 Soul-Bells rung! The Garden Pet-Door is unlocked, RUN!"
- Look: a pale silver cone bell on a dark wooden stand, with a violet light.
