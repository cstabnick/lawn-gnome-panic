Two glowing shards of glaze. Both must be collected before the gnome can shatter the Porcelain Kiln Ward.

## In the games

- One shard lies in the Entry Parlor, and one in the room after it.
- Each shard sits on a hideable piece of furniture when its room has one.
- Look: a small cyan crystal with a point light, drawn in the same bright glaze colour as the ward.
- Each pickup shows the count, for example "Soul-Glaze Shard (1/2) collected."
