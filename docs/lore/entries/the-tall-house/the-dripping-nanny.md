A low, wide Gnome-Soul of wet clay and dark cloth that crawls on four jointed legs. It does not stand up to hunt. It comes in under the furniture after the gnome, and hiding under a table does not keep it out.

## In the games

- One of the Gnome-Souls that a house may hold (see [gnome-souls](gnome-souls.md)). It starts in room 3 of the house's order, whichever room that is for the seed.
- It ignores hiding: a gnome crouched under a table can still be hit by it. Its sight is short (6.5 units with the lantern lit, 3 in the dark).
- It hears a sprint from a little farther than most (1.2 times).
- Its presence is heard before it is seen: drips in a slow rhythm, and a claw-scrape on the floorboards when it moves close.
- Its lines are about wet clay and floorboards: "drip... drip... under the bed", "i crawl where the tall ones never look".
- It does not hover like the others. Its legs scuttle when it moves.

## Loose threads

The games never say why it drips, or where the water comes from.
