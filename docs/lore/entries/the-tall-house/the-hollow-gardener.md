The first of the two Gnome-Souls. It starts in the third room and patrols the house from there.

## In the games

- It starts in room 2, counting from the Entry Parlor as room 0.
- Its patrol route runs through room centres and doorways.
- Its base speed is 1.85, a little slower than the Porcelain Watcher.
- The director steers a patrolling Gnome-Soul out of the gnome's room. Sometimes it nudges one into the next room for a near miss.
