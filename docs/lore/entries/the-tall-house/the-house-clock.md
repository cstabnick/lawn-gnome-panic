A tall wooden longcase clock with a pendulum behind a small glass door and a painted face. It stands in some of the house's rooms and ticks once a second.

## In the games

- It is one of the house's giant furniture pieces, placed in the Entry Parlor and the Porcelain Parlor. Its case is solid, so it blocks a gnome's path.
- It ticks and tocks at the gnome from where it stands, from the nearest clock within about 14 units. The sound comes from its own side of the room.
- The Hush silences the clock along with the rest of the house.
- Its face has a thirteenth number scratched in. Nobody in the games says who scratched it.

## Loose threads

The games never say whether the clock is right about the time, or who sets it.
