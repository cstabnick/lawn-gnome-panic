A pale Gnome-Soul with its eyes shut and one finger held to where its lips would be. It can barely see. It listens for the gnome, and where it stands the house goes quiet.

## In the games

- One of the Gnome-Souls that a house may hold (see [gnome-souls](gnome-souls.md)). It starts in room 4 of the house's order, whichever room that is for the seed.
- It sees almost nothing: 2.5 units with the lantern lit, 1.5 in the dark. Hiding under furniture gets the gnome down to about one unit.
- It hears a sprint from farther than any other (1.6 times the usual distance), so running about the house brings it.
- The house's drone, creaks and clock fall silent when the Hush is within about 12 units, and a thin high ring creeps into the ears when it is within about 4.5. The gnome's own heartbeat and footsteps still sound.
- If it passes within a couple of units of a gnome who is hiding, it brushes past without seeing them. That is a near miss, not a hit (the achievement "Shh").
- Its voice is a breath shaped into words, with no pitch to it: "hush... hush... no one is here".

## Loose threads

The games never say whether the silence is the Hush's doing, or whether the house was quiet before it came.
