A tall, thin Gnome-Soul with a long brass snuffer on a pole and an amber glow. It is drawn to a lit flame the way a moth is, and it cannot see a gnome in the dark at all.

## In the games

- One of the Gnome-Souls that a house may hold (see [gnome-souls](gnome-souls.md)). It starts in room 1 of the house's order, the second room, whichever room that is for the seed.
- While the gnome's ceramic lantern is lit, it sees the gnome from across most of a room (15 units). With the lantern out, it sees only about 3 units.
- Hiding under furniture helps much less against it while the lantern is lit (4.5 units against 2.2 for the others).
- Its alert is a match struck on brass, then a flame catching. Its lines are about lamps: "pretty flame... let it gutter", "the wick is tired. it always goes out".
- It patrols the middle of the house.

## Loose threads

The games never say what it snuffs the lights for, or what it does with the flames it puts out.
