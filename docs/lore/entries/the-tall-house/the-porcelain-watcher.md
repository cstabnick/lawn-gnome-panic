The second of the two Gnome-Souls. It starts in the last room, the Moonlit Conservatory, close to the Garden Pet-Door.

## In the games

- It starts in the last room, room 5 in the house's order.
- Its first patrol target is the room before it.
- Its base speed is 1.95, a little faster than the Hollow Gardener.
- The director steers a patrolling Gnome-Soul out of the gnome's room, the same as for the other one.
