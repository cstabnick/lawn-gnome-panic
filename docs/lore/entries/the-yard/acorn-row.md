The shopping street of Club Gnome, south of the town square and reached by the path past the Gnome Creator statue. Its
shops take a gnome's acorns out of the Oakcount and give back gear, hair and, one day, furniture.

## What gnomes say
- "Acorns buy gear at the Market. Chips are for the Gnomecino." (Chicory Waymark, by the fountain)
- "Two shops, one furniture house, and a great deal of pavement." (Rosemary Brickwell)
- "The sawdust is ready. The shop is not, but it is nearly." (Hazel Dovetail)

## In the games
- Club Gnome: the row, the path south of the square. Three doors and a sign:
  - The Gnome Market (Marjoram Crate's counter; the dressing room at the back is Poppy Thimble's).
  - The Gnome Salon (Lavender Shearwell's chair; new hair styles for acorns).
  - Dovetail Furniture, marked "coming soon" (Hazel Dovetail's; the shop is not open yet).
