The armored enemies of The Dry Yard. They stand in for mowers in the desert world of Lawn Gnome Panic.

## In the games
- The description reads: "Small tanks with ears. They do not stop for gnomes."
- The green-tier enemy in the desert is an armadillo, and the kill text names it "an Armadillo".
- The bosses of the desert levels are giant armadillos, called THE GIANT ARMADILLO. Level 2-3 has four of them, and they roll.
