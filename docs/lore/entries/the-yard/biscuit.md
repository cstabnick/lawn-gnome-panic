The Tall Ones' giant dog, known to the crew as "the Great Biscuit". He is good. He is not careful.

## In the games
- Incregnomtal: arrives in Week 3. Befriended through games of fetch, he digs out moles and can learn six tricks (Fetch, Stay Off, Dig Here, Fetch the Paper, Chase, and Pull the Roller).
- Not the same dog as Gnome Company's Giant Goofy Fetch Dog (see ruling R-012).
