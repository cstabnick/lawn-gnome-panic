The pub where gnomes play blackjack around one table. Five CPU gnomes fill the seats and banter as they play.

## In the games
- Gnome Blackjack is set at a pub table of gnomes. CPU gnomes fill the five seats and banter in speech bubbles, muttering every line aloud.
- A bar, stools, patrons and cocktail tables stand round the blackjack table.
- Players can watch from the table, or go First person and see the table from their own gnome's eyes.
