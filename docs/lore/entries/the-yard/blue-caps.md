The blue-hatted side of the gnome battle at the Front. They fight the Red Caps.

## In the games
- Gnome Front: Blue Caps and Red Caps, 12 a side. The team names in the code are BLUE CAPS and RED CAPS.
- Lawn Prix's The Front course: the Blue Caps fight on the other side of the road, and their hats are blue.
