Chervil Hammerclod, cook and owner of the Spore & Sprout: a stout, cheerful gnome in a red hat and a purple apron who measures
everything by the pinch.

## What gnomes say
- "Two stews, one mushroom coffee, and a pinch of salt for luck."
- "Spores in the cellar, sprouts on the sill. Nobody asked me to grow them. They just started."
- "Eat slowly. The mushrooms have been in the dark all week and they feel it."

## In the games
- Snigms: the cafe is open from 06:00 to 14:00 and again from 17:00 to 22:00, with a break on the square at 14:00.
