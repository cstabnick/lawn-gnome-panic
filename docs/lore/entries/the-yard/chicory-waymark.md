The guide by the fountain in Club Gnome's square: cheerful, bespectacled, and the one gnome who knows where everything
is. Chicory is the only gnome who speaks to the player as a player, and only to explain controls and places (R-134).

## What gnomes say
- "Welcome! Shops line the edges; the fountain keeps the middle."
- "Acorns buy gear at the Market. Chips are for the Gnomecino."
- "Mind the mowers. They cross the square and never look down."

## In the games
- Club Gnome: stands by the fountain in the town square. Until the player has opened the guide once, Chicory calls out
  now and then and the Help button on the HUD pulses.
- The guide panel ("Hi! I'm Chicory.") answers three questions: where am I, how do I play games (tap the yellow button,
  or press Shift+Tab), and what are acorns (see [the Oakcount](oakcount.md)).
