The Gnomecino's cashier, behind the cage. Dry and exact, and there are no refunds: she sells chips for acorns and never
buys them back (R-135).

## What gnomes say
- "Acorns in, chips out. Every chip is counted twice."
- "The house always wins, but the house is very polite about it."
- "Slots are by the pillar, blackjack by the tables."

## In the games
- Club Gnome: the Cashier counter in the [Gnomecino](gnomecino.md). Acorns go in from the [Oakcount](oakcount.md) and
  chips come out. There are no refunds.

## Loose threads
- Where the acorns the house keeps end up. Clary does not say, and the house is not telling.
