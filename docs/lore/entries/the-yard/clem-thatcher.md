Clem Thatcher, landlord of the Lantern Inn in Pebblecross. Opens at five in the evening, closes at one in the morning, and
keeps the lamp lit at the door for anyone walking home late. Favourite gift: mushroom.

## What gnomes say
- "Last orders are at half twelve. Last-last orders are at one. That's the lot."
- "Mushrooms in the shade behind the inn. Don't tell the others where."

## In the games
- Runs the inn from 17:00 to 01:00. The bar and the rooms are through the front door.
