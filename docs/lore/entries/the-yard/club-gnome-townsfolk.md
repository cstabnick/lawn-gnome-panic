The regulars of Club Gnome: townsfolk who stroll the square, the park and Acorn Row on fixed loops, and have something to
say about the weather, the mowers and the fountain.

## What gnomes say
- Thistle Mugsworth: "Lovely day for it. Any day is lovely if the mower stays off."
- Clover Pegg: "I lost an acorn on that bench once. It is probably still there."
- Burdock Ladle: "The fountain splashes a bit more on Thursdays. Nobody knows why."
- Primrose Kettleby: "Pick the crocuses, leave the daisies. Daisies complain."
- Dill Spindle: "Someone left a spoon on the bench. It is mine now."
- Rosemary Brickwell: "My hedge is taller than your hat. Please stop climbing it."
- Sage Bucketby: "Sit on a stool, not a barrel. Barrels are for the cellar."

## In the games
- Club Gnome: Thistle, Clover, Burdock and Sage in the town square and the Gnomecino; Primrose and Dill in Links Park;
  Rosemary on Acorn Row. They walk closed loops and are not shopkeepers.

## Loose threads
- Whether the best acorns fall anywhere in particular. Thistle asks and nobody will say.
