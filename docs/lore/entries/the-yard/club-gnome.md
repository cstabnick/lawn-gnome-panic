The Yard's town club and square, where every gnome of the arcade walks about and chats. Its buildings and paths are
the front doors of the Yard's games (R-011).

## What gnomes say
- "Meet you at the fountain." Everyone does, sooner or later.
- Nobody remembers who built the club. The fountain was there first, and the shops grew round it.
- Mind the mowers: a runaway one has crossed the square before, and the notice board still says so.

## In the games
- Club Gnome: seven places. Town Square (the fountain, the Gnomecino, the Arcade, THE ALLEY bowling, the Music Hall, the
  Gnome Party pavilion, the Gnome Creator statue), the Gnomecino (casino; its card room is THE PUB; the Cashier and the
  Prize Counter, see [Gnomecino](gnomecino.md)), the Arcade (a cabinet per game), Links Park (the tee of ISLAND LINKS
  where golf carts have right of way, the dock out to The Sea where Gnaft's raft ties up, the camp of THE FRONT with
  Red Cap and Blue Cap banners, the Giant Pup's kennel, and a gap in the Fence with Fescue beyond), and Acorn Row (the
  Gnome Market, the Gnome Salon and Dovetail Furniture, reached by the path south of the square past the Gnome Creator
  statue; see [Acorn Row](acorn-row.md)).
- The square has a guide by the fountain, [Chicory Waymark](chicory-waymark.md), and regulars who stroll the square,
  the park and Acorn Row ([Club Gnome Regulars](club-gnome-townsfolk.md)). Acorns and chips are kept in the
  [Oakcount](oakcount.md).
