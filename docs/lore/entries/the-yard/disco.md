Disco is a living frisbee with googly eyes and noodle arms. He whirls a gnome round like a hammer throw in Gnome Disc.

## In the games
- Gnome Disc is a mode of Gnome Golf. Disco grabs the gnome by the boots and whirls him round.
- The gnome flies flat and spinning, toward a chain basket at the pin.
- Hold the whirl too long, about 3 seconds, and Disco gets dizzy and lets go on his own, in a random direction.
- Tap FLAIL in mid-air to make the gnome flap. Each throw allows two flails.
