Doris Fennel, retired, and sits on the bench by the pond every afternoon. Watches the ducks and keeps the count. Favourite
gift: pebble.

## What gnomes say
- "Sit down, love. The pond's been here longer than the bench, and it's not going anywhere."
- "Seventeen ducks this afternoon. Eighteen yesterday. Don't ask me where the other one went."

## In the games
- Sits on the green bench by the pond, 13:00 to 17:00.
