Keeper of the Gnomecino's Prize Counter. Fennel wears a High Roller Top Hat and loves a winner who spends; the good hats
are worth the wait.

## What gnomes say
- "Chips in, prizes out. Mind the glass on the cabinet."
- "Nothing here is free, but the good hats are worth the wait."
- "The top hat is for high rollers. Everyone else, admire it."

## In the games
- Club Gnome: the Prize Counter in the [Gnomecino](gnomecino.md). Prizes are bought with chips (see
  [the Oakcount](oakcount.md)). Six prizes are still "in the workshop" on "Coming soon" placards.
