Fernwick Stoutpot, postman of Pebblecross. Walks the round, and picks up bottle caps off the kerb as he goes. Favourite
gift: bottlecap.

## What gnomes say
- "Post's in by noon, and it's only late if somebody's sat on it."
- "Found a cap by the post office. Shiniest one this month. Yours, if you want it."

## In the games
- Works the post office, 08:00 to 12:00, then the round from 12:00 to 16:00. He stops for a walk round at noon.
