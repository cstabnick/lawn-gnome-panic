The gate that ends the back yard map in Faster Than Lawn. Cross the yard to it to win.

## In the games
- The back-yard map is a set of beacons joined by links: fights, text events and a shop. Hop along the links to reach the gate.
- The title screen says: "Cross the back yard to the Garden Gate and beat the Grand Zero-Turn."
- The win screen reads "THE GARDEN GATE IS YOURS!". A loss reads "YOUR MOWER IS MULCH".
