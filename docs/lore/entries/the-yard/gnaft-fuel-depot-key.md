An island of red fuel drums and a little gas pump. Its crates are the fuel that the gas mower needs.

## In the games

- Its theme is red fuel drums and a little gas pump.
- Its three crates hold fuel, fuel with scrap, and fuel with scrap.
- In the progression, this island comes after Scrapyard Isle and unlocks the gas mower.
