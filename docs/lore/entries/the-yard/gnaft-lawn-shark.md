A fin that circles the raft and now and then chews a bite out of an edge tile of the lawn. Bonk it with F or lose the tile.

## In the games

- It circles the raft, and every so often chews an edge tile.
- The bite is marked by a red ring. Get to the ring and press F, or the tile is lost.
- The end-of-game stats count the sharks bonked and the tiles lost to sharks.
