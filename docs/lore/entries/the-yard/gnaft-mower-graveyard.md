An island of wrecked riding mowers, half buried in sand. Its crates hold the mower parts for the riding mower.

## In the games

- Its theme is wrecked riding mowers half buried in sand.
- Each of its three crates holds one mower part, along with scrap or fuel.
- The riding mower takes three parts to craft. This island is the last step in the progression.
