The first island with junk to mine: heaps of rusty scrap and a broken shed. Its crates hold scrap, dirt, seed and planks.

## In the games

- Its theme is heaps of rusty scrap and a broken shed.
- Its three crates hold scrap with planks, scrap with dirt, and scrap with seed and planks.
- Scrap is what the push mower is crafted from. In the progression, this island comes first.
