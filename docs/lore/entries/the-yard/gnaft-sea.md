The open water around a gnome's floating patch of grass. Junk drifts past, and walking off the edge means swimming.

## In the games

- The gnome's start is a small patch of floating grass, which the gnome grows, mows and builds on.
- Walk off the edge to swim. Swim back into the raft or an island to climb out.
- Floating debris drifts past the raft. A hook on a line snags it and reels it in.
- Islands are solid. Drifting into one docks the raft there.
- There is a day and night cycle with stars and a moon.

## The Yard's sea

The same sea washes round Gnome Golf's island: every minute or two the castaway from Gnaft drifts past the course on
his grass raft (`games/gnome-golf/src/ambient.ts`), and Gnome Golf's sea mower lives in it.
