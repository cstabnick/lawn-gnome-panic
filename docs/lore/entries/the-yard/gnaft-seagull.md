A pest that lands on the tallest grass and eats it until the gnome shoos it away.

## In the games

- A gull needs a tile that has grown to a minimum height. It eats the grass, then hops to a fresher tile when done.
- Shoo it by walking at it, or press F. It drops a grass seed when it flees.
- The end-of-game stats count the gulls scared off.
