A neon bowling alley where gnomes with bowl cuts play ten-pin. Its robot PA calls the big moments.

## In the games
- Ten-pin bowling in a neon gnome alley. One to four players take turns on one device, or two to four bowl online.
- The CPU bowlers are Big Lou, Granny Gnertrude, Robo-Gnome 3000 and Gnome Chad.
- Some strikes, turkeys, spares and gutter balls play a cartoon on a vintage alley score monitor instead of the result card.
