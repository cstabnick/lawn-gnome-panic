Gnome Chad, the Gutter Prince: a CPU bowler in Gnome Bowling with a white hat and a cyan shirt. He is the least skilled of the four.

## What gnomes say
- After a strike: "Bro!" Or "Boom. Gnome Chad, baby."
- After a gutter ball: "Gutter? Nah, that was on purpose."
- After a split: "Split? Bro, that is a vibe."

## In the games
- He has the lowest skill of the four CPU bowlers.
- The announcer introduces him as "the Gutter Prince".
