The Yard's town square, where every gnome of the arcade walks about and chats. Its buildings and paths are the front
doors of the Yard's games (R-009).

## What gnomes say
- "Meet you at the fountain." Everyone does, sooner or later.
- Nobody remembers who built the town. The fountain was there first, and the shops grew round it.

## In the games
- Gnome Town: four places. Town Square (the fountain, the Gnomecino, the Arcade, the bowling alley, the Music Hall, the
  Gnome Party pavilion, the Gnome Creator statue), the Gnomecino (casino), the Arcade (a cabinet per game) and Links
  Park (golf tee, the dock where Gnaft's raft ties up, the Front's camp, the Giant Pup's kennel, a gap in the Fence to Gnomomia).
