Fifty-acorn-cent cardboard pull-tab slips sold behind the bar at the Gnomecino, printed with rows of smiling red-capped gnomes, moss-green goblins, ponies, snails, and spotted toadstools.

## What gnomes and goblins say

- Three red-capped gnomes wins the grand acorn pot; three moss-green goblins wins a round for the table; three ponies, three snails, or three spotted toadstools pay out smaller handfuls of acorns.
- Gnomes insist the game is named for the *trolling* banter at the Blackjack Pub; goblins insist it is named for them, and bang on the bar-rails with tin spoons whenever three green faces line up.
