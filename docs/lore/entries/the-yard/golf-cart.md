A cart that crosses the Island Links road in Lawn Prix, and a riding lawn mower in Gnome Golf.

## In the games
- Lawn Prix: two golf carts cross the Island Links road. They have right of way, and touching one spins you out.
- Gnome Golf: the golf cart is a riding lawn mower. Before each shot it is parked just off the golfer's left, with the playing partner.
- Between shots the golfer and partner ride it to the ball, routed round ponds and the sea.
