Granny Gnertrude, the Granny Hammer: a CPU bowler in Gnome Bowling with white hair, glasses and a lavender hat.

## What gnomes say
- After a strike: "Oh my stars!" Or "Back in my day pins were heavier."
- After a gutter ball: "Oh dear, into the gutter again."
- After a spare: "Granny does not miss twice."

## In the games
- The announcer introduces her as "the Granny Hammer".
- She can be added to a local game with + ADD CPU.
- In Pebblecross she knits under the oak on the green, mornings and afternoons, 09:00 to 15:00.
