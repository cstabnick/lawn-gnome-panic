Furniture maker at Dovetail Furniture on [Acorn Row](acorn-row.md). Sawdust on the hands, and a shop that is not open
yet.

## What gnomes say
- "The sawdust is ready. The shop is not, but it is nearly."
- "A chair you build yourself sits better. Trust me."
- "Benches belong in the Yard, not the house. I make both."

## In the games
- Club Gnome: the Dovetail Furniture door on Acorn Row is marked "coming soon". Hazel is seen through the window and
  tells you to save your acorns for the [Oakcount](oakcount.md).
