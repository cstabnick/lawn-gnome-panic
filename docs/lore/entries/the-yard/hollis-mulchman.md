Hollis Mulchman, mower mechanic at the Yard Shed in Pebblecross. Wary of mowers, with good reason: the notice board by
the shed says so. Favourite gift: pebble.

## What gnomes say
- "I fix mowers. I don't have to like them."
- "Keep your hands off the blade and your eyes on the notice board."

## In the games
- Works the Yard Shed with Big Lou, 08:00 to 16:00.
