The golf course of the yard, with fairways, gnomes putting out and golf carts that have right of way.

## In the games
- Lawn Prix's Island Links course: the road runs down the fairways past gnomes putting out. Two golf carts cross it.
- Gnome Golf: nine holes on a little island. Seagulls circle the shore and a sea mower leaps out of the water to catch balls.
- In Gnome Golf the golf cart is a riding lawn mower, parked beside the golfer at each shot.
