Stylist at the Gnome Salon on [Acorn Row](acorn-row.md). Lavender can cut hair under a gnome's hat without taking the
hat off: a gnome's hat is who they are (canon), so it never comes off, and hair styles change under it.

## What gnomes say
- "New hair for acorns. Curls are a commitment, dear."
- "Sit down, close your eyes, and mind the scissors."
- "Bring a friend. The second chair is lonely otherwise."

## In the games
- Club Gnome: the Gnome Salon's chair. Hair styles are bought for acorns from the [Oakcount](oakcount.md). Lavender says
  "Gorgeous! Your new look suits you." after a cut.

## Loose threads
- Why the second chair is lonely. Lavender does not say who sat in it last.
