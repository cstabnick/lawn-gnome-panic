The fast, pointy enemies of The Dry Yard: horned lizards and racing lizards.

## In the games
- The horned lizard is the yellow-tier enemy. Its description reads: "They are pointy on purpose."
- The racing lizard is the red-tier enemy, named "a Racing Lizard (the fast one)".
- Level 2-2 is billed as "Faster lizards."
