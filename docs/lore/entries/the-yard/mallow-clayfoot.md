Mallow Clayfoot, retired mower mechanic: an old gnome in a faded green hat, round glasses and a white beard, who has spent forty
years with oil and blades and sits on the park bench most afternoons.

## What gnomes say
- "Forty years I fixed mowers. The one that crossed the square? Not mine. Mine went in a ditch on purpose."
- "The notice board says mind the mowers. Best advice this square ever had."
- "Oil, blades and a cup of tea. That is all a gnome needs from a Tuesday."

## In the games
- Snigms: the park bench from 09:00 to 13:00 and again from 13:00 to 18:00, with a cup of tea at the cafe at noon.
- Mallow knows the mower that crossed the square and the notice board about it (see [Club Gnome](club-gnome.md)).

## Loose threads
- Whether the mower that crossed the square was ever found, or whether it went into the ditch on purpose after all.
