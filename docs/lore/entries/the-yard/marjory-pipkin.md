Marjory Pipkin, teacher at the Acorn Club school in Pebblecross. Runs the school as a club, with acorns on the register.
Favourite gift: clover.

## What gnomes say
- "Sit down, and count your acorns. Then we'll start."
- "A clover in the hand is worth two on the lawn. I've checked."

## In the games
- Teaches at the school, 09:00 to 15:00.
