What a lawn becomes after Old Saturday passes on Mowing Day. The crew rakes it into the compost and starts the next week stronger.

## In the games
- Incregnomtal: the prestige currency earned each Mowing Day (`floor(sqrt(clippingsThisWeek / 1000))`). Spent on a 5-branch skill tree with free respec every Mowing Day, while every Lifetime Mulch ever earned gives a permanent +2% production bonus.
