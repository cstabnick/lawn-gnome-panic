Nettle Dibbleworth, baker of the Crumb & Crock in Pebblecross. Up at five every morning, in the bakery from five until
one. Favourite gift: crumb.

## What gnomes say
- "Up at five, down by one. Bread doesn't wait for anybody."
- "Bring me a crumb and I'll tell you what's in the loaf."

## In the games
- Works the bakery on weekdays, 05:00 to 13:00. Her house is the first one on the green.
