Nettle Haybale, postgnome: a tall, bearded gnome in a yellow hat who carries the letters across the Yard and has never read
one.

## What gnomes say
- "I carry the letters. I never read the letters. That is the job."
- "Snail-mail is slow. The bills are slower. Somebody always pays them, though."
- "Got one for you. Does not say who it is from. They never do."

## In the games
- Snigms: the post office from 07:00 to 15:00, the square until 17:00, then the park until 20:00.
- Nettle is the one neighbour who knows the bills come in, and says so without saying who posts them.

## Loose threads
- Who posts the bills. Nettle carries them and does not know, and says so with a shrug.
