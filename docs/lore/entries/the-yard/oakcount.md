A gnome's acorn account: the one purse every game in the Yard shares. It shows the acorns and chips in the top left
corner of Club Gnome, and it follows the gnome from game to game.

## What gnomes say
- "Acorns are your money, kept in your Oakcount (top left)." (Chicory Waymark)
- "Acorns in, chips out. Every chip is counted twice." (Clary Tillbox, at the Gnomecino cage)

## In the games
- Club Gnome: acorns go in from the welcome gift, the daily visit gift, and the acorns lying under the trees (walk over
  one to pick it up). Acorns go out at the shops on [Acorn Row](acorn-row.md).
- One way into the [Gnomecino](gnomecino.md): acorns buy chips, and chips never turn back into acorns. The house keeps
  the acorns. Chips buy only Prize Counter prizes.
