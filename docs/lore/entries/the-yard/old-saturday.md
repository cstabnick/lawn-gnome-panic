The runaway riding mower that rounds the Bare Patch every Mowing Day. Nobody is in the seat; who starts it each week is a loose thread.

## In the games
- Incregnomtal: sweeps the lawn at the end of every week (or when called early with the shed bell), turning the week's Clippings into Mulch.
- Buying the capstone node "Tame the Mower" lets the crew climb aboard and steer Old Saturday across the lawn to win Yard of the Year.
