The alley's robot PA announcer: an over-the-top voice that calls the big moments with air horns, organ and a sad trombone.

## What gnomes say
- Lines are spoken by the browser's speech voice, pitched down and slowed for the robot effect.
- The announcer calls strikes, doubles, turkeys, hambones, spares, splits and gutter balls itself.

## In the games
- The announcer can be switched off on the title screen.
- Its stingers are an air horn, an organ and a sad trombone.
