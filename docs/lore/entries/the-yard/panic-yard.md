The back yard of Lawn Gnome Panic, where a gnome dodges mowers for golden acorns. Lawn Prix runs a race course round the same yard.

## In the games
- Lawn Gnome Panic: a gnome dodges lawn mowers in 3D and collects golden acorns across a garden lawn.
- Lawn Prix's Panic Yard course: the road runs round the yard, past a cottage, a shed and a garden.
- Runaway mowers cross the road on this course. Touch one and you spin out.
