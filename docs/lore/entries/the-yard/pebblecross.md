A terrace town of nine homes round a village green with a pond, a bakery, a post office, a potting shed, the Yard Shed,
the Acorn Club school, the Lantern Inn and an old oak. It sits on the far side of the Garden Gate from the Panic Yard (R-033).

## What gnomes say
- "Mind the pond edge, the pebbles are loose again." Somebody always is.
- "Bread's out at five, and it's gone by seven." Nettle keeps the Crumb & Crock to its hours and nobody argues.
- "Pop in at the Lantern before one, or you'll be talking to the door." Clem shuts up at one, on the dot.

## In the games
- Pebblecross: a single-player town on a clock. Each resident keeps their own hours (the bakery, the post office round,
  the potting shed, the Yard Shed, the school, the inn, the bench by the pond) and the day runs from 07:00 on a Monday.
- Forage: thirteen spots round the green and the pond, the oak, the kerb by the post office and the bakery back step give
  the gifts the residents like. Friendship grows with gifts.
- The town is one level, built in the level editor's format, so its places are markers and its buildings carry labels.

## Loose threads
- Who planted the oak. Gertrude says it was there before the village. Nobody has asked the oak.
- Why the pond is never dry, even in the hot weeks. The residents mention it and change the subject.
