The red-hatted side of the gnome battle at the Front. They fight the Blue Caps.

## In the games
- Gnome Front: Red Caps and Blue Caps, 12 a side. The team names in the code are RED CAPS and BLUE CAPS.
- Lawn Prix's The Front course: the Red Caps fight on one side of the road, and their hats are red.
