The green, yellow and red mowers. Gnomes ride them in Gnome Front, and they chase the gnome in Lawn Gnome Panic.

## In the games
- Gnome Front: ride the green, yellow and red riding mowers to get around fast. The Green Mower is the slowest, the Yellow Mower is in between, and the Red Mower is the fast one with the least health.
- Lawn Gnome Panic: the same three tiers are enemies, named "a Green Mower", "a Yellow Mower" and "a Red Mower (the fast one)". The smallest tier is "a Push Reel Mower".
- Lawn Prix: runaway mowers cross the road on Garden Loop, Panic Yard and The Front.
