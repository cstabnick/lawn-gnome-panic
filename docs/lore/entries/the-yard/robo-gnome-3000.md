Robo-Gnome 3000, the Bowling Machine: a grey robot gnome and the most skilled CPU bowler in Gnome Bowling.

## What gnomes say
- After a strike: "Strike protocol complete. Beep boop."
- After a gutter ball: "Error. Gutter detected. Rebooting."
- After a split: "Split detected. Probability low."

## In the games
- It has the highest skill of the four CPU bowlers. Its tests check that it averages more pins than Gnome Chad.
- The announcer introduces it as "the Bowling Machine".
