A mower that leaps out of the water like a fish to catch a golf ball.

## In the games
- A ball that lands in water splashes, costs +1 stroke and goes back to where it was hit from.
- One time in four, a sea mower leaps out of the water and catches the ball. The message reads "A sea mower got it!"
- It is the same penalty as a splash. The leap is just a show.
- The same water rule applies in Gnome Disc, Together and the party slice.
