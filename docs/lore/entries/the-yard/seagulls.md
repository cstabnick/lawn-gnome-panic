The gulls that circle the shore of Island Links, with a "kee-ow" call every now and then.

## In the games
- Six gulls circle a point just offshore. The point moves when the hole changes.
- A gull call plays every 8 to 20 seconds. It is two short downward chirps.
- The gulls are ambient. They read the game state and never change it.
