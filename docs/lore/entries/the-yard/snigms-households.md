A gnome's home life in the Yard: one gnome, one cottage on its own lawn plot, and a job to pay the bills. The Tall Ones'
rent arrives as snail-mail, and it is paid in acorns.

## What gnomes say
- "The bills come every midnight. Nobody sees who posts them."
- "Carpool's at the corner. Mind the mailbox on the way."

## The truth
A household is one gnome in a small house on a lot of lawn. The house is built wall by wall, and a gnome needs its bed,
toilet, bath, fridge, stove and telly to stay well. The money is acorns, the same treasure the Yard counts in everywhere
(see [acorns](../core/acorns.md)). The bills are the Tall Ones' rent in snail-mail form: they arrive at midnight and
are never signed.

The four jobs the gnome can take:
- **Gardener**: weeds, hedges and fertiliser, early starts.
- **Mailgnome**: delivers letters across the Yard before the dogs wake.
- **Mushroom Chef**: evening shifts at the Spore & Sprout.
- **Mower Mechanic**: oil, blades and the occasional escaped mower.

The gnome walks to a carpool stop by a mailbox at the lot's south-west corner to leave for work and comes back there.

## In the games
- Snigms: the house, the needs (hunger, energy, bladder, hygiene, fun), the traits (Neat, Glutton, Sleepy, Goofy,
  Hustler) and the four jobs, with three levels each.

## Loose threads
- Who posts the bills, and who the Tall Ones are paying, stays unanswered. Nobody in a Snigms house has seen a sender.
- Whether the Spore & Sprout's mushrooms come from the same lawn as the rest of the Yard is not said.
