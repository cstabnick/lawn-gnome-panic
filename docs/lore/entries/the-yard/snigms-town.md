The row of shops and cottages behind the fountain on the Yard's Town Square. A gnome's cottage is a walk from here, and
the evening out is spent on the back row: the Spore & Sprout, the post office, the park and the square itself.

## What gnomes say
- "Meet you at the fountain." Everybody says it. Nobody ever has the fountain in mind.
- "Mind the mowers. The notice board says so, and the notice board has never been wrong."

## The places
- **The Spore & Sprout**: a mushroom cafe with a mushroom coffee for a few acorns. The Mushroom Chef's shifts are here.
- **The post office**: where the letters come in before the postgnome takes them round the Yard. The bills arrive from
  here, and nobody at the post office has seen who sends them.
- **The fountain and the square**: the stone square with the fountain at its heart. Every gnome of the back row meets here.
- **The park**: a bench, a picnic table, a hedge and a broadleaf tree. The old gnomes sit here in the afternoons.
- **The cottages**: a small home behind each neighbour, on the lane off the back row.

## The truth
The back row is the Town Square's quiet end (see [Club Gnome](club-gnome.md) and ruling R-033). It is where the gnomes who
work for the Yard live and shop, and where the snigms who live on a lawn plot come to be seen. The bills still arrive as
snail-mail at midnight, and a gnome can spend the evening out here for a few acorns of cheer.

## In the games
- Snigms: the town is a level document (`games/snigms/src/data/levels/town.level.json`), edited in the level editor. The
  gnome walks to the gate, travels, and comes back for bedtime. The neighbours keep to a routine (see the neighbour entries).

## Loose threads
- Where the Spore & Sprout gets its mushrooms, and whether they are from the same lawn as the rest of the Yard, is not said.
- Who posts the bills, and why the post office never opens its letters, stays unanswered.
