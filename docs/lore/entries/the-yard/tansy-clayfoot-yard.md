Tansy Clayfoot, gardener at the Potting Shed in Pebblecross. Grows whatever the green will take and gives away the rest.
Favourite gift: dandelion. Not to be confused with the fixer in Turfton (apocrypha), who is a different gnome.

## What gnomes say
- "If it's green, it's a plant. If it's a plant, it wants a pot."
- "Dandelions are weeds to some and salad to me. Bring me one and find out which."

## In the games
- Works the Potting Shed, 09:00 to 17:00. Her house is the one next to the shed.
