The dirt lot behind the Panic Yard fence where Foreman Yarrow Teacup's crew tries to grow a proper lawn.

## In the games
- Incregnomtal: its wooden sign changes as the lawn's Charm rises — "The Bare Patch", "The Patch", "The Lawn", "The Good Lawn", "Pride of the Fence", and finally "Yard of the Year".
- Lawn Prix karts buzz past on the road behind the fence, and stray acorns and golf balls occasionally land on the grass.
