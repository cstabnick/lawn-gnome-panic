The biggest machine in the yard: a green and yellow harvester too tall to jump over.

## What gnomes say
- Banner when it arrives: "A COMBINE APPROACHES. Of course there is a boss fight. This is a roguelike."
- Banner when it falls: "COMBINE DEFEATED. The lawn is safe. For now. (It is never safe.)"
- Lobby tip: "The combine arrives at 4:00. It is not here to help."

## In the games
- Lawn Gnome Panic: the boss of the lawn. Level 1-3 has three of them. Its boss bar reads THE COMBINE.
- Gnome Front: a vehicle you can ride. It rolls over hedges, pots and stones, and smaller vehicles give way to it. Bots circle a flag until it is owned.
- Lawn Prix: a background vehicle that crosses The Front road.
- In the desert world the boss is THE GIANT ARMADILLO instead (see The Dry Yard).
