The battle front of the yard, where Red Caps and Blue Caps fight over five lawn landmarks. Lawn Prix's The Front course races straight through it.

## In the games
- Gnome Front: 12 gnomes a side, you plus bots, fight over five flags. The flags are named Birdbath, Veggie Patch, Fountain, Shed and Compost Heap. Holding more flags drains the enemy's tickets.
- Lawn Prix's The Front course: Red Caps and Blue Caps fight on both sides of the road.
- THE COMBINE crosses the road on this course. The course blurb says: "Mind THE COMBINE."
