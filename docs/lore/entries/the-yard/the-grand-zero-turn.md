The boss ship at the end of Faster Than Lawn's back yard. You must beat it to reach the Garden Gate.

## In the games
- In the code the boss enemy ship is named The Grand Zero-Turn.
- The title screen says: "Cross the back yard to the Garden Gate and beat the Grand Zero-Turn."
