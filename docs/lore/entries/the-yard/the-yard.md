The shared back yard of the arcade games. Most of the games happen next door to each other, and their vehicles cross the road between them.

## In the games
- Lawn Prix: its four courses sit beside each other. Garden Loop, Panic Yard, The Front and Island Links.
- Lawn Gnome Panic (sample-3d): the garden lawn, and a desert world.
- Gnome Front: the Red Caps and Blue Caps battle at the Front.
- Gnome Golf: golf on a little island. Island Links shows gnomes playing golf beside the road.
- Gnome Bowling: a neon alley. Gnome Blackjack: a pub table.
- Faster Than Lawn: a back-yard map that leads to the Garden Gate.
- Gnome Party and Gnomylpics: the party board and the Gnome Olympics.
- Music Room and Mower Chain: the other games in the yard.
