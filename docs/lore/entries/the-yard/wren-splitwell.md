Wren Splitwell: a young gnome in a teal hat and an orange coat, bored of the square and certain the Arcade is the only real
place in the Yard.

## What gnomes say
- "Meet you at the fountain. Everybody says that. Nobody ever has the fountain in mind."
- "The Arcade has a cabinet that eats acorns. I am going to beat it one day."
- "If you see a mower coming, do not wave at it. I learned that the hard way."

## In the games
- Snigms: the square in the morning, the park in the afternoon and the square again in the evening.
- Wren is the neighbour who runs about the most. When a gnome is in town, Wren comes to see it.

## Loose threads
- What happened to the mower Wren waved at, and why Wren will not say.
