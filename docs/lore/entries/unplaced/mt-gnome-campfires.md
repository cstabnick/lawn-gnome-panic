Three campfires up the cliff. They are rest points and checkpoints: a downed climber respawns at the last one reached.

## In the games

- Each campfire is a ring of stones around a fire, on its own rest plateau.
- Reaching one makes it the active checkpoint. A higher one replaces it.
- A downed climber presses C to respawn at the checkpoint.
- The stamina readout shows FIRE when the climber is near one.
