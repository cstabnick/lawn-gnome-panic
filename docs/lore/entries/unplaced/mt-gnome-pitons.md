Anchors a climber drives into the rock with R. Resting on one, or climbing near one, costs less stamina.

## In the games

- Press R to place a piton. The HUD counts them, starting at 2.
- Near a piton, the stamina drain on steep rock is halved.
- Resting on a piton lets the climber recover stamina.
