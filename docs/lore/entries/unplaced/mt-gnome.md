A procedural mountain for co-op climbing in first person. Its cliff is a grid of ledges, plateaus and boulders, and its summit has a flag and a beacon.

## In the games

- The default mountain is generated from seed 424242.
- Three campfire rest plateaus sit up the cliff. They are checkpoints.
- The summit plateau sits at height 145, with a summit flag and a beacon.
- Downed climbers respawn at a checkpoint, or wait for a teammate to revive them.
