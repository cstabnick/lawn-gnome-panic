## Goal
The board feels alive and looks richer: every piece gently waves (a slow sway and bob that ripples across the board like wind over a garden), pieces cast soft shadows so they look 3D, the board sits in a framed, raised tray, and the level background is a pretty gradient instead of a flat colour.

## Change
- `games/gnome-sweet-gnome/src/view/pieceSprite.ts`: size everything from the texture's real width (no 64 constant). Put the art inside an inner Container named `'art'` so the waving can transform it without fighting the outer container's tweens (BoardView tweens the outer container's x/y/scale/alpha/angle). Under the art, add a soft shadow: the same texture tinted black (`setTint(0x000000)`), alpha 0.25, offset +4% of cell down and right, slightly squashed (scaleY 0.9) — so each piece looks lifted off the grass.
- `games/gnome-sweet-gnome/src/view/BoardView.ts`:
  - **Wave**: one `scene.events.on('update', ...)` handler (removed in `destroy()`), not one tween per piece. Each frame, for every piece container in the map, get its `'art'` child and set `angle = 4 * sin(t * 1.6 + phase)`, `y = -0.025 * cell * (0.5 + 0.5 * sin(t * 2.1 + phase))` (a small hop), and `scaleY = 1 + 0.03 * sin(t * 2.1 + phase + 0.6)` (squash and stretch), where `t` is seconds and `phase = (col * 0.55 + row * 0.35)` from the piece's current cell (keep a `Map<id, {r, c}>` updated in resync/after falls, or compute from the container's x/y). This makes a diagonal wave roll across the board. Gnome pieces wave a little more (angle 7). The shadow does not move with the art (it stays put, so the hop reads as 3D). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: if `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches`, use 1/4 of the amplitudes.
  - **Board tray**: behind the cells, a Graphics frame: a drop shadow (dark, alpha 0.35, offset 6 px down, drawn as a rounded rect a bit larger than the board), a wooden or garden-hedge rim 10-14 px wide around the board (rounded corners 18 px, a lighter top edge and a darker bottom edge for a bevel), and an inner dark line. Holes (`live === false`) show the tray colour, not grass. Redraw it in `layout()`.
  - Cells: keep the checker of `TEX.cellLight`/`TEX.cellDark`, sized with `setDisplaySize(cell, cell)`.
  - Make every other image in BoardView texture-size independent (weeds, stones, fx sprites): `setDisplaySize` or scale from `image.width`.
- `games/gnome-sweet-gnome/src/scenes/LevelScene.ts`: only replace the background: a full-screen vertical gradient (Graphics `fillGradientStyle(top, top, bottom, bottom, 1)` + `fillRect`) from a dusk purple `0x4a2f7a` at the top to a deep plum `0x1f1430` at the bottom, a few soft glowing orbs in kind colours drifting slowly (keep what exists, but use `blendMode ADD` and lower alpha ~0.12), and a row of dark rolling-hill silhouettes along the bottom 12 % of the screen. Rebuild it on resize the way the current background is. Change nothing else in LevelScene.
