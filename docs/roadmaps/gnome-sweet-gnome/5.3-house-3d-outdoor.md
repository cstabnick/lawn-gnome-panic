## Goal
Every house item and backdrop of the areas 'yard' and 'cottage' looks 3D (volume, light from the top-left, shading, highlights, contact shadows) instead of flat cartoon shapes, using the shared helpers in `src/house/shade.ts`.

## Read first
- `games/gnome-sweet-gnome/src/house/shade.ts`: the 3D helpers. `lighten(c, t)`, `darken(c, t)`, `groundShadow(g, x, y, w, h)` (soft shadow ellipse on the ground), `gloss(g, x, y, w, h)` (white highlight), `blob(g, x, y, w, h, color)` (shaded ellipse with gloss and rim light), `ball(g, x, y, r, color)` (sphere), `slab(g, x, y, w, h, color, depth)` (box with front, lighter top face and darker right side; x, y = top-left of the front), `panel(g, x, y, w, h, color, radius)` (bevelled rounded panel), `cylinder(g, x, y, w, h, color)` (upright cylinder; x, y = centre of the bottom). Do not change shade.ts; if you need another shape, write a local helper in your file in the same style.
- `games/gnome-sweet-gnome/src/house/houseArtOutdoor.ts`: your file. Every `DrawFn(g, x, y, w, h, colors)` draws an item centred on (x, y) about w x h (colors = [main, second, accent]); every `BackdropFn(g, done)` fills 1000 x 700.
- `games/gnome-sweet-gnome/src/house/houseData.ts`: the slots of your areas (art key, x, y, w, h, variants).
