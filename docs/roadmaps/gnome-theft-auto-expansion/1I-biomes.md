## Goal
`buildBiomes` fills the world outside the city: a desert with Fort Sandpit and a working airfield, a dense forest, a snowy tundra, and farmland. None of it is on a grid: scattered, clustered and organic, with villages and landmarks. It returns the colliders, pedestrian points and parked mowers; the ground meshes, the city and the highways are drawn by world.ts.
