## Goal
Make driving, crashing, shooting and looking feel right, and put the engine pieces in place that E14 (dirt bike, rally) and later epics build on: a collision model that does not snag on round or large prefabs, a believable airtime and landing, a camera that never snaps, an optional first-person view, and more ways to lose parts.

## Scope (what is in, what is explicitly out)
In: the eleven items above. Out: new vehicles (E14, E08), repair/insurance (E08), the Options panel layout (E07, but this epic adds its own rows: first-person toggle, screenshake slider if cheap), weather particles (E06), the bug sweep (E03 owns L10 sprinklers, L16 sandpit wall, L23 bridges, L24 raft; where they touch the same collider code, E02 fixes the collision *model* and E03 fixes the *placements*). Do not rebalance traffic density. L93 changes how NPCs steer, not where they go (routes and errands stay).

## Code pointers (confirmed)
All paths under `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/` unless noted.

**L01 collision.**
- `collide.ts`: `pushOutOfBoxes` (circle vs AABB, one circle per vehicle, radius `v.spec.radius`), `sweepCircleBoxes` (substeps of 0.4 r, removes into-wall displacement so it slides), `wallBounce(vx, vz, nx, nz, restitution=0.15, mu=0.4)`. Every collider is an axis-aligned `Box` (`types.ts`), so a round prefab (water tower, tree trunk, fountain, silo) gets a square box padded to its diagonal. Likely root cause #1: scenery colliders are made from prefab bounds in `world.ts`/`biomes.ts` (`addSafeBox`) and are far bigger than the visible shape; #2: a vehicle's single circle of `spec.radius` (config.ts `VEHICLES`, e.g. clog-cruiser radius 2.4, watering-can-steamer 3.8) is larger than the hull, and when a corner pushes along the normal each substep the circle can wedge between two boxes (no unstick).
- Callers: `vehicles.ts` `moveVehicle` (~line 481) and the bump path (~773), `simdrive.ts` `stepSimDrive` (~145, sim physics model), `craft/aircraft.ts` (~57), `boats.ts` `stepBoat` (uses `pushOutOfBoxes` only, no sweep, `speed *= 0.35` on any hit: a boat that touches a bank loses nearly all speed each frame).
- Clog cruiser and the canal: `config.ts` `WATER.courses.canal` is `{ depth: 0.85, bank: 1.5, freeboard: 1.6 }` and the canal water path is 10 m wide (`data/levels/cloverfield-farms.level.json`, id `canal`, `y = -170`), with `across()` in `water.ts` (~232) giving a soft edge of 0.6 m. `waterworks.ts` places `arch-road-bridge` (scaleX/scaleZ only, `ARCH_DECK = 6.5`) and `canal-lock-gate` at `canalLocks()` (WATER.canalLocks `[0.25, 0.75]`) with no `solid` boxes for them. A 4.8 m hull in a 10 m canal with a 0.35 draft in 0.85 m of water is tight; check the gate prefab width, the bridge pier prefab footprint and the `stepHull` grounding (`craft/hull.ts`) before blaming the radius.
- Plan: give `Box` an optional `shape: 'round'` (cx, cz, r) handled in `pushOutOfBoxes` as circle-circle (kept backward compatible: boxes without it behave as today); shrink padded boxes; add an unstick (if pushed out by more than r in a frame, or hit two opposing normals, resolve along the larger normal only); soften boat contact (scale speed loss by impact, not a flat 0.35).

**L14 plane reverse.** `craft/flight.ts` fixed-wing branch: `speed = Math.max(0, ...)` with the comment "planes never reverse" (line ~48-52); the rotor/buoyant branch already has `reverseFrac`. Legacy piloted Crop Duster path is `vehicles.ts` `stepPilotedPlane`. Allow slow reverse only when `onFloor` and `speed <= ~1` and throttle < 0 (cap about 0.15 maxSpeed, new `reverseFrac` on the plane specs in `craft/spec` / `config.ts`), never in the air; check `craft/aircraft.ts` and `craft/hull.ts` for planes on water.

**L18 gnome clipping at speed.** Order of frame in `app.ts`: `stepVehicle`/`stepSimDrive` (~1593-1608), `syncVehicleMesh(v, 0)` (~1645), `player.updateRiding` (~1646), then later `for (const veh of vehicles) syncVehicleMesh(veh, dt)` (~1708) which applies body suspension heave/pitch (`suspension.ts`) AFTER the rider was placed from `seatWorld` (`vehicles.ts` ~799, `v.object.localToWorld`). So at speed the body moves after the seat sample and the gnome lags/clips. Likely fix: place the rider after the dt-sync (or parent the rider model to the vehicle object, as NPC riders already are: `v.rider`, `traffic.ts` ~544), and apply rider lean (`simdrive.ts` `updateRiderLean`) in local space. Also check `driverHands.ts` `poseHands` (stretch clamp `MIN_STRETCH`/`MAX_STRETCH`) and `mount.ts` `seatWorld`.

**L53 more parts lost.** `debris.ts` (`createDebris`, `detach`, `burst`, `DEBRIS.max = 24`, `hiddenBetween(prefabId, from, to)` reads `PrefabDef.damage[i].hide`), wired by `fleetRuntime.ts` (`setPartsHooks`), `fleet.ts`, `vehicles.ts` `damageVehicle`/`setPrefabDamage`. Parts are only the ones a prefab damage stage hides, plus 3 scrap prefabs (`mower-scrap-*` in `packages/prefabs/src/catalog/gta-fleet.ts`). Plan: add damage stages with more `hide` names to the fleet prefabs (mirrors, bumpers, canopy, exhaust, headlight, door) in `gta-fleet.ts` / the glaze catalogs, add impact-driven loss (a hard `wallBounce` impact or a vehicle-vehicle bump detaches the part nearest the contact side, `vehicles.ts` bump code ~631-700), roll-over loss, and raise `DEBRIS.max`. `npm test -w packages/prefabs` and `debris.test.ts` must pass.

**L55 airtime and landing.** Arcade model: `vehicles.ts` `stepOther` (~421-431): airborne gravity is `v.vy -= 22 * dt`; ramp launch `stunts.ts` `RAMP_LAUNCH.arcadeBoost = 1.15` (~308); landing snaps `v.y = heightAt` and `v.vy = 0` (stunts.ts ~326). Sim model: `@lawn/vehicle` `controller.ts` `GRAVITY = 9.81` default (line 14) while `simdrive.ts` builds `new VehicleController(mowerConfig(...), { ground })` (~89) without a `gravity` option, i.e. real 9.81 on a game whose mowers are tiny: that is the "floaty" feel. The bounce on landing comes from suspension (`packages/vehicle/src/config.ts` spring/damper rates, bump stop in `simdrive.ts` ~157 `SIM_FLOOR`). Plan: pass `gravity` (about 18-22) to the sim controller, raise damping/bump-stop restitution on landing, keep `RAMP_LAUNCH` so jump distances (`stunts.ts` `evaluateStuntJump`, `airTime >= 0.65 && dist >= 12`, `ramps.test.ts`, `stunts.test.ts`) still qualify, retune those thresholds if needed. Do it behind one tuning const per model.

**L57 acorn shells.** Firing is in `app.ts` ~1739-1790 (`weapons.update`, then `if (weapons.ammo < ammoBefore)` plays the shot and kicks the camera: the single place to eject a casing). Muzzle: `player.muzzle(muzzle)` / `weapons.muzzleWorld`. Guns: `weapons.ts`, `GUNS` in config.ts. Casings: a small pooled debris system (new `casings.ts`, modelled on `debris.ts` pieces: fall, bounce, rest on `heightAt`, fade, cap about 40) using a new prefab `acorn-shell` (add to `packages/prefabs/src/catalog/`, see rule in AGENTS.md; tint by gun). Skip while swimming/driving? Decision: eject while on foot and from vehicles, off in the water.

**L76 first person + L77 camera jumps.** `camera.ts` `createChaseCamera`; `CameraMode = 'foot' | 'drive' | 'fly' | 'chute'` (`types.ts` ~343) and the `ChaseCamera` interface; `VIEW` table; `update(dt, target, targetYaw, mode, input, world, extra)` called from `app.ts`. Jumps (L77), likely causes found in the code:
  1. `look = clipPoint(pivot, rawLook, ...)` (the over-the-shoulder point) is NOT smoothed, so turning next to a wall/corner flips it between `pivot` and the shoulder in one frame, and the camera `lookAt(look)` swings; ease `look` (use the existing `shoulder` smoothing but clamp the clip distance with a recover rate like `CLIP_RECOVER_RATE`).
  2. `clipDist` uses a 0.25 m march with `isBlocked` on `pushOutOfBoxes(..., radius CAM_RADIUS)`: a ray grazing a corner flips blocked/unblocked per step; clip snaps IN immediately (`safeDist < clippedDist`) by design. Add a short in-ease and hysteresis, and skip boxes lower than the look height.
  3. `chase.yaw` auto-follow (`followRate`) fights the player's drag when the camera clips; the follow runs on `targetYaw` only while `sinceDrag > followAfter`.
  4. `heightAt` rising-ground rule in `isBlocked` (`ground > baseGround + 0.3`) pulls the camera in on slopes and kerbs.
  Add a test in `terrainCamera.test.ts` style (existing file) that sweeps the camera around a box corner and asserts the per-frame position and yaw deltas stay under a limit.
  First person: add `'first'` to the mode set or a `firstPerson` flag handled in `update`: camera at the gnome's head (`target.y + ~1.4`; on a vehicle use `seatWorld`), yaw/pitch from input, FOV 70-80, hide the player model's head/hat (`player.model`) and keep gun aim through `chase.aimDir`. Toggle key (V) and a touch button in `hud.ts`/`input.ts` (44 px target), plus an Options row (`options.ts` `createOptions`). Aim blend (`setAim`, `AIM` in `gunMods.ts`) must still work. Cutscenes, showroom and mount sequences force third person.

**L78 crack driving gnomes.** Blast/melee path already yanks the driver: `weapons.ts` ~330-345 (`ctx.traffic.ejectDriver(v)` then `ctx.npcs.spawnFleeing`). The bullet path (`weapons.ts` vehicle loops at ~386, ~418, ~454; `app.ts` `hitTest` ~1183 only asks `npcs.hitTest`) damages the vehicle (`VEHICLE_SHOT_DAMAGE`) but never the driver. Plan: add a driver hit zone (a sphere at `seatWorld(v)` + head height, radius about 0.45) in the hitscan vehicle loops; a hit cracks the NPC driver through the same code as a cracked pedestrian (`app.ts` ~1183-1200: `npcs.crack`, `quests.onCracked`, `watch.addHeat(HEAT.crackPedestrian)`, achievements `crackAchievements`), and the car coasts to a stop (`traffic.ts` `ejectDriver` ~843 removes it from the population; add a `crackDriver(v)` that leaves the car parked). Also allow punching a driver from the side (the on-foot melee in `weapons.ts` ~325). Cops (`v.driver === 'police'`) keep their own path (`watch.crack`).

**L91 civilian mods.** `traffic.ts` spawns ghosts/vehicles via `createVehicle`; dressed vehicles carry `v.look` (a `@lawn/glaze` `Loadout`, `vehicles.ts` `createVehicle` ~261, `setVehicleLook`). `fleet.ts` `applyVariant`/`FLEET` already gives parked cars variants. Plan: in traffic spawn, with probability about 0.15 roll 1-2 random mods (paint finish, tyre, lamp, spoiler, ornament) from the glaze catalogs through `normalizeLoadout`, by body kind, using a seeded rng so impostors (`trafficImpostors.ts`) and online sync (`vehicleSync`) stay consistent. Mods must not give gameplay stats (cosmetic only for NPCs).

**L93 NPC driving.** NPC cars are steered in `traffic.ts` (per-driver state `drivers`, wedge recovery `wedgedT`/`reverseSteer`, `finish()` ~line 445), which only produces `{ throttle, steer }` and calls `stepVehicle(v, c, dt, world)` (`vehicles.ts` `stepOther`): the ARCADE model (`v.yaw -= steer * turnRate * grip * dt`, instant heading, no weight). Only the player's vehicle gets the physics chassis: `simdrive.ts` `createSimDrive(v)` (`new VehicleController(mowerConfig(v.spec, current, ...))`) and `stepSimDrive`, enabled by `simActive()` = `!current.arcade`. Presets are in `handling.ts` (`MODES`: arcade, casual (default, `DEFAULT_MODE_ID`), hybrid "Open World", simcade, plus silly tunes drift/dew; `vehicleConfig(spec, tune, setup, rideHeight)`), implemented in `packages/vehicle` (`controller.ts`, `config.ts`, `assists.ts`). Ghost (off-screen) cars are pure road-graph maths in `trafficSim.ts` and need no change. Plan: give near, visible NPC cars (and rivals in `raceTrack/raceAi.ts`, the follow leader in `quests/objectivesDrive.ts`) a sim chassis built from the CASUAL tune regardless of the player's preset (so NPCs never depend on the player's mode and work when the player picks arcade, simcade or drift), driven by a pure-pursuit controller (target point on the lane polyline, speed from curvature and look-ahead `traffic.ts` ~383, braking before turns, existing yield/stop logic kept) that outputs the same steer/throttle/brake the player's input feeds `VehicleController.update`. Cost control: sim chassis only within about 60 m of the camera and for at most N cars (reuse `cull.ts`/`trafficImpostors.ts` tiers); beyond that keep the arcade step with a yaw-rate limit and lateral smoothing so the handover has no pop (start with `ctrl.place(pos, yaw, speed)`). Reuse `stepSimDrive` collision (sweeps boxes, bounces). Tests in `traffic.test.ts`/`trafficSim.test.ts`: a car follows a 90-degree lane corner without leaving the road under each preset, and NPC steering does not read `simdrive.ts` `current`. Online mirrors send pose, not chassis (`vehicleSync`, `remotes.ts`), so they are unaffected. Measure with `perfOverlay.ts`.

- Camera first person vs aim and shoulder logic is intricate: first person is a separate branch at the top of `update`, never mixed into the chase maths.
- Clog cruiser/canal may be a world-placement issue (lock gate or bridge pier box) rather than the hull circle: reproduce with a script in `boats.test.ts` style (drive the real world, `buildWorld`) before changing radii. If a placement is the cause, fix the placement here and tell E03.
- L53 touches many prefabs: cap it at about 6 new detachable parts across the fleet bodies; not a per-vehicle rework.
