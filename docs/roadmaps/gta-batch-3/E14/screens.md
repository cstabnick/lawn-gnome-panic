## Acceptance criteria

- (a) Vignettes: Rutbottom 5 (Clary's countdown trestle, umbrellas on the rail, Knobbly in the Berm, boots at the bike
  wash, Teasel's cones); Capwood 5 (bruised cap, abandoned picnic, half-dug hole at the buried woodpile, photographer at
  the Ring, the Warden's fines board); the Capwood Run 6 (the fast clock, hairpin camp "SEND IT", lost at the summit,
  the Pop tyre wall, the hubcap stump, the mower parked in the Gill Ford).
- (b) Signs: 15 at the bowl, 7 in Capwood, 10 along the route (Section Nine, Cap-water, whoops, the berm remembers...).
- (c) Residents: Clary Mudguard, Two-Ton Teasel, a pit mechanic, a mud-pie seller, a spectator (bowl); Warden
  Chanterelle Stook writing tickets, a spore-sweeper, a forager, a photographer (Capwood); Lugnut Lulu, Marshal Sprocket,
  a hairpin spectator, a ford marshal in waders (route).
- (d) Sound and life: crowd murmur and cheers on the grandstand with an animated crowd; forest wind, drips, birds by day,
  crickets and an owl by night, drifting spores and glowing caps after dark; crowd at the paddock, water at the fords.
- (e) This page.
