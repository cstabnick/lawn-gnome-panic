## Acceptance criteria (a)-(e)

- **(a) three vignettes per area.** Estates: nine (lunch break on a girder, the lone dandelion ringed by cones, plans
  blowing off the table, a spirit level balanced on a brick watched from a deck chair, spilled bricks with a "sorry"
  stack, a doorstep with a doormat and paper for a house nobody lives in, a crane bucket of wildflowers, a tea urn with
  nine mugs, a dug-up garden gnome facing the hoarding). Vineyard: a pruning break with an open bottle and a thimble
  glass, the gardener's cold-night watch post, the stolen Black Dew drum and empty fuel cans by the lodge. Manor: croquet
  abandoned mid-game, tea for two with one chair never pulled out, a barrow of pulled wildflowers by a clipped topiary
  and an HOA pamphlet stand. Croft: the last owner's loaded hand cart, a ring of weeds left round the weeding barrow,
  "APPLES FREE. PLEASE." crates. Camps: each kind is a small scene (tent and fire, two tents round a kettle, shack with
  antlers, stand over a feeder, blind with stools, drying rack and bedroll).
- **(b) signs with jokes.** Estates: four hoarding signs per site ("LEVEL IS KIND", "PLOTS FROM 1 TO 1", "ALL HOMES
  IDENTICAL. ALL HOMES VACANT.", ...), the gate boards "COMING SOON: SAMENESS", every show-home numbered 1. Vineyard: "THIS
  ROW IS CROOKED ON PURPOSE", "PICKERS: ONE GRAPE FOR THE BASKET, NONE FOR THE MOUTH", Chicory's "KNOCK. THEN WAIT. THEN
  GO AWAY.". Manor: "VISITORS: ROUND THE BACK. THE KNOCKER IS FOR THE CHARTER.", "PLEASE DO NOT FEED THE TOPIARY", the
  portraits inside. Croft: the For Sale board ("SOLD: TO A GNOME WHO SHOULD KNOW BETTER").
- **(c) a resident doing something specific.** Hard-hat crews walking yard, half-built house, crane; Chard Levelright
  by his caravan; a night watchman after dark. Chicory Snips walking his rows with shears (and telling you off the row);
  the Countess on her terrace; Footman Pell inside. Sleeping hunters at eight camps. Farmer Bramble at the croft gate
  (quest giver).
- **(d) ambient sound and life.** Site hammer, mixer and crane whir; vine snips; frost-pot smoke and crackle on cold
  nights; the knocker; campfire crackle and snores; deer barks and goat bleats; herds grazing and bolting.
- **(e) screenshots.** Above, three or more per area.
