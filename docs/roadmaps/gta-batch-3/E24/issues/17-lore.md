## Change
- New entries in `docs/lore/entries/apocrypha/` (front matter like the examples: `tier: apocrypha`, `setting:
  apocrypha`, `era: now`, `status: draft`, `games: [gnome-theft-auto]`, `related`, `sources:
  [docs/roadmaps/gta-batch-3/E24-jobs-2.md]`), each with a lead line, "What gnomes say", "In the games", "Loose threads":
  - `acorn-diner-menu.md` (kind: thing) "The Acorn Diner menu": the eight dishes (Acorn-Flour Cake, Dew-Poached Clover,
    Grub-Free Root Stew, Toasted Acorn Toastie, Dandelion Fritters, Mushroom Coffee, Mulch-Bean Chilli, Pie of the Day),
    one line each; rush hour when the highway coach pulls in at noon and after dusk; Frank rings the end-of-rush bell.
  - `chrome-knuckle-cass.md` (kind: person) "Chrome-Knuckle Cass": foreman of the Pay 'n' Glaze custom shop in
    Turfton; hands out client work orders from a pink pegboard; judges every car by whether the client cried (happy).
  - `pay-n-glaze-work-orders.md` (kind: custom or thing) "Pay 'n' Glaze work orders": clients drop their mower at the
    lot with a brief and a parts budget; the mechanic glazes it to the brief; the client rates it on the spot.
  - `le-petit-groan.md` (kind: place) "Le Petit Groan": a comedy cellar off the Grand Platz in Pardonlin; brick wall,
    one mic, ten seats; audiences come to judge, not to laugh; it has never had a laugh on record; tomatoes are
    provided by the market for "cultural reasons".
  - `mireille-sans-rire.md` (kind: person) "Mireille Sans-Rire": the club's booker, who has never laughed; books any
    gnome who asks, especially the bad ones, because the room deserves them.
- Update `sage-ladle.md` "In the games": the chef job is built by E24 (not E18): Sage runs the pass in the diner
  kitchen, the player works shifts from the "Work a shift" spot; levels Dishwasher to Head Cook.
- Update `route-south-acorn-diner.md` "In the games": the kitchen is a walk-in interior (`acorn-diner-kitchen`),
  entered from the west front.
- Update `pay-n-glaze.md` "In the games": the mechanic job (client work orders at the Turfton lot).
- Rulings (append to `docs/lore/rulings.md`, format exactly like R-192: Question / Ruling / Applies to / Date
  2026-11-25 / Why):
  - R-196: what the Acorn Diner serves and why the stew is "grub-free" (it is; nobody asks; the capitals are a
    reassurance, not a joke).
  - R-197: who customises client mowers at Pay 'n' Glaze and how they are paid (a mechanic paid per car by the shop,
    the client pays parts within a budget; the "Signature Series" loyalty re-stamp still applies).
  - R-198: comedy in Pardonlin: Le Petit Groan's audience grades rather than laughs; heckling is a recognised art;
    tomatoes are thrown underarm only, by custom.
- `docs/lore/INDEX.md`: if entries are listed there by hand, add the five; if it is generated by `npm run lore`, do
  not edit it by hand (read the README to know which).
