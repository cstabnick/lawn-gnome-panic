## Change
New entries in `docs/lore/entries/apocrypha/` (front matter like the examples: `tier: apocrypha`, `setting:
apocrypha`, `era: now`, `status: draft`, `games: [gnome-theft-auto]`, `related`, `sources:
[docs/roadmaps/gta-batch-3/E25-jobs-3.md]`), each with a lead line, "What gnomes say", "In the games", "Loose threads":
- `turfton-bin-depot.md` (place): the Turfton Bin Depot beside the Compost Heap on the Mossy Wood edge; rounds go out
  every day because every day is bin day; the load is tipped into the Heap (which is why the Heap is so warm);
  "DAYS WITHOUT A SPILL: 0".
- `mags-wheelie.md` (person): Mags Wheelie, depot foreman, hi-vis since birth, knows every bin in Turfton by its lid;
  takes her tea facing the Heap; hands out rounds from a whiteboard.
- `bin-lorry.md` (thing/vehicle): the Bin Lorry, a cab-over lorry with a garden compost bin for a back and a yellow
  lift arm on the kerb side; the HOA fines spills, so neatness is pay.
- `fire-station-no-1.md` (place): Turfton Fire Station No. 1, "the Damp House", on the Mossy Wood edge; motto "Wet Is
  Best"; the bell, the practice shed that has burned down eleven times, the chili pot.
- `hydra-pumpwell.md` (person): Chief Hydra Pumpwell, chief of the Turfton Volunteer Fire Brigade; brass helmet;
  believes no fire should be allowed to finish; keeps a saucer out for Asphalt.
- `watering-can-pumper.md` (thing/vehicle): the Watering-Can Pumper, a red fire engine built round a giant watering
  can; the rose is the nozzle; the ladder is on the roof; it refills at kerb hydrants.
- `gnomazon.md` (organisation): Gnomazon, "Everything, Eventually": a parcel company that sells anything and
  delivers most of it; the smile-arrow logo; a gentle parody of giant online shops, not a villain.
- `gnomazon-fc-1.md` (place): Fulfilment Centre FC-1 in the Dust Flats: the packing floor, the quota, the break room
  that is coming soon, the returns pile of identical hats.
- `kpi-9.md` (person, a machine): KPI-9 "Kippy", the manager-bot on one wheel; speaks in metrics; means well; was
  Associate of the Month every month (it votes).
- Update `asphalt-the-cat.md` "In the games" (or add the section): Asphalt is sometimes found up a lamp post on fire
  calls and comes down the Pumper's ladder, unimpressed.
- Update `turfton-compost-heap.md` "In the games": the Bin Depot tips into it (E25).
- Rulings: **R-199** where Turfton's rubbish goes and who collects it (the bin crew, into the Compost Heap; spills are
  an HOA offence, fined against the crew's pay); **R-200** who fights fires in Turfton (a volunteer brigade from Fire
  Station No. 1; clay gnomes fear fire less than falls, but sheds and toadstools burn; water from kerb hydrants);
  **R-201** what Gnomazon is in Gnome Theft Auto (a parody parcel company with one fulfilment centre, FC-1; its
  manager-bots are earnest, not sinister; associates are paid per parcel with no tips).
- `docs/lore/settings.md`: no new setting (these places are in Gnome Theft Auto's apocrypha). If `docs/lore/INDEX.md`
  is hand-written, add the entries; if generated by `npm run lore`, do not edit by hand (the README says which).
