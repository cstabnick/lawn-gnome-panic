Vignettes in E28's own ground: the customs queue at the Grand Wicket (suitcases, crate), the lost tourist on the
Hatwheel's island (open suitcase, a signpost with too many arms), the mower the Unmown swallowed. Signs: the Wicket
signs and the Unmown sign (painted symbols, no text). Residents: the four traffic-hat wardens. Ambience: day beds for
`continental` (town hum, water) and `preserve` (leaves, wind, water). The town's and the preserve's own storytelling is
E30's and E31's.
