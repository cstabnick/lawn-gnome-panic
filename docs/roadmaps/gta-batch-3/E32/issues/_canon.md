## E32 canon (decided by the lead; every writer uses these names and facts)

- Hedgeworth Links is now **nine real holes**. For as long as anyone has been a member, Dame Peony Gravelrake promised
  "holes four to eight, next season". The card always said nine (R-194). E32 builds them in the **Bottom Garden**: the
  Tall One's bottom garden south of the old south fence, which was a wild strip of meadow, a potting shed, an old hedge
  maze and a vegetable patch. Thrift Hosepipe laid them out by hand over three seasons; Basil Divot named none of them
  (Peony did).
- **The Turn Gate**: an open garden gate in the old south fence (the fence E15's yard had as its south side), between
  hole 3's green, hole 9's tee and the Bottom Garden. Its arch board says HOLES 4 TO 8 on the course side and THE TURN on
  the garden side.
- Hole 4 **Peony's Promise** (par 4): the long-promised hole. Its green still has the **opening ribbon** strung across
  the front on two posts, with Peony's ceremonial scissors (the ribbon is waiting to be cut: the side quest
  `sq-hedgeworth-peonys-promise` has the player cut it), a marquee with bunting left up for an opening nobody held,
  stacked rolls of new turf.
- Hole 5 **The Maze** (par 4): the Tall One's old hedge maze, clipped into a golf hole. The hedges stop a ball; a high
  wedge can go over. Members get lost in it.
- Hole 6 **The Drip** (par 3): a water carry over **the Drip**, the overflow pool below Hosepipe Pond, fed by a leaking
  hosepipe that Thrift has been "meaning to fix". A giant stone birdbath stands in the middle of it; birds bathe in it
  and watch the golfers. The pond mower does not go here; balls in the Drip are a stroke, same rule as Hosepipe Pond.
- Hole 7 **The Potting Shed** (par 3): teed off the flat roof of the Tall One's potting shed, up a ladder, downhill
  over the cabbages. The roof is the only tee in the garden with a view.
- Hole 8 **The Rhubarb Patch** (par 4): a dogleg left round (or over) the Tall One's vegetable patch: giant rhubarb,
  cabbages, a cold frame, a wheelbarrow, slug trails. A small green ringed by giant rhubarb and sunflowers.
- Then back through the Turn Gate to hole 9, **The Ninth**, which is now really the ninth. Course par 34.
- Distances in gnome yards (R-193). Tone: the yard's dry, polite, slightly self-important club humour; no years or
  dates (gnome lore avoids calendar years); jokes about the promise, the mortgage, moles nobody has, the grass growing.
- Ruling for all this: **R-226** (written by the lore issue; E17/E26 may also be writing rulings in parallel, the lead
  renumbers at merge).
