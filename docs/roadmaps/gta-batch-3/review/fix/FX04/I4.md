## Goal
At full lock every vehicle corners flat as a tray. The decision (D31): a visual body roll per kind, fed from sideways
acceleration: the Deux-Pots rolls 8-10 degrees onto its outside springs, two-wheelers bank INTO the turn 10-15 degrees,
vans a little. Visual only: no physics change. The wiring already exists; this issue fills in `bodyRoll.ts`.

## Sign conventions (derived; trust them)
- `accelRight` > 0 means the vehicle is turning right. `k = clamp(accelRight / LEAN_FULL_ACCEL, -1, 1)`.
- Target roll value `t = -k * roll` (radians). For a car (`roll` > 0) turning right, t < 0: it leans left, to the outside.
  For a bike (`roll` < 0) turning right, t > 0: it leans right, into the turn. Both `bank` and `shell` use "+ = leans to
  the vehicle's right" (bank: three.js local +Z rotation; shell: suspension roll + = right side down), so the same `t` works for both.

## Change
- `bodyRoll.ts`: per-vehicle state in a `WeakMap<Vehicle, {...}>` (prevYaw, smoothed accel, current angle). `stepBodyRoll`:
  - read `roll = profileFor(v.kind).roll ?? 0`; if 0, or `v.spec.flies` / `v.spec.boat`, the angle eases to 0.
  - sideways accel as above; smooth it (rate 8/s); target `t = -k * roll`; while the vehicle is airborne
    (`aboveGround(v.x, v.y, v.z) > 0.15` from `./terrain` for arcade, `v.sim.ctrl.telemetry.groundedWheels === 0` for the sim) the target is 0;
    the angle follows the target with a light spring feel (e.g. critically damped, ~6/s), clamped to |roll|.
  - Two-wheelers (roll < 0) put it all in `bank`. Cars (roll > 0): `shell` when `v.rig` exists, else `bank` capped at
    +-0.06 rad (so a car without a sprung shell does not visibly lift its wheels).
  - Parked and unmanned (`v.driver === 'none' && v.speed === 0`): ease to 0.
  - A remote (online) vehicle: same maths from its synced yaw and speed (that is fine; no special case needed).
  - `bodyRollOf` returns the current `{ bank, shell }` (a shared zero object when the vehicle has no state).
- New `bodyRoll.test.ts` (vitest; fake `Vehicle` objects like `bump.test.ts`'s `make()` with `sim` undefined and `rig` set/unset):
  - a Deux-Pots (with a fake `rig: {}`) turning left at 12 m/s with a yaw rate of 1.2 rad/s for 2 s settles at a shell roll
    of +8 to +10 degrees (leaning right = outside of a left turn) and bank 0.
  - a Wespa turning right settles at a bank of +10 to +15 degrees (into the turn), shell 0.
  - an HOA van rolls < 2 degrees; a vehicle with no `roll` stays at 0; airborne (y 2 m above flat ground; `terrain` heightAt
    is 0 on flat test ground, see `bump.test.ts` / `suspension.test.ts` for how tests treat terrain) eases back to 0.
  - finite for dt 0.05 and for a 180-degree yaw jump (angleDiff wraps).
