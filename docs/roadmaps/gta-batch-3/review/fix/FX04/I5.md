## Goal
Every stunt ramp is the same black-grey wedge with glowing cyan guide rails and yellow emissive chevrons; in Gnorwin
Clayfoot Park (`park-fountain`, (-42, 36)) it reads as an undressed black slab with neon edges across the civic lawn, and
Dean's Leap (campus, (1238, 126.5)) looks generic. Decision D21 (option c: skin ramps to their place, keep one small cue):
- parks get timber and turf, with a "NO RAMPING (PLEASE)" council sign;
- Dean's Leap gets old planks and packed earth, a faded painted wheelchair symbol and a brass plate "DONATED BY THE CLASS OF ——";
- dirt and rally ramps get earth and tyres;
- every ramp keeps a painted white lip line and yellow chevrons, so players can still read it;
- cyan appears only as a faint ring while that ramp's stunt is active (airborne off it).

## Change
- New `rampSkins.ts` (mesh building only; no game logic):
  ```ts
  export type RampSkin = 'park' | 'deans' | 'dirt' | 'city' | 'dock' | 'snow';
  /** Which skin a ramp wears: park-fountain -> park; deans-leap -> deans; desert, forest and farmland ramps -> dirt;
   *  clay-mart-dock -> dock; tundra -> snow; other city ramps -> city. */
  export function rampSkin(ramp: StuntRamp): RampSkin;
  /** Build every ramp's dressed mesh into the scene (geometry merged per material across ramps: keep it to about 12 draw
   *  calls in total) and return the faint cyan activity rings, one per ramp id, hidden. */
  export function buildRampMeshes(scene: THREE.Scene, ramps: readonly StuntRamp[]): Map<string, THREE.Object3D>;
  ```
  The wedge keeps its exact shape (same top slope, lip face and side triangles as `buildWedgeGeo`; move that function
  here). Per skin:
  - park: timber sides and lip face (warm wood), a turf top (grass green, slightly darker than the lawn), and a slim timber
    edge board along each top edge; a "NO RAMPING (PLEASE)" `coastSign` on a short post beside the entry corner, facing the
    approach (e.g. w 1.6, h 0.5, post 0.8, white board, dark green paint), set ~1.2 m outside the footprint so a car does not drive through it.
  - deans: weathered plank top (grey-brown, plank seams as thin darker strips laid 1-2 cm proud across the slope, decal layer),
    packed-earth sides (a compacted brown), a faded off-white wheelchair symbol painted on the slope (a canvas texture on a
    plane on the decal layer, about 2 m across, opacity ~0.6; null-safe without a DOM like `coastSign` is), and a small brass
    plate (`coastSign('DONATED BY THE\nCLASS OF ——', { w: 0.9, h: 0.35, bg: brass 0xb08d3c, fg: dark 0x3b2a10, post: 0.6 })`) beside the entry.
  - dirt: an earth-brown body (top a little lighter than the sides), and two `tyre-pile` prefabs against the sides near the lip
    (tucked within 0.6 m of the side face, outside the footprint) plus one at an entry corner.
  - city: painted plywood top (pale ochre) on dark steel sides. dock: steel checker-plate grey top, yellow-and-black kerb on the sides. snow: packed snow top (off-white, slightly blue) with timber sides.
  - every skin: a painted white lip line (a thin box across the top at the lip, 2 cm proud of the slope, not emissive), and
    the three yellow chevrons (keep their size and 0.045 lift; keep the colour 0xffc82c but drop the emissive to ~0.1).
  - no cyan anywhere in the ramp body; remove the glowing rails.
  - the activity ring: a flat `THREE.RingGeometry` (or a thin torus) of radius ~max(width, length)/2 + 1 around the ramp
    centre, cyan 0x38f2ff, `transparent: true, opacity: 0.35, depthWrite: false`, on `flatLayer(..., LAYER.decal)`, lifted 0.06 above
    the ground, `visible = false`.
  All materials `MeshStandardMaterial` with sensible roughness; `castShadow` / `receiveShadow` like today.
