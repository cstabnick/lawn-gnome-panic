halfDepth, height]`, turned by `yaw`) and fails where a sample is on a carriageway: within 5 m of a city grid road
line (`ROAD_LINES` in `src/config.ts`, `ROAD_WIDTH` 10) inside the city, or within half a highway's width of a highway
polyline (`highways()` in `src/levels.ts`). Current failures in your files:
- `diner-rush`: `gsr-cafe-table` at [4.4, 262] (`quests/stages/story/s13-diner-on-route-south.ts`, line ~53)
- `m54-heist-board`: `mountain-guard-rail` at [282.5, 32.6] (and its two siblings at z 36.6 / 40.6 may be close)
  (`quests/stages/story/s54-the-heist-board.ts`, lines ~47-49)
- `m54-wally-caravan`: `caravan` at [466, -75.6] (same file, line ~79)
- `m58-barn-yard`: `croft-barn` at [706, 34] and `haystack` at [718.5, 42.5]
  (`quests/stages/story/s58-the-reeves-barn.ts`, lines ~69-71)
(The `double-pot-tunnel` and `cams-lockup` failures belong to other issues: ignore them.)

## Change
For each failing prop decide:
1. It is a deliberate roadblock across the road (guard rails closing a road for the heist, say): add `overRoad: true`.
   Read the stage's comments and look/marks to decide; say in your report why.
2. Otherwise move it the least distance that takes its whole footprint off the carriageway (onto the verge, the yard,
   the apron). Write a small throwaway script or compute by hand from the road data; the test tells you when it is
   clear. If a moved prop is the subject of the stage's marks, cameras, walks or vehicle marks (for example marks in
   front of the barn doors), move those by the same offset so the stage stays consistent, and keep
   `src/quests/stages.test.ts` passing (it checks marks and paths are not inside colliders and drive paths stay on roads).
3. For the barn: if the barn would have to move more than ~6 m, rather turn or shrink it (`scale`, `solid` scaled the
   same) and report it.
Do not change scene files (`cutscene/scenes/**`); if a scene stands actors at the old spot of a moved prop, list the
scene id and coordinates in your report instead.
