# GTA: engine sounds per vehicle

Request: "make engine sounds by vehicle different with different pitches and the like".

Before: every engine came from `engineModel.ts engineProfile(spec)`, a guess from mass and acceleration, played by one
harmonic recipe in `engineVoice.ts`. Vehicles of similar size sounded alike, the push bike and Tessie had an engine,
and damage or a soaked engine changed nothing.

After: `src/sound/engines.ts` holds a data table with an engine character for every vehicle kind (waveform, pitch range
and curve, gears, whine, turbo spool, built-in misfire, idle loudness, exhaust noise, silent), resolved per vehicle
(fleet variant id, then kind, then the old guess as the fallback), with `registerEngineSound(id, def)` for vehicles
added later. Damage and waterlog add misfires, roughness and a pitch sag; a stalled engine coughs. Other players'
vehicles sit above ambient traffic in the mix and win engine voices first.

Characters: mowers putt, zero-turn buzz + turbo whine, lowrider rumble with a lope, mulch spreader / combine / lawn
roller / boot float / steamer chug (combine has gearbox whine), van / taxi / coach / clog cruiser rumble (coach turbo),
pot rod and barrow buggy two-stroke buzz with pops, golf cart electric hum + motor whine (near silent at idle),
outboard rasp on the small boats, drone + chop on plane and Whirligig; push bike and Tessie silent.

Online: no new messages. Remote vehicles already reach `watchVehicles.ts` as `driver: 'remote'`; they now get mix 1.0
against traffic 0.75. Mobile: no UI.
