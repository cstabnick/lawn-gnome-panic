the tubing on Loch Moss, plus one ruling, and `npm run lore` passes.

## Change
New entries in `docs/lore/entries/apocrypha/` (tier apocrypha, setting apocrypha, era now, status draft, games
[gnome-theft-auto], sources include `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/jobs/defs.ts` and
`docs/roadmaps/gta-sim-jobs-and-tubing.md`). Each: one-line summary, a short "Field Notes" section, "What gnomes say"
(one or two lines), "In the games" (what the player actually does, in plain words, numbers from defs.ts), "Loose threads".
1. `kerbstone-cabs.md` (faction): Mabel Kerbstone's cab company. The Turfton Taxi is her own cab; Kerbstone Cabs also
   hires any gnome with a spare seat by the shift, from the cab rank booth near the Central Bus Stop. A roof light goes
   on whatever you drive. Fares hail you at the kerb; nervous fares, gnomes in a hurry, chatty ones, now and then a
   celebrity in sunglasses. This answers the Turfton Taxi loose thread "Who keeps the meter, and where the Acorns go":
   the meter is Mabel's, and the Acorns go into a biscuit tin at the rank, paid out at the end of each shift.
   related [turfton-taxi, mabel-kerbstone, turfton-bus-stop, kerbside-exchange].
2. `sorting-office-sid.md` (person): Sid, the Pigeon-Drop Couriers depot foreman in Clay-Mart Industrial. Ground crew
   for an air company: the pigeons drop parcels from altitude, Sid's gnomes deliver the ones that must arrive in one
   piece (fragile, hot food, VIP envelopes, the heavy crates, and the mail round). Hates Snail-Mail Express on
   principle, admires their record in private. related [pigeon-drop-couriers, snail-mail-express, clay-mart-industrial
   if that entry exists (check with `npm run lore -- find "Clay-Mart Industrial"`)].
3. `wallys-charters.md` (faction or place): Wind-Sock Wally's charter desk at the Desert Airfield: charter flights, scenic
   loops over the loch, Thorne Tower and Pebblewick, and cargo drops on the Dust Flats; a 150-Acorn pilot's licence
   written on a seed-tin lid. related [wind-sock-wally, desert-airfield, hedge-hopper / sycamore-copter /
   dandelion-drifter entries if they exist].
4. `purser-tolly.md` (person): the Steamer's purser at the Loch Moss dock ticket booth, the ferryman's sister, who sells
   the tickets he counts twice; hires boat drivers (80-Acorn licence) for the water taxi and the Steamer loop (dock,
   reeds, north loch, Tessie's isle, Mossbank). Answers the Watering-Can Steamer loose thread about the acorns (she banks
   them). related [watering-can-steamer, loch-moss, mossbank-boathouse].
5. `loch-moss-tubing.md` (concept): towing tubes behind a boat on Loch Moss: the tube rack by the Mossbank Boathouse (5
   Acorns a tube), up to three riders on ropes, lean to swing wide, hop the wake, falling off is half the fun, and the
   buoy run (six orange buoy gates round the open loch, a time trial). Tessie is said to enjoy watching. related
   [loch-moss, mossbank-boathouse, tessie].
6. One ruling in `docs/lore/rulings.md` (next free R-number): **Question:** Can a Turfton gnome hold a job, and who pays
   them? **Ruling:** yes, by the shift: Kerbstone Cabs (Mabel), Pigeon-Drop's ground crew (Sid), Wally's Charters and
   the Loch Moss Steamer Co. (Purser Tolly) hire any gnome, pay in Acorns per job, and keep a ledger of levels; the
   Kerbside Exchange's contracts stay separate one-off jobs. A gnome can ride as another gnome's fare (online), and the
   company covers the fare. **Applies to:** apocrypha, gnome-theft-auto. **Date:** 2026-11-23. **Why:** the user asked
   for convincing sim jobs that work online.
Do not add a row to settings.md (not a new game).
