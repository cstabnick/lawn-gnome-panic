## Goal
The named places of the sim jobs (land destinations, boat stops, the ferry loop, landing pads, scenic sights, cargo
drop zones, street names) exist with checked coordinates, and the gig generators build a believable Gig for every job.

## Read first
- `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/jobs/places.ts` and `src/jobs/gen.ts`: the stubs to fill (keep every export and signature).
- `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/jobs/types.ts`: Gig, Stop, StopAction, GigKind, Personality, ParcelKind, JobPerks, Surge.
- `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/jobs/defs.ts`: JOB_TUNING (taxi, courier, pilot, boat numbers), JOBS.
- `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/config.ts` lines 77-145: `BASE` (Fort Sandpit; `padX`, `padZ`, `gateX`, `gateZ`),
  `AIRFIELD` (x, z, yaw, length), `HOSPITAL`, `SIDE_VALVE.bay`, `GNOMECINO`, `THORNE_TOWER`, `CLAY_MART`, `AMPHITHEATER`
  (each has x, z).
- `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/terrain.ts`: `waterAt(x, z)` (null on dry land, else `{ surface, depth }`),
  `heightAt(x, z)`, `slopeAt(x, z)`. They work in vitest without a scene (see `src/water.test.ts`).
