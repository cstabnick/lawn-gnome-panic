## Goal
`src/jobs/visuals.ts` draws what the sim jobs show in the world: waiting fares at the kerb, NPC passengers seated in
any vehicle (mine or a remote player's), parcels on board, roof signs, the depot kiosks with a dispatcher gnome each,
the Mossbank tube rack, and falling cargo crates. Cheap: capped counts, shared models, nothing per frame when idle.

## Change
Only `src/jobs/visuals.ts` (plus a test file). Rules:
- Looks from a seed: write `lookFromSeed(seed: number): GnomeLook` (exported) with a tiny deterministic rng (e.g.
  mulberry32(seed)) picking from the same palettes as `randomTownLook` (copy the arrays; do not change gnomes.ts). The
  same seed gives the same look on every client (passengers are relayed online by seed).
- Waiting fares: `showWaiting(id, x, z, yaw, seed, pose)` keeps at most MAX_WAITING (oldest is hidden first). Each: a
  gnome model (`makeGnome(scene, lookFromSeed(seed))`), y = `heightAt(x, z)` from '../terrain', an animator playing
  `pose` looped; `update` calls `model.update(gnomeState(x, y, z, yaw, 0), dt)` then `animator.apply(model, dt)`. A
  floating marker above its head: a small yellow cone (shared geometry and material, one per waiting gnome) bobbing.
  Pool the gnome models: hidden ones are kept and reused (look changes need a new model; rebuild only when the seed
  differs; dispose the old one with its geometries).
- Seated passengers: `setPassengers(v, seats, mood)`: for each [seat, seed] (seat 1..passengerCount(v.spec)), a gnome
  model added to `v.object` at `seatPos(v.spec, seat)`, sitting (`gnomeState(0,0,0,0,0,true)` in local space: the model
  is a child of v.object, so position it locally with `model.object.position.set(...)` and `rotation.y = 0`), playing
  'plane-passenger' for aircraft (v.spec.flies), 'seasick' when mood is 'seasick', 'hold-on' when 'nervous',
  'wave-from-seat' when 'happy', else 'golf-cart-passenger'. Never more than MAX_SEATED seated models in total (skip
  extras). Calling it again with the same pairs does nothing; [] removes this vehicle's passengers.
- Parcels: `setParcels(v, kinds)`: up to 5 small prefabs (parcel-box, parcel-hot-food, parcel-fragile, parcel-heavy,
  parcel-vip, parcel-mail by kind: food -> parcel-hot-food, fragile -> parcel-fragile, heavy -> parcel-heavy, vip ->
  parcel-vip, mail -> parcel-mail) stacked behind the driver's seat (local z = spec.seat[2] - 0.6, y = spec.seat[1]),
  scaled 1.2. Cache built prefabs per kind and `.clone()` them.
- Roof sign: `setSign(v, job)`: taxi -> 'taxi-roof-sign', courier -> 'courier-roof-sign', boat -> 'ferry-pennant',
  pilot -> none. Placed on top: compute the vehicle's bounding box ONCE per vehicle (THREE.Box3().setFromObject(v.object)
  in local terms: subtract v.object.position) and set the sign at (0, top + 0.02, 0) local, scale 1 / v.spec.scale is
  NOT needed (v.object is unscaled). null removes it.
- `clearVehicle(v)`: removes all three kinds from v.
- `buildDepots(world)`: for each job, and a fifth 'tubes' rack at target (-702, -548) with prefab 'tube-rack', find a
  dry, flat, clear spot near the depot target with `findSafeSpot(x, z, probe, { radius: 2.2, minDist: 0, maxDist: 40,
  maxSlope: 0.25, maxWater: 0 })` where the probe is `{ ground: heightAt, water: (x, z) => waterAt(x, z)?.depth ?? 0,
  slope: slopeAt, blocked: (x, z, r) => boxesBlocked(world.boxes, world.half)(x, z, r) || !circleClearOfStreets(x, z, r) }`
  (terrain functions from '../terrain'; check the SpotOpts field names in lockup/types.ts and use those). No spot -> skip
  that depot. Place `buildPrefab(prefab)` at the spot (y = ground), rotation.y = spot yaw. A dispatcher gnome stands 1.5 m
  in front (+z of the kiosk, rotated with it) facing out, playing 'phone-call' (taxi), 'take-notes' (courier), 'look-around'
  (pilot), 'vendor-call' (boat, tubes) looped, its look from the seed `1000 + index`. Collider: a Box around the kiosk
  (half size 1.0, or 1.6 for 'pigeon-drop-depot'; h 2.6). Return spots (job, x, z, yaw) and boxes. Register kiosks and
  dispatchers for distance culling: `registerCull(obj, 'prop')` from '../cull' (read cull.ts header for the class names
  and pick the one used for shops/props).
- `dropCrate(x, y, z, vx, vz)`: a 'cargo-crate' clone falling with gravity 18 and that ground velocity, stopping on
  `heightAt` (or the water surface from `waterAt`), staying 20 s, then removed. At most 4 at once.
- `update(dt, camX, camZ)`: returns at once when there are no waiting gnomes, seated passengers, crates or depots within
  140 m (keep a cheap flag); hides (visible = false) and skips animating anything farther than 140 m.
- New `src/jobs/visuals.test.ts`: `lookFromSeed(5)` twice deep-equal and different from `lookFromSeed(6)`; with a
  `new THREE.Scene()` and a fake vehicle (`{ object: new THREE.Group(), spec: VEHICLES['golf-cart'] }` cast to Vehicle,
  VEHICLES from '../config'): setPassengers adds one child at seat 1 and [] removes it; showWaiting above MAX_WAITING
  keeps MAX_WAITING visible. (If building gnome models fails in node, test lookFromSeed only.)
