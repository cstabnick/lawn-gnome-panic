## Goal
Twelve new Gnome Theft Auto achievements for the sim jobs and tubing, each with its own wearable reward prefab (the
job uniforms), in the shared achievements catalog and prefab library.

## Read first
- `docs/roadmaps/prefab-library/_common.md`: prefab modelling rules, data rules, examine voice (ignore its Environment).
- `packages/prefabs/src/types.ts` (PrefabDef, `wear`, uses 'wear-head' / 'wear-back' / 'wear-neck' / 'hold').
- `packages/prefabs/src/catalog/gta-fish-gear.ts`: finished GTA reward gear (a hat 'angler-bucket-hat', a back item
  'wicker-creel', a neck item 'lure-necklace' with `wear: { at: [...] }`, a hand item) to copy.
- `packages/achievements/src/catalog.ts` around the `gta-carpool` entry: the entry format (`id, game:
  'gnome-theft-auto', gameTitle: 'Gnome Theft Auto', title, description, target?, reward: { slot, prefabId, label }`).
- `packages/achievements/src/achievements.test.ts`: reward prefab must exist and carry the slot's use; each prefab is
  granted by exactly one achievement.
- `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/jobs/defs.ts`: `JOB_ACHIEVEMENTS` (the ids and rewards below come from there).
