## Goal
`src/tubing/view.ts` draws tubing: up to 9 tow ropes that sag when slack, NPC riders sitting on tubes (their own tube
model with a seated gnome, falling off shows an empty tube), the 12 buoy-run buoys bobbing on the loch, and a marker on
the next gate. Cheap: pooled objects, shared geometry and materials.

## Change
Only `src/tubing/view.ts` (plus `src/tubing/view.test.ts`).
- Ropes: MAX_ROPES `THREE.Line`s made up front, each with a BufferGeometry of 10 points (Float32Array, updated in place,
  `attributes.position.needsUpdate = true`), one shared `LineBasicMaterial({ color: 0xf2e2b0 })`, `frustumCulled = false`.
  `ropes(list)`: for rope i < list.length set visible and fill the points from a to b with a sag: `sag = max(0, rope -
  dist) * 0.45 + 0.05`, point k at t = k/9: lerp a->b, y -= sag * 4 * t * (1 - t). Hide the rest. No allocation.
- NPC tubes: `npcTube(key, v)`: a pool of at most MAX_NPC_TUBES entries, each `{ group, tube: buildPrefab('tow-tube'),
  gnome (makeGnome(group, lookFromSeed(seed))), animator }`. Rebuild the gnome only when the seed changes. Position the
  group at (x, y, z), rotation.y = yaw, rotation.z = -lean * 0.35; the gnome sits at (0, 0.32, 0) local, playing 'tube-ride'
  looped (switch to 'tube-cheer' for 1.2 s now and then, every 7-11 s, deterministic from the key); `riderUp` false hides
  the gnome. null releases the entry (hide, keep for reuse). More keys than the pool: ignore the extra.
- `buildBuoys()`: once; for each gate both buoys from buoysOf: a 'tow-buoy' clone at y = waterAt(x, z)?.surface ?? 0;
  keep them in a list; `registerCull(obj, ...)`.
- `highlightGate(i)`: a single ring marker (THREE.Mesh of a TorusGeometry radius gate.width / 2, tube 0.15, emissive
  yellow material, laid vertical across the gate: rotation so it faces along the gate yaw) at gate i's centre, y = water
  surface + 1.5; null hides it.
- `update(dt, camX, camZ)`: buoys bob: y = waterAt(...).surface (only for buoys within 300 m, every other frame is fine);
  NPC gnomes: `model.update(gnomeState(0, 0, 0, 0, 0, true), dt)` then `animator.apply(model, dt)`; skip entries
  farther than 300 m (hide them).
- `src/tubing/view.test.ts`: with `new THREE.Scene()`: ropes([one seg]) makes exactly one visible line whose middle point
  is lower than the ends when slack; npcTube with 8 keys keeps at most MAX_NPC_TUBES; npcTube(key, null) hides it.
  (If gnome models cannot be built in node, test ropes only.)
