## Change
- `packages/level/src/math.test.ts`: `smoothstep` (0 below, 1 above, 0.5 at the middle), `clamp`, `distToSeg` (point beside a segment, past each end, zero-length segment), `mulberry32` (same seed gives the same first 5 values, different seeds differ, values in [0, 1)), `bytesToBase64`/`base64ToBytes` round trip for 0, 1, 2, 3 and 100000 random-looking bytes (use `mulberry32` to make the bytes).

- `packages/level/src/deltas.test.ts`: `DeltaGrid` empty -> `sample` is 0 and `isEmpty()` true; `setUnits/getUnits` at negative and chunk-border coordinates (`-1`, `0`, `63`, `64`, `-64`, `-65`); writing 0 into a missing chunk allocates nothing; clamping at +-32767; `sample` equals `get` at integer points and is the bilinear blend at (0.5, 0.5) of four known corners; `applyPatch` forward and undo; `toJSON/fromJSON` round trip (values equal, all-zero chunks dropped, base64 chunk decodes to 8192 bytes); `fromJSON` throws on a wrong-sized chunk; `bounds()`; `PatchBuilder` (two dabs touching the same vertex merge to first `before` / last `after`; a vertex that returns to its start value is dropped by `build()`; `bounds()` null when empty).
- `packages/level/src/walkgrid.test.ts`: `bakeTile` over a 10 x 10 m rect with a `solid` that is true inside a circle: `solidAt` at cell centres agrees with the function, `undefined` outside the tile; the tile's `minX/minZ` are multiples of 0.25; `encodeRle/decodeRle` round trip for all-zero 300 x 300 (90000 cells: a run longer than 65535), all-one, alternating bits, and random bits from `mulberry32`; `decodeRle` throws when the run lengths do not add up to `count`; two overlapping tiles and `tilesIn(rect)`; `deleteTile`; `WalkGrid.toJSON/fromJSON` round trip keeps every bit and `inputHash`.
- `packages/level/README.md` (new): what the package is, the document layers (copy the meaning from the comments in `types.ts`), the order ground is built in (the game's base, then `stamps`, then `deltas`), the three file forms (`*.level.json`, `*.deltas.json`, `walk.bake.json`) with a small example of each, the units and the +z-south convention, and "a game opts into the layers it needs; `data` is the game's own".
