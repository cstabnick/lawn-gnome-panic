## Environment
Read `docs/roadmaps/level-format/_common.md` first and follow it. Browser tool: no dev server; keep the polyline maths pure and tested.

## Goal
The Road tool: draw dirt roads inside a zone as polylines, move their points, insert and remove points, delete a road, with undo for every change. A road is a `PathDef` with `kind: 'road'` in the zone's `paths`; the game draws it, keeps flora off it and colours the ground along it.

## Change
- `tools/level-editor/src/tools/roadMath.ts` (new, pure): 
  - `nearestOnPolyline(points: [number, number][], x: number, z: number): { index: number; t: number; x: number; z: number; dist: number }` (closest point on the segment `index -> index + 1`, `t` in 0..1);
  - `insertPoint(points: [number, number][], index: number, x: number, z: number): [number, number][]` (returns a new array with `[x, z]` inserted after `index`);
  - `removePoint(points: [number, number][], index: number): [number, number][] | null` (a new array without that point, or `null` when `points.length <= 2`, because a road needs two points);
  - `nearestPoint(points: [number, number][], x: number, z: number, maxDist: number): number` (index of the closest point within `maxDist`, else -1).
- `tools/level-editor/src/tools/roadMath.test.ts` (new): each function, including endpoints, a zero-length segment, and the `null` cases.
- `tools/level-editor/src/tools/road.ts`: implement.
  - Panel: a "New road" button (toggles draw mode, class `on`), a list of the zone's roads (`def.paths` entries with `kind === 'road'`; button text `<id> (<n> points)`; click selects it and focuses the camera at its first point), and for the selected road: Width number input (default 3, step 0.5, min 1; `editZone('Edit road width', ...)`) and a Delete road button (`editZone('Delete road', ...)`). A hint line: `New road: click points, Enter or double-click to finish, Esc to cancel. Selected road: drag a point; Shift+click a segment adds a point; click a point then Delete removes it.`
  - Draw mode: clicks (`viewport.pick`) append points to a draft (kept only in the tool); show the draft in `ctx.viewport.overlay` as a `THREE.Line` (colour `0xffd24a`, `depthTest: false`, `renderOrder 12`, y = `heightAt + 0.5` per point) plus a small sphere at each point and a live "rubber band" segment to the cursor. Enter or double-click with at least 2 points: `ctx.editZone('Add road', ctx.zone, def => { (def.paths ??= []).push({ id: 'road-' + Math.random().toString(36).slice(2, 7), kind: 'road', points: draft.map(p => [round1(p[0]), round1(p[1])]), width: 3 }); })`, select it and leave draw mode. Escape cancels the draft. Coordinates are rounded to 0.1 m. Only paths with `kind === 'road'` are ever touched by this tool.
  - Edit mode (a road is selected, not drawing): show its points as small spheres (radius 0.6, `depthTest: false`) and its line. Left-down on a point (`nearestPoint`, 2 m) starts a drag: on `pointermove` set the point on the live `PathDef` directly and `ctx.touch(zoneRectOfDef)` (live preview, `zoneRect` from `import { zoneRect } from '../state'`); on `pointerup` put the point back at its start and then `ctx.editZone('Move road point', ...)` with the final position (so undo is right). Shift+left-down near a segment (`nearestOnPolyline` within 3 m, and not near a point) inserts a point there via `editZone('Add road point', ...)`. Click on a point selects it (highlight it); `Delete`/`Backspace` removes the selected point with `removePoint` through `editZone('Remove road point', ...)` (when `removePoint` returns `null` say `A road needs at least 2 points` in `ctx.status`). Left click elsewhere on the terrain near a different road (within 3 m of one of its segments) selects that road.
  - Rebuild the overlay whenever `ctx.events.changed` / `zone` fires or the selection changes. `activate()` attaches listeners to `ctx.viewport.dom` and `window` (`keydown`, `pointerup`); `deactivate()` removes all of it, the overlay objects (dispose geometries/materials) and cancels a draft or a drag.
