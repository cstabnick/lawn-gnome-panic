# Rules for the Pay 'n' Glaze prefab issues (epic 1, issues 1L to 1T)

Also read, in this order: `docs/roadmaps/pay-n-glaze/_common.md`, then `docs/roadmaps/prefab-library/_common.md` (the prefab format, modelling rules, examine voice, checks; **its rules apply here except where this file overrides them**), then `packages/prefabs/src/types.ts`, `packages/prefabs/src/palette.ts`, and `packages/glaze/src/catalog.ts` (the product names and blurbs your prefabs draw: your prefab `name` is the product's name, your `id` is the product's `prefab` id).

You own ONE file in `packages/prefabs/src/catalog/` (named in your issue). It already exists as an empty array exported under a fixed name; fill that array. It is already wired into `catalog/index.ts`. Do not touch any other file.

## Every Pay 'n' Glaze mod prefab

- `category: 'prop'`, `uses: ['place']`, `emoji: []`, `tags` include `'glaze'`, the slot word (`'tyre'`, `'rim'`, `'spoiler'`...) and the noun. No `footprint`, no `pivots` (so the 3-unit size limit in `catalog.test.ts` applies: the largest side must stay under 3; these are all well under 1.5).
- `about`: one plain line: what it is, which slot of `@lawn/glaze` uses it, and what tints. `examine`: 5 lines in the Gnomes of Gnomomia voice (see the prefab-library common file); gnomes never know what an object really is, so describe by look. Lore for this shop: Pay 'n' Glaze is a hot-pink-and-chrome custom paint franchise (slogan "New Coat. Who Dis?"); it is part of Gnome Theft Auto's Turfton, where gnomes drive mowers; nothing bleeds, nothing gets wet (fired clay cracks and is glued).
- Authored for a vehicle **1.2 wide**; `@lawn/glaze` scales a mod by (vehicle width / 1.2). So a spoiler is about 1.1 wide, a hood ornament about 0.2 tall.
- The test requires the lowest point at y = 0 (+-0.03) and the model centred on x and z. **The origin is the attachment point, and the model rises from y = 0.** Each slot's own placement rule is below.
- Parts: chunky, low-poly, flat-shaded; 4 to 30 parts; `seg` 4 to 12. Use palette names or `0xRRGGBB`. Use `c: 'tint'` (and set `tint`) only where the issue says the product takes a colour.
- No named parts and no `group` (keeps the whole prefab baked into one or two meshes), except where the issue says otherwise.
- Check: `npx tsc --noEmit -p packages/prefabs` and `npx vitest run packages/prefabs/src/catalog.test.ts -t "glaze-"` (every test whose name contains one of your ids must pass), then `npx vitest run packages/glaze/src/catalog.test.ts` (the id check: it passes once every product's prefab exists; ids owned by other workers may still be missing).

## Slot placement rules (the code that places them is in `packages/glaze/src/{wheels,lamps,fittings,steer}.ts`)

- **Tyres** (`glaze-tyre-<id>`): a wheel with its axle along **x**, hub at `[0, 0.5, 0]`, **diameter 1** (spans y 0..1 and z -0.5..0.5), **width 1** (x -0.5..0.5; the code scales x by the real width). The tread/sidewall only (no rim: a rim prefab goes inside). Rubber is `0x1a1a1a`-ish. Build the round shape from `cyl` parts with `rot: [0, 0, 90]` (a cylinder lying along x), `seg` 14 to 16. Fit the sidewall as a slightly narrower cylinder so it looks like a tyre, not a drum.
- **Rims** (`glaze-rim-<id>`): the same frame (axle along x, hub at `[0, 0.5, 0]`, diameter 1 filling y 0..1) but the disc and spokes only. The **outer face points to +x**; depth goes toward -x (keep x within -0.45..0.45). Parts that should take the player's rim colour use `c: 'tint'` (set `tint: 0xc8ccd2`); a hub centre or accent may use palette colours. The code scales the rim to about 0.62 of the tyre's diameter.
- **Steering wheels** (`glaze-steer-<id>`): a flat horizontal wheel lying in the x-z plane, centred on x and z, standing on y = 0 and no taller than 0.07, outer radius about 0.2 (stock wheels are 0.22). 12 o'clock is +z. The player's hands hold it at `(+-gripR, ., 0)`, so put the rim there (see the per-style radius in the issue). The wheel turns about its own vertical axis. `c: 'tint'` for the rim (set `tint`), palette colours for spokes and hub.
- **Lamps** (`glaze-lamp-<id>`): one lamp housing (for `bar`, the whole bar). The bulb's centre is at `[0, 0.1, 0]` (the code places the prefab so this point sits on the lamp position), the housing reaches `+z` up to 0.13 (the lens) and back to `-0.1` (into the body). About 0.2 wide and 0.2 tall (`bar`: 1.1 wide, 0.12 tall). Include a `glow: true` lens part facing +z (it shows even by day as a pale lens; the real light is added by the code).
- **Spoilers** (`glaze-spoiler-<id>`): origin at the centre of the base on the body (the rear deck), front toward +z, the model rising from y = 0. About 1.1 wide (x -0.55..0.55), 0.12 to 0.5 tall. `c: 'tint'` is NOT used: spoilers keep their own colours.
- **Seats** (`glaze-seat-<id>`): a seat cover/cushion that sits over the stock seat. Origin = the middle of the seat's top surface; front +z; cushion about 0.5 wide, 0.4 deep (z -0.2..0.2), 0.05 to 0.12 thick, with a backrest rising behind it (z about -0.2, up to 0.35 to 0.6) where the style has one. Fabrics use palette names or numbers.
- **Ornaments** (`glaze-ornament-<id>`): origin at the centre of the base that bolts onto the bonnet; at most 0.3 tall and 0.25 wide; facing +z.
- **Extras** (`glaze-extra-<id>`): see the issue (each is placed at a different mount point).
