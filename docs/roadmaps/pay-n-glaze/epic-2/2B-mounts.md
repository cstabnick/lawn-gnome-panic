Depends on: none (epic 1 has landed)

## Environment
Read `docs/roadmaps/pay-n-glaze/_common.md` and `docs/roadmaps/pay-n-glaze/epic-2/_common2.md` first. You own only `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/glazeMounts.ts` and a new `games/gnome-theft-auto/src/glazeMounts.test.ts`.

## Goal
Hand-tuned Pay 'n' Glaze mounts for each prefab GTA's ground vehicles use (lamps, rear/hood/seat/dash points, decal panels, steering grips), plus steering variants that bring the wheel within a seated gnome's reach, so a gnome's hands can hold the wheel and turn with it.

## Contract
