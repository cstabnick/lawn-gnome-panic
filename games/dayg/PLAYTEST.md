# DayG playtest

One session takes about 30 to 45 minutes. Run one on desktop and one on a phone. Start the game with
`npm run dev -w games/dayg` and open http://localhost:5206 (use `?seed=1` for the same world every time, or let it pick).

Give the player no hints beyond the first line. Note the time each step takes.

## The session

Do these in order. Tick each one and note what got in the way.

- [ ] Start at the Waystation.
- [ ] Climb down the ladder.
- [ ] Search the forecourt crate. You need the rope (it is in the crate).
- [ ] Climb a headstone with the rope.
- [ ] Find water at the Tap.
- [ ] Light a fire at dusk.
- [ ] Get scratched, then treat it (salt wash, or scrape and seal).
- [ ] Sleep at the Waystation.
- [ ] Die once, then read the recap.

## Phone checks

- [ ] Could you reach every button: move stick, ACT, PACK, CROUCH, UP, HIT, VIEW?
- [ ] Did the stick feel like it could sprint, and did the look-drag work with one finger on the right side?
- [ ] Did the HUD and the prompt stay clear of the notch and the bottom edge?

## The form

Ask these after the session. Write the answers down in the player's words.

1. Did you die fairly? Rate 1 (unfair) to 5 (fair), and say why.
2. Did you change something in the world? What?
3. Did you want to come back?
4. How long did you spend walking, and how long doing things?
5. What confused you?
6. Phone only: could you reach every button?

## What to watch (observer)

- Time to the first Hatless (how long until you see one).
- How often the player looks at their needs (the HUD bars or the stats).
- Whether the player climbs, and which climb (route, rope, ladder).
- Where the player goes first and whether they find the Tap.
- Moments of "I did not know I could do that".

## Record

Put each session in one note: the date, the device, the seed, the form answers, the observer notes, and any bug you hit
(with the URL, so the seed can be reused).
