# Credits

All models, textures, sounds and voices are generated in code (three.js primitives, canvas textures, WebAudio, and the browser's built-in speech synthesis). No external assets.

`src/models/gnome.ts` and `src/models/config.ts` are copied from `games/gnome-golf` (originally `games/sample-3d`). The change: `GnomeLook.bowlCut` gives the gnome a bowl haircut and perches its hat on top.
